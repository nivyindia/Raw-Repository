# 📁 Document Library — Nivy Global

---

### Category 1: Foundational & Strategic Documents

*These documents ensure your partner understands your business, goals, and brand identity.*

1. **Company Overview:**
    - **Mission, Vision, and Value Proposition:** What you stand for and why you exist.
    - **Company History & Culture:** Helps them represent you authentically.
    - **Target Market Definition:** Who you are selling to (demographics, firmographics, psychographics).
2. **Brand Guidelines:**
    - **Official Brand Guide:** Logos, color palettes, fonts, and voice/tone guidelines.
    - **Brand Messaging Pillars:** Key themes and differentiators they must consistently communicate.
    - **"Do's and Don'ts" for Communication:** Specific language to use or avoid.
3. **Product/Service Information:**
    - **Product/Service Fact Sheets & Brochures:** Detailed features and specifications.
    - **Competitive Analysis:** Who your main competitors are and your key advantages.
    - **Pricing Sheets & Tiers:** List prices, discount structures, and service tiers.
    - **Objection Handling Guide:** Common customer objections and suggested responses.

---

### Category 2: Sales & Marketing Process Documents

*These documents define the "playbook" for how they will operate on your behalf.*

1. **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Buyer Personas:**
    - Detailed descriptions of your perfect customer, including their goals, challenges, and buying journey.
2. **Sales Process & Methodology:**
    - A visual map of your sales cycle from lead to close.
    - Definitions of each stage (e.g., Prospecting, Qualification, Demo, Proposal, Closed-Won).
    - The sales methodology you use (e.g., MEDDIC, Challenger Sale, SPIN).
3. **Marketing Strategy & Assets:**
    - **Marketing Plan:** Overview of channels, key campaigns, and calendar.
    - **Content Library:** Access to whitepapers, case studies, blog posts, videos, and presentations.
    - **Email Templates & Sequences:** For outreach, nurture, and follow-up.
    - **Social Media Guidelines:** Policies for representing the company online.
4. **Lead Management Protocol:**
    - **Lead Qualification Criteria:** What makes a Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL) vs. a Sales Qualified Lead (SQL)? (e.g., BANT: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline).
    - **Lead Handoff Process:** How and when leads are transferred from marketing to sales, and what information is required.

---

### Category 3: Operational & Legal Documents

*These are the formal agreements and tools that govern the relationship.*

1. **Master Service Agreement (MSA) or Contract:**
    - The formal legal contract outlining scope, terms, fees, confidentiality, and termination clauses.
2. **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA):**
    - A signed NDA is non-negotiable to protect your customer data, strategies, and intellectual property.
3. **Scope of Work (SOW):**
    - A detailed document specifying exact deliverables, key performance indicators (KPIs), reporting schedules, and timelines.
4. **Technology & System Access:**
    - **CRM Access (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot):** With appropriate permissions and training.
    - **Marketing Automation Platform (e.g., Marketo, Pardot):** For executing campaigns.
    - **Communication Tools (e.g., Slack, Microsoft Teams):** For daily collaboration.
    - **Document Repositories (e.g., Google Drive, SharePoint):** For shared assets.

---

### Category 4: Performance & Reporting Documents

*These documents establish how success will be measured and communicated.*

1. **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Targets:**
    - A clear list of agreed-upon metrics. Examples:
        - **Marketing:** Leads generated, MQLs, Cost per Lead, Website Traffic.
        - **Sales:** SQLs, Conversion Rate, Deal Size, Sales Cycle Length, Revenue Generated.
    - **Targets & Goals:** Specific, measurable quarterly or annual targets.
2. **Reporting Templates & Cadence:**
    - Agreed-upon report formats (e.g., a weekly dashboard, a monthly performance review deck).
    - Schedule for reporting and review meetings.

### Category 5: Formal Authorization & Partnership Credentials

These documents officially empower your partner to act on your behalf and can be used to build credibility with prospects.

### 1. Authorization Letters (Also known as "Letters of Authorization" or "Partner Letters")

These are formal letters on your company letterhead that grant specific authority to the partner. They are often shown to prospects to legitimize the partner's outreach.

- **General Sales & Marketing Authorization Letter:**
    - **Purpose:** To formally introduce the partner as your authorized representative. This is crucial for them to avoid being seen as cold-callers without a mandate.
    - **Content Should Include:**
        - Partner company name and address.
        - Statement authorizing them to represent, market, and sell your products/services.
        - Specific territory, market, or product line they are authorized for (if applicable).
        - Effective date and duration of the authorization.
        - A point of contact at your company for verification.
        - Signature of a senior executive (e.g., CEO, VP of Sales).
- **Specific Authority Letters:**
    - **Price Quotation Authorization:** Authorizes them to issue official price quotes.
    - **Proposal Authorization:** Authorizes them to submit and negotiate formal proposals.
    - **Contract Signing Authority (Less Common):** This is a major step. It grants the partner the power to legally bind your company in a contract. **This is typically reserved for very mature and trusted partnerships and requires a separate, very detailed legal agreement (a Power of Attorney is more common here).**

### 2. Partner Certificates & Badges

These are used for marketing and building trust. They show the world that the partner is officially aligned with you.

- **Partner Program Certificate:**
    - A certificate or digital badge confirming they are an "Authorized Reseller," "Certified Partner," or "Strategic Marketing Partner" of your company.
    - This can be displayed on their website, in their email signatures, and in their sales presentations.
- **Training & Certification Certificates:**
    - Documents proving that the partner's team has completed your specific training programs.
    - Examples: "Product Specialist Certified," "Sales Accreditation," "Technical Proficiency Certified."
    - This is a strong signal to prospects that the partner deeply understands your solutions.

### 3. Co-Branded Marketing Materials

While not a single document, these are collateral you create *together*.

- **Co-Branded Case Studies & Success Stories:** Highlighting projects you've collaborated on.
- **Co-Branded Sales Presentations & Datasheets:** Featuring both logos and a unified message.
- **Co-Branded Webinar Content and Landing Pages.**

### Category 6: Deeper Legal & Financial Documents (For Advanced Partnerships)

For partnerships that involve revenue sharing or reselling, more complex documents are required.

- **Partner Agreement (or Channel Partner Agreement):**
    - This is a comprehensive legal contract that supersedes a simple MSA. It specifically outlines:
        - **Reseller/Distributor Terms:** If they are selling your product.
        - **Referral Fee Structure:** The commission or bounty for leads that convert.
        - **Revenue Sharing Model:** How shared revenue from co-sold deals is split.
        - **Territorial/Exclusivity Rights:** Any exclusive rights to a region or market.
        - **Intellectual Property (IP) Usage:** Clear rules on how they can use your trademarks.
- **Power of Attorney (PoA - Limited):**
    - As mentioned, this is a significant legal instrument that grants the partner the authority to perform specific legal acts on your behalf, such as signing contracts. **This is rare and should only be considered with extensive legal counsel.**
- **W-9 / W-8BEN Form (for US):**
    - For tax purposes, you will need the partner's official tax identification information to issue payments and report earnings to the tax authorities.
