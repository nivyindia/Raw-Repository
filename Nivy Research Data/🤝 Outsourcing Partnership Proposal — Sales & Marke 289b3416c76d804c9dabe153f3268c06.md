# **Reliable Global Sales & Marketing Outsourcing Strategy (Tier 2/3 Countries)**

### **Core Principles**

1. **Multiple Master Partners per Country** (1–3)
    - Ensures backup if one underperforms
    - Encourages competition and performance
    - Reduces dependency on a single agency
2. **Multiple Sub-Agencies per Master Partner** (5–8)
    - Wide coverage across the region or country
    - Allows you to select the **top-performing 5–6 sub-agencies**
    - Increases speed of scaling and client acquisition
3. **Performance-Based Filtering**
    - After 3–6 months, evaluate sub-agencies and master partners
    - Retain **top performers** only
    - Replace underperforming partners with backups already onboarded
