## 📜 Rasam — Step by Step SOP

1. **Dulhe wale aate hain** — swagat hota hai
2. **Bade log baithte hain** — dono parivar ke mukhiya
3. **Pandit ji (agar ho)** — choti puja karwate hain
4. **Tilak ceremony** — Dulhan ke papa / bade bhai dulhe ko tilak lagate hain
5. **Thali arpan** — chawal, supari, paise, kapde, mithai ki thali di jaati hai
6. **Shagun / lifafa** exchange hota hai
7. **Dulhan ko gifts** — dulhe side se kapde / jewellery
8. **Khana-peena** — sab milkar khate hain
9. **Photos** — zaroor le, family ke saath bhi
10. **Date confirm** — shadi ki date publicly announce karein
