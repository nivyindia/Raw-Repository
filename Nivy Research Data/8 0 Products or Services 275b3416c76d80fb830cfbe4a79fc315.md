### **Core Service Categories**

### **1. Search Engine Optimization (SEO)**

*Improve your website's visibility in organic search results (like Google) to attract qualified traffic without paying for ads.*

**What's Included:**

- **Technical SEO Audit:** Identify and fix website errors (crawling, indexing, site speed, mobile-friendliness).
- **Keyword Research:** Discover the most valuable search terms your customers are using.
- **On-Page Optimization:** Optimize page content, titles, meta descriptions, and headers.
- **Off-Page SEO & Link Building:** Earn high-quality backlinks from reputable websites to build authority.
- **Local SEO:** Optimize for "near me" searches (Google Business Profile management, local citations).
- **Monthly Reporting:** Track rankings, traffic, and conversions.

**Sample Pricing Packages:**

- **Starter SEO (Local Business):** $750 - $1,500/month
    - Ideal for small businesses targeting a specific city.
    - Limited number of keywords and pages.
    - Basic local SEO setup.
- **Professional SEO (National SMB):** $1,500 - $5,000/month
    - Most common package for growing businesses.
    - Comprehensive technical and on-page SEO.
    - Ongoing content strategy and link building.
- **Enterprise SEO (E-commerce/National):** $5,000 - $15,000+/month
    - For large sites with thousands of pages.
    - Advanced technical architecture.
    - International/multi-language SEO.
    - Dedicated account team.

---

### **2. Pay-Per-Click (PPC) Advertising Management**

*Run and manage paid advertising campaigns on platforms like Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising to get immediate, targeted traffic.*

**What's Included:**

- **Campaign Strategy:** Goal setting, audience targeting, and budget allocation.
- **Keyword & Audience Research:** Find the most profitable keywords and demographic targets.
- **Ad Copy & Creative Development:** Write compelling ad text and design visuals.
- **Landing Page Optimization:** Ensure pages convert visitors into leads/customers.
- **Continuous Management:** Daily bid adjustments, A/B testing, and negative keyword pruning.
- **Monthly Reporting:** Track ROI, Cost Per Acquisition (CPA), and other key metrics.

**Sample Pricing Structure:**

- **Management Fee Model:** Typically **15% - 20% of monthly ad spend**.
    - *Example: If you spend $5,000/month on ads, the management fee would be $750 - $1,000.*
- **Flat Fee + Percentage:** **$500 - $1,000 + 10% of ad spend**.
- **Flat Fee Model:** **$750 - $3,000+/month** (based on campaign complexity). *Ad spend is separate.*

    - of ad spend or flat fee).

    - Ad spend is an additional cost.

### **Common Pricing Models**

1. **Project-Based Pricing:** A fixed fee for a one-time project with a well-defined scope (e.g., website redesign).
2. **Monthly Retainer:** An ongoing monthly fee for continuous services (most common for SEO, PPC, SMM). This allows for strategic, long-term growth.
3. **À La Carte Pricing:** Clients can pick and choose individual services (e.g., buy 4 blog posts without a full retainer).
4. **Performance-Based Pricing:** A model where the agency's fee is tied to achieving specific results (e.g., cost per lead). This is less common and often requires a hybrid model.

### **Important Notes for Clients**

- **Ad Spend is Separate:** For PPC and Social Ads, the budget you pay to the platform (Google, Meta) is separate from the agency's management fee.
- **Contracts:** Most retainers require a 3, 6, or 12-month minimum commitment, as SEO and content marketing take time to show results.
- **Custom Proposals:** The above are estimates. Every business is unique, and a reputable agency will provide a custom proposal after a discovery call.
- **Reporting & Communication:** Ensure your package includes transparent monthly reporting and regular communication (e.g., bi-weekly calls).
