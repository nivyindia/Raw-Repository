## 1️⃣ ROLE DEFINITION (SEND THIS FIRST)

### 📌 Role Name

Client Outreach & Demand Generation Team

### 🎯 OBJECTIVE

Generate **qualified client interest** from US & European businesses for approved services, and hand over **ready-to-talk prospects**.

---

## 2️⃣ WHAT THIS TEAM DOES / DOES NOT DO

### ✅ THEY DO

- Contact pre-scraped client leads
- Start conversations
- Identify need
- Qualify interest
- Book calls / hand over

### ❌ THEY DO NOT

- Scrape leads
- Pitch pricing
- Close deals
- Promise results
- Choose vendors

---

## 3️⃣ INPUT THEY RECEIVE (NO EXCEPTIONS)

From **Lead Scraping Team**:

- Company name
- Decision maker
- Email / LinkedIn
- Industry
- Geography

They **never modify or add leads**.

## 5️⃣ STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

### 🔹 STEP 1: Open Lead Sheet

- Follow sequence only
- One lead = one attempt per day
- Max 2 follow-ups

---

### 🔹 STEP 2: SEND INITIAL OUTREACH (COPY-PASTE)

### 📩 Cold Email Script — Client Side

```
Subject: Quick question about {{company}}

Hi {{Name}},

We’re currently working with businesses in {{industry}}
who are looking to improve {{specific outcome}}.

Just wanted to check —
is this something you’re currently handling or exploring at {{company}}?

If yes, I can share relevant options.

Best,
{{Team}}

```

---

### 💬 LinkedIn Message Script

```
Hi {{Name}},
Quick question related to {{problem}} at {{company}}.
Is this something you’re currently working on?

```

---

### 🔹 STEP 3: REPLY HANDLING (VERY IMPORTANT)

### If they reply **“Yes / Interested”**

```
Thanks for confirming.

To understand better:
1. What challenge are you facing currently?
2. Are you looking to solve this in the next 30–60 days?

Based on this, I can connect you with the right option.

```

---

### If they reply **“Send details / pricing”**

```
Happy to.

Before that, just to ensure relevance:
• Are you currently using any provider?
• What outcome are you expecting?

This helps us share the right solution.

```

---

### If they say **“Not interested”**

- Mark as **Not Interested**
- Do not reply again

---

## 6️⃣ QUALIFICATION CRITERIA (STRICT)

A lead is **QUALIFIED ONLY IF**:

- Decision maker confirmed
- Clear problem stated
- Timeline mentioned OR openness shown
- Willing to talk / explore

❌ “Just browsing” = NOT qualified

---

## 7️⃣ HANDOVER RULE

Once qualified:

- Book call **OR**
- Hand over to Founder / Sales / Ops

**Required handover note (1 paragraph):**

- Company
- Problem
- Urgency
- Any constraint mentioned

## 9️⃣ KPIs (ONLY THESE MATTER)
