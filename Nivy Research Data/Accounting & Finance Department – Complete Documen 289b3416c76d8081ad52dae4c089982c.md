# Accounting & Finance Department – Complete Document Checklist

# 

### **1. Strategy & Planning**

1. Accounting & Finance Strategy / Plan
2. Department Goals & KPIs (profitability, cash flow, reporting accuracy)
3. Annual & Quarterly Budget Planning
4. Financial Forecasting / Projections Document

---

### **2. Processes & SOPs**

1. Accounts Payable SOP
2. Accounts Receivable SOP
3. Bank Reconciliation SOP
4. Payroll Process SOP
5. Tax Filing & Compliance SOP (GST, TDS, Income Tax, etc.)
6. Expense Management SOP
7. Internal Audit & Review SOP
8. Client Billing / Invoicing SOP
9. Financial Close Process SOP

---

### **3. Reporting & Monitoring**

1. Monthly / Quarterly / Annual Financial Statement Templates
2. Cash Flow Statements & Reports
3. Profit & Loss Statements
4. Balance Sheet Templates
5. Budget vs Actual Reports
6. KPI Dashboards (Revenue, Expenses, Margin, Collections, Liabilities)

---

### **4. Compliance & Legal Documents**

1. Tax Compliance Documentation (GST, TDS, Income Tax, Professional Tax, etc.)
2. Financial Policies & Procedures Manual
3. Internal Controls & Approval Matrix
4. Audit & Regulatory Compliance Checklist
5. Confidentiality & Data Security Guidelines

---

### **5. Templates & Tools**

1. Invoice Templates
2. Receipt & Payment Vouchers Templates
3. Expense Reimbursement Forms
4. Client Billing Templates
5. Financial Analysis Templates (ratios, margin analysis)

---

### **6. Training & Knowledge Base**

1. Onboarding Checklist for Finance Team Members
2. Training Manuals for Accounting Software (QuickBooks, Tally, Zoho Books, etc.)
3. Standard Operating Manuals for recurring financial processes
4. FAQ / Knowledge Base for internal finance queries

---

### **7. Optional / Advanced**

1. Forecasting & Scenario Planning Templates
2. Vendor & Contract Payment Management SOPs
3. Risk Management & Contingency Planning
4. Investment & Fund Management Guidelines
5. Loan / Credit Management SOP
