# HR Department – Complete Document Checklist

# 

### **1. Strategy & Planning**

1. HR Strategy / Department Plan
2. Workforce Planning & Resource Allocation
3. Department Goals & KPIs (attrition rate, employee satisfaction, hiring efficiency)
4. HR Budget & Payroll Planning

---

### **2. Recruitment & Onboarding**

1. Job Descriptions & Role Profiles
2. Recruitment SOP (advertising, screening, interview process)
3. Candidate Evaluation Templates
4. Offer Letter Templates
5. Employee Onboarding Checklist & SOP
6. Probation & Confirmation Process

---

### **3. Policies & Compliance**

1. Employee Handbook / HR Policies
2. Leave, Attendance & Time-off Policies
3. Code of Conduct & Workplace Ethics Guidelines
4. Sexual Harassment & Workplace Safety Policies
5. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure Guidelines
6. Compliance Documentation (labor laws, statutory filings)

---

### **4. Payroll & Benefits**

1. Payroll Process SOP
2. Salary & Compensation Structure Document
3. Benefits & Perks Guidelines (healthcare, allowances, incentives)
4. Bonus & Incentive Policy
5. Employee Tax & PF / ESI Compliance Guidelines

---

### **5. Performance Management**

1. Performance Appraisal Templates & SOP
2. Goal Setting & KPI Documentation
3. Employee Feedback & Review Forms
4. Training & Development Plans
5. Employee Recognition & Rewards Policy

---

### **6. HR Reporting & Analytics**

1. HR Dashboard Templates (headcount, attrition, leave balances, performance metrics)
2. Recruitment & Hiring Reports
3. Attendance & Leave Reports
4. Employee Engagement / Satisfaction Survey Templates

---

### **7. Employee Relations & Communication**

1. Grievance Redressal SOP & Forms
2. Exit / Offboarding Process SOP
3. Exit Interview Templates & Knowledge Capture
4. Internal Communication Guidelines
5. Conflict Resolution & Escalation Guidelines

---

### **8. Optional / Advanced**

1. Succession Planning Templates
2. Career Development & Mentorship Guidelines
3. HR Audit & Process Improvement Log
4. HR Compliance Checklist (statutory & legal obligations)
5. Employee Reference & Verification SOP
