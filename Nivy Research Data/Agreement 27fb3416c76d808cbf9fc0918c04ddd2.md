### **Key Agreement Points: Digital Marketing Teacher Contract**

**1. Role & Relationship**

- Independent Contractor status (not an employee). Responsible for their own taxes and insurance.

**2. Compensation & Payment Terms**

- Paid on a **per-student share basis** (commission), with the percentage varying by the client's plan.
- Clear payment schedule (e.g., within 15-30 days of company receiving client payment).
- **No retainer, salary, or benefits.** Performance-based only.

**3. Teacher's Core Responsibilities**

- **Training:** Deliver training to startup agencies (students) provided by the company.
- **Sales Funnel:** Conduct **3-7 day double trials**, follow up with provided leads, and **close deals**.
- **Content Creation:** Create, update, and document all curriculum, PPTs, videos, and SOPs.
- **Brand Management:** Setup, update, and manage the company's portfolio, websites, and online platforms.

**4. Company's Obligations**

- Solely responsible for generating and providing leads to the Teacher.
- Provide the Teacher with necessary base materials and brand assets.

**5. Exclusivity & Non-Compete**

- All work must be performed **under the company's brand name** only.
- **Non-Circumvention:** Cannot solicit or poach company-provided leads for personal or competitive gain.
- **Non-Compete:** While under contract, cannot provide similar services for direct competitors.

**6. Intellectual Property (IP) - CRITICAL**

- **Company Owns All Work Product:** All materials created for the company (curriculum, videos, SOPs, etc.) are the **sole property of the company**.
- "Work Made for Hire" clause explicitly assigning all rights to the company.

**7. Confidentiality**

- Must keep all company, client, and business process information strictly confidential, during and after the contract.

**8. Term & Termination**

- Contract duration and renewal terms.
- Termination for Cause (e.g., breach of contract).
- Termination without Cause (e.g., with 14-30 days written notice).
- Payout of all earned commissions upon termination.

**9. Liability & Indemnification**

- **Limitation of Liability:** Company's total liability is capped at a certain amount (e.g., total commissions paid in the last 6 months).
- **Indemnification Clause:** Teacher agrees to cover losses if the company gets sued due to the Teacher's actions, negligence, or breach of contract.

**10. Service Standards & Compliance**

- Teacher must perform services in a professional and workmanlike manner, adhering to company-provided guidelines.
- Teacher must comply with all applicable laws (e.g., data privacy, marketing laws).

**11. Dispute Resolution**

- Agreement to resolve disputes through **Binding Arbitration** or **Mediation** (often faster and cheaper than court).
- Specification of the **Governing Law** (which state/country's laws will be used).

**12. Non-Disparagement**

- Both parties agree not to make false, derogatory, or damaging statements about each other.

**13. Use of Name & Likeness**

- The company has the right to use the Teacher's name, image, and bio for marketing and on company platforms.

### **Additional Important Clauses:**

**14. Non-Solicitation of Staff**

- The Teacher agrees not to solicit, hire, or attempt to hire any of the Company's employees or contractors during the term of the agreement and for a period of (e.g., 12 months) after its termination.

**15. Right to Audit (if payment is based on metrics)**

- The Company has the right to audit the Teacher's records related to student conversions and deal closures to ensure accurate commission reporting. This is a safeguard against being underpaid.

**16. Data Protection & Privacy Compliance**

- The Teacher must strictly comply with all data protection laws (like GDPR, CCPA) when handling lead or student information. They must use data only for the Company's business and delete it upon request or contract termination.

**17. Technology & Equipment**

- The Teacher is responsible for providing their own computer, software, internet connection, and any other equipment needed to perform the services.

**18. Key Person Clause / No Subcontracting**

- The services must be performed personally by the signed Teacher. They cannot outsource, delegate, or subcontract the work to another person without the Company's prior written consent. This ensures quality control.

**19. Trial Period / Probationary Clause**

- The initial (e.g., 60 or 90 days) of the agreement is a trial period. During this time, either party can terminate with minimal (e.g., 24-48 hours) notice. This allows for a low-risk assessment of performance.

**20. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Minimum Targets**

- The Teacher must maintain specific, measurable performance targets (e.g., a minimum conversion rate from trial to paid student, or a minimum number of deals closed per month). Failure to meet these KPIs consistently may be grounds for termination.

**21. Moral Turpitude / Reputational Protection**

- The Company has the right to immediately terminate the agreement if the Teacher is involved in any act of moral turpitude (e.g., fraud, dishonesty) or any activity that brings the Company's brand into public disrepute or scandal.

**22. Force Majeure**

- Neither party is liable for failure to perform its obligations due to events beyond its reasonable control, such as natural disasters, war, pandemics, or major internet outages.

**23. Entire Agreement Clause**

- This document represents the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, emails, or oral agreements. Any changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.

**24. Severability**

- If a court finds any single clause of this agreement to be unenforceable or invalid, the rest of the agreement will remain in full force and effect.

### **Final Additional Clauses to Close All Gaps:**

**25. "No Guarantee" Clause**

- The Company makes no guarantees or warranties regarding the income potential, number of leads, or conversion rates for the Teacher. Earnings are based solely on performance.

**26. "Cure Period" (Right to Fix Breaches)**

- If the Teacher breaches the agreement, they will have a specified period (e.g., 10 business days) to "cure" or fix the breach after receiving written notice from the Company, before termination is enacted.

**27. "No Partnership" Clause**

- Explicitly states that this agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture, or franchise. The Teacher has no authority to bind the Company to any contracts or debts.

**28. "Assignment" Clause**

- The Teacher cannot assign or transfer this agreement to another person without the Company's written consent. The Company, however, may assign the agreement to a successor entity (e.g., if the company is sold).

**29. "Publicity" Clause**

- The Teacher grants the Company the right to use their name, likeness, voice, and professional bio in connection with the services and for promotional purposes (e.g., on the company website as a "Trainer").

**30. "Notices" Clause**

- Specifies the official method for all communications (e.g., email to a specific address) and when a notice is considered delivered. This prevents "I didn't get the email" disputes.

**31. "Waiver" Clause**

- States that if either party fails to enforce a right under the agreement, it does not mean they waive the right to enforce it in the future.

**32. "Background Check" Right**

- Reserves the Company's right to conduct a background check (professional and/or criminal) as a condition of engagement.

**33. "Costs and Attorneys' Fees"**

- In the event of a legal dispute, the prevailing party is entitled to recover its reasonable legal fees and costs from the other party. This discourages frivolous lawsuits.

**34. "Survival" Clause**

- Clearly lists which clauses remain in effect even *after* the agreement terminates. This typically includes: **Confidentiality, Intellectual Property, Non-Solicitation, Non-Circumvention, and Limitation of Liability.**
