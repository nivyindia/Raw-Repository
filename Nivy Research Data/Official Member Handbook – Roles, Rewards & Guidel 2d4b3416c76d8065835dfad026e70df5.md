# NIVY JOBS & LEARNING COMMUNITY

### Official Member Handbook (Public)

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION

**Nivy Jobs & Learning Community** is a global, contribution-based community created to help people access:

- Verified job opportunities
- Genuine free courses and tools
- Learning and career growth resources

This community works on a **give-first model**:

👉 Those who contribute gain visibility, trust, and opportunities.

This is **not a discussion or chatting group**.

It is a **value-driven opportunity-sharing platform**.

---

## 2. PURPOSE OF THE COMMUNITY

The community exists to:

1. Share verified jobs from trusted sources
2. Share genuine free or audit-mode courses
3. Help students, freshers, and freelancers grow skills
4. Encourage contribution, responsibility, and learning
5. Recognize contributors with certificates and roles

Only **active and genuine contributors** benefit fully.

---

## 3. COMMUNITY STRUCTURE

The community contains multiple focused groups.

Each group has a **specific purpose**.

### Groups Inside the Community

1. **Jobs Board**
    
    – Only job postings
    
    – No discussion
    
2. **Free Courses & Tools**
    
    – Only free courses, tools, and learning resources
    
    – No discussion
    
3. **Tasks • Points • Certificates**
    
    – Information about tasks, contribution, and recognition
    
4. **Announcements**
    
    – Important updates
    
    – Admin-only posting
    

❌ There are **no general discussion groups**

❌ Casual chatting is not allowed

---

## 4. GENERAL RULES (FOR ALL MEMBERS)

All members must follow these rules:

- Use the **given templates only**
- Post **verified and genuine information**
- No fake jobs
- No paid or pirated courses
- No self-promotion without approval
- No spam, links without context, or misleading posts

Violations may result in:

- Post deletion
- Temporary mute
- Permanent removal from the community

Quality is more important than quantity.

---

## 5. JOB POSTING GUIDELINES

### What Can Be Posted

- Full-time jobs
- Internships
- Freelance or contract roles
- Remote or onsite opportunities

Jobs must come from **reliable sources**.

---

### Mandatory Job Posting Template

Every job post must follow this format:

```
Job Type: (Full-time / Internship / Freelance)
Company / Platform:
Role:
Location: (Remote / Onsite)
Experience Level:
Salary / Stipend: (If available)
Source Link:
Who should apply:

```

❌ Posts without this template will be deleted

❌ Fake or misleading jobs will lead to removal

---

## 6. COURSE & TOOL POSTING GUIDELINES

### What Can Be Posted

- Free courses
- Audit/free tracks from platforms
- Official tools and learning resources
- Government or company-backed learning programs

Only **legal and genuine** resources are allowed.

---

### Mandatory Course Posting Template

```
Course Provider:
Course Name:
Skill Category:
Level: (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced)
Duration:
Free or Audit:
Certificate: (Yes / No)
Why useful (1 line):
Official Link:

```

❌ Paid, pirated, or downloaded courses are strictly prohibited

❌ No template = post deleted

---

## 7. CONTRIBUTION & POINT SYSTEM (OVERVIEW)

This community runs on **contribution**.

Members can earn points by:

- Posting valid jobs
- Posting valid free courses or tools
- Completing community tasks
- Inviting active contributors

Points help in:

- Recognition
- Certificates
- Priority access to opportunities

Exact point values and tasks are shared in the **Tasks group**.

---

## 8. CERTIFICATES & RECOGNITION

Active contributors may receive certificates such as:

- Community Contributor
- Job Curator
- Learning Curator
- Community Promoter

Certificates are:

- Issued periodically
- Based on quality contribution
- Verified by Nivy Alliance

Certificates help members showcase **community contribution and experience**.

---

## 9. REFERRALS & COMMUNITY GROWTH

Members are encouraged to invite others who can **contribute meaningfully**.

Referral benefits are given only when:

- The invited member joins
- The invited member actively contributes

Inviting inactive or spam members gives **no benefit**.

---

## 10. CAMPUS & STUDENT PARTICIPATION

Students and college members are welcome.

Active students may get opportunities such as:

- Campus contributor recognition
- Ambassador certificates
- Learning and internship priority

Participation must remain **genuine and rule-compliant**.

---

## 11. IMPORTANT COMMUNITY PRINCIPLES

- Contribution comes before rewards
- Quality is more important than volume
- Opportunities are earned, not demanded
- Rules exist to protect everyone’s value

This community rewards **effort, honesty, and consistency**.

---

## 12. CONNECTION WITH NIVY ALLIANCE

This community acts as an **entry platform**.

Highly active and serious contributors may later be:

- Recognized for higher opportunities
- Invited to advanced platforms
- Considered for collaboration

Such invitations are **selective and invite-only**.

---

## 13. FINAL NOTE

If you are here only to consume, you may stay limited.

If you contribute, you grow.

> Learn • Share • Contribute • Grow
> 

— **Team Nivy Alliance**
