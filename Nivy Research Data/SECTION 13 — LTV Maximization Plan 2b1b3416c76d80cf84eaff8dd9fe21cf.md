# **1. Value Ladder for Services**

A value ladder is a structured list of offers that increase in value, price, and complexity.

### **Stage 1 — Entry Offers ($99–$499)**

- Audit (SEO, Ads, Social)
- Landing page optimization
- Competitor analysis
- Funnel rebuild blueprint
- Email copywriting bundle

Purpose:

**Convert cold leads into paying clients fast.**

---

### **Stage 2 — Core Offers ($500–$2,000/month)**

- Social media management
- SEO monthly package
- Google Ads management
- Meta ads management
- Content + creative bundle
- CRM + automation setup

Purpose:

**Stable monthly retainer foundation.**

---

### **Stage 3 — High-Value Offers ($2,000–$7,500/month)**

- Full funnel build + management
- Paid ads + CRO + analytics bundle
- Complete marketing department outsourcing
- Advanced automation + email marketing

Purpose:

**Increase client dependency & deepen integration.**

---

### **Stage 4 — Enterprise Offers ($10,000–$50,000/month)**

- Dedicated team (PM + designers + media buyers + content writers)
- Multi-channel paid ads (Google + Meta + TikTok + YouTube)
- Quarterly strategy + consulting
- Full funnel + CRM + lifetime analytics

Purpose:

**Long-term, high-margin relationships.**

---

# **2. Upsell and Cross-Sell Map**

### **Upsells (Increase service quality):**

- Basic Ads → Ads + CRO
- SEO → SEO + high-authority PR
- SMM → SMM + content studio
- Content package → Full brand package

### **Cross-sells (Add complementary services):**

- Website build → SEO + blog content
- Google ads → Landing page redesign
- Social media → Influencer marketing
- Email marketing → Automation workflows

### **Strategic Upsell Timing**

- Week 4 → Introduce small upsell
- Month 2 → Add cross-sell
- Month 3 → Introduce bigger bundle
- Month 6+ → High-ticket pitch

---

# **3. Retention Strategies**

### **Service-based retention**

- Weekly performance reports
- Monthly review calls
- Quarterly business growth reviews
- Dedicated customer success manager

### **Emotional retention**

- Overcommunication
- Transparent reporting
- Setting expectations clearly
- Celebrating wins

### **Technical retention**

- Automation setup
- CRM ownership
- Technical dependencies (funnels, dashboards)
- Long-term data management

**Goal:**

Client must feel:

→ “If I leave this agency, I lose momentum.”
