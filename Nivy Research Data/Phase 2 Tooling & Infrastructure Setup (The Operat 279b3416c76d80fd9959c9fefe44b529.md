# Phase 2: Tooling & Infrastructure Setup (The "Operational Engine")

---

### **1. The Core Odoo Setup: Your Agency's Central Nervous System**

Instead of using disparate tools, you'll leverage Odoo's integrated applications. Start with the core modules that are most critical for a service-based business.

- **CRM (Customer Relationship Management):**
    - **Purpose:** This is your sales and lead management heart. It tracks every interaction from a first-time inquiry to a closed deal.
    - **Odoo Setup:**
        - **Pipeline Stages:** Create a visual sales pipeline (e.g., New Lead > Contacted > Proposal Sent > Negotiation > Won/Lost).
        - **Lead Scoring:** Qualify leads automatically based on criteria (e.g., budget, niche fit).
        - **Automate Email Follow-ups:** Set up automated emails to nurture leads after they download a guide or request a quote.
    - **Benefit:** All client communication history is in one place, making your sales process transparent and efficient.
- **Project Management:**
    - **Purpose:** This is where you will deliver your services. Each client is a project.
    - **Odoo Setup:**
        - **Project Templates:** Create templates for your core services (e.g., "SEO Starter Package," "Social Media Retainer"). Each template has pre-defined tasks.
        - **Tasks & Subtasks:** Break down work into actionable tasks. Assign them to team members, set deadlines, and track time.
        - **Kanban Boards:** Visualize your workload. Move tasks from "To Do" to "In Progress" to "Done."
        - **Timesheets:** Integrated time tracking on tasks is crucial for measuring profitability.
    - **Benefit:** Standardizes service delivery, ensures nothing falls through the cracks, and provides clarity to your team and clients.
- **Invoicing & Accounting:**
    - **Purpose:** Manage your finances, send professional invoices, and track payments.
    - **Odoo Setup:**
        - **Products & Services:** Create "products" in Odoo for each of your services (e.g., "Monthly SEO Audit," "Facebook Ad Management").
        - **Recurring Invoices:** Automate monthly retainer invoices for your clients.
        - **Online Payments:** Connect a payment acquirer (like Stripe or PayPal) so clients can pay invoices online with a click.
        - **Expense Tracking:** Have team members log expenses (e.g., ad spend, software tools) directly against a client project.
    - **Benefit:** Tight integration between projects and accounting means you can easily see if a client is profitable.
- **Website Builder:**
    - **Purpose:** To create and manage your agency's professional website.
    - **Odoo Setup:** Use the drag-and-drop editor to build your site. Key pages: Home, Services, Case Studies, About Us, Contact.
    - **Pro Tip:** Integrate the **CRM** with your website forms. When a lead submits a "Contact Us" form, it automatically creates a new lead in your Odoo CRM pipeline.

### **2. Essential Third-Party Tools that Integrate with Odoo**

While Odoo is powerful, you'll still need best-in-class marketing and analytics tools. The goal is to connect them to Odoo where possible.

- **Marketing & Analytics Stack:**
    - **Google Analytics 4 (GA4) & Google Tag Manager:** Non-negotiable for tracking website traffic and client campaign performance. This data is for reporting and strategy.
    - **SEO Tools:** **Ahrefs, SEMrush, or Moz.** Use these for keyword research, competitor analysis, and tracking rankings. This data informs the tasks you create in Odoo Projects.
    - **Social Media Management:** **Buffer, Hootsuite, or Later.** While Odoo has basic social media features, these tools are more robust for scheduling, publishing, and analytics across multiple client accounts.
- **Communication & Collaboration:**
    - **Slack or Microsoft Teams:** For instant, internal team communication. It's faster and more fluid than Odoo's built-in chat for day-to-day conversations.
    - **Google Workspace or Microsoft 365:** For professional email (`yourname@youragency.com`), document creation (Docs/Word, Sheets/Excel), and calendar management. You can often sync calendars with Odoo.

### **3. Professional Online Presence Setup**

- **Your Agency Website (Built on Odoo):**
    - **Must-Have Pages:**
        - **Homepage:** Clear UVP, core services, and strong call-to-action (CTA).
        - **Services:** Detailed pages for each of your core service packages.
        - **Case Studies/Portfolio:** The most important page. Show results with data (e.g., "Increased organic traffic by 150% in 6 months").
        - **About Us:** Build trust by introducing your team and your story.
        - **Blog/Resources:** To demonstrate expertise and attract organic traffic.
    - **Contact Form:** This should feed directly into your Odoo CRM as a new lead.
- **Social Media Profiles:**
    - **LinkedIn:** Your most important platform for B2B networking and credibility. Keep it updated.
    - **Other Platforms (Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter):** Choose based on your niche. Consistency is key.

### **Implementation Plan for Odoo**

1. **Sign Up:** Start with Odoo Online for ease of use. You can begin with a free trial.
2. **Priority Modules:** Activate and configure these modules first: **CRM, Project, Invoicing, and Website.**
3. **Data Migration:** Input your initial data: Create your "Service" products, set up your sales pipeline stages, and build your project templates.
4. **Team Training:** Get your team (even if it's just you) comfortable using the system. Odoo has good documentation and tutorials.
5. **Integration:** Connect your third-party tools. Use Odoo's app store to find connectors for tools like Slack, Google Calendar, and PayPal.

By using Odoo as your core, you build a unified system where your CRM, projects, and finances talk to each other, eliminating data silos and saving you countless hours of administrative work.
