**What is Nivy Alliance?**

Nivy Alliance is Nivy's strategic partnership and B2B alliance division. It manages structured relationships with external partners, collaborators, institutional networks, and corporate alliances that amplify Nivy's reach and service delivery across all markets.

---

## 🎯 Vision & Mission

**Vision:** Build a global network of aligned partners who extend Nivy's capabilities and market reach without requiring Nivy to scale headcount.

**Mission:** Identify, onboard, and manage strategic alliances — referral partners, white-label partners, resellers, institutional collaborators, and co-service providers — through a structured, transparent, and mutually beneficial framework.

---

## 🤝 Alliance Types Nivy Will Manage

| Alliance Type | Description | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔁 Referral Partners | Companies or individuals who refer clients to Nivy in exchange for commissions | 🔴 High |
| 🏷️ White-Label Partners | Agencies that resell Nivy services under their own brand | 🔴 High |
| 🏢 Institutional Partners | Universities, incubators, accelerators hosting Nivy programs | 🟡 Medium |
| 🌐 Cross-Market Partners | Local firms in UAE, UK, AU who co-deliver with Nivy | 🟡 Medium |
| 🎓 Education Partners | Training orgs that distribute Nivy Academy content | 🟢 Future |
