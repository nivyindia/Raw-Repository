## 📝 Hiring Process — Stage by Stage

**Stage 1 — Role Definition**

- Write the Job Description using the JD template
- Get sign-off from the division lead
- Set salary band + employment type

**Stage 2 — Posting**

- Post to: LinkedIn, Indeed, Internshala (IN), Seek (AU), Reed (UK)
- Use the [Hiring Post Templates](https://www.notion.so/2c9b3416c76d80d69380cf42f8b8ff50) for each platform
- Add to the Internal Job Listings page

**Stage 3 — Screening**

- Review applications within 3 business days
- Use the [Recruitment & Filtering Process](https://www.notion.so/2c9b3416c76d80569438c3488caeec33)
- Shortlist max 5 candidates per role

**Stage 4 — Interview**

- 2-stage process: Discovery Call (30 min) → Task/Panel Interview
- Use the [Freelancer Screening Questions](https://www.notion.so/2bcb3416c76d80ae972fdba9f197e0bf) for contract roles

**Stage 5 — Offer & Onboarding**

- Send offer letter within 48 hours of decision
- Trigger [Employee Onboarding Checklist](https://www.notion.so/354b3416c76d816c83d0fce00a5e6ef4) on Day 1
