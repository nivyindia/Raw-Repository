# Partnership Payment Structure

# **Scalable Outsourcing Partnership Payment Structure**

## **Core Philosophy**

- **Performance-Based Graduation**: Partners start small and earn better terms through consistent performance
- **Territory-Based Commission Tiers**: Better markets unlock with proven results
- **Team Growth Support**: Payment structure supports partner's employee costs
- **Risk-Reward Balance**: Low initial risk for both parties with high upside potential

---

## **Phase 1: Foundation Period (Months 1-3) - "Proving Ground"**

### **Target Territories**: India, Southeast Asia, Middle East

### **Team Structure**: 1-2 People (Partner + 1 Junior)

| Component | Structure | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Base Stipend** | **₹15,000/employee/month** | Covers basic operational costs |
| **Performance Commission** | **8%** of closed deal value | Base incentive for results |
| **Employee Cost Coverage** | Additional ₹5,000 per verified hired employee | Supports team building |
| **Monthly Target** | ₹2,00,000 in closed revenue | Realistic starting point |
| **Safety Net** | Minimum ₹10,000 if <65% target achieved | Ensures basic sustainability |

**Example Monthly Calculation (2 people team):**

- Base Stipend: ₹15,000 × 2 = ₹30,000
- Employee Support: ₹5,000 × 2 = ₹10,000
- Commission (if hit ₹2L target): ₹2,00,000 × 8% = ₹16,000
- **Total: ₹56,000**

---

## **Phase 2: Growth Period (Months 4-6) - "Established Partner"**

### **Unlock Conditions**: Achieve 80%+ of targets for 2 consecutive months

### **New Territories**: UK, Australia, Western Europe

### **Team Structure**: 3-5 People

| Component | Structure | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Base Stipend** | **₹20,000/employee/month** | Better cost coverage |
| **Performance Commission** | **12%** of closed deal value | Increased motivation |
| **Employee Cost Coverage** | Additional ₹7,500 per verified employee | Better team support |
| **Monthly Target** | ₹5,00,000 in closed revenue | Scaled expectations |
| **Team Bonus** | ₹25,000 for hitting team target | Team performance reward |

**Example Monthly Calculation (4 people team):**

- Base Stipend: ₹20,000 × 4 = ₹80,000
- Employee Support: ₹7,500 × 4 = ₹30,000
- Commission (if hit ₹5L target): ₹5,00,000 × 12% = ₹60,000
- Team Bonus: ₹25,000
- **Total: ₹1,95,000**

---

## **Phase 3: Scale Period (Month 7+) - "Premium Partner"**

### **Unlock Conditions**: Consistently exceed targets by 25%+ for 3 months

### **Premium Territories**: USA, Canada, Enterprise Clients

### **Team Structure**: 6-10 People (Full Pod)

| Component | Structure | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Base Stipend** | **₹25,000/employee/month** | Professional team support |
| **Performance Commission** | **15%** of closed deal value | Premium market rates |
| **Employee Cost Coverage** | Additional ₹10,000 per verified employee | Quality talent support |
| **Monthly Target** | ₹12,00,000 in closed revenue | Enterprise-level expectations |
| **Accelerator Bonus** | **18%** on revenue above target | Heavy over-performance incentive |
| **Leadership Bonus** | ₹50,000 quarterly for team management | Management compensation |

**Example Monthly Calculation (8 people team hitting ₹15L):**

- Base Stipend: ₹25,000 × 8 = ₹2,00,000
- Employee Support: ₹10,000 × 8 = ₹80,000
- Commission (on target ₹12L): ₹12,00,000 × 15% = ₹1,80,000
- Accelerator (on excess ₹3L): ₹3,00,000 × 18% = ₹54,000
- **Total: ₹5,14,000** (+ quarterly leadership bonus)

## **Performance Thresholds & Safety Nets**

### **Target Achievement Payout Matrix**

| Achievement Level | Base Stipend | Commission | Territory Access |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **< 50% Target** | 50% of base | 5% commission only | Current tier only |
| **50-65% Target** | 75% of base | 8% commission | Current tier only |
| **65-80% Target** | 100% of base | Full commission | Current tier + testing next |
| **80-100% Target** | 100% of base + bonus | Full commission + bonus | Automatic tier upgrade |
| **>100% Target** | 100% + bonus + accelerator | Accelerated rates | Premium tier access |

## **Employee Verification & Cost Support**

### **To Qualify for Employee Cost Support:**

1. Employee must be dedicated to your account
2. Minimum 6-month employment contract
3. Regular performance reporting required
4. Monthly verification calls with team members

### **Supported Employee Roles:**

- Sales Development Representatives (SDRs)
- Account Executives (AEs)
- Digital Marketing Specialists
- Team Leads/Managers

---

## **Implementation Framework**

### **Monthly Reporting Requirements:**

- Employee time allocation
- Activity metrics (calls, emails, meetings)
- Pipeline value created
- Closed deals with territory breakdown
- Client satisfaction scores

### **Quarterly Business Reviews:**

- Performance against targets
- Territory performance analysis
- Team growth planning
- Commission structure review
- Strategy alignment

This structure ensures that as your outsourcing partner grows and succeeds, their compensation grows proportionally while covering their increasing employee costs. The territory-based progression motivates them to perform better to access more lucrative markets.
