# **Done - STEP 1: Decide Your Business Idea & Model**

Before you do anything, be clear on what your business is.

**For your case:**

- **Business Type:** International accounting & tax advisory + software/SaaS solutions.
- **Services:** Tax consulting, accounting services, SaaS tools for finance management.
- **Target Audience:** SMEs, startups, international businesses, freelancers.
- **Revenue Model:**
    - Consulting fees (hourly/project)
    - SaaS subscription (monthly/annual)
    - Value-added services (audits, automation, reporting)

**Action:** Write a simple **business model canvas**:

- What you sell
- Who you sell to
- How you make money
- How you deliver your services

---

# **Done - STEP 2: Register Your Business Legally**

1. Choose a **business structure**:
    - Private Limited Company (best for international credibility)
    - LLP (if lower compliance)
    - Sole Proprietor (simplest, but less credibility)
2. **Register the company** in your country (India: MCA portal for Private Limited)
3. Apply for **tax registrations**: PAN, GST, etc.
4. Open a **business bank account**
5. Consider **trademarks or IP protection** for your SaaS/software

**Tip:** Legal compliance upfront prevents huge problems later.

---

# **Done - STEP 3: Plan Your Finances**

- **Startup Budget:** Office (even home-based), software tools, website, marketing, team
- **Cash Flow Planning:** How much money do you need each month to survive?
- **Pricing:** Decide fees for consulting and SaaS subscriptions
- **Funding:** Self-funded, bank loan, or investor-backed

**Tip:** Start lean — focus on minimum costs until you have clients paying.

---

# **Done - STEP 4: Build Your Service/Product**

- **For Consulting:**
    - Define services clearly: International tax, accounting, payroll, audits
    - Prepare templates, calculators, and SOPs for delivery
- **For SaaS/Software:**
    - Decide core features (invoice automation, tax compliance tools, reports)
    - Develop MVP (Minimum Viable Product) first
    - Test with a small group of clients before full launch

---

# **STEP 6: Acquire Customers (Marketing & Sales)**

**Start small, focus on quality leads:**

1. **Network & Referrals:** Start with people you know
2. **Content Marketing:** Blog posts, guides, webinars, newsletters
3. **Email Marketing:** (we already created sequences)
4. **LinkedIn Outreach:** Target CFOs, finance heads, business owners
5. **Paid Ads:** Optional later for international reach (Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads)

**Tip:** Focus on **niche audience first**, then scale globally.

---

# **STEP 7: Deliver & Retain Customers**

- Provide **excellent service consistently**
- Collect **feedback & testimonials**
- Upsell SaaS tools or additional services
- Keep communication regular via **emails, reports, or calls**

**Tip:** Happy clients are your best marketing tool.

---

# **STEP 8: Build Systems & Team**

Once you have clients coming in:

- **Document processes:** Onboarding, service delivery, invoicing
- **Hire smart:** Start with 1–2 people (finance/accounting + marketing)
- **Automate where possible:** Accounting software, CRM, email sequences
- **Track KPIs:** Revenue, clients, retention, cash flow

**Tip:** Your business should **run without you doing everything manually**.

---

# **STEP 9: Monitor & Improve**

- Regularly check:
    - Profit & loss
    - Client satisfaction
    - Employee performance
    - Market trends
- Adapt quickly — if something isn’t working, fix it.

---

# **STEP 10: Scale Internationally**

Once your systems are solid and revenue steady:

- Expand to new countries (local tax knowledge required)
- Partner with local accountants/consultants
- Promote SaaS subscriptions globally
- Automate marketing, customer support, and onboarding

---

### **Summary: Your First 90 Days Checklist**

1. Decide service & business model
2. Register legally + bank account
3. Budget & pricing
4. Build service templates & SaaS MVP
5. Create website & brand
6. Start lead generation (network, email, LinkedIn)
7. Deliver service & collect testimonials
8. Automate & document processes
9. Hire first team members
10. Track KPIs & optimize

---

💡 **Key Mindset:** Running a business is **gradual**, and perfection comes later. Focus on **clients, cash flow, and repeatable systems** first, then scale.
