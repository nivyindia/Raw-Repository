## **1. Document Overview & Governance**

### **1.1. Preamble & Purpose**

This Annexure A ("the Schedule") is a formal and integral component of the Sales Compensation & Incentive Policy of Billion Dreams United (Nivy) ("the Company"). Its primary purpose is to establish a transparent, measurable, and equitable framework for setting performance expectations and calculating corresponding monetary incentives for the designated sales role. This document aims to align individual objectives with the company's strategic goals for the specified period.

### **1.2. Binding Nature & Scope**

This Schedule is a binding document upon signature by all parties. It supersedes any prior verbal or written agreements regarding sales targets and incentives for the Effective Period. Any modifications must be made in writing and follow the amendment process outlined in Section 10.

### **1.3. Effective Period**

The terms and conditions outlined in this Schedule are exclusively applicable for the performance cycle commencing on **1st October 2025** and concluding on **31st December 2025**. Incentives will be calculated and paid for achievements within this window, based on the revenue recognition rules defined in Section 4.

### **1.4. Applicable Roles**

This annexure is specifically tailored for the position of **Business Development Executive (BDE)**. For the avoidance of doubt, this includes all employees formally designated as BDEs within the Company's sales hierarchy during the Effective Period.

### **1.5. Key Definitions**

For the purpose of this Schedule, the following terms are defined as:

- **"Target"** refers to the monthly and quarterly revenue goals specified in Section 2.
- **"Incentive"** refers to the total variable compensation earned, comprising the Monthly Performance Incentive and the Quarterly Overachievement Bonus.
- **"Revenue"** or **"Achieved Revenue"** refers to the portion of client payment that has been successfully received by the Company and recognized for incentive calculation.
- **"PIP"** refers to Performance Improvement Program, a structured intervention for employees not meeting minimum performance standards.

## **2. Sales Target Framework & Performance Standards**

### **2.1. Target Allocation Basis & Philosophy**

The Company operates on a revenue-driven sales model. Therefore, the primary Key Performance Indicator (KPI) for the BDE role is the **Monthly Revenue Target**. This metric is chosen to directly correlate effort with business growth and financial contribution.

### **2.2. Target Periodicity Post Probation Period: Monthly & Quarterly Goals**

Targets are structured to encourage both consistent monthly performance and sustained quarterly excellence.

- **Monthly Target:** The primary operational goal. Each BDE is required to achieve a recurring monthly revenue target of **$9,000 (USD)**.
- **Quarterly Target:** The cumulative goal for the three-month period. The quarterly target is derived as **$27,000 (USD)** ($9,000 x 3 months).

### **2.3. Target Periodicity during Probation Period: Monthly & Quarterly Goals**

Targets are structured to encourage both consistent monthly performance and sustained quarterly excellence.

- **Monthly Target:** The primary operational goal. Each BDE is required to achieve a recurring monthly revenue target of **$1,000 (USD)**.
- **Quarterly Target:** The cumulative goal for the three-month period. The quarterly target is derived as **$,000 (USD)** ($9,000 x 3 months).

### **2.3. Minimum Performance Threshold & Consequences**

To maintain employment standards and ensure business continuity, all BDEs must meet minimum performance thresholds:

### **2.3.1. Monthly Minimum Performance Requirement**

- Employees must achieve **at least 65% of their monthly revenue target ($5,850 USD)** in each calendar month.
- Performance will be reviewed in **two monthly checkpoints**: Mid-month (15th) and Month-end.

### **2.3.2. Performance Improvement Program (PIP) Triggers**

A BDE will be automatically placed on a Performance Improvement Program (PIP) under the following conditions:

- **First Instance:** Failure to achieve 65% of monthly target in any single month.
- **Second Instance:** Failure to achieve 65% of monthly target for two consecutive months.

### **2.3.3. PIP Structure & Duration**

- **Duration:** 30 days from formal notification
- **PIP Targets:** Specific, measurable goals set at 55% of normal monthly targets
- **Support Provided:** Enhanced coaching, additional training resources, and daily performance tracking
- **Review Frequency:** Weekly performance reviews with the Sales Manager

### **2.3.4. Consequences of PIP Failure**

If an employee fails to meet PIP targets:

- **First PIP Failure:** Formal written warning and extension of probation period (if applicable)
- **Second PIP Failure:** Initiation of termination proceedings
- **Immediate Termination:** Consistent performance below 50% for three consecutive months

### **2.4. Target Distribution & Strategic Focus**

To ensure a balanced portfolio and strategic focus across the Company's service offerings, the total monthly revenue target is distributed across core service lines with the following weightage:

| Service Line | Weightage | Monthly Target Value (USD) | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Digital Marketing Services** | 50% | $4,500 | High-growth area, driving recurring revenue |
| **Tax & Compliance Services** | 30% | $2,700 | Core service with high client retention |
| **Accounting & Bookkeeping** | 20% | $1,800 | Foundational service, client entry point |

### **2.5. Target Adjustment & Force Majeure Clause**

The Company recognizes that market conditions are dynamic. Therefore, the targets may be subject to revision under exceptional circumstances.

- **Revision Triggers:** Fundamental market shifts, service line launches/discontinuations, territory reassignments, or force majeure events.
- **Authority to Revise:** Requires joint approval of **Head of Sales** and **CMO/CEO/Director**.
- **Process & Communication:** Minimum 15 days' written notice via official annexure amendment.

---

### **Performance Management Philosophy**

The Company believes in transparent performance management with clear consequences. The PIP process is designed as a supportive mechanism to help struggling employees succeed. Regular feedback, training, and resources will be provided throughout the performance management cycle to ensure every employee has the opportunity to meet expectations.

# **3. Comprehensive Incentive Structure**

## **3.1. Incentive Philosophy & Design Principles**

### **3.1.1. Compensation Philosophy**

Billion Dreams United (Nivy) believes in rewarding performance excellence through a transparent, equitable, and motivating incentive structure. Our compensation model is designed to:

- Attract and retain top sales talent in the competitive digital marketing and financial services industry
- Drive business growth while maintaining service quality and client satisfaction
- Create clear career progression paths tied to measurable performance outcomes
- Foster a culture of high performance with appropriate risk-reward balance

### **3.1.2. Core Design Principles**

- **Transparency:** Every component of the incentive structure is clearly defined, calculable, and communicated
- **Acceleration:** Higher performance yields disproportionately higher rewards through tiered accelerators
- **Fairness:** Equal opportunity for all BDEs with clear, objective measurement criteria
- **Sustainability:** Structure ensures business profitability while rewarding individual contributions
- **Compliance:** All incentives adhere to statutory requirements and internal governance standards

## **3.2. Monthly Performance Incentive Structure**

### **3.2.1. Tiered Commission Model Architecture**

### **Performance Band Definitions**

The monthly incentive structure comprises seven distinct performance bands, each with specific behavioral and outcome expectations:

| Achievement Band | Performance Level | Behavioral Indicators | Business Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **0% - 64%** | Below Minimum | Inconsistent activity, poor pipeline management | Negative ROI |
| **65% - 69%** | Minimum Threshold | Basic activity compliance, limited pipeline | Break-even |
| **70% - 89%** | Developing | Consistent effort, improving skills | Positive contribution |
| **90% - 99%** | Strong | Reliable performer, good client relationships | Strong ROI |
| **100% - 119%** | Excellent | Strategic selling, value-based conversations | High ROI |
| **120% - 149%** | Outstanding | Exceptional skills, mentor to peers | Exceptional ROI |
| **150%+** | Exceptional | Industry expert, innovative approaches | Maximum ROI |

### **3.2. Monthly Performance Incentive Structure**

### **Commission Slabs & Payout Calculation**

| Performance Band | Achievement Range | Commission Rate | Behavioral Expectations | Example: $9,000 Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Below Minimum** | 0% - 64% | 0% | Inconsistent performance, needs improvement | $5,000 revenue = $0 |
| **Base Protection** | 65% - 69% | 3% of total revenue | Meets minimum standards, basic activity | $6,200 revenue = $186 |
| **Growth Layer** | 70% - 89% | 5% of total revenue | Consistent effort, improving skills | $8,000 revenue = $400 |
| **Strong Performer** | 90% - 99% | 7% of total revenue | Reliable, good pipeline management | $9,500 revenue = $665 |

**Key Calculation Rules:**

- Incentive calculated on **entire monthly revenue**, not incremental
- Same rates apply for all service combinations
- Only **received client payments** qualify for incentive calculation
- For partial months: Daily target = Monthly target ÷ working days

# **4. Payout Schedule & Administrative Procedures**

## **4.1. Payout Timeline & Frequency**

### **Monthly Incentive Payout Cycle**

- **Calculation Period:** 1st to last day of each calendar month
- **Verification Window:** 1st to 7th of following month
- **Approval Deadline:** 10th of following month
- **Payout Date:** 15th of following month (or next working day)
- **Payment Method:** Direct bank transfer with detailed payslip

### **Quarterly Bonus Payout Cycle**

- **Calculation Period:** Full quarter (3 months)
- **Verification Window:** 1st to 15th of following month
- **Approval Deadline:** 20th of following month
- **Payout Date:** Last working day of the month following quarter-end

## **4.2. Verification & Approval Workflow**

### **Three-Stage Verification Process**

1. **Sales Manager Review**
    - Validates deal authenticity and CRM documentation
    - Confirms revenue attribution accuracy
    - Approves performance metrics and achievement percentages
2. **Finance Department Verification**
    - Confirms client payment receipt and revenue recognition
    - Validates calculation accuracy and compliance
    - Ensures proper documentation and audit trails
3. **HR Compensation Review**
    - Final approval of payout amounts
    - Compliance with policy guidelines
    - Maintenance of employee compensation records

### **Documentation Requirements**

- Complete CRM deal records with client signatures
- Payment receipts and accounting confirmations
- Monthly performance dashboards
- Manager approval forms

## **4.3. Clawback & Deferral Policies**

### **Clawback Conditions**

- Client refunds or cancellations within 90 days of service start
- Discovery of policy violations or misrepresentation
- Client payment bounce or dishonored cheques
- Proven data manipulation or ethical breaches

### **Deferral Circumstances**

- Incomplete client documentation
- Pending client payments
- Employee on extended leave during payout period
- Ongoing investigation or dispute resolution

### **Clawback Implementation**

- 15-day written notice to employee
- Deduction from future incentive payouts
- Maximum 3 monthly installments for recovery
- Right to appeal through formal grievance process

## **4.4. Tax & Compliance Treatment**

- All incentives subject to applicable TDS/income tax
- Separate line item on payslip for transparency
- Annual tax statement provided by January 31st
- Compliance with local labor laws and regulations

## **6. Revenue Recognition & Deal Management Framework**

### **6.1. Comprehensive Revenue Recognition Policy**

### **6.1.1. Revenue Recognition Point**

A sale is considered complete and revenue is recognized for incentive purposes **only upon receipt of the complete payment from the client** in the Company's designated bank account. Partial payments will be recognized proportionally for incentive calculation.

### **6.1.2. Payment Verification Process**

- **Daily Reconciliation:** Finance department verifies all client payments against invoices
- **CRM Integration:** Automated payment confirmation updates in CRM system
- **48-Hour Processing:** Payments reflected in incentive calculations within 48 hours of receipt
- **Exception Handling:** Disputed payments require Sales Manager approval for incentive release

### **6.2. Deal Credit Assignment Rules**

### **6.2.1. Primary Credit Assignment**

Credit for a deal is assigned based on a **first-touch, last-touch** hybrid model:

- **Lead Registration:** BDE who first registers the lead in CRM with complete client details
- **Deal Ownership:** BDE who conducts the first meaningful client interaction
- **Closure Responsibility:** BDE who is the primary point of contact through contract signing

### **6.2.2. CRM Documentation Requirements**

- **Mandatory Fields:** Client contact details, lead source, initial conversation summary
- **Timeline Updates:** Regular activity logging (calls, emails, meetings)
- **Final Documentation:** Signed contract, payment terms, service specifications
- **48-Hour Rule:** All client interactions must be logged within 48 hours

### **6.3. Shared Deal & Team Selling Protocols**

### **6.3.1. Pre-Approved Split Deals**

When multiple BDEs legitimately contribute to closing a deal:

- **Pre-Approval Required:** Split must be approved by Sales Manager before deal closure
- **Written Agreement:** Both parties must sign split agreement form
- **Common Split Ratios:** 70/30, 60/40, or 50/50 based on contribution level
- **Minimum Contribution:** 20% contribution required for any split consideration

### **6.3.2. Territory & Account Handoffs**

- **Geographic Territories:** Clear geographic boundaries defined in separate document
- **Account-Based Territories:** Large accounts assigned specifically by Sales Manager
- **Handoff Protocol:** Formal handoff meeting required with documented notes
- **Referral Credit:** 10% referral bonus for internal lead referrals that convert

### **6.4. Deal Dispute Resolution Process**

### **6.4.1. Informal Resolution**

- **Step 1:** Direct discussion between involved BDEs (48-hour window)
- **Step 2:** Mediation by immediate Sales Manager (24-hour decision)

### **6.4.2. Formal Escalation**

- **Step 3:** Written submission to Head of Sales with all documentation
- **Step 4:** Review committee decision within 72 hours
- **Final Authority:** Head of Sales decision is binding and final

### **6.4.3. Documentation Requirements for Disputes**

- CRM activity logs and time stamps
- Email communication chains
- Meeting notes and call recordings (if available)
- Client confirmation (when appropriate)

## **8. Performance Compliance & Employment Status**

### **8.1. Performance Improvement Program (PIP) Details**

### **8.1.1. PIP Activation Triggers**

- **Monthly Performance:** Below 65% of target for one month
- **Consistent Underperformance:** Below 75% for two consecutive months
- **Quality Issues:** Multiple client complaints or policy violations
- **Behavioral Issues:** Unprofessional conduct affecting team performance

### **8.1.2. PIP Structure & Duration**

- **Standard PIP:** 30-day improvement period
- **Extended PIP:** 60-day period for complex performance issues
- **PIP Components:** Specific goals, daily tracking, weekly reviews, training plan
- **Success Metrics:** Clear, measurable targets for PIP completion

### **8.1.3. PIP Support Resources**

- **Dedicated Coach:** Assigned senior BDE or Sales Manager
- **Training Modules:** Customized skill development sessions
- **Reduced Targets:** PIP targets set at 75% of standard monthly goals
- **Enhanced Tools:** Additional marketing support and lead allocation

### **8.2. Employment Status Impact**

### **8.2.1. Probation Period Status**

- **Eligibility:** Employees on probation are eligible for full incentives
- **Accelerated Confirmation:** Exceptional performance can lead to early confirmation
- **Extended Probation:** PIP activation automatically extends probation period
- **Probation Completion:** Requires consistent achievement of minimum thresholds

### **8.2.2. Resignation & Termination Provisions**

**Voluntary Resignation:**

- **Notice Period:** Incentives earned until last working day are payable
- **Processing:** Final incentive payout with full and final settlement
- **Documentation:** Complete handover notes and CRM updates required

**Involuntary Termination:**

- **Performance-Based:** No incentives payable if terminated for performance issues
- **Policy Violation:** Immediate forfeiture of all unpaid incentives
- **Misconduct:** Fraud, data manipulation, or ethical violations void all incentives

**Termination for Cause:**

- Immediate termination for gross misconduct
- Complete forfeiture of all pending incentives
- Potential recovery of recent incentives if misconduct affected results

### **8.3. Misconduct & Policy Violations**

### **8.3.1. Zero Tolerance Violations**

- **Data Manipulation:** Falsifying CRM records or sales data
- **Client Poaching:** Stealing clients from other BDEs or territories
- **Ethical Breaches:** Misrepresentation to clients or company
- **Confidentiality Violation:** Sharing sensitive company or client information

### **8.3.2. Disciplinary Actions**

- **First Offense:** Written warning and incentive forfeiture for current period
- **Second Offense:** Final written warning and quarter incentive forfeiture
- **Third Offense:** Immediate termination with cause
