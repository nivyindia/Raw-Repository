# 🗓️ 30-Day Online Presence Plan — Nivy Advisory

Perfect! Here’s a **practical “First 30 Days Online Presence Plan” for Nivy Advisory** so you can go live quickly, look professional, and start attracting clients.

---

# 

### **Goal:** Launch a credible, lead-generating online presence quickly, without over-investing.

---

## **Week 1: Set the Foundation**

**1. Domain & Hosting**

- Buy a professional domain: `www.nivyadvisory.com`
- Choose reliable hosting (SiteGround, Bluehost, or WordPress hosting)

**2. Professional Email**

- Set up `info@nivyadvisory.com` and `sales@nivyadvisory.com`
- Use Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 for professionalism

**3. Branding**

- Logo (corporate, clean, trustworthy)
- Brand colors and fonts (2–3 colors max)
- Tagline: “International Tax & Accounting Experts”

**4. LinkedIn Setup**

- Create company page: logo, tagline, about us, services
- Update founder profile: professional photo, bio, expertise

---

## **Week 2: Website Launch (Minimum Viable Website)**

**Pages to Create:**

1. **Home Page** – Brief intro, value proposition, CTA (“Book Free Consultation”)
2. **Services Page** – Consulting + SaaS services listed clearly
3. **About Us** – Company story, team, credibility
4. **Contact Page** – Form, email, phone, optional map

**Extras (Optional but Recommended):**

- Lead magnet download (e.g., “Global Tax Compliance Checklist”)
- Newsletter sign-up form

**Tips:**

- Keep design clean and mobile-friendly
- Add testimonials or logos of early clients if possible

---

## **Week 3: Content & Authority**

**1. Lead Magnet**

- Prepare 1–2 downloadable guides:
    - “Top 10 International Tax Mistakes Companies Make”
    - “Checklist: Stay Compliant Globally”

**2. LinkedIn Content**

- Post 1–2 times this week:
    - Quick tip for businesses
    - Industry insight or news
- Connect with 50–100 target prospects (CFOs, finance managers, startups)

**3. Email Marketing Setup**

- Integrate newsletter form with website
- Start building email list from leads/contacts

---

## **Week 4: Go Live & Generate Leads**

**1. Launch Announcement**

- LinkedIn post: introduce Nivy Advisory, services, and lead magnet
- Optional: Email announcement to your contacts

**2. Lead Capture**

- Ensure newsletter/lead magnet is working correctly
- Automate welcome email (use our email sequences from earlier)

**3. Analyze & Optimize**

- Check website traffic (Google Analytics)
- Monitor LinkedIn engagement
- Track email sign-ups and leads

---

## **Ongoing After Day 30**

- Publish 1–2 blog posts or guides per month
- Continue LinkedIn posting 1–2 times per week
- Send monthly newsletter with tips, updates, or case studies
- Gradually expand to other social platforms if needed
- Update and refine website and lead magnet content

---

### ✅ **Key Takeaways**

- You **don’t need a massive online presence**—focus on credibility + lead capture
- Website + professional email + LinkedIn + lead magnet = enough to start
- You can **scale and polish over time** as you get clients and revenue
