# Linkedin Profile

### **What to Add to Your Featured Section:**

1. **Case Study PDFs or Blog Posts:** This is the #1 most effective item.
    - **What it is:** Create a 1-2 page PDF or a link to a blog post that details a successful project. Use the **Challenge -> Solution -> Results** framework.
    - **Example:** "How We Reduced CAC by 30% for a SaaS Client Using Targeted LinkedIn ABM" or "A Web Design Case Study: Boosting CRO by 45% with UX Optimization."
    - **Why it works:** It provides deep, measurable proof of your success and directly relates to the services you offer (PPC, ABM, Web Dev, Creative).
2. **Video Testimonials:**
    - **What it is:** A short (60-90 second) video of a happy client praising your work.
    - **Why it works:** Video is highly engaging and social proof from a third party is incredibly trustworthy. It puts a face and a story to your results.
3. **Portfolio Samples (Images/Video):**
    - **What it is:** Direct links or images of your best work.
    - **Examples:**
        - A screenshot of a high-performing ad creative you designed.
        - A short, impactful clip from an explainer video you produced.
        - A "before and after" screenshot of a landing page you optimized.
    - **Why it works:** It gives an instant, visual demonstration of your quality and style.
4. **Links to Lead Magnets or Free Tools:**
    - **What it is:** Feature a link to a valuable resource you've created, like an eBook ("The Ultimate Guide to B2B Paid Social"), a checklist ("Website CRO Audit Checklist"), or a free tool.
    - **Why it works:** This positions you as an expert and provides value upfront, generating leads directly from your profile.
5. **Presentation Slides (e.g., from Webinars):**
    - **What it is:** Upload a SlideShare presentation or a PDF deck from a talk you've given on a relevant topic (e.g., "Mastering LinkedIn Ads for B2B").
    - **Why it works:** It establishes your thought leadership and subject matter authority.

### **How to Structure Each Project for Maximum Impact:**

1. **Compelling Project Title:** Be specific and include the service and goal (e.g., "CRO for SaaS Landing Page" instead of just "Website Project").
2. **Concise Description (1-2 sentences):** What was the objective and your role?
3. **The Gold: Key Achievements / Results:** **Use bullet points and lead with metrics.** Quantify everything possible (use percentages, dollar amounts, timeframes).
    - *Weak:* "Helped improve conversion rate."
    - *Strong:* "**Increased conversion rate by 45%** in 3 months through A/B testing and UX improvements."
4. **Associate with Your Experience:** Link the project to the company or role you were in when you completed it.
5. **Add Media (CRUCIAL):** **This is non-negotiable.** Upload or link to:
    - A **screenshot** of the landing page, ad creative, or video still.
    - A **short video** clip of the explainer video or motion graphic.
    - A **one-page case study PDF** (you can create this easily in Canva).
    - A **link** to the live webpage or video.

By adding projects in this detailed, results-oriented way, you transform your profile from a list of duties into a proven portfolio of success, making you incredibly attractive to potential B2B clients.
