## **SECTION 2: MANDATORY CERTIFICATION PROGRAMS**

### **2.1 Core Certification Requirements**

**2.1.1 Company Foundation Certification:**

- **Target Audience:** All team members
- **Duration:** 2 weeks (40 hours)
- **Delivery:** Online self-paced + virtual instructor-led
- **Validity:** 2 years
- **Recertification:** 8-hour refresher course

**2.1.2 Product Knowledge Certification:**

- **Target Audience:** Sales and delivery teams
- **Duration:** 3 weeks (60 hours)
- **Delivery:** Blended learning with practical assessments
- **Validity:** 18 months
- **Recertification:** Product update training

### **2.2 Role-Specific Certifications**

**2.2.1 Sales Professional Certification:**

- **Required For:** All sales personnel
- **Duration:** 4 weeks (80 hours)
- **Components:**
    - Sales methodology mastery
    - Product demonstration skills
    - Client negotiation techniques
    - CRM system proficiency
- **Assessment:** Practical sales simulation

**2.2.2 Technical Specialist Certification:**

- **Required For:** Technical pre-sales and delivery staff
- **Duration:** 6 weeks (120 hours)
- **Components:**
    - Technical architecture understanding
    - Solution design principles
    - Implementation methodologies
    - Troubleshooting and support
- **Assessment:** Technical solution design project

**2.2.3 Management Leadership Certification:**

- **Required For:** Team leads and managers
- **Duration:** 8 weeks (160 hours)
- **Components:**
    - Team leadership principles
    - Performance management
    - Business strategy development
    - Financial management basics
- **Assessment:** Business case development

## **SECTION 5: ASSESSMENT & EVALUATION**

### **5.1 Certification Assessment Methods**

**5.1.1 Knowledge Assessments:**

- **Format:** Multiple choice, true/false, scenario-based
- **Passing Score:** 80% minimum
- **Retake Policy:** 3 attempts maximum
- **Security:** Proctored or online monitoring

**5.1.2 Practical Assessments:**

- **Format:** Real-world scenarios and simulations
- **Evaluation:** Rubric-based scoring
- **Assessors:** Certified trainers or managers
- **Feedback:** Detailed performance review

**5.1.3 Performance Evaluations:**

- **Frequency:** Quarterly skill assessments
- **Method:** Manager observations and client feedback
- **Documentation:** Formal evaluation records
- **Improvement Plans:** For underperforming areas

### **5.2 Certification Maintenance**

**5.2.1 Continuing Education Requirements:**

- **Annual:** Minimum 40 hours of training
- **Product Updates:** Mandatory within 30 days of release
- **Compliance Training:** Annual refresher required
- **Skill Development:** Quarterly workshops

**5.2.2 Recertification Process:**

- **Notice:** 60-day advance notification
- **Requirements:** Updated training completion
- **Assessment:** Current knowledge verification
- **Grace Period:** 30 days post-expiration
