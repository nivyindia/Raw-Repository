# 💼 Nivy Jobs — Division Home

<aside>
💼

**What is Nivy Jobs?**

Nivy Jobs is the talent acquisition and recruitment division of the Nivy Group. It manages all hiring across every division — from full-time roles to VA contracts, freelancers, and internships. This is the single source of truth for open roles, interview processes, job description templates, and onboarding checklists.

**Status:** In Planning — Division being structured

📝 **Owner:** HR Lead | 🔄 **Last Updated:** April 2026 | 🌍 **Markets:** IN · US · UK · CA · AU · UAE

</aside>

## 🎯 Purpose

Nivy Jobs exists to make hiring fast, consistent, and scalable across all Nivy divisions — regardless of market or role type. Every open role flows through this division. Every JD, interview process, and offer template is standardised here.
