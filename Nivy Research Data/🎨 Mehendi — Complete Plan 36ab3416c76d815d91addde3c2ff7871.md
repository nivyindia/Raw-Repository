## 📜 Rasam — Step by Step SOP

1. **Artist aaye** — design discuss karo (photos dikhao)
2. **Dulhan pehle** — bridal full design shuru (2-3 ghante)
3. **Baaki ladies** — helper artist ya main artist (baari baari)
4. **Khana serve karo** — artist kaam karte waqt snacks / dinner
5. **Music + entertainment** — gana-bajaana, entertainment
6. **Mehendi sookne do** — 4-6 ghante minimum
7. **Lemon-sugar paste lagao** — color gehre ke liye
8. **Rat bhar rehne do** — subah chhadao
9. **Subah warm hath se saf karo** (pani se nahi!)

> 💡 **Special Shot:** Dulhe ka naam Dulhan ki mehendi mein chhupaao — photographer ko zaroor batao!
> 

> 💡 **Tip:** Dulhan ke haath mein kuch nahi pakadwao mehendi lagne ke baad — fresh rakhein!
