### **Pro Tips for Presenting This Deck:**

- **Emphasize the Synergy:** This is your unique selling proposition. Constantly refer back to the powerful connection between the two service lines.
- **Address the "But I'm Not an Expert" Objection:** Reassure them that you provide the training, systems, and back-end support for both complex service areas. They are the relationship and business manager.
- **Tailor the Financials:** Be prepared to discuss the investment and potential returns in more detail. Having a one-page financial summary handout is a great idea.
- **Show Enthusiasm:** Your passion for the Nivy model will be contagious.

This presentation positions Nivy as an innovative and sophisticated franchise opportunity, perfectly suited for today's interconnected global business environment. Good luck
