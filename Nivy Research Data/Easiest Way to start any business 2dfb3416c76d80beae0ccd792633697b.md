# PART 3: REAL RISKS IF YOU TAKE THEIR CLIENTS

### ⚠️ 1. Legal Risk (Highest Danger)

Most companies have:

- Non-solicitation clauses
- Non-compete clauses
- Confidentiality clauses

They can:

- Send legal notice
- Freeze payments
- Sue for damages
- Force settlement

You don’t need a signed contract — **proof of intent is enough**.

---

### ⚠️ 2. Reputation Risk (Silent Killer)

- Agencies talk privately
- Founders share names
- You get quietly blacklisted
- Future partnerships close permanently

This damage is invisible but fatal.

---

### ⚠️ 3. Client Trust Risk

Clients think:

> “If he can switch companies once, he can switch again.”
> 

Result:

- Low retention
- Price pressure
- No referrals
- High churn

---

### ⚠️ 4. Scaling Risk

- No serious investor
- No enterprise clients
- No long-term partnerships
- You remain a small operator forever

---

### ⚠️ 5. Psychological Trap

You start thinking:

> “Shortcut is normal”
> 

This:

- Destroys discipline
- Blocks system thinking
- Keeps you reactive, not strategic

---

# PART 4: WHEN TAKING CLIENTS IS ACTUALLY ALLOWED (RARE CASE)

Only if **explicitly written**:

- White-label agreement
- Client ownership clause in your favor
- Transition clause after X months
- Written mutual consent

Without this → **never do it**.
