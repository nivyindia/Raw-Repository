# Steps to Outsource

Here’s a clear, structured **step-by-step guide to outsourcing your sales and marketing work**, especially useful for a digital marketing, tax, or accounting agency like yours 👇

---

## 🧭 **Phase 1: Planning & Strategy**

### 1. **Define Your Goals**

Clearly outline what you want from outsourcing:

- Increase lead generation?
- Improve brand awareness?
- Book more client meetings?
- Expand into new markets?

📄 *Deliverable:* “Outsourcing Goals Document” (1-page summary)

---

### 2. **Decide What to Outsource**

Break down tasks into **core vs. non-core**:

| Sales | Marketing |
| --- | --- |
| Lead generation | Content creation |
| Cold calling / email outreach | Social media management |
| Appointment setting | SEO, SEM, ads |
| CRM management | Branding & design |
| Proposal follow-ups | Campaign analytics |

📄 *Deliverable:* “Scope of Work (SOW)” defining tasks to outsource.

---

### 3. **Set Budget & KPIs**

Decide your monthly/quarterly budget and define measurable success metrics:

- Cost per lead / acquisition
- Conversion rate
- Lead quality
- ROI on campaigns

📄 *Deliverable:* “Performance & Budget Framework”

---

## 🤝 **Phase 2: Finding & Selecting an Agency**

### 4. **Prepare an RFP (Request for Proposal)**

Include:

- About your company
- Objectives
- Scope of work
- Deliverables
- Target audience
- Timeline & budget range
- Evaluation criteria

📄 *Deliverable:* “RFP Document” (You already started this — I can refine it if you’d like.)

---

### 5. **Research & Shortlist Agencies**

Look for:

- Relevant industry experience
- Case studies and client testimonials
- Cultural/communication fit
- Pricing model (fixed, commission, performance-based)

📄 *Deliverable:* “Vendor Comparison Sheet”

---

### 6. **Evaluate Proposals & Conduct Interviews**

Ask:

- How do they generate and qualify leads?
- What tools and platforms do they use (HubSpot, Apollo, etc.)?
- How often will they report progress?
- What’s their onboarding process?

📄 *Deliverable:* “Evaluation Matrix”

---

## 🧩 **Phase 3: Contracting & Onboarding**

### 7. **Negotiate and Sign Contract**

Include clauses for:

- Deliverables & KPIs
- Confidentiality (NDA)
- Payment terms
- Termination & renewal
- Performance reviews

📄 *Deliverable:* “Service Agreement + NDA”

---

### 8. **Provide Access & Assets**

Give them what they need:

- Brand guidelines, logos
- CRM access
- Website, social media admin rights
- Past marketing reports, client personas

📄 *Deliverable:* “Agency Onboarding Kit”

---

### 9. **Kickoff Meeting**

Align on:

- Goals & KPIs
- Communication schedule
- Reporting format (weekly/monthly)
- Project management tool (Asana, ClickUp, Slack, etc.)

📄 *Deliverable:* “Kickoff Plan + Shared Workspace Setup”

---

## 📈 **Phase 4: Execution & Optimization**

### 10. **Monitor & Communicate Regularly**

- Weekly check-ins
- Monthly performance reports
- Quarterly strategy reviews

Use dashboards to track:

- Leads generated
- Campaign ROI
- Funnel performance

📄 *Deliverable:* “Performance Dashboard”

---

### 11. **Optimize & Scale**

- Double down on what works
- Adjust campaigns and messaging
- Explore new lead channels (LinkedIn, ads, email, partnerships)

📄 *Deliverable:* “Quarterly Growth Report”

---

## 🧾 **Optional: Supporting Documents You Should Prepare**

1. Company profile / sales deck
2. Service brochures
3. Target client personas
4. Past campaign reports (if any)
5. Brand style guide
6. Pricing or package details
7. FAQ / policies (payment, refund, support)
