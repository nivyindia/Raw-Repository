# **DEAL & ACCOUNT MANAGER — SOP (UAE COMPANIES)**

**Role Objective:** Manage verified buyer-supplier matches, finalize deals, track delivery, and ensure smooth transaction completion.

---

## **PHASE 1: HANDOVER RECEIPT & INITIAL REVIEW**

**Step 1.1: Receive Handover**

- Input: Verified & soft-confirmed match from Growth & Qualification Lead
- Includes: Buyer & Supplier info, verified documents, ICP details, product, MOQ, timeline, payment terms

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** All info complete → proceed to Step 1.2
- **Fail:** Missing critical info → request clarification from Growth Lead

**Step 1.2: Initial Compliance Check**

- Verify documents (trade license, VAT, certifications)
- Confirm buyer & supplier alignment on ICP, product, and MOQ

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Documents valid & alignment confirmed → Phase 2
- **Fail:** Any document invalid or mismatch → return to Growth Lead

## **PHASE 2: DEAL FINALIZATION / NEGOTIATION**

**Step 2.1: Schedule Deal Discussion**

- Schedule call/meeting with both buyer & supplier (UAE business hours)

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Both parties confirm → proceed
- **Fail:** One party unavailable → reschedule once; if repeated → tag as “On Hold”

**Step 2.2: Confirm Pricing & Payment Terms**

- Agree on final price (AED / FOB / CIF)
- Confirm payment method: Advance / LC / 30–60 day credit

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Terms agreed → proceed
- **Fail:** Terms rejected by either party → renegotiate or fail deal

**Step 2.3: Confirm Delivery Timeline & Logistics**

- Validate feasibility of supply, delivery within agreed timeline
- Confirm UAE-specific logistics: local shipping, customs, port clearance if applicable

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Timeline feasible → proceed
- **Fail:** Timeline unrealistic → renegotiate or fail deal

**Step 2.4: Deal Agreement Documentation**

- Prepare signed deal confirmation (can be email confirmation, PO, or signed contract)
- Store all docs in CRM

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Agreement signed & documented → proceed to Phase 3
- **Fail:** Document incomplete / unsigned → follow up until completion

---

## **PHASE 3: ORDER CONFIRMATION & PAYMENT TRACKING**

**Step 3.1: Buyer Order Confirmation**

- Confirm buyer officially places order via PO or signed email

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Order confirmed → proceed
- **Fail:** Buyer delays >3 days → follow up; if repeated → deal on hold

**Step 3.2: Supplier Acknowledgement**

- Confirm supplier accepts order, confirms capacity, MOQ, and timeline

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Supplier acknowledges → proceed
- **Fail:** Supplier unable to fulfill → return to Phase 7 of Growth Lead to find alternate supplier

**Step 3.3: Payment Tracking**

- Track initial payment / advance per agreed terms
- Confirm receipt before release of goods

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Payment received → proceed
- **Fail:** Payment delayed > agreed timeline → escalate / pause order

---

## **PHASE 4: DELIVERY TRACKING & LOGISTICS**

**Step 4.1: Supplier Dispatch Tracking**

- Confirm shipment dispatched per agreed timeline
- Collect tracking info / Bill of Lading / airway bill

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Shipment dispatched → proceed
- **Fail:** Supplier delay → follow up; escalate if repeated

**Step 4.2: Buyer Receipt Confirmation**

- Confirm buyer receives goods in agreed quantity & quality
- Record feedback / issues if any

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Goods received & accepted → proceed to Phase 5
- **Fail:** Goods damaged / short supply → initiate dispute resolution / replacement

---

## **PHASE 5: POST-DEAL FOLLOW-UP & ISSUE RESOLUTION**

**Step 5.1: Quality / Compliance Check**

- Verify goods meet quality & UAE regulatory compliance

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Compliant → proceed
- **Fail:** Non-compliant → coordinate with supplier for replacement / refund

**Step 5.2: Payment Settlement Tracking**

- Ensure final payment received per contract terms
- Confirm payment transferred to supplier

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Payment settled → close deal
- **Fail:** Payment issue → escalate to finance / legal team

**Step 5.3: Feedback Collection**

- Collect buyer & supplier feedback for future improvement
- Record satisfaction rating, issues faced, improvement notes

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Feedback received → complete
- **Fail:** No feedback → mark and follow up once

---

## **PHASE 6: DEAL CLOSURE & CRM UPDATE**

**Step 6.1: Close Deal in CRM**

- Update deal status: Closed – Success / Closed – Failed
- Attach all documents (PO, invoices, payments, correspondence, tracking)

**Step 6.2: Record Lessons Learned**

- Record delays, disputes, compliance issues, best suppliers/buyers

**Step 6.3: Archive / Reporting**

- Prepare weekly/monthly deal report
- Highlight success rate, pending issues, failed deals

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Deal completed successfully, all records updated → archive
- **Fail:** Any unresolved issue → escalate to manager / legal team
