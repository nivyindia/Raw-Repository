## 🌍 **PHASE 2: Master Partner Program Launch**

> Goal: Find and onboard the first few Master Sales Partners in selected regions.
> 

**1️⃣ Identify Target Regions**

- Start with **3-5 key regions** (e.g., India, UAE, UK, Philippines, Africa).
- Research local business networks, B2B sales consultants, and agencies.

**2️⃣ Create Partner Selection Criteria**

- Industry experience (B2B sales / consulting)
- Local network size
- Management capability
- Willingness to invest time & resources

**3️⃣ Outreach & Recruitment**

- LinkedIn campaigns, franchise portals, and partner invitation emails.
- Personal outreach to business development consultants or agencies.
- Schedule partnership demos (Zoom / Google Meet).

**4️⃣ Onboarding & Training**

- Conduct **“Master Partner Induction Week”** — online training sessions.
- Assign each master partner a **Partner Success Manager**.
- Provide them:
    - Sales scripts & templates
    - Marketing toolkit
    - CRM access
    - Reporting format
    - Sub-partner recruitment guide

---

## 🧭 **PHASE 3: Sub-Partner Expansion**

> Goal: Master partners start recruiting and managing their own local teams.
> 

**1️⃣ Provide a Sub-Partner Onboarding Kit**

- Sub-partner agreement (HQ-approved)
- Training material & SOPs
- Lead management guide
- Branding do’s & don’ts
- Commission structure chart

**2️⃣ Train Masters to Train Others**

- “Train-the-Trainer” sessions — teach master partners how to onboard sub-partners.
- Use LMS or recorded video training modules for consistency.

**3️⃣ Introduce Partner Portal Access**

- Let sub-partners log leads, track payouts, and access marketing material.

**4️⃣ Monitor Quality**

- Monthly partner review calls.
- Feedback and performance dashboard.

---

## 💰 **PHASE 4: Monetization & Revenue Flow**

> Goal: Start generating revenue through direct sales and commissions.
> 

**1️⃣ Activate Lead Channels**

- HQ runs paid ads → distribute leads to regional master partners.
- Masters generate local leads through their own outreach.

**2️⃣ Ensure Clear Payout Flow**

- Client → HQ invoice
- HQ → Master Partner commission
- Master Partner → Sub-partner commission

**3️⃣ Launch Incentive Programs**

- Quarterly bonuses for top sales regions.
- Awards for partner recruitment and retention.

**4️⃣ Introduce Renewal & Recurring Plans**

- Encourage partners to focus on recurring services (retainers, subscriptions).

---

## 📈 **PHASE 5: Optimization & Global Scale**

> Goal: Expand regionally, improve systems, and build brand power.
> 

**1️⃣ Evaluate and Audit**

- Quarterly partner performance audit.
- Identify best-performing regions.

**2️⃣ Build a Partner Council**

- Top 5 partners become part of a “Global Advisory Board.”

**3️⃣ Upgrade Systems**

- Move from spreadsheets → full CRM automation.
- Build your own branded partner portal or app.

**4️⃣ Expand Internationally**

- Introduce **Regional Directors** (continent-level managers).
- Launch PR and co-branding campaigns.

**5️⃣ Annual Global Partner Meet**

- Announce new programs, success stories, and global expansion goals.
