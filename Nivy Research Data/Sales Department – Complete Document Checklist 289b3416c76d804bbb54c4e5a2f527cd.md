# Sales Department – Complete Document Checklist

# 

### **1. Strategy & Planning**

1. Sales Playbook / Strategy Document
2. Sales Goals & KPIs
3. Territory & Account Assignment
4. Target Client List / Ideal Customer Profile (shared with Marketing)
5. Lead Scoring & Qualification Guidelines

---

### **2. Processes & SOPs**

1. Sales Process SOPs (step-by-step from lead generation → closure)
2. CRM / Lead Management Guide
3. Proposal & Quotation Templates (with pricing & packages from Marketing)
4. Follow-up & Nurture Templates (emails, calls, meetings)
5. Objection Handling Guide
6. Communication & Meeting Guidelines
7. Escalation & Support Protocols

---

### **3. Legal & Compliance**

1. Sales Agreement / Contract Template
2. Non-Disclosure / Confidentiality Guidelines

---

### **4. Reporting & Monitoring**

1. Sales Dashboard & Reporting Templates
2. Pipeline Review Templates
3. Performance Scorecards / Review Templates

---

### **5. Training & Knowledge Base**

1. Product / Service Manual (from Marketing)
2. Competitive Intelligence Sheet
3. FAQ Knowledge Base
4. Onboarding Checklist for New Sales Team Members

---

### **6. Incentives & Motivation**

1. Commission Structure & Bonus Policy
2. Recognition & Performance Reward System

---

### **7. Optional / Advanced Documents**

1. Process Improvement Log (lessons learned, best practices)
2. Client Onboarding Guide (from closure → active client setup)
3. Referral / Partner Program Guidelines (if applicable)
