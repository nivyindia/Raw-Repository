# ANNEXURE H: DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Annexure H forms an integral part of the Master Sales Agency Partner Agreement dated [Date] between Billion Dreams United (Nivy) and [Master Partner Company Name].

## **SECTION 1: DEFINITIONS & INTERPRETATION**

### **1.1 Definitions**

**1.1.1 "Applicable Data Protection Laws"** means all applicable privacy and data protection laws including but not limited to:

- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by CPRA
- India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
- Local data protection laws in all operating territories

**1.1.2 "Controller"** means Billion Dreams United (Nivy) who determines the purposes and means of processing personal data.

**1.1.3 "Processor"** means Master Partner who processes personal data on behalf of the Controller.

**1.1.4 "Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

**1.1.5 "Processing"** means any operation performed on personal data.

**1.1.6 "Data Subject"** means the individual to whom personal data relates.

**1.1.7 "Supervisory Authority"** means an independent public authority established for data protection.

### **1.2 Relationship of Parties**

**1.2.1 Controller-Processor Relationship:**

- Company acts as Data Controller
- Master Partner acts as Data Processor
- Sub-Partners act as Sub-Processors
- Clear chain of responsibility established

## **SECTION 3: DATA PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS**

### **3.1 Processing Instructions**

**3.1.1 Documented Instructions:**

- Process personal data only on documented instructions from Controller
- No processing for Processor's own purposes
- Immediate notification if instruction violates data protection law
- Written confirmation of instruction understanding

**3.1.2 Instruction Compliance:**

- Maintain record of all processing instructions
- Regular compliance verification (quarterly)
- Employee training on instruction adherence
- Escalation procedures for instruction conflicts

### **3.2 Confidentiality**

**3.2.1 Personnel Confidentiality:**

- All personnel with data access must sign confidentiality agreements
- Regular confidentiality training (semi-annually)
- Background checks for personnel with significant data access
- Ongoing confidentiality obligations post-employment

**3.2.2 Security of Processing:**

- Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures
- Regular security assessments and updates
- Encryption of personal data in transit and at rest
- Access controls and authentication mechanisms

### **3.3 Security of Processing**

**3.3.1 Technical Measures:**

- Encryption (AES-256) for data at rest and in transit
- Multi-factor authentication for system access
- Regular security patches and updates
- Network security and intrusion detection

**3.3.2 Organizational Measures:**

- Data protection policies and procedures
- Security awareness training programs
- Incident response and business continuity plans
- Regular security audits and assessments

### **3.4 Sub-Processing**

**3.4.1 Authorization Requirement:**

- No sub-processing without Controller's prior written authorization
- Maintain list of authorized sub-processors
- Provide 30-day notice for new sub-processor appointments
- Right to object to sub-processor appointments

**3.4.2 Sub-Processor Obligations:**

- Flow-down of data protection obligations to sub-processors
- Sub-processor compliance monitoring
- Liability for sub-processor breaches
- Sub-processor audit rights

## **SECTION 4: DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS**

### **4.1 Assistance with Data Subject Requests**

**4.1.1 Request Handling:**

- Implement procedures to assist with data subject requests
- Respond to Controller within 48 hours of receiving request
- No direct response to data subjects without Controller approval
- Maintain records of all data subject requests

**4.1.2 Types of Assistance:**

- Access, rectification, and erasure requests
- Restriction of processing objections
- Data portability requests
- Automated decision-making objections

### **4.2 Specific Rights Assistance**

**4.2.1 Right to Access:**

- Provide mechanism to extract personal data
- Assist in providing data subjects with their data
- Verify identity before providing access
- Maintain access logs

**4.2.2 Right to Erasure:**

- Implement data deletion procedures
- Confirm deletion upon completion
- Maintain deletion records
- Handle technical limitations appropriately

**4.2.3 Right to Object:**

- Implement opt-out mechanisms
- Process objections within required timeframes
- Update marketing preferences immediately
- Confirm objection processing

## **SECTION 5: DATA BREACH MANAGEMENT**

### **5.1 Breach Notification**

**5.1.1 Notification Timeline:**

- Notify Controller without undue delay upon becoming aware of breach
- Maximum notification time: 24 hours
- Initial notification may be in phases with more information following
- Final detailed notification within 72 hours

**5.1.2 Notification Content:**

- Nature of the personal data breach
- Categories and approximate number of data subjects affected
- Categories and approximate number of personal data records concerned
- Likely consequences of the personal data breach
- Measures taken or proposed to address the breach

### **5.2 Breach Response**

**5.2.1 Immediate Actions:**

- Contain and mitigate the breach
- Preserve evidence for investigation
- Notify relevant internal stakeholders
- Cooperate with Controller's investigation

**5.2.2 Investigation Requirements:**

- Root cause analysis
- Impact assessment
- Remediation plan development
- Implementation of corrective actions

### **5.3 Breach Documentation**

**5.3.1 Record Keeping:**

- Document all personal data breaches
- Include facts, effects, and remedial actions
- Maintain records for minimum 3 years
- Available for supervisory authority review

- No independent retention decision-making

**9.1.2 Legal Requirements:**

- Comply with legal retention requirements
- Document legal basis for extended retention

- Secure storage during retention period

### **9.2 Data Deletion**

**9.2.1 Deletion Procedures:**
