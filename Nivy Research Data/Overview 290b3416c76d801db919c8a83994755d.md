# Overview

### **Sales Compensation & Incentive Policy - Key Components**

### **1. Policy Administration**

- **Effective Date & Version Number**
- **Policy Purpose & Objectives**
- **Scope & Eligibility** (Which roles does this apply to)
- **Review Cycle** (When the policy is typically reviewed)

### **2. Compensation Structure**

- **Fixed Salary Details**
- **Variable Pay Components**
- **On-Target Earnings (OTE)** - Total potential at 100% target achievement

### **3. Performance Metrics**

- **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
- **Revenue Targets** (Monthly/Quarterly)
- **Sales Quotas**
- **Measurement Periods**

### **4. Incentive Calculation**

- **Commission Rates & Tiers**
- **Bonus Structures**
- **Accelerators** (Higher rates for over-achievement)
- **SPIFFs/Contests** (Short-term incentives)

### **5. Payout Terms**

- **Payout Schedule** (When incentives are paid)
- **Payment Method**
- **Clawback Provisions** (Conditions for reclaiming paid incentives)
- **Hold Policies**

### **6. Sales Crediting**

- **Territory/Account Assignment**
- **Split Commission Rules**
- **Deal Registration Process**
- **Revenue Recognition Rules**

### **7. Performance Management**

- **Quota Assignment Process**
- **Performance Reviews**
- **Performance Improvement Plans**
- **Dispute Resolution Process**

### **8. Policy Governance**

- **Amendment Procedures**
- **Communication of Changes**
- **Compliance Requirements**
- **Record Keeping**

### **9. Employee Acknowledgment**

- **Signature Section**
- **Understanding Confirmation**
- **Agreement to Terms**

### **Key Benefits of This Structure:**

- **Transparency** - Clear earning potential
- **Motivation** - Rewards performance
- **Flexibility** - Easy to update
- **Fairness** - Consistent application
- **Legal Protection** - Well-documented terms

This structure ensures your sales team understands exactly how they earn and what's expected, while protecting the company's interests.
