### **Important Notes for this Salary Slip (CTC ₹35,000):**

1. **No EPF Deduction:** As requested, the Provident Fund (EPF) component has been removed from the salary structure.
2. **No ESI Deduction:** Since the Gross Salary (₹35,000) is above the ESI ceiling of ₹21,000, no ESI is deducted.
3. **Professional Tax (PT):** A small professional tax (typically ₹200-₹300/month in many states) may be deducted, which would slightly reduce the net payable amount. This is not included in the calculation above. The final in-hand salary would be **CTC minus Professional Tax**.
4. **Tax Implications:** Without EPF deductions, the employee's tax liability may be higher as there is no long-term savings component to claim under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act.
5. **Component Breakdown:** The structure maintains a standard breakdown with Basic Salary at 60% of CTC and HRA at 33.33% of Basic, which is optimal for tax planning purposes. The Special Allowance is the flexible, fully taxable balancing amount.
