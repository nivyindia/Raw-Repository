## 📫 Invitation Card Plan

### Card Design & Printing

- [ ]  Card ka design decide kare (traditional / modern / both)
- [ ]  Card mein likhi jaane wali information:
    - Dulha + Dulhan ka naam
    - Date, Time, Venue
    - Sari rasamon ki dates (Haldi, Mehendi, Baraat, Reception)
    - Contact person ka number
    - Address + Google Maps link (agar digital)
- [ ]  Design proof ek baar poore parivar ko dikhaye
- [ ]  Printing press / online order de
- [ ]  **Total cards print kare = Guests + 15% extra**
- [ ]  Cards collect kare aur count kare
