## **SECTION 7: PERFORMANCE REVIEW & REMEDIATION**

### **7.1 Performance Review Schedule**

**Quarterly Business Reviews:**

- Timing: Within 15 days of quarter-end
- Participants: Company management and Master Partner leadership
- Agenda: Performance analysis, strategic planning, issue resolution

**Monthly Performance Check-ins:**

- Timing: First week of each month
- Focus: Progress against monthly targets, corrective actions

### **7.2 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) Triggers**

Automatic PIP initiation when:

- Two consecutive months below 80% of revenue target
- Customer satisfaction scores below minimum for two consecutive periods
- Sub-partner recruitment behind schedule by more than 30 days
- Team composition below minimum requirements

### **7.3 PIP Structure**

**Duration:** 30 days

**Components:**

- Specific performance gaps identified
- Clear improvement targets
- Required actions and resources
- Weekly progress checkpoints
- Consequences of non-improvement
