# **Nivy Founder Exit / Autonomy Readiness Checklist**

---

## **1️⃣ SOPs & Knowledge Base**

| Task | Checkpoint / Metric | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All core operations documented (Finance, HR, Sales, Marketing, Ops, IT) | SOPs exist and are regularly updated | ☐ |
| Knowledge base available for onboarding, troubleshooting, templates | Employees can solve issues without asking you | ☐ |
| Templates for contracts, reports, dashboards, emails | Standardized & accessible | ☐ |
| Version control / update mechanism in place | SOPs are reviewed quarterly | ☐ |

**Goal:** Any employee can follow the SOP to complete tasks successfully.

---

## **2️⃣ Leadership & Accountability**

| Task | Checkpoint / Metric | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Department heads / managers clearly defined | One manager per functional area | ☐ |
| Decision-making boundaries defined | Managers know what can be decided autonomously | ☐ |
| Escalation process documented | Only exceptions go to CEO | ☐ |
| Leadership training / empowerment | Managers trained on SOPs, KPIs, & crisis handling | ☐ |

**Goal:** Teams solve problems without your constant input.

---

## **3️⃣ Dashboards & KPIs**

| Task | Checkpoint / Metric | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Real-time dashboards implemented | Finance, Sales, Marketing, Ops, HR, IT, BD | ☐ |
| KPIs defined & measurable | OKRs & performance metrics automated | ☐ |
| Alerts & exceptions configured | Only notify CEO for deviations beyond threshold | ☐ |
| Reporting schedule automated | Daily, weekly, monthly updates without manual input | ☐ |

**Goal:** CEO can view company health at a glance.

---

## **4️⃣ Automation**

| Task | Checkpoint / Metric | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payroll, tax filing, benefits, compliance | Automated & error-checked | ☐ |
| Invoicing, billing, accounting reconciliations | Auto-generated & reconciled | ☐ |
| Marketing campaigns, lead nurturing, social posting | Automated workflows & triggers | ☐ |
| IT monitoring, DevOps pipelines, backups | Automated alerts and resolution processes | ☐ |
| HR workflows (attendance, leaves, appraisals) | Automated approvals & notifications | ☐ |

**Goal:** Critical operations continue without manual intervention.

---

## **5️⃣ Teams & Culture**

| Task | Checkpoint / Metric | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employees understand KPIs & OKRs | Each role knows success metrics | ☐ |
| Leadership empowerment | Teams can make decisions within boundaries | ☐ |
| Training & onboarding self-service | Employees can onboard & train themselves | ☐ |
| Engagement & recognition automated | Feedback loops, performance reviews, surveys in place | ☐ |

**Goal:** Staff operate effectively, culture is maintained, and morale is high.

---

## **6️⃣ Crisis & Contingency Planning**

| Task | Checkpoint / Metric | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Risk management plans in place | Finance, IT, HR, Operations, Legal | ☐ |
| Backup systems & failover | Servers, cloud, data, operations continuity | ☐ |
| Emergency escalation documented | Who handles emergencies if CEO is absent | ☐ |
| Testing & drills | Teams practice contingency scenarios | ☐ |

**Goal:** Company continues functioning even during emergencies.

---

## **7️⃣ Founder/CEO Role**

| Task | Checkpoint / Metric | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily operational involvement eliminated | Tasks are executed by teams | ☐ |
| CEO only handles strategic initiatives | High-level decisions only | ☐ |
| Can take 1–2 weeks off without disruptions | Dashboards & alerts suffice | ☐ |
| Strategic reviews scheduled | Quarterly or exception-based | ☐ |

**Goal:** CEO functions purely as visionary & strategist.

---

## **✅ How to Use This Checklist**

1. **Audit each area:** Tick only when the process is fully implemented and verified.
2. **Test autonomy:** Take short breaks and monitor operations remotely via dashboards.
3. **Gradually step back:** Move from daily supervision → weekly check-ins → monthly oversight.
4. **Refine SOPs & dashboards:** Continuous improvement until no operations stop in your absence.

---

💡 **Rule of Thumb:**

> When all checkboxes are ticked, dashboards show healthy KPIs, teams solve problems autonomously, and operations continue during your absence, your company can run without you.
