### **Software Setup Tasks for Automated Outreach System**

This list extracts only the technical configuration steps, providing a clear checklist for you or a tech assistant to execute.

---

### **(Last me -Category 1: CRM & Meeting Booking (Core Hub)**

| Software | Setup Tasks |
| --- | --- |
| **Odoo (Free CRM)** | 1. Create a free account. <br> 2. Customize the deal pipeline stages: `Lead` -> `Contacted` -> `Meeting Booked` -> `Proposal Sent` -> `Closed Won` -> `Closed Lost`. <br> 3. Create required contact properties: `Lead Source`, `Channel`, `Notes`. <br> 4. Install the HubSpot tracking code on your website/landing page. |
| **Calendly** | 1. Create a free/pro account. <br> 2. Set your available meeting hours and buffer times. <br> 3. Create a meeting event type (e.g., "15-Minute Discovery Call"). <br> 4. Connect your calendar (Google/Outlook) to avoid double-booking. <br> 5. Customize the booking page with your logo and questions (e.g., "What is your biggest challenge?"). <br> 6. **Integration:** Connect Calendly to HubSpot CRM to automatically create a contact and log the meeting when someone books. |

### **Category 2: Email Outreach Automation**

| Software | Setup Tasks |
| --- | --- |
| [**Instantly.ai](http://instantly.ai/) / [SmartReach.io](http://smartreach.io/)** | 1. Create an account. <br> 2. **Connect Sending Domains:** Add and verify 2-3 domains for sending (e.g., `mail.youragency.com`). Warm them up using the tool's built-in warmup feature. <br> 3. **Create Sending Accounts:** Create email accounts for each domain (e.g., `john@mail.youragency.com`). <br> 4. **Build Sequences:** Create your first email sequence: <br>    - Email 1: Value-based outreach. <br>    - Email 2: Follow-up (add more value). <br>    - Email 3: Breakup email / final offer. <br> 5. **Settings:** Set up open/click tracking. Configure bounce handling. <br> 6. **Import Leads:** Upload your first list of target emails and assign them to the sequence. |

---

### **Category 3: LinkedIn & Social Automation**

| Software | Setup Tasks |
| --- | --- |
| [**Expandi.io](http://expandi.io/) / Dux-Soup** | 1. Create an account. <br> 2. **Connect LinkedIn Profile:** Login via the dashboard to connect your profile securely. <br> 3. **Configure Safety Settings:** Set daily connection limits (e.g., 30-40 requests/day), random delays between actions, and weekly pause days to mimic human behavior and avoid bans. <br> 4. **Build Campaign:** <br>    - **Search Criteria:** Input your target audience (e.g., "Title: Marketing Director, Industry: Marketing & Advertising"). <br>    - **Sequence:** Create an automated sequence: <br>        Step 1: Send Connection Request with personalized note. <br>        Step 2: (If connected) Send Follow-up DM with value. <br>        Step 3: (If no response) Send final DM with Calendly link. <br> 5. **Activate Campaign.** |
| **SocialBee / [Later.com](http://later.com/)** | 1. Create an account. <br> 2. **Connect Social Profiles:** Connect your LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, Pinterest company pages/profiles. <br> 3. **Create Categories:** Match your content pillars (e.g., "Tips," "Case Studies," "Behind the Scenes"). <br> 4. **Setup Posting Schedule:** Define how many times per day/week to post on each platform. <br> 5. **Upload Batched Content:** Upload all your pre-made carousels, images, and video posts. Write captions and hashtags. <br> 6. **Configure RSS Feeds** (Optional): Add industry blog RSS feeds for automatic content sharing. |

### **Category 4: Messaging App Automation (Use with Caution)**

| Software | Setup Tasks |
| --- | --- |
| **ManyChat / WATI** | 1. Create an account. <br> 2. **Connect Channel:** Connect your WhatsApp Business API number or Facebook Messenger page. <br> 3. **Build Flows:** Create automated conversation workflows using a visual builder: <br>    - **Flow 1: Welcome Message.** Triggered when someone messages first. <br>    - **Flow 2: Broadcast.** Send your offer to a list of numbers. <br>    - **Flow 3: Keyword Response.** e.g., If someone texts "AUDIT", send them the lead magnet link. <br> 4. **Integration:** Connect the tool to your CRM to log contacts. |

### **Category 5: Landing Page & Website**

| Software | Setup Tasks |
| --- | --- |
| **Carrd / Leadpages** | 1. Create an account. <br> 2. **Choose a template** for a lead magnet landing page. <br> 3. **Build the page:** <br>    - Add headline (e.g., "Get Your Free Lead Gen Audit Checklist"). <br>    - Add description. <br>    - Add an email capture form. <br>    - Add a "Download Now" button that triggers after email submission. <br>    - Add a secondary button: "Book a Free Strategy Call" (links to Calendly). <br> 4. **Integrate:** Connect the form to your email marketing software (e.g., Mailchimp) or CRM to capture emails automatically. |
