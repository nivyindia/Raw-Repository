# 💡 What is Nivy Global?

> 🟢 **Status: Live** | Owner: Nivy Global Lead | Last Updated: April 2026
> 

**Nivy Global** is Nivy Digital's international business expansion division. We handle everything related to growing Nivy's presence beyond borders — signing partner agreements, setting up franchise models, developing client proposals, and building strategic alliances worldwide.

If you are a new partner, potential franchisee, or team member joining Nivy Global, this page will orient you.

| Area | Description |
