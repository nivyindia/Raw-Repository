## 📜 Rasam — Step by Step SOP

1. **Subah snaana aur taiyaari** — ghar ke sab log taiyaar hote hain
2. **Madwa sthaapit kiya jaata hai** — purush log milkar bamboo / lakdi se madwa banate hain
3. **Sthaan shuddhi** — pandit ji se gangajal ya hawan se jagah pavitra ki jaati hai
4. **Ganesh puja** — pehle Ganpati ji ki sthapana aur puja
5. **Kuldevta / Grih devta puja** — ghar ke devta se shadi ka ashirwad maanga jaata hai
6. **Suhaginon ki rasam** —  5 ya 7 suhagan striyan dulha / dulhan ko tilak lagati hain
7. **Madwa ko sajaya jaata hai** — phool, patta, mauli se
8. **Prasad vitaran** — sab ko halwa / kheer ka prasad diya jaata hai
9. **Khana** — sab milkar khate hain
10. **Shubhkamnayein** — bade ashirwad dete hain
