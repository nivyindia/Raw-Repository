# 📌 Roles, Responsibilities & Targets

## 1. **Lead Generation BDEs (India)**

**Objective:** Build a consistent pipeline of qualified leads using automation + research.

**Responsibilities & Tasks:**

- Research & build targeted lead lists (industries, decision-makers, emails, LinkedIn).
- Use automation tools (Apollo, Lemlist, Instantly, Smartlead, LinkedIn outreach).
- Personalize & run cold email + LinkedIn drip campaigns.
- Maintain CRM (tagging leads, tracking replies, updating status).
- Qualify responses (separate warm leads vs junk).
- Book appointments for closers.

**Targets / KPIs:**

- 300–500 verified leads added per month (per BDE).
- 5,000+ outreach emails/messages sent per month (with automation).
- 10–20 warm leads/month (interested responses).
- 3–5 appointments booked/month for closers.

---

## 2. **Closers (PH / Freelance Digital Marketers)**

**Objective:** Convert qualified leads into paying clients under your brand.

**Responsibilities & Tasks:**

- Conduct discovery & qualification calls.
- Present service offerings using brand-approved pitch decks & scripts.
- Overcome objections and negotiate pricing.
- Close deals and follow through till contract/payment.
- Maintain deal records in CRM (HubSpot/Pipedrive/Zoho).
- Upsell/cross-sell additional services when possible.

**Targets / KPIs:**

- 10–15 discovery calls/month.
- 3–5 new deals closed/month.
- Closing ratio: ~20–25% of warm leads.
- Revenue target per closer: $10k–15k/month.

**Compensation Structure (example):**

- Commission: 10–15% of deal value.
- Optional small retainer: $200–400/month.

---

## 3. **Campaign / Content Specialists (Freelance or Part-time)**

**Objective:** Support lead-gen and sales with high-converting content & campaigns.

**Responsibilities & Tasks:**

- Create & optimize landing pages, ads, and nurture sequences.
- Write case studies, blogs, client success stories.
- Design email templates, social posts, ad creatives.
- Test campaigns (Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads).
- Provide analytics & recommendations to improve conversions.

**Targets / KPIs:**

- Deliver 4–6 high-quality content assets/month (blogs, case studies, creatives).
- Improve email campaign open rate to 30%+ and reply rate to 5–8%.
- Paid ads CTR 2%+ (depending on platform).
- Support closers with 1–2 new case studies/testimonials per month.

---

## 4. **Management / You (Owner/Director)**

**Objective:** Orchestrate, monitor KPIs, and keep the team aligned to P&L goals.

**Responsibilities & Tasks:**

- Build & update sales playbooks (scripts, objection handling, CRM workflows).
- Hire, train, and onboard BDEs/closers.
- Monitor CRM dashboards weekly (pipeline, revenue forecast).
- Review BDE & closer performance weekly.
- Ensure specialists deliver content that matches brand tone.
- Reinvest profits into scaling (more BDEs, higher-ticket clients, ads).

**Targets / KPIs:**

- Maintain profit margin ≥60%.
- Ensure $40k/month revenue → grow by 15–20% monthly.
- BDE-to-closer ratio: ~2:1 at scale.
- Pipeline coverage: 3–4x revenue target at any time.

## 🔹 Step 1: Lead Generation (India)

**Role:** BDEs (India) + Automation Tools

- **Headcount:** Start with 2–3 BDEs.
- **Salary Range:** $400–600/month each.
- **Tools Budget:** $200–500/month (Apollo, Instantly, Lemlist, Smartlead, LinkedIn scraping).
- **Responsibilities:**
    - Build + clean lists.
    - Run automated cold email + LinkedIn sequences.
    - Track open/reply rates.
    - Pass warm leads to closers.

**Cost Estimate (3 BDEs + tools):** $1,600–2,300/month.

👉 Each BDE should deliver ~200–300 qualified leads/month with automation.

## 🔹 Step 2: Closers (PH / Freelance Digital Marketers)

**Role:** Freelance Closers under your brand policies.

- **Compensation:** Commission only OR small retainer + commission.
    - Retainer (optional): $200–400/month.
    - Commission: 10–15% of closed deal value.
- **Structure:**
    - Work as your “Sales Executives.”
    - Use your playbooks, CRM, and brand email.
    - Incentivized purely on results → no heavy fixed cost.

**Cost Estimate:** If they close $20k/month revenue → 12% commission = $2,400 payout.

👉 Net still profitable since you’re running ~60% margin.
