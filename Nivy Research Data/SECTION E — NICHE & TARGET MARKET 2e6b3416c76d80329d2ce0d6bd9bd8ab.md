---

## **1. Our Niche**

**Nivy Nexus** specializes in **B2B trade facilitation and supply chain enablement**.

We focus on:

- **Verified supplier discovery** for buyers
- **End-to-end trade coordination**
- **Risk-free bulk sourcing**
- **Domestic and international trade enablement**

We do **not compete with suppliers or buyers**; instead, we **bridge the gap** between demand and supply safely and efficiently.

---

## **2. Ideal Buyer Profile**

We work best with:

- **SMEs, distributors, and wholesalers**
- **Importers and exporters**
- **Businesses seeking verified, bulk, or repeat suppliers**
- **Companies needing structured coordination and risk reduction**

**Buyer expectations we fulfill:**

- Verified supplier access
- Transparent pricing and process
- Secure and reliable transaction flow
- Support with compliance and delivery

---

## **3. Ideal Supplier Profile**

We onboard suppliers who:

- Are **legally registered and compliant**
- Have **capacity to deliver bulk or repeat orders**
- Maintain **quality and reliability standards**
- Can provide **documentation and references**
- Are ready for **structured trade processes**

**Supplier benefits we provide:**

- Access to genuine buyers
- Risk reduction through verification
- Coordinated deals with clear terms
- Long-term partnership opportunities

---

## **4. Industries Served**

While our model is **industry-agnostic**, we have strong experience in:

1. FMCG & Cosmetics
2. Manufacturing & Industrial Goods
3. Construction Materials
4. Textiles & Apparel
5. Packaging & Raw Materials

**Custom sourcing for other industries** is also handled case by case.

---

## **5. Geographic Scope**

- **Domestic (Pan-India)** — Verified suppliers across all major states
- **International / Export-Ready Suppliers** — For buyers seeking global supply
- **Global Buyers** — Coordinated via Nivy Nexus processes and verification

---

## **6. Buyer–Supplier Matching Philosophy**

- Only **verified suppliers** are presented to buyers
- Only **credible buyers** are introduced to suppliers
- Every match is **risk-mitigated, process-driven, and transparent**
- Focus is on **long-term trade partnerships**, not one-time transactions
