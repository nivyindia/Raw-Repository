## **The Equity/Share Agreement Structure**

### **Smart Equity Options:**

**Option A: Revenue Share**

- 2-5% of revenue from clients you helped them acquire
- Limited to 12-24 months

**Option B: Small Equity**

- 1-3% company equity
- Only for businesses with high growth potential

**Option C: Performance Bonus**

- One-time bonus when they hit specific milestones
- Lower risk for both parties

## **Risk Mitigation**

### **Potential Risks & Solutions:**

**Risk:** Businesses take free training and disappear
**Solution:** Carefully select committed partners with growth potential

**Risk:** Equity becomes worthless
**Solution:** Focus on businesses with real traction and potential

**Risk:** Brand dilution with too many partners
**Solution:** Be selective - quality over quantity
