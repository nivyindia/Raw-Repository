## 📜 Rasam — Step by Step SOP

1. **Dulhe wale pahunchte hain** — swagat aur aarti
2. **Bade log baithte hain** — dono families introduce hoti hain
3. **Pandit ji puja shuru karte hain** — mangal aarti, sankalp
4. **Tilak ceremony** — dulhan ke papa / bade bhai dulhe ko tilak lagate hain
5. **Ring exchange** — dulha-dulhan ek doosre ko ring pehnate hain
6. **Gifts exchange** — dono families kapde, mithai, lifafa exchange karte hain
7. **Aarti aur ashirwad** — bade log ashirwad dete hain
8. **Photos / group photos** — family ke saath zaroor le
9. **Khana** — sab milkar khate hain
10. **Date confirm / announce** — shadi ki date sabko batayein
