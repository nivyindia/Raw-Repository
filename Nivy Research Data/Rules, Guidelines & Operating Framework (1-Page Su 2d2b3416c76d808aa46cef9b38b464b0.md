# **Nivy Alliance – Rules, Guidelines & Operating Framework (1-Page Summary)**

## 1. What is Nivy Alliance?

Nivy Alliance is a **private, admin-curated business exchange** where verified companies share **genuine business requirements** and receive **trusted introductions** without public exposure or spam.

The alliance is built on **confidentiality, reciprocity, and quality control**.

---

## 2. How It Works (Simple Flow)

1. **Member submits a requirement** to the Admin (buyer details hidden)
2. **Admin verifies** the requirement for seriousness & clarity
3. **Verified requirement is posted** in the group (no buyer contact shown)
4. **Interested members respond** via Admin with capability proof
5. **Admin matches best-fit suppliers**
6. **Direct buyer–seller introduction** happens privately
7. **Deal discussion happens outside the group**

---

## 3. Membership Rules

✔ Only **registered businesses / agencies / service providers**

✔ Freelancers allowed **only if approved by Admin**

✔ Members must be **active contributors**, not silent observers

✔ Fake profiles, brokers without authority, or spammers are **not allowed**

---

## 4. Posting Rules (Very Important)

✅ Only **business requirements**, partnerships, or verified opportunities

❌ No direct selling pitches in the group

❌ No posting phone numbers, emails, or WhatsApp links

❌ No DMing members without Admin approval

❌ No job spam, crypto, MLM, or irrelevant promotions

**Violation = Immediate Warning or Removal**

---

## 5. Confidentiality & Trust Policy

🔒 Buyer identity remains **confidential until mutual interest**

🔒 Screenshots, forwarding, or external sharing is **strictly prohibited**

🔒 Group content is **internal & protected**

Any breach results in **permanent ban**.

---

## 6. Gamification & Contribution System

Members earn **priority access & visibility** by:

⭐ Posting verified requirements

⭐ Successfully fulfilling deals

⭐ Providing referrals or partnerships

⭐ Maintaining professional conduct

Top contributors get:

- Faster approvals
- Featured visibility
- Early access to premium leads
- Strategic partnership opportunities

---

## 7. Admin Authority

Admins have full rights to:

- Approve or reject members
- Verify or decline requirements
- Control introductions
- Remove members without explanation if rules are violated

Admin decisions are **final** to protect ecosystem quality.

---

## 8. What This Alliance Is NOT

❌ Not a WhatsApp marketing group

❌ Not a cold-pitching space

❌ Not a free lead dumping ground

❌ Not a public marketplace

It is a **controlled, trust-based business ecosystem**.

---

## 9. Member Commitment

By joining Nivy Alliance, you agree to:

✔ Follow all rules

✔ Respect confidentiality

✔ Maintain professional standards

✔ Contribute value, not noise
