The Growth Engine is Nivy's strategy and experimentation division for business development, outreach, and platform model building. It contains the master playbook, founder strategy docs, outreach targets, research notes, campaign experiments, and all key decisions made during Nivy's growth phase.

## 🗺️ What's Inside Each Folder

### 🎯 Strategy & Frameworks

The master thinking layer — all frameworks, playbooks, and blueprints that define how the Growth Engine operates.

- **Growth Engine Strategy** — The complete 7-engine growth map and master tactics table
- **Growth Engine Playbook** — Day-to-day operating guide for the growth function
- **Platform Business Model Blueprint** — Full platform model: org, revenue, retention, tools
- **Universal Framework — Nivy Platform Model** — Overarching business framework across all markets
- **Master Hierarchy Table** — Structure and role hierarchy across the business
- **Master Index** — Global growth strategies reference (10-layer master index)

### 📋 Planning & Decisions

Active planning documents, founder packs, and a permanent record of key decisions.

- **Founder Master Pack** — Founder-level reference and operating document
- **Active Planning Notes** — Short-term priorities and active planning
- **Decisions Log** — Permanent record of all key decisions with context
- **Dept-wise Framework** — Department-level operational framework

### 📣 Outreach & Campaigns

All campaign execution materials — target lists, channel strategies, email sequences, and distribution plans.

- **Outreach Target List** — B2B outreach targets by market
- **Campaign Test Scenarios** — A/B tests and outreach experiments
- **Email Campaign Scenarios** — Email campaign test runs and templates
- **Inbound Marketing** — Inbound lead strategy and channel plan
- **Public Promotion Engine (PPE)** — Public and crowd-based promotion strategy
- **Distribution System** — How we distribute and deliver across channels
- **Offline Enquiry & Facilitation Outlets** — Offline channels and referral facilitation
- **Supplier Outreach Strategy** — Targeting and outreach to supply-side partners
- **Demand Outreach Strategy** — Demand-side outreach strategy and scripts

### 🔬 Research & Intelligence

Raw research, market intelligence, and operational SOPs that inform growth decisions.

- **Research Notes & Findings** — Raw discovery notes from market research
- **Research & Discussi A photo on Notes** — Discussion logs and strategic insights
- **Sales Research** — Sales-side market and competitor research
- **General Public Use Cases** — Use case research across general public segments
- **Supply-Demand Matching SOP** — SOP for matching supply to demand

### 🤝 Partnerships & Growth Ops

Referral programs and growth team operations.

- **Refer & Earn Program** — Referral program strategy and activation
- **Hiring — Growth Team** — Open roles and hiring plan for the division
