# Social Media Lead Generation Automation Blueprint

# **PHASE 1: CONTENT STRATEGY FOR LEAD CAPTURE**

### **Content Pillars:**

1. **Value Posts** (80%) - Educate & engage
2. **Soft Offer Posts** (15%) - Lead magnets
3. **Hard Offer Posts** (5%) - Direct sales

### **Lead Magnet Posts:**

- "Comment 'PDF' below for free template"
- "DM 'STRATEGY' for free audit"
- "Click link in bio for free [service]"

## **PHASE 2: AUTOMATED RESPONSE SYSTEM**

### **Instant DM Automation:**

**When user comments/DMs trigger words:**

```
Trigger: "PDF", "TEMPLATE", "GUIDE"
Auto-Reply: "Thanks! Get your free [resource] here: [link] + Would you like [related free service]?"

```

```
Trigger: "STRATEGY", "AUDIT", "CONSULT"
Auto-Reply: "I'll help with that! Please share your email for personalized audit: [link]"

```

```
Trigger: "PRICING", "COST", "PRICE"
Auto-Reply: "Let me connect you with our specialist. Please share: 1) Your business type 2) Current monthly revenue 3) Biggest challenge"

```

## **PHASE 3: MULTI-PLATFORM FUNNEL**

### **Platform-Specific Strategies:**

**Instagram:**

- Story polls → "Want results? DM 'RESULTS'"
- Lead forms in ads
- "Link in Bio" tools with email capture

**Facebook:**

- Group membership → "Welcome DM" automation
- Messenger bots for qualification
- Lead ads with auto-form fill

**LinkedIn:**

- Connection requests → Auto follow-up sequence
- "Open to work" profile targeting
- Content → "DM for case study" offers

**Twitter:**

- Automated replies to relevant tweets
- Lead capture via pinned tweet
- "Click to DM" campaigns

## **PHASE 4: LEAD QUALIFICATION & SCORING**

### **Automated Qualification DM Sequence:**

```
DM 1 (Instant): "Thanks for your interest! Quick question: What's your #1 marketing challenge right now?"

DM 2 (2 hours later if no response): "Following up - would you like me to send specific strategies for [their industry]?"

DM 3 (24 hours later): "Not sure if you saw my previous message. We're offering free [service] to 5 businesses this week. Interested?"

```

### **Lead Scoring:**

- +10: Follows/connects
- +20: Likes/engages with content
- +30: Comments/DMs trigger word
- +50: Shares email/phone
- +100: Books call directly

## **PHASE 5: SALES HANDOFF AUTOMATION**

### **Hot Lead Triggers:**

- **Score 75+:** Auto-send Calendly link via DM
- **Score 100+:** Immediate WhatsApp transfer + personal call
- **Specific request:** Auto-assign to available BDE

### **Warm Lead Nurture:**

- Add to retargeting ads audience
- Invite to free webinar
- Send case studies via DM

## **PHASE 6: CROSS-PLATFORM SYNC**

### **Automated Platform Integration:**

```
Instagram Lead → Add to Facebook Custom Audience → Retarget with Messenger Bot

```

```
LinkedIn Connection → Auto-invite to Facebook Group → Group welcome sequence

```

```
Twitter Engager → Add to Email List → Welcome sequence

```
