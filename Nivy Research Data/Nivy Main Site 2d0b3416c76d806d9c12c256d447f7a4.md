## 🔑 Important Things You Were Missing (Very Important)

### 1️⃣ Brand Positioning Pages (Most Missed)

- **Why Nivy Empires**
- **Our Approach**
    
    👉 These build **Tier-1 trust**, not sales pages.
    

---

### 2️⃣ Clear Separation of Roles

- Main website = **Authority & Ecosystem**
- Subdomains = **Selling & Conversion**

Never mix these.

---

### 3️⃣ Footer Is Strategic (Not Decorative)

Your footer should:

- Link all subdomains
- Show ecosystem strength
- Carry legal trust

---

### 4️⃣ No Pricing on Main Website

❌ Pricing here weakens brand

✅ Pricing only on subdomains

---

### 5️⃣ No Heavy Forms on Main Website

Use:

- “Business Enquiry”
- “Partnership Enquiry”
    
    Not long service forms.
    

---
