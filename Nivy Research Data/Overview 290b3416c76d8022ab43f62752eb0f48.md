## **Annexure A: Sales Target & Incentive Schedule – Outline**

### **1. Document Overview**

- **Purpose of Annexure:** Define targets, incentives, and payout structure linked to the Sales Compensation Policy.
- **Effective Period:** Start and end date of the applicable performance cycle (e.g., FY 2025–26, Q1 2026).
- **Applicable Roles:** Specify which positions or designations this annexure applies to (e.g., BDE, Sr. BDE).

---

### **2. Sales Target Framework**

- **Target Allocation Basis:** (e.g., revenue, number of clients, deals closed, geography, product line).
- **Target Periodicity:** Monthly, quarterly, and annual goals.
- **Target Distribution:** Percentage weight for each product/service or territory.
- **Adjustment Clause:** Authority to revise targets based on market or operational changes.

---

### **3. Incentive Structure**

- **Incentive Components:** Commission, performance bonus, special campaigns, etc.
- **Incentive Formula:** Clear formula for calculating payout (e.g., % of achieved revenue).
- **Achievement Bands/Tiers:**
    - 0–99% of target – no payout or reduced rate
    - 100–120% – standard payout
    - 121–150% – higher tier/accelerator rate
- **Cap or Ceiling Clause:** Whether there’s a maximum payout limit.
- **Illustrative Examples:** (optional but useful for clarity and transparency).

---

### **4. Payout Schedule & Conditions**

- **Frequency:** Monthly or quarterly incentive payments.
- **Verification:** Requirement for finance or management approval before payout.
- **Deferral/Clawback:** Conditions for delayed payment or recovery (client refund, cancellation, etc.).
- **Tax Deductions:** All incentives subject to applicable taxes (TDS, etc.).

---

### **5. Special Incentive Programs**

- **SPIFFs & Contests:** Temporary or campaign-specific bonuses.
- **Eligibility & Duration:** Who can participate and campaign timeframe.
- **Measurement Metrics:** Specific KPIs tied to special programs.
- **Approval & Communication:** All special programs require written communication from management.

---

### **6. Revenue Recognition Rules**

- **Recognition Point:** When the sale is considered complete (e.g., client payment received).
- **Credit Assignment:** Who gets credit for the deal (lead source, territory owner, etc.).
- **Shared Deals:** Split rules for multiple contributors.

---

### **7. Target Revisions & Exceptions**

- **Revision Triggers:** Market changes, product launches, territory reassignment, etc.
- **Authority to Revise:** Head of Sales / HR approval.
- **Documentation:** Written notice to employee required for any target changes.

---

### **8. Non-Compliance & Disqualification**

- **Misconduct Clause:** Incentives forfeited if found guilty of data manipulation or policy violation.
- **Resignation/Termination Rule:** Incentives payable only if employee is on payroll at payout date.
- **Probation Clause:** Special conditions if employee is under probation.

---

### **9. Acknowledgment & Acceptance**

- **Employee Declaration:** Confirmation of understanding and acceptance of targets & incentive terms.
- **Signatures:**
    - Employee
    - Reporting Manager
    - HR Representative
    - Effective Date of Agreement

---

### **10. Annexure References**

- **Linked Documents:** Reference to the main Sales Compensation & Incentive Policy.
- **Version Control:** Annexure version number and change log (date and nature of modification).
