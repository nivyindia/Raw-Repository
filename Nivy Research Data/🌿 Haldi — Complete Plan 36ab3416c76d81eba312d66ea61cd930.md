## 🛒 Saaman List

### Haldi Saaman

- [ ]  Haldi powder — 500g minimum
- [ ]  Ubtan mix: besan (250g) + haldi + gulabjal + malai / cream
- [ ]  Sarson tel / Nariyal tel
- [ ]  Kachi haldi (pieces)
- [ ]  Multani mitti (optional)

### Ceremony Saaman

- [ ]  Peeta (wooden stool / chauki)
- [ ]  Thali (for haldi)
- [ ]  Kalash (water)
- [ ]  Diya + ghee
- [ ]  Flowers / petals (yellow / orange)
- [ ]  Mango leaves
- [ ]  Naariyal
- [ ]  Chawal (akshat)

### Kapde

- [ ]  Dulhe ke liye **peele kapde** (kurta-pajama)
- [ ]  Dulhan ke liye **peeli saree / suit**
- [ ]  Old towels / chadars (2-3, jo kharaab ho sakti hain)

### After Haldi

- [ ]  Warm water nahane ke liye
- [ ]  Ubtan / scrub for cleaning
- [ ]  Fresh kapde ready

## 📜 Rasam — Step by Step SOP

1. **Dulha / Dulhan tayaar ho** — peele kapde pehne
2. **Peete par baithe** — wooden stool / chauki par
3. **Diya jalaye** — maa sabse pehle
4. **Maa haldi lagaye** — haath, chehre, pair
5. **Phir baaki parivar** — har koi thodi thodi haldi lagaye
6. **Phoolon ki varsha** — agar ho
7. **Photos** — Photographer alert! (yeh ek fun moment hai)
8. **Haldi sookne do** — 30-45 min tak nahaye nahi
9. **Warm water se nahaye**
10. **Fresh kapde pehno** — tayaari shuru!

> 💡 **Tip:** Dulha aur Dulhan ki haldi alag-alag ghar mein hoti hai — alag coordinator rakhein dono jagah.
