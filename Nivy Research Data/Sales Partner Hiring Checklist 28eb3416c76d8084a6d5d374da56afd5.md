# Sales Partner Hiring Checklist

**Stage 1: Partner Attraction / Initial Contact**

**Goal:** Provide info to attract and qualify potential partners.

**Checklist:**

- [ ]  Sales Outsourcing Proposal (PDF/Deck)
- [ ]  Company Brochure / One Pager
- [ ]  Company Presentation Deck (for video calls)
- [ ]  Market Overview / Opportunity Summary
- [ ]  Ideal Partner Criteria (skills, region, experience)
- [ ]  FAQ Sheet for initial queries
- [ ]  Contact Info for inquiries (email/phone/WhatsApp)

**Optional Add-ons:**

- Short welcome video / CEO message
- Case studies or client testimonials

---

## **Stage 2: Evaluation / Partner Application**

**Goal:** Assess interest and suitability.

**Checklist:**

- [ ]  Partner Application Form (Google Form or PDF)
- [ ]  NDA / Confidentiality Agreement (preliminary)
- [ ]  Clarification email on partnership model & commissions
- [ ]  Schedule discovery call or Zoom session

**Optional Add-ons:**

- Sample commission sheet or earning examples
- Overview of partner growth path (Bronze/Silver/Gold tiers)

---

## **Stage 3: Agreement / Onboarding Confirmation**

**Goal:** Formalize partnership.

**Checklist:**

- [ ]  Sales Outsourcing / Channel Partner Agreement (signed)
- [ ]  NDA (if separate)
- [ ]  Partner Authorization / Appointment Letter
- [ ]  Welcome Letter (personalized, sets tone for relationship)

**Optional Add-ons:**

- CEO or Founder welcome note
- Partner ID card / certificate (digital version)

---

## **Stage 4: Access & Tools Setup**

**Goal:** Give partner everything they need to start selling.

**Checklist:**

- [ ]  CRM / Lead Management Tool Access (HubSpot, Zoho, Google Sheets)
- [ ]  Email/Communication Channel Access (Slack / WhatsApp group / Telegram)
- [ ]  Shared Drive / Resource Hub (Google Drive / Notion)
- [ ]  Sales Funnel / Process Map
- [ ]  Reporting Sheet / Lead Tracker
- [ ]  Login Credentials to any internal tools
- [ ]  Training Schedule / Video Links
- [ ]  Product / Service Catalogue

**Optional Add-ons:**

- Pre-filled example of lead or deal entry
- Access to dashboards showing their targets and progress

---

## **Stage 5: Training / Enablement**

**Goal:** Ensure partners know the products, sales scripts, and processes.

**Checklist:**

- [ ]  Product Demos / Explainer Videos
- [ ]  Sales Scripts for calls, emails, WhatsApp
- [ ]  Objection Handling Document
- [ ]  Competitor Analysis Sheet
- [ ]  Customer Persona & Target Market Profiles
- [ ]  Live or recorded onboarding webinars
- [ ]  FAQ Handbook for selling

**Optional Add-ons:**

- Upsell / cross-sell guides
- Tips for handling enterprise vs SMB clients

---

## **Stage 6: Marketing Support**

**Goal:** Provide partners with ready-to-use marketing collateral.

**Checklist:**

- [ ]  Social Media Post Templates (Canva / PSD)
- [ ]  Ads Creatives (FB, IG, LinkedIn)
- [ ]  Video / Reels (pre-edited)
- [ ]  Email / WhatsApp Campaign Templates
- [ ]  Co-branding Guidelines
- [ ]  Partner Landing Page (if applicable)

**Optional Add-ons:**

- Event banners, brochures, or physical marketing kits
- Sample press release or campaign copy

---

## **Stage 7: Financial / Incentives Setup**

**Goal:** Make payouts and commission structures clear.

**Checklist:**

- [ ]  Commission & Incentive Sheet (with examples)
- [ ]  Payout Policy (schedule & method)
- [ ]  Sample Invoice Template
- [ ]  Partner Performance / Leaderboard Dashboard Access

**Optional Add-ons:**

- Referral bonus sheet
- Milestone-based rewards overview

---

## **Stage 8: Monitoring / Support / Engagement**

**Goal:** Ensure ongoing alignment, performance tracking, and relationship building.

**Checklist:**

- [ ]  Reporting / Deal Submission Schedule
- [ ]  Feedback Form / Issue Reporting Form
- [ ]  Regular Check-in Calls / Meetings
- [ ]  Partner Community Access (Slack / Telegram / Discord)
- [ ]  Monthly Partner Newsletter
- [ ]  Performance Review Reports
- [ ]  Recognition / Award Certificates
- [ ]  Leaderboard / Top Partner Updates

**Optional Add-ons:**

- Quarterly refresher training
- Partner growth & advancement opportunities

---

## **Stage 9: Optional Offline / Physical Kit**

**Checklist:**

- [ ]  Printed Partner Handbook
- [ ]  Product Catalogue (physical)
- [ ]  Partner Certificate / ID Card (physical)
- [ ]  Branded Merchandise (pen, notebook, mug, badge, etc.)
- [ ]  Welcome Letter (printed, signed)

---

## 💡 **Extra Tips / Items Often Missed**

- Ensure **all login credentials and access links are sent only after agreement signed**.
- Include **tracking sheet / checklist for each partner** — mark stages completed.
- Maintain a **shared “Partner Portal”** for all resources to avoid multiple emails.
- Include **contact list for support**: sales, operations, finance, marketing.
- Prepare **a one-page visual “Partner Success Map”** — shows steps from onboarding → earning → growth.
- Optional **gamification**: badges, leaderboards, bonus tiers to motivate partners.
