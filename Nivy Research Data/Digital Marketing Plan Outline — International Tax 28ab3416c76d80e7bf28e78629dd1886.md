### **PART 7: SALES FUNNEL & PROCESS**

**7.1. Lead-to-Client Funnel**

- **Awareness Stage (Top of Funnel):**
    - **Target:** Strangers who are experiencing the pain points.
    - **Content:** Blog posts, YouTube videos, social media content.
    - **Goal:** Attract and educate. Capture contact information.
- **Consideration Stage (Middle of Funnel):**
    - **Target:** Leads who have shown interest (e.g., downloaded a guide).
    - **Content:** Webinars, free audit offers, case studies, email nurture sequences.
    - **Goal:** Build trust and demonstrate expertise. Move them towards a conversation.
- **Decision Stage (Bottom of Funnel):**
    - **Target:** Qualified leads who are considering working with you.
    - **Content:** Free consultation offer, proposal, contract, detailed case studies.
    - **Goal:** Convert into a paying client.
- **Advocacy Stage (Post-Purchase):**
    - **Target:** Current clients.
    - **Content:** Onboarding process, regular reporting, check-ins.
    - **Goal:** Retain and turn into advocates (referrals, testimonials).

**7.2. Sales Process & Conversion**

- **Step 1: Lead Captures**
    - Offer a lead magnet (e.g., "The 5-Point Marketing Audit") in exchange for email.
    - Use website forms, landing pages, and social media ads.
- **Step 2: Nurture Sequence**
    - Automate an email sequence that delivers value and builds trust.
    - Include case studies, testimonials, and educational content.
- **Step 3: Qualification Call**
    - When a lead requests a consultation or shows high intent, schedule a call.
    - Use a pre-qualification questionnaire to ensure they fit the ICP.
- **Step 4: The "Partnership" Pitch (Sales Call)**
    - Focus on understanding their business, goals, and challenges.
    - Present your agency as a solution, emphasizing the guarantee and partnership model.
    - Use a structured presentation that includes:
        - Their current situation and goals.
        - Your proposed strategy (high-level).
        - The guarantee and how it works.
        - Pricing and next steps.
- **Step 5: Proposal & Contract**
    - Send a detailed proposal that reiterates the discussion.
    - Use a contract that clearly outlines the terms, including the guarantee conditions.
- **Step 6: Onboarding**
    - Once signed, kick off the onboarding process (see 7.3).

**7.3. Client Onboarding & Retention**

- **Onboarding Process:**
    - **Kick-off Meeting:** Deep-dive into their business (the "Business Growth Workshop").
    - **Setup:** Access to tools, accounts, and reporting.
    - **Strategy Presentation:** Detailed plan for the first 90 days.
    - **Regular Cadence:** Set up weekly or bi-weekly check-in calls.
- **Retention Strategies:**
    - **Transparent Reporting:** Monthly reports showing progress towards goals.
    - **Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs):** High-level meetings to review past performance and plan ahead.
    - **Proactive Communication:** Regularly update clients on campaign performance and industry changes.
    - **Value-Added Services:** Offer additional insights, training, or resources to help their business.
