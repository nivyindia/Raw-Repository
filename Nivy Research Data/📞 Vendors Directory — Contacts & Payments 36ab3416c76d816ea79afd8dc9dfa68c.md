## ✅ Vendor Confirmation SOP

1. **Pehle meeting** → Rate, service, terms samjhe
2. **Written agreement** → Amount, date, time, cancellation policy
3. **Advance payment** → Receipt lo
4. **1 hafte pehle call** → Confirm kare
5. **1 din pehle reminder** → Time aur venue confirm
6. **Day-of contact** → Vendor ka mobile coordinate mein rakhe
7. **Final payment** → Kaam ke baad, receipt lo
