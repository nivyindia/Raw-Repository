# MASTER FAQ – FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (ALL-IN-ONE)

This section answers **almost every question clients usually ask before purchasing social media services in the UAE**. It is written to remove confusion, build trust, and speed up sales decisions.

---

## GENERAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

**Q1. What exactly do you do in social media management?**

We plan content, design creatives, write captions, schedule posts, manage engagement, run ads (if included), track performance, and optimize for visibility and leads.

**Q2. Do you only post content or also help with leads and sales?**

We do both. Organic posting builds trust and visibility, while paid ads and funnels are used for lead generation and enquiries.

**Q3. Which platforms do you manage?**

Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok. Platform selection depends on the package and business type.

**Q4. Do you work with UAE-based businesses only?**

Our primary focus is UAE, but we also serve international brands targeting UAE audiences.

**Q5. Do you sign an NDA or service agreement?**

Yes. A simple service agreement/NDA can be signed for data and brand protection.

---

## CONTENT & CREATIVES

**Q6. Who creates the content?**

Our in-house creative and strategy team handles content planning, design, and copywriting.

**Q7. Will the content match my brand identity?**

Yes. Fonts, colors, tone, and messaging are aligned with your brand guidelines.

**Q8. Do you provide Arabic content?**

Arabic content is included in higher packages or available as a paid add-on.

**Q9. Can I approve posts before publishing?**

Yes. Content calendars are shared in advance for approval.

**Q10. How many revisions are allowed?**

Up to 2 revision rounds per creative are included.

---

## REELS, VIDEOS & SHOOTS

**Q11. Do you create reels and videos?**

Yes. Reels and short-form videos are included in Growth and higher packages.

**Q12. Do you handle video shoots?**

On-site shoots are not included by default but can be arranged at extra cost.

**Q13. Can clients provide their own videos?**

Yes, we can edit and optimize client-provided footage.

---

## PAID ADS & LEAD GENERATION

**Q14. Is ad spend included in the package price?**

No. Ad spend is paid directly by the client to Meta/Google.

**Q15. Do you guarantee leads or sales?**

No agency can legally guarantee results. We guarantee professional execution and optimization.

**Q16. How many leads will I get?**

Leads depend on budget, offer, industry, and market demand.

**Q17. Who owns the ad account?**

The ad account is owned by the client for full transparency.

**Q18. Do you run WhatsApp lead campaigns?**

Yes, WhatsApp-integrated lead funnels are supported.
