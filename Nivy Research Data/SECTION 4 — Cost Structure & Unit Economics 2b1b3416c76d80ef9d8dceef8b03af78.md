## **1. Cost Per Service (Ads, SEO, SMM, Websites, Funnels, etc.)**

Below are **typical delivery costs** for agencies using Indian Tier 2/3 freelancers, with **international pricing benchmarks**.

### **Facebook/Google Ads Management**

- Client charges:
    - US/UK: **$300–$1,000/month**
- Delivery cost:
    - Freelancer: **$100–$250/month**

### **SEO**

- Client charges: **$400–$1,500/month**
- Delivery cost: **$120–$300/month**

### **Social Media Management (SMM)**

- Client charges: **$300–$700/month**
- Delivery cost: **$80–$180/month**

### **Website Development**

- Client charges:
    - Landing page: **$300–$700**
    - Full website: **$1,500–$5,000**
- Delivery cost:
    - Landing page: **$80–$150**
    - Full website: **$300–$800**

### **Sales Funnels**

- Client charges: **$800–$2,500**
- Delivery cost: **$150–$350**

### **Email Marketing Setup + Monthly**

- Setup: **$300–$600**
- Monthly: **$250–$700**
- Delivery cost: **$40–$120**

### **Branding Packages**

- Client charges: **$350–$1,000**
- Delivery cost: **$40–$100**

## **3. Sales Commission Models**

Sales reps from Tier 2/3 countries are usually commission-only.

### **Standard Commission Model**

- **15%–25%** of first payment

### **Recurring Commission Model**

- 10% for monthly retainers
- Commission released only after client payment

### **Bonus Model**

- $50 bonus for every 3 closed clients
- $200 bonus for $10,000 revenue in a month

### **Why commission-only?**

→ Zero risk to agency

→ More scalable

→ Unlimited sales headcount

## **5. CAC (Client Acquisition Cost)**

CAC depends on the channel:

### **Cold Email + LinkedIn Outreach**

- Costs:
    - Tools: $50–$100
    - Data scraping tools: $20–$50
    - Email domains: $10
- CAC range: **$20–$80 per client**

### **Paid Ads**

- Ads spend: $500–$2,000/month
- CAC from ads: **$200–$600 per client**

### **Commission-only sales reps**

- CAC = their commission (15–25%)

### **Benchmarked International CAC**

- Good CAC: **$50–$150**
- Great CAC: **$20–$50**

---

## **6. Contribution Margin**

Contribution margin = Revenue – Direct Costs (freelancers + delivery)

### Example (SMM)

- Client pays: $500
- Freelancer cost: $100
- Tools allocation: $20
- Contribution margin: **$380 (76%)**

### Example (SEO)

- Client pays: $900
- Freelancer cost: $250
- Contribution margin: **$650 (72%)**

### Ideal Agency Contribution Margins

- **70%+** = Excellent
- **60%+** = Good
- **50%+** = Minimum acceptable

---

## **7. Average Revenue Per Client (ARPC)**

International agencies usually fall into these ranges:

| Client Type | Monthly Revenue |
| --- | --- |
| Low Ticket | $300–$500 |
| Mid Ticket | $600–$1,200 |
| High Ticket | $1,500–$5,000 |
| One-Time Projects | $300–$2,500 |

Your ideal ARPC target:

- **$700–$1,200 per client/month**

---

## **8. Time to Deliver vs Cost to Deliver**

### **SMM**

- 1–2 hours/day
- Freelancer: $80–$120/month → high margin

### **SEO**

- 8–15 hours/month
- Freelancer: $150–$300 → medium margin

### **Ads (Meta/Google)**

- 8–10 hours/month
- Freelancer: $150–$250 → high margin

### **Website Development**

- 1–4 weeks
- Freelancer: $200–$400
- One-time high profit

### **Funnels**

- 1–2 weeks
- Freelancer: $150–$300 → high margin

### **Insight**

Services like SMM and Ads are **high margin + recurring**, so they form the agency’s financial backbone.

---

## **9. Gross & Net Margin Per Service**

### **Gross Margin = Revenue – Direct Costs**

Usually:

- SMM: **70–85%**
- SEO: **60–75%**
- Ads: **65–80%**
- Websites: **60–85%**
- Funnels: **65–85%**

### **Net Margin (after tools + commissions + misc.)**

After deducting:

- Tools cost
- Commissions
- Operational costs
- Refunds (rare)

Net margins usually:

- **30–55%** for a well-run international agency
- **20–30%** in scale mode (more ads, more hiring)
