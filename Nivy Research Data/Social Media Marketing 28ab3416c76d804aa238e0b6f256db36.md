## 🧩 **2️⃣ Ideal Content Mix**

You should maintain a **70–30 ratio**:

**70% Value / Education / Proof** → Builds trust

**30% Promotion / Offers** → Converts leads

Here’s a proven content mix 👇

### 🏢 **A. Company Credibility Posts**

*(Builds trust and shows you’re established)*

- “Who We Are” / Company introduction post
- Meet the Founders / Team introduction
- Behind the scenes (office, culture, meetings)
- Certificates / Awards / Partnerships
- Milestone posts (100+ clients, 5 years, new office, etc.)
- Company values or mission statement

🧱 **Goal:** To show your company is **real, structured, and credible**.

---

### 💼 **B. Service & Offer Posts**

*(Show what you actually do — but in a smart, valuable way)*

- Carousel explaining your main services (e.g. Tax, Accounting, Digital Marketing)
- “Problems we solve for our clients”
- “How we work / Our process” post
- Comparison post: “In-house vs Outsourcing”
- Client case study or success story
- “Before & After” type examples

🎯 **Goal:** Make your expertise *visible and practical*.

---

### 📊 **C. Educational / Value Posts**

*(Shows you are an expert and not just a seller)*

- Industry tips or best practices
- “Top 5 mistakes businesses make in…”
- Infographics or explainers
- “Did you know?” series
- 60-second video tips or reels
- Tax deadlines, marketing trends, or compliance updates

🎓 **Goal:** Build authority and attract inbound clients.

---

### ❤️ **D. Social Proof & Testimonials**

*(This is your most powerful trust-builder)*

- Screenshot or designed testimonial
- Video testimonial
- “Client success story” in story/reel format
- Google review highlights

🌟 **Goal:** People trust people — social proof makes your brand feel *safe to work with.*

---

### 🌐 **E. Engagement / Brand Personality Posts**

*(Make your profile human, not robotic)*

- Festival greetings (Diwali, Christmas, New Year, etc.)
- “Meet the team” spotlight
- Company culture or team outings
- Polls, Q&A, or “Did you know?” posts
- Motivation / leadership quotes (but branded)

🧠 **Goal:** Build connection and warmth — your company becomes relatable.

---

### 🏆 **F. Authority / PR Content**

*(Advanced layer — shows thought leadership)*

- Founder’s post on LinkedIn about strategy, success, or mindset
- Collaboration announcements
- Webinars, events, or speaking engagements
- “We were featured in…” type post
- Press release highlights

📈 **Goal:** Position your company as an *industry leader*.

## 🎨 **6️⃣ Designing Your Posts**

You don’t need to hire a designer right away.

Use **Canva** — a free, beginner-friendly design tool.

### Steps in Canva:

1. Go to [https://canva.com](https://canva.com/)
2. Search **“Instagram Post”** or **“LinkedIn Post”** templates.
3. Choose a clean, modern template.
4. Replace with:
    - Your **brand colors**
    - **Logo**
    - **Text and images**
5. Download as `.png` file.

💡 Tip: Create 5–6 templates (for different post types) and reuse them every week — keeps everything consistent.

## 📊 **9️⃣ Tracking Performance**

Once you’ve posted for 3–4 weeks, track what works best.

| Metric | What It Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Reach / Impressions** | How many people saw your post |
| **Engagement (Likes, Comments)** | How interesting people found it |
| **Click / Website Visits** | If your post is converting viewers to potential clients |
| **Leads / Messages** | Direct results |

In Meta or LinkedIn Analytics, you’ll see which types of posts work best — focus on those.
