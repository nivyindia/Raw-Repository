# SECTION 15 — Accountability System

# 

## **1. Who Approves Payouts**

- CEO final approval for all payouts above a defined threshold
- Finance Manager approves routine payouts
- Department heads validate work completion before payout request moves to finance

## **2. Finance Roles (CFO-Lite System)**

- **Finance Manager:** oversight, budgeting, financial forecasting
- **Accounts Executive:** invoices, reconciliations, vendor payments
- **Payroll Coordinator:** freelancer + employee payouts
- **Financial Analyst:** monthly reports, dashboards, KPIs

## **3. Monthly Audit Checklist**

- Bank reconciliation
- Pending invoices check
- Freelancer payout audit
- Expense categorization review
- Profitability sheet update
- Cash flow projection update
- Compliance & documentation check

## **4. Sales Commission Approval Flow**

- Salesperson submits commission log
- CRM cross-verification by Sales Manager
- Finance validates payment received from client
- CEO approval if commission exceeds threshold
- Commission payout issued

## **5. Freelancer Payout Approval Flow**

- Project Manager verifies task completion + QA
- Timesheet / deliverables attached
- Finance verifies billing rate + hours or package
- CEO approves payouts above defined cap
- Payout processed via automated system

## **6. Financial Reporting Structure**

- **Weekly:** cash flow updates, outstanding client payments, project profitability snapshot
- **Monthly:** P&L statement, balance sheet, department performance report, cost analysis
- **Quarterly:** tax planning sheet, growth forecast, budget vs actual variance report
- **Yearly:** complete financial audit & strategic financial plan
