## Suggested Positioning

- Market as **“Outsourced U.S. Tax & Accounting Partner”** for SMBs, startups, and agencies.
- Offer a **Free 30-Minute Consultation** + **1-Month Free Trial (Bookkeeping)** to onboard clients easily.
- Use U.S.-friendly software (QuickBooks Online, Xero, Gusto, ADP, Avalara, etc.).
- Mention **“CPA Reviewed”** for trust — even if your team works under a CPA network partner.
