**Nivy's Position:** We occupy the unique, uncontested space as the **Integrated Growth Partner**. We are the single provider that manages both a company's growth engine and its compliance framework, ensuring they work in harmony.

- **Industries:** E-commerce, SaaS, Technology, Professional Services, and Manufacturing.
