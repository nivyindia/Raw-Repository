## 📌 Is page ka istemal kaise karein

1. Neecha di gayi table mein apni shadi ki date likhein
2. Har hafte 2-3 important kaam chunein
3. Kaam hone par ✅ lagayein
4. Jo nahi hua wo agle hafte carry forward karein
5. **Ek kaam ek vyakti ke zimme** — confusion nahi hoga

## 🔔 Daily Reminder Format

> Har shaam ye 3 sawaal poochho:
> 

> 1. **Aaj kya hua?** (Complete the checkboxes)
> 

> 2. **Kal kya karna hai?** (Next day ke 3 priority tasks)
> 

> 3. **Kuch miss ho raha hai?** (Other pages check karo)
>

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes — Jo Log Karte Hain (Aap Mat Karna)

- ❌ Cards bahut late print karna → 3 mahine pehle order do
- ❌ Caterer ko late headcount dena → 2 hafte pehle de do
- ❌ Photographer ko brief nahi karna → Shot list pehle do
- ❌ Cash ready nahi hona → 3 din pehle sab nikalo
- ❌ Bahar ke guests ka accommodation late arrange karna
- ❌ Puja saaman last minute khareedna → 1 hafte pehle ready karo
- ❌ Family ko duties assign nahi karna → 1 hafte pehle assign karo
- ❌ Emergency kit nahi rakhna → zaroor rakhein
- ❌ Generator backup na rakhna → bijli gaye toh?
- ❌ Vendors ki receipts nahi lena → HAMESHA lo
