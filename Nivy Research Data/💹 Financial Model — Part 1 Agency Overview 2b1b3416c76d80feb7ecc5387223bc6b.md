# **SECTION 1 — Agency Overview & Financial Model**

## **1. Business Model Explanation**

Your digital marketing agency operates on an **international client + distributed workforce model**, where:

- **Clients are from high-paying Western markets** (US/UK/CA/EU).
- **Service delivery is done through Indian freelancers**, hired on a per-project basis.
- **Sales teams from Tier 2/3 countries** work purely on commission, keeping fixed costs close to zero.
- The agency focuses on being **hyper-lean**, **scalable**, and **margin-optimized** by removing permanent overhead.

The revenue model combines:

- **Client project fees** (project-based or retainer-based)
- **Sales commission-based acquisition** (pay only when you get revenue)
- **Freelancer payout model** (pay only when a project is sold)

This creates a **zero fixed salary, pay-per-performance infrastructure** ideal for fast scaling.

Client acquisition is done through:

### **Inbound Channels**

- Email outreach
- LinkedIn outreach
- Content authority building (case studies, results, niche positioning)

### **Outbound Channels**

- SDRs (commission-based) messaging prospects
- Freelancers assisting with outreach (optional)
- Cold email automation using tools

### **Why Western markets?**

- Higher budgets (USD, GBP, EUR)
- Higher ROI expectations
- Better communication & transparency
- Faster payment clearance

Your financial planning relies on **USD/EUR inflows**, which increases your agency margins due to INR-based expenses.

## **3. Commission-Based Sales Team Structure**

Sales staff from tier2/3 countries are onboarded internationally using:

- **Pure commission model** (0 fixed cost)
- **15% – 25% commission on revenue**
- **Monthly bonuses** for hitting client acquisition targets
- **Residual commissions** on recurring client retainers (optional)

Benefits:

- Unlimited scalability → add more SDRs anytime
- Salespeople are motivated because they earn immediately
- No risk to the agency, as payouts happen **only after client payment**
