## **Execution Tips**

1. **Phase 1 (Week 1–3):** Brochure, PPT, explainer video — focus on **first impression materials**.
2. **Phase 2 (Week 2–4):** Full proposal, one-pager, infographics — detailed follow-up materials.
3. **Phase 3 (Week 3–6):** Training, onboarding kits, CRM guides — for partners who are committed.
4. **Phase 4 (Ongoing):** Case studies, testimonials, webinars, interactive tools — support ongoing recruitment and partner engagement.

---

💡 **Key Tip:**

- Keep all formats **consistent in branding, messaging, and tone**.
- Always start with **visual, high-impact materials** for initial outreach — detailed docs come later.
- Use **checklists and templates** to save time for both HQ and partners.
