# 1. What Documentation Needed in starting

Documentation includes:

- SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures) for every department
- Workflows for client onboarding, accounting, HR, marketing, sales
- Templates, reports, checklists

This is important **for consistency, scaling, and training**, but it **doesn’t need to be complete at day one**.

---

## **2. What to Document Before Starting**

Focus on the **core activities that affect your business immediately**:

1. **Client acquisition & delivery**
    - How you sign clients
    - How you onboard them
    - How you deliver your service (accounting reports, tax filings, consultations)
2. **Finance basics**
    - How to issue invoices
    - Track payments
    - Record expenses
3. **Legal / compliance**
    - Contracts & NDAs
    - Tax compliance rules
    - Company registration documents
4. **Basic marketing**
    - Lead capture (website forms, email sequences)
    - Social media branding templates

---

## **3. What Can Wait**

You can document advanced processes **after you start and get clients**:

- Advanced HR & payroll documentation
- Detailed multi-level department SOPs (finance team hierarchy, marketing analytics workflows)
- Automation sequences for SaaS scaling
- Global expansion process documentation

The idea: **start delivering and earning**, then refine and formalize processes as your team grows.

---

## **4. Recommended Approach**

- **Start with a “living documentation” mindset**:
    - Create simple docs/templates for critical tasks
    - Update them weekly as you discover better ways
- **Keep it flexible**: Early on, processes will evolve quickly
- **Prioritize by impact**: Focus on what affects client satisfaction and cash flow first
