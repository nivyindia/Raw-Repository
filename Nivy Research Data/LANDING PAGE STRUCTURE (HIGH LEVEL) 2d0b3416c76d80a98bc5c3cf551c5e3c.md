# 🎨 DESIGN RULES (VERY IMPORTANT)

### Colors

- Dark blue / charcoal / black base
- White space heavy
- One accent color max (gold / muted green)

### Fonts

- Headings: clean serif or strong sans
- Body: simple, readable sans

### Layout

- No long paragraphs
- Plenty of spacing
- Calm, confident pacing

### Avoid

- Testimonials (initially)
- Counters (“500 members”)
- Marketing hype words

---

# 🧠 FINAL POSITIONING LINE (USE IN META / FOOTER)

> Nivy Alliance is a private reciprocal business ecosystem where access is earned by sharing real demand — not by selling services.
