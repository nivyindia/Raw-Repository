---

## **1. Define Your Goals**

Before sending a single email, know what you want:

- Generate leads for your SaaS product
- Nurture existing leads (educate, keep engaged)
- Promote services (consulting, software solutions)
- Build brand authority

**Tip:** Pick 1–2 primary goals first, don’t try everything at once.

---

## **2. Build Your Email List**

You need **people who actually want your emails**. Methods:

- **Website sign-ups:** Newsletter, free resource (eBook, checklist, report)
- **Lead magnets:** Free tools, templates, calculators related to your software
- **Events/webinars:** Collect emails from participants
- **Existing contacts:** Clients, partners, subscribers (with permission)

**Do not buy lists** — for SaaS/IT B2B, quality matters more than quantity.

---

## **3. Choose an Email Marketing Platform**

Some options:

- **HubSpot** (CRM + automation, good for B2B SaaS)
- **Mailchimp** (easy & popular for newsletters)
- **ActiveCampaign** (automation & segmentation)
- **Sendinblue** (affordable, good for international outreach)

**Tip:** Start with free plans if budget is low, upgrade as your list grows.

---

## **4. Segment Your Audience**

Not all emails are for everyone. Segmentation improves results:

- By industry (finance, healthcare, retail, etc.)
- By role (CEO, CTO, accountant)
- By stage (new lead, engaged, existing client)
- By geography (important for international SaaS)

---

## **5. Plan Your Email Content**

Types of emails to send:

1. **Welcome email** – Introduce your company, give a lead magnet
2. **Educational / value emails** – Tips, industry trends, how-to guides
3. **Promotional emails** – Software features, offers, demos
4. **Case studies / social proof** – Show client success stories
5. **News / updates** – Product launches, blogs, events

**Tip:** Follow the 80/20 rule — 80% value, 20% promotion.

---

## **6. Design Your Emails**

- Keep branding consistent (colors, logo, font)
- Short subject lines, clear CTA (Call-to-Action)
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Visuals: screenshots, icons, charts for SaaS features

---

## **7. Automation & Funnels**

Set up **automated email sequences** for:

- **Lead nurturing**: When someone signs up, send 3–5 emails over 1–2 weeks
- **Trial users**: Guide them through product onboarding
- **Re-engagement**: Remind inactive users with tips/offers

Automation saves time and keeps leads warm.

---

## **8. Track Metrics**

Track key metrics to measure success:

- Open rate (are they reading?)
- Click-through rate (are they clicking links?)
- Conversion rate (are they signing up / buying?)
- Bounce & unsubscribe rate (clean your list regularly)

---

## **9. Optimize & Scale**

- Test subject lines (A/B testing)
- Refine timing and frequency
- Personalize content using name, company, role
- Segment more deeply as your list grows
