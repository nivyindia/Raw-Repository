# **SECTION 2 — Cash Flow Clarity System**

## **1. Monthly Revenue Tracking System**

Create a structured system to track every dollar earned:

- Maintain **Monthly Revenue Sheet** (Google Sheets / Odoo / Wave / Zoho Books).
- Log each client with:
    - Client name
    - Country
    - Service type
    - Invoice amount
    - Currency (USD/EUR/GBP)
    - Payment method (Stripe, PayPal, Wise, Payoneer)
    - Invoice date
    - Payment date
    - Recurring or one-time
- Track expected revenue vs received revenue.
- Maintain an **Aging Report** to monitor delayed payments (7/15/30/60 days).

Purpose:

→ Gives you visibility on how much money is coming this month and next month.

---

## **2. Multi-Currency Income Flow (USD/EUR → INR)**

Plan and track all income from international clients:

### **Currency Inflow**

- Collect client payments in **USD, GBP, EUR, CAD**, etc.
- Use **Stripe, PayPal, Wise Business, Payoneer** to receive funds.
- Maintain a **ledger** showing:
    - Gross amount received
    - Gateway fee
    - Conversion rate
    - Final INR received

### **Monthly Conversion Strategy**

- Convert money in **batches**, not one-by-one
- Convert when USD-INR or EUR-INR is high
- Keep 20–30% in USD/EUR for tool payments

Purpose:

→ Protects margins by optimizing conversion rates.

## **4. Freelancer Payouts**

Create a transparent freelancer payment workflow:

- Pay freelancers **per-project or hourly**.
- Maintain structured payout slabs for each service:
    - Social media management
    - Google Ads
    - Facebook Ads
    - SEO
    - Email Marketing
    - Website development
- Track:
    - Project name
    - Assigned freelancer
    - Total payout amount
    - Due date
    - Payment date

### **Payout Cycles**

- Weekly payouts (for fast delivery teams)
- Bi-weekly payouts
- Monthly consolidated payouts

Purpose:

→ You only pay freelancers after collecting client money → zero cashflow risk.

## **5. Sales Commission Structure**

Transparent, scalable commission system:

### **Commission Model**

- **15% – 25%** on project revenue
- **Recurring commissions** for retainer clients
- **Tiered bonuses**:
    - 3 clients/month → +$100 bonus
    - 5 clients/month → +$250 bonus
    - 10 clients/month → +$700 bonus

### **Tracking**

Maintain commission sheets with:

- Salesperson name
- Source (LinkedIn/Email/Referral)
- Deal value
- Commission %
- Commission payout date
- Pending commissions

Purpose:

→ Motivates sales teams and helps predict payouts vs revenue.

## **7. Profit Reserve**

Create a **Profit Safety Reserve**:

### **Profit Distribution Rule**

Adopt a simple safe profit model:

- **50% Operating expenses**
- **30% Profit reserve**
- **20% Founder withdrawal**

Or use **Profit First Method**:

- Income → Allocate to 4 accounts:
    - Profit
    - Tax
    - Owner’s Pay
    - Operating Expenses

### **Reserve Target**

Build a minimum:

- **3 months operating reserve**
- **6 months advanced reserve** for scaling

Purpose:

→ Ensures you survive slow months and scale without stress.
