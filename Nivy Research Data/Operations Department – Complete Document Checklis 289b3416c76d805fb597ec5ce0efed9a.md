# Operations Department – Complete Document Checklist

# 

### **1. Strategy & Planning**

1. Operations Strategy / Plan
2. Department Goals & KPIs (efficiency, turnaround time, client satisfaction)
3. Resource & Capacity Planning Document
4. Budget Allocation & Expense Tracking Guidelines

---

### **2. Processes & SOPs**

1. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for Digital Marketing Services
    - Campaign creation, content publishing, ads management, reporting
2. SOPs for Accounting & Tax Services
    - Bookkeeping, reconciliations, tax filings, payroll, invoicing
3. Client Onboarding Workflow
4. Client Offboarding / Project Closure SOPs
5. Task Assignment & Workflow Guidelines
6. Quality Assurance / Review SOPs
7. Escalation & Issue Resolution Protocols

---

### **3. Tools & Systems**

1. CRM / Project Management SOPs
2. Accounting Software Guidelines (QuickBooks, Zoho Books, etc.)
3. Marketing Tools SOPs (Analytics, Ads Platforms, Email Automation)
4. Shared Drive / File Management Guidelines

---

### **4. Reporting & Monitoring**

1. Daily / Weekly / Monthly Operations Dashboard Templates
2. Client Status & Progress Reporting Templates
3. Resource Utilization & Productivity Reports
4. SLA Tracking & Compliance Reports

---

### **5. Training & Knowledge Base**

1. Employee Onboarding Checklist
2. Training Manuals for Marketing Services
3. Training Manuals for Accounting & Tax Services
4. Internal Knowledge Base / FAQs
5. Process Improvement Logs & Lessons Learned

---

### **6. Legal & Compliance**

1. Internal Compliance Guidelines (tax rules, accounting standards, GDPR, data protection)
2. Client Contract Execution & Documentation SOPs
3. Confidentiality & Data Security Guidelines

---

### **7. Templates & Documents**

1. Standard Client Reports Templates
2. Templates for Invoices, Receipts, Financial Statements
3. Campaign Reporting Templates
4. Checklists for Recurring Tasks (monthly bookkeeping, campaign audits, etc.)

---

### **8. Optional / Advanced**

1. Vendor / Freelancer Management SOPs
2. Automation & Integration Guidelines
3. Client Feedback / Satisfaction Collection SOP
4. Risk Management / Contingency Plan
