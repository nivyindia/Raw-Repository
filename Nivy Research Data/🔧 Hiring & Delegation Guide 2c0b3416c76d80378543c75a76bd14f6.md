# **✅ Notes on Execution**

1. **Start with Step 1 (VA Intern)**: This reduces 70% of your operational load.
2. **Hire HR / Hiring Assistant next**: Helps scale the hiring process for all positions.
3. **Leadership (Mini-COO, Project Delivery Lead, Marketing Head, Sales Head)** comes next — you only interview and approve these.
4. **Then hire functional roles** (social media, content, sales, delivery) under their respective department heads.
5. **Community & Program Coordinator** are last — they maintain ongoing operations and freelancer ecosystem.
6. **All hires are freelancers initially** — low cost, flexible, scalable.
7. **VA and HR build systems** while you take strategic interviews and assign department heads.

---

# **💡 How This Makes Company Autonomous**

- VA handles all operational/admin tasks.
- HR manages hiring & onboarding continuously.
- Department heads manage their teams independently.
- Mini-COO coordinates departments & ensures KPI tracking.
- Marketing & Sales generate leads and revenue.
- Delivery team executes client projects.
- Program Coordinator & Community Manager maintain freelancer ecosystem.

At this stage, **the company can run without your daily involvement**, and revenue starts coming in as marketing, sales, and delivery run smoothly.
