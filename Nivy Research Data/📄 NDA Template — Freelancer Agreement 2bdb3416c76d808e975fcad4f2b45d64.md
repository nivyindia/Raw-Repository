This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("NDA") is made part of the Freelancer Agreement between Billion Dreams United (operating under the trademark ‘Nivy’) and

**[Freelancer's Full Legal Name/Business Name],** an independent contractor, with an address at [Freelancer's Full Address] ("Contractor").

**Clause 1: Definition of Confidential Information**
1.1 “Confidential Information” means all non-public information, in any form, disclosed by Billion Dreams United / Nivy to the Freelancer.
1.2 This includes, but is not limited to:
    (a) Business and client data (e.g., sales figures, financials, strategies, campaign metrics);
    (b) Operational materials (e.g., SOPs, frameworks, scripts, automations, systems);
    (c) Access credentials and digital assets (e.g., logins, dashboards, tools, templates, datasets);
    (d) Any information concerning clients, partners, employees, pricing, or proprietary methodologies.
1.3 Any information marked as confidential, or which a reasonable person would treat as confidential given the nature of the information or circumstances of disclosure, is included.
1.4 All derivatives, summaries, or analyses created from such information are also considered Confidential Information.

**Clause 2: Permitted Use of Confidential Information**
2.1 The Confidential Information is disclosed solely for the purpose of enabling the Freelancer to perform their assigned tasks and services for Billion Dreams United / Nivy.
2.2 The Freelancer shall use the Confidential Information exclusively for this authorized purpose and for no other personal or professional use, unless prior written consent is obtained.

**Clause 3: Obligations of Confidentiality**
3.1 The Freelancer shall maintain the strict confidentiality of all Confidential Information.
3.2 The Freelancer shall not disclose, reveal, or make available any Confidential Information to any third party, including but not limited to family, friends, other clients, competitors, or freelancers.
3.3 The Freelancer shall not share, publish, post, or upload any Confidential Information on any public or private platform, including social media, websites, cloud storage, or messaging applications, without express prior authorization.
3.4 The Freelancer may only copy, reproduce, or store Confidential Information to the extent necessary for the performance of their assigned work.
3.5 The Freelancer shall implement reasonable security measures to prevent unauthorized access, including securing devices and safeguarding any provided login credentials.
3.6 The Freelancer must immediately notify Billion Dreams United / Nivy of any suspected or actual unauthorized access, disclosure, or breach of confidentiality.

**Clause 4: Prohibition on Unauthorized Use**
4.1 The Freelancer shall use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing their assigned tasks and services for Billion Dreams United / Nivy.
4.2 The Freelancer shall not, directly or indirectly:
    (a) Use Confidential Information for personal or financial gain, or for any competitive advantage;
    (b) Use Confidential Information to create, develop, or offer similar services, strategies, systems, or solutions for any other party;
    (c) Repurpose, replicate, or reuse the Agency’s internal frameworks, scripts, creative content, copywriting, or processes for other clients or personal projects;
    (d) Reverse-engineer, modify, or adapt any of the Agency’s systems, processes, or intellectual property for external use;
    (e) Exploit any information related to the Agency’s clients for personal benefit, parallel work, or future business opportunities.
4.3 These non-use obligations shall continue to apply even after the termination of the Freelancer’s engagement with the Agency.

**Clause 5: Exclusions from Confidential Information**
5.1 The obligations under this NDA shall not apply to information that:
    (a) Is or becomes publicly available through no fault or breach by the Freelancer;
    (b) Was already lawfully in the Freelancer’s possession prior to disclosure by the Agency, as evidenced by written records;
    (c) Is lawfully received from a third party who is not under any obligation of confidentiality to the Agency;
    (d) Is independently developed by the Freelancer without reference to or use of the Agency’s Confidential Information, as supported by written documentation.
5.2 If the Freelancer is legally compelled to disclose any Confidential Information by law, regulation, or court order, they shall:
    (a) Promptly notify the Agency in writing prior to disclosure; and
    (b) Provide reasonable cooperation to the Agency in seeking a protective order or other remedy to limit the disclosure.

**Clause 6: Term and Survival of Confidentiality Obligations**
6.1 The Freelancer’s confidentiality obligations under this NDA shall commence from the first point of disclosure of any information, including any informal sharing prior to the signing of this agreement.
6.2 The obligations of confidentiality and non-use shall remain in full effect:
    (a) Throughout the entire duration of the Freelancer’s engagement with the Agency; and
    (b) For a period of five (5) years following the termination of such engagement; **or**
    (c) For as long as the information remains confidential, **whichever period is longer**.
6.3 Notwithstanding the above, obligations pertaining to the following categories of information shall survive **indefinitely**:
    (a) Intellectual Property;
    (b) Work-Product created for the Agency;
    (c) Internal Systems and architecture;
    (d) Proprietary Business Processes;
    (e) Client Information and personal data.

    (d) Are approached for work that competes with the Agency’s services;

14.3 The Freelancer shall not use the Agency’s time, tools, data, strategies, client information, or resources for personal projects, other clients, side businesses, or external agencies.
14.4 The Freelancer shall maintain professional conduct, avoid any action that may harm the Agency’s reputation, and adhere to all provided ethical guidelines.

**Clause 17: Device and Infrastructure Security**
17.1 To ensure the secure handling of information, the Freelancer shall maintain a secure working environment, including:
    (a) Using a dedicated, updated computing device (laptop or desktop) with:
        (i) Up-to-date antivirus and anti-malware software;
        (ii) Firewall enabled;
        (iii) The latest operating system and security updates installed;
        (iv) Strong password or biometric protection.
    (b) Avoiding the use of public Wi-Fi networks for Agency work. If necessary, a reputable Virtual Private Network (VPN) must be used.
    (c) Not using shared or public computers (e.g., cyber cafés) for any Agency-related tasks.
    (d) Implementing robust security measures, including Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on all relevant accounts and using strong, unique passwords.
    (e) Ensuring that no unauthorized screen recording, sharing, or monitoring software is active during Agency work.
    (f) Keeping all Agency data logically and physically segregated from personal data and accounts.
17.2 The Agency reserves the right to request reasonable proof of compliance with these security requirements.

    (e) Exposure of login credentials or passwords;
    (f) Any suspicious activity related to Agency accounts or platforms.
18.2 The Freelancer must cooperate fully with the Agency in investigating, mitigating, and remedying any breach and in implementing measures to prevent recurrence.
18.3 Failure to report a security breach promptly shall be treated as negligent misconduct and a breach of this Agreement.
