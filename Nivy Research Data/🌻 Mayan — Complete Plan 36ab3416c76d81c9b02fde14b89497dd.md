## 📜 Rasam — Step by Step SOP

1. **Subah muhurat mein shuru hoti hai** — pandit ji ya bade ka ashirwad
2. **Dulha / dulhan ek chowki par baith jaate hain** — purane kapdon mein
3. **Puja thali layi jaati hai** — diya, dhaaga, phool ke saath
4. **Pehli ubtan** — mama / maasi ya badi bhabhi lagate hain (pahle right hand)
5. **Baaki mahilayein baari-baari se** ubtan lagate hain
6. **Geet gaaye jaate hain** — shaadi ke geet, lok geet
7. **Yeh silsila 4–5 din chalta hai** — roz ek baar
8. **Antim din** — dulha / dulhan ko snaan karaya jaata hai
9. **Prasad aur mithai** — sab ko diya jaata hai
10. **Nazar utaari** — kuch ghar bujahe karte hain nazar utaarne ki rasam
