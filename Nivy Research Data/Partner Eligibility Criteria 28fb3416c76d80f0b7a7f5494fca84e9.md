# Partner Eligibility Criteria

# **MINIMUM ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS FOR MASTER SALES AGENCY PARTNER**

## **🏢 COMPANY & LEGAL REQUIREMENTS**

### **1.1 Business Registration & Legal Status**

- **Minimum Age:** Company registered for at least 2 years
- **Legal Structure:** Properly registered entity (LLC, Pvt Ltd, Corp, etc.)
- **Business License:** Valid business license in home country
- **Tax Compliance:** Clean tax record with no major violations
- **Legal History:** No history of fraud, IP theft, or major legal disputes

### **1.2 Financial Stability**

- **Minimum Annual Revenue:** $250,000 USD (verified)
- **Profitability:** Profitable for at least 1 year
- **Bank References:** Positive banking relationship with clean history
- **Credit Score:** Minimum 650 business credit score (or equivalent)
- **No Bankruptcy:** No bankruptcy filings in past 5 years

---

## **👥 TEAM & ORGANIZATIONAL REQUIREMENTS**

### **2.1 Core Team Structure**

**Minimum Team Size:** 15 full-time employees
**Required Roles:**

- **Sales Director/Head:** 1 (5+ years sales leadership)
- **Sales Managers:** 2 (3+ years team management)
- **Senior Sales Executives:** 5 (2+ years B2B sales)
- **Sales Support/Operations:** 3
- **Admin/HR:** 2
- **IT/Technical Support:** 2

### **2.2 Key Personnel Qualifications**

**Sales Director Must Have:**

- 8+ years in sales leadership
- Experience managing teams of 10+ people
- Proven track record in international sales
- Degree in Business/Marketing or equivalent

**Sales Team Must Have:**

- 70% of sales team with 3+ years experience
- English fluency (written and spoken)
- CRM system proficiency
- Digital sales tools experience

---

## **💼 SALES EXPERIENCE & TRACK RECORD**

### **3.1 Sales Performance History**

**Revenue Track Record:**

- **Minimum:** $1M+ in annual sales (verified)
- **Growth:** Consistent YoY growth (minimum 15%)
- **Client Retention:** 80%+ client retention rate
- **Deal Size:** Average deal size $10,000+

**Industry Experience:**

- **Required:** 3+ years in IT/Digital Marketing/Professional Services
- **Preferred:** Experience in multiple service categories
- **Vertical Expertise:** At least 2 industry verticals

### **3.2 Client Portfolio**

**Current Client Base:**

- **Minimum:** 25 active clients
- **Enterprise Clients:** At least 3 clients with $50,000+ annual spend
- **International Clients:** Experience serving multiple countries
- **Reference Clients:** 5+ client references willing to provide testimonials

---

## **🛠 TECHNOLOGY & INFRASTRUCTURE**

### **4.1 Technical Capabilities**

**Required Systems:**

- **CRM:** Salesforce, HubSpot, or equivalent enterprise CRM
- **Communication:** Professional email, video conferencing, project management tools
- **Security:** Basic cybersecurity measures, antivirus, firewalls
- **Backup:** Regular data backup systems

**Technical Skills:**

- **AI Tools:** Proficiency in ChatGPT, AI presentation tools
- **ERP Systems:** Experience with enterprise resource planning
- **Digital Marketing Tools:** SEO, social media, analytics platforms
- **Collaboration Tools:** Slack, Teams, or equivalent

### **4.2 Office & Operations**

**Physical Infrastructure:**

- **Office Space:** Dedicated professional office (minimum 1,500 sq ft)
- **Meeting Facilities:** Conference rooms for client meetings
- **Technology:** Reliable internet, phone systems, computing equipment
- **Backup Power:** UPS/generator for business continuity

## **📋 APPLICATION & VETTING PROCESS**

### **10.1 Documentation Requirements**

**Mandatory Submission Documents:**

1. **Company Registration Certificates** (all jurisdictions)
2. **3 Years Financial Statements** (audited preferred)
3. **Client Portfolio** with revenue details
4. **Team Organizational Chart** with resumes
5. **Client References** (minimum 5)
6. **Business Insurance Certificates**
7. **Tax Compliance Certificates**
8. **Office Lease/Proof of Operations**
9. **Technology Stack Documentation**
10. **Case Studies** (3+ successful client engagements)

### **10.2 Evaluation Process**

**Phase 1: Document Review** (7-10 days)

- Complete application package review
- Financial viability assessment
- Team capability evaluation

**Phase 2: Management Interviews** (3-5 days)

- Founder/CEO interview
- Sales leadership interviews
- Operations team assessment

**Phase 3: Client Reference Checks** (5-7 days)

- Client satisfaction verification
- Service quality assessment
- Business practices validation

**Phase 4: Final Approval** (3-5 days)

- Committee review and decision
- Contract negotiation
- Onboarding planning

---

## **🚨 AUTOMATIC DISQUALIFICATION CRITERIA**

### **Immediate Rejection Conditions:**

- ❌ Less than 2 years in business
- ❌ Annual revenue below $250,000
- ❌ Team size below 15 people
- ❌ No international sales experience
- ❌ History of legal/ethical violations
- ❌ Insufficient financial reserves
- ❌ No professional office space
- ❌ Unable to provide client references
- ❌ Poor credit or financial history
- ❌ No existing technology infrastructure

---

## **💡 RECOMMENDED PREPARATION**

### **Before Applying:**

1. **Financial Preparation:**
    - Ensure clean financial records

2. **Team Development:**
    - Hire key leadership positions
    - Document team structure and roles
    - Implement training programs
3. **Infrastructure Investment:**
    - Upgrade technology systems
    - Secure professional office space
    - Implement CRM and sales tools
4. **Client Relationships:**
    - Secure client testimonials
    - Document case studies
    - Improve client satisfaction scores
