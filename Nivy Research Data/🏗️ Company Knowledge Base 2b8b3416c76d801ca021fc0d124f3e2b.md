# 🧩 **The 5 Layers of Company Systems**

## **Layer 1 — Major Systems (Core Systems)**

Foundational systems required for running the business.

## **Layer 2 — Minor Systems (Support Systems)**

Systems that improve efficiency, speed, quality, and organization.

## **Layer 3 — Micro-Systems (Tiny Optimization Systems)**

- Naming conventions
- Labeling conventions
- Folder color coding
- Quick-start guides
- 2-minute SOPs
- Shortcut systems
- Status tagging systems
- Approval tags
- Micro-productivity rules

## **Layer 4 — Behavioral Systems (Human & Cultural Systems)**

- Communication habits
- Leadership routines
- Accountability norms
- Conflict resolution style
- Feedback culture
- Decision-making behavior
- Transparency habits
- Trust-building behavior
- Information sharing style

## **Layer 5 — Evolution Systems (Adaptability & Growth Systems)**

- Quarterly reinvention system
- New tool adoption mechanism
- Market trend scanning
- Competitor disruption monitoring
- New service launch framework
- Client needs evolution analysis
- Innovation culture processes
- Fail-fast review cycles
- Strategic pivot framework
