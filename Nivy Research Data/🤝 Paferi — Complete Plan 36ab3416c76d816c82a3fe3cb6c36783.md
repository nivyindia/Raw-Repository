## 📜 Rasam — Step by Step SOP

1. **Dulha-dulhan taiyaar hote hain** — naye kapdon mein
2. **Mandir / kul devta ke paas jaate hain** — pehle darshan aur ashirwad
3. **Dulhan ka ghar mein pravesh** — aarti utaari jaati hai, paaon rakhna (rice / kumkum se)
4. **Bade log ashirwad dete hain** — saas, sasur, nana-nani, dada-dadi
5. **Gifts exchange** — dulhan ko nayi kapde, jewellery di jaati hai
6. **Puja** — pandit ji ke saath ghar mein chhoti puja
7. **Relatives milte hain** — naye parivar ke log dulhan se milte hain
8. **Khana** — sab milkar khate hain
9. **Photos** — naye parivaar ke saath group photos
10. **Bida** — dulhan ke maike wale waapis jaate hain (agar aaye the)
