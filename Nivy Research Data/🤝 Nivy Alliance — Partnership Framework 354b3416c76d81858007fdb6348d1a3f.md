## 🏆 Partner Tier Structure

| Tier | Name | Criteria | Benefits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🟢 Tier 1 | Strategic Alliance | 5+ referrals/quarter OR 100k+ deal value | Revenue share 15%, co-branding, priority support |
| 🟡 Tier 2 | Growth Partner | 2–4 referrals/quarter OR 30–100k deal value | Revenue share 10%, co-marketing |
| ⚪ Tier 3 | Referral Partner | 1+ referral/quarter | Commission 7.5% |

## 📋 Partner Onboarding Process

**Step 1 — Identification**

Identify potential partner via LinkedIn, events, warm intro, or outreach.

**Step 2 — Discovery Call** *(30 min)*

Understand their business, client base, and how Nivy can add value. Use the [ICP framework](https://www.notion.so/2d2b3416c76d80c78159f3294779ff58).

**Step 3 — Proposal**

Share the Nivy Alliance Partner Pack (to be created). Agree on tier and terms.

**Step 4 — Agreement**

Sign the Partner Agreement (template to be added to templates_database).

**Step 5 — Onboarding**

Send [Alliance Email Onboarding Sequence](https://www.notion.so/2d2b3416c76d8063aee6caeac664649d). Add partner to clients_database with Brand = Nivy Alliance.

**Step 6 — Quarterly Review**

Review partner performance quarterly. Upgrade/downgrade tier as appropriate.
