# **1. Website (MANDATORY)**

**Your website MUST have these pages:**

| Page | What to add | Minimum content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | What you do, who you serve, why you’re different | Hero headline + 3 services + trust logos + CTA |
| About | Team, story, mission | 1 founder photo + 1 paragraph |
| Services | Digital marketing + accounting pages | Min. 200 words per service |
| Portfolio/Case Studies | Past work (even if small) | 3 sample case studies (can be simulated or internal project cases) |
| Testimonials | Social proof | Minimum 5 reviews |
| Contact | Simple form + WhatsApp + email | Instant trust |

### **Absolute Minimum Social Proof for Website**

- **5–10 testimonials** (can be from India + international mix)
- **3 sample case studies** (Reels ad growth, website redesign, lead gen campaign, GST assistance case, etc.)

# **2. Google Business Profile (MANDATORY)**

This is the **#1 trust signal** when someone searches your agency.

**Minimum Required:**

- **10 high-quality reviews**
- **5 photos** (office, laptop workspace, team, certificates, screenshots)
- Business timings + services + WhatsApp link
- 1–2 monthly posts

# **3. LinkedIn Company Page (MANDATORY FOR INTERNATIONAL CLIENTS)**

### **Minimum required content**

| Content Type | Minimum Qty |
| --- | --- |
| Company posts | 10 posts |
| Case studies | 3 |
| Testimonials | 5 |
| Team introduction | 1 |
| Service breakdown carousel | 2 |
| Stat/value posts | 4 |

### **Minimum metrics to look professional**

- **300–1000 followers**
- **50–200 likes average across the profile total** (not per post)
- **5–10 comments total across all posts**

*(No need for viral numbers — clients only check if you're active and real.)*
