# Quick notes to follow after Day 14 (next steps — ongoing)

- **Days 15–21:** Run interviews, give skill tests, finalize hires. (Onboard first cohort using the Onboarding Checklist.)
- **Week 4:** Mentors assign first internal contribution tasks (unpaid); evaluate performance after 2 weeks.
- **End of Month 1 (Day 30):** Decide project allocation for proven performers and start milestone payments.
- **Month 2–3:** Promote top performers to small leadership roles; begin delegating recruitment & onboarding to your VA/HR hire.
