# ⚙️ **Example: Sales Department Experimentation Phase (Automated + Outsourced)**

## 🧩 **Objective**

Test karna hai —

> “Kya hum commission-based freelancers se 10 clients la sakte hain Tax & Accounting service ke liye 15 din me?”
> 

---

## **Phase 1: Define & Structure**

| Element | Example |
| --- | --- |
| 🎯 Goal | 10 qualified clients acquire karna (15 days) |
| 📊 Success Metric | Conversion rate ≥ 20%, Avg client value ₹5,000+ |
| 🧾 SOP | Step-by-step sales process likha hua (lead → pitch → close → onboarding) |
| 📋 Templates | Cold message, email, follow-up message, objection handling script |

---

## **Phase 2: Outsource Execution**

**Task:**

Hire 2–3 freelance sales reps (Upwork, Internshala, or commission-based agents)

**Setup:**

- Unko SOP + templates do
- Daily 1-hour performance reporting Google Form me
- Leads dene ke liye CRM ya Excel sheet ready rakho

**Example:**

> Freelancers LinkedIn, Facebook Groups, or WhatsApp leads pe outreach karte hain
> 
> 
> Har call/email ka result Google Form me bharte hain
> 

---

## **Phase 3: Automation Setup**

### 📩 Lead & Follow-up Automation

| Tool | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Google Sheets / Airtable** | Lead list + status track |
| **Mailchimp / Instantly.ai** | Automated cold email campaign |
| **WhatsApp Business API (Wati / Twilio)** | Auto reminders “Hey [Name], did you check our Tax Plan offer?” |

### 🧠 Data Collection Automation

- Freelancers ek Google Form bharte hain:
    - Lead name, status, feedback, objection reason
- Form → auto sync hota hai Airtable ya Notion dashboard me

### 📊 Dashboard Automation

- Airtable me live data view:
    - Total leads contacted
    - Conversions
    - Top performer (freelancer-wise performance)
    - Client satisfaction ratings

---

## **Phase 4: Analyze & Iterate**

**After 15 days:**

- Data dikha:
    - 2 freelancers ne milkar 60 leads contact kiye
    - 12 clients convert hue → 20% conversion rate ✅
    - Feedback: Price objection common

**Next Step:**

- Pricing tweak karo
- Better objection-handling script add karo
- Experiment repeat for next 30 days with updated process

---

## **Phase 5: Scale Up**

- Jo freelancers perform kar rahe hain → unko retain karo
- 2 aur naya hire karo
- Automation process same rakho (just scale database & email campaigns)
- Ab ye poora process daily aapke bina bhi chalega

---

## ✅ **Result**

- Manual stress kam
- Data clear
- Freelancers + tools ka mix system aapke liye kaam karta hai
- Aap sirf weekly review & decisions pe focus karte ho

---

Aise hi system aap **har department (marketing, operations, HR)** me bana sakte ho —

bas ek **clear experiment + automation + outsourcing** framework follow karna hota hai.
