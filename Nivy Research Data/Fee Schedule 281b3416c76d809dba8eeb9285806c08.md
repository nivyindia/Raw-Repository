### **2. Performance Bonus Structure**

Associates may be eligible for a discretionary performance bonus based on the following project profit margins achieved by the Company:

| Client Satisfaction Score (CSAT) | Project Completion | Bonus (% of Project Profit) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| > 95% | Delivered ahead of schedule | 15% |
| 90% - 95% | Delivered on schedule | 10% |
| 85% - 90% | Delivered on schedule | 5% |
| < 85% or Late Delivery | No Bonus Eligible | 0% |
- **Bonus Eligibility Criteria:** Bonuses are awarded solely at the Company's discretion. To be eligible, an Associate must have no major quality revisions requested by the client or Company.
- **Calculation:** "Project Profit" is defined as the total fee paid by the client to the Company, minus the Associate's Fixed Fee and any direct, project-specific costs incurred by the Company (e.g., software, plugins, ad spend).

### **3. Referral Fees**

For clients successfully referred by the Associate where the work is completed by another service provider, the Associate shall receive a referral fee of **15% of the Company's Net Profit** from the first contract with that client.

**Eligible Services for Referral:**

- Web Development
- Search Engine Optimization (SEO) Packages
- Digital Advertising Management (e.g., Google Ads, Meta Ads)
- Brand Strategy & Consulting

### **Important Terms & Conditions**

1. **Payment Terms:** All fees listed are exclusive of GST/taxes. Payments are processed within 15 days of month-end via bank transfer. The Company reserves the right to withhold payment for work that does not meet quality standards until satisfactory revisions are made.
2. **Performance Bonus Disclaimer:** The Performance Bonus is a discretionary incentive, not a guaranteed part of compensation. The Company's assessment of client satisfaction and exceptional performance is final and binding.
3. **Amendment & Notification:** The Company reserves the right to amend this Fee Schedule at its discretion to reflect changes in market rates, service offerings, or business strategy. Associates will be provided with **thirty (30) days' written notice** of any changes via email or the Company's project management portal.
4. **Continued Service as Acceptance:** An Associate's continued provision of services to the Company after the effective date of an updated Fee Schedule constitutes their full and binding acceptance of the new terms.

### **Key Benefits of This Separate Document:**

- **Legal Protection:** The disclaimer and acknowledgment section solidifies that the bonus is discretionary and that continued work implies acceptance of new terms.
- **Operational Flexibility:** You can change prices, add services, or adjust bonuses by simply issuing a new "Effective Date" document and notifying associates, without ever touching the main contract.
- **Clarity & Professionalism:** It provides a clear, referenceable document for both you and your associates, preventing disputes over pay.
- **Scalability:** You can easily create different schedules for different associate levels or regions in the future.
