# 👥 HR Department

<aside>
👥

**What is this department?**

HR manages hiring, onboarding, team structure, employee records, performance, and company culture across all Nivy markets.

**Who should use this:** HR lead, team managers, and all staff for onboarding and policy reference.

📝 **Owner:** HR Lead | 🌍 **Markets:** US · UK · CA · AU · UAE

</aside>

---

## 📄 Key Pages

- **HR Overview** — Full HR department overview and people strategy
- **HR Dashboard** — Live headcount, open roles, and team metrics
- **Employee Handbook** — Company policies, values, leave, and conduct guidelines
- **SOP: Recruitment Process** — End-to-end hiring SOP
- **Organizational Structure** — Org chart and role hierarchy

---

## 📋 Active SOPs

All HR SOPs are in 📋 SOPs & Processes → SOP Directory. Filter by Department = HR.

---

## 📊 KPIs to Track

- Open roles vs filled roles
- Time to hire (target: < 30 days)
- Employee retention rate
- Onboarding completion rate
