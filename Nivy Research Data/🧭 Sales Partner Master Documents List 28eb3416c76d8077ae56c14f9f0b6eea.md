## **1️⃣ Must-Have Tools & Skills for Sales Outsourcing Partners**

These are tools and competencies that allow a partner to **operate independently**:

### **A. CRM & Sales Management**

- **CRM tools** (for lead tracking, reporting, pipeline management)
    - Examples: HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Freshsales
- Ability to generate reports, update deal status, and manage follow-ups

### **B. ERP / Operations Management**

- ERP familiarity (if relevant to your business, e.g., inventory, order tracking, billing)
    - Examples: Tally, SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Odoo

### **C. Communication & Collaboration**

- **Email Management:** Gmail / Outlook, professional email etiquette
- **Messaging & Collaboration:** Slack, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp Business
- **Video Conferencing:** Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams

### **D. Marketing & Lead Generation Tools**

- Social media platforms (Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram) for lead sourcing
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator (if targeting B2B clients)
- Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Drive) for sharing reports & documents
- Optional: Email marketing tools (Mailchimp, SendinBlue)

### **E. Productivity & Tracking**

- Task management tools: Trello, Asana, Monday.com, Notion
- Spreadsheet skills (Excel / Google Sheets) for lead & revenue tracking
- Calendar & scheduling tools (Google Calendar, Calendly)

### **F. Optional Advanced Tools**

- Analytics: Google Analytics, Power BI, Tableau (if analyzing campaigns)
- Marketing automation: HubSpot workflows, ActiveCampaign
- Proposal & e-sign tools: DocuSign, PandaDoc, Canva for presentations

These are critical for **smooth onboarding** and immediate productivity:

1. **Sales Experience**
    - Previous experience in B2B / B2C sales
    - Handling multiple clients simultaneously
2. **Communication Skills**

4. **Lead Generation Ability**

5. **Self-Motivation & Discipline**
    - Can work remotely without constant supervision
    - Can follow processes and timelines independently

## **3️⃣ How to Verify Their Skills / Work Proof**

You need **evidence** before onboarding — so you don’t waste time.

### **A. Request Work Samples**

- CRM screenshots (obscure client info if confidential)
- Previous sales reports / dashboards
- Lead lists or pipeline they managed (anonymized)
- Sales presentations or proposals they’ve created

### **B. Ask for References**

- Previous employers, clients, or managers
- Ask specifically about targets achieved, tools used, and reliability

### **C. Small Paid Test Project**

- Assign a short task:
    - Research X leads and enter into CRM
    - Send 5 cold outreach emails/messages (with guidance)
    - Prepare a small sales report
- Evaluate speed, quality, and accuracy

### **D. Interview & Practical Questions**

- Ask them to **demonstrate CRM or tool skills live**
- Ask scenario-based questions:
    - “How would you manage 50 leads per week?”
    - “How would you track follow-ups and lost deals?”

### **E. Certificates or Course Proof (Optional)**

- Some CRMs / tools have official certification (e.g., HubSpot, Salesforce)
- Previous ERP or sales certifications
