# Reachout

Below is a **clear, end-to-end, practical roadmap** for **tying up with companies**—from idea → proposal → outreach → closing → starting work. This is written so you can **actually execute it**, not just understand it.

---

## PHASE 1: PREPARE (Before contacting any company)

### 1. Define the Tie-up Clearly

Be very specific.

- **What are you offering?**
    - Product / Service / Partnership / Distribution / Consulting / Sponsorship
- **Who is it for?**
    - Industry, company size, decision maker
- **What problem do you solve for them?**
- **What result will they get?**
    - More sales, lower cost, better branding, compliance, faster execution

👉 If you can’t explain this in **1–2 lines**, stop and refine.

**Example**

> “We help mid-sized FMCG brands reduce influencer marketing cost by 30% while increasing reach.”
> 

---

### 2. Identify Ideal Companies

Create a **target list**.

For each company, note:

- Company name
- Industry
- Decision maker role (Founder, Marketing Head, HR Head, Ops Head)
- LinkedIn / Email
- Why this company is a good fit

🔹 Start with **20–30 companies**, not 200.

---

### 3. Create Basic Assets (Must-Have)

You don’t need everything—just these:

1. **1-page proposal (PDF or Doc)**
2. **Pitch message (email / LinkedIn)**
3. **Intro deck (5–8 slides)** – optional but powerful
4. **Pricing / engagement model**

---

## PHASE 2: DRAFTING THE PROPOSAL

### Proposal Structure (Simple & Effective)

**Page 1–2 max**

1. **Introduction**
    - Who you are
    - Why you’re reaching out
2. **Problem Understanding**
    - Show you understand THEIR pain
3. **Your Solution**
    - What you’ll do
    - How it works
4. **Value to Company**
    - Tangible benefits (numbers if possible)
5. **Engagement Model**
    - Duration
    - Deliverables
    - Commercials (or “to be discussed”)
6. **Next Steps**
    - Call / pilot / demo

**Tip:**

Do NOT talk too much about yourself. Talk about **them**.

---

## PHASE 3: REACH OUT (This is where most people fail)

### 1. Find the Right Person

Avoid “[info@company.com](mailto:info@company.com)”.

Use:

- LinkedIn
- Company website
- Apollo / RocketReach (if available)

Target:

- Founder (small companies)
- Department Head (mid/large companies)

---

### 2. Outreach Message Framework

### Email / LinkedIn Message (Short & Personal)

**Structure:**

1. Personal context
2. Problem insight
3. Clear value
4. Soft CTA

**Example:**

> Hi [Name],
> 
> 
> I noticed your team is expanding into [X]. Many companies at this stage struggle with [specific problem].
> 
> We’ve helped similar teams achieve [result] using [your solution].
> 
> Would you be open to a 15-minute call to explore if this could make sense for you?
> 
> Best,
> 
> Abhi
> 

🔹 Keep it **under 120 words**.

---

### 3. Follow-up System (Very Important)

Most deals close after follow-ups.

- Day 3: Gentle nudge
- Day 7: Value add / insight
- Day 14: Final check

**Example follow-up:**

> Just checking in—happy to share a quick use case if helpful.
> 

---

## PHASE 4: DISCOVERY CALL (DO NOT PITCH HARD)

### Goal of the Call:

❌ Not to sell

✅ To understand & qualify

### Call Flow (30 mins)

1. **Intro (5 min)**
    - Who you are
    - Agenda
2. **Discovery (15 min)**
    
    Ask questions like:
    
    - What’s your current process?
    - What’s not working?
    - What would success look like?
    - Budget & timelines?
3. **Solution Mapping (5 min)**
    - Connect their problem to your offering
4. **Next Steps (5 min)**
    - Proposal / Pilot / Commercial discussion

---

## PHASE 5: CLOSING THE DEAL

### 1. Send Customized Proposal

After the call, update your proposal with:

- Their exact problem
- Agreed scope
- Clear pricing
- Timeline
- Responsibilities (yours & theirs)

---

### 2. Handle Objections Calmly

Common objections:

- “Too expensive”
- “Let’s think”
- “Not now”

Respond with:

- Clarification
- Options (pilot / phased plan)
- ROI explanation

---

### 3. Close Formally

Once agreed:

- Written confirmation (email / WhatsApp)
- Contract / MoU
- Advance payment (if applicable)

**Never start work without written confirmation.**

---

## PHASE 6: START WORK PROFESSIONALLY

### 1. Onboarding

- Kick-off call
- Share timeline
- Define communication channel

### 2. Deliver Quick Wins

First 7–14 days should show:

- Progress
- Visibility
- Confidence

### 3. Relationship Management

- Regular updates
- Monthly review
- Look for upsell / long-term contract

---

## SIMPLE FLOW SUMMARY

**Idea → Target companies → Proposal → Outreach → Discovery Call → Custom Proposal → Close → Kick-off → Deliver**
