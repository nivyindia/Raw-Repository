### **1. Introduction & Executive Summary**

Nivy is a dynamic agency specializing in **Digital Marketing** and **Tax & Accounting** services. We are seeking a strategic partner to outsource our entire sales and marketing function. Our goal is to accelerate growth, increase our client base, and establish a strong market presence by leveraging the expertise of a specialized sales and marketing firm.

This RFP outlines our requirements and invites qualified vendors to submit proposals detailing their approach, capabilities, and pricing to manage and execute our sales and marketing strategies.

### **2. Company Background**

Nivy provides a dual-focused service offering:

- **Digital Marketing:** Services including SEO, SEM/PPC, Social Media Marketing, Content Creation, and Web Development for businesses looking to enhance their online presence.
- **Tax & Accounting:** Services including tax preparation, filing, bookkeeping, financial consulting, and compliance for individuals and small-to-medium-sized businesses.

Our target market includes startups, SMEs, and entrepreneurs who need robust digital strategies and/or reliable financial management.

### **3. Project Objectives & Goals**

The primary objective is to generate a consistent pipeline of qualified leads and convert them into paying clients. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) include:

- **Lead Generation:**
    - Generate a minimum of [e.g., 50] qualified marketing leads per month.
    - Generate a minimum of [e.g., 20] qualified tax/accounting leads per month.
- **Sales Conversion:**
    - Achieve a lead-to-client conversion rate of [e.g., 15% - 20%].
    - Onboard a minimum of [e.g., 8-10] new retainer clients per quarter.
- **Brand Awareness:**
    - Increase website traffic by [e.g., 40%] within 6 months.
    - Grow our social media following and engagement rates significantly.
- **Revenue:**
    - Directly contribute to [e.g., ₹XX Lakhs] in new revenue within the first year.

### **4. Scope of Services Required**

The selected vendor will be responsible for the following:

**A. Strategic Planning:**

- Develop a comprehensive 12-month sales and marketing strategy.
- Define target audience personas for both service verticals.
- Conduct a competitive analysis.

**B. Marketing Execution:**.

- **Social Media Management:** Manage and grow our presence on LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, and other relevant platforms.
- **Email Marketing:** Build and execute lead-nurturing email campaigns.

**C. Sales Execution:**

- **Lead Qualification:** Vet all incoming leads based on predefined criteria (budget, authority, need, timeline).
- **Outreach:**  email outreach, and LinkedIn sales navigator campaigns.
- **Appointment Setting:** Schedule qualified sales meetings/demos for our internal principals or account managers.
- **Sales Pipeline Management:** Manage and report on the sales pipeline using a CRM.
- **Client Onboarding Support:** Assist in the smooth handover from sales to service delivery.

### **5. Proposal Requirements**

Please structure your proposal to include the following sections:

1. **Company Profile:** Overview of your firm, years in business, and core competencies.
2. **Relevant Experience:** Case studies or detailed examples of past work, specifically with agencies in digital marketing, professional services (tax, accounting, legal), or B2B sectors.
3. **Proposed Team:** Bios and experience of the key team members who will be assigned to our account.
4. **Strategic Approach:** Your high-level strategy for the first 90 days and beyond. How will you approach the two distinct service lines?
5. **Technology & Tools:** Detail the CRM, marketing automation, analytics, and communication tools you will use.
6. **Pricing & Fee Structure:**
    - Provide a detailed breakdown of costs (e.g., monthly retainer, performance-based bonuses, project-based fees).
    - Clearly state what is included in the base fee and what would be considered additional (e.g., ad spend budget).
    - Proposed contract term.
7. **Reporting & Communication:** Outline your proposed reporting frequency, format, and key metrics you will track. Specify the communication protocol (e.g., weekly calls, a dedicated account manager).
8. **References:** Provide at least two client references.
