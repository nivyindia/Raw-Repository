## 📁 OS Structure — 6 Sections

Open any section to browse its contents:

- 🏢 **Company Foundation** — Business model, business plan, company documents, org structure
- ⚙️ **Operations & Departments** — All department hubs: CEO, Sales, Operations, HR, Technology, Project Management + all SOPs
- 💰 **Finance & Accounting** — Financial dashboards, reports, forecasts, and accounting resources
- 📣 **Marketing & Sales** — Marketing strategy, brand guidelines, campaigns, and sales resources
- 📚 **Knowledge & Resources** — Company wiki, templates, investor materials
- 🤖 **Systems & Technology** — System databases, automation logs, client portal
