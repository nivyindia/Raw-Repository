# 📏 Code of Conduct & Non-Poaching Policy

Here is a **professional, clear, and strict** **Code of Conduct + Non-Poaching Rules** for your digital marketing & accounting agency team (part-timers, pocket-money earners, outreach assistants, interns, sales helpers, etc.)

****
*(All team members must read and agree before joining)*
**1️⃣ Professional Behaviour Rules
1. Be Respectful**
• Speak politely with clients, team members, and seniors.
• No abusive, rude, or unprofessional language.
**2. Follow Instructions & Training**
• Use only the messaging templates and scripts provided.
• Do not use your own offers, claims, or wrong information.
**3. Maintain Confidentiality**
• All client data, phone numbers, documents, and strategies belong to the company.
• Never share internal details with outsiders.
**4. Stay Active**
• Minimum 1 hour/day of outreach work is required.
• Inform seniors if you are unavailable.
**5. No Multiple Fake Client Leads**
• Do not submit fake leads or wrong numbers.
• Strict action will be taken immediately.
**2️⃣ Non-Poaching & Anti-Stealing Rules**
These rules are **mandatory** to protect the company and ensure a fair system for all.
**6. No Contacting Clients Personally**
• You may *only* contact clients through official scripts for the purpose of outreach.
• You **cannot**:
    ◦ Take client conversations to your personal number
    ◦ Offer your own freelance services
    ◦ Divert clients to someone else
    ◦ Make side deals
    ◦ Sell competitor services
**7. No Stealing or Sharing Leads**
• All leads generated belong strictly to the company.
• You cannot share leads:
    ◦ With other companies
    ◦ With friends/family
    ◦ For personal projects
**8. No Poaching Team Members**
• You may not:
    ◦ Invite teammates to join your other work
    ◦ Recruit members for any other business
    ◦ Offer them freelance work privately
**9. No Copying Company Processes**
• Do not copy:
    ◦ Scripts
    ◦ Training methods
    ◦ Funnels
    ◦ Outreach strategy
    ◦ SOPs
    ◦ Group lists
    ◦ Client lists

For personal use or to start similar work.
**10. No Competing Agency/Service**
• You cannot run or join any competing digital marketing, GST, filing, or accounting service using our strategy.
**3️⃣ Integrity & Compliance Rules
11. Only Real Work Allowed**
• No automation tools, bots, or fake engagement.
• No spammy or illegal actions.
**12. Keep Communication Clean**
• No flirting, personal asking, or unprofessional chat with clients or team members.
**13. Follow Legal & Ethical Guidelines**
• Misrepresentation of services is strictly prohibited.
• Fraud, cheating, or fake promises will result in immediate removal.
**14. Protect Company Reputation**
• Do not post negative comments or reviews.
• If you face any issue, contact your senior leader privately.
**4️⃣ Consequences of Violating Rules**
Breaking any of these rules can lead to:
❌ Immediate removal from the team

❌ Permanent ban from future opportunities

❌ Loss of earnings/bonuses

❌ Legal action (in case of data theft or fraud)

❌ Certificate & level cancellation
