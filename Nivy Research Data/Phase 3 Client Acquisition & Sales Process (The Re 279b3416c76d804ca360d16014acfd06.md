# Phase 3: Client Acquisition & Sales Process (The "Revenue Engine")

# **1. Service Packaging & Pricing Strategy**

### **A. Create Tiered Service Packages**

**Goal:** Make it easy for clients to understand what they're buying and why.

**Structure:**

- **Starter Package** (~$1,000-2,500/month)
    - Ideal for: Small businesses, 3-6 month engagements
    - Includes: Basic SEO, social media management, monthly reporting
    - Limits: 5 keywords, 8 posts/week, email support
- **Growth Package** (~$3,000-5,000/month) **← YOUR TARGET PACKAGE**
    - Ideal for: Established SMBs, 6-12 month engagements
    - Includes: Comprehensive SEO, PPC management, content creation, weekly optimization
    - Limits: 15 keywords, dedicated account manager
- **Enterprise Package** (~$7,500+/month)
    - Ideal for: Large businesses, 12+ month contracts
    - Includes: Full-service digital marketing, marketing automation, dedicated team
    - Limits: Custom solutions, strategic planning

### **B. Develop Your Pricing Philosophy**

- **Value-Based Pricing:** Price based on results delivered, not hours worked
- **Retainer Model:** 90% of your business should be monthly retainers
- **Minimum Engagement:** 3-month minimum contract with 30-day notice period

## **2. Sales Funnel Development**

### **A. Top of Funnel: Attract Strangers**

**Content Marketing Strategy:**

- **Blogging:** 2-3 posts/week targeting your niche's pain points
    - Examples: "Why [Niche] Businesses Fail at Facebook Ads," "SEO Checklist for [Industry]"
- **Lead Magnets:** Create high-value free offers
    - Website SEO Audit
    - Social Media Strategy Template
    - PPC Performance Calculator

**Social Proof Building:**

- Create detailed case studies with specific metrics
- Collect video testimonials from happy clients
- Show client logos (with permission) on your website

### **B. Middle of Funnel: Convert Visitors to Leads**

**Landing Pages:**

- Dedicated pages for each lead magnet
- Clear value proposition + simple form (name, email, company)
- Automatic redirect to thank you page with download

**Email Nurture Sequence:** (7-10 emails over 3 weeks)

- Day 1: Thank you + deliver promised resource
- Day 3: Additional tip related to download
- Day 7: Case study showing results
- Day 14: Invitation to free consultation

### **C. Bottom of Funnel: Convert Leads to Clients**

**Consultation Booking Process:**

- Use Calendly or Odoo's appointment scheduling
- 30-minute discovery call format
- Automated confirmation and reminder emails

## **3. Sales Process & Conversion System**

### **A. Pre-Call Preparation**

**Qualification Checklist:**

- Budget: Minimum $1,500/month
- Authority: Decision-maker on call
- Need: Clear pain point you can solve
- Timeline: Ready to start within 30 days

**Research Before Each Call:**

- Review their website and current marketing
- Check their social media presence
- Understand their competitive landscape

### **B. The Discovery Call Structure (30 minutes)**

**First 10 mins: Diagnosis**

- "What's not working with your current marketing?"
- "What have you tried before?"
- "What would solving this mean for your business?"

**Next 10 mins: Solution Framework**

- "Based on what you've shared, here's how we'd approach this..."
- Present 2-3 key strategies specific to their situation
- Share relevant case study snippets

**Final 10 mins: Next Steps**

- Discuss which package might fit best
- Outline onboarding process
- Schedule follow-up proposal presentation

### **C. Proposal & Closing**

**24-Hour Proposal Delivery:**

- Customize your package template based on discovery call
- Include 3-month projected outcomes
- Clearly state what's included (and what's not)

**Follow-up Sequence:**

- Day 1: Send proposal + schedule follow-up call
- Day 3: Check if questions + offer mini-audit
- Day 7: Final check-in before closing file

## **4. Networking & Partnership Strategy**

### **A. Strategic Partnerships**

**Identify Complementary Businesses:**

- Web development agencies
- PR firms
- Business consultants
- Commercial printers

**Partnership Structure:**

- Formal referral agreement (15-20% commission)
- Joint marketing efforts
- Regular check-in meetings

### **B. Direct Outreach**

**LinkedIn Strategy:**

- Connect with 10-15 ideal clients weekly
- Personalize connection requests
- Share valuable content daily
- Engage with target clients' content

**Email Outreach:**

- Highly personalized emails (3-5/day)
- Reference specific observations about their business
- Offer specific, quick-win ideas

## **5. Odoo CRM Setup for Sales Pipeline**

### **A. Pipeline Stages Configuration**

1. **New Lead** → 2. **Qualified** → 3. **Discovery Call Held** → 4. **Proposal Sent** → 5. **Negotiation** → 6. **Won/Lost**

### **B. Automation Rules**

- **Lead Scoring:** Automatically prioritize leads based on:
    - Website pages visited
    - Download lead magnet
    - Request consultation
- **Follow-up Tasks:** Automatically create tasks for sales team
- **Email Templates:** Pre-written templates for each stage

### **C. Sales Dashboard Metrics to Track**

- Lead conversion rate (%)
- Average sales cycle length (days)
- Proposal-to-close ratio (%)
- Client acquisition cost
- Lifetime value per client

## **6. Onboarding Process Development**

### **A. Pre-Onboarding (After Contract Signed)**

- Send welcome package and contract
- Set up client portal access
- Schedule kickoff meeting

### **B. Kickoff Meeting Structure**

- Review goals and KPIs
- Discuss communication protocols
- Access gathering (website, social media, analytics)
- Set up reporting dashboard

### **C. First 30-Day Success Plan**

- Week 1: Audit and strategy development
- Week 2-3: Initial implementation
- Week 4: First performance report + strategy adjustment

## **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for Phase 3**

- **Monthly Qualified Leads:** 15-20
- **Discovery Call Conversion Rate:** 40-50%
- **Proposal-to-Close Rate:** 25-35%
- **Client Acquisition Cost:** < $1,000
- **Sales Cycle Length:** 14-21 days

This phase requires consistent execution. The goal is to create a repeatable system that generates 2-4 new clients per month consistently.
