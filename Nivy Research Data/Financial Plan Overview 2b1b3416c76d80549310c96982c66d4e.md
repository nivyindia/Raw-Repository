# Financial Plan Overview

A complete **Financial Plan for a company** usually includes the following major topics. This is a universal structure used in startups, small businesses, and large companies:

---

# ✅ **Complete List of Topics in a Company’s Financial Plan**

## **1. Executive Financial Summary**

- High-level overview of financial goals
- Current financial health
- Key financial highlights (revenue, profit, runway)
- Summary of required funding (if any)

---

## **2. Revenue Model**

- How the company makes money
- Pricing strategy
- Revenue streams (products, services, subscriptions, etc.)
- Expected customer lifetime value
- Revenue assumptions

---

## **3. Cost Structure**

### **A. Fixed Costs**

- Salaries & wages
- Rent
- Software & tools
- Utilities
- Insurance

### **B. Variable Costs**

- Cost of goods sold (COGS)
- Freelancer/contractor payouts
- Marketing spend
- Sales commissions

---

## **4. Operating Expenses (OPEX)**

- Admin expenses
- Marketing & sales expenses
- HR & recruitment
- Legal & compliance
- R&D or development costs
- IT & infrastructure

---

## **5. Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)**

- Equipment purchases
- Machinery
- Office infrastructure
- Technology/servers
- Long-term asset investments

---

## **6. Funding Requirements**

- Total capital needed
- How the funds will be used
- Funding timeline
- Return expectations
- Debt vs equity structure (if applicable)

---

## **7. Forecasted Financial Statements**

Includes projections for **3–5 years**:

### **A. Projected Profit & Loss (Income Statement)**

- Revenue
- Gross profit
- Operating expenses
- Net profit

### **B. Projected Balance Sheet**

- Assets
- Liabilities
- Equity

### **C. Projected Cash Flow Statement**

- Inflows & outflows
- Operational cash flow
- Working capital requirements

---

## **8. Break-Even Analysis**

- Break-even point
- Contribution margin
- Unit economics

---

## **9. Financial Ratios & KPIs**

### **Profitability Ratios**

- Gross margin
- Net margin
- EBITDA

### **Liquidity Ratios**

- Current ratio
- Quick ratio

### **Efficiency Ratios**

- Inventory turnover
- Asset utilization

### **Other KPIs**

- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- Customer lifetime value (LTV)
- Churn rate
- Revenue growth rate

---

## **10. Scenario & Sensitivity Analysis**

- Best-case scenario
- Base-case scenario
- Worst-case scenario
- Sensitivity to price changes, cost changes, etc.

---

## **11. Risk Assessment & Mitigation**

- Financial risks
- Market risks
- Operational risks
- Contingency plans
- Emergency fund allocation

---

## **12. Working Capital Management**

- Cash management
- Accounts receivable strategy
- Accounts payable strategy
- Inventory planning
- Short-term financing plans

---

## **13. Tax Planning**

- Tax liabilities
- Tax-saving schemes
- Depreciation planning
- GST, TDS, income tax, corporate tax planning

---

## **14. Dividend & Profit Distribution Policy**

- How profits will be distributed
- Founder compensation
- Reserve creation
- Reinvestment strategy

---

## **15. Exit Strategy (Optional)**

- IPO
- Acquisition
- Merger
- Buyout
- Investor exit strategy

---

## **16. Budgeting & Budget Control**

- Annual budget
- Quarterly/monthly budgets
- Variance analysis (budget vs actual)
- Budget revision process

---

## **17. Cash Burn Rate & Runway (Especially for startups)**

- Monthly burn rate
- Cash runway (how long the business can survive)
- Burn optimization strategies

---

## **18. Financial Assumptions**

Detailed list of every assumption used in forecasts:

- Market size growth
- Conversion rates
- Pricing assumptions
- Cost assumptions
- Hiring plans
- Productivity assumptions
- Inflation assumptions

---

## **19. Department-Wise Financial Plans**

Each department gets its own plan:

- Marketing budget plan
- Sales budget plan
- HR & hiring cost plan
- Operations financial plan
- Tech/product development plan
- Admin/overheads plan

---

## **20. Payroll & Hiring Financial Planning**

- Hiring timeline
- Salary slabs
- Compensation structure
- Appraisals & increments
- Incentives & bonuses
- Benefits planning

---

## **21. Asset Management Plan**

- Asset acquisition schedule
- Depreciation
- Asset disposal
- Asset maintenance cost

---

## **22. Loan Management / Credit Planning**

- Current loans
- Interest payments
- EMI schedules
- Lines of credit
- Future borrowing requirements
- Debt servicing capability

---

## **23. Financial Governance Policies**

- Internal audits
- Approval limits
- Expense approval system
- Internal controls
- Fraud prevention
- Compliance requirements

---

## **24. Insurance & Financial Safety Measures**

- Business insurance
- Liability insurance
- Employee insurance
- Data & cyber insurance
- Insurance budgeting

---

## **25. Strategic Investment Plan**

If the company invests money:

- Long-term investments
- Short-term investments
- Real estate
- Portfolio diversification
- ROI tracking

---

## **26. Cost Optimization Strategy**

- Cost-cutting plan
- Vendor negotiation
- Automation planning
- Outsourcing plan
- Process optimization

---

## **27. Multi-Year Roadmap**

- 1-year plan
- 3-year plan
- 5-year plan
- Financial milestones
- Expansion funding plan

---

## **28. Grants, Subsidies & Government Schemes**

- Grants you can apply for
- Startup subsidies
- Manufacturing incentives
- Exports incentives
- Government tax benefits

---

## **29. Currency & Forex Planning (If international business)**

- Forex risk
- Exchange rate fluctuations
- International payments
- Hedging strategy

---

## **30. Financial Documentation Index**

A final section that lists all financial docs:

- Past financials
- Bank statements
- Loan statements
- Agreements & contracts
- Licenses, registrations
- Tax documents

---
