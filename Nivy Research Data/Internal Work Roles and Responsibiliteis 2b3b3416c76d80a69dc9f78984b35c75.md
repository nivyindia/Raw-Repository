## Detailed Internal Work Breakdown

### **SOP Creation Responsibilities:**

**Executives:** Strategic SOPs, company policies, compliance frameworks
**Managers:** Departmental processes, team workflows, quality standards
**Team Leads:** Daily procedures, tool usage, task execution
**Seniors:** Methodology documentation, best practices
**Juniors:** Work logs, task checklists

### **Documentation Duties:**

**Executives:** Company policies, strategic plans, board reports
**Managers:** Department manuals, process flows, performance reports
**Team Leads:** Team procedures, work instructions, progress reports
**Seniors:** Technical documentation, methodology guides
**Juniors:** Daily logs, task records, data entries

### **Interview Responsibilities:**

**Executives:** Final rounds for leadership positions
**Managers:** Department hires, technical assessments
**Team Leads:** Team member selection, skill evaluation
**Seniors:** Technical interviews, peer assessments
**Juniors:** No interview responsibilities

### **Onboarding Tasks:**

**Executives:** Welcome leadership, culture introduction
**Managers:** Department orientation, role clarification
**Team Leads:** Team integration, tool training
**Seniors:** Mentor assignments, skill transfer
**Juniors:** Self-onboarding completion

### **Access Management:**

**Executives:** Master access approval, security policies
**Managers:** Department access, tool permissions
**Team Leads:** Team access requests, usage monitoring
**Seniors:** Technical access requirements
**Juniors:** Basic tool access only

### **Knowledge Base Contribution:**

**Executives:** Strategic content, company vision
**Managers:** Department knowledge, process guides
**Team Leads:** Team procedures, troubleshooting
**Seniors:** Technical guides, best practices
**Juniors:** Basic how-to articles, FAQs

### **Team Training:**

**Executives:** Leadership development, strategic thinking
**Managers:** Department skills, process training
**Team Leads:** Daily procedures, tool usage
**Seniors:** Technical skills, methodology training
**Juniors:** Peer learning, self-training

### **Process Improvement:**

**Executives:** Strategic optimization, company-wide efficiency
**Managers:** Department processes, workflow optimization
**Team Leads:** Daily procedures, team efficiency
**Seniors:** Technical processes, methodology improvement
**Juniors:** Feedback provision, suggestion sharing

### **Performance Reviews:**

**Executives:** Leadership team evaluation
**Managers:** Department team assessments
**Team Leads:** Team member performance
**Seniors:** Peer feedback, self-assessment
**Juniors:** Self-review preparation

### **Client Handovers:**

**Executives:** Strategic client relationships
**Managers:** Key account transitions
**Team Leads:** Project deliverables, data handovers
**Seniors:** Technical deliverables, documentation
**Juniors:** Task completion reports

## Internal Work Time Allocation Guidelines:

**Executives:** 40% internal work, 60% external/client work
**Managers:** 50% internal work, 50% team/client work

**Team Leads:** 30% internal work, 70% team execution
**Seniors:** 20% internal work, 80% project work
**Juniors:** 10% internal work, 90% task execution

This matrix ensures every role contributes to company building while maintaining focus on their primary responsibilities.
