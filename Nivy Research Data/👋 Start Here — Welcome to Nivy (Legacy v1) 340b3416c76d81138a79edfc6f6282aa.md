## 🌟 What is Nivy Digital?

**Nivy Digital** is an international multi-division company built to connect people, businesses, and opportunities. We operate across multiple verticals — from global business expansion and community-based marketplaces, to digital marketing, talent development, and social impact.

Think of Nivy Digital as a **group of companies under one roof**, all working together with shared systems, values, and a lean, community-powered model. Our team includes full-time staff, part-time contributors, freelancers, and community members across the globe.

Our core belief: **small, smart teams + an empowered community can do the work of a much larger organisation**.

## 🏢 Our Divisions — The Nivy Family

Each division is its own focused unit. Here's what each one does in plain English:

| Division | What It Does | Who It's For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🌐 **Nivy Global** | Expands Nivy's business internationally. Handles partner agreements, franchising, proposals, and global clients. | Business development team, leadership |
| 🔗 **Nivy Nexus** | Runs the buyer-seller community platform. Connects businesses, freelancers, and buyers especially in UAE markets. | Community managers, outreach team |
| 🚀 **Nivy Next** | The innovation arm. Launches new products, initiatives, and experiments. | Innovation leads, founders |
| 🎓 **Nivy Academy** | Provides training and learning for team members and community. Hosts courses, onboarding, and skill development. | All staff, community members |
| 🤝 **Nivy Alliance** | Manages B2B partnerships, community governance, and the network of contributors and collaborators. | Partnership team, community leads |
| ❤️ **Nivy Care Foundation** | Handles CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) and social impact projects. | Foundation team |
| 💼 **Nivy Advisory** | Offers advisory and consulting services. Manages brand strategy and client-facing consulting work. | Advisory consultants, client teams |
| 💼 **Nivy Jobs** | Posts jobs, manages recruitment, and runs the hiring process across all Nivy companies. | HR, hiring managers |
| 💰 **GAAP** | Handles all accounting, finance compliance, and GAAP-standard financial reporting. | Finance team, CA |

## 💻 How Our Notion Workspace is Organised

Our Notion is structured in **3 tiers**:

**Tier 1 — Company-Wide (start here):**

- 🏠 **Nivy HQ** — This page. Company overview, glossary, org chart, announcements.
- 🧠 **Nivy OS** — The engine room. All master databases, SOPs, projects, templates.
- 💼 **Business Plan** — Financial plans, market analysis, growth strategy.
- 💰 **GAAP** — Accounting and compliance.

**Tier 2 — Divisions (find your team):**

Each Nivy division has its own teamspace. Find yours in the table above and click in.

**Tier 3 — Departments (inside Nivy OS):**

Marketing, Sales, HR, Finance, Operations, Technology — all live inside Nivy OS as departments.
