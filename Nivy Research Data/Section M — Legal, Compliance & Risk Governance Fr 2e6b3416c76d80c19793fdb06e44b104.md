## **M. Legal & Compliance Framework**

---

### **1. Legal Entity & Brand Structure**

- **Parent Company:** Billion Dreams United
- **Trademark:** NIVY®
- **Service Brand:** Nivy Nexus
- **Jurisdiction:** India (with UAE market operations via partners)

---

### **2. Regulatory Compliance (India)**

- Business registration as per Indian law
- PAN, GST (where applicable)
- MSME / Udyam Registration (if applicable)
- Income Tax & statutory filings
- Data protection practices aligned with Indian IT Act

---

### **3. Regulatory Compliance (UAE – Partner Model)**

- Operations conducted through **licensed local partners**
- Adherence to UAE commercial, trade, and data protection laws
- No unauthorized direct trading without local compliance
- Clear cross-border service agreements

---

### **4. Contractual Safeguards**

- Buyer–Facilitator–Supplier agreements
- Defined scope of facilitation responsibilities
- Clear fee, commission, and payment clauses
- Limitation of liability clauses
- Confidentiality & non-circumvention clauses

---

### **5. Payment & Financial Governance**

- Transparent invoicing practices
- Agreed payment milestones
- Success-based fee structures
- No commingling of funds unless contractually agreed
- Record-keeping for audits and compliance

---

### **6. Risk Classification & Mitigation**

| Risk Type | Mitigation Strategy |
| --- | --- |
| Supplier fraud | Multi-layer verification |
| Buyer default | Pre-qualification & payment milestones |
| Delivery delays | Logistics partner coordination |
| Regulatory breach | Partner-led compliance checks |
| Data misuse | Access controls & NDAs |

---

### **7. Data Privacy & Confidentiality**

- Secure handling of business data
- Restricted access to sensitive documents
- NDA-based engagements
- No resale or misuse of client data

---

### **8. Ethical Business Conduct**

- Zero tolerance for bribery or illegal facilitation
- No misleading claims
- Transparent communication
- Fair partner treatment

---

### **9. Dispute Resolution**

- First-level internal resolution
- Arbitration clause in contracts
- Jurisdiction defined contractually
- Written communication records maintained

---

### **10. Insurance & Liability (Future Scope)**

- Professional indemnity insurance (planned)
- Cyber risk coverage (planned)
- Partner risk-sharing frameworks

---
