# 2️⃣ Core Roles (Only What You Need)

## A. Head of Sales (MOST IMPORTANT HIRE)

**Country:** 🇵🇭 Philippines or 🇺🇸 Tier-1 contractor

**Count:** 1 person only

**Responsibilities:**

- Own revenue target
- Train closers
- Fire non-performers
- Daily numbers review
- Commission disputes resolution

**Pay:**

- No fixed salary OR small base ($500–800)
- **5–10% of total closed revenue**

👉 This person replaces **10 managers**.

---

## B. Closers (Deal Closing)

**Country:** 🇵🇭 Philippines OR Tier-1 freelancers

**Count:** 2–3 max

**Responsibilities:**

- Take booked calls
- Close deals
- Upsell services

**Pay:**

- **15–30% commission per closed deal**
- No base (or tiny base for Philippines)

---

## C. Sales Managers / Team Leads

**Country:** 🇵🇭 Philippines

**Count:** 1 manager per 6–8 setters

**Responsibilities:**

- Script enforcement
- Daily reporting
- Motivation & discipline
- Quality control

**Pay:**

- $400–600 base
- 3–5% override commission

---

## D. Setters / Outreach Agents (Volume Layer)

**Country:** 🇧🇩 Bangladesh / 🇵🇰 Pakistan

**Count:** scalable (start with 6–10)

**Responsibilities:**

- Cold email
- LinkedIn DM
- WhatsApp outreach
- Lead qualification

**Pay:**

- Commission-only OR $150 base + incentives
- $5–10 per qualified meeting

👉 Easily replaceable = zero headache.
