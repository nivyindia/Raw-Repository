Tier-2 buyers sit **between logic and emotion**.

### REQUIRED online assets (minimum):

| Asset | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |

| Active Instagram / LinkedIn | “They are real & active” |
| Client proof posts | Reduces risk |

| Google presence | Search validation |

| Asset | Why it matters |
