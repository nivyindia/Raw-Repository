# **PHASE 5: QUALIFICATION (UAE)**

### **Objective**

Identify serious UAE-based buyers and suppliers who meet ICP requirements, capture their data, and apply **pass/fail rules** for each lead.

---

## **Step 12: Schedule Qualification Calls**

**Inputs Required:**

- Leads marked “Interested” in Phase 4
- Contact info (email / phone / WhatsApp / LinkedIn)

**Execution Rules:**

1. Contact lead to confirm call date & time (UAE working hours: Sun–Thu, 9 AM–6 PM).
2. Send confirmation via WhatsApp / email.
3. Log date/time in CRM / Google Sheet.

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Lead confirms call → proceed to Step 13/14.
- **Fail:** No response after 2 attempts → tag “Follow-Up Later” → do not proceed.

---

## **Step 13: Conduct Buyer Qualification Call**

**Call Objective:** Confirm buying need, ICP fit, and readiness.

**Data to Capture (UAE-specific):**

- Company Type (LLC / Freezone / Sole Establishment)
- Product Requirement (specs, HS codes if applicable)
- Purchase Volume & Timeline
- Budget (AED range)
- Decision Maker
- Current Supplier (optional)
- Compliance requirements (MOHAP, ESMA, Dubai Municipality)
- previous purchase bills

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Meets ICP, has immediate/near-term buying intent → Score High or Medium → proceed to Step 15
- **Fail:** Outside ICP, no clear buying requirement, or timeline too far → tag “Rejected – Buyer” → remove from pipeline

---

## **Step 14: Conduct Supplier Qualification Call**

**Call Objective:** Confirm supply capability, ICP fit, and readiness.

**Data to Capture (UAE-specific):**

- Company Type (LLC / Freezone / Sole Establishment)
- Product Categories / SKUs
- Production Capacity & MOQ
- Delivery Timelines
- Pricing (AED / FOB / CIF)
- Certifications (ISO, ESMA, MOHAP)
- Payment Terms (Advance / LC / 30–60 days credit)
- Export / Domestic Capability
- previous supply bills

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Meets ICP, can supply required volume within timeline → Score High or Medium → proceed to Step 16
- **Fail:** Cannot meet demand, insufficient capacity, or ICP mismatch → tag “Rejected – Supplier” → remove from pipeline

---

## **Step 15: Capture Buyer Requirement Data**

**CRM / Sheet Fields:**

- Product, quantity, timeline, budget, decision maker, compliance, score

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** All mandatory fields filled, score High or Medium → proceed to verification (Phase 6)
- **Fail:** Missing mandatory info, unclear requirements → attempt clarification once; if still incomplete → reject

---

## **Step 16: Capture Supplier Capability Data**

**CRM / Sheet Fields:**

- Product, capacity, MOQ, timelines, certifications, payment terms, score

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** All mandatory fields filled, score High or Medium → proceed to verification (Phase 6)
- **Fail:** Missing mandatory info, cannot meet ICP → attempt clarification once; if still incomplete → reject

---

### **Phase-5 Deliverables (UAE)**

✔ Qualified buyers & suppliers (High/Medium score)

✔ Failed / rejected leads tagged clearly in CRM

✔ Ready for Phase 6: Verification

# **PHASE 6: VERIFICATION (UAE)**

### **Objective**

Verify UAE buyers and suppliers are **legally registered, credible, and operational**, with **pass/fail rules** for verification.

---

## **Step 17: Request Buyer Verification Documents**

**Documents Required (UAE):**

- Trade License (DED / Freezone Authority)
- VAT Registration Certificate (if applicable)
- Chamber of Commerce membership (optional)
- Bank reference / recent invoice (optional)

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** All mandatory documents received and authentic → proceed to Step 19
- **Fail:** Missing or invalid documents → follow-up once; if not resolved → tag “Failed Verification – Buyer”

---

## **Step 18: Request Supplier Verification Documents**

**Documents Required (UAE):**

- Trade License (LLC / Freezone / Sole Establishment)
- VAT Registration Certificate
- Product Certifications (ISO, ESMA, MOHAP)
- Proof of production / capacity (photos, invoices)
- Bank reference (optional)

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** All mandatory documents received and authentic → proceed to Step 20
- **Fail:** Missing or invalid documents → follow-up once; if not resolved → tag “Failed Verification – Supplier”

---

## **Step 19: Review Buyer Documents**

**Checklist:**

- Trade License valid on **DED / Freezone portal**
- VAT registration valid (**Federal Tax Authority portal**)
- Company name matches CRM record

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Verification complete, documents authentic → Verified → move to Phase 7
- **Fail:** Any document invalid or mismatch → Rejected / Hold

---

## **Step 20: Review Supplier Documents**

**Checklist:**

- Trade License valid
- VAT registration valid
- Product certifications valid (ISO / ESMA / MOHAP)
- Capacity proof matches claimed output
- Operational presence confirmed (website, LinkedIn)

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Fully verified → Verified → move to Phase 7
- **Fail:** Invalid / insufficient documents → Rejected / Hold

---

## **Step 21: Assign Verification Status**

| Status | Meaning | Pass/Fail Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verified | Fully compliant, ready for matching | Pass |
| Partially Verified | Minor missing info, follow-up required | Pass after follow-up |
| Not Verified | Cannot proceed, fail verification | Fail → remove from pipeline |

**Data Capture:**

- Verification date
- Verified by
- Notes on gaps/issues
- Update CRM / Google Sheet
