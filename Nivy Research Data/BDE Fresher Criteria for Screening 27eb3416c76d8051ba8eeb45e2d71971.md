# BDE Fresher : Criteria for Screening

### Core Philosophy for a BDE Fresher

You are hiring for **attitude and aptitude**, not experience. Look for a candidate with raw talent that you can mold.

---

### Part 1: Criteria for Screening the CV/Application

Since they are freshers, you cannot judge by work history. Look for these indicators instead:

| Criteria | What to Look For (The "Green Flags") | Why It Matters for a BDE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Proactiveness & Initiative** | **- Internships:** Any sales, marketing, or customer-facing internship. <br> **- Projects:** Academic projects that involved market research, surveys, or presentations. <br> **- Extracurriculars:** President of a club, event organizer, fundraising lead, participation in college fests. | Shows they don't wait to be told what to do. They go out and create opportunities. |
| **2. Communication & Persuasion** | **- Debating/MUN:** Participation in debates, Model UN, elocution competitions. <br> **- Content Creation:** A well-written CV and cover letter. A professional LinkedIn profile. Blogging or public speaking. | The core of the job is clear, persuasive communication. |
| **3. Resilience & Grit** | **- Sports:** Participation in competitive sports, especially team sports. <br> **- Overcoming Challenges:** Any mention of overcoming a personal or academic hurdle. <br> **- Long-term Commitments:** Sticking with a project or activity for a long period. | Sales is full of rejection. They need the mental toughness to hear "no" 99 times to get one "yes." |
| **4. Basic Business Acumen** | **- Relevant Coursework:** Mention of marketing, economics, or business classes. <br> **- Curiosity:** Their cover letter shows they've researched your company and industry. | Shows they are genuinely interested in business, not just "getting a job." |

**Red Flags in a CV:**

- A completely generic, mass-applied CV with no mention of your company.
- No extracurricular activities or projects (indicates a passive personality).
- Poor communication skills evident in the cover letter or CV.

### Part 2: Criteria for the Interview & Assessment

This is where you truly separate the best from the rest.

### A. The Phone Screening Round (5-10 minutes)

**Goal:** Test communication skills and initial enthusiasm.

- **Criteria:**
    - **Clarity & Confidence:** Is their voice clear and confident? Do they speak in a structured manner?
    - **Punctuality:** Did they pick up the call on time and were they prepared?
    - **Enthusiasm:** Do they sound energetic and interested?

### B. The Practical Assignment / Role-Play

This is the **most important step** for a sales fresher.

- **The Task:** "Prepare a 5-minute sales pitch for our product/service [give a brief description] and deliver it to us."
- **What to Assess:**
    - **Confidence & Body Language:** (Even on video) Do they maintain eye contact? Is their posture open and confident?
    - **Persuasion & Clarity:** Can they explain the product's value proposition clearly? Do they focus on benefits, not just features?
    - **Objection Handling:** After the pitch, throw a common objection (e.g., "It's too expensive," "I'm happy with my current provider"). Do they get flustered, or do they try to handle it calmly?
    - **Preparation:** Did they understand the product basics? A candidate who does minimal research has low initiative.

### C. The In-Depth Interview (Face-to-Face/Virtual)

Use behavioral and situational questions. Look for stories that demonstrate key sales traits.

| Quality to Test | Sample Interview Questions | What a Great Answer Looks Like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Resilience & Grit** | "Tell me about a time you failed at something important and how you handled it." | Focuses on what they learned, how they bounced back, and didn't give up. Doesn't blame others. |
| **Persuasion Skills** | "Tell me about a time you had to convince a professor or a group to see things your way." | Describes a clear situation, their persuasive tactics, and a positive outcome. |
| **Handling Rejection** | "How would you feel if a prospect hung up on you? What would you do next?" | Acknowledges the sting but focuses on moving to the next call immediately. Shows emotional control. |
| **Competitiveness & Drive** | "What motivates you more: money, recognition, or learning?" | The best answer often ties all three together, showing ambition and a desire to grow. |
| **Coachability** | "If I told you your sales approach was all wrong, how would you react?" | Shows humility and a willingness to learn. "I'd ask for specific feedback and work on improving immediately." |
| **Goal-Oriented** | "What was the last significant goal you set for yourself and how did you achieve it?" | Provides a specific, measurable goal and a clear plan of action they took to reach it. |

### The Ideal BDE Fresher Profile: A Summary

The perfect candidate will demonstrate a powerful combination of the following:

1. **Unbreakable Resilience:** They see "no" as a step closer to "yes." They are not easily discouraged.
2. **Infectious Enthusiasm:** They are energetic, positive, and that energy is palpable over the phone and in person. People are drawn to them.
3. **Quick Learning Ability:** They can quickly understand your product, your customer, and your sales process.
4. **Strong Communication Skills:** They are articulate, good listeners, and can explain complex things simply.
5. **A Burning Desire to Succeed:** They are intrinsically motivated by achievement, growth, and winning. This is often more important than any specific skill.

**Final Pro-Tip:** During the process, ask yourself: **"Would I buy something from this person?"** If the answer is a confident "yes," you've likely found your candidate.
