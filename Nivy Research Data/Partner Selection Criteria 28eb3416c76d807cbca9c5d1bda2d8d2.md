# Partner Selection Criteria

---

## **1️⃣ Essential Qualification Criteria for Sales Outsourcing / Franchise Partners**

### **A. Personal & Demographic**

- Full name
- Age (e.g., 21+ or as per local regulations)
- Location / City / State / Country (to check market coverage)
- Contact info (phone, email)

### **B. Educational Background**

- Minimum education level (e.g., High School, Graduate, Postgraduate)
- Relevant professional courses (optional, if applicable to your product/service)

### **C. Professional / Business Experience**

- Years of experience in business, sales, or marketing
- Industry experience relevant to your product/service
- Previous ownership or management of business (if franchise model)
- Experience handling teams (if applicable)

### **D. Financial Capability**

- Minimum investment capability (if required for franchise)
- Ability to sustain operations until break-even
- Access to working capital for operational expenses

### **E. Skill-Based**

- Sales & negotiation skills
- Customer relationship management
- Digital literacy / tech-savvy (if using CRM, dashboards, online marketing)
- Communication skills (local language + English, if required)

### **F. Business Setup / Infrastructure (Optional)**

- Office / store location availability (if offline sales required)
- Equipment / tools available (PC, internet, phone)
- Legal entity / registration (if required for tax or franchise purposes)

### **G. Motivation / Alignment**

- Interest in selling your product/service
- Understanding of market demand in their area
- Commitment to your brand’s ethics and code of conduct

### **H. References / Validation (Optional but powerful)**

- Professional references
- Past employer or client references

---

## **2️⃣ Optional Screening Criteria for Digital-First Sales Partners**

- Active presence on social media / digital marketing experience
- Number of leads / clients handled in previous roles
- Experience with online tools (CRM, social media ads, email marketing)
- Track record of achieving sales targets

## **3️⃣ Suggested Form Structure**

**Section 1: Personal Information**

- Name, DOB, contact info, address, LinkedIn (optional)

**Section 2: Education & Professional Experience**

- Highest education completed
- Current or previous job/business role
- Years of relevant experience
- Industry / domain expertise

**Section 3: Financial & Infrastructure**

- Available investment / operational budget
- Office setup / resources
- Ability to handle minimum operational requirements

**Section 4: Skills & Competencies**

- Sales experience / achievements
- Digital / technical proficiency
- Team management experience (if relevant)

**Section 5: Motivation & Alignment**

- Why are you interested in partnering?
- Expected monthly/quarterly target
- Understanding of local market

**Section 6: References (Optional)**

- 1–2 professional references (Name, Contact, Relationship)

**Section 7: Consent & Declaration**

- Consent to share information
- Declaration of truthfulness of info
- Agreement to terms & evaluation

## **1️⃣ Must-Have Tools & Skills for Sales Outsourcing Partners**

These are tools and competencies that allow a partner to **operate independently**:

### **A. CRM & Sales Management**

- **CRM tools** (for lead tracking, reporting, pipeline management)
    - Examples: HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Freshsales
- Ability to generate reports, update deal status, and manage follow-ups

### **B. ERP / Operations Management**

- ERP familiarity (if relevant to your business, e.g., inventory, order tracking, billing)
    - Examples: Tally, SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Odoo

### **C. Communication & Collaboration**

- **Email Management:** Gmail / Outlook, professional email etiquette
- **Messaging & Collaboration:** Slack, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp Business
- **Video Conferencing:** Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams

### **D. Marketing & Lead Generation Tools**

- Social media platforms (Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram) for lead sourcing
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator (if targeting B2B clients)
- Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Drive) for sharing reports & documents
- Optional: Email marketing tools (Mailchimp, SendinBlue)

### **E. Productivity & Tracking**

- Task management tools: Trello, Asana, Monday.com, Notion
- Spreadsheet skills (Excel / Google Sheets) for lead & revenue tracking
- Calendar & scheduling tools (Google Calendar, Calendly)

### **F. Optional Advanced Tools**

- Analytics: Google Analytics, Power BI, Tableau (if analyzing campaigns)
- Marketing automation: HubSpot workflows, ActiveCampaign
- Proposal & e-sign tools: DocuSign, PandaDoc, Canva for presentations

## **2️⃣ Other Skills / Competencies**

These are critical for **smooth onboarding** and immediate productivity:

1. **Sales Experience**
    - Previous experience in B2B / B2C sales
    - Handling multiple clients simultaneously
2. **Communication Skills**
    - Clear verbal & written communication
    - Professional email and chat etiquette
3. **Digital Literacy**
    - Can navigate CRMs, ERPs, and cloud tools with minimal support
    - Can generate reports and interpret basic analytics
4. **Lead Generation Ability**
    - Can research prospects online (LinkedIn, Google, directories)
    - Understands cold outreach via email / phone / social media
5. **Self-Motivation & Discipline**
    - Can work remotely without constant supervision
    - Can follow processes and timelines independently

## **3️⃣ How to Verify Their Skills / Work Proof**

You need **evidence** before onboarding — so you don’t waste time.

### **A. Request Work Samples**

- CRM screenshots (obscure client info if confidential)
- Previous sales reports / dashboards
- Lead lists or pipeline they managed (anonymized)
- Sales presentations or proposals they’ve created

### **B. Ask for References**

- Previous employers, clients, or managers
- Ask specifically about targets achieved, tools used, and reliability

### **C. Small Paid Test Project**

- Assign a short task:
    - Research X leads and enter into CRM
    - Send 5 cold outreach emails/messages (with guidance)
    - Prepare a small sales report
- Evaluate speed, quality, and accuracy

### **D. Interview & Practical Questions**

- Ask them to **demonstrate CRM or tool skills live**
- Ask scenario-based questions:
    - “How would you manage 50 leads per week?”
    - “How would you track follow-ups and lost deals?”

### **E. Certificates or Course Proof (Optional)**

- Some CRMs / tools have official certification (e.g., HubSpot, Salesforce)
- Previous ERP or sales certifications
