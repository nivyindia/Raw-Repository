**What is Nivy OS?**

Nivy OS (Operating System) is the backbone of our entire company. It contains all the core databases that every team uses — departments, projects, SOPs, reports, templates, company documents, and analytics. Think of it like the engine room of a ship: you might not see it from the outside, but everything runs because of it.

## 🗂️ The 8 Core Databases

All database names have been updated to plain English. Click any database below to open it directly.

| # | Database | What It's For | Who Uses It |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 🏢 **departments_database** | Lists every department — goals, heads, KPIs, linked SOPs/projects | All team leads, leadership |
| 2 | 🚀 **projects_database** | Tracks all active and past projects across every division | Project managers, team leads |
| 3 | 📋 **sop_database** | All Standard Operating Procedures — how we do things, step by step | Every team member |
| 4 | 📁 **company_documents_database** | Official documents — contracts, agreements, policies | Leadership, legal, admin |
| 5 | 📊 **reports_database** | All reports — financial, marketing, operations | Managers, leadership |
| 6 | 📌 **templates_database** | Ready-to-use templates for proposals, SOPs, emails, and more | Everyone |
| 7 | 📈 **analytics_database** | Business analytics and performance data | Leadership, growth team |
| 8 | 🗃️ **all_pages_index** | Master index of every page in the workspace by category | Admin, anyone searching |
