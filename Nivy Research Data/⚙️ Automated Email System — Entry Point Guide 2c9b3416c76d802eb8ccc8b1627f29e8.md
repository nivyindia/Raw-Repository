## 1️⃣ Automated Email System (ENTRY POINT)

**Purpose:**

New joiner should start work **without talking to anyone**.

**Includes:**

- Welcome email
- Role overview (1–2 lines)
- Clear statement: *Not a salary job, performance-based*
- Link to **Instruction Page**
- Link to **Join Discord**
- Link to **Tracking Sheet**
- Support contact (only for doubts)

**Tools:**

- Gmail + Filters OR
- Zoho Mail OR
- Google Workspace

---

## 2️⃣ Instruction Page (MOST IMPORTANT)

**This is the brain of your system**

**What this page must contain (short & clear):**

1. Who we are
2. What your role is
3. Daily / Weekly / Monthly tasks
4. Exact outreach process (step-by-step)
5. Scripts & form link
6. Commission & earning structure
7. Rules + non-poaching
8. Rewards & growth path
9. “Start Working Now” section

**Where to build it:**

- Google Sites ✅ (best, free, simple)
- Notion (good, but needs login)
- Simple WordPress page (later)

---

## 3️⃣ Central Resource Folder (Toolkit)

**Purpose:**

Everything downloadable in one place.

**Contains:**

- Outreach scripts (text + PDF)
- Commission sheet
- Daily task checklist
- Code of conduct
- FAQs
- Training PDFs/videos

**Where to store:**

- Google Drive (shared folder, view-only)

---

## 4️⃣ Lead Collection System

**Flow:**

Prospect → Google Form → You

**Includes:**

- Google Form (lead capture)
- Auto response email to lead
- Sheet linked to form

**Tool:**

- Google Forms + Google Sheets

---

## 5️⃣ Pocket Money Earner Tracking Sheet

**They update this daily**

**Columns:**

- Date
- Platform
- Lead Name
- Contact
- Form Shared (Yes/No)
- Form Submitted (Yes/No)
- Status

**Tool:**

- Google Sheets (one master + individual copies later)

---

## 6️⃣ Discord Workspace (Support Only)

**Purpose:**

No DM chaos. Structured help only.

**Channels:**

- 📢 announcements (read-only)
- 📘 instructions
- 🧾 scripts
- ❓ support-questions
- 🏆 leaderboard

---

## 7️⃣ Commission & Rewards System

**Must be visible but simple**

Includes:

- Commission per service
- Monthly earning range
- Bonus milestones
- Certificates
- Team leader eligibility
- Full-time opportunity rule

*(Detailed explanation only inside Instruction Page)*

## 8️⃣ Performance Review System (Lightweight)

**Monthly only**

Tracks:

- Leads sent
- Forms submitted
- Conversions
- Consistency

**Outcome:**

- Continue
- Promote
- Bonus

**Add later when team grows**

- Tasks

- Earnings
- Rewards
- Scripts
- Training

**Tool options:**

- Google Sheets (start)

1. Applicant fills Google Form
2. Auto email is sent
3. Email → Instruction Page
4. Instruction Page → Toolkit + Discord + Tracking Sheet
5. Person starts work **same day**
