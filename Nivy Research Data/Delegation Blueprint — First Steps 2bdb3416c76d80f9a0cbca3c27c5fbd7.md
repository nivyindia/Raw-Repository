- Creating Google Forms
- Creating Google Sheets (trackers)

- Setting up and organising Slack channels
- Creating basic documents
- Uploading content to website (simple edits in Porto theme)

### 🔹 **2. Hiring & Recruitment Support**

They can do all **backend hiring work**:

- Posting job openings everywhere (LinkedIn, Naukri, WhatsApp groups, Internshala, FB groups)

- Gathering applications
- Shortlisting based on simple filters
- Scheduling interviews for you
- Sending reminders to candidates
- Creating interview lists
- Maintaining hiring sheets
- Making sure all positions have applicants
- Contacting more candidates when needed

---

### 🔹 **3. Onboarding Work**

They can handle:

- Sending onboarding forms
- Adding freelancers to Slack
- Sending the rules and guidelines
- Sharing your onboarding documents
- Creating department channels
- Adding department heads to the correct channels
- Creating intro messages
- Maintaining freelancer list
- Updating the “new joiner status” daily

---

### 🔹 **4. Company Support & Small Tasks**

Anything small or repetitive, such as:

- Updating website text/images
- Uploading posts to Instagram/LinkedIn
- Organising files properly
- Preparing lists
- Updating tasks in Trello/ClickUp
- Monitoring attendance / reporting
- Following up with freelancers
- Sending reminders
- Creating reports for you
- Maintaining everything clean and organised

---

**These decisions must be made by YOU.**

But all the heavy work around these decisions → **they will handle.**

## Step 2

# 🧩 **FINAL HIRE ORDER (Simple List)**

1️⃣ **Mini-COO / Operations Manager**

2️⃣ **Freelancer Hiring & Onboarding Lead**

3️⃣ **Project Delivery Lead**

4️⃣ **Marketing & Sales Head**

5️⃣ Support freelancers (lead gen, content, VAs, project freelancers)

---

# 

---

# 🔥 **1. Mini-COO / Operations Manager (FIRST PRIORITY)**

This is the **most critical hire** because:

- They take 70% of your mental load.
- They coordinate *all other departments*.
- They organise documents, Slack, Forms, Website.
- They help hire and manage the other freelancers.
- They make sure rules are followed.
- They become your “brain” and “execution engine.”

**Without this role, the other departments will still bring work back to YOU.**

With a Mini-COO → *You become free immediately.*

👉 **So hire this FIRST.**

---

# 🔥 **2. Freelancer Hiring & Onboarding Lead (SECOND PRIORITY)**

Why second?

Because:

- You need a flow of freelancers coming in.
- They will help you hire the Marketing Head, Delivery Head, etc.
- They set up Slack & onboarding.
- They build the team under the Mini-COO.

This is **essential** for scaling your Global Freelancer Program.

---

# 🔥 **3. Project Delivery Lead (THIRD PRIORITY)**

Once operations & hiring are set, get this person.

They will:

- Manage project freelancers
- Handle client work
- Ensure delivery without you
- Control quality
- Maintain project trackers

This removes another major load from your head.

---

# 🔥 **4. Marketing & Sales Head (FOURTH PRIORITY)**

Once the foundation is ready, hire them.

They will:

- Run lead gen
- Bring clients
- Handle content
- Manage outreach & CRM

This grows the business once the backend team is ready.

---

# ⭐ **Why this order works perfectly**

Because you’re currently:

- Exhausted
- Overwhelmed
- Losing track of systems
- Unable to manage everything

So first you need someone who **stabilizes your internal world**, not someone who brings in more clients and work.

If you hire marketing first → you will get more work → more stress → more chaos.

If you hire project delivery first → they will still ask you 100 questions.

But if you hire **Mini-COO first**, they take the stress away BEFORE growth starts.
