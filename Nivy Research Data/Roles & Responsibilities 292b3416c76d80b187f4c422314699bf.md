### 🏛️ **Corporate & Operations Leadership**

| Role | Responsibilities | Key Tasks / Activities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. CEO / Managing Director** | - Define and lead company vision, mission, and growth strategy- Oversee all departments to ensure alignment with corporate goals- Manage stakeholder, board, and investor relationships- Ensure financial sustainability and corporate governance- Drive innovation, partnerships, and new market opportunities | - Approve strategic plans, budgets, and annual goals- Review performance reports from CXOs and department heads- Meet with board members, investors, and key partners- Approve high-level hiring and policies- Represent the company in major events and negotiations |
| **2. COO / Chief Operating Officer / General Manager** | - Manage daily operations and ensure efficient business processes- Translate strategic goals into operational plans- Supervise Operations, HR, IT, and Administrative teams- Optimize resource utilization and service delivery- Maintain cross-department coordination | - Develop standard operating procedures (SOPs)- Oversee operations budgets and timelines- Approve departmental KPIs and monitor progress- Ensure smooth inter-department workflows- Resolve operational bottlenecks and performance issues |
| **3. Operations Director** | - Implement and monitor the company’s operational strategy- Lead production, service delivery, and logistics teams- Maintain compliance with quality and efficiency standards- Identify and implement process improvements | - Oversee execution of operations plans- Manage departmental schedules and reporting- Coordinate with finance and HR for resource planning- Review project and service KPIs- Conduct audits of operational processes |
| **4. Production / Service Managers** | - Oversee day-to-day service or production delivery- Ensure client satisfaction and timely execution- Manage team leads and field staff- Track quality, productivity, and performance metrics | - Assign daily/weekly work tasks- Conduct quality checks and ensure SLA compliance- Report performance and issues to Operations Director- Resolve client or internal escalations- Maintain logs, schedules, and efficiency reports |
| **5. Team Leads / Supervisors** | - Supervise small teams and ensure task execution- Provide feedback, training, and support to staff- Communicate goals and progress to managers- Maintain discipline, safety, and quality compliance | - Allocate work and monitor progress- Train new staff on SOPs and tools- Conduct daily stand-ups or check-ins- Record performance metrics and submit reports- Handle attendance and shift planning |
| **6. Frontline / Field Staff** | - Execute operational tasks as per SOPs- Maintain service quality and customer satisfaction- Report on issues, challenges, and client feedback- Follow safety and compliance standards | - Deliver assigned projects/services- Complete daily logs or checklists- Communicate updates and client concerns- Attend training sessions- Maintain tools, systems, or client documentation |

---

💡 **Notes for Hiring (Summary):**

- These roles build the **execution backbone** of the organization.
- Ideal hiring flow:
    
    → CEO defines strategy → COO operationalizes → Directors manage execution → Managers ensure delivery → Supervisors and staff implement.
    
- Tools commonly used: ERP (SAP, Odoo), Task Management (Asana, ClickUp), HRMS, and SOP dashboards.
