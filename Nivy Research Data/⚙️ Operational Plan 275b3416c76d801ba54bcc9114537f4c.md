### **Digital Marketing Agency: Operational Plan**

**1.0 Executive Summary**
This operational plan outlines the internal structure, processes, and resources required for [Agency Name] to effectively deliver its digital marketing services. It covers team roles, technology, service delivery workflows, quality control, and key performance indicators (KPIs) to ensure scalability, profitability, and client satisfaction.

**2.0 Agency Structure & Team Roles**
A hybrid structure organized by function and client pods ensures expertise and accountability.

- **Leadership:**
    - **Managing Director/Owner:** Overall strategy, P&L, business development, and key client relationships.
    - **Operations Manager:** Manages workflows, resource allocation, team productivity, and internal systems.
- **Service Delivery (The "Pods"):**
    - **Account Managers (2-3):** Primary client contact. Responsible for communication, strategy presentation, reporting, and upsell. They own the client relationship.
    - **SEO Specialists (2):** Execute technical audits, on-page optimization, and link-building strategies.
    - **PPC Specialist (1):** Manages all paid advertising campaigns (Google, Microsoft, Paid Social).
    - **Content Creators (2):** Include writers and a graphic designer/video editor. Produce blogs, social content, ads creative, and website copy.
    - **Social Media Manager (1):** Develops calendars, creates posts, manages communities, and executes organic strategy.
- **Support:**
    - **Web Developer (Part-time/Contract):** Handles website builds, maintenance, and technical fixes.

**3.0 Technology & Tools Stack**
Investment in the right tools is critical for efficiency and delivery.

- **Project Management:** **Asana** or **ClickUp** for task delegation, timelines, and client portal access.
- **CRM & Sales:** **HubSpot** or **Salesforce** to manage leads, client history, and contracts.
- **Communication:** **Slack** for internal teams; **Zoom** for client meetings.
- **SEO:** **Ahrefs** or **Semrush** (for keyword research, ranking tracking, competitor analysis), **Screaming Frog** (for technical audits).
- **PPC:** Native platforms (Google Ads, Microsoft Advertising) and **Google Analytics 4** for conversion tracking.
- **Social Media:** **Meta Business Suite**, **Buffer** or **Hootsuite** (for scheduling and reporting).
- **Email Marketing:** **Klaviyo** or **Mailchimp** (for execution and automation).
- **Design:** **Canva** (for quick graphics), **Adobe Creative Cloud** (for advanced design and video).
- **Finance:** **QuickBooks Online** for invoicing, expenses, and profitability reporting.

**4.0 Service Delivery Process & Workflows**
A standardized process ensures consistency and quality across all clients.

**Phase 1: Onboarding (Weeks 1-2)**

1. **Kick-off Meeting:** Account Manager, key specialists, and client meet to align on goals, KPIs, and timelines.
2. **Access & Audit:** Client provides all necessary logins. Team conducts initial audits (SEO, PPC, Social) to establish a baseline.
3. **Strategy Document:** Account Manager delivers a customized 90-day plan for client sign-off.

**Phase 2: Monthly Execution Cycle***A recurring 4-week cycle for retainer clients.*

- **Week 1: Planning & Strategy**
    - Account Manager leads an internal briefing on client priorities for the month.
    - Content calendar is finalized.
    - PPC and SEO strategies are updated based on previous month's performance.
- **Week 2-3: Execution**
    - Content team produces and designs assets.
    - SEO Specialist implements on-page changes and pursues links.
    - PPC Specialist launches new ad tests and optimizes campaigns.
    - Social Manager schedules posts and engages with the community.
- **Week 4: Reporting & Analysis**
    - Specialists pull data from all platforms.
    - Account Manager compiles a comprehensive Client Performance Report.
    - **Monthly Client Call:** Account Manager presents report, analyzes results, and proposes next month's strategy.

**Phase 3: Quarterly Business Review (QBR)**

- A deep-dive meeting to review progress against annual goals.
- Present high-level insights and strategic adjustments.
- Discuss contract renewal and potential scope expansion.

**5.0 Quality Assurance & Control**

- **Two-Tier Review:** All client-facing work (content, ads, reports) is reviewed by a senior specialist or the Operations Manager before delivery.
- **Checklists:** Standardized checklists for onboarding, blog publishing, and campaign launch to prevent errors.
- **Client Satisfaction Surveys:** Sent quarterly via email to gauge happiness and gather feedback.
- **Internal Retrospectives:** Monthly team meeting to discuss what went well, what didn’t, and how to improve processes.

**6.0 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
We measure our success internally by tracking these metrics:

- **Client Health:**
    - Client Retention Rate (>85% target)
    - Net Promoter Score (NPS) (>50 target)
    - Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) Growth
- **Operational Efficiency:**
    - Profitability per Client (Revenue - Cost of Delivery)
    - Employee Utilization Rate (Target: ~80%)
    - On-Time Delivery of Projects & Reports (>95%)
- **Service Performance (Averages across all clients):**
    - SEO: Organic Traffic Growth, Keyword Ranking Improvements
    - PPC: Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), Cost Per Lead (CPL)
    - Social: Engagement Rate, Follower Growth

**7.0 Financial Management**

- **Pricing & Invoicing:** All retainers are invoiced in advance on the 1st of the month via QuickBooks. Project-based work requires a 50% deposit.
- **Expense Tracking:** All tool subscriptions and ad spend (on behalf of clients) are tracked and allocated to specific clients for accurate profitability calculation.
- **Profitability Analysis:** The Operations Manager runs a monthly report showing the profit margin for each client, identifying clients that may be under-serviced or unprofitable.

**8.0 Contingency & Risk Management**

- **Client Churn:** Maintain a pipeline of new leads equal to 150% of monthly revenue targets to offset unexpected churn.
- **Key Person Risk:** Cross-train team members on essential tasks. Ensure all client information and processes are documented in a central wiki (e.g., **Notion** or **Confluence**).
- **Platform Changes:** Dedicate time for team members to stay updated on industry changes (e.g., Google algorithm updates, iOS privacy changes) and adapt strategies accordingly.
