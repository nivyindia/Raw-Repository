**Implementation Notes:**

- Prioritize Odoo modules for **CRM, Projects, Accounting, Email Marketing, Website**.
- Keep **external tools for advanced design, communication, analytics, and client engagement**.
- Odoo can reduce the number of subscriptions and centralize workflows for higher perceived professionalism.
