# **Freelance Lead Generator Contract**

### **Step 1: Update the Core Compensation & Payment Section**

- **Replace** any old commission models with the new **Performance-Based Commission Model**.
- **Define the three payment types:**
    1. **Lead Commission:** 10-15% of first contract value for qualified leads that convert to clients.
    2. **Performance Bonuses:** 5-10% bonus for exceeding monthly lead targets.
    3. **Retainer Upsell Bonus:** 5% additional commission for converting project work to retainer contracts.
- **Specify Payment Terms:**
    - Processed within **15 days of client's first payment receipt**.
    - Made in **INR** via bank transfer.
    - Company reserves the right to qualify/disqualify leads based on BANT criteria.

### **Step 2: Add the New "Pricing and Commission Structure" Clause**

- **Independent Pricing:** Company sets client pricing independently based on market rates and value delivered.
- **Business Model Acknowledgement:** Associate explicitly agrees that company operates on a **service delivery model** where lead generation is separate from service execution.

### **Step 3: Add Critical Operational & Protection Clauses**

- **Lead Assignment:** Company has the **sole right** to assign lead territories or industries; **no minimum lead quota** guaranteed.
- **Lead Qualification Control:** Company can **reject or accept leads** based on BANT criteria at its discretion.
- **Client Scope Limitation:** Associate **cannot negotiate** directly with clients on pricing or service scope.
- **Non-Solicitation Clause:** Associate cannot solicit company clients or converted leads for **24 months** after termination.
- **Lead Ownership Clause:** All leads and client relationships **belong to company** once introduced.
- **Performance Metrics Clause:** Define metrics (lead conversion rate, lead quality score, monthly targets).
- **"No Minimum Earnings" Disclaimer:** Explicitly state this is performance-based with **no guaranteed income**.
- **Client Transition Clause:** Upon termination, associate must smoothly **transfer all lead conversations and CRM access** to company.
- **Non-Disparagement Clause:** Associate cannot disparage company, clients, or sales processes.
- **"At-Will Lead Assignment":** Company can **pause, reduce, or change** lead territories at its sole discretion.
- **Communication SLA:** Mandate lead submission within **24 hours** of qualification and response times of **2 hours** for company communications.
- **CRM Tracking Obligation:** Associate must use company CRM systems to track and report all lead activities.

### **Step 4: Add the "Performance Security Deposit" Clause**

- **Withhold 10%** of all commissions as **Performance Security Deposit**.
- **Release in Full** only upon successful contract completion, smooth lead transition, and no contract breaches.
- **Forfeit in Full** if associate solicits clients, discloses commission structures, or breaches non-circumvention terms.
- Include **Sales Performance Bond Acknowledgement** and **Arbitration Clause** for disputes.

### **Step 5: Enhance Attractiveness with Benefits & Growth**

- **Add a "Growth Opportunities & Benefits" Section:**
    - **Sales Skill Development:** Free access to sales training, closing techniques, and negotiation workshops.
    - **Career Advancement:** Clear path from Lead Generator to Senior Closer to Sales Manager roles.
    - **Performance Recognition:** "Top Closer" monthly bonuses, "Lead Quality Champion" awards.
    - **Brand Association:** Official "Business Development Associate" designation.
    - **Resource Access:** Free access to premium lead generation tools and databases (for top performers).
- **Portfolio Usage Rights:** Allow associates to showcase conversion rates and deal sizes in their portfolio (with company approval).
- **Testimonial Exchange:** Company provides sales performance certificates and LinkedIn recommendations.
- **Create a "Why Join Our Sales Team?" Section** summarizing benefits.

### **Step 6: Final Bulletproofing Clauses**

- **Business Model Protection:** Associate must **not disclose commission structures or lead sources** to clients or competitors.
- **White-Label Sales Requirement:** All sales activities must represent **company brand only**; freelancer status is confidential.
- **Non-Circumvention - Sales Model:** For 24 months post-termination, associate cannot work with **any leads introduced to company**.
- **Brand Value Contribution:** Acknowledge that client conversions depend on company's **service reputation, case studies, and delivery capability**, not just lead generation efforts.

## **Separate COMMISSION SCHEDULE Document**

**LEAD COMMISSION STRUCTURE:**

- Website Development Projects: 12% of first contract value
- SEO Retainers: 10% of 6-month contract value
- Social Media Management: 8% of 3-month contract value
- Digital Advertising: 15% of first month's ad spend + setup fee

**PERFORMANCE BONUSES:**

- Exceed monthly target by 25%: +5% bonus on all commissions
- Exceed monthly target by 50%: +10% bonus on all commissions
- Lead quality score >90%: +5% bonus on converted leads
- Project to retainer conversion: +5% additional commission

**LEAD QUALIFICATION CRITERIA (BANT):**

- **Budget:** Minimum ₹50,000 project size
- **Authority:** Decision-maker contact
- **Need:** Specific marketing challenges
- **Timeline:** Ready to start within 30 days
