## **1. Risk Management Philosophy**

Nivy Nexus follows a **risk-prevention-first approach**.

Our goal is not to react to disputes, but to **reduce the probability of risk before money, goods, or commitments move**.

We manage risk through:

- Verification
- Documentation clarity
- Staged execution
- Clear role boundaries

> Most B2B disputes happen due to ambiguity. Our systems are built to remove ambiguity early.
> 

---

## **2. Key Risk Areas We Address**

### **A. Supplier Risk**

- Fake or shell suppliers
- Over-commitment beyond capacity
- Unrealistic pricing
- Poor delivery discipline

### **B. Buyer Risk**

- Non-serious or speculative buyers
- Payment delays or defaults
- Unclear or changing requirements

### **C. Transaction Risk**

- Miscommunication
- Documentation gaps
- Cross-border compliance mismatches
- Logistics responsibility confusion

---

## **3. Risk Mitigation Mechanisms**

- Multi-stage supplier verification
- Buyer credibility screening
- Requirement feasibility checks
- Sample & specification confirmation
- Clear payment milestone alignment
- Written confirmations at each stage

---

## **4. Payment Risk Handling**

- Payment terms are defined **before order confirmation**
- No blind payment routing
- Milestone-based payment recommendations
- No pressure on either party to release funds prematurely

> Nivy Nexus coordinates payment logic but does not force or hold funds unless explicitly agreed.
> 

---

## **5. Dispute Handling Approach**

When issues arise, Nivy Nexus follows a **structured resolution approach**:

### **Step 1: Clarification & Documentation Review**

- Review agreed terms
- Identify deviation points

### **Step 2: Mediation**

- Neutral facilitation between buyer and supplier
- Focus on practical resolution, not blame

### **Step 3: Corrective Alignment**

- Revised timelines or scope (if mutually agreed)
- Ethical exit if resolution is not feasible

---

## **6. What We Do NOT Do in Disputes**

- We do not take legal ownership of disputes
- We do not enforce payments or penalties
- We do not replace legal or arbitration bodies

Our role is **facilitative, not judicial**.

---

## **7. Force Majeure & External Risks**

Nivy Nexus acknowledges risks beyond control, including:

- Logistics disruptions
- Customs delays
- Regulatory changes
- Natural events

Such risks are communicated transparently, and parties are advised to plan contingencies.

---

## **8. Escalation & Exit Policy**

- Ethical exit is preferred over forced continuation
- Non-performing parties may be removed from the network
- Repeat violations result in permanent disqualification

---

## **9. Why This Matters (India & UAE Context)**

- Cross-border legal enforcement is complex
- Cultural and compliance differences exist
- Payment cycles vary significantly

**Our risk framework protects relationships and capital.**
