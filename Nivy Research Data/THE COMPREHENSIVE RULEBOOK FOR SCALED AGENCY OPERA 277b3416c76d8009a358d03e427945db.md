### **SECTION 0: CORE PHILOSOPHY & DEFINITIONS**

- **0.1 Philosophy:** This agency operates as a centralized command center. All client-facing elements are a unified brand. All workers are replaceable components within a system. The system itself is the product.
- **0.2 Definitions:**
    - **The Agency / Central Command:** The management company (you). Owner of brand, processes, and client relationships.
    - **Closer:** A commission-only sales agent. Their only role is to convert assigned leads into signed contracts.
    - **Freelancer:** A project-based executor. Their only role is to complete predefined tasks for approved milestones.
    - **Project Manager (PM):** A salaried employee of The Agency. The primary client contact after sale and the manager of all freelancers.
    - **Lead:** A potential client, generated and owned solely by The Agency.

---

### **SECTION 1: CENTRAL COMMAND PROTOCOLS (YOUR NON-NEGOTIABLE DUTIES)**

**1.1. Lead Generation & CRM Management:**

- **1.1.1.** All leads must be generated through channels owned by The Agency (e.g., `agency.com` website, Agency LinkedIn profile, Agency-run ads).
- **1.1.2.** Every lead must be instantly logged in the central CRM ([e.g., HubSpot CRM]) with tags for source, value, and assigned status.
- **1.1.3.** The lead assignment algorithm is as follows:
    - New leads are distributed via a round-robin system to closers marked "Active".
    - If a closer does not make first contact within **4 business hours**, the lead is automatically reassigned.
    - Unresponsive leads are recycled into a "Nurture" sequence owned by The Agency.

**1.2. The Agency Playbook (The Single Source of Truth):**

- **1.2.1.** The Playbook is a digital, password-protected document (e.g., Notion, Coda). Access is revoked upon termination.
- **1.2.2.** **Volume I: Sales:** Contains the exact call script, email templates, price list, objection handling guide, and eligibility criteria (e.g., "Do not sell SEO to clients with a website under 5 pages").
- **1.2.3.** **Volume II: Service Delivery:** Contains for each service (e.g., "SEO Package Alpha"):
    - **Deliverables List:** Exact number and type of outputs (e.g., "5 x 1500-word blog posts").
    - **Tools:** Mandatory software to be used (e.g., "Ahrefs for keyword research, Grammarly for editing").
    - **Reporting Template:** The exact format and KPIs for client reports (e.g., "Google Data Studio dashboard sent every 30th of the month").
    - **Quality Checklist:** A step-by-step list for PMs to approve freelancer work.
- **1.2.4.** **Volume III: Brand & Voice:** Contains approved email sign-offs, bios, profile pictures for code names, and a list of prohibited words.

**1.3. Financial Control:**

- **1.3.1.** All client contracts must use The Agency's standard template, be signed via The Agency's e-signature tool ([e.g., DocuSign]), and include clauses that work is performed by "team members & partners" of The Agency.
- **1.3.2.** All client invoices must be sent from The Agency's accounting software ([e.g., QuickBooks Online]).
- **1.3.3.** A standardized "Freelancer Rate Card" will be maintained. E.g., "1500-word SEO blog post: $45".

---

### **SECTION 2: CLOSER AGREEMENT & PROTOCOLS**

**2.1. Onboarding:**

- **2.1.1.** Must sign a **Independent Contractor Agreement** with strict NDA and Non-Solicit clauses (minimum 24 months).
- **2.1.2.** Must complete a mandatory 60-minute training video dissecting the Sales Playbook.
- **2.1.3.** Must pass a 20-question multiple-choice test with a 100% pass rate. Two attempts allowed.

**2.2. Operational Rules:**

- **2.2.1.** Will be provided with a dedicated Agency email alias (e.g., `alex.wright@agency.com`) configured to forward to their personal email. **All client emails must be sent from this alias.**
- **2.2.2.** Will be provided with a Agency VoIP number ([e.g., OpenPhone]) for all client calls. Personal numbers are strictly prohibited.
- **2.2.3.** **The Script is Law.** The provided call script must be followed. Deviation based on "feel" is grounds for immediate deactivation.
- **2.2.4.** All sales calls must be recorded. The recording must be uploaded to a designated Google Drive folder named "[Client Name] - [Date]" within **1 hour** of the call ending.
- **2.2.5.** The use of any communication channel not pre-approved by The Agency (e.g., WhatsApp, personal SMS) is forbidden.

**2.3. Commission & Offboarding:**

- **2.3.1.** Commission is calculated as a percentage of the first invoice value that successfully clears The Agency's bank account (e.g., 10%).
- **2.3.2.** Commission is paid on the 15th of the month for all clients who paid in the previous month.
- **2.3.3.** If a client defaults or requests a refund within 90 days, the commission paid will be deducted from the closer's next payment(s).
- **2.3.4.** Upon deactivation, all access (email, CRM, Playbook) is revoked immediately. The Agency retains all rights to leads and client relationships.

---

### **SECTION 3: FREELANCER AGREEMENT & PROTOCOLS**

**3.1. Onboarding & Vetting:**

- **3.1.1.** Must sign the **Independent Contractor Agreement**.
- **3.1.2.** Must complete a **paid test project** based on a real task from the Service Delivery Playbook. The test is paid only upon successful completion to standard.
- **3.1.3.** Must be onboarded to The Agency's Project Management tool ([e.g., Asana]), Time Tracking tool ([e.g., Harvest]), and Communication tool ([e.g., Slack]).

**3.2. Work Protocols:**

- **3.2.1.** **Absolute Non-Communication Clause:** Any freelancer who contacts a client directly for any reason will be terminated immediately and will forfeit all unpaid earnings.
- **3.2.2.** All work must be submitted through the PM tool. The submission must comment "@PM [Name]" and include a link to the time-tracked entry.
- **3.2.3.** All work must be pre-approved by the PM using the Quality Checklist before it is ever seen by the client.
- **3.2.4.** Revisions requested by the PM must be completed within the originally agreed timeframe of the milestone.

**3.3. Payment:**

- **3.3.1.** Freelancers must submit their predefined milestones for each project into Asana for PM approval *before* work begins.
- **3.3.2.** Payment for a milestone is processed only after the PM marks both the milestone and the associated time-tracked entry as "Approved".
- **3.3.3.** Freelancer invoices are paid on a Net 7 terms from the date of PM approval.

### **SECTION 4: THE PROJECT MANAGER (PM) - THE CRITICAL HIRE**

**4.1. Role Definition:** The PM is the **human embodiment of The Agency brand** after the sale. This is a salaried, full-time position.

**4.2. Core Responsibilities:**

- **Client Facing:**
    - Receive the warm handoff from the closer via a standardized email intro.
    - Conduct the official client onboarding call.
    - Send all reports and updates to the client.
    - Handle all client questions, complaints, and requests.
- **Internal Facing:**
    - Assign approved tasks to freelancers based on the Service Blueprint.
    - **Quality Control:** Review all freelancer work against the checklist before sending it to the client.
    - Approve freelancer milestones and initiate payment processing.
    - Be the sole point of communication for freelancers regarding client work.

This framework is designed to be your operational bible. Enforce it without exception to build a scalable, brand-safe, and profitable machine.

### **Agency Rulebook: The Commission-Only Operating System**

**Objective:** To scale client acquisition and service delivery rapidly using a distributed workforce, while maintaining absolute brand control, quality, and profitability. All operatives must adhere to these rules without exception.

---

### **Section 1: The Central Command Rules (Your Responsibilities)**

1. **Lead Generation & Ownership**
    - **Rule 1.1:** The Agency is the sole generator of all leads. Methods include paid ads, content marketing, referrals, or lead lists. No closer or freelancer is permitted to generate their own leads for the Agency.
    - **Rule 1.2:** All leads are property of the Agency and must be entered into the central CRM ([e.g., HubSpot, Salesforce]) immediately upon acquisition.
    - **Rule 1.3:** You, or a designated Lead Manager, will assign leads to closers based on capacity, performance, or specialty.
2. **Pricing & Packaging**
    - **Rule 1.4:** The Agency sets all pricing. A definitive "Price List" and service package menu will be provided to all closers. No discounts or alterations to pricing are permitted without written approval from management.
3. **The Sacred Process: The Agency Playbook**
    - **Rule 1.5:** The "Agency Playbook" is the ultimate source of truth. It contains:
        - **Brand Voice Bible:** Approved language, tone, and key messaging.
        - **The Master Sales Script:** The exact script for phone calls, including opening, qualifying questions, handling objections, and closing.
        - **The Pitch Deck:** The only approved presentation for sales calls.
        - **Email Templates:** Pre-written templates for follow-ups, proposals, and onboarding.
        - **Service Blueprints:** For every service offered, a detailed list of deliverables for each package (e.g., "SEO Starter Package includes: 4 blog posts, 5 technical fixes, monthly report").
    - **Rule 1.6:** Access to leads is contingent on passing a 100% score on a test covering the Playbook.

---

### **Section 2: Rules for the Commission-Only Closer**

1. **Identity & Representation**
    - **Rule 2.1:** Closers will use a code name (e.g., "Alex Wright") provided by the Agency. Real names are not to be used with clients.
    - **Rule 2.2:** Closers represent themselves as an "Account Executive" or "Growth Consultant" of [Agency Name]. They must never reveal they are a third-party freelancer.
2. **The Sales Process**
    - **Rule 2.3:** The Master Sales Script must be followed verbatim. Deviation is grounds for immediate termination and forfeiture of all commissions.
    - **Rule 2.4:** All sales calls must be conducted via a recorded line (e.g., Zoom recording enabled). The recording link must be sent to a designated Agency folder within 1 hour of the call's conclusion.
    - **Rule 2.5:** All communication must use Agency-owned channels:
        - Email must be sent from a designated Agency email address (e.g., `closer@agency.com`) via a shared inbox tool (e.g., Front, Missive).
        - Calls should use a Agency-owned VoIP number (e.g., Twilio) that forwards to their personal phone.
    - **Rule 2.6:** The closer's role ends immediately upon contract signing. They must introduce the client to the Project Manager via a pre-written email template and then cease all direct communication.
3. **Commission & Payment**
    - **Rule 2.7:** Commission is paid only after the client has signed the contract **and** paid the first invoice.
    - **Rule 2.8:** The commission rate is a fixed percentage of the first payment (e.g., 10%). The rate structure is non-negotiable.
    - **Rule 2.9:** If a client churns within a [90-day] refund period, the commission will be clawed back from the closer's future earnings.

---

### **Section 3: Rules for the Execution Freelancer**

1. **Onboarding & Vetting**
    - **Rule 3.1:** All freelancers must pass a paid test project. The project must be delivered exactly to the specifications in the Agency Playbook's Service Blueprint.
    - **Rule 3.2:** All freelancers must sign a comprehensive Independent Contractor Agreement that includes strict NDA, Non-Solicit, and Non-Compete clauses.
2. **Work Delivery & Communication**
    - **Rule 3.3:** All work must be delivered through the Agency's Project Management tool (e.g., Asana, ClickUp). No direct deliveries to the client.
    - **Rule 3.4:** **Zero direct communication with the client is permitted.** All communication must go through the Project Manager. Freelancers are invisible to the client.
    - **Rule 3.5:** Freelancers must use time-tracking software ([e.g., Harvest, Clockify]) for all work, with tasks described in detail.
3. **Milestones & Payment**
    - **Rule 3.6:** Before work begins, the freelancer must break down the project into clear, predefined milestones in the PM tool. Each milestone must have a strict definition of "Done" (e.g., "Client approves final design").
    - **Rule 3.7:** The Project Manager must approve a milestone as "Complete" before payment is released.
    - **Rule 3.8:** Payment for a milestone will be processed within [Net 7] days of Agency approval. Late or substandard work delays payment.

---

### **Section 4: The Project Manager (PM) - The Keystone Role**

- **Rule 4.1:** The PM is the first and most critical hire. They are a **salaried employee** of the Agency.
- **Rule 4.2:** The PM is the **single point of contact** for the client after the sale. They own the relationship.
- **Rule 4.3:** The PM's responsibilities include:
    - Receiving the handoff from the closer.
    - Onboarding the client.
    - Briefing the freelancer using the Service Blueprint.
    - Reviewing all freelancer work **before** it is sent to the client.
    - Managing all client communication, expectations, and reporting.
    - Approving milestones and triggering freelancer payments.
    - Conducting weekly/bi-weekly calls with the client.

---

### **Section 5: Enforcement & Consequences**

- **Rule 5.1:** A single violation of the identity, communication, or process rules (e.g., direct client contact, not using a script) results in immediate removal from the platform and forfeiture of all outstanding earnings.
- **Rule 5.2:** consistently poor performance (e.g., low close rate, poor client feedback on delivered work) results in removal from the active roster.
- **Rule 5.3:** Attempting to solicit a Agency client for direct business is a direct violation of the contract and will result in legal action.

By adhering to this system, you control the brand, the process, the money, and the client relationship. The freelancers and closers become plug-and-play components in your machine, allowing for scale without sacrificing control.
