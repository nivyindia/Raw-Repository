## 📜 Vidai SOP — Step by Step

1. **Pheras complete** — Ashirwad ke baad
2. **Dulhan bade logo ke pair chhuye** — maa-papa, daada-daadi
3. **Saheliyon se mili** — emotional!
4. **Chawal haath mein liya** — peeth peeche phekne ke liye
5. **Car tak aaye** — bhai ya saheli saath
6. **Chawal pheka** — ghar ki samriddhi ke liye
7. **Phoolon ki varsha** — mehmaanon ne kiye
8. **Car mein baithi** — photographer shot!
9. **Car rowaana** — Dulhe ke ghar ke liye
10. **Greh Pravesh** — Dulhe ke ghar mein swagat → Greh Pravesh page dekhe

> 💡 **Ek khaas baat:** Dulha aur Dulhan dono ko bolo ki is moment ko poora jeeye — camera ke liye nahi, dil ke liye.
