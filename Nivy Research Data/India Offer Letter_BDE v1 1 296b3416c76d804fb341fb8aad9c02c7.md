**2.6. Clawback Policy** 

All incentives and commissions are paid on **realized revenue** (i.e., after the client's payment is successfully received by the Company). If a client cancels a contract or fails to make a payment within **90 days** of the incentive payout, the Company reserves the right to claw back the corresponding incentive amount from your future payouts.

**2.7. Incentive Payout Timing** 

Incentives and commissions earned in a given calendar month will be calculated after the month-end closing process. The calculated amount will be paid out along with the fixed salary of the **following month**, subject to the client's payment being fully realized by the Company.
