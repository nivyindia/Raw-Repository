### **1. Policy Administration**

**Policy Purpose & Objectives**

This Sales Compensation & Incentive Policy is established by Billion Dreams United ("the Company"), operating under the brand name Nivy, to achieve the following objectives:

- **Align Interests:** Directly link the efforts and achievements of our sales team with the strategic growth and revenue goals of Nivy.
- **Attract & Retain Talent:** Offer a competitive, transparent, and rewarding compensation structure to attract and retain top-performing sales professionals.
- **Drive Performance:** Motivate the sales team to exceed targets through clear, fair, and achievable incentives.
- **Ensure Clarity & Transparency:** Provide a definitive framework that ensures every team member understands how their performance translates into earnings, minimizing disputes and fostering a culture of trust.

**Scope & Eligibility**

This policy applies to all full-time, commissioned sales employees of Billion Dreams United (Nivy) whose primary responsibilities involve direct revenue generation. Eligibility for the variable pay components (commissions, bonuses) outlined in this policy is contingent upon active employment in a designated sales role at the time of payout.

- **Primary Eligible Roles Include:**
    - Sales Development Representatives (SDRs)
    - Account Executives (AEs)
    - Regional Sales Managers
    - Key Account Managers
- Specific compensation plans and targets will be customized for each role and communicated individually.

**Review Cycle**

This policy is designed to be dynamic and aligned with our business goals. It will undergo a formal review and potential amendment on an **annual basis**, coinciding with the Company's fiscal year planning cycle for the following year.

- The official review period will be **Q4 (October - December)** of each calendar year.
- Ad-hoc reviews may be conducted in response to significant market shifts, structural reorganization, or changes in business strategy. Any mid-cycle changes will be communicated in accordance with the **Policy Governance** section.

### **2. Definitions**

- **MRT (Monthly Revenue Target):** The pre-defined revenue goal an employee is expected to achieve in a calendar month, as set forth in the "Sales Target & Incentive Schedule."
- **QRT (Quarterly Revenue Target):** The pre-defined revenue goal for a fiscal quarter, typically a multiple of the MRT, as set forth in the "Sales Target & Incentive Schedule."
- **Realized Revenue:** Revenue from a client deal that is fully received and processed by the Company's finance department. Only Realized Revenue qualifies for commission payouts.
- **SPIFFs:** Short-term, special incentive contests designed to promote specific products, services, or strategic initiatives over a defined period

**Part 2: Compensation Structure**

**2.1 Total Compensation Components**

The total compensation for an eligible sales employee consists of two primary components:

1. A **Fixed Base Salary**, and
2. A **Variable Incentive Pay**.
    
    Together, these components make up the employee's Total On-Target Earnings (OTE).
    

**2.2 Fixed Base Salary**

The Fixed Base Salary is a guaranteed amount paid periodically (typically monthly) as per the company's standard payroll cycle. This component is not contingent upon the achievement of sales targets and is subject to applicable statutory deductions.

**2.3 Variable Incentive Pay**

The Variable Incentive Pay is a performance-linked earnings component calculated based on the achievement and/or exceedance of pre-defined sales targets and key performance indicators (KPIs), as outlined in **Annexure A: Sales Target & Incentive Schedule**. This pay is "at-risk" and is earned solely upon the fulfillment of the policy's terms.

**2.4 On-Target Earnings (OTE) Definition**

On-Target Earnings (OTE) represents the total annual compensation an employee can expect to earn upon achieving 100% of their assigned quotas and targets.

- **Formula:** OTE = Fixed Base Salary + Target Variable Incentive at 100% Quota Achievement.
- **Example:** An employee with a base salary of ₹600,000 per year and a target variable incentive of ₹400,000 has an OTE of ₹1,000,000.
- **OTE Realization:** Actual earnings may be lower or higher than OTE based on performance. The OTE is a reference point and not a guaranteed income.

### **Part 3: Performance & Metrics**

**3.1 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

Incentive calculations will be primarily based on the achievement of pre-defined Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). The primary KPI for this policy is **Revenue Generated** from qualified sales. Additional KPIs, such as the number of new logos, customer retention rates, or product-specific targets, may be introduced and will be explicitly detailed in **Annexure A**.

**3.2 Revenue Targets & Sales Quotas**

- **Monthly Revenue Target (MRT):** The predetermined revenue amount an employee is expected to achieve in a calendar month.
- **Quarterly Revenue Target (QRT):** The predetermined revenue amount an employee is expected to achieve in a calendar quarter.
    
    Specific MRT and QRT figures for each role are defined in **Annexure A**. These quotas are designed to be fair, attainable, and aligned with business objectives.
    

**3.3 Measurement Periods**

Performance against targets is measured over standard calendar periods:

- **Monthly Cycle:** From the 1st to the last day of the calendar month.
- **Quarterly Cycle:** From the 1st day of the quarter to the last (e.g., Jan 1 - Mar 31).
    
    Incentives are calculated at the end of each measurement period.
    

**3.4 Link to Annexure A (The Dynamic Schedule)**

All specific targets, rates, special incentives, and active KPIs for a given period are contained within **Annexure A: Sales Target & Incentive Schedule**. This annexure is a dynamic document that will be updated periodically. The latest published version of Annexure A is binding and forms an integral part of this Policy. Employees are responsible for reviewing the latest version of Annexure A.

### **Part 4: Incentive Calculation & Rules**

**4.1 Commission Rates, Tiers & Accelerators**

- **Commission Rates:** The standard percentage of revenue paid out as incentive. Base rates are defined in **Annexure A**.
- **Tiered Structure:** Incentives may be calculated using a tiered structure, where higher achievement unlocks a higher commission rate on revenue within that tier.
- **Accelerators:** For performance significantly exceeding quota (e.g., over 100%), an accelerated commission rate may apply to revenue beyond the threshold, as specified in **Annexure A**. This is designed to reward over-achievement.

**4.2 Bonus Structures & SPIFFs/Contests**

- **Bonuses:** Lump-sum payments for achieving specific, pre-defined milestones (e.g., closing the largest deal of the quarter, achieving 110% of quota). Details will be communicated in **Annexure A** or via separate official memos.
- **SPIFFs (Sales Performance Incentive Funds):** Short-term, limited-time incentives to promote specific products, clear inventory, or drive specific behaviors. Rules, eligibility, and payout amounts for SPIFFs will be communicated separately and are subject to their specific terms.

**4.3 Sales Crediting & Territory/Account Assignment**

- **Crediting:** Sales revenue is credited to the individual sales employee who is the officially assigned "Owner" of the opportunity in the company's CRM system at the time of the deal's closure.
- **Territory/Assignment:** Territories, accounts, and leads are assigned at the company's discretion. Changes to assignments will be communicated formally.

**4.4 House Account Policy**

- Certain strategic accounts ("House Accounts") may be designated and managed directly by the company or leadership. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, no sales incentive will be paid on revenue generated from House Accounts.

**4.5 Split Commission Rules**

- In cases where two or more employees legitimately collaborate on a single deal, a commission split may be applied.
- A **Split Commission Form** must be completed and signed by all parties and their manager *before* the deal is closed. Management reserves the right to approve all split arrangements.

**4.6 Deal Registration Process**

- Employees must register qualifying sales opportunities in the company's designated CRM system according to the established process and timelines to be eligible for incentive credit.
- Deal registration helps in tracking and protects the employee's claim to the opportunity.

**Part 5: Payout Terms & Conditions**

**5.1 Revenue Recognition Rules**

- A sale is considered "complete" and revenue is eligible for incentive calculation only upon the company's formal recognition of revenue.
- This typically occurs when:
    - A signed contract or agreement is fully executed by both parties.
    - The first payment associated with the deal is received by the company.
    - The product/service is delivered or the project is officially kicked off as per the company's operational process.
- For multi-payment deals, incentives are calculated and paid only on the revenue that has been actually collected by the company.

**5.2 Payout Schedule & Method**

- Incentives earned in a given calendar month will be calculated after the month-end close and paid along with the employee's regular base salary in the **following month**.
- All incentives will be paid via the company's standard payroll process and are subject to applicable income tax and other statutory deductions.

**5.3 Impact of Leave & Absence on Quotas**

- **Paid Leave:** If an employee is on any form of company-approved paid leave for more than 5 consecutive business days in a measurement period, their sales targets (MRT/QRT) for that period will be prorated accordingly.
- **Unpaid Leave:** During any period of unpaid leave, no incentives will accrue, and sales targets will be prorated.

**5.4 Hold Policies**

- The company may place an incentive "on hold" if a deal is under internal review, suspected of violating company policy, or if the client payment is significantly overdue.
- Payouts will be released once the review is satisfactorily completed or payment is received.

**5.5 Clawback/Recovery Provisions**

- If a client terminates a contract and a full or partial refund is issued, the corresponding incentive paid to the employee for that deal will be recovered ("clawed back").
- The clawback will be deducted from the employee's future incentive payouts or salary. If no future payouts are due, the employee is required to repay the net amount to the company.

**5.6 Payout Conditions upon Termination/Resignation**

- To be eligible for any final incentive payout, an employee must be in good standing (e.g., not terminated for cause) at the time of their departure.
- The company will pay out all earned and validated incentives for deals where revenue was recognized and collected by the company up to the employee's last working day.
- This final payout will be processed along with the employee's full and final settlement.

**Part 6: Governance & Administration**

**6.1 Quota Assignment & Territory Protection**

- Sales quotas and territories are assigned at the sole discretion of the company's Sales Leadership, with the goal of ensuring fair and equal opportunity.
- The company will make reasonable efforts to protect assigned territories and accounts. However, the company reserves the right to reassign territories, accounts, or quotas at any time based on business needs. In the event of a reassignment, pipeline and commission handling will be communicated at that time.

**6.2 Performance Reviews & Improvement**

- Performance against the targets in this Policy will be formally reviewed on a quarterly basis between the employee and their manager.
- Consistent underperformance (e.g., below 80% of quota for two consecutive quarters) may result in the employee being placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP), which could affect continued employment.

**6.3 Dispute Resolution Process**

- Any dispute regarding incentive calculation or payment must be submitted in writing to the employee's direct manager and a designated HR representative within **15 calendar days** of the payout date.
- The dispute must clearly state the nature of the discrepancy and include all supporting documentation (e.g., CRM records, deal documents). Disputes raised after the 15-day window will not be considered.
- The Sales Leadership and HR will investigate and provide a written resolution within 15 business days. Their decision will be final and binding.

**6.4 Policy Amendment Procedure**

- Billion Dreams United(Nivy) reserves the right to amend, modify, or discontinue this Policy or any part of it, including **Annexure A**, at any time.
- For material changes to the *main Policy*, the company will provide a minimum of **30 days' notice** to affected employees.
- Changes to **Annexure A** (e.g., targets, rates, specials) will be communicated via an updated published version, effective from the stated "Effective Period."

**6.5 Plan Interpretation & Final Authority**

- In the event of any ambiguity or dispute regarding the interpretation of this Policy, the interpretation of the Sales Leadership and HR, as approved by the company's management, shall be final and binding.

**6.6 Confidentiality**

- The specific terms, conditions, and structures outlined in this Policy and its Annexure A are confidential and proprietary to [Company Name]. Employees are strictly prohibited from disclosing this information to any third party, including competitors.

**6.7 Governing Law**

- This Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of [State/Country], and any legal disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in [City, State/Country].

**6.8 Record Keeping**

- The company will maintain records related to sales performance and incentive calculations for a period of at least two years. Employees are also encouraged to maintain their own records.
