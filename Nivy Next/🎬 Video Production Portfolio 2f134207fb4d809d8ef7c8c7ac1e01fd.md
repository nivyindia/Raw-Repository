## 🎥 **Case Study 1 — Dollar Shave Club Viral Launch Video**

### 📌 Client & Project

**Brand:** Dollar Shave Club

**Video Type:** Viral brand video & launch campaign

### 🎯 Objective

Build awareness in a highly competitive razor market and drive initial sales and subscriptions.

### ⚠️ Challenge

Dollar Shave Club entered a space dominated by giants like Gillette — they needed *brand visibility and instant engagement* with limited marketing budget.

### 🛠 Solution

Created a **professionally edited, humorous, personality‑driven video** communicating product value quickly and memorably.

### 📈 Impact / Results

- The video went **viral with millions of views** soon after launch.
- **12,000+ orders** came in within 48 hours of release.
- The company grew into a *$1 billion brand* acquired by Unilever. ([Viral Idea Marketing](https://www.viralideamarketing.com/post/case-studies-companies-that-grew-their-brand-with-professional-video-editing?utm_source=chatgpt.com))
