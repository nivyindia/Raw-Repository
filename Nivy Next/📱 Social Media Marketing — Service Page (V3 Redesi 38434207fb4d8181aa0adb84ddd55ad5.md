# 16. FAQ

**Which platforms should we be on?**

We recommend platforms based on where your actual buyers spend time, not which platforms are trending. This is decided during the audit.

**Do you create the visual content too?**

Yes — copy, creative direction, and visual assets are part of every package, produced by our design team.

**How do you measure success beyond likes and followers?**

We report on engagement quality, reach, and where applicable, leads or inquiries attributable to social content.

**Can you manage community responses in our brand voice?**

Yes — we build a documented voice guide with you before managing any community interactions.

**Do you run paid social too?**

Organic social management is this service. Paid social campaigns are handled under our Social Media Advertising service — often paired together.

**What if we already have an internal social person?**

We can support them with strategy, content production capacity, or take over specific platforms — whichever fits your team structure.
