## 🧩 7️⃣ GOVERNANCE & REVIEW LOOP (GOVERNANCE SOP)

Governance defines *how decisions are made, reviewed, and improved* within your organization.

It ensures that **your digital marketing agency stays compliant, data-driven, and performance-focused**, even at 2000+ employee scale.

---

### 🔹 PURPOSE

To maintain organizational discipline, performance visibility, and continuous improvement through **structured review cycles, audits, and reporting systems**.

---

### 🔹 GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE OVERVIEW

```
BOARD & CEO
   ↓
EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP (CXOs)
   ↓
DEPARTMENT HEADS (HODs)
   ↓
TEAM LEADS
   ↓
INDIVIDUAL EMPLOYEES

```

Each level has defined reporting, meeting cadences, and feedback responsibilities.

---

### 🔹 TYPES OF GOVERNANCE MEETINGS

| Meeting Type | Frequency | Participants | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily Stand-up** | Daily | Teams | Quick progress & blockers review |
| **Weekly Review** | Weekly | Team Leads + HODs | KPI check-ins, task updates |
| **Monthly Department Review** | Monthly | HODs + CXOs | Department performance, OKR progress |
| **Quarterly Strategy Review** | Quarterly | CEO + Board + HODs | OKR scoring, goal recalibration |
| **Annual Strategy Audit** | Yearly | Board + CEO + External Advisors | Evaluate success vs. vision, redesign yearly strategy |

---

### 🔹 MONTHLY GOVERNANCE SOP (Step-by-Step)

**WEEK 1 — PLAN**

- Review previous month’s performance data (Dashboards).
- Identify gaps, blockers, and areas of improvement.
- Define new priorities & OKRs for the month.

**WEEK 2 — EXECUTE**

- Departmental heads review KPI progress with team leads.
- Adjust workflows or assign new initiatives as needed.
- Finance and HR align budgets and manpower planning.

**WEEK 3 — REPORT**

- Each department prepares a monthly report:
    - OKR Progress %
    - Budget vs Spend
    - Key Wins / Losses
    - Automation Improvements
- Data consolidated into a **Master Performance Dashboard** (Power BI / Notion).

**WEEK 4 — REVIEW & DECIDE**

- CEO and CXOs conduct a **Monthly Governance Meeting**:
    - Review all reports & dashboards
    - Identify 3 key wins and 3 improvement areas per department
    - Document decisions and assign responsible owners
- Updates are logged in **Notion Governance Tracker**

---

### 🔹 QUARTERLY GOVERNANCE CYCLE

**Quarter Start:**

Set company-wide OKRs → Cascade into departments.

**Mid-Quarter:**

Departmental review for progress (50% OKR checkpoint).

**Quarter End:**

OKR scoring → Publish quarterly report → Adjust goals for next cycle.

**Deliverables:**

- Quarterly Performance Report (All Departments)
- Financial Summary Report
- OKR Scorecard
- Team & Leadership Ratings
- Process Improvement Plan

---

### 🔹 YEARLY GOVERNANCE CYCLE (Strategic Audit)

**Purpose:**

To evaluate the entire year’s strategic success against the company’s Vision, Mission, and Strategic Pillars.

**Steps:**

1. Collect department-wise annual reports.
2. Review financials, OKRs, and performance dashboards.
3. Conduct leadership review sessions (CEO + Board).
4. Identify what worked / what didn’t.
5. Finalize new goals, budgets, and franchise plans for next year.

**Output:**

- “Year in Review” Report
- Updated Strategic Blueprint
- Revised SOPs and Policy Additions
