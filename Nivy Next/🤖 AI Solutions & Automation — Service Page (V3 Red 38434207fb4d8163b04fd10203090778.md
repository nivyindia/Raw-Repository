# 16. FAQ

**How is this different from just buying a Zapier subscription?**

Tools like Zapier and Make are the engine — we're the engineers. We audit your actual process, design the logic, build it properly, and document it so it doesn't break in three months.

**We tried automation before and it broke. Why would this be different?**

Most broken automations fail because no one documented them or built in error-handling. Every build we ship includes documentation and a handover so your team can maintain it.

**Do we need a CRM already in place?**

No. We can automate around what you have, fix a broken CRM setup, or help you choose and implement one.

**How fast can we see results?**

Most single-workflow builds launch in 2–4 weeks. Clients typically recover the investment within the first month of usage.

**Will AI replace our team?**

No — automation removes repetitive manual work so your team can focus on judgment calls, relationships, and closing.

**What if our process changes after the automation is built?**

Builds are documented and modular, so workflows can be adjusted by your team or by us under a maintenance retainer.
