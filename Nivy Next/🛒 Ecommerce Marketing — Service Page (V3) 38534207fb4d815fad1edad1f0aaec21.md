No — pricing above is the management fee only. Ad spend is billed separately and goes directly to Google/Meta, fully transparent to you.

**Do you require us to migrate platforms?**

No. We work within your existing Shopify, WooCommerce, or Magento setup rather than forcing a migration.

**How fast can email/SMS flows start generating revenue?**

Most core flows (abandoned cart, post-purchase) start contributing within 2–3 weeks of launch, since they're triggered automatically by existing traffic.

**Can you take over campaigns that are already running?**

Yes. We audit what's live, preserve what's working, and restructure what isn't — without pausing active revenue.

**Do you support international currency and shipping logic?**

Yes — campaign and feed setup account for market-specific pricing, currency, and shipping rules across US, UK, CA, AU, and UAE.

**What if our margins are too thin for paid ads right now?**

We'll say so. Our first move is always an audit — if retention or CRO is the better lever before scaling paid spend, we'll recommend that instead.
