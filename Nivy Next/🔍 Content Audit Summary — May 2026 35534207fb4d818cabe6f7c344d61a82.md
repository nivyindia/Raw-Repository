## 🔴 CRITICAL GAPS IDENTIFIED

### 1. No Real Proof Exists

**Finding:** All case studies and testimonials are sample/placeholder content. Zero real client data.

**Risk:** Extremely high. No credibility signal on any platform.

**Action Required:**

- Interview 2–3 existing clients within 30 days
- Build 1 real case study per completed major engagement
- Collect real testimonials (written or audio) — use title/role if full name not permitted
- Target: 5 real case studies + 8 real testimonials within 6 months

### 2. No Directory Reviews

**Finding:** Clutch, GoodFirms, TechBehemoths, and Upwork have zero client reviews.

**Risk:** TechBehemoths is actively warning visitors away due to lack of reviews.

**Action Required:**

- Complete profiles on Clutch and GoodFirms immediately
- Request reviews from 3–5 recent clients with a templated outreach email
- Target: 5+ reviews on Clutch within 90 days

### 3. Inconsistency Between Pages

**Finding:** The Tier-1 Client-Facing Profile (Business Profile page) uses different positioning, different email ([hello@thenivy.com](mailto:hello@thenivy.com) vs [contact@thenivy.com](mailto:contact@thenivy.com)), and weaker AI-first language than the Master System.

**Risk:** Confusing to prospects who see multiple pages.

**Action Required:**

- Update Business Profile page to align with Master System positioning
- Standardise email to: [contact@thenivy.com](mailto:contact@thenivy.com) across all pages
- Audit all service pages for consistent brand voice

### 4. No Google Business Profile

**Finding:** No Google Business Profile exists for Nivy Next.

**Risk:** Invisible in local/branded search. Credibility gap for international prospects doing due diligence.

**Action Required:**

- Create and verify Google Business Profile immediately
- Use the description from Directory Version pages
- Add all service categories, location, website, and phone

### 5. Website Has Known Technical Issues

**Finding:** WordPress site and jobs subdomain have been compromised with injected casino spam content.

**Risk:** Critical. SEO damage, credibility destruction, security breach.

**Action Required (Urgent):**

- Full malware scan and removal
- File integrity check on WordPress installation
- Remove/clean injected content from all affected pages
- Submit clean sitemap to Google Search Console post-remediation

## ⚠️ MEDIUM GAPS — Needs Improvement

### 6. No Real Portfolio

**Finding:** No organised portfolio of past work exists in the workspace or on the website.

**Action:** Create a portfolio page structure by service type. Add screenshots, mockups, and deliverable samples from completed projects.

### 7. No Certifications Verified

**Finding:** Certifications are listed (Google Ads, Meta Blueprint, etc.) but none are verified or displayed publicly.

**Action:** Upload certification badges. Display on website and directory profiles. Update as certifications are renewed.

### 8. Thought Leadership Content Doesn’t Exist Yet

**Finding:** Blog/insights section is a content backlog with no published articles.

**Action:** Publish 2 strategic articles per month based on the topic backlog in Part 3. Prioritise: AI automation for B2B, international digital marketing, VA systems.

### 9. Metadata Missing from Legacy Pages

**Finding:** Service pages, the client-facing profile, and several other existing pages have no metadata applied.

**Action:** Apply the standard metadata block to all legacy pages using the tagging system guide.
