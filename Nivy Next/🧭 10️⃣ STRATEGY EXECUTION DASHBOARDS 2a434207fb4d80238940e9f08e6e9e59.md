### 🔹 1️⃣ EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD (Company-Level)

**Purpose:**

Monitor the company’s overall health — financial, strategic, operational, and growth performance.

| Metric | Description | Source | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue (MTD/YTD) | Total income from all channels | Finance | Real-time |
| Profit Margin % | Gross & net profitability | Finance | Monthly |
| OKR Achievement % | Progress on yearly objectives | Governance | Quarterly |
| Lead Conversion Rate | Total leads vs. closed deals | CRM | Weekly |
| Client Retention Rate | % of clients retained | Client Portal | Monthly |
| Team Productivity Index | Output vs. hours worked | HR/PM tool | Weekly |
| Automation Index | % of workflows automated | CTO System | Quarterly |

💡 **Tools:** Power BI, Google Data Studio, Notion Dashboards, Klipfolio

### 🔹 REVIEW & ACTION LOOP (Monthly Dashboard Governance)

**Step 1 — Review:**

Each HOD reviews dashboards weekly, identifies red zones.

**Step 2 — Report:**

CXOs consolidate data into Executive Dashboard summary.

**Step 3 — Decide:**

Leadership meeting to adjust priorities or budgets.

**Step 4 — Act:**

Departmental teams implement new tasks or process fixes.

**Step 5 — Record:**

Updates logged in **Governance Tracker (Notion)** for traceability.
