## Case Study 1 — AI Automation | Recruitment | 🇺🇸 United States

**Client:** Mid-sized recruitment and staffing agency | US Market | 10–30 employees

**Challenge:**

The team was manually working through 200+ inbound candidate and employer leads every week. Response times averaged 48 hours — slow enough for warm leads to go cold. Recruiters were spending more than half their working week on admin and qualification instead of actual placements. The CRM was updated inconsistently, and there was no standardised process across the team.

**Solution:**

Nivy Next built a three-layer AI automation system over six weeks. Layer one: an AI qualification bot that engages every inbound lead immediately, asks structured screening questions, and scores responses against the firm's hiring criteria in real time. Layer two: automated follow-up sequences for qualified leads (instant confirmation + calendar booking), no-shows (3-touchpoint rebook), and cold leads (reactivation at 7 and 30 days). Layer three: full CRM workflow integration so all records, statuses, and recruiter notifications update automatically without human intervention.

**Results:**

- Response time: 48 hours → under 5 minutes
- 80% reduction in manual qualification time
- 22% increase in booked discovery calls
- Zero leads lost to slow follow-up post-launch

**Timeline:** 6 weeks to full deployment

> *"The system Nivy Next built didn't just speed up our process — it changed the whole way we work. Our recruiters are now spending their time on conversations that matter instead of chasing leads. The results speak for themselves."*
> 

> — **Operations Director, Recruitment Firm | United States**

## Case Study 3 — Email Marketing | E-Commerce | 🇦🇺 Australia

**Client:** Fashion DTC brand | Australian Market | 11–30 employees

**Challenge:**

The brand was driving consistent traffic through paid social and had a growing email list of 18,000 subscribers. Email was contributing 0% of monthly revenue — they had no automation flows, no segmentation, and sent an occasional bulk newsletter that averaged 9% open rates. Revenue was entirely dependent on paid acquisition.

**Solution:**

Nivy Next built a full Klaviyo email revenue system from scratch: 7 core automation flows (welcome series, abandoned cart, post-purchase, browse abandonment, win-back, VIP loyalty, and product review request), full audience segmentation by purchase history and lifecycle stage, branded email templates, and a monthly newsletter programme. Domain authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) fixed a deliverability issue that had been silently suppressing open rates.

**Results:**

- Email revenue: 0% → 31% of total monthly revenue within 3 months
- Open rates: 9% → 38%
- Click-through rate: 0.8% → 6.4%
- Abandoned cart recovery generating an additional $14,000/month on autopilot

**Timeline:** All flows live within 4 weeks; revenue impact visible by month 2

> *"Email was always the thing we kept saying we'd fix properly. Nivy Next built the entire system in under a month. It now runs completely on autopilot and generates nearly a third of our revenue. We wish we'd done this two years ago."*

## Case Study 5 — Social Media Advertising | Health & Wellness | 🇨🇦 Canada

**Client:** Health and wellness subscription brand | Canadian Market | 6–20 employees

**Challenge:**

The brand was running Meta Ads with a broad audience, single-campaign structure, and 1.1x ROAS — barely breaking even on paid social. No retargeting, no lookalike audiences, and ad creative that hadn't been refreshed in four months. The brand had strong organic engagement but couldn't translate social presence into paid performance.

**Solution:**

Nivy Next rebuilt the entire Meta Ads account architecture. New campaign structure: cold prospecting with 4 audience variants (interest, lookalike 1%, LAL 2–5%, broad with interest exclusions), 7-day retargeting campaign for visitors who hadn't purchased, 30-day cart abandonment campaign, and an LTV expansion campaign targeting previous purchasers. Ad creative rebuilt with platform-native hooks, short-form video, and monthly creative refresh cycles.

**Results:**

- ROAS: 1.1x → 3.6x within 60 days
- CPM reduced 34% through audience precision
- 7-day retargeting campaign: 6.2x ROAS
- Revenue from paid social up 185% on same monthly ad budget

**Timeline:** Account rebuild in week 1; results visible by week 4; full performance by week 8

> *"We knew our Meta Ads were underperforming but we didn't know how badly until Nivy Next audited the account. The rebuild took one week and within two months we'd nearly quadrupled our return on ad spend. Game-changing."*
