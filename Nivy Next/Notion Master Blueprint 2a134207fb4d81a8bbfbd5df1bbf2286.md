### How to use this "100-Person" Logic:

1. **For You (CEO):** You visit the **Home Dashboard** for high-level strategy and the **Department Hubs** to check on the progress of your 12 VPs/Heads.
2. **For Employees:** They visit their specific **Department Page**. Because of the "Master Database" system, they have instant access to the *Company SOPs, Templates, and Training* they need without leaving their own department's area.
3. **For Maintenance:** If you need to change a "Company Policy," you update the **Master SOP Database** once. It automatically reflects everywhere in the company instantly.

### Why this structure succeeds:

- **No Redundancy:** You aren't storing the same "NDA template" in 12 different folders. You store it in the **Master Template DB** and link to it.
- **Scalable:** When you hire your 101st person, they only see what is relevant to their department, keeping the workspace clutter-free.
- **Clear Ownership:** Each of your 12 departments has a designated space. If a process is broken, you know exactly which Department Page to audit.
