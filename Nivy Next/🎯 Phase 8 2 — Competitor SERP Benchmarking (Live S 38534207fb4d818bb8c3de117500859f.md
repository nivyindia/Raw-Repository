# Keyword: "digital marketing agency uk"

**Actual organic competitors showing up today** (not brand-perceived competitors — genuine SERP occupants):

- Found ([found.co.uk](http://found.co.uk)) — B2B-focused, leaning into AI/brand-optimisation-for-LLMs angle
- Bird Marketing ([bird.co.uk](http://bird.co.uk)) — award-led full-service positioning, UK HQ + global branches
- [Clutch.co](http://Clutch.co)'s UK directory page itself ranks (aggregator competing for the head term)
- Semrush's UK agency directory page (same aggregator dynamic)
- Passion Digital ([passion.digital](http://passion.digital)) — "search-led performance marketing," leans into AI/LLM discovery messaging
- Nautilus Marketing, FINN Partners, Atomic Digital Marketing — full-service London/UK shops

**Pattern worth noting:** Several top-ranking UK agencies (Found, Passion Digital) are already messaging around AI/LLM-era search ("brand optimisation for AI platforms," "GEO") rather than just traditional SEO. Nivy Next's AI-native positioning is on-trend, but the SEO and Marketing Hub page copy should make sure it explicitly mentions answer-engine/LLM visibility, not just classic SERP ranking — otherwise it reads a step behind the pages currently ranking.

**Also note:** Directory aggregators (Clutch, Semrush's own agency list, DesignRush) occupy real estate in this SERP. This *reinforces* Phase 3.4/6.1 — a strong Clutch profile isn't just a backlink/trust play, it's actual visibility real estate for this exact keyword.
