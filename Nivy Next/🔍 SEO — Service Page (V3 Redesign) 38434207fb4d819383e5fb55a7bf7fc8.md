# 16. FAQ

**How long until we see results?**

Technical fixes can show movement within weeks. Competitive keyword rankings typically take 3–6 months to mature — SEO compounds rather than spikes.

**We tried SEO before and saw nothing. Why would this work?**

Most failed SEO engagements skip the technical foundation or target the wrong keywords. We audit first, then build a roadmap tied to buyer intent, not vanity traffic.

**Will a site redesign hurt our existing rankings?**

It can if not planned properly. We manage redirects, technical migration checklists, and ranking monitoring through any redesign.

**Do you guarantee rankings?**

No one ethically can guarantee a #1 ranking — search algorithms aren't controlled by any agency. We commit to a process, transparent reporting, and realistic targets.

**Can SEO work alongside paid ads?**

Yes — they compound. Paid drives immediate traffic while SEO builds a free, compounding channel underneath it.

**Do you write the content yourselves?**

Yes, with AI-assisted drafting reviewed and edited by strategists who understand your industry and audience.
