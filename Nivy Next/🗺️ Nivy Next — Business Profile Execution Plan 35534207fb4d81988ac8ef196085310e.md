## 🎨 A. BRAND IDENTITY SELECTION

### ✅ Selected Palette: Refined Usability Palette (Dark Theme Primary)

After reviewing the full Branding — Nivy Next Visual Identity page, the **Dark Theme Palette** is selected as the primary system for all brand assets. The light theme variant applies to print/brochure contexts.

| Role | Color Name | HEX | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary Accent | Cyan Glow | `#0AEFFF` | CTAs, buttons, highlights, icons |
| Secondary Accent | Sapphire Blue | `#0047FF` | Headers, links, section titles |
| Tertiary Accent | Violet Pulse | `#7A00FF` | Charts, banners, visual energy |
| Background Primary | Midnight Gray | `#0E1117` | Slide decks, hero sections |
| Background Secondary | Graphite Gray | `#22252A` | Cards, panels |
| Background Tertiary | Slate Gray | `#2C2F36` | Callouts, stat blocks |
| Heading Text | Cool White | `#E6F2FF` | Dark mode headings |
| Body Text | Light Graphite | `#C9D1D9` | Dark mode body |
| Light Base | Frost White | `#F5F8FA` | Brochure, documents |

**Signature Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #0AEFFF 0%, #0047FF 50%, #7A00FF 100%)`

**Typography:**

- Headings: **Poppins Bold (700)**
- Subheadings: **Inter Medium (500)**
- Body: **Inter Regular (400)**
- Data / Tables: **IBM Plex Mono**

## 🧠 D. UX + MESSAGING STRATEGY

**Positioning Angle:** AI-first international growth partner — not a vendor, a system

**Authority Level:** Premium / Global Agency — confident but not arrogant

**Tone of Voice:** Strategic · Sharp · Direct · Globally Literate

**Conversion Strategy:**

- Website: Lead with outcomes, not services. Drive to discovery call.
- Brochure: Build trust through specificity. Leave wanting to know more.
- Deck: Open with the pain, close with the proof. Make saying yes easy.
- Social: Give before asking. Authority first, CTA second.
- Directory: Facts + specifics + proof. No fluff — platforms penalise it.

**What to NEVER say:** "We are a one-stop-shop", "We go above and beyond", "We're passionate about digital", "We help businesses grow" (too vague)

**What ALWAYS to say:** Specific outcomes, specific markets, specific mechanisms, specific timelines.
