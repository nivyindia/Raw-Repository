## 🧭 1.4.2.2 Governance Dashboard Blueprint – Digital Marketing Agency

### 🎯 **Purpose:**

To provide real-time visibility into company-wide performance, compliance, and decision metrics — enabling data-driven governance at every level (Board → Executive → Department → Teams).

---

### 🔹 1️⃣ Dashboard Layers Overview

| **Dashboard Type** | **Primary Audience** | **Update Frequency** | **Data Source / Tool** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Corporate Governance Dashboard** | Board of Directors, CEO | Quarterly | ERPNext + Metabase |
| **Executive Governance Dashboard** | ExCom (CEO, COO, CFO, CMO, CHRO, CTO, Compliance) | Weekly | ERPNext + Metabase + Nextcloud |
| **Departmental Governance Dashboard** | Department Heads | Weekly | OpenProject + ERPNext |
| **Compliance & Audit Dashboard** | Compliance Officer, Internal Auditor | Monthly | Nextcloud / ERPNext |
| **People & Culture Dashboard** | HR, CEO | Monthly | ERPNext HR Module |
| **Risk & Security Dashboard** | CTO, COO, Risk Officer | Monthly | Grafana + Security Logs |

---

### 🔹 2️⃣ CEO Governance Dashboard

**Objective:** Unified company performance and compliance overview.

| **Metric / KPI** | **Description** | **Data Source** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total Revenue vs Target | Monthly revenue achievement ratio | ERPNext Finance |
| Profit Margin | Net profit % after expenses | ERPNext |
| Client Retention Rate | % of clients retained per month | CRM Module |
| New Projects Started | Number of new client campaigns | OpenProject |
| Team Utilization | % of employee capacity used | ERPNext HRMS |
| Policy Compliance Score | % of departments meeting policy standards | Compliance Tracker |
| Risk Alerts | Count of open or unresolved risk items | Risk Register |
| Employee Satisfaction Index | Based on monthly HR survey | ERPNext HRMS |
| Governance Health Index | Weighted score from KPIs across all departments | Calculated via Metabase |

---

### 🔹 3️⃣ COO (Operations) Governance Dashboard

**Objective:** Monitor project delivery, process efficiency, and operational KPIs.

| **Metric / KPI** | **Description** | **Data Source** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active Projects | Total ongoing projects by department | OpenProject |
| Delivery Timeliness | % of projects delivered on or before deadlines | OpenProject |
| Client Feedback Score | Avg satisfaction score post-delivery | CRM / Feedback Form |
| Departmental SLA Adherence | % of SLA compliance | SOP Tracker |
| Workflow Bottlenecks | Delayed tasks or teams | Jira / OpenProject |
| Resource Allocation Chart | Current vs optimal staffing levels | ERPNext HR |
| Policy Violation Incidents | Non-adherence events | Compliance Tracker |

---

### 🔹 4️⃣ CFO (Finance) Governance Dashboard

**Objective:** Maintain fiscal transparency, budget adherence, and financial compliance.

| **Metric / KPI** | **Description** | **Data Source** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget vs Actual Spend | Comparison of approved vs spent budget | ERPNext |
| Expense by Department | Monthly breakdown by function | ERPNext |
| Vendor Payments Pending | Unpaid or delayed vendor invoices | ERPNext Finance |
| Profitability per Project | Gross margin per client | ERPNext Projects |
| Cash Flow Summary | Real-time inflow and outflow | ERPNext |
| Audit Exceptions | Number of financial discrepancies found | Audit Report |
| Tax & Compliance Status | Filing and payment tracking | Compliance Dashboard |

---

### 🔹 5️⃣ CHRO (HR & People Governance) Dashboard

**Objective:** Oversee culture, performance, and workforce efficiency.

| **Metric / KPI** | **Description** | **Data Source** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headcount Overview | Total, by department, by country | ERPNext HR |
| Attrition Rate | Monthly % of employee exits | HRMS |
| Hiring Progress | Open vs filled positions | Recruitment Tracker |
| Performance Ratings | % of employees meeting/exceeding KPIs | Performance Module |
| Training Completion | % of employees completing governance training | LMS / Outline |
| Policy Awareness Test Score | Average compliance quiz score | HR Governance Tracker |
| Employee Happiness Index | Anonymous survey results | Survey Tool |

---

### 🔹 6️⃣ CMO (Marketing & Brand Governance) Dashboard

**Objective:** Ensure marketing performance aligns with strategic goals.

| **Metric / KPI** | **Description** | **Data Source** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign ROI | ROI for ad and content campaigns | Marketing Analytics |
| Lead-to-Client Conversion | % of leads turned into paying clients | CRM |
| Brand Sentiment | Positive vs negative mentions online | Social Listening Tool |
| Creative Turnaround Time | Avg time for creative approval | Project Tracker |
| Marketing Spend Efficiency | Cost per lead vs target | ERPNext Finance |
| Compliance with Brand Guidelines | % of approved brand usage | Brand Governance SOP Tracker |

---

### 🔹 7️⃣ Compliance Officer Dashboard

**Objective:** Track policy compliance, audits, and risk management.

| **Metric / KPI** | **Description** | **Data Source** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Policy Compliance % | % of policies fully followed across depts | Compliance Tracker |
| Internal Audit Status | Completed vs pending audits | Audit Log |
| Risk Register Heatmap | Visualization of high/medium/low risks | Nextcloud Sheets / Grafana |
| Data Access Violations | Unauthorized access incidents | Security Log |
| Corrective Actions Closed | % of compliance issues resolved | Compliance Action Register |
| Next Policy Review Due | Countdown to next audit cycle | Policy Calendar |

---

### 🔹 8️⃣ Board of Directors Dashboard

**Objective:** High-level visibility of governance, financials, and compliance.

| **Metric / KPI** | **Description** | **Data Source** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quarterly Revenue & Profit | Topline company performance | ERPNext |
| Governance Scorecard | Weighted summary of all governance KPIs | Metabase |
| Risk Heatmap | Key company-level risks | Risk Register |
| Policy & Audit Status | Summary of all audits and compliance | Compliance Officer Reports |
| Strategic Project Status | Progress on company-wide initiatives | OpenProject |
| Board Meeting Summary | Latest minutes and action items | Nextcloud Board Folder |

---

### 🔹 9️⃣ Visual Governance Dashboard Design (Metabase / Grafana layout idea)

```
[ Top Bar KPIs ]
   - Revenue | Profit Margin | Client Retention | Policy Compliance | Risk Level | Employee Happiness

[ Middle Panels ]
   - Departmental Performance Cards (Marketing | Operations | HR | Finance)
   - Policy Compliance Heatmap
   - Active Risk Tracker

[ Bottom Section ]
   - Upcoming Governance Meetings
   - Pending Approvals / Action Items
   - Audit & Review Calendar

[ Sidebar ]
   - Switch View → (CEO / CFO / HR / Compliance / Board)

```

### 🔹 🔁 Data Refresh Cycle

| **Data Source** | **Refresh Frequency** | **Responsible Role** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ERPNext (Finance, HR, Projects) | Daily | CFO / HR / COO |
| OpenProject (Tasks, KPIs) | Real-time | PMO |
| Nextcloud / Outline (Docs, Policies) | Weekly Sync | Compliance Officer |
| Metabase / Grafana (Visualization) | Daily Auto-Sync | IT / Data Engineer |
| CRM (Sales Metrics) | Daily | CMO / Sales Lead |
