# 📄 Qualification Questions

## Qualification Questions

Ask these to determine if the prospect is a good fit before pitching a package.

---

### Business Basics

1. What type of business do you run, and who are your main customers?
2. How long have you been in business?
3. What’s your current monthly revenue range?

### Marketing Situation

1. How are you currently getting clients or leads?
2. What’s been your biggest challenge with marketing?
3. Have you worked with a marketing agency before? What happened?

### Decision & Budget

1. Who else is involved in making this decision?
2. Do you have a monthly budget allocated for marketing?
3. What does success look like for you in the next 3–6 months?

---

> 💡 **Tip:** Listen more than you talk. The answers guide your package recommendation.
