# 📄 Ads SOP

## Ads SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)

Follow this process for managing and optimizing client ad campaigns.

---

## Step 1 — Campaign Setup

- Define campaign objective (leads, traffic, conversions)
- Set up ad account structure: Campaign > Ad Set > Ad
- Configure targeting, budget, schedule

## Step 2 — Ad Creation

- Write primary text, headline, and CTA
- Design ad creative (Canva or provided assets)
- Set up tracking (Pixel, conversion events)

## Step 3 — Launch

- Review all settings before going live
- Confirm pixel is firing
- Take screenshot of initial setup for records

## Step 4 — Daily Monitoring (First 7 Days)

- Check spend, CPL, CTR, ROAS daily
- Flag any underperforming ads
- Make micro-adjustments to bids and audiences

## Step 5 — Weekly Optimization

- Pause low performers
- Scale winning ads
- Test new creatives if needed

## Step 6 — Reporting

- Log weekly performance in Report Template
- Summarize insights for client
