# 16. FAQ

**Do I need all 12 disciplines to get results?**

No. Most clients start with 1–2 (commonly SEO or Paid Ads) and expand as results compound. We'll recommend the right starting mix during the scoping call.

**Our last agency ran ads but we couldn't tell if they worked. How is this different?**

We set up attribution and conversion tracking before any spend goes live, so every channel's ROI is visible from week one — not estimated after the fact.

**Can you take over an existing campaign mid-flight?**

Yes. We audit what's running, keep what's working, and restructure what isn't — without pausing live revenue-generating activity.

**Do you handle both organic and paid channels?**

Yes — SEO, content, and social are organic; PPC and social advertising are paid. Most Growth and Partner clients run a mix of both.

**How fast will we see results?**

Paid channels typically show signal within 2–4 weeks. SEO and content compound over 90+ days. We set expectations per channel during scoping.

**What if our ROAS is currently negative?**

That's a common starting point. Our first move is always a full audit to find where spend is leaking before we recommend new budget.
