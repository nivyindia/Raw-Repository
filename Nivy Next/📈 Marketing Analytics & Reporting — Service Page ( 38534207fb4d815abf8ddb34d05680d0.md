# 16 · FAQ

**Our GA4 is already set up — do we need this?**

Probably. Over 70% of GA4 setups we audit are missing critical conversion events, have incorrect attribution windows, or have unfiltered internal traffic skewing data. We audit first and report what we find.

**What is Looker Studio?**

Looker Studio (formerly Google Data Studio) is a free visualisation tool that connects to all your data sources — Google Ads, Meta, GA4, Klaviyo, HubSpot — and displays them in one live, auto-updating dashboard.

**How do you handle post-iOS attribution issues?**

We implement the Meta Conversions API (server-side tracking), use GA4 modelled data, and set up blended MER (marketing efficiency ratio) reporting that gives you a reliable truth even where platform attribution is degraded.

**Can you connect our CRM to the dashboard?**

Yes. We connect HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce, and other CRMs to your dashboard so you can see leads, pipeline, and closed revenue attributed to marketing activity.

**Do you build the dashboards yourself?**

Yes — all dashboards are built by our team and handed over fully documented. You own all dashboards and data connections.

**What’s the difference between your reporting and what our ad platforms show?**

Platform reporting (Google, Meta, LinkedIn) is self-attributing and therefore inflated. A click attributed by Meta might also be attributed by Google. Our unified attribution model de-duplicates credit and gives you a more accurate picture of true channel performance.
