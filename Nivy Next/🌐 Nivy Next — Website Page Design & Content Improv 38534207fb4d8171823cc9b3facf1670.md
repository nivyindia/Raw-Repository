# 🌍 International Client Optimisation — 10 Rules That Apply to EVERY Page

> Apply these rules before writing a single word of copy. These are non-negotiable for international conversion.
> 
1. **Outcome in the headline** — Every H1 names a result, not a service. "Rank Higher, Get Found, Win Clients" not "SEO Services."
2. **Market trust signals** — Every page shows at least one US/UK/AU/UAE signal (flag, market mention, case study location).
3. **GDPR compliance** — All forms include GDPR checkbox + Privacy Policy link. Required for UK/EU clients.
4. **Response SLA** — State "All inquiries responded within 24 business hours" on all contact-adjacent pages.
5. **Payment clarity** — Show payment methods (Stripe, Wire Transfer, PayPal) on Pricing and service pages.
6. **Timezone availability** — State "Available EST · GMT · AEST business hours" on Contact and About pages.
7. **No Level 1 pricing** — Remove entry-level pricing from all public pages. Show Starter/Growth/Scale only.
8. **Real proof only** — No sample case studies or placeholder testimonials on any live page.
9. **3-month minimum note** — Add commitment note on all pricing sections before the client asks.
10. **Currency clarity** — All prices in USD. Add: "GBP, AUD, CAD equivalents available on request."
