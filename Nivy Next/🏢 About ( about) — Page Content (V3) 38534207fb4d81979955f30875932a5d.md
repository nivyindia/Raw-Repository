# 3 · Our Story

**2022 — The Beginning**

Nivy was founded with a single conviction: that businesses trying to grow internationally deserve better than a fragmented stack of vendors, each optimising their own slice with no one responsible for the result. We started with a small team focused on digital marketing and consulting — and a strict policy of measuring success by client outcomes, not activity.

**2023 — Expanding the Stack**

As client needs deepened, we expanded into web development, creative services, and early AI exploration. The multi-service model took shape — not because we wanted to be everything to everyone, but because our clients kept asking us to handle more, and we kept delivering.

**2024 — Going AI-First**

We made a deliberate, company-wide shift: embedding AI into every service line. Not as a gimmick, but as a genuine operating system. Automation, content research, lead qualification, client reporting — AI became the infrastructure that made our team faster and our clients' results better.

**2025 — International Scale**

Active client operations across five international markets. Nivy Next formally launched as the dedicated digital innovation division of Nivy Digital, with a full 18-service capability stack, an international team structure, and the infrastructure to serve 100+ client engagements simultaneously.

**2026 — Full Launch**

The complete Nivy Next platform: 18 services, AI-native operations across every engagement, and a team of specialists who treat every client's growth like their own business problem.
