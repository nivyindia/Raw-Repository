# 📄 Roleplay Practice Scripts

## Roleplay Practice Scripts

Practice these roleplay scenarios before getting on real sales calls.

---

## Scenario 1 — The Interested But Uncertain Prospect

**Prospect:** *"I’m interested but not sure if digital marketing will work for my business."*

**VA Practice Response:**

> *"That’s a really common concern, and it’s a smart question. Can I ask — how are you currently getting new clients? [Listen] Got it. The reason digital marketing works for businesses like yours is [insert relevant reason]. In fact, one of our clients in a similar situation saw [result] in just [timeframe]."*
> 

---

## Scenario 2 — The Price Objection

**Prospect:** *"This is more than I expected to spend."*

**VA Practice Response:**

> *"I totally understand. Can I share something? If we help you land even [X] new clients from this, the package pays for itself [Y]x over. The real question is: what’s the cost of NOT getting those clients?"*
> 

---

## Scenario 3 — The “I Need to Think About It”

**Prospect:** *"Let me think about it and get back to you."*

**VA Practice Response:**

> *"Of course — this is an important decision. Can I ask what the main thing is you’re still thinking through? Is it budget, timing, or something about the service?"*
>
