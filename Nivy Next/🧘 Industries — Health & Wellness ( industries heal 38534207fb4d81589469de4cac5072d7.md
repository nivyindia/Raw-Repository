# Digital Marketing for Health & Wellness Brands

Health and wellness brands face a narrower creative lane than most industries — claims need to be compliant, trust has to be earned visually and editorially, and local discovery often matters as much as broad brand reach. Nivy Next builds marketing for health and wellness businesses with that reality built in from the brief, not flagged after the fact.

## Where We Help Most

**Brand authority content.** Editorial and social content that builds credibility without overstating outcomes — a balance that matters more in this category than almost any other.

**Local SEO and Google Business Profile optimisation.** For wellness brands with a physical or service-area presence, local discoverability is often the highest-leverage channel available.

**Compliant creative production.** Graphic design and video that meets platform and category advertising standards without losing visual impact.

**Content marketing for trust-building.** Long-form and social content that educates first — the format that performs best for converting skeptical health and wellness audiences.
