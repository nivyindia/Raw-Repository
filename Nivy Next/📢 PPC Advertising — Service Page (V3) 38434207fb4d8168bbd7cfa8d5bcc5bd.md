# 4 · Is This Right For You?

**Strong fit if:**

- You want qualified leads or sales within weeks, not months
- You have a product or service with proven demand and a website that converts
- You’re willing to invest in properly managed ad spend ($1,500+ per month)
- You want complete transparency on spend, performance, and ROI

**Not the right fit if:**

- Your offer or landing page doesn’t convert yet — fix that first
- Your budget is under $1,000/month — too small to compete in most markets
- You want to “try it for one month” — PPC requires a learning period to perform

# 16 · FAQ

**How much should I spend on ads?**

For most competitive markets, we recommend a minimum of $2,000–$3,000/month in ad spend to gather meaningful data and compete effectively. We advise on budget allocation based on your goals and market.

**How quickly will I see results?**

PPC can generate traffic and leads from day one. The first 4–6 weeks is the learning phase. Most clients see strong performance improvements by month 2–3.

**Do you take a percentage of ad spend?**

No. We charge a fixed management fee. Your ad spend goes directly to the platforms.

**What if campaigns aren’t performing after 3 months?**

We conduct a full diagnostic, retest our hypotheses, and restructure where needed. Underperformance is always investigated transparently.

**Do you manage Meta Ads too?**

Our PPC service covers Google and Microsoft ecosystems. For Meta, LinkedIn, and TikTok, see our Social Media Advertising service.

**Will I own my ad accounts?**

Always. Your Google and Microsoft Ads accounts remain in your name. We manage as invited partners. If we part ways, you keep everything.
