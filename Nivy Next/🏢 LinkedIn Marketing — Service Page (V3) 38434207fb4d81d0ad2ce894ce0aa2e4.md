# 16 · FAQ

**How many leads can I expect from LinkedIn?**

This varies by industry, ICP, and offer quality. In our experience, well-executed LinkedIn strategies generate 4–15 qualified conversations per month within 60–90 days. We set realistic expectations at the strategy stage.

**Do you write the content in my name?**

Yes. We interview you or extract insights from your expertise, then ghostwrite in your voice. You review and approve everything before it’s published.

**Is this compliant with LinkedIn’s terms?**

Yes. We use LinkedIn-compliant outreach tools within daily activity limits and always prioritise human-first, personalised messaging.

**Do I need Sales Navigator?**

For outreach campaigns, yes — it dramatically improves targeting precision. For content-only programmes, it’s optional. We advise at the strategy stage.

**Can you manage my VA team’s LinkedIn too?**

Yes — we can manage multiple personal profiles under our Scale tier. Common for agencies, consulting firms, and recruitment companies.

**What results can I expect in month one?**

Month one is primarily profile optimisation, strategy, and content launch. Outreach begins in week 2–3. Meaningful pipeline movement typically shows by month 2–3.
