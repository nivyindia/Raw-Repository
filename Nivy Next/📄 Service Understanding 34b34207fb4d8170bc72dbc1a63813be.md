## Self-Test: Can You Answer These?

### Social Media Marketing

- What platforms do we manage?
- How many posts per month in each package?
- What is engagement rate and why does it matter?

### Google Ads

- What is PPC?
- How does Google decide which ad to show?
- What is a good CPL?

### Lead Generation

- What is a lead magnet?
- What is a landing page?
- What happens after a lead is captured?

### Funnel System

- Name the 5 stages of a funnel
- What is a tripwire?
- What is retargeting?
