# 📄 Basic Marketing Concepts

## Basic Marketing Concepts

Every VA at Nivy Digital must understand these foundational marketing concepts.

---

## Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| CPL | Cost Per Lead — how much it costs to get one lead |
| ROAS | Return on Ad Spend — revenue generated per $1 spent on ads |
| CTR | Click-Through Rate — % of people who click on an ad |
| Funnel | A step-by-step system to take someone from stranger to client |
| Retargeting | Showing ads to people who have already visited your site |
| Organic | Traffic / growth that happens without paid ads |
| Paid | Traffic / growth driven by ad spend |
| Engagement Rate | % of followers who interact with a post |

---

## The Marketing Funnel

1. **Awareness** — People discover the brand
2. **Interest** — They want to learn more
3. **Consideration** — They’re evaluating options
4. **Decision** — They’re ready to buy
5. **Retention** — They become repeat clients
