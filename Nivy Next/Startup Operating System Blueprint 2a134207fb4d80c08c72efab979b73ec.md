# Startup Operating System Blueprint

## Startup Operating System Blueprint

### I. FOUNDATION & GOVERNANCE (Start Smart)

1. **Legal & Compliance Setup** – company registration, licenses, contracts
2. **Financial & Accounting System** – invoicing, bookkeeping, expense tracking
3. **Documentation & Knowledge Hub** – store SOPs, ideas, and policies (e.g., Notion or Google Drive)
4. **Data Security & Backup Basics** – password manager, cloud backup, access control

---

### II. OPERATIONS & DELIVERY

1. **Project & Task Management System** – manage clients, projects, tasks (e.g., ClickUp, Trello)
2. **SOP & Workflow Management** – record repeatable processes
3. **Client Onboarding & Delivery Pipeline** – checklist from lead → delivery → feedback
4. **Quality Control & Review Process** – regular audits, client satisfaction tracking

---

### III. SALES & MARKETING

1. **Sales & CRM System** – track leads, deals, and clients (e.g., HubSpot, Zoho)
2. **Marketing Content & Branding System** – brand assets, posts, campaigns
3. **Lead Generation & Funnel** – website forms, social ads, email funnels
4. **Customer Retention & Feedback Loop** – gather reviews, referrals

---

### IV. TEAM & PEOPLE SYSTEMS

1. **HR & Recruitment Process** – hiring templates, interview checklists
2. **Payroll & Benefits (Basic)** – automate salary & expense reimbursements
3. **Training & Development Tracker** – internal learning, upskilling list
4. **Team Communication Hub** – Slack / WhatsApp / Notion team page

---

### V. TECHNOLOGY & AUTOMATION

1. **IT & Tools Stack** – central list of software & logins
2. **Automation Setup** – integrate routine workflows (e.g., Zapier, Make)
3. **Cloud Storage & Access Control** – structured folders by department
4. **Website & Digital Presence System** – company site, social handles, email domain

---

### VI. STRATEGY & PLANNING

1. **Vision, Mission, & Core Values** – 1-page strategy summary
2. **Goal & OKR System** – track quarterly goals (Notion / Excel OKR tracker)
3. **Analytics Dashboard** – simple spreadsheet or Google Data Studio
4. **Review & Feedback System** – monthly team/business performance review

---

### VII. PARTNERS & GROWTH

1. **Vendor & Partner Directory** – freelancers, suppliers, contacts
2. **Growth & Collaboration Tracker** – joint ventures, affiliate or referral programs
3. **Client Community / Newsletter System** – build recurring engagement

---

### VIII. CONTINUITY & IMPROVEMENT

1. **Backup & Business Continuity Plan** – ensure operations can resume easily if you’re away
