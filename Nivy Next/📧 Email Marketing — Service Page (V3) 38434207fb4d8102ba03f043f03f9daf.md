1. **Email Audit & Platform Review** — Audit existing emails, assess deliverability, review automation gaps

4. **Copy & Design** — Write all email copy and design branded templates for each sequence
5. **Build, Test & Launch** — Build automations in your ESP, A/B test, verify tracking, and go live
6. **Optimise & Report** — Monthly performance review, A/B test analysis, ongoing sequence improvement

**Which email platform do you work with?**

We work with Klaviyo, ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp, HubSpot, ConvertKit, and Brevo. If you’re not sure which is right for you, we’ll recommend based on your business model.

**Do you write the email copy?**

Yes — all email copy is written by our team in your brand voice. You review and approve before anything goes live.

**How quickly can automation flows be live?**

Most core flows can be built, tested, and live within 3–4 weeks of engagement start.

**What’s a good email list size to start?**

You can start with any list size. We’ve built programmes for lists of 500 and lists of 500,000. Strategy scales with your list.

**What if my deliverability is poor?**

We run a deliverability audit at the start of every engagement. If your domain reputation needs repair, we have a warm-up and remediation process.

**Do you manage campaigns and automations together?**

Yes. Our Growth and Scale tiers cover both — evergreen automations running in the background plus regular campaign sends to keep your list engaged.
