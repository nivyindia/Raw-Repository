# 16. FAQ

**What's the difference between this and Social Media Marketing?**

Social Media Marketing is organic content and community management. Social Media Advertising is paid campaigns — they're often run together for compounding results.

**Is LinkedIn Ads worth it for B2B?**

Yes, when targeted correctly. It's typically a higher cost-per-click than Meta but often produces higher-quality B2B leads.

**Our creative keeps getting fatigued. Can you fix that?**

Yes — we build a structured creative testing and refresh cadence so performance doesn't decay between campaigns.

**Do you need access to our existing ad accounts?**

Yes, we typically work within your existing ad accounts to preserve learning data and historical performance.

**How is retargeting different from cold campaigns?**

Retargeting reaches people who already engaged with your brand (site visitors, video viewers) — typically a much lower cost per conversion than cold audiences.

**What budget do we need to make this worthwhile?**

It varies by platform and market, but most clients see meaningful signal starting around $2,000–$4,000/month in ad spend.
