# 📄 Social Media Marketing

## What is Social Media Marketing?

Managing and growing a brand’s presence on platforms like Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and TikTok.

---

## What We Do

- Content creation (graphics, captions, reels)
- Posting schedule management
- Community engagement
- Growth strategy

## Client Pain Points We Solve

- “I don’t have time to post”
- “I don’t know what to post”
- “My account isn’t growing”

## How to Explain This to a Client

> *"We handle your entire social media — from content creation to posting to engaging with your audience. You focus on running your business, we handle your online presence."*
> 

## Key Results We Deliver

- Consistent posting
- Growing follower count
- Increased brand visibility
