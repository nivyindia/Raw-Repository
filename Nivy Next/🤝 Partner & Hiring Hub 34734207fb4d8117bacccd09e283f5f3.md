# 🤝 Partner & Hiring Hub

This hub contains everything related to hiring agencies, onboarding partners, managing freelancers, and running the Nivy Next partner ecosystem.

---

Nivy Next is building a reliable global execution partner network. We collaborate with structured agencies and vetted freelancers who can deliver consistently, follow SOPs, and scale with us across service lines.

**Who we partner with:**

- Registered agencies with 5+ team members
- Freelancers with proven international delivery experience
- White-label execution partners
- Channel and referral partners
