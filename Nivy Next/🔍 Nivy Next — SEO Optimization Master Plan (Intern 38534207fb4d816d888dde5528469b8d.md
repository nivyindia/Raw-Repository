# 🆕 New Pages Required

## 1. Refund & Cancellation Policy (/refund-cancellation-policy)

**Why critical:** Tier 1 clients (UK, US) do not sign contracts with agencies without reading a cancellation policy. This page builds trust, reduces close friction, and is referenced from Terms of Service, Pricing, and Footer.

**URL:** `/refund-cancellation-policy`

**SEO Meta Title:** `Refund & Cancellation Policy | Nivy Next`

**SEO Meta Description:** `Understand Nivy Next's cancellation notice periods, refund eligibility, and dispute process. Clear, fair terms for clients in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and UAE.`

**Index:** Yes — this page should be indexed (it builds trust and ranks for branded searches)

**Internal links from:** Pricing page, Terms of Service, Book a Call, Footer (every page)

**Content to write (full brief):**

- **Section 1 — Overview:** 2–3 sentences. "We aim to make every engagement clear, fair, and friction-free. This policy outlines how to cancel, what happens to in-progress work, and when refunds apply."
- **Section 2 — Cancellation Notice Period:** Either party may cancel a retainer by providing 30 calendar days' written notice to [contact@thenivy.com](mailto:contact@thenivy.com). Project-based engagements are governed by the agreed statement of work.
- **Section 3 — What Happens at Cancellation:** Work completed and delivered before the cancellation date is non-refundable. Work in progress is delivered in its current state within the 30-day notice window. All client account access and assets are handed over within 5 business days of engagement end.
- **Section 4 — Refund Eligibility:** Refunds ARE available for: prepaid retainer amounts covering periods after the confirmed cancellation date (pro-rated); duplicate/erroneous billing. Refunds are NOT available for: completed project deliverables; advertising spend already deployed on platforms (Google, Meta, LinkedIn); third-party tool/software costs incurred on the client's behalf; work already delivered under a milestone project.
- **Section 5 — How to Request:** Submit in writing to [contact@thenivy.com](mailto:contact@thenivy.com) with company name, invoice reference, and reason. Acknowledged within 2 business days. Resolved within 10 business days.
- **Section 6 — Processing Timeline:** Approved refunds processed within 7–14 business days. Issued in the same currency and payment method as the original transaction.
- **Section 7 — Disputes:** Unresolved disputes follow the process outlined in the Terms of Service.
- **Section 8 — Governing Law:** Same jurisdiction as Terms of Service (Founder to confirm: UK / India / Delaware).
- **Section 9 — Contact:** [contact@thenivy.com](mailto:contact@thenivy.com) | Response within 24 business hours.

# 🏗️ Phase 1 — Technical SEO Foundation

**Timeline:** Weeks 1–2 (before any page goes live)

**Owner:** Developer + SEO Lead

**Goal:** Site is technically clean, fast, indexable, and internationally signal-correct before a single page launches.

## 1.1 — Crawlability & Indexability

- [ ]  Create and submit **XML sitemap** (`/sitemap.xml`) — include all live pages, exclude `/thank-you`, `/book-a-call` confirmation pages, any admin routes
- [ ]  Write **robots.txt** — allow all service, blog, legal, and market pages; disallow `/admin`, `/dashboard`, any staging URLs
- [ ]  Add **canonical tags** to every page — self-referencing by default; cross-reference any URL variants that risk duplicate content (e.g., `/services/ecommerce-marketing` vs. any legacy path)
- [ ]  Configure **301 redirects** for any old URLs if domain has prior history
- [ ]  Create a branded **custom 404 page** — Nivy Next design, links to Homepage / Services / Contact
- [ ]  Verify **SSL certificate** is active — all pages load on HTTPS only, zero mixed-content warnings
- [ ]  Confirm **www vs non-www** is standardised with consistent redirect

## 1.2 — Site Speed & Core Web Vitals

**Target:** Lighthouse score >90 on all core pages | LCP <2.5s | FID/INP <200ms | CLS <0.1

- [ ]  Implement **Cloudflare CDN** (free plan covers essentials) — critical for Tier 1 market load times from an India-hosted server
- [ ]  Compress and serve all images as **WebP** format
- [ ]  Implement **lazy loading** for below-fold images and third-party embeds (Calendly, chat widgets)
- [ ]  Minify CSS, JS, and HTML
- [ ]  Enable **browser caching** headers
- [ ]  Load Calendly and any chat widget **on-demand** (not on initial page load)
- [ ]  Test Core Web Vitals on both **mobile and desktop** — UK and AU visitors are mobile-heavy
- [ ]  Run a **Lighthouse audit** on: Homepage, 1 service page, About, Pricing before launch

## 1.3 — International SEO: hreflang Tags

> The single most important technical SEO action for international client acquisition. Without hreflang, Google cannot associate your pages with the correct target markets.
> 

**Strategy:** Single domain with `/markets/` subdirectories (already planned in Phase 4 Content Plan). hreflang points from core pages to market-specific versions.

**Implementation (add to `<head>` on every core page):**

```html
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="en-gb" href="https://www.thenivy.com/markets/uk" />
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="en-us" href="https://www.thenivy.com/markets/us" />
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="en-ca" href="https://www.thenivy.com/markets/canada" />
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="en-au" href="https://www.thenivy.com/markets/australia" />
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="en-ae" href="https://www.thenivy.com/markets/uae" />
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="x-default" href="https://www.thenivy.com/" />
```

- [ ]  Each market page hreflang also points back to the main homepage (`x-default`)

## 8.3 — India-Hosted Server: International Latency Strategy

> A server based in India serving UK and US visitors creates additional latency — Cloudflare CDN (Phase 1) partially addresses this, but the following measures ensure maximum speed for Tier 1 markets.

- [ ]  Enable **Cloudflare's Tiered Cache** — ensures popular pages are served from the nearest POP without hitting the origin server
- [ ]  **DNS TTL** set to 300s or lower for faster DNS propagation if server changes are ever needed
