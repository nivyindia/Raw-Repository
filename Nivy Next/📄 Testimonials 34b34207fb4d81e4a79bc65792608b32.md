## Testimonials

Collect and store all client testimonials here for use in sales and marketing.

---

## How to Collect Testimonials

- Ask after a positive result
- Send a short form (3 questions max)
- Offer to write a draft they can approve

## Testimonial Questions to Ask Clients

1. What problem were you facing before working with us?
2. What results have you seen since we started working together?
3. Would you recommend Nivy Digital to others? Why?
