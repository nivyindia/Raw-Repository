# 8 · Our Process

1. **CRO Audit** — Full conversion audit: funnel analysis, heatmaps, session recordings, UX review
2. **Hypothesis Development** — Prioritise conversion barriers by impact and effort, build test hypotheses
3. **Test Design** — Create A/B test variants (copy, design, layout, CTA, social proof)
4. **Implementation** — Deploy tests via testing tool, ensure proper tracking
5. **Analysis** — Monitor statistical significance, analyse results, determine winners
6. **Implement & Iterate** — Roll out winning variants, begin next test cycle, report monthly

# 16 · FAQ

**How much traffic do I need for CRO to work?**

We recommend a minimum of 1,000 monthly visitors to a specific page or funnel for A/B tests to reach statistical significance in a reasonable timeframe. Below 500/month, we switch to a qualitative-first approach.

**How long before I see results?**

First month is audit and tracking setup. First A/B tests run in month 2. Winning variants are typically identified by weeks 6–8. Revenue impact compounds as more tests complete and are implemented.

**Do you redesign my website?**

No — we test changes on top of your existing site. We don’t recommend full redesigns before testing, as they destroy your existing baseline data.

**What if a test doesn’t improve conversions?**

That’s a result too. Negative or neutral tests eliminate bad ideas before they’re shipped permanently. Every test teaches us something. We learn and move to the next hypothesis.

**Can you run CRO alongside my paid media?**

Absolutely — this is the ideal setup. CRO and paid media are most powerful together: better landing pages make every ad dollar work harder.

**Do you implement the winning test variants?**

Yes — we implement all winning variants. Our team handles the technical deployment in your CMS or testing tool.
