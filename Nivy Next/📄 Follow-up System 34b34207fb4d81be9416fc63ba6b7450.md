# 📄 Follow-up System

## Follow-Up System

Use this to systematically follow up with prospects who didn’t close on the first call.

---

## Follow-Up Schedule

| Day | Action | Message Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Same-day recap email | Value summary + next step |
| Day 3 | Check-in message | Soft nudge |
| Day 7 | Case study share | Proof + re-engage |
| Day 14 | Final follow-up | Last call |

---

## Follow-Up Templates

### Day 1 Recap

> *"Hey [Name], great speaking with you today! As discussed, we can help you [result]. Here’s the package we talked about: [link]. Let me know if you have any questions!"*
> 

### Day 3 Check-In

> *"Hey [Name], just checking in — did you get a chance to look at the proposal? Happy to answer any questions."*
