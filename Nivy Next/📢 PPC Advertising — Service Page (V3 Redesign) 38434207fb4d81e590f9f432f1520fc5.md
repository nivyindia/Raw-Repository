# 16. FAQ

**Do you manage the ad spend or just the strategy?**

We manage campaigns end-to-end — strategy, build, optimisation, and reporting. Ad spend is billed directly by the platform, separate from our management fee.

**Our ROAS is currently negative. Can this be fixed?**

That's a common starting point. Our first move is always a full account audit to find where spend is leaking before recommending new budget.

**How fast can we launch?**

For most accounts, we can audit and restructure within 1–2 weeks, with optimised campaigns live shortly after.

**Can you take over a campaign mid-flight without pausing it?**

Yes — we audit live accounts and migrate improvements without interrupting active, revenue-generating campaigns.

**Do you build the landing pages too?**

Yes, our design and development teams build pages matched specifically to your ad campaigns and offers.

**What's the minimum ad spend to make this worthwhile?**

It depends on your market and CPC, but most clients see meaningful signal starting around $3,000–$5,000/month in spend.
