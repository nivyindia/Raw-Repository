# 16. FAQ

**How long does a full brand identity take?**

A Brand Launch Pack typically takes 2–3 weeks. Full brand and collateral builds take 3–4 weeks.

**Will we be able to edit the files ourselves?**

Yes — every project includes full source file handover (Figma, AI, or PSD) plus editable Canva templates where applicable.

**Do you only do branding, or ongoing design too?**

Both. We offer one-time brand builds and monthly design retainers for ongoing asset production.

**Our last designer's files weren't usable by our team — what's different here?**

We hand over complete, organised source files and a guidelines document so your team can extend the brand independently.

**Can you design for web and app interfaces too?**

Yes, our UI/UX design works directly alongside our Website and App Development teams.

**How is pricing determined?**

One-time projects are scoped by deliverable list; retainers are priced by monthly asset volume.
