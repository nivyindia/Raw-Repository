# 16 · FAQ

**Which platform should I advertise on?**

It depends on your audience and objective. B2B businesses typically see stronger returns on LinkedIn. B2C and e-commerce brands usually perform best on Meta and TikTok. We recommend based on your ICP, not what’s popular.

**How much should I spend?**

For meaningful testing and optimisation, we recommend $2,000–$3,000/month minimum in ad spend. Below that, the algorithm doesn’t have enough data to learn and optimise effectively.

**How long before I see results?**

First 2–4 weeks is the testing and learning phase. Most clients see scalable performance by week 5–8.

**Do you create the ad creatives?**

Yes — copy, static visuals, and short-form video (Growth and Scale tiers). We handle all creative production as part of the service.

**What’s the difference between this and Social Media Marketing?**

Social Media Marketing (SMM) is organic — building your audience through content. Social Media Advertising is paid — using budget to reach targeted audiences beyond your followers. Both work best together.

**Will I own all the accounts and creative assets?**

Yes. You own all accounts, creative files, and audience data. Always.
