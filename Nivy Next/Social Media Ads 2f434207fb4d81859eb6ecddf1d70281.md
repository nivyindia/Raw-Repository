# FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (SOCIAL MEDIA ADS – UAE)

### General Questions

**Q1. Do you guarantee leads or sales?**

No. We do not guarantee results. Advertising performance depends on many factors such as offer, market demand, budget, competition, and landing page quality. We focus on continuous optimization and ROI improvement.

**Q2. Is ad spend included in your package price?**

No. Ad spend is always **separate** and paid directly by the client to platforms like Meta, Google, TikTok, etc. Our fee is only for strategy, setup, management, and optimization.

**Q3. What is the minimum ad budget required in UAE?**

We recommend a minimum of **AED 2,000–5,000/month** to start testing. Competitive industries require higher budgets for meaningful data.

**Q4. Which platforms do you advertise on?**

We work with Facebook & Instagram (Meta), TikTok, Snapchat, Google Ads, and LinkedIn depending on your business and audience.

**Q5. How soon can I see results?**

Initial leads or traffic usually start within **7–14 days**, but stable performance typically takes **30–60 days**.

---

### Setup & Execution

**Q6. Do you set up ad accounts and tracking?**

Yes. We handle ad account setup, Business Manager, pixel installation, Conversion API, and event tracking (package dependent).

**Q7. Will you create ad creatives and copies?**

Yes. Ad creatives (images/videos) and ad copywriting are included as per package limits.

**Q8. Do you provide landing pages?**

Landing page strategy and guidance are included. Full landing page design is available as an add-on or included in higher packages.

**Q9. Can you run WhatsApp lead ads?**

Yes. WhatsApp, instant forms, website leads, calls, and DM-based campaigns are all supported.

**Q10. Do you run Arabic ads?**

Yes. Arabic or bilingual ads are available (copy and creative depend on package or add-on).

---

### Performance & Optimization

**Q11. How do you optimize ads?**

We optimize audiences, creatives, placements, bidding strategies, budgets, and funnels based on data.

**Q12. What KPIs do you track?**

Cost per lead (CPL), cost per acquisition (CPA), click-through rate (CTR), conversion rate, ROAS (for e-commerce), and overall ROI.

**Q13. How often do you optimize campaigns?**

- Starter & Growth: weekly / bi-weekly
- Performance & Enterprise: weekly to daily

**Q14. Will I get reports?**

Yes. You receive monthly reports. Higher packages include dashboards and strategy calls.

---

### Payments & Contracts

**Q15. What are the payment terms?**

Management fees are billed **monthly in advance**. Ad spend is paid directly to platforms.

**Q16. Is there a minimum commitment?**

Yes. Minimum commitment is **1–3 months**, depending on the package.

**Q17. Can I stop ads anytime?**

Yes, after the minimum commitment period.

**Q18. Are refunds available?**

No refunds once ad management work has started.

---

### Ownership, Access & Trust

**Q19. Who owns the ad account and data?**

You retain **100% ownership** of ad accounts, pixels, data, and audiences.

**Q20. Will you share full access?**

Yes. Full transparency and access are provided.

**Q21. Do you sign NDA or keep data confidential?**

Yes. Client data, ad data, and business information are kept strictly confidential.

---

### Scalability & Support

**Q22. Can you scale ads if results are good?**

Yes. Scaling is done gradually to maintain performance and control CPL/CPA.

**Q23. Can you handle multiple locations or branches?**

Yes. We can manage multi-location and multi-city campaigns across the UAE.

**Q24. Do you provide sales or CRM support?**

CRM integration and lead management support are available in higher packages or as add-ons.

**Q25. What if ads don’t work initially?**

We analyze data, adjust creatives, audiences, and offers. Ads require testing and optimization before stable results.
