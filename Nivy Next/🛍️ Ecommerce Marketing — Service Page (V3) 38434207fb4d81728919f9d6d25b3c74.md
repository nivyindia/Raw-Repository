# 16 · FAQ

**What revenue level do I need to be at to work with you?**

We recommend a minimum of $50K/month in revenue. Below that, some of our full-funnel strategies won’t have enough data to optimise properly. We can advise on the right entry point.

**Do you work with Shopify only?**

No. We work with Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, and custom ecommerce builds.

**What’s the difference between your ecommerce and digital marketing services?**

Ecommerce Marketing is a specialised package built specifically for product-based DTC brands. It combines the relevant channels (paid social, Google Shopping, email, SEO, CRO) into a coordinated growth system, with ecommerce-specific attribution and retention strategy.

**How do you handle creative for ads?**

We develop creative strategy and briefs, and can produce static and video ad creative. For UGC, we advise on creator sourcing and direction.

**Can you set up subscription and loyalty programmes?**

Yes — retention infrastructure is included in our Growth and Scale tiers.

**What attribution model do you use?**

We recommend data-driven attribution where sufficient data exists, supplemented by blended MER (marketing efficiency ratio) reporting. We implement Triple Whale or Northbeam for clients needing cross-channel clarity.
