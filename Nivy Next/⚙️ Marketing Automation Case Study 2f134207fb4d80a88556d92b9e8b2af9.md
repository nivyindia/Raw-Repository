## ✅ 1. B2B SaaS Automated Nurture & Conversion — USA

**Client Type:** SaaS Enterprise | USA

**Industry:** B2B Software

**Services:** Marketing Automation, Lead Nurturing, CRM Integration

### 🎯 Objective

Automate lead nurturing to convert trial users to paid customers and reduce manual sales intervention.

### ⚠️ Challenge

- Low trial-to-paid conversion (5.8%)
- Manual follow-ups causing missed leads
- High churn during onboarding

### 🛠 Strategy

- Behavioral-based automated email sequences
- Multi-step drip campaigns for onboarding & retention
- CRM-triggered notifications for sales team

### 🚀 Execution

- 20 automated sequences covering onboarding, upsell, and retention
- CRM-integrated lead scoring and activity tracking
- Personalized messaging based on feature usage

### 📈 Results

- **+158% trial-to-paid conversion rate**
- **34% lead response time**
- **+41% increase in onboarding engagement**
