# 📄 Full Conversation Flow (Start → Close)

## Full Conversation Flow

This is the end-to-end guide for a VA sales conversation from first contact to close.

---

## Stage 1 — Opening (0–2 min)

- Greet warmly
- State purpose quickly
- Build rapport with 1 observation about their business

## Stage 2 — Discovery (3–8 min)

- Ask qualification questions
- Listen actively, take notes
- Identify their #1 pain point

## Stage 3 — Presentation (5–10 min)

- Present the most relevant service/package
- Connect it directly to their pain point
- Share a relevant case study or result

## Stage 4 — Objection Handling (2–5 min)

- Listen fully before responding
- Use the Price Objection Responses from Pricing System
- Reframe to ROI

## Stage 5 — Close (1–2 min)

- Ask for the decision: *"Does this feel like the right fit?"*
- Offer next step: onboarding call, contract, or payment link

## Stage 6 — Follow-Up

- If no close, trigger Follow-up System
