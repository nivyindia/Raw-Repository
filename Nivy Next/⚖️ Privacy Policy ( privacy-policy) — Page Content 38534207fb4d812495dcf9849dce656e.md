# Privacy Policy

Nivy Next ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit [www.thenivy.com](http://www.thenivy.com), why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have over it — including the additional rights granted to residents of the UK and European Union under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

If you have any questions about this policy or how your data is handled, contact us at [contact@thenivy.com](mailto:contact@thenivy.com).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**Information you provide directly:**

- Name (first and last)
- Email address
- Company name and country
- Service interest and indicative monthly budget (submitted via our Contact form)
- Any message or details you choose to share with us
- Call scheduling details when you book a call via our Calendly integration (name, email, selected time, and any notes you provide)
- Messages you send through our website chatbot, Nina

**Information collected automatically:**

- IP address and approximate location
- Browser type, device type, and operating system
- Pages visited, time on page, and referral source
- Cookie identifiers (see Section 5 — Cookies)

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data (health, financial account details, government ID numbers, etc.) through our website. Payment details for active client engagements are collected and processed directly by Stripe and are never stored on our own systems.

---

## 2. How We Collect This Information

- Through forms on our website (primarily the Contact page and Book a Call page)
- Through our Calendly scheduling widget
- Through our website chatbot, Nina
- Through cookies and analytics tools (Google Analytics 4) as you browse the site
- Directly from you, if you email us or speak with us on a call

---

## 3. Why We Collect This Information

We process your information for the following purposes, on the following legal bases under GDPR:

| Purpose | Legal Basis |
| --- | --- |
| Responding to your enquiry | Legitimate interest / steps prior to entering a contract |
| Scheduling and managing discovery calls | Steps prior to entering a contract |
| Delivering services under an active engagement | Performance of a contract |
| Sending you marketing or service-related updates | Consent (you may withdraw this at any time) |
| Website analytics and improving site performance | Legitimate interest |
| Maintaining financial and tax records | Legal obligation |

We will never use your information for a purpose materially different from the one you gave it to us for, without asking first.

---

## 4. Who We Share It With

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, under any circumstances. We do share it with a limited number of service providers ("processors") who help us run our business, each bound by their own data protection obligations:

| Processor | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Calendly | Call scheduling and calendar management |
| Google Analytics 4 (Google) | Website traffic and behaviour analytics |
| HubSpot | CRM, enquiry management, and email communications |
| Stripe | Payment processing for active client engagements |

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our legal rights, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets (in which case you would be notified).

## 5. International Data Transfers

Nivy Next is headquartered in India and serves clients across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and UAE. If you are located in the UK or EU, your information may be transferred to and processed in India and other countries where our processors operate. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards — including Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms required by UK/EU data protection law — to ensure your data continues to receive an adequate level of protection.

## 6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to operate correctly and to understand how visitors use the site.

| Cookie Type | Purpose | Typical Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strictly Necessary | Core site functionality (navigation, form submission, security) | Session |
| Analytics (GA4) | Understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it | Up to 14 months |
| Functional | Remember preferences (e.g. chatbot conversation state) | Up to 30 days |

When you first visit our site, you'll be shown a cookie consent banner where you can accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your preference at any time through that banner, or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings to block or delete cookies — though doing so may affect how parts of the site function.

## 8. Your Rights

If you are located in the UK or EU, you have the following rights under GDPR with respect to your personal data:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Rectification** — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erasure** — ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to our legal retention obligations
- **Portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Object** — object to our processing your data, including for marketing purposes
- **Restrict Processing** — ask us to limit how we use your data while a request is resolved

To exercise any of these rights, email [contact@thenivy.com](mailto:contact@thenivy.com). We will respond within one month, as required by GDPR. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority — in the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

If you are a California resident, you have similar rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what personal information we collect and to request its deletion. We do not sell personal information.
