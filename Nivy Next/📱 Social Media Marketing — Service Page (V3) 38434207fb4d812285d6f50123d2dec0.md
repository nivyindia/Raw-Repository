# 16 · FAQ

**How long before we see growth?**

Most clients see meaningful engagement improvement within 60 days. Follower growth and inbound enquiries typically become consistent by month 3–4. Social media is a compounding channel.

**Which platforms do you manage?**

Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, TikTok, X (Twitter), YouTube, and Pinterest. We recommend platforms based on your audience — not what’s trendy.

**Do we need to provide content ideas?**

No. We handle ideation, production, and strategy. You’ll review and approve the monthly calendar before anything is published.

**Will the content sound like us?**

Yes. We run a brand voice discovery session at the start of every engagement to capture your tone, language preferences, and what to avoid.

**Do you handle paid social too?**

Paid social (Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads) is a separate service. See our Social Media Advertising page.

**What happens to the accounts if we stop?**

You own all your accounts, content, and assets. We handover everything fully documented.
