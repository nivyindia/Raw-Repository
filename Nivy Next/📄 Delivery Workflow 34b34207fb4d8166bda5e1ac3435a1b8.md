# 📄 Delivery Workflow

## Service Delivery Workflow

This is the master workflow for delivering all Nivy Digital services.

---

## Monthly Cycle

| Week | Tasks |
| --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Content planning, strategy review, ad audit |
| Week 2 | Content creation, ad optimization |
| Week 3 | Content scheduling, mid-month performance check |
| Week 4 | Reporting, client communication, next month planning |

---

## Daily Workflow (per VA)

1. Check all client ad accounts — flag anomalies
2. Post/schedule any pending content
3. Reply to comments/DMs (if community management is included)
4. Log daily tasks in project tracker
