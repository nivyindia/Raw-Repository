# 📄 Funnel System

## What is a Funnel System?

A step-by-step marketing system that guides a prospect from awareness to paying client.

---

## Funnel Stages

1. **Awareness** — Ad or content brings them in
2. **Interest** — Landing page or lead magnet captures them
3. **Consideration** — Email sequence or retargeting nurtures them
4. **Decision** — Offer page or call closes them
5. **Retention** — Follow-up keeps them or upsells them

## Client Pain Points We Solve

- “People visit my site but don’t buy”
- “I don’t have a system, just random marketing”
- “I need something that works while I sleep”

## How to Explain This to a Client

> *"A funnel is your automated sales machine. It captures leads, nurtures them, and guides them to buy — all without you having to manually follow up with every person."*
