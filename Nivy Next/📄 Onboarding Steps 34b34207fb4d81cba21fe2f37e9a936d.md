# 📄 Onboarding Steps

## Client Onboarding Steps

Follow these steps in order for every new client.

---

### Step 1 — Welcome & Contract

- Send welcome email
- Share contract for signature
- Collect first payment

### Step 2 — Onboarding Form

- Send client the Onboarding Form
- Review answers with the team

### Step 3 — Access Collection

- Collect all required logins and assets (see Access Checklist)

### Step 4 — Kickoff Call

- Schedule 30–60 min kickoff call
- Walk through strategy, expectations, and timelines

### Step 5 — Internal Briefing

- Brief the delivery team on client goals and package

### Step 6 — Execution Begins

- Follow the Service Delivery System to start work
