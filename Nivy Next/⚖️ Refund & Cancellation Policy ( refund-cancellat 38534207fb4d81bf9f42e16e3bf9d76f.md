# Refund & Cancellation Policy

## Overview

We aim to make every engagement clear, fair, and friction-free. This policy outlines how to cancel, what happens to in-progress work, and when refunds apply. It applies to all clients of Nivy Next, regardless of location.

## Cancellation Notice Period

Either party may cancel a retainer agreement by providing **30 calendar days' written notice** to [contact@thenivy.com](mailto:contact@thenivy.com). Project-based engagements are governed by the cancellation terms set out in the agreed Statement of Work (SOW) for that project.

## What Happens at Cancellation

- Work completed and delivered before the cancellation date is **non-refundable**.
- Work in progress is delivered in its current state within the 30-day notice window.
- All client account access, files, and assets are handed over within **5 business days** of the engagement end date.

## Refund Eligibility

**Refunds ARE available for:**

- Prepaid retainer amounts covering periods after the confirmed cancellation date (pro-rated)
- Duplicate or erroneous billing

**Refunds are NOT available for:**

- Completed project deliverables
- Advertising spend already deployed on platforms (Google, Meta, LinkedIn)
- Third-party tool or software costs incurred on the client's behalf
- Work already delivered under a milestone-based project

## How to Request a Refund

Submit your request in writing to [contact@thenivy.com](mailto:contact@thenivy.com), including your company name, invoice reference, and reason for the request.

- Acknowledged within **2 business days**
- Resolved within **10 business days**

## Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are processed within **7–14 business days**, issued in the same currency and via the same payment method as the original transaction.

## Disputes

Any dispute that cannot be resolved through the refund request process above follows the dispute resolution process outlined in our Terms of Service.

## Governing Law

This policy is governed by the laws of **Delaware, United States** — *pending Founder confirmation; to be aligned with the jurisdiction confirmed for the Terms of Service.*

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Email [contact@thenivy.com](mailto:contact@thenivy.com). We respond to all enquiries within **24 business hours**.
