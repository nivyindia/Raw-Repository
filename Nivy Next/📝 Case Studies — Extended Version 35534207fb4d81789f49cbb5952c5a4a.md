## 📊 EXTENDED CASE STUDY 1

### Scaling a B2B SaaS Company from 40 to 210 Qualified Leads Per Month

*Industry: B2B SaaS | Market: United Kingdom | Services: Digital Marketing, AI Automation, CRM Integration*

---

#### 🔍 THE SITUATION

A UK-based B2B SaaS company providing workflow automation tools for mid-market enterprises came to Nivy Next with a familiar but painful challenge: they had a genuinely strong product, a proven sales team, and a healthy existing customer base — but their lead pipeline was inconsistent, largely dependent on referrals, and showing no predictable pattern of growth.

Their existing marketing consisted of a basic Google Ads account that hadn't been optimised in 18 months, an undifferentiated LinkedIn presence with minimal content, and no marketing automation beyond a basic Mailchimp newsletter.

The leadership team had recently closed a seed round and needed to demonstrate consistent pipeline growth to their investors within two quarters.

---

#### 🎯 THE STRATEGY

After a two-week discovery phase that included a full funnel audit, ICP interviews, and a competitive positioning analysis, Nivy Next identified three core leverage points:

1. **Demand capture was broken** — The Google Ads account was targeting broad, unqualified intent terms with a landing page that had a 2.1% conversion rate. This was the fastest fix.
2. **Demand generation was missing** — No systematic effort existed to create demand through content or social. LinkedIn was treated as a CV rather than a distribution channel.
3. **Lead qualification was manual and slow** — Leads were being entered into a CRM manually, scored by gut feeling, and followed up inconsistently. This was creating a false picture of pipeline quality.

The strategy was structured around three simultaneous workstreams:

- Rebuild Google Ads with intent-driven targeting and a redesigned landing page
- Launch a LinkedIn thought leadership programme for the two co-founders
- Build a lead scoring and qualification automation using Zapier, HubSpot, and a custom scoring model

---

#### ⚙️ THE EXECUTION

**Month 1 — Foundation**

- Full Google Ads rebuild: restructured campaign architecture, rewrote ad copy, built new landing page with conversion-focused layout
- ICP-driven keyword targeting replacing broad match terms
- HubSpot audit and data cleanup; existing 1,400 contacts re-segmented
- LinkedIn profile optimisations for both founders; content calendar drafted

**Month 2 — Activation**

- LinkedIn publishing programme launched: 3 posts per week per founder, distribution amplified through employee advocacy
- Lead scoring model built and integrated: scoring based on industry, company size, engagement behaviour, and email open patterns
- Automated nurture sequences built for three lead segments: hot (immediate follow-up), warm (7-day nurture), cold (30-day content sequence)
- First A/B test on landing page launched

**Month 3–4 — Optimisation**

- Winning landing page variant deployed (improved conversion: 2.1% → 4.8%)
- Google Ads ROAS optimised month-on-month
- LinkedIn generating consistent inbound DMs; two enterprise conversations opened organically
- Full CRM automation live: zero manual lead entry, all leads scored and routed automatically

---

#### 📈 THE RESULTS

| Metric | Before | After (Month 4) | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly qualified leads | 40 | 210 | **+425%** |
| Landing page CVR | 2.1% | 4.8% | **+129%** |
| Cost per qualified lead | £148 | £62 | **−58%** |
| LinkedIn follower growth | 820 | 3,200 | **+290%** |
| Sales cycle length | 38 days | 25 days | **−34%** |
| CRM data accuracy | ~40% | 98% | **+145%** |

---

#### 💬 CLIENT FEEDBACK

*"Before Nivy Next, our pipeline was a black box. We had no idea where leads came from, why some converted, or what was actually working. They gave us a system. Now I can look at our CRM on any given Monday and know exactly what our next quarter looks like."*

- Co-Founder & CEO, B2B SaaS Platform, United Kingdom

---

#### 💡 KEY LESSON

The biggest unlock wasn't the ads or the content — it was connecting all three parts of the funnel into a single, trackable system. Most agencies fix one thing. The compound effect of fixing everything simultaneously is where real growth happens.

## 📊 EXTENDED CASE STUDY 2

### Taking a D2C Brand from Unprofitable Ads to 4.1x ROAS in Six Months

*Industry: D2C E-Commerce | Market: Australia | Services: Paid Social, CRO, Creative Strategy*

---

#### 🔍 THE SITUATION

An Australian direct-to-consumer skincare brand had been running Facebook and Instagram ads for 14 months with a peak ROAS of 1.3x — meaning they were losing money on every ad dollar spent. Their previous agency had been running a single ad set with the same creative for six months, providing monthly PDF reports with no actionable commentary.

The brand had genuine product quality — a 4.7-star rating across 380+ reviews on their Shopify store. The gap was entirely in their marketing infrastructure.

---

#### 🎯 THE STRATEGY

Nivy Next's audit identified three critical failures:

1. **Creative fatigue** — same 4 ad creatives running for 6 months with no refresh
2. **No testing infrastructure** — no systematic A/B framework, no creative learning loop
3. **Landing page misalignment** — ads driving to the homepage (conversion rate: 1.8%) with no product-specific landing pages

Strategy: Build a creative testing engine, rebuild the campaign architecture, and rebuild landing pages for each product category.

---

#### ⚙️ THE EXECUTION

**Month 1:** Creative audit, 12 new ad creatives produced (UGC-style, static, video), campaign architecture rebuilt with structured testing framework.

**Month 2–3:** Systematic creative testing: 3 new creatives per week tested, winners scaled, losers killed within 7 days. Landing pages built for 4 product categories.

**Month 4–6:** Winning creative formats scaled, Advantage+ Shopping campaigns layered in, retargeting flows rebuilt with sequential messaging.

---

#### 📈 THE RESULTS

| Metric | Before | After 6 Months | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ROAS | 1.3x | 4.1x | **+215%** |
| Monthly revenue | A$28,000 | A$47,000 | **+68%** |
| Landing page CVR | 1.8% | 4.4% | **+144%** |
| Cost per purchase | A$67 | A$21 | **−69%** |
| Creative refresh rate | Every 6 months | Every 7 days | Systematic |

---

#### 💬 CLIENT FEEDBACK

*"We'd spent 14 months and tens of thousands of dollars with our previous agency and never once broke even on ads. Nivy Next fixed it in 90 days. By month six we were the most profitable we'd ever been. I only wish we'd switched sooner."*

- Founder, D2C Skincare Brand, Sydney, Australia
