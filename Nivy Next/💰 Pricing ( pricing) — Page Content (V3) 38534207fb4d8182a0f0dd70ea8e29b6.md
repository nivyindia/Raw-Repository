# 2 · How Our Pricing Works

Every service is available in three tiers:

**🔹 Starter** — Single-focus engagement. Best for testing a new channel or launching from zero. Month-to-month flexibility.

**🔷 Growth** — Multi-deliverable programme. Best for scaling across channels with more volume, more frequency, and more strategic depth. 3-month minimum.

**💠 Scale** — High-output, multi-market or enterprise-grade engagement. Custom scoping on most services. 3-month minimum, often 6.

> Ad spend, media budgets, and third-party tool subscriptions are always separate from management fees. You own all accounts. Always.

# 10 · Pricing FAQ

**Do you charge in my local currency?**

All pricing is in USD. GBP, AUD, and CAD equivalents are confirmed at scoping.

**Are there setup fees?**

No setup fees on standard Starter, Growth, or Scale retainers. Custom AI builds and enterprise projects include a one-time scoping and build fee, detailed in your proposal.

**Is ad spend included in the management fee?**

Never. Your ad spend goes directly to Google, Meta, LinkedIn, or TikTok — billed to your card, fully visible to you. We charge a management fee for our time and expertise.

**What's the minimum commitment?**

Starter plans are month-to-month. Growth and Scale tiers carry a 3-month minimum — enough time to deliver results worth measuring.

**Can I combine services?**

Yes — most clients on Growth or Scale tiers combine 2–3 services. Growth Systems bundles above are pre-packaged combinations. Custom multi-service combinations are scoped on a discovery call.

**What happens after the minimum term?**

Plans continue month-to-month with 30 days' notice to pause or exit. No auto-escalation, no surprise price increases.

**Do you offer refunds?**

We don't offer refunds on work delivered. If you're unhappy with any element of the engagement, we fix it. We have a strong track record of doing exactly that.
