# 📐 18-Section Master Template (Reference)

- View the full 18-section template structure
    
    
    | # | Section | What Goes Here | Notes |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | 1 | Hero | Outcome H1 + sub-copy + 2 CTAs + trust line | Service-specific outcome in H1 |
    | 2 | Trust Bar | Platform/tool logos for this service | Change per service |
    | 3 | Problems We Solve | Pain list 7-9 specific problems | Personal, not generic |
    | 4 | Is This Right For You? | Qualification checklist | From original template |
    | 5 | Services Included | Grid of capabilities + descriptions | Solve the problems |
    | 6 | Business Impact Stats | 4-6 large stat cards | Real or sourced numbers only |
    | 7 | Why Nivy Next | 4 feature cards, outcome-focused | Works with your stack |
    | 8 | Our Process | 6-step numbered timeline | Consistent 6 steps |
    | 9 | Popular Use Cases | Company-type scenarios + mini-results | 4-6 examples |
    | 10 | Deliverables Checklist | Everything included, specific | No vague items |
    | 11 | Industries Served | Emoji + industry name grid | Consistent across all 18 |
    | 12 | Case Study Preview | 1-2 mini case studies with numbers | Real numbers only |
    | 13 | Testimonial | 1 service-relevant testimonial | Match to this service |
    | 14 | Technology Stack | Tools used for this service | From original template |
    | 15 | Pricing | 3-tier (Starter / Growth / Scale) | See V3 Part F |
    | 16 | FAQ | 6 questions incl. 1 scary objection | Service-specific |
    | 17 | Related Services | 2-3 cross-sell services with context | From original template |
    | 18 | Final CTA | Provocative headline + 2 buttons | Write specifically per service |
