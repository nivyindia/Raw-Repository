## 9. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These Terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with these Terms or any engagement will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

*Drafting note for Founder: England & Wales is recommended as the default jurisdiction for an internationally-facing agency. It is well-recognised and trusted by clients in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia; it creates no disadvantage relative to an India jurisdiction for international clients; and it avoids the perception that dispute resolution would require the client to litigate in India. If you prefer India (Uttar Pradesh) for operational cost reasons, or Delaware (USA) for US enterprise clients, the clause above should be replaced accordingly and reviewed by a solicitor (for UK) or attorney (for US/Delaware) before publication. The governing law is the most important clause that enterprise buyers check before signing.*
