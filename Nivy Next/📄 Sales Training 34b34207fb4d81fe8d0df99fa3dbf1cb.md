# 📄 Sales Training

## Sales Training Guide

This is the core sales training for all Nivy Digital VAs.

---

## The 5 Rules of Sales at Nivy Digital

1. **Listen more than you talk** — You can’t solve a problem you don’t understand
2. **Sell outcomes, not features** — Clients don’t care about posts; they care about leads
3. **Handle objections with empathy** — Never argue; always reframe
4. **Follow up consistently** — Most deals close after 3–5 touchpoints
5. **Know your numbers** — Be able to show ROI instantly

---

## The VA Sales Mindset

> *"I’m not selling them a service. I’m solving their biggest business problem."*
> 

---

## Key Sales Skills to Develop

- Active listening
- Objection handling
- Storytelling with case studies
- Closing with confidence
- Following up without being pushy
