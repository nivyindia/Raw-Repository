# 🔑 Universal Content Improvement Rules

> These rules apply to EVERY page on the site. Apply before writing a single word of copy.
> 

**Rule 1 — Outcome-First Headlines**

Every H1 and H2 must state a business outcome, not a service description. Not "SEO Services" — "Rank Higher, Get Found, Convert More." The client's goal, not your service name.

**Rule 2 — International Trust Signals on Every Page**

Every page must show: (a) markets served with flags, (b) one certification or tool badge, (c) a response/onboarding commitment. International clients need reassurance on every page, not just the homepage.

**Rule 3 — Specificity Over Generality**

Replace every vague claim with a number, a timeframe, or a named outcome. "We improve your results" → "Clients typically see a 30–60% reduction in cost per acquisition within 90 days."

**Rule 4 — Replace All Placeholder Content Before Launch**

Zero tolerance for template emails ([amer.hq@yourcompany.com](mailto:amer.hq@yourcompany.com)), sample testimonials marked "To add real testimonials:", or case studies marked "Sample." These are trust-killers for international enterprise clients.

**Rule 5 — GDPR Compliance Language**

Every form must include a GDPR consent checkbox. Every page footer must link to Privacy Policy. UK/EU clients will not engage with sites that skip this.

**Rule 6 — Consistent CTA Architecture**

Primary CTA: service-specific ("Get a Free SEO Audit"). Secondary CTA: "Book a Strategy Call". Never two identical CTAs on the same page. Never a generic "Contact Us" as the only option.

**Rule 7 — Social Proof Proximity**

A testimonial or stat must appear within 2 scroll lengths of every CTA. If you ask someone to act, show them why they should trust you first.
