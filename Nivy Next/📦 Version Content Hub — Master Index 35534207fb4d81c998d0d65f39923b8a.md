## 📋 Version Key

| Version | Best Used For | Length | Tone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🌐 Website | Hero copy, service pages, about page | Medium (150–400 words) | Clear, outcome-focused, modern |
| 📄 Brochure | PDF proposals, print, email attachments | Compact (100–250 words) | Persuasive, visual-friendly |
| 📊 PPT / Sales Deck | Slide presentations, pitches | Short (30–80 words per slide) | Bold, punchy, bullet-driven |
| 📱 Social Media | LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter/X | Very short (50–150 words) | Hook-based, engaging, direct |
| 🗂️ Business Directory | Clutch, Upwork, GoodFirms | Professional (100–200 words) | Trust-focused, platform-optimised |
| 📝 Extended | Proposals, long-form, enterprise | Long (400–800 words) | Detailed, authoritative |
