# 🎨 Part D — Design System

- 🎨 Colour System
    
    
    | Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Primary Background | Deep Navy | #0A0F1E | Hero sections, dark panels |
    | Secondary Background | Midnight | #111827 | Cards, sections |
    | Surface | Dark Slate | #1C2333 | Card backgrounds |
    | Border | Subtle Blue | #1E3A5F | Dividers, card outlines |
    | Brand Accent | Electric Blue | #3B82F6 | CTAs, links, highlights |
    | Accent 2 | Vivid Cyan | #06B6D4 | AI/tech elements |
    | Premium Gold | Warm Gold | #F59E0B | Premium tier, awards |
    | Text Primary | Pure White | #FFFFFF | Headlines on dark |
    | Text Secondary | Cool Grey | #94A3B8 | Body copy |
    | Success | Emerald | #10B981 | Results, positive metrics |
    
    **Design principle:** Dark-first (builds premium, tech-forward perception) with bright accent CTA buttons.
    
- ✍️ Typography System
    
    **Primary Font (Headings):** Inter or Syne
    
    - H1: 64–80px, weight 800, tight letter-spacing
    - H2: 40–48px, weight 700
    - H3: 28–32px, weight 600
    - H4: 20–24px, weight 600
    
    **Body Font:** Inter — 16–18px, weight 400, line-height 1.6
    
    **Optional accent font:** Bricolage Grotesque or Space Grotesk for key taglines
    
    **Rule:** Never more than 3 font sizes in a single section. Headlines readable in 2 seconds on mobile.
    
- 📱 UX & Layout Principles
    1. **Above-the-Fold Rule** — Within 3 seconds, visitor must understand: who you are, what you do, who it's for, what to do next.
    2. **3-Trust-Signal Rule** — Every page: 1 certification/logo/stat + 1 testimonial/rating + clear company identity visible without scrolling.
    3. **Mobile-First** — Single column, 44px minimum touch targets, sticky header with click-to-call, horizontal-scroll trust strips.
    4. **Page Speed** — Target <2.5 second load on 3G. Core Web Vitals all green. WebP images, lazy loading, preload fonts.
    5. **Sticky CTA Header** — After 300px scroll, Book a Call always visible.
    6. **International SEO Setup** — hreflang tags (Phase 2), region-specific keywords, Schema markup: Organization, Service, FAQ, LocalBusiness.

- 🤖 Chatbot — Full Specification
    
    ## Strategy: Build In-House
    
    **The opportunity:** Your chatbot IS the best live demo of what you sell. Add a subtle note: *"This assistant is built on the same AI automation stack we build for our clients."*
    
    ## Three-Layer System
    
    **Layer 1 — AI Chatbot (24/7):** Voiceflow + Claude API | Bot name: "Nina" or "Nex" | Position: bottom-right | Opens after 5 seconds
    
    **Opener:** "Hi! I'm Nina, Nivy Next's AI assistant. Are you looking to grow your business, automate your operations, or build something new?"
    
    **Conversation Flow:**
    
    1. "What brings you to Nivy Next today?" → A) Marketing | B) Website/App | C) Automation | D) Not sure
    2. "What's your biggest challenge right now?" → 3–4 quick-select + free text
    3. "Would you like to book a free 30-minute strategy call?" → Yes (Calendly) | Tell me more | Cost? | Not ready (email capture)
    
    **Layer 2 — Human Handoff:** [Crisp.chat](http://Crisp.chat) | <5 min response during business hours | Trigger on "human" / "speak to someone"
    
    **Layer 3 — WhatsApp Sticky:** Bottom-left, always visible, pre-populated template message
    
    ## What NOT to Do
    
    - Don't use generic chat widget without customisation
    - Don't trigger popup immediately on page load — wait 5–10 seconds
    - Don't hide the WhatsApp button — highest mobile conversion action
    - Don't use robotic phrases like "I'm happy to help with that!"
