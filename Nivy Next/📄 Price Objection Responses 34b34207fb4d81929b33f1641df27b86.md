# 📄 Price Objection Responses

## Price Objection Responses

Use these when a prospect says the price is too high.

---

### Objection: “It’s too expensive”

> *"I understand. Can I ask — compared to what? If one new client from this campaign is worth $[X], the package pays for itself. What’s stopping you from making that investment?"*
> 

### Objection: “I can’t afford it right now”

> *"That’s actually why clients come to us — because they can’t afford NOT to grow. Let’s start with our Starter package and scale from there."*
> 

### Objection: “I found someone cheaper”

> *"Cheaper is great if it works. We compete on results, not price. Can I show you what we’ve delivered for similar businesses so you can compare apples to apples?"*
> 

### Objection: “I need to think about it”

> *"Of course. What’s the main thing holding you back? If it’s budget, timing, or trust — let’s address that now."*
>
