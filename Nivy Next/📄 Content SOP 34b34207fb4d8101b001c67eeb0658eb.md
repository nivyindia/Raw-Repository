# 📄 Content SOP

## Content SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)

Follow this process for every content piece created for clients.

---

## Step 1 — Content Planning

- Review content calendar
- Identify upcoming posts for the week
- Check brand guidelines

## Step 2 — Content Creation

- Design in Canva (or agreed tool)
- Write caption using brand voice
- Add relevant hashtags

## Step 3 — Internal Review

- VA Lead reviews for quality and brand alignment
- Request revision if needed

## Step 4 — Client Approval (if required)

- Share content via approval link or DM
- Wait for sign-off before scheduling

## Step 5 — Scheduling

- Schedule via approved tool (Buffer / Later / Meta Planner)
- Confirm posting time and platform

## Step 6 — Performance Check

- 48–72 hours after posting, log engagement metrics
- Flag top/low performers for strategy review

---

*[Adapt this SOP per client based on their approval preferences]*
