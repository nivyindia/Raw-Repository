# 16 · FAQ

**How quickly will I rank in Google Maps?**

Most clients see meaningful map pack movement in 6–8 weeks. Top 3 positions in competitive markets typically take 3–4 months of consistent effort.

**What’s the most important factor for local ranking?**

Google’s three local ranking factors are proximity, relevance, and prominence. We optimise for all three: your GBP completeness and activity (relevance), your review count and quality (prominence), and local landing pages (relevance for service areas).

**Can you manage multiple locations?**

Yes. Our Scale tier is built for businesses with 3–10 locations, each receiving individual GBP management and local strategy.

**How do you get more reviews?**

We build a systematic review request workflow — email sequences, QR codes, in-person prompts, and SMS campaigns — that makes it easy for happy customers to leave a review at the right moment.

**Do you respond to negative reviews?**

Yes. We monitor all reviews and respond within 24 hours — both positive and negative. Negative review responses are crafted to protect your reputation and demonstrate professionalism.

**Do you run Google Ads for local businesses?**

Yes — Local Services Ads and geo-targeted Google Search campaigns are available as an add-on or as part of our Growth and Scale packages.
