# 16. FAQ

**How long does a typical website build take?**

A Starter site launches in 3–4 weeks. Larger or custom builds take 4–12+ weeks depending on scope.

**Will I be able to edit the site myself after launch?**

Yes — every build includes CMS training so your team can manage content without a developer.

**What if my current site is on a platform you don't use?**

We can rebuild on Webflow, WordPress, or Next.js, or work within your existing stack depending on the goal.

**Do you handle SEO during the build?**

Yes, technical SEO foundations (speed, schema, meta, site structure) are included in every build.

**My last agency disappeared after launch — what's different here?**

Every project includes a defined post-launch support window and documentation, so you're never stranded.

**Can you integrate with our CRM and ad platforms?**

Yes — CRM, analytics, and ad-pixel integration are part of every build, not an afterthought.
