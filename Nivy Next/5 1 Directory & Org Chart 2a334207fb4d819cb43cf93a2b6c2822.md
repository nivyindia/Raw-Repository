## 🧱 **POSITION LEVEL SYSTEM (For All Departments)**

| Level | Position Type | Example Titles | Reports To | Scope & Decision Power | Key Skills | Approx. Monthly Salary Band (₹ INR) | Description / Responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **L8** | **Founder / CEO** | CEO, Founder, Managing Director | Board / Owner | Full strategic control, defines company vision, approves budgets, hires C-suite | Visionary thinking, leadership, strategic finance, M&A | ₹5L – ₹15L+ | Leads company, sets global direction, oversees P&L and all C-level leaders |
| **L7** | **C-Suite Executive** | COO, CMO, CSO, CTO, CFO, CHRO, CDO | CEO | Full-department authority, policy and goal setting, budget management | Domain mastery, decision making, leadership, analytics | ₹2.5L – ₹6L | Leads entire department, aligns departmental goals with company strategy |
| **L6** | **Director / Head / VP** | Global Ops Head, Marketing Head, Tech Director | C-Suite | Manages multiple sub-departments or regions, defines departmental KPIs | Strategic management, team building, operational leadership | ₹1.2L – ₹3L | Translates strategy into actionable plans for divisions and managers |
| **L5** | **Manager / Regional / Senior Lead** | Country Manager, Project Manager, Campaign Manager | Director / Head | Leads teams of 5–20, approves campaigns/projects, reports on performance | Leadership, project management, communication, planning | ₹80K – ₹2L | Oversees daily execution, ensures targets and quality across teams |
| **L4** | **Senior Executive / Specialist / Team Lead** | SEO Lead, QA Lead, Finance Analyst, Brand Manager | Manager / Lead | Executes high-level tasks, supervises small teams, mentors juniors | Technical expertise, reporting, client communication | ₹50K – ₹1.2L | Executes campaigns, delivers quality outputs, provides performance reports |
| **L3** | **Executive / Associate** | Content Executive, Ads Executive, Developer, HR Associate | Senior Executive / Manager | Task execution, deliverables under supervision | Technical skill, reliability, speed, accuracy | ₹25K – ₹70K | Performs day-to-day work and contributes to project goals |
| **L2** | **Assistant / Coordinator / Junior** | Marketing Assistant, HR Coordinator, Sales Coordinator | Executive / Team Lead | Supports operations, prepares reports, data entry, scheduling | MS Office, admin support, organization | ₹15K – ₹35K | Provides support to main team, learns processes |
| **L1** | **Intern / Trainee / Apprentice** | Intern (SEO, Design, Sales, HR, etc.) | Executive / Manager | Learning role, task-based contribution | Curiosity, learning speed, adaptability | ₹5K – ₹15K | Training-focused position for skill development before hiring full-time |
