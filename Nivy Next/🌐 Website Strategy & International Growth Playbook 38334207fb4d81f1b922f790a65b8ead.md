# 🎨 Part C — Design Direction & Visual System

> This section defines the visual language that will make Nivy Next look internationally credible, premium, and modern — comparable to top-tier agencies in the US and UK.
> 
- 🎨 Color System
    
    ## Recommended Color Palette
    
    | Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Primary Background | Deep Navy | #0A0F1E | Hero sections, dark panels |
    | Secondary Background | Midnight | #111827 | Cards, sections |
    | Surface | Dark Slate | #1C2333 | Card backgrounds |
    | Border | Subtle Blue | #1E3A5F | Dividers, card outlines |
    | Brand Accent | Electric Blue | #3B82F6 | CTAs, links, highlights |
    | Accent 2 | Vivid Cyan | #06B6D4 | Secondary highlights, AI/tech elements |
    | Premium Gold | Warm Gold | #F59E0B | Premium tier, awards, highlights |
    | Text Primary | Pure White | #FFFFFF | Headlines on dark backgrounds |
    | Text Secondary | Cool Grey | #94A3B8 | Body copy, supporting text |
    | Success | Emerald | #10B981 | Results, positive metrics |
    
    **Design principle:** Dark-first (builds premium, tech-forward perception) with bright accent CTA buttons that command attention.
    
- ✍️ Typography System
    
    ## Typography
    
    **Primary Font (Headings):** Inter or Syne
    
    - H1: 64–80px, weight 800, tight letter-spacing
    - H2: 40–48px, weight 700
    - H3: 28–32px, weight 600
    - H4: 20–24px, weight 600
    
    **Body Font:** Inter
    
    - Body: 16–18px, weight 400, line-height 1.6
    - Small: 14px, weight 400
    - Labels: 12–13px, weight 500, uppercase, tracked
    
    **Optional accent font:** Bricolage Grotesque or Space Grotesk for key taglines
    
    **Hierarchy rule:** Never use more than 3 font sizes in a single section. Headlines should be readable in 2 seconds on a phone screen.
    
- 🖼️ Visual Component Library
    
    ## Core Visual Elements
    
    **1. Hero Section**
    
    - Full-viewport height, subtle animated grid or particle background (CSS/JS, lightweight)
    - Gradient overlay from dark navy to transparent
    - Bold headline left-aligned (more editorial, more credible than centred)
    - CTA buttons floating above the background
    
    **2. Cards**
    
    - Rounded corners: 12–16px radius
    - Subtle border: 1px semi-transparent blue
    - Glassmorphism effect (backdrop-filter: blur, semi-transparent background)
    - Hover state: slight lift (box-shadow) + border brightens
    
    **3. Stat Counters**
    
    - Large animated count-up numbers (triggered on scroll)
    - Thin label below in grey
    - Accent colour for the number itself
    
    **4. CTA Buttons**
    
    - Primary: Electric Blue fill, white text, 8px rounded, padding 14px 28px
    - Secondary: Transparent with Electric Blue border and text
    - Hover: Scale 1.02 + brightness up
    
    **5. Section Separators**
    
    - Alternating dark/light panels (not constant divider lines)
    - Diagonal or curved section breaks (SVG clip-path)
    - Subtle grid line overlay on dark sections
    
    **6. Trust Badges**
    
    - White logos on dark strip
    - Slightly desaturated to avoid colour clashing
    - Horizontal scroll on mobile
    
    **7. Country/Market Flags**
    
    - Flag emojis or SVG flag icons paired with country name in small label
    - Used in: hero section, geographic reach, testimonials
- 📱 UX & Layout Principles
    
    ## International UX Standards
    
    **1. Above-the-Fold Rule**
    
    Within 3 seconds of landing, a visitor must understand: who you are, what you do, who it's for, and what to do next. Nothing else goes above the fold.
    
    **2. The 3-Trust-Signal Rule**
    
    Every page must have at least 3 trust signals visible without scrolling: a certification badge OR client logo OR stat, a real testimonial quote or rating, and clear company identity (name, location, contact).
    
    **3. Mobile-First Design**
    
    US, UK, and AU browse heavily on mobile. Design mobile-first: single column, 44px minimum touch targets, sticky header with click-to-call icon, horizontal-scroll trust strips.
    
    **4. Page Speed Standards**
    
    Target: <2.5 second load on 3G. Core Web Vitals all green. WebP images with lazy loading. Preload critical fonts. No heavy JS on initial load.
    
    **5. Navigation Structure**
    
    - Desktop: Logo | Services (dropdown) | Results | About | Blog | [Book a Call] (filled button)
    - Mobile: Hamburger to full-screen overlay
    - Services dropdown: Mega-menu with icon + service name cards
    
    **6. Sticky CTA Header**
    
    After 300px scroll, a sticky header bar appears with the Book a Call CTA button always visible.
    
    **7. International SEO Setup**
    
    - HTML lang attribute per target market
    - hreflang tags for regional targeting (Phase 2)
    - Region-specific keywords in page titles and meta descriptions
    - Schema markup: Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ
    - Google Search Console geographic target settings
