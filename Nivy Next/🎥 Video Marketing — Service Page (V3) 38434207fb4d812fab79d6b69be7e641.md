# 16 · FAQ

**Do I need to be on camera?**

Not necessarily. We can work with screen recordings, stock footage, animations, voiceovers, and B-roll for most video types. If on-camera content is part of the strategy, we provide direction and coaching.

**What if I have existing footage?**

Send it over. We can edit and repurpose existing raw footage, podcasts, webinars, or presentations into polished short-form and long-form content.

**How long before video builds results?**

Short-form video can generate engagement quickly. YouTube channel growth typically becomes significant at 3–6 months. Consistency is the #1 determinant of results.

**Do you produce video ads?**

Yes. Growth and Scale tiers include video ad creative for YouTube, Meta, LinkedIn, and TikTok placements.

**Can you manage the YouTube channel end-to-end?**

Yes — including upload, optimisation, community tab management, and analytics reporting.

**What’s the typical turnaround for video edits?**

5–7 business days for short-form. 7–10 business days for long-form. We plan 2–3 weeks ahead on the content calendar to avoid bottlenecks.
