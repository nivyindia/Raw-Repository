## 🎁 BENEFITS MATRIX (WHAT PARTNERS GET IN RETURN FOR CONTRIBUTION)

> These benefits are **conditional** and **phase-unlocked**. More contribution = more access, revenue, and priority.
> 

| Contribution Provided by Agency | Benefits They Unlock |
| --- | --- |
| Old / Idle Leads, CRM Data | Priority onboarding, faster trial approval, early paid pilot |
| Warm Leads / Referrals | Revenue share on closed deals, repeat project access |

| Execution Bandwidth | Guaranteed monthly work allocation, volume stability |
| Data Assets / Scraping / Enrichment | Higher share %, internal tool access |

> **Rule:** Benefits are not promised upfront. They are **earned, unlocked, and retained** through continuous contribution.

## 🧠 Strategic Note

This phase-wise model ensures:

- No heavy ask before value
- Contribution grows only after results
- Partners self-select seriousness
- Founder retains leverage at every stage

This is a **platform-style growth engine**, not a vendor network.

---

## ⚖️ IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION (NO "FREE WORK" CONFUSION)

- Phase 1 contributions are **unused, low-cost, or idle assets** for agencies (old leads, idle capacity, past work, partial data).
- No agency is asked to sacrifice active revenue or core clients upfront.
- Higher-value contributions are **earned only after** international clients, systems, and revenue are delivered.
- Contribution escalation is **performance-triggered**, not forced.

> This protects agencies from risk and keeps the founder in full control while scaling globally.
