### Who We Are

Nivy Next is the digital innovation arm of Nivy — a multi-division organisation headquartered in Lucknow, India, built to serve ambitious businesses at every stage of their growth journey.

We operate across seven high-impact service verticals: digital marketing, AI automation, web development, app development, graphic design, video production, and virtual assistant services. What makes us different isn’t the range — it’s the integration. Every service is connected to a single strategy, delivered by one team, and measured against one goal: **your business results**.
