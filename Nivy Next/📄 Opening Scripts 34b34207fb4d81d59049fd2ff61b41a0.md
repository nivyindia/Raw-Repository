# 📄 Opening Scripts

## Opening Scripts

Use these when starting a conversation with a prospect via DM, email, or phone.

---

### Script 1 — Cold Outreach (DM/Email)

> *"Hi [Name], I noticed [observation about their business]. We help [target type] businesses generate more leads through [service]. Would you be open to a quick 10-minute call this week?"*
> 

### Script 2 — Warm Follow-Up

> *"Hey [Name], following up from [context]. I'd love to show you a quick example of results we got for a similar business. 10 minutes this week?"*
>
