# 16 · FAQ

**How long does SEO take to show results?**

Meaningful ranking movement typically appears in 3–4 months. Significant traffic growth is common by 6–9 months. SEO compounds — the longer you invest, the more defensible your organic position becomes.

**Can you guarantee top rankings?**

No — and any agency that does is misleading you. Google's algorithm evolves constantly and is proprietary. We commit to rigorous, ethical strategy and full transparency on results.

**Do you use black-hat tactics or link spam?**

Never. All link acquisition is earned through outreach, digital PR, and genuine content value. Shortcuts cause Google penalties that take years to reverse.

**What happens if my rankings drop?**

We investigate immediately — analysing algorithm updates, technical regressions, and competitor behaviour. You receive a full impact analysis and corrective action plan within 72 hours.

**Do I need to write the content myself?**

No. Our team handles content strategy, production, and optimisation. You review and approve before anything goes live.

**Do you handle international SEO?**

Yes — including hreflang implementation, market-specific keyword research, and multi-domain or subdirectory structures across US, UK, CA, AU, and UAE.
