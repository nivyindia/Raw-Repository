## 📱 SLIDE 01 — TITLE / INTRO SLIDE

**Slide Title:** *(not shown — visual only)*

**Content:**

- Headline: **Scale Smarter. Grow Faster. Go Global.**
- Tagline: AI-First Digital Services for Growth-Stage Businesses
- Website: [www.thenivy.com](http://www.thenivy.com)

**Speaker Notes:** Open with a strong pause. Let the headline land. Don’t introduce yourself immediately — let the visual do the first impression.

**Design Instruction:** Full dark background. Signature gradient applied to headline text or as underline. Logo small, top-right. Subtle animated background (optional): floating cyan particles or mesh grid.

## ⚠️ SLIDE 02 — THE PROBLEM

**Slide Title:** The Growth Wall

**Content:**

- “Most businesses trying to scale hit the same wall.”
- 🗡️ Fragmented vendors, zero strategy alignment
- 💸 Poor marketing ROI, no attribution
- ⏰ Slow time-to-market on builds and campaigns
- 🤖 No AI integration — still operating manually
- 🔄 5 agencies. 5 invoices. 0 unified results.

**Speaker Notes:** Ask the room: “How many of you are managing more than 2 external agencies right now?” Pause. “This is the wall.”

**Design Instruction:** 5-point icon list. Each problem line with an icon. Problem icons in amber/red-tone (muted, not Nivy brand — contrast the pain). Large bold statement at top in white. Background: dark, sparse.
