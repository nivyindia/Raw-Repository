# 🎵 Brand Sound & Audio Identity

## 🧠 How to Select & Apply the Right Music

- **Match the mood to content purpose**
    - Brand intro / big reveal → futuristic, cinematic, rising energy
    - Educational/informational post → calmer, ambient tech beat
    - Testimonial or case study → motivational with a positive chorus
- **Keep your brand palette in mind** — since your brand uses bold cyan/blue/violet + dark background, music with clean beats, digital synths, and some dynamic rise works best
- **Mind the licensing** — even if free, check commercial use rights, attribution requirements, and usage in ads
- **Duration & versioning**
    - For social reels (15-30 sec) pick a short loop or use fade-in/out
    - For longer videos (1–3 mins) pick full track or edit the build up, climax, resolution
- **Layering & Branding**
    - Logo animation: consider an audio “hit” or sonic logo (5-8 sec) using the same musical palette (synth, soft whoosh)
    - Under voice-over: keep music at lower volume so message remains clear
- **Consistency**
    - Choose 2-3 tracks across your brand to rotate; builds audio identity
    - Use similar BPM range (~100-120) so mood stays consistent across content
