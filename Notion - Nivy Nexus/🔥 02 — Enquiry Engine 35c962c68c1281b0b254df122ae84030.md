# The 90-Minute Daily Routine (Summary)

| Time | Task | Platform |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9:00 AM | Post 1 enquiry post | LinkedIn |
| 9:10 AM | Post in 2 Facebook / WhatsApp groups | Facebook / WA |
| 9:20 AM | Submit 10 contact forms manually | Browser |
| 9:50 AM | Reply to ALL DMs and comments (last 24h) | All platforms |
| 10:10 AM | Send WhatsApp broadcast to 10–15 contacts | WhatsApp |
| 2:00 PM | Check platforms, DM everyone who engaged | All |
| 5:00 PM | Log conversations in Lead Tracker | Notion |

# Lead Qualification Routing

| Signal | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Needs accounting / tax / marketing **now**, has budget | 🔴 HOT → Book call within 24 hours → Pitch service packages |
| Good business, outsources regularly, not urgent | 🟡 WARM → Invite to Nivy Alliance Slack → Nurture 30–45 days |
| Wrong fit, no budget, individual freelancer | ⚪ COLD → Log and move on |
