- [ ]  Go to [slack.com](http://slack.com) → Create a new workspace → Name it **Nivy Alliance**

## 2. Onboarding Form ([Tally.so](http://Tally.so))

- [ ]  Go to [tally.so](http://tally.so) → Create a free account → New form
- [ ]  Title: **Nivy Alliance — Member Application**
- [ ]  Add these 6 questions:
    1. Full name
    2. Company name and website
    3. Country and industry
    4. What do you currently outsource or plan to outsource? *(text field)*
    5. What type of partners, vendors, or suppliers are you looking for? *(text field)*
    6. How did you hear about Nivy Alliance? *(text field)*
- [ ]  Add a thank-you message: *"Thanks for applying. You’ll receive your Slack invite within 24 hours."*
- [ ]  Copy the form link and paste it into the Slack #start-here welcome message above
- [ ]  Share form link with yourself and test the full flow

## 4. LinkedIn Profile Optimisation

Before posting any enquiries, your LinkedIn profile must look credible. Tier-1 professionals will check your profile before responding.

- [ ]  Profile photo: professional, clear, real face
- [ ]  Headline: *"Building Nivy Alliance — Private Business Exchange | Accounting & Growth Services for UK & US Companies"*
- [ ]  About section must include:
    - Who you help (UK/US businesses, agencies, startups)
    - What you do (accounting, tax, digital marketing)
    - What Nivy Alliance is (1 sentence: private network where businesses share verified requirements)
    - A soft CTA (DM to connect or learn more)
- [ ]  Featured section: add your Tally onboarding form link
- [ ]  Set location to UK or USA (whichever market you’re targeting first)
- [ ]  Connect with at least 50 relevant people before posting (agency owners, founders, accountants)
