# Slack Channel Structure

| Channel | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| #start-here | Pinned rules + onboarding form link (read-only) |
| #introductions | Every new member posts who they are + what they need |
| #opportunities | Admin posts weekly business requirements and leads |
| #member-spotlight | One member featured per week |
| #resources | Templates, tools, guides shared by admin |
| #general | Open discussion, networking prompts |

---

# Weekly Community Content Schedule

| Day | Channel | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | #opportunities | Post 1–2 member requirements |
| Tuesday | #general | Networking prompt |
| Wednesday | #resources | Share one useful tool or template |
| Thursday | #member-spotlight | Feature one member |
| Friday | #general | Open prompt ("What are you working on?") |
| Monthly | Zoom call | 60-min informal networking call |

# Monetization Approach (Critical)

**Never pitch inside the community.** Let trust build naturally. After a member has been active 2–4 weeks, reach out privately:

> "Hi [Name], great having you in the Alliance. Noticed you mentioned [specific need they posted]. This is actually something we handle directly at Nivy — [accounting / tax / digital marketing]. Would a quick 15-minute call be useful?"
> 

This converts at 3–5x the rate of cold outreach because trust is already established.
