# 📋 Executive Summary {#executive-summary}

Nivy Growth is Nivy's flagship digital marketing and IT services division — a full-stack B2B agency that helps international businesses grow through SEO, paid ads, social media management, web/app development, automation, and accounting/compliance services.

The Nivy Growth model is built on **outbound-led client acquisition** (VA-driven cold outreach + social selling), **retainer-based revenue**, and a **geo-targeted expansion** strategy that prioritizes countries with high CPL (client payment levels) and low competition for remote services.

# ⚙️ Automation Architecture {#automation}

## End-to-End Client Acquisition Funnel

```
[Lead Research] → [Email/LinkedIn Outreach] → [Auto Follow-up Sequence] → [Meeting Booked] → [Proposal Sent] → [CRM Updated] → [Contract Signed] → [Onboarding Triggered]
```

## Automation Flows

### Flow 1 — Lead Generation (Apollo → CRM)

- **Tool:** [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) scrapes leads by country, industry, company size
- **Filter:** Decision makers (CEO, CMO, Founder, Head of Marketing)
- **Export:** To HubSpot CRM automatically via Zapier
- **Enrichment:** Clay adds LinkedIn URLs, phone numbers, tech stack

### Flow 2 — Cold Email Sequence ([Instantly.ai](http://Instantly.ai))

- **Day 0:** Initial email (personalized intro + value prop)
- **Day 3:** Follow-up 1 (case study or result)
- **Day 7:** Follow-up 2 (alternative CTA)
- **Day 14:** Final break-up email
- **On Reply:** Zapier moves lead to "Interested" pipeline in HubSpot

### Flow 3 — LinkedIn Outreach (Expandi)

- **Step 1:** View profile (triggers awareness)
- **Step 2:** Send connection request with note
- **Step 3:** Message 1 (intro + value)
- **Step 4:** Message 2 (case study link)
- **On Response:** VA takes over manually

### Flow 4 — Proposal + Contract Automation

- **Tool:** PandaDoc or DocuSign
- **Trigger:** Meeting marked "Qualified" in CRM
- **Action:** Auto-send proposal template with client details pre-filled
- **On Sign:** Stripe invoice triggered, Notion client workspace created

### Flow 5 — Client Onboarding (Automated)

- **Trigger:** Contract signed
- **Action 1:** Welcome email sequence (ConvertKit) fires
- **Action 2:** Client Notion workspace created from template
- **Action 3:** ClickUp project created with standard task template
- **Action 4:** Slack channel created and client invited
- **Action 5:** Kickoff meeting link sent via Calendly
