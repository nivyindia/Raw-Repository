# Lead → Client Flow

```
Lead responds to post or contact form
\u2193
DM Qualification (HOT / WARM / COLD)
\u2193
HOT \u2192 Book discovery call within 24 hours
\u2193
30-Minute Discovery Call
\u2193
Send proposal (Touch 1 \u2014 same day)
\u2193
No reply \u2192 5-Touch Follow-Up Sequence (Days 3, 7, 14, 30)
\u2193
Closed \u2192 Onboard + begin upsell over 90 days
Not closed \u2192 Invite to Alliance \u2192 nurture long-term
```

# The 3 Paths Revenue Comes From

**Path 1 — Direct (Enquiry Method):** Hot leads who respond to posts and need services now. Fastest revenue. Book call → pitch → close.nn**Path 2 — Community Upsell (30–60 days):** Slack members who experience your value over time and ask about services. Higher close rate than cold. Offer a free audit call first.nn**Path 3 — Referrals (60–90 days):** Members refer other businesses to you. No cost of acquisition. Compounds over time and becomes the primary channel by Month 4+.
