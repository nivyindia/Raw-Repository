# 📞 Sales Call Script — 30-Minute Discovery Call

> Use this script on every discovery call. Follow the sequence. Do not pitch until you have fully understood the problem. The sale happens in the questions, not the pitch.
> 

---

# Before The Call

- [ ]  Look up the company on LinkedIn and their website (2 minutes)
- [ ]  Note: company size, what they do, how long they’ve been operating
- [ ]  Check if they mentioned a specific problem in their DM or contact form reply
- [ ]  Have your Notion Lead Tracker open to log notes live
- [ ]  Have your Calendly link ready to send a follow-up booking if needed
- [ ]  Have your service packages page open for reference

---

# The 30-Minute Discovery Call Script

## OPEN (2 minutes)

**"Thanks for taking the time, [Name]. Quick agenda — I’ll ask you a few questions to understand your situation, and then I’ll share how we’ve helped similar businesses. Does that work?"**

> Always get a verbal yes. It creates micro-commitment.
> 

---

## UNDERSTAND THEIR BUSINESS (5 minutes)

1. **"Tell me a bit about [Company Name] — what do you do and who do you work with?"**
    
    > Let them talk. Don’t interrupt. Take notes.
    > 
2. **"How long have you been running the business?"**
3. **"Roughly how many people are on your team?"**
4. **"What markets are you primarily in — UK only or international as well?"**

---

## DIG INTO THE PAIN (10 minutes)

> This is the most important part. Never skip it.
> 

**For accounting leads:**

1. **"How are you currently handling your accounting and bookkeeping?"**
    
    > Listen for: in-house, freelancer, local accountant, doing it themselves, behind on it.
    > 
2. **"What’s the biggest frustration with how it’s set up right now?"**
    
    > Listen for: slow turnaround, not getting advice, missed deadlines, no visibility on numbers.
    > 
3. **"When did you last get proper management accounts — a clear view of your P&L and cash position?"**
    
    > If the answer is "never" or "last year" — that’s your opening.
    > 
4. **"Has the business ever had an HMRC issue, a missed deadline, or a penalty?"**
    
    > Pain amplifier. If yes, get the details.
    > 

**For marketing leads:**

1. **"What’s your current approach to getting new clients or customers?"**
2. **"Are you running any paid ads right now? If so, what platforms?"**
3. **"What does your cost per lead or cost per acquisition look like? Are you happy with it?"**
4. **"If you could fix one thing about your marketing in the next 90 days, what would it be?"**

---

## QUALIFY BUDGET & DECISION-MAKING (5 minutes)

1. **"Just so I pitch the right thing — is there a monthly budget you’re working with for this?"**
    
    > Don’t be afraid of this question. If they have no budget, they’re not a client.
    > 
2. **"Is this a decision you can make yourself, or are there other stakeholders involved?"**
    
    > If others are involved, ask: "What would they need to see to say yes?"
    > 
3. **"What’s your timeline — are you looking to sort this out in the next few weeks, or is this more longer-term planning?"**

---

## PRESENT THE SOLUTION (5 minutes)

> Only pitch now. Not before.
> 

**"Based on everything you’ve shared, here’s what I’d recommend..."**

- Name the specific package (Starter / Growth / Launch / Scale)
- Explain **3 specific things** it solves from their answers
- Give the monthly price
- Mention one relevant result: "We helped a similar [agency/startup/ecom brand] [specific outcome] within [timeframe]."

**"Does that feel like it addresses the main problem?"**

> Wait for their response before moving on.
> 

---

## HANDLE OBJECTIONS (5 minutes)

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "It’s too expensive" | "Understood. Can I ask — what’s the cost of NOT fixing this? An HMRC penalty alone is typically £500–2,000. Our Growth package pays for itself in one avoided penalty." |
| "We’re happy with our current accountant" | "That’s great. What would have to change for you to even consider switching?" |
| "Let me think about it" | "Of course. What specifically do you need to think through? I’d rather answer any questions now than leave them open." |
| "Can you do it cheaper?" | "I can’t move on price, but I can adjust scope. If we started with just [core element], that would bring it to [£X]. Does that work as a starting point?" |
| "We don’t have budget right now" | "Understood — when does your situation typically change? I’d rather follow up at the right time than at the wrong one." |

---

## CLOSE (3 minutes)

**If they’re ready:**

> "Brilliant — here’s what happens next. I’ll send you a short proposal by [tomorrow] with exactly what’s included and the onboarding process. Does that work?"
> 

**If they need time:**

> "No problem. I’ll follow up on [specific day]. In the meantime, if any questions come up, just reply to this email."
> 

**If not a fit:**

> "Sounds like the timing isn’t right. I’ll check back in [30/60] days. In the meantime, you’re welcome in the Alliance network — let me send you the invite."
>
