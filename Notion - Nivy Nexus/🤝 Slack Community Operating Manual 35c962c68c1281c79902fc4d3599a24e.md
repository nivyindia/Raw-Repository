## Platform Setup

**Platform: Slack** (free workspace)

Workspace name: **Nivy Alliance**

**Channels to create:**

| Channel | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| #start-here | Pinned rules + onboarding form link |
| #introductions | Every new member posts who they are + what they need |
| #opportunities | Admin posts weekly business requirements and leads |
| #member-spotlight | One member featured per week |
| #resources | Templates, tools, guides |
| #general | Open discussion, networking prompts |

---

## Onboarding Form (Tally or Google Form)

Every new member must complete this before getting access to full channels.

**Questions:**

1. Full name and company name
2. Company website
3. Country and industry
4. What do you currently outsource or plan to outsource?
5. What type of partners, vendors, or suppliers are you looking for?
6. How did you hear about Nivy Alliance?

*No form submitted = access limited to #start-here only.*

## How You Monetize From the Community

**Do not pitch inside the community.** Let trust build naturally.

After a member has been active for 2–4 weeks, reach out privately:

```
Hi [Name], great having you in the Alliance.

Noticed you mentioned [specific need they posted]. 
This is actually something we handle directly at Nivy — 
[accounting / tax filing / digital marketing].

Would a quick 15-minute call be useful to see if we can help?
```

This converts at a much higher rate than cold outreach because trust is already established.

## Monthly Zoom Call Template

**Format (60 minutes):**

- 0:00–0:05 — Welcome, quick intro from admin
- 0:05–0:25 — Each member: 60-second intro (name, company, what you do, what you need)
- 0:25–0:50 — Open networking / discussion / Q&A
- 0:50–1:00 — Admin posts summary of requirements shared in call to #opportunities

**Invite message to send in #general:**

```
📞 Monthly Nivy Alliance Networking Call — [Date] at [Time] UK/EST

Join us for 60 minutes of informal networking.
Everyone gets 60 seconds to introduce themselves and share 
what they’re looking for.

Zoom link: [LINK]

Reply 👋 if you’re joining so I can keep a headcount.
```
