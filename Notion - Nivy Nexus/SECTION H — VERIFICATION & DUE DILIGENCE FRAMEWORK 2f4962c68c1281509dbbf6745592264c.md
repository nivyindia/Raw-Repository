# **SECTION H — VERIFICATION & DUE DILIGENCE FRAMEWORK**

*(India & UAE Markets)*

---

## **1. Verification Philosophy**

Nivy Nexus follows a **multi-layer verification model** to reduce fraud, misrepresentation, and execution risk in cross-border and domestic B2B trade.

Verification is **process-based, not assumption-based**.

Only suppliers and buyers that meet minimum trust thresholds are introduced.

---

## **2. Supplier Verification — India**

Suppliers operating in India are verified on the following parameters:

### **A. Legal & Business Identity**

- GST Registration
- PAN & business registration proof
- Factory / warehouse address verification
- Authorized signatory confirmation

### **B. Operational Capability**

- Manufacturing or supply capacity validation
- MOQ & scalability assessment
- Machinery / sourcing capability (where applicable)
- Workforce & operational continuity check

### **C. Trade Credibility**

- Past B2B transaction references (where available)
- Pricing realism analysis (to avoid fake low quotes)
- Delivery commitment history

### **D. Compliance Readiness**

- GST compliance
- Export readiness (IEC, documentation awareness)
- Industry-specific compliance (if applicable)

Only suppliers clearing these checks are **onboarded or shortlisted**.

---

## **3. Supplier Verification — UAE**

For suppliers based in UAE or operating as export entities:

### **A. Legal & Corporate Verification**

- Trade License (Mainland / Free Zone)
- Company registration details
- Authorized signatory verification

### **B. Operational Legitimacy**

- Physical office / warehouse existence
- Supply or sourcing capability validation
- Export readiness and logistics familiarity

### **C. Trade & Pricing Credibility**

- Pricing consistency vs market benchmarks
- Ability to support international shipping terms
- Reliability in documentation and communication

### **D. Compliance Awareness**

- Export regulations and customs documentation
- Payment and invoicing standards (international trade norms)

---

## **4. Buyer Verification (India & UAE)**

To protect suppliers, buyers are also screened on:

- Business legitimacy
- Nature of requirement (genuine vs speculative)
- Payment intent and seriousness indicators
- Trade experience (where applicable)

This ensures **balanced trust on both sides**.

---

## **5. Fraud Prevention Mechanisms**

- No blind sharing of contacts or pricing
- Controlled introductions only after verification
- Staged communication through Nivy Nexus
- Documentation confirmation before deal progression
- Early warning checks for unrealistic pricing or timelines

---

## **6. Verification Limitations (Transparency Clause)**

- Verification is based on **available documents and reasonable checks**
- Nivy Nexus does **not replace statutory audits or inspections**
- Final commercial risk remains with buyer and supplier

> Verification reduces risk, it does not eliminate commercial responsibility.
> 

---

## **7. Re-Verification & Continuous Monitoring**

- Supplier performance is reviewed post-deal
- Repeat transactions strengthen trust score
- Non-performing suppliers are filtered out

---

## **8. Why This Framework Matters (India & UAE Context)**

- High fraud risk in cross-border sourcing
- Fake intermediaries & shell companies
- Pricing manipulation in international trade
- Documentation and compliance mismatches

**Nivy Nexus exists to remove these risks before money moves.**
