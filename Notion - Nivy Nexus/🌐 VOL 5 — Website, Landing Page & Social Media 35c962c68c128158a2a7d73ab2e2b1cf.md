# 🏠 1. Two Web Presences You Need

You need **two separate web presences** — one for Nivy (the parent company) and one for Nivy Alliance (the community product). They serve different audiences.

- [**thenivy.com**](http://thenivy.com) — Corporate credibility for prospects who Google you after receiving a message. This is where clients verify you are real before booking a call.
- **Nivy Alliance Landing Page** — The single page you send to every prospect. It converts curiosity into a form submission.

# 🌎 3. Website 2 — Nivy Alliance Landing Page

**URL options (in order of preference):**

- [nivallianz.com](http://nivallianz.com) or [nivallianza.com](http://nivallianza.com) (standalone domain, premium feel)
- [nival.co.uk](http://nival.co.uk) (short, UK-focused)
- [thenivy.com/alliance](http://thenivy.com/alliance) (easiest to set up, uses existing domain)

**The page must contain these sections in this exact order:**

| Section | What It Says / Shows | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Hero | “Nivy Alliance — A Private Business Exchange for Verified UK Companies” + subline + CTA: Apply To Join | Tells them what this is and creates exclusivity |
| 2. The Problem | “Finding reliable outsourcing partners is slow, risky, and expensive. LinkedIn is noisy. Directories are full of unverified vendors.” | Resonates with the exact frustration your ICP feels |
| 3. The Solution | 3 bullet points: Verified members only | Real outsourcing needs posted daily | Direct introductions, no middlemen | Explains what Alliance is without jargon |
| 4. Who It’s For | Icons for: Digital Agencies | SaaS Companies | IT Firms | E-commerce Brands | Accounting Practices | Logistics Companies | Self-selection — right people feel seen, wrong people exit |
| 5. What Members Get | Access to weekly outsourcing leads | Private Slack community | Introductions to verified partners | Priority matching by category | The value proposition made concrete |
| 6. Social Proof | Even “Join 40+ founding members” or 2–3 short quotes. If zero: “Be among the first 50 founding members — founding members get priority matching for life” | Removes the fear of joining something empty |
| 7. Application Form | Embed Tally form: Company name, Your role, Website, What services you outsource, What services you offer | The conversion action |
| 8. Footer | Nivy Alliance | Operated by Nivy | [thenivy.com](http://thenivy.com) | [contact@thenivy.com](mailto:contact@thenivy.com) | LinkedIn link | Trust and legal compliance |

**Build tool:** Use Framer (free tier), Webflow (free tier), or a clean WordPress page. Design must feel premium: dark navy or slate background, white text, one accent colour (gold or electric blue), no stock photos.

# 📱 4. Social Media Platform Strategy

You need to be present on **6 platforms**. Follow the priority order below — do not try to do all at once.

## Platform 1 — LinkedIn (HIGHEST PRIORITY)

Your entire ICP (UK/USA business owners, founders, directors) lives here. This is where you post enquiries, do outreach, build authority, and close clients.

**Personal Profile (Abhi’s):**

Headline: “Helping UK & International Businesses Find Trusted Partners | Founder @ Nivy Alliance | Accounting • Marketing • Digital”

Professional headshot, banner image for Nivy, 500+ connections before serious outreach, Featured section with Alliance landing page link.

**Posting schedule (personal profile):**

| Day | Post Type | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Enquiry post (as buyer) | “We’re looking for a UK-based SEO agency for a 6-month retainer...” |
| Tuesday | Value / insight post | “3 things UK businesses get wrong about outsourcing accounting...” |
| Wednesday | Enquiry post (different category) | “Urgently need an IT support firm in Manchester...” |
| Thursday | Social proof / case study | “We helped a London e-commerce brand cut their tax bill by 30%...” |
| Friday | Alliance community post | “Nivy Alliance is open to 10 more founding members this week...” |

## Platform 2 — Instagram (BRAND AUTHORITY)

**Handle:** @thenivy or @nivyalliance

**Content (3x per week):** Behind-the-scenes of building the Alliance, quote cards, short reels (“What is Nivy Alliance in 60 seconds”), client wins (anonymised).

**Bio:** Nivy Alliance | Private B2B Exchange | UK Business Community | Apply → [link in bio]

## Platform 3 — Facebook (GROUP OUTREACH)

UK business Facebook groups are active. Post enquiries there in Phase 1.

**Top groups to join and post in:** UK Business Forums, Digital Marketing UK, Startups UK, E-commerce Entrepreneurs UK, UK Accounting & Finance Professionals, Virtual Assistants & Remote Workers UK, UK Freelancers Network, London Business Network, Manchester Business Hub, Birmingham Business Community.

## Platform 4 — WhatsApp Business (WARM OUTREACH)

Direct, high open rate. Best for UAE market, Indian diaspora in UK, and warm contacts.

- WhatsApp Business account with Nivy logo, description, website link, business hours
- WhatsApp Business Catalogue: list your 3 main services with prices
- Create broadcast lists by category (UK leads / UAE leads / India leads / Warm contacts)

## Platform 5 — YouTube (AUTHORITY BUILDING — Phase 2+)

1 video per month minimum. Topics: What is Nivy Alliance and how it works, How to find trusted outsourcing partners in the UK, Accounting and tax tips for UK small businesses, Case studies.

## Platform 6 — Google Business Profile (LOCAL TRUST)

When anyone Googles “Nivy” or “Nivy Alliance”, this appears in the Knowledge Panel. Free to set up at [google.com/business](http://google.com/business).

- Business name: Nivy | Category: Business Consulting, Accounting, Digital Marketing
- Address: Lucknow office | Hours: Mon–Fri 9AM–6PM IST
- Photos: office, team, logo (minimum 5 photos)
- Once live: ask every client for a Google review. 10+ reviews with 4.5+ stars transforms credibility overnight.
