## 💡 LinkedIn Algorithm Tips

- Post 3-5x per week (text posts, carousels, polls, short videos)
- Always respond to comments within 1 hour of posting
- Engage on 10 posts before publishing your own
- Use 3-5 hashtags per post (not 20+)
- Tag relevant people/companies when appropriate
- Articles rank on Google — publish at least 2/month
- Use "Open to Work" or "Hiring" banners to increase visibility

# 🔍 9. GOOGLE DISCOVERABILITY STRATEGY

## Make Your Name Searchable on Google

### Steps:

1. **Create a personal website** at `yourname.com` or `yournamerecruiter.com`
    - Use your name + "International Recruiter" in H1 heading
    - Add meta title: "[Name] | International Recruiter & Talent Acquisition Specialist"
    - Add meta description with all core keywords
2. **Publish LinkedIn articles** — these index on Google within 48 hours
3. **Claim Google Business Profile** if working independently
4. **Guest post** on HR/recruitment blogs with a byline
5. **Be quoted** in press as an expert recruiter
6. **Podcast appearances** — show notes rank on Google
7. **Create a Google Site** (free) as a supplemental web presence

### Target Google Phrases:

- "[Your Name] recruiter"
- "international recruiter [city]"
- "executive search [industry] [city]"
- "global talent acquisition specialist"
