## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nivy Growth is a worldwide B2B client acquisition agency. Business development representatives contact companies across 50 countries through legitimate outreach channels, identify decision-makers, gather business intelligence, and convert them into long-term retainer clients for Nivy's digital marketing and accounting/compliance services.

**The Global B2B Opportunity:**

There are 333+ million SMEs globally. Over 70% are under-served by digital marketing agencies. Over 80% of small businesses in developing markets lack professional accounting support. Nivy Growth operates at the intersection of both gaps — across every continent.

**Core Value Proposition:**

- For digital marketing clients: Get more leads, clients, and revenue through proven data-driven systems
- For accounting clients: Save money on compliance, tax planning, and financial management
- For Nivy: Predictable retainer revenue from a diversified multi-country client base
- For Nivy Empires: The B2B revenue engine that funds all other divisions

**Revenue Target:**

- Month 6: ₹50L/month
- Month 12: ₹1.5 Crore/month (combined global retainers)

## 6. SALES & MARKETING PLAN — WORLDWIDE

> 💡 **Core Strategy Shift:** Don't ask *"How do I sell services?"* — ask *"How do I build a business-owner network around Nivy?"* The community becomes the lead generation engine, authority engine, referral engine, and partnership engine simultaneously.
> 

### The Enquiry Method — Primary Acquisition

Instead of direct pitching, post **opportunity-style content** that makes people self-qualify:

- *"Looking for agency partners in the UK"*
- *"Seeking business owners open to referral partnerships"*
- *"Looking for founders for growth networking"*
- *"Seeking UAE businesses for collaborations"*

**Enquiry Flow:**

```
Enquiry post
→ Responses
→ Lead capture
→ AI qualification
→ Community invite
→ Relationship nurture
→ Partnerships / Clients / Referrals
```

### Community Structure — Nivy Business Network

Do NOT create obvious sales groups. Create value-driven communities:

- Nivy Business Growth Network *(main group — broad networking)*
- Nivy Agency Partner Circle
- Nivy AI Automation Network
- Nivy Tax & Business Network
- Nivy Startup Growth Community

**Hybrid Model:**

| Layer | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Main Group | Founder intros, announcements, referral exchanges, opportunities |
| Niche Groups | Agency Partners, AI Automation, Tax & Accounting, E-commerce, Freelancers |
| Premium Inner Circle | High-value businesses (later stage) |

### Community Content Pillars (5-Pillar Mix)

| Pillar | % | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opportunities | 35% | Client requirements, partner requests, freelance work, referrals |
| Educational Value | 25% | Quick growth tips, automation ideas, AI tools, frameworks |
| Discussions | 20% | Founder threads, collaboration requests, networking |
| Case Studies | 10% | Automation wins, business growth results |
| Promotions | 10% max | Nivy service offers, events |

1. **Min 0–5:** Warm-up — confirm their current situation

5. **Min 35–40:** Address objections
6. **Min 40–45:** Next steps — propose free audit OR send proposal within 48 hours

- Send within 48 hours of discovery call
- Use PandaDoc template with custom video walkthrough (Loom)
- Include: Problem summary, proposed solution, timeline, pricing, case studies, guarantee
- Follow up: Day 2, Day 5, Day 8 if no response
- Close offer: "Proposal is valid for 7 days at this rate"
