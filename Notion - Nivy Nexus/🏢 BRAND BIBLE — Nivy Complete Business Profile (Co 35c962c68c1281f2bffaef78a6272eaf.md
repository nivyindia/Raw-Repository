# ✅ 1. THE BRAND DECISION — Landing Page vs Full Website

## Short Answer: Build Both — In The Right Order

**WEEK 1 — Build the Alliance Landing Page FIRST**

Reason: This is what you send to every prospect immediately. A single high-converting page built in 2–3 days generates more revenue faster than a 10-page website built over 3 weeks. You need this before outreach begins.

**WEEK 2–3 — Build the [thenivy.com](http://thenivy.com) Corporate Website**

Reason: Prospects Google you after receiving your message. If [thenivy.com](http://thenivy.com) looks broken, the deal dies silently. The corporate site is the credibility check — not the discovery mechanism.

**MONTH 2+ — Expand with Blog, Case Studies, Careers**

Reason: These drive SEO and long-term inbound. Start them early but don't let them delay outreach.

## Why NOT just a landing page alone:

A landing page converts curiosity. A website converts trust. For B2B services at £500–£2,000/month per client, buyers will Google you. If the website is missing, 60–70% of hot leads quietly disappear.

## The exact build order:

1. Nivy Alliance Landing Page — Framer or Webflow (Days 1–3)
2. Fix [thenivy.com](http://thenivy.com) WordPress security (Days 1–3, parallel)
3. [thenivy.com](http://thenivy.com) homepage + services + about + contact (Days 4–14)
4. Case studies + blog + careers (Month 2+)

# 🗣️ 6. BRAND VOICE & TONE GUIDE

## The Nivy Voice Is:

- **Confident, not arrogant** — We know what we're doing, but we're here to help, not impress.
- **Direct, not blunt** — We say what we mean without filler. No corporate jargon.
- **Expert, not academic** — Plain language that a busy business owner understands on first read.
- **Warm, not casual** — Professional warmth. Respected peer, not a prospect number.
- **Exclusive, not elitist** — Nivy Alliance feels like being let into something special.

## The Nivy Voice Is NOT:

- Generic ("We are a leading provider of world-class solutions...")
- Over-promising ("Guaranteed results in 30 days")
- Salesy ("ACT NOW, LIMITED TIME OFFER")
- Weak ("We try our best...")
- Overly formal or legal-sounding

## Tone by Context

| Context | Tone |
| --- | --- |
| Website headlines | Confident, benefit-led, clear |
| LinkedIn posts | Conversational, insight-led, human |
| Email outreach | Peer-to-peer, brief, specific |
| Pitch decks | Structured, data-backed, outcome-focused |
| Social media bios | Punchy, specific, benefit-first |
| Client proposals | Professional, detailed, outcome-focused |
| Onboarding emails | Warm, reassuring, clear next steps |
| Error / rejection messages | Empathetic, solution-oriented |

## Writing Rules (Apply Always)

1. Lead with the benefit, not the feature
2. Use "you" and "your" more than "we" and "our"
3. Short sentences. Maximum 20 words per sentence in web copy.
4. Never use: synergy, leverage, holistic, end-to-end, best-in-class, world-class, seamless
5. Specificity beats generality always ("We saved £18,000 in tax" beats "We save you money")
6. Every piece of copy must have one clear next action

# 🎨 14. VISUAL IDENTITY GUIDELINES

## Colour Palette

- **Primary:** Deep Navy — #0F1C2E (backgrounds, headlines, professional sections)
- **Accent:** Electric Gold — #D4A843 (CTAs, highlights, key numbers, Alliance branding)
- **Secondary Accent:** Slate Blue — #3A5A8C (secondary buttons, links, tags)
- **Neutral Light:** Off-white — #F8F7F4 (backgrounds, card backgrounds)
- **Neutral Dark:** Charcoal — #2C2C2C (body text)
- **Success:** Muted Green — #2D6A4F (confirmation messages, success states)

## Typography

- **Headline font:** Inter Bold or Sora Bold (modern, clean, professional)
- **Body font:** Inter Regular (highly readable at all sizes)
- **Accent font (optional for Alliance):** Playfair Display (adds premium feel for pull quotes)
- All fonts are free on Google Fonts.

## Spacing & Layout

- Maximum content width: 1200px
- Section padding: 80px top/bottom on desktop, 48px on mobile
- Use white space aggressively — premium brands breathe
- Never use more than 2 font sizes on one section

## Photography & Imagery Rules

- No generic stock photos of handshakes, suits, or skyscrapers
- Use: abstract geometric patterns, dark backgrounds with accent-colour elements, clean data visualisations, real team photos when available
- For social media: text-on-dark-background cards perform better than stock images for B2B

## Logo Usage

- Always use full logo (wordmark) on white or light backgrounds
- Use icon-only version (favicon/social avatar) at small sizes
- Never stretch, rotate, or place on a competing colour background
- Minimum clear space: equal to the height of the letter N in the logo
