# 🧠 1. The Core Idea (Final Version)

Nivy Alliance is a **private, invite-only business exchange** where verified companies share real outsourcing needs and get matched with trusted suppliers and partners. You run it. You also sell accounting, taxation, and digital marketing services to the members.

---

# 🔄 2. How The Enquiry Method Works (And Why It Feels Real)

You post as a **buyer** looking for a service — an accounting firm, a marketing agency, a developer. Sellers respond eagerly because you are the demand. You qualify them, invite the best ones into the Alliance community. Inside, they post their own needs. Your services become the natural solution over time.

# 📌 3. The 4 Sources of Real Enquiries

1. **Your own needs** — Services your business genuinely requires (bookkeeping, SEO, developers, tools). Every growing business needs these. Post them.
2. **Your clients’ needs** — If you have even one client, they have vendors they need. Ask them what they’re looking for and post it on their behalf. You become a procurement facilitator.
3. **Community needs** — Once the Alliance has members, post their requirements publicly (anonymously). Each member’s need becomes a new enquiry post. The community feeds itself.
4. **Category research** — Post in categories you’re evaluating for future use. “We’re researching logistics partners for Q3” is a real statement if you intend to explore it.

This is not deception. It is what procurement teams and buying communities do every day. You are a **hub of verified demand**. That is a legitimate and valuable role.
