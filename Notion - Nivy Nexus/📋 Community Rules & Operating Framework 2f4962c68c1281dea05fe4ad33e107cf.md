# **Nivy Alliance**

## **Rules, Regulations & Operating Guidelines**

*(Mandatory for All Members)*

---

## 1. Purpose of These Rules

Nivy Alliance is a **private, admin-curated business exchange** built on **trust, reciprocity, and verified intent**.

These rules exist to:

- Protect genuine buyers and service providers
- Eliminate spam, solicitation, and low-quality behavior
- Maintain a high-trust, Tier-1 professional ecosystem

**By joining Nivy Alliance, every member agrees to follow these rules without exception.**

---

## 2. Nature of Nivy Alliance (Important Clarification)

- Nivy Alliance is **NOT**:
    - A marketplace
    - A lead-selling platform
    - A freelancing group
    - A cold outreach channel
- Nivy Alliance **IS**:
    - A reciprocal business exchange
    - A demand-first ecosystem
    - An admin-facilitated introduction network
    - A professional procurement & partnership environment

---

## 3. Membership Eligibility Rules

### 3.1 Who Can Join

Only **verified business entities** are allowed:

- Registered companies, agencies, firms, or enterprises
- Decision-makers or authorized representatives
- Businesses with real outsourcing or partnership needs
- Established service providers with delivery proof

### 3.2 Who Is Not Allowed

The following are **strictly prohibited**:

- Individual freelancers or solo consultants
- Lead sellers or brokers
- Mass marketers or outreach agencies
- Anonymous or unverifiable entities

---

## 4. Core Reciprocity Rule (Non-Negotiable)

> Give Demand to Receive Demand
> 

To participate meaningfully in Nivy Alliance, every member **must**:

- Submit at least **one genuine business requirement**
    
    **OR**
    
- Clearly declare what services they **regularly outsource**

❌ No requirement = No access

❌ No contribution = No visibility

This rule ensures:

- High intent
- Balanced supply & demand
- Zero spam

---

## 5. Requirement Submission & Posting Rules

### 5.1 Submission Process

- All requirements must be **submitted to the Admin**
- Requirements are **verified before posting**
- Fake, vague, or misleading requirements are not allowed

### 5.2 Posting Method

- Buyer identity and contact details are **NOT disclosed publicly**
- Requirements are posted **anonymously**
- Only relevant details necessary for evaluation are shared

### 5.3 Introduction Process

- If a genuine fit is identified:
    - Admin contacts the buyer privately
    - Buyer decides whether to proceed
    - Only then are introductions facilitated

---

## 6. Communication & Conduct Rules

### 6.1 Strictly Prohibited

Members **must NOT**:

- Send cold DMs to buyers or other members
- Pitch services without admin approval
- Post generic “We provide XYZ” promotions
- Spam comments, replies, or messages
- Pressure buyers for responses or decisions

### 6.2 Allowed Communication

Members **may**:

- Respond only to **admin-approved opportunities**
- Ask clarification questions **through admin**
- Communicate professionally after formal introduction

---

## 7. Confidentiality & Data Protection

- Buyer requirements are confidential by default
- Buyer identities are protected until mutual consent
- Screenshots, forwarding, or external sharing is prohibited
- Strategic or sensitive business information must be respected

**Violation of confidentiality = Immediate removal**

---

## 8. Role of Nivy Alliance (Neutral Facilitation)

- Nivy Alliance acts as a **neutral facilitator**
- Introductions are based on:
    - Relevance
    - Capability
    - Ecosystem balance
- Nivy Alliance:
    - Does NOT negotiate commercial terms
    - Does NOT guarantee outcomes
    - Does NOT favor any member (including internal entities)

All contracts, pricing, and execution are handled **directly between businesses**.

---

## 9. Gamification & Access Levels

### Level 0 – Viewer

- Joined but no requirement submitted
- No access to opportunities

### Level 1 – Demand Contributor

- Submitted verified requirement
- Can view ecosystem activity

### Level 2 – Active Responder

- Responds meaningfully to matched opportunities
- Eligible for introductions

### Level 3 – Trusted Exchange Partner

- Successful exchanges completed
- Priority visibility & faster introductions

### Downgrades

- Inactivity
- Poor conduct
- Rule violations

---

## 10. Activity & Update Requirements

- Members must:
    - Update requirements every **30 days**
    - Confirm continued relevance or close the requirement
- Stale or abandoned requirements may be removed
- Inactive members may be downgraded or archived

---

## 11. Enforcement & Penalties

### Immediate Removal for:

- Fake requirements
- Spamming or cold pitching
- Confidentiality breach
- Misrepresentation
- Repeated rule violations

### Admin Authority

- Admin decisions are **final**
- No public disputes or arguments allowed
- Appeals may be submitted privately and respectfully

---

## 12. Fees & Monetization (Transparency)

- There is **no fee for access** at present
- Nivy Alliance may offer optional paid services such as:
    - Managed execution
    - Compliance handling
    - Escrow coordination
    - Verified partner programs

**The reciprocity principle will never be compromised.**

---

## 13. Amendments & Updates

- These rules may be updated to protect ecosystem quality
- Members will be notified of material changes
- Continued participation implies acceptance of updates

By joining **Nivy Alliance**, you confirm that:

- You understand this is a **private, professional ecosystem**
- You agree to all rules, guidelines, and enforcement actions
- You value trust, reciprocity, and long-term partnerships
