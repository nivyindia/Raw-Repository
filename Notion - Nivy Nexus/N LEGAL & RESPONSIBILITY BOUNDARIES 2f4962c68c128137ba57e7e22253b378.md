### **1. Facilitator Role Clarification**

Nivy Nexus operates strictly as a **business facilitation and coordination platform**.

We connect buyers with verified suppliers and assist in communication, documentation, and process flow.

We do **not act as a buyer, seller, manufacturer, or owner of goods** unless explicitly stated in writing.

---

### **2. Contract Responsibility Disclaimer**

All commercial contracts, purchase orders, and agreements are **directly executed between the buyer and the supplier**.

Nivy Nexus is **not a contractual party** to the trade unless formally agreed.

---

### **3. Quality Responsibility Disclaimer**

Product quality, specifications, certifications, and compliance remain the **sole responsibility of the supplier**.

Buyers are encouraged to conduct inspections or third-party quality checks where required.

---

### **4. Payment Responsibility Disclaimer**

Payments are made **directly between buyer and supplier** as per agreed commercial terms.

Nivy Nexus does not hold client funds unless explicitly agreed under a separate written arrangement.

---

### **5. Logistics Responsibility Disclaimer**

Shipping, customs clearance, insurance, and delivery are handled by the **supplier or appointed logistics partners**.

Nivy Nexus may assist in coordination but does not assume logistics liability.

---

### **6. Force Majeure Philosophy**

Neither party shall be held responsible for delays or failures caused by events beyond reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters, regulatory changes, political disruptions, or global supply chain interruptions.

---

### **7. Jurisdiction (High-Level)**

- Governing jurisdiction is defined **within individual commercial agreements**
- Primary operational jurisdiction: **India**
- Cross-border engagements may include **UAE or other agreed jurisdictions**

---

### **Client-Facing Trust Note**

> These boundaries exist to ensure clarity, transparency, and fair responsibility allocation—protecting all parties involved.
