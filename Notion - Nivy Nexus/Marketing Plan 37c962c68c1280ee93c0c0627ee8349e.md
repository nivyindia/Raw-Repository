## What You Post (Example Requirement)

> *"Looking for a white-label digital marketing partner for ongoing client work – budget $5k–$10k/month"*
> 

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | White-label partner needed for SEO, PPC, and Web Development |
| **Service Needed** | SEO, Google Ads, Social Media Management, Website Development, VA Services |
| **Budget** | $5,000 – $10,000 per month (minimum commitment) |
| **Who can apply** | Established agencies only – must have 10+ team members and client case studies |
| **Location** | Remote – but agency must be US, UK, Canada, Australia, or EU based |
| **Payment terms** | 50% advance, 50% on delivery |
| **Volume** | 5–10 client projects per month to start, scaling to 50+ |

**Why this filters out freelancers:**

- Minimum budget ($5k+) eliminates individuals
- "Agencies only" + "10+ team members" filters solo players
- "Case studies required" ensures credibility
