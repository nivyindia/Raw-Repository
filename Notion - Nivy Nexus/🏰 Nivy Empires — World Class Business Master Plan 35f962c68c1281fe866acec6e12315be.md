## ⚡ How This Will Be Executed — Phase by Phase

### 🟡 PHASE 1 — FOUNDATION (Week 1–4)

**Goal:** Set up the entire infrastructure before spending a rupee on marketing.

| Task | Owner | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Register brand entities & domains | Founder | Week 1 |
| Set up all social profiles (employees) | Team | Week 1–2 |
| Build Google Forms + Sheets CRM | Tech | Week 2 |
| Set up n8n automation flows | Tech | Week 2–3 |
| Onboard training partner (Model A) | Founder | Week 2 |
| Identify & contract real influencer (Model C) | Founder | Week 3 |
| Build outreach scripts for all 3 models | Marketing | Week 3–4 |
| Set up payment gateways by country | Finance | Week 4 |

---

### 🟠 PHASE 2 — LAUNCH (Week 5–8)

**Goal:** First leads, first revenue, first data.

| Task | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Model A: 500 job applicants collected | Week 6 |
| Model B: 200 business enquiries sent | Week 5 |
| Model C: Influencer first 10K followers | Week 8 |
| First course commission earned | Week 6 |
| First B2B client closed | Week 7–8 |
| First influencer paid subscribers | Week 8 |

---

### 🔴 PHASE 3 — SCALE (Month 3–6)

**Goal:** Systemise, automate, and multiply.

- Hire 2–3 outreach employees per model
- Expand to 2nd and 3rd priority countries
- Launch paid ads (Meta + LinkedIn)
- Increase influencer to 50K+ followers
- Model A: 50+ course conversions/month
- Model B: 5–10 retainer clients
- Model C: 200+ paid subscribers

---

### 🟢 PHASE 4 — EXPANSION (Month 7–12)

**Goal:** Multi-country revenue machine running.

- Full operations in India, UK, USA, UAE
- All 3 models running in parallel
- Team of 10–15 people
- Monthly revenue target: ₹10–25 Lakhs / $12,000–$30,000
- Begin investor/partnership conversations
- Launch Nivy Empires as parent holding brand
