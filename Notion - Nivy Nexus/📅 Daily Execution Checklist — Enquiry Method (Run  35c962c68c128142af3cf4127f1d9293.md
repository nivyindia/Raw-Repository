to see if there’s any synergy?

[Your name]
[Your number]
[Calendly or booking link]

## 💬 DM Qualification Script

When someone responds to any post:

**Message 1 (within 5 mins):**

*“Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out! Just to point you in the right direction — what type of business do you run and what’s the main challenge you’re trying to solve right now?”*

**Message 2 (after they answer):**

*“That makes a lot of sense. We’ve helped similar businesses with exactly this. Would a quick 15-minute call be worth it to see if we can do the same for you? I have slots [Day] and [Day] this week.”*

**Message 3 (if they say yes):**

*“Great — here’s my booking link: [link]. See you then!”*

**If they’re a hot lead (need services now):** Book call → sell directly.

**If they’re a warm lead (good fit, not urgent):** *“I also run a small private business network where members share leads and requirements — might be useful for you. Want me to send details?”* → Invite to Slack.
