# 💬 DM Qualification Script

When anyone responds to any post or contact form — use this exact flow.

**Message 1 — Send within 5 minutes of response:**

> *"Hi [Name], thanks for getting in touch! Just so I can point you in the right direction — what type of business do you run and what’s the main challenge you’re trying to solve right now?"*
> 

**Message 2 — After they answer:**

> *"That makes a lot of sense. We’ve helped similar businesses with exactly this. Would a quick 20-minute call be worth it to see if we can help? I have slots [Day] and [Day] this week."*
> 

**Message 3 — If they say yes:**

> *"Perfect — here’s my booking link: [Calendly]. Pick whatever works and I’ll send a confirmation."*
> 

**Message 4 — If they don’t reply after 24 hours:**

> *"Just checking back in — did you get a chance to think about it? No pressure either way."*
> 

## Routing After Qualification

| Signal | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Needs accounting / tax / marketing **now**, has budget | 🔴 HOT → Book call immediately → Pitch service packages |
| Good business, outsources regularly, not urgent | 🟡 WARM → *"I also run a private business network — members share leads and requirements. Want details?"* → Invite to Slack |
| Wrong fit, no budget, individual freelancer | ⚪ COLD → Log and move on |

# ⚠️ Rules For Phase 1

1. **Never post the same enquiry twice in the same week** on the same platform
2. **Reply to every single DM within 2 hours** during business hours
3. **Never pitch your services in the initial message** — always qualify first
4. **Log every conversation** in the Lead Tracker the same day
5. **Never skip a day** — consistency compounds. One missed day breaks momentum.
