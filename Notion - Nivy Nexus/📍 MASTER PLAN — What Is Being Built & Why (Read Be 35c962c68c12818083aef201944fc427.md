# 🧠 The Core Idea (Final Version)

Nivy Alliance is a **private, invite-only business exchange** where verified companies share real outsourcing needs and get matched with trusted suppliers and partners. You run it. You also sell accounting, taxation, and digital marketing services to the members.

## How The Enquiry Method Works (And Why It Feels Real)

You post as a **buyer** looking for a service — an accounting firm, a marketing agency, a developer. Sellers respond eagerly because you are the demand. You qualify them, invite the best ones into the Alliance community. Inside, they post their own needs. Your services become the natural solution over time.

**How to keep enquiries 100% real and ethical:**

You act as a **Demand Aggregator**. This means:

1. **Your own needs** — Services your business genuinely requires (bookkeeping, SEO, developers, tools). Every growing business needs these. Post them.
2. **Your clients' needs** — If you have even one client, they have vendors they need. Ask them what they're looking for and post it on their behalf. You become a procurement facilitator.
3. **Community needs** — Once the Alliance has members, post their requirements publicly (anonymously). Each member's need becomes a new enquiry post. The community feeds itself.
4. **Category research** — Post in categories you're evaluating for future use. "We're researching logistics partners for Q3" is a real statement if you intend to explore it.

This is not deception. It is what procurement teams and buying communities do every day. You are a **hub of verified demand**. That is a legitimate and valuable role.

## PHASE 2 — Semi-Automation (Days 31–60) 🤖 Remove The Manual Work

**Goal:** Automate the high-volume, repetitive tasks so you go from 10 contact forms/day to 100+/day without extra effort. Keep human touch for DMs and calls.

**What gets automated in Phase 2:**

### 2A — Contact Form Automation (n8n + Puppeteer)

- n8n workflow scrapes Google Maps for target businesses (agencies, IT firms, accountants) by city and category
- Puppeteer auto-fills and submits their website contact forms
- Volume: 50–100 submissions per day, unattended
- Cost: Free (n8n self-hosted) or $20/month (n8n cloud)

### 2B — Gmail Reply Detection (n8n)

- n8n monitors your Nivy Gmail inbox
- When a reply arrives → automatically tags it in Notion CRM (Lead Name, Company, Status: RESPONDED)
- Sends you a Slack or WhatsApp notification instantly
- You only need to check your notifications — not refresh Gmail manually

### 2C — Tally Form → Slack Auto-Invite (n8n)

- When someone submits the Alliance onboarding form on Tally
- n8n automatically sends them the Slack invite link via email
- Tags them in Notion CRM as JOINED
- Posts their intro in #introductions automatically

There is no public-facing page for Nivy Alliance anywhere. Right now, when a prospect receives an invite to join the Alliance:

**What's needed:** A dedicated landing page ([thenivy.com/alliance](http://thenivy.com/alliance) or a standalone domain) with: headline, value proposition, who it's for, what members get, social proof (even 1–2 testimonials or "founding member" framing), and a CTA to apply via the Tally form.

All outreach is being done from a personal LinkedIn profile. There is no company page for Nivy Alliance, which means:

**What's needed:** LinkedIn Company Page for "Nivy Alliance" with a complete profile, banner, about section, and at least 5 posts before any serious outreach begins.

The plan references cold email as a Phase 3 channel but there is zero setup:

### 3B — WORKFLOW 1: Contact Form Automation (Core Engine)

**What it does:** Automatically finds target companies, extracts their contact form URL, writes a personalised message, fills the form, and submits it. Replaces 2 hours of manual work per day with zero human effort.

**n8n Workflow — Step by Step:**

```
TRIGGER: Schedule — runs every day at 8:00 AM

STEP 1 — SCRAPE TARGETS
Node: HTTP Request → Apify Actor (Google Maps Scraper)
Input: Category (e.g. "digital marketing agency") + City (e.g. "London")
Output: List of 50 companies with name, website, phone, address

STEP 2 — FILTER ALREADY-CONTACTED
Node: Notion → Query "Lead Tracker" database
Logic: Skip any company where website URL already exists in Notion
Output: Clean list of new targets only

STEP 3 — FIND CONTACT FORM URL
Node: HTTP Request → fetch company website homepage
Node: Code (JavaScript) → scan HTML for /contact, /get-in-touch, /enquiry paths
Output: Contact form URL for each company

STEP 4 — AI MESSAGE GENERATION
Node: HTTP Request → Claude API
Prompt: "You are writing a brief, human-sounding enquiry for a business called [company name] in the [industry] sector. The enquiry is from Nivy Alliance, a UK business exchange. We are looking for [service type] providers. Write a 3-sentence contact form message that sounds like a real buyer, not a template. Do not mention automation."
Output: Personalised message (unique per company)

STEP 5 — SUBMIT CONTACT FORM
Node: Execute Command → Puppeteer script
Actions: Open browser → navigate to contact form URL → fill in: Name (Nivy Alliance), Email (outreach@nivallianz.com), Subject ("Business Partnership Enquiry"), Message (from Step 4) → click Submit
Output: Success / fail status

STEP 6 — LOG TO NOTION CRM
Node: Notion → Create Page in "Lead Tracker" database
Fields: Company name, website, industry, city, form submitted date, message used, status = CONTACTED
Output: Record created in Notion

STEP 7 — NOTIFY YOU
Node: Slack message OR WhatsApp (via Twilio)
Message: "✅ 50 contact forms submitted today. Check Notion for full log."

Volume: 50–200 submissions per day, fully automated
Your effort: Zero (after setup)
```
