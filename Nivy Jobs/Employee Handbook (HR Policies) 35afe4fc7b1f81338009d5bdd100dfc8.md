## Section A — Employee Motivation & Expectations

**Top Employee Motivators:**

1. Recognition & Appreciation — awards, certificates, trophies, stage recognition
2. Career Growth & Learning — promotions, training, upskilling, entrepreneurship support
3. Purpose & Meaning — work that contributes to company mission
4. Work-Life Balance — flexibility, remote options, health & wellness
5. Compensation & Benefits — fair pay, bonuses, incentives, financial security
6. Job Security — confidence in long-term employment
7. Autonomy & Trust — freedom to innovate, no micromanagement
8. Positive Work Culture — transparent leadership, inclusive environment
9. Challenging & Engaging Work — creativity-driven tasks
10. Leadership & Management Quality — mentors, not dictators

**Company Expectations from Employees:**

1. Performance & Productivity — meet KPIs, maintain quality
2. Professionalism — punctuality, attendance, workplace conduct
3. Accountability — take ownership, admit mistakes
4. Teamwork — cooperate, share knowledge, respect diversity
5. Communication — clear, respectful, transparent updates
6. Adaptability & Learning — adopt new technologies, keep upskilling
7. Integrity & Ethics — honesty, confidentiality, fairness
8. Customer Focus — excellent service, professional complaint handling
9. Innovation & Initiative — suggest improvements, proactive action
10. Alignment with Company Values — support mission and culture

## Section F — Workplace Policies

**Attendance & Leave:**

- All leave requests via official HR system
- Leave types: Annual, Sick, Maternity/Paternity, Emergency, Unpaid

**Anti-Harassment & Equal Opportunity:**

- Zero tolerance for harassment
- Equal opportunity for all employees

**Grievance Redressal:**

- All grievances recorded in Grievance Register
- Investigated within 5 working days
- Full confidentiality maintained

**Disciplinary Process (Progressive):**

1. Verbal Warning
2. Written Warning
3. Final Written Warning
4. Suspension or Termination

> Severe violations (harassment, theft, violence) may result in immediate termination.
> 

**Exit Policy:**

- Required notice period must be served
- Exit interview mandatory
- All company property returned before clearance
- Final pay processed within 15 working days
