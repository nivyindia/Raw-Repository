- 5,000+ Trainers & Coaches

This fits well with your existing Nivy ecosystem of freelancer partners, outsourcing, hiring funnels, agencies, and cross-niche business networks.

Since your goal is not just a job board but a **massive talent acquisition ecosystem**, I would build it in phases.

# Phase 1: Build the Talent Database (Month 1)

**Goal:** Collect 1,000–5,000 talent profiles before worrying about employers.

### Step 1: Create Talent Categories

Create separate forms/pages for:

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Corporate Jobs | HR, Sales, Accounts, Tax, Marketing |
| Freelancers | SEO, PPC, Designers, Developers |
| Creators | UGC, Influencers, Content Creators |

| Fitness | Zumba, Yoga, Gym Trainers |
| Education | Tutors, Faculty, Trainers |
| Students | Freshers, Interns |
| Skilled Workers | Electricians, Drivers, Technicians |
