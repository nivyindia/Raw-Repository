# HR Department Policies

## Purpose

To establish clear standards and procedures for managing the workforce, ensuring compliance with labor laws, supporting employee well-being, and maintaining a productive, respectful workplace.

## Scope

Applies to all HR team members, hiring managers, and employees involved in recruitment, training, payroll, performance management, and employee relations.

---

## 3.1 Recruitment & Hiring

- All hiring must follow approved manpower plans and budget allocations
- Job descriptions must be approved before posting
- Candidate evaluation must be fair, merit-based, and non-discriminatory
- Background checks (employment history, references) required before offer

## 3.2 Onboarding & Orientation

All new hires must complete a formal orientation covering:

- Company history, mission, and values
- Workplace rules and policies
- Benefits and payroll procedures
- Safety and compliance guidelines
- All employment forms and contracts completed before first day

## 3.3 Performance Management

- Formal reviews conducted annually (or bi-annually)
- Managers must set clear KPIs at the start of each review cycle
- Underperforming employees placed on Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

## 3.4 Training & Development

- Training needs assessed quarterly with department heads
- HR arranges internal/external training to address skills gaps
- Training participation records maintained for compliance

## 3.5 Leave & Attendance

- All leave requests via official HR system
- Leave categories: Annual, Sick, Maternity/Paternity, Emergency, Unpaid
- HR monitors attendance and addresses excessive absenteeism

## 3.6 Compensation & Benefits

- Payroll processed monthly per company guidelines
- Salary increments and bonuses based on performance reviews
- HR administers health insurance, allowances, retirement plans

## 3.7 Employee Relations & Grievances

- Open-door communication for employee concerns
- All grievances recorded in Grievance Register
- Investigated within **5 working days**
- Full confidentiality maintained

## 3.8 Disciplinary Procedures

1. Verbal Warning
2. Written Warning
3. Final Written Warning
4. Suspension or Termination

> Severe violations (harassment, theft, violence) = immediate termination
> 

## 3.9 Termination & Resignation

- Employees must serve required notice period (30 days)
- Exit interview mandatory
- All company property returned before clearance
- Final pay processed within 15 working days

## 3.10 HR Records & Confidentiality

- Accurate, secure personnel files maintained for each employee
- Access restricted to authorized HR staff only
- Personal information handled per data protection laws
