# 90-Day Fresher Course Structure

> Built on the framework: **Learn → Practice → Master → Lead**
> 

---

## Pre-Course (Before Day 1)

- Welcome Kit & Pre-Course Survey sent to all freshers
- Short video from CEO/Head of HR welcoming them
- Anonymous survey: *“What are your biggest fears and expectations from your first job?”*
- **Objective:** Make them feel valued; gather data on misconceptions

---

## Phase 1: The Reality Check (Days 1–30) — “The Eye Opener”

**Focus:** Shattering illusions and setting the right expectations.

| Week | Day | Topic | Duration | Key Activity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | The Grand Illusion vs. The Real World | 1.5 hrs | How Social Media & Movies Sold You a Lie |
| 1 | 3 | Why You’re Here: Building Roots, Not Picking Fruit | 1 hr | The Tree Analogy Workshop |
| 2 | 8 | Global Work Ethics: Lessons from the World | 2 hrs | German, Japanese, American, Singaporean models |
| 2 | 10 | Your First Job is Your Gurukul, Not Your ATM | 1 hr | A Letter to My Future Self |
| 3 | 15 | What Your Boss Really Does (Hidden Struggles) | 1.5 hrs | The hidden costs of running a business |
| 3 | 17 | The 90-Day Grind: Embracing the Suck | 1 hr | Role-play: Handling a difficult client call |
| 4 | 22 | Money Mindset: Salary vs. Earning Potential | 1.5 hrs | 12 months patience = 10x income in 36 months |
| 4 | 24 | Mid-Month Review & Reflection | 1 hr | Sharing circle: One illusion shattered this month |

---

## Phase 2: The Toolbox (Days 31–60) — “The Skill Up”

**Focus:** Equipping with non-negotiable skills for success.

| Week | Day | Topic | Duration | Key Activity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 31 | The Power of Process: Why SOPs Are Your Best Friend | 1 hr | Create a personal SOP for your most repetitive task |
| 5 | 33 | Ownership & Accountability | 1.5 hrs | Blameless Post-Mortem: Analyze a real project mistake |
| 6 | 38 | Proactive Communication: Don’t Wait, Initiate | 2 hrs | Writing pro emails, updates, asking for help |
| 6 | 40 | Kaizen: The Art of Continuous Improvement | 1 hr | Find and suggest one small process improvement |
| 7 | 45 | Resilience 101: Bounce Back from Rejection | 1.5 hrs | Framing “No” as data, not failure |
| 7 | 47 | Time & Energy Management | 1.5 hrs | The Deep Work challenge — 2hrs uninterrupted focus |
| 8 | 52 | The Art of Feedback: Giving, Receiving, Growing | 2 hrs | Radical Candor role-play |
| 8 | 54 | Mid-Program Review | 1 hr | Present: Most valuable skill learned so far |

---

## Phase 3: The Launchpad (Days 61–90) — “The Future You”

**Focus:** From foundational employee to valued, strategic team player.

| Week | Day | Topic | Duration | Key Activity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9 | 61 | From Task-Doer to Value-Creator | 1.5 hrs | Connect daily tasks to company revenue and goals |
| 9 | 63 | Building Your Personal Brand at Work | 1.5 hrs | Craft your 30-second value intro |
| 10 | 68 | Networking & Visibility (Without Being Sleazy) | 2 hrs | Build genuine relationships with seniors & mentors |
| 10 | 70 | Strategic Career Thinking: Your 2-Year Plan | 1.5 hrs | Map your next 5 roles and skills needed |
| 11 | 75 | Financial Literacy for Freshers | 1.5 hrs | How to manage, save & invest your first paycheck |
| 11 | 77 | Final Project Workshop | 1 hr | Mentorship session for Day 90 presentation |
| 12 | 82 | Well-being & Avoiding Burnout | 1 hr | Boundaries, disconnecting, mental health |
| 12 | 84 | Graduation Ceremony Rehearsal | 1 hr | Dry run of final presentations |
| 13 | 90 | GRADUATION DAY | 2 hrs | Present 90-Day Journey & 2-Year Vision to leadership |

> Graduates receive a **“Foundation Builder” Certificate**
>

## Ongoing Gamification

- **Root Growth Tracker** — visual dashboard where freshers earn points for completing modules, attending sessions, and achieving weekly targets
- **Leaderboard** — weekly top performers recognized publicly
- **Badges:** Anchor (Level 1), Catalyst (Level 2), Ethics Champion (Level 3)
