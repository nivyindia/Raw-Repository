# DM Templates & Sales Qualification (BANT)

Use these for LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram outreach. Short, conversational, and non-pushy.

---

## Stage 1: Awareness — Cold Connect

**Goal:** Connect + spark curiosity without being pushy.

1. Hey [First Name], I came across your profile and noticed you’re doing amazing work in [industry/role]. Thought it’d be great to connect and exchange insights!
2. Hi [First Name], I work with [industry professionals] who often struggle with [pain point]. Just wanted to connect — maybe we can share some ideas.
3. Hello [First Name], saw your post on [topic] — really insightful! I’d love to stay connected and follow your work.
4. Hey [First Name], quick one — are you currently working on [specific challenge]? I’ve been talking to others in [their industry] dealing with the same.
5. Hi [First Name], I recently published a quick guide on [topic]. I think you’d find it useful. Want me to send it over?

---

## Stage 2: Interest — Build Relationship

**Goal:** Provide value + start a deeper conversation.

1. Hey [First Name], I thought you might like this: [blog/video/checklist link]. How are you currently approaching [pain point]?
2. Hi [First Name], I noticed a lot of [their role/industry] are struggling with [problem]. Is that something you’ve also been running into?
3. Hey [First Name], we’re hosting a short session on [topic]. It’s free and practical — want me to send you the invite?
4. Hi [First Name], just wanted to share a quick stat: [industry stat]. How are you seeing this play out in your work?
5. Hey [First Name], I’d love to hear your thoughts on [topic]. Can I send you a quick case study about how others are handling it?

---

## Stage 3: Consideration — Show Proof

**Goal:** Share success stories, social proof, relevance.

1. Hi [First Name], we recently helped [similar company] cut down [problem] by [XX%]. Would you like to see how they did it?
2. Hey [First Name], curious — have you ever compared [solution A] vs [solution B]? I put together a quick breakdown. Want me to share?
3. Hi [First Name], one of our clients said: “[short testimonial].” Thought you’d find this interesting since you’re working on something similar.
4. Hey [First Name], I noticed [specific detail about their company]. I think [Your Solution] could help you streamline that. Want me to show you a quick example?
5. Hi [First Name], if you could save [X hours / ₹X] per month on [process], would that make a big impact for your team?

---

## Stage 4: Decision — Conversion

**Goal:** Encourage action — demo, trial, sign-up.

1. Hey [First Name], we’re offering a free [X-day trial/demo]. Want me to set it up for you?
2. Hi [First Name], quick note — we’re running a special offer until [date]. Should I send you the details?
3. Hey [First Name], we make it risk-free with [guarantee/trial]. You can try it without any commitment. Should I send you the link?
4. Hi [First Name], you’ve seen how [Your Product] can help with [pain point]. Are you ready to take the next step?
5. Hey [First Name], we’re almost full for this month’s onboarding. Should I reserve a slot for you?

---

## Stage 5: Post-Purchase — Retention & Referrals

**Goal:** Keep them engaged, expand relationship.

1. Hi [First Name], welcome aboard! 🙌 Here’s a quick-start guide to help you get the most out of [Your Product]. Want me to send it?
2. Hey [First Name], just checking in — how’s your experience so far? Anything I can help with?
3. Hi [First Name], many of our customers upgrade to [feature/add-on] because it helps them [benefit]. Want me to show you how it works?
4. Hey [First Name], your results are impressive! Would you be open to sharing a testimonial we can feature?
5. Hi [First Name], we’ve launched a referral program! If you know someone who’d benefit, both of you get [reward]. Want me to send details?

## BANT Qualification Framework

**Use BANT to decide if a prospect is worth pursuing before spending time on a pitch.**

| Factor | What to Find Out | Example Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **B**udget | Can they afford your services? | “What’s your current monthly marketing spend?” |
| **A**uthority | Are they the decision-maker? | “Who makes the final call on marketing vendors?” |
| **N**eed | Do they have a real problem you solve? | “Are you looking to get more leads, sell faster, or build brand presence?” |
| **T**imeline | When do they plan to act? | “When are you planning your next campaign or launch?” |

### Discovery Question Types

**Open-Ended** — Encourage detailed answers

- “What’s your biggest challenge in getting inquiries right now?”
- “How do you currently promote your services online?”

**Probing** — Dig deeper

- “You mentioned Facebook ads aren’t working — can you share what campaigns you’ve run so far?”

**Impact** — Show the cost of inaction

- “If this continues, how will it affect your upcoming targets?”

**Solution-Oriented** — Lead towards your service

- “If we could get you 50% more inquiries within 3 months, how valuable would that be?”
