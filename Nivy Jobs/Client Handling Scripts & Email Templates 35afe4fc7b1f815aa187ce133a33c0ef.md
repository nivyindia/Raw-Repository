# Client Handling Scripts & Email Templates

Use these templates at each stage of the funnel. Replace all `[placeholders]` before sending.

---

## Stage 1: Awareness — Cold Outreach

**Goal:** Introduce yourself, spark curiosity, share free value.

**Email 1 — Quick Intro**

> Subject: Quick question, [First Name]
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], I work with [industry/professionals like them] to help with [problem they face]. We’ve helped companies like [client example] achieve [result]. Would it make sense to share a short resource that could help you with [pain point]?
> 

**Email 2 — Educational Resource**

> Subject: [First Name], thought you’d find this useful
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], I noticed [relevant trend/problem in their industry]. We recently put together a quick [guide/checklist/article] on how to [solve problem]. Here’s the link 👉 [Insert link]. How are you currently handling [problem]?
> 

**Email 3 — Problem Statement**

> Subject: Struggling with [common pain point]?
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], a lot of [their role/industry] tell me they spend hours on [pain point]. That’s why we created a solution that makes [problem] simple. Would you like me to send a quick explainer?
> 

**Email 4 — Value Share (No Pitch)**

> Subject: A quick tip for [topic]
> 

> 
> 

> Hey [First Name], just wanted to share a quick tip: [insert short valuable tip]. If you’d like, I can share a few more strategies working really well in [industry]. Would that be helpful?
> 

**Email 5 — Soft Connect**

> Subject: Let’s connect, [First Name]
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], I’ve been following your work in [industry/role] and thought it’d be great to connect. We’ve helped professionals like you with [problem → solution → result]. Would you be open to a quick call next week?
>

## Stage 2: Interest — Nurture & Build Trust

**Goal:** Educate, share insights, warm them up.

**Email 1 — Insight Share**

> Subject: [First Name], here’s what we’re seeing in [industry]
> 

> 
> 

> I thought you’d find this interesting: [insight/data/stat]. We’ve been helping businesses address this by [your solution]. Does this sound like something you’re also experiencing?
> 

**Email 2 — Free Guide**

> Subject: Free guide on [topic]
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], I just published a free guide on [topic/problem] with practical steps to [achieve result]. 👉 Download here: [link]. Would love your thoughts!
> 

**Email 3 — Webinar Invite**

> Subject: Join us for [topic] webinar
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], we’re hosting a short webinar on [topic] where we’ll share how companies are solving [pain point]. It’s on [date/time]. Want me to save you a seat?
> 

**Email 4 — Educational Story**

> Subject: A quick story about [problem]
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], one of our clients, [client name], was struggling with [pain point]. After using [Your Product], they achieved [result]. If this sounds familiar, maybe I can share how they made it work?
> 

**Email 5 — Resource Roundup**

> Subject: Top resources for [pain point]
> 

> 
> 

> Hey [First Name], here are a few resources I thought you’d find valuable: [Resource 1], [Resource 2], [Resource 3]. Which of these is most relevant to you right now?
>

## Stage 3: Consideration — Show Proof & Value

**Goal:** Share testimonials, case studies, comparisons.

**Email 1 — Case Study**

> Subject: How [similar company] achieved [result]
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], we recently helped [similar company] cut [problem] by [XX%]. Here’s their story 👉 [case study link]. Could something like this help at [prospect’s company]?
> 

**Email 2 — Comparison**

> Subject: A quick comparison for you
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], many ask how [Your Product] compares with [alternative]. We put together a quick breakdown 👉 [link]. Happy to walk you through it.
> 

**Email 3 — ROI Example**

> Subject: What if you could save [X hours / $X] a month?
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], on average our clients save [X hours / $X] every month. I’d be happy to calculate what that could look like for [prospect’s company].
> 

**Email 4 — Social Proof**

> Subject: 100+ companies trust us for [solution]
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], over [XX] businesses use [Your Product] to solve [problem]. Here’s what [client name] said: “[Short testimonial]”. Could we explore if this is a fit for your team?
> 

**Email 5 — Personalization**

> Subject: Tailored for [prospect’s company]
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], I took a quick look at [company/website] and noticed [specific observation]. I believe [Your Product] could help you [achieve result]. Want me to prepare a quick custom plan?
>

## Stage 4: Decision — Close the Deal

**Goal:** Offer demo, free trial, discount, create urgency.

**Email 1 — Free Trial/Demo**

> Subject: Try [Your Product] free for [X days]
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], why not experience [Your Product] firsthand? We’re offering a free [X-day trial/demo]. Shall I set this up for you?
> 

**Email 2 — Limited Time Offer**

> Subject: Special offer ends [date]
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], we’re running a [discount/offer] until [date]. Would you like me to reserve this deal for you?
> 

**Email 3 — Risk Removal**

> Subject: 100% risk-free for you
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], we offer a [money-back guarantee / cancel anytime trial]. You can try [Your Product] with zero risk. Would you like to get started today?
> 

**Email 4 — Call to Action**

> Subject: Ready to move forward, [First Name]?
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], the next step would be [signing up / onboarding]. Should I send over the link?
> 

**Email 5 — FOMO / Scarcity**

> Subject: Only [X spots] left this month
> 

> 
> 

> Hey [First Name], we’re nearly at capacity for [month]. Can I save a spot for you?
>

## Stage 5: Post-Purchase — Onboarding & Retention

**Goal:** Keep customer engaged, upsell, build loyalty.

**Email 1 — Welcome**

> Subject: Welcome aboard, [First Name]!
> 

> 
> 

> We’re thrilled to have you! Here are 3 things to help you get started: [Onboarding guide], [Support link], [Community link].
> 

**Email 2 — Onboarding Follow-up**

> Subject: Need help getting started?
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], how’s your experience so far? I can schedule a quick walkthrough to make sure you’re set up for success. Want me to arrange this?
> 

**Email 3 — Upsell**

> Subject: Unlock more with [feature/add-on]
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], many customers upgrade to [premium plan] because it gives them [specific benefit]. Want me to show you how this could help?
> 

**Email 4 — Testimonial Request**

> Subject: Can we feature your success story?
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], would you be open to sharing your experience with [Your Product]? We’ll feature your story on our site.
> 

**Email 5 — Referral Request**

> Subject: Share the love, earn rewards 💡
> 

> 
> 

> Hi [First Name], do you know someone who’d benefit from [Your Product]? When you refer them, you both get [reward]. Here’s your referral link: [link].
>
