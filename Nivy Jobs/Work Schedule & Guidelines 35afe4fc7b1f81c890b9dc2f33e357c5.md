**Available Shifts:**

- Afternoon Shift: 1:00 PM – 10:00 PM (9 hrs)

## 3. Screen Time & Breaks (Workfolio)

We use **Workfolio** for coaching and quality support — not spying.

**What Workfolio tracks:**

- Active Screen Time
- Application & Website Usage
- Random Screenshots (for quality checks)
- Overall Activity Levels

**Expected active screen time:**

- Full-Time (9 hrs): **7.5 hours** active + 1.5 hrs break (1 hr lunch + 2 x 15 min tea)
- Part-Time (4.5 hrs): **3.75 hours** active + 45 min break (30 min meal + 1 x 15 min tea)

## Work Ethics & Performance Rewards

Work ethics constitute **25% of your overall performance score**, directly impacting salary increments and incentives.

**Core Work Ethics Rewarded:**

1. **Punctuality & Discipline** — shift adherence, timely reports, camera on in meetings
2. **Ownership & Accountability** — take responsibility, communicate delays proactively
3. **Integrity & Transparency** — honest screen time, work hours only for professional tasks
4. **Teamwork & Collaboration** — support colleagues, participate actively
5. **Initiative & Proactive Learning** — volunteer for challenges, apply feedback

## WFH for Trainees: Myths vs Reality

| Myth | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| WFH means more free time | Requires more discipline, not less |
| Can work from anywhere | Need a dedicated, quiet, private workspace |
| No one will notice if I’m late | You’re MORE accountable via Workfolio tracking |
| It’s isolating | You must proactively stay connected via meetings and chat |

**What trainees SHOULD do:**

- Have a dedicated workspace (desk, chair, good lighting, strong internet)
- Follow a structured daily routine
- Communicate proactively when stuck
- Understand that tracking = support, not surveillance

**What trainees should NOT do:**

- Multitask with household chores during work hours
- Treat it as a completely flexible “anytime” job
- Work from bed, cafes, or noisy places
