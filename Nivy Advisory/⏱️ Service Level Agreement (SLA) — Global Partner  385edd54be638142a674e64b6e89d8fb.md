# ⏱️ Service Level Agreement (SLA) — Global Partner Tier

---

## 1. Purpose

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") sets out the response times, deliverable commitments, and escalation process for Global Partner-tier clients of Nivy Advisory.

---

## 2. Response Time Commitments

| Request Type | Target Response Time |
| --- | --- |
| General email/portal message | Within 1 business day |
| Urgent compliance/deadline matter | Within 4 business hours |
| Scheduled monthly bookkeeping close | Delivered within 10 business days of month-end |
| Quarterly business review | Scheduled and delivered within agreed calendar |
| Annual tax filing | Delivered for client review at least 10 business days before the relevant statutory deadline, provided client information was received per the agreed timeline |

---

## 3. Dedicated Team

Global Partner clients are assigned:

- A named Account Manager as primary point of contact
- A dedicated team pod (strategist/CPA, bookkeeping specialist, advisory lead) as relevant to the scope

---

## 4. Escalation Process

If a response time commitment is missed:

1. Notify your Account Manager directly.
2. If unresolved within 1 business day, escalate to the Nivy Advisory Operations lead via [contact@thenivy.com](mailto:contact@thenivy.com) with "SLA Escalation" in the subject line.
3. Nivy Advisory will acknowledge the escalation within 4 business hours and provide a resolution plan.

---

## 5. Exclusions

This SLA does not cover delays caused by:

- Client-side delays in providing required information or documentation
- Force majeure events (e.g., natural disasters, government system outages such as tax authority portal downtime)
- Third-party software outages outside our control (e.g., QuickBooks/Xero downtime)

---

## 6. Reporting Cadence (Reference)

| Cadence | Deliverable |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly | Snapshot update (where applicable to engagement scope) |
| Monthly | Financial statements / bookkeeping close summary |
| Quarterly | Business review meeting |

---

## 7. Review

This SLA is reviewed annually or upon material change to the scope of the Global Partner engagement, and updates are communicated in writing.
