# Pitch Framework

> The structured approach to presenting Nivy Tax's services in a compelling, client-centred way.
> 

---

## The P.A.S.S. Framework

**P — Problem** | **A — Amplify** | **S — Solution** | **S — Social Proof**

---

### P — Problem (Name it)

> *"Based on what you've shared, it sounds like [specific problem — e.g., 'your books are 3 months behind and tax season is coming']."*
> 

Mirror back the exact language they used. This shows you listened and builds instant trust.

### A — Amplify (Make it real)

> *"If that continues, the risk is [consequence — e.g., 'filing penalties, cash flow surprises, or an IRS notice at the worst time']."*
> 

Don't be dramatic — be honest. Help them feel the cost of inaction.

### S — Solution (Specific, not generic)

> *"What we'd do for you specifically is [customised solution — e.g., 'catch up your books in the first 30 days, then maintain them monthly with a dedicated bookkeeper plus quarterly tax reviews']."*
> 

Always make it feel tailored. Even if it's the standard package, describe it in their context.

### S — Social Proof (Validate)

> *"We've done this for [similar business type]. [Brief outcome — e.g., 'They went from 4 months behind to clean books in 6 weeks, and we caught $8,000 in missed deductions in year one.']"*
>
