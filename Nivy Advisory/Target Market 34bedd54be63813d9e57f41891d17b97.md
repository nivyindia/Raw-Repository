# Target Market

> Understanding who we serve is as important as understanding what we offer. This page defines our ideal client profiles.
> 

---

## 🎯 Primary Target Markets

### 1. US-Based Small & Mid-Size Businesses

- Revenue: $100K – $5M annually
- Need: Outsourced bookkeeping + tax compliance
- Pain: Overwhelmed by US tax complexity, no in-house accountant
- Ideal package: Starter or Growth

### 2. Indian Companies with US Operations

- Expanding into the US market
- Need: Cross-border compliance, transfer pricing, entity setup
- Pain: Navigating two different tax systems simultaneously
- Ideal package: Growth or Scale + Advisory

### 3. E-Commerce & SaaS Businesses

- Selling across multiple states / countries
- Need: Sales tax nexus compliance, multi-currency bookkeeping
- Pain: Complex platform integrations, subscription revenue recognition
- Ideal package: Growth or Scale

### 4. Startups & Funded Companies

- Seed to Series B stage
- Need: Clean books for investors, R&D credits, equity comp accounting
- Pain: High growth + complexity with small teams
- Ideal package: Growth + CFO Add-On

### 5. Professional Services Firms

- Law firms, consultancies, medical practices
- Need: Revenue recognition, job costing, compliance
- Pain: Billable-hour business model creates revenue complexity
- Ideal package: Growth or Scale

---

## 🚫 Who We Do NOT Target

- Micro-businesses with < $20K/year revenue (margin too low for full service)
- One-time personal tax filers (not our core model)
- Companies that want the cheapest possible option above all else
