# Freelancer / Partner Model

> Nivy Tax operates with a flexible, scalable talent model. This page explains how we engage external collaborators.
> 

---

## Model Overview

Core permanent staff handle client relationships, quality control, and strategy. **Freelancers and VA partners** handle volume execution — bookkeeping, data entry, tax prep support, and outbound sales.

---

## Types of External Collaborators

### 1. Freelance Accountants / Bookkeepers

- Hired per project or on monthly retainer
- Assigned to specific client accounts under supervision
- Must pass a skills assessment before assignment
- Paid per task, per deliverable, or per hour

### 2. VA Sales Partners

- Handle outbound outreach and initial qualification
- Work from standardized scripts and playbooks (see Section 4)
- Compensated: base retainer + commission per closed deal
- KPIs tracked weekly (calls, conversions, pipeline value)

### 3. Affiliate / Referral Partners

- Businesses or professionals who refer clients to Nivy Tax
- Earn a referral commission on first 3 months of client revenue
- No operational involvement required

### 4. White-Label Partners

- Accounting firms or agencies that resell Nivy Tax services under their own brand
- Managed under a separate White-Label Agreement
- Full backend support provided by Nivy Tax team

---

## Engagement Standards

- All freelancers sign an NDA and service agreement before starting
- Access to client data is role-restricted and logged
- Performance reviewed monthly; contracts renewed or terminated based on output
