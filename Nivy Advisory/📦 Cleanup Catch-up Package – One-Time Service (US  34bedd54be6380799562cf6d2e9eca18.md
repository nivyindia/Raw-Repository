## 🔹 13. FAQs (Expanded + Practical)

**Q: What is cleanup bookkeeping?**

→ It’s the process of **rebuilding and correcting past accounting records** that are incomplete, inaccurate, or inconsistent. We go month-by-month to record missing transactions, fix errors, and reconcile balances so your books reflect the **true financial position** of your business.

**Q: Why is this important?**

→ If your books are wrong, your decisions are wrong. Inaccurate books can lead to:

- Incorrect profit calculation
- Over/under payment of taxes
- Cash flow surprises
- Issues with CPAs, lenders, or investors
    
    Clean books give you **reliable numbers for decisions and compliance**.
    

**Q: How long does it take?**

→ Typically **3–15 days**, depending on:

- Number of months pending
- Transaction volume
- Data availability/quality
    
    We’ll confirm a **clear timeline after initial review**.
    

**Q: Can you handle large backlogs?**

→ Yes. We routinely handle **6–24+ months** of backlog. For very large datasets, we split work into **phases** with checkpoints to maintain quality and speed.

**Q: Will my books be fully accurate after this?**

→ Yes. We ensure:

- All accounts are **reconciled** (bank, cards, gateways)
- Errors are corrected
- Reports match actual balances
    
    You receive **audit-ready, reliable financials**.
    

**Q: What if I don’t have all data?**

→ We guide you step-by-step to retrieve it (bank downloads, platform exports, CPA requests). If something is still missing, we use **best-practice reconstruction** and clearly document assumptions.

**Q: Is this a one-time service?**

→ Yes. It’s a **reset** of your books. After cleanup, we strongly recommend moving to **monthly bookkeeping** to keep everything accurate going forward.

**Q: Will this help with taxes?**

→ Absolutely. Clean books are the **foundation for correct tax filing**. This reduces back-and-forth with your CPA and helps avoid errors and penalties.

**Q: Do you fix errors from previous accountants?**

→ Yes. We identify issues like duplicates, wrong categories, unreconciled balances, and **systematically correct them**.

**Q: Can you work urgently?**

→ Yes. We can prioritize your case for **urgent timelines** (e.g., tax deadlines). Rush timelines may involve **additional fees** and faster data turnaround from your side.

**Q: What will I receive at the end?**

→ You’ll get **clean books in your accounting system**, plus finalized **P&L, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow**, and a **reconciliation summary**.

**Q: Will you communicate with my CPA?**

→ If needed, yes. We can **share reports and clarify queries** so your CPA can proceed smoothly.
