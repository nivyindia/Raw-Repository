# 📁 4. VA Sales Playbook

> This is the operational sales manual for Virtual Assistants. Follow this section step-by-step to qualify leads, pitch services, handle objections, and close clients consistently.
> 

---

## Contents

- **Opening Scripts** — How to start a conversation (cold outreach, referrals, inbound)
- **Qualification Questions** — The key questions to assess fit before pitching
- **Conversation Flow** — The structured path from hello to proposal
- **Pitch Framework** — How to present our services compellingly
- **Package Recommendation Logic** — How to match the right package to each client
- **Follow-up System** — The cadence and templates for nurturing leads

---

## VA Golden Rules

1. Always qualify before pitching
2. Lead with problems, not services
3. Recommend the right fit — never oversell
4. Follow up consistently — most clients close after 3–5 touches
