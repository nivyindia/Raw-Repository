## 2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be considered under the following conditions:

| Service Type | Refund Eligibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Consultation / Advisory Calls** | Refund only if the call was **not delivered or canceled by Nivy Empires**. Non-attendance by client does **not qualify** for refund. |
| **Accounting / Tax Filing / Compliance Services** | Partial or full refunds **only if Nivy fails to deliver agreed services** within the timeline specified in the service agreement. |
| **Business Setup / Registration Services** | Refund may apply if **Nivy is unable to process the registration** due to internal error. Any external charges (government fees, bank fees, third-party charges) are **non-refundable**. |
| **Digital Deliverables / Reports / Documents** | Refund applicable **only if the deliverables are incorrect or incomplete** due to Nivy’s error. |

> Note: Refunds are not applicable for delays caused by client in providing required documents or approvals.

## 3. Refund Process

1. Submit a **refund request in writing** via email: [**support@nivyempires.com**](mailto:support@nivyempires.com)
2. Include:
    - Invoice number
    - Service name
    - Reason for refund
3. Nivy Empires will **review the request within 7–10 business days**
4. Approved refunds will be processed **within 15 business days** via the **original payment method**

---

## 4. Adjustments & Credits

- In certain cases, instead of a refund, Nivy may offer **service credits or adjustments** for future services.
- This is usually done when the **service was partially delivered** or the client wants to **switch packages**.
