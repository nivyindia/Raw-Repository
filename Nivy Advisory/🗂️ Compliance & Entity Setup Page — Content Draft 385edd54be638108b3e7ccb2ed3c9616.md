## The Cost of Getting Entity Setup Wrong

The entity structure you choose on day one affects your tax rate, your ability to repatriate profits, your personal liability, your VAT/GST obligations, and your path to a future exit or fundraise — for years.

Most founders get incorporation advice from lawyers who specialise in one country, or from online incorporation services that process forms but don't understand the cross-border implications. Neither of them is asking: *How does this structure interact with where you live, where your clients are, and where you plan to go next?*

That's the question we start with — before we file anything.

## How It Works

1. **Market and structure evaluation call** — we start by understanding where you are, where your revenue comes from, where you want to go, and what you're building toward. Entity choice is a strategic decision, not a form-filling exercise.
2. **Entity recommendation, in writing** — you receive a written recommendation: which jurisdiction, which entity type, why, and what the ongoing compliance obligations will be. If more than one option is viable, we lay out the trade-offs clearly.
3. **Incorporation and registration** — we manage the process end-to-end, coordinating with local partner firms where required. You receive confirmation of incorporation, your registration numbers, and all founding documents.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Should I incorporate in a different country from where I live?**

It depends on your activity, your clients, your tax residency, and what you're building toward. There are legitimate reasons to incorporate in Singapore or the UAE — and there are situations where it creates unnecessary complexity and cost. We won't recommend offshore incorporation unless it genuinely makes sense for your specific situation.

**What's the difference between a UAE Free Zone company and a Mainland LLC?**

Free Zone companies can operate within the free zone and internationally, but have historically been restricted from trading directly with UAE mainland customers (this is changing under recent reforms). Mainland LLCs can trade freely within the UAE but require a local sponsor arrangement in some activities. The right choice depends on who your customers are and what you're selling. See our [UAE country page](https://app.notion.com/countries/uae/) for a full breakdown.

**Do you handle bank account opening?**

We guide you through the process and provide introductions where we have existing relationships. Bank account opening in UAE and Singapore in particular involves significant due diligence from the bank — we help you prepare the documentation and set the right expectations on timeline.

**What ongoing compliance is required after incorporation?**

This varies by jurisdiction. In the UK, you'll file a Confirmation Statement and annual accounts with Companies House. In Singapore, you'll file annual returns with ACRA and corporate tax returns with IRAS. In the UAE, VAT filings and corporate tax returns are required. We manage all of these for you as part of ongoing compliance service.

**Can you help if I've already incorporated but think I chose the wrong structure?**

Yes. A structure review is a separate engagement — we review your current setup, your tax position, and your business trajectory, and give you a written recommendation on whether to stay, restructure, or add an entity. Restructuring has tax implications; we map those before recommending it.
