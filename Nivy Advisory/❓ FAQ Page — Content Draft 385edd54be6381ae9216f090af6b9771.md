## Category 1 — General / Getting Started

- How does Nivy Advisory work if I operate in more than one country?
- Do I need to be a registered business to work with you, or can individuals use your services too?
- How quickly can you onboard me?
- What software do you work with (Xero, QuickBooks, etc.)?

## Category 2 — Pricing & Packages

- How is pricing determined — is it a flat fee or based on complexity?
- Can I start on Essentials and upgrade to Growth or Global Partner later?
- Do you charge extra for adding a new country mid-engagement?
- Is there a contract minimum or can I cancel anytime?

## Category 3 — Tax & Compliance

- Will I get a single point of contact, or different people per country?
- How do you handle catch-up filing if I'm behind in one or more jurisdictions?
- Do you guarantee filing deadlines are met?
- What happens if a tax authority audits one of my filings?

## Category 4 — Cross-Border Specific

- I have obligations in two countries — do you coordinate the filings so they're consistent with each other?
- Do you hold direct licensing in every country, or do you work with local partner firms?
- Can you help me decide where to incorporate if I'm expanding into a new market?

## Category 5 — Security & Data

- How is my financial data protected?
- Where is my data stored, and does that vary by region?
- Who has access to my financial information internally?
