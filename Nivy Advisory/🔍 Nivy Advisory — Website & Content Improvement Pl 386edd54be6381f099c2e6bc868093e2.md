# Phase A — SEO Foundation

> **Do this before any page goes live.** SEO decisions made post-launch cost 3–6 months of recovery. URL structure, schema, and meta strategy must be locked before dev begins.
> 

## A1. Keyword Research & Page-Level Keyword Map

**Gap:** No keyword research exists. Every page needs a primary keyword, 2–3 secondary keywords, and a search intent classification.

**Action items:**

- [ ]  Research primary keywords for each of the 6 country pages (e.g. "CPA for US expats", "UK self assessment for non-residents", "accounting for UAE free zone company")
- [ ]  Research primary keywords for each of the 4 service pages (e.g. "cross-border bookkeeping service", "virtual CFO for international businesses")
- [ ]  Research long-tail keywords for FAQ page and blog articles
- [ ]  Build a master keyword map table: one row per page, columns = Primary KW | Monthly Volume (est.) | Intent | Secondary KWs | Competitor ranking
- [ ]  Prioritize by: high commercial intent + low competition first (service and country pages), informational intent second (blog)

**Recommended tools:** Ahrefs / Semrush / Google Search Console (post-launch)

## A2. Meta Title & Description Templates — All Pages

**Gap:** No meta titles or descriptions exist. These are a direct ranking and CTR factor.

**Action items:**

- [ ]  Write meta titles for every Phase 1 page: format `[Primary KW] | Nivy Advisory` — under 60 characters
- [ ]  Write meta descriptions for every Phase 1 page: 140–155 characters, CTA verb, primary KW near start
- [ ]  Country pages: include jurisdiction name and tax authority acronym (IRS, HMRC, ATO etc.) — these are high-intent search phrases
- [ ]  Service pages: include credential signal ("CPA-prepared", "ACCA-credentialed") — differentiates from non-credentialed competitors in SERP

**Template examples:**

- Home: `Cross-Border CPA, Bookkeeping & Tax | Nivy Advisory`
- US page: `US Tax Filing for Expats & Businesses | IRS-Experienced CPAs | Nivy Advisory`
- UK page: `UK Self Assessment & Corporation Tax | HMRC-Focused Accountants | Nivy Advisory`

## A3. URL Structure — Decision Required

**Gap:** Part 5.3 flags this as Not Started. Blocks dev build.

**Recommendation:** Subdirectory structure under a single `.com` domain:

- `/services/cpa-tax/`
- `/countries/united-states/`
- `/countries/united-kingdom/`
- `/blog/`

**Rationale:** Single domain consolidates domain authority. Subdomains split it. ccTLDs require separate SEO effort. For one brand in English across multiple countries, subdirectories win.

**Action items:**

- [ ]  Confirm domain name and finalize URL slug structure
- [ ]  Build complete URL map for all Phase 1 pages before dev begins
- [ ]  Use full country name in slugs (`/countries/united-states/`) not abbreviations (`/us/`) — better for long-tail keyword matching

## A4. Schema Markup Plan

**Gap:** Part 5.3 mentions structured data but no implementation plan exists.

**Action items:**

- [ ]  Home page: `Organization` schema (name, URL, logo, sameAs, contactPoint)
- [ ]  Service pages: `ProfessionalService` schema
- [ ]  Country pages: `LocalBusiness` or `VirtualLocation` schema with country-specific region fields
- [ ]  FAQ page: `FAQPage` schema — each Q&A as `Question` + `acceptedAnswer`
- [ ]  Blog articles: `Article` schema with author, datePublished, dateModified
- [ ]  Breadcrumb schema on all interior pages
- [ ]  Aggregate rating schema (Phase 2, once real reviews collected)

## A5. hreflang Implementation

**Gap:** Current plan says hreflang not needed between English-speaking markets. This is incorrect.

**Correction:** hreflang IS needed between `en-US`, `en-GB`, `en-AU`, `en-CA`, `en-SG` when serving locale-specific content. Google uses it to serve the right country page to the right user.

**Action items:**

- [ ]  Add hreflang tags on all country pages pointing to self with correct locale
- [ ]  Add `x-default` tag pointing to Home on all country pages
- [ ]  UAE page: add `ar` language alternate if/when Arabic version is created

## A6. Internal Linking Map

**Gap:** No internal linking strategy exists. Internal links distribute authority and guide crawlers.

**Action items:**

- [ ]  Map every country page → linked service pages
- [ ]  Map every service page → relevant country pages + pricing
- [ ]  Home → all country pages + all service pages
- [ ]  Blog articles → at least 2 commercial pages each
- [ ]  FAQ answers → relevant service, country, or blog pages
- [ ]  Create a visual internal link map before dev handoff

# Phase B — Content Depth Improvements

> Current service page drafts are 200–300 word outlines. Competitors for cross-border CPA searches (Greenback, Taxes for Expats, Bright!Tax) publish 1,200–2,500 word service pages with comparison tables, calculators, and detailed processes. Thin content ranks poorly and converts poorly.
> 

## B1. Service Page Expansion

All 4 service pages need expansion from outline to full landing page.

**Additions required for each service page:**

- [ ]  **Outcome framing** — replace "What's Included" with "What You'll Have After Working With Us"
- [ ]  **Comparison table** — Nivy Advisory vs. local-only CPA vs. DIY software (3 columns, 6–8 rows)
- [ ]  **Expanded process section** — 1–2 sentences per step, not just labels
- [ ]  **Jurisdiction-specific examples** — e.g. CPA & Tax page: "If you're a UK LTD director also filing a US return, here's where the overlap gets costly without coordination"
- [ ]  **Pricing anchor** — "Starting from [X]" or "Fixed-fee projects from [X]" even if exact prices deferred
- [ ]  **FAQ block** — 4–6 FAQs embedded on the service page itself, with schema markup
- [ ]  **Testimonial slot** — placeholder with correct dimensions for Phase 2 population
- [ ]  **Cross-links** — links to: related country pages, pricing, at least 1 blog article

**Target word count per service page:** 900–1,400 words

## B2. Country Page Depth — Tier 1

Tier 1 country pages (US, UK, Canada) should be the most detailed pages after Home.

**Additions required:**

- [ ]  **Deadline calendar snippet** — a visible table of 4–6 most important annual deadlines for businesses and individuals in that country
- [ ]  **"Common mistakes" section** — e.g. "3 things US expats get wrong about FBAR" — high-intent, shareable, SEO-friendly
- [ ]  **Expat vs. business split** — address both audiences clearly, not blended
- [ ]  **Tax treaty note** — flag the relevant tax treaty for US/UK, US/Canada, UK/Australia and link to blog article
- [ ]  **Entity types table** — LLC vs. Ltd vs. Inc — what each looks like from the other country's tax perspective
- [ ]  **Dual CTA** — "I'm a business" and "I'm an individual/expat" routing to same contact page with pre-filled context

## B3. Country Page — Tier 2 Priority Additions

Full parity is Phase 3, but pull forward 2 critical items now:

- [ ]  **UAE page:** Free zone vs. mainland breakdown (the #1 UAE corporate tax question) — minimum a 4-row table
- [ ]  **Singapore page:** Holding company structure section — this is the primary reason clients are in Singapore, must be addressed directly

## B4. Home Page Additions

Home draft is structurally strong. Missing:

- [ ]  **Social proof above fold** — one concrete data point in trust strip: e.g. "50+ businesses across 6 countries" or "[X] successful cross-border filings"
- [ ]  **Lead magnet teaser** — "Download the 2025/2026 Cross-Border Tax Deadline Calendar" banner between Countries and Pricing sections
- [ ]  **Founder note or short video** — 60-second video or written founder note; financial services clients respond to face + credibility signal before handing over sensitive data
- [ ]  **Software partner logo strip** — Xero, QuickBooks, FreshBooks logos as trust signals (even without client logos)

## B5. Pricing Page Improvements

- [ ]  **Price anchors** — "Starting from" per tier, ideally a fixed-fee example for most common service
- [ ]  **Comparison table** — what's included per tier, row by row
- [ ]  **"Most Popular" badge** on Growth tier
- [ ]  **Pricing FAQ block** — 5 questions: Can I change tiers? What's not included? Annual discount? What currency? How does billing work internationally?
- [ ]  **Soft guarantee note** — e.g. "If you're not satisfied after the first month, we'll refund the onboarding fee" — reduces new client risk perception
- [ ]  **Currency display note** — which currency is displayed + how international billing works

## B6. About Page — Credentials & Trust

Highest trust risk in the entire site.

- [ ]  **Lock verified credentials before launch** — CPA/ACCA/CA license numbers must be real and verifiable; do not go live with placeholders
- [ ]  **Decide team display format now** — individual bios with credential numbers, or firm-level credential statement per jurisdiction
- [ ]  **Add "How We Protect Your Data" section** — financial services clients are risk-aware; data security messaging on About converts where compliance disclaimers don't

# Phase C — Conversion & UX

## C1. Lead Capture Strategy

**Gap:** Only CTA is "Book a Free Consultation" — a high-commitment ask. Need a lower-friction capture option.

- [ ]  Add secondary CTA on Home, country pages, and service pages: "Download the [Country] Tax Deadline Calendar"
- [ ]  Add exit-intent or 60%-scroll-triggered email capture: offer the tax calendar as incentive
- [ ]  Contact page: add pre-qualification dropdown ("What are you looking for?": Business tax / Individual tax / Bookkeeping / Advisory / Not sure)
- [ ]  Book a Call page: embed Calendly (or equivalent) directly — never send users off-site just to book

## C2. CTA Hierarchy & Placement

- [ ]  Every page: one primary CTA (Book a Call) + one secondary CTA (Download Calendar / See Pricing)
- [ ]  Country pages: test specific CTA — "Book a Call for [Country] Tax" vs. generic
- [ ]  Service pages: bottom CTA should echo page topic — "Get Started with CPA-Prepared Tax Filing" not generic
- [ ]  Sticky header CTA on all pages

## C3. Form Design

- [ ]  Keep contact form to 4 fields max for initial inquiry: Name, Email, Country, Brief description
- [ ]  Add: preferred contact time + timezone selector — critical for 12-hour span between APAC and Americas
- [ ]  Confirmation page after submission must state: what happens next, expected response time, who contacts them

## C4. Mobile-First Design Notes

Currently absent from all content drafts.

- [ ]  Write mobile-first brief for dev: all CTAs thumb-reachable, table-heavy pages get accordion/scroll treatment
- [ ]  Country flag strip on Home: touch-friendly scrollable row on mobile, not desktop grid
- [ ]  Deadline calendar tables: accordion or horizontal scroll on mobile

## E1. Blog Topic Cluster Architecture

- [ ]  Define 3 pillar topics: (1) Cross-Border Tax, (2) International Business Setup, (3) Expat Finance
- [ ]  Each pillar gets a long-form pillar page (2,000–3,000 words) linking to all cluster articles

- [ ]  Example cluster — Cross-Border Tax: UK-US tax treaty explained | FBAR for UK residents | Self-Assessment for US citizens in the UK | HMRC vs IRS — who gets paid first?

6 guides already outlined. Add:

- [ ]  Give each guide a search-intent title, not generic: e.g. "The Complete Guide to UK Corporation Tax for Foreign-Owned Companies"
