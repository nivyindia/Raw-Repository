## Free Zone vs. Mainland — Which Should You Choose?

This is the single most common question UAE-bound businesses ask, and the right answer depends on where your customers and operations actually are — not just where it's cheapest to incorporate.

| Factor | Free Zone | Mainland |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ownership | 100% foreign ownership as standard | 100% foreign ownership permitted for most activities (post-2021 reform) |
| Corporate Tax rate | 0% on "qualifying income" if Qualifying Free Zone Person conditions are met; 9% otherwise | 9% on profits above the AED 375,000 threshold |
| Where you can trade | Restricted from direct trade with the UAE mainland without a local distributor or branch | Unrestricted access to the UAE mainland market |
| Office requirement | Flexi-desk or shared office options widely available, often lower cost | Physical office typically required; higher setup overhead |
| Best fit | Export-focused, holding companies, e-commerce, consultancies serving clients outside the UAE | Businesses selling directly to UAE consumers or government, retail, F&B, on-the-ground services |
| Visa allocation | Tied to office package size (e.g. flexi-desk = limited visas) | Generally more flexible visa allocation tied to office size and activity |

**The catch most businesses miss:** qualifying for the Free Zone's 0% rate isn't automatic. You must maintain adequate substance in the UAE, derive income only from "qualifying activities," and keep non-qualifying income below a de minimis threshold — fail any of these and the *entire* income (not just the excess) can become taxable at 9%. We review this eligibility position annually, not just at setup.
