# Financial Reporting

> Plain-language explanation of the reports we produce and what they mean.
> 

---

## Core Reports We Produce

### Profit & Loss Statement (P&L)

Shows revenue, expenses, and net profit/loss for a period. Used for performance review, tax prep, and investor reporting. Delivered monthly.

### Balance Sheet

Shows assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time. Used for loan applications and investor due diligence. Delivered monthly/quarterly.

### Cash Flow Statement

Shows cash in and out. Used for cash planning and identifying shortfalls early. Delivered monthly.

### Budget vs. Actual Report

Compares planned vs. actual financial performance. Delivered monthly (Growth and Scale clients).

---

## How We Deliver

Via secure client portal (PDF + Excel), with a plain-English commentary, by the 10th of each month.
