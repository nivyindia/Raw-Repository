# ♿ Accessibility Statement

**Applies to:** [www.thenivy.com](http://www.thenivy.com)

**Last updated:** [Insert date on publish]

---

## 1. Our Commitment

Nivy Advisory is committed to ensuring this website is accessible to the widest possible audience, including people with disabilities, regardless of which of our markets (US, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, Singapore) they're accessing it from.

---

## 2. Standards We Aim For

We aim to meet **WCAG 2.1 Level AA** (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines), which is the standard referenced by accessibility regulations and expectations in most of our markets, including:

- UK Equality Act 2010 expectations for digital services
- Australian Disability Discrimination Act expectations
- US ADA-related digital accessibility expectations
- Ontario's AODA (if serving Ontario-based clients specifically)

---

## 3. What We're Doing

- Ensuring sufficient color contrast for text and interactive elements
- Providing alt text for meaningful images
- Ensuring the site is navigable by keyboard alone
- Using semantic HTML structure for screen reader compatibility
- Providing clear, consistent navigation and form labeling
- Testing key user journeys (contact form, booking widget, pricing page) with accessibility tools periodically

---

## 4. Known Limitations

We are continuously improving accessibility. If you encounter any barrier while using this site, we want to know about it (see Section 5).

---

## 5. Feedback & Contact

If you experience any accessibility difficulty using this website, please contact us at [**contact@thenivy.com**](mailto:contact@thenivy.com) with:

- The page/URL where the issue occurred
- A description of the problem
- The assistive technology/browser you were using, if relevant

We will respond and aim to resolve reported issues promptly.
