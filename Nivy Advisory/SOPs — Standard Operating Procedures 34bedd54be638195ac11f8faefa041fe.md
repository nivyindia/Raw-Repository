# SOPs — Standard Operating Procedures

> Standard Operating Procedures for Nivy Tax's core service delivery. Every team member should follow these procedures exactly.
> 

---

## SOP 1: Monthly Bookkeeping Close

**Frequency:** Monthly, by the 14th of each month

**Steps:**

1. Import all bank and credit card transactions
2. Categorise every transaction against the chart of accounts
3. Match invoices to payments received
4. Post payroll entries (if applicable)
5. Reconcile all accounts (balance = zero difference)
6. Review for unusual items or large variances
7. Run trial balance and verify
8. Prepare P&L, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement
9. Internal senior review and sign-off
10. Upload to client portal with commentary

**Quality Check:** No bank account should be unreconciled. No transaction should be uncategorised.

---

## SOP 2: Tax Return Filing (US Federal)

**Frequency:** Annual (entity-dependent deadlines)

**Steps:**

1. Collect all required documents (see Document Checklist)
2. Review prior year return for carryforwards and elections
3. Import trial balance from accounting software
4. Prepare return draft in tax software
5. Review for all applicable deductions and credits
6. Internal peer review (second sign-off required)
7. Share draft with client for review and approval
8. Obtain signed authorisation (e-sign)
9. E-file return
10. Confirm acknowledgement and send copy to client
11. Log completion and set next year reminder

---

## SOP 3: Month-End Client Communication

**Frequency:** Monthly, within 2 days of report delivery

**Steps:**

1. Deliver report pack via portal (per Workflow Overview)
2. Send notification email with summary highlights
3. Flag any action items the client needs to take
4. Record delivery in CRM / client log
5. Follow up if no acknowledgement within 48 hours
