# Client Confidentiality Policy – Nivy Empires

**Effective Date:** [Insert Date]

At **Nivy Empires**, we respect the **confidentiality of our clients’ personal, financial, and business information**. This policy outlines how we collect, protect, and manage client information in accordance with professional and legal standards.

---

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All clients, prospects, and business partners of Nivy Empires
- All information shared with us via **consultations, email, website forms, phone calls, or in-person meetings**
- All employees, contractors, and affiliates handling client information

---

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to:

- Personal identification information (name, address, contact details)
- Financial information (bank accounts, tax returns, statements)
- Business information (company structure, transactions, strategies, trade secrets)
- Legal documents and agreements
- Any other information marked or understood as confidential

---

## 3. Collection and Use

- We collect only the information **necessary to provide our services**, including accounting, taxation, audit, advisory, and business setup services
- Data is used solely for the purpose of:
    - Providing professional services
    - Communication and client support
    - Legal and regulatory compliance
    - Internal reporting and quality assurance

---

## 4. Protection of Client Information

- All client data is handled **securely and confidentially**
- Security measures include:
    - Access restrictions to authorized personnel only
    - Encryption of sensitive electronic data
    - Secure storage of physical documents
    - Regular audits and monitoring

---

## 5. Sharing and Disclosure

- We **do not share confidential client information** with third parties, except:
    - When required by law or regulatory authorities
    - With trusted service providers **under strict confidentiality agreements**
    - With client consent
- Employees and partners are **bound by confidentiality agreements** and professional ethical obligations.

---

## 6. Retention of Confidential Information

- Confidential information is retained **only as long as necessary** for delivering services or as required by law
- After retention, data is securely **deleted or anonymized**

---

## 7. Breach Notification

- Any suspected breach of confidentiality will be **immediately investigated**
- Clients affected will be notified in accordance with local laws and regulations
- Corrective measures will be taken to prevent recurrence

---

## 8. Client Responsibilities

- Clients should provide **accurate and complete information**
- Clients should **safeguard their own access credentials** (login, emails, documents shared electronically)

---

## 9. Compliance with Laws and Professional Ethics

- Nivy Empires complies with:
    - **ICAI ethical standards** (India)
    - **UAE legal and regulatory requirements**
    - **International best practices** for client data and confidentiality
