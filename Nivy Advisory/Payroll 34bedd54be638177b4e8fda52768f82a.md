# Payroll

> Plain-language explanation of our payroll management service.
> 

---

## What Is Payroll Management?

Payroll is calculating and paying employee salaries, wages, bonuses, and deductions — ensuring all tax withholdings and filings are done correctly and on time.

---

## What We Include

- Monthly / bi-weekly / weekly payroll calculation
- Employee and contractor payments
- Federal and state payroll tax withholding and filing
- W-2 and 1099 preparation and filing
- New employee setup (W-4, I-9)
- Benefits deductions (health insurance, 401k)
- Payroll compliance checks (FLSA, state wage laws)

## Tools We Use

Gusto | ADP | Paychex | QuickBooks Payroll
