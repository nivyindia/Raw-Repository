# Testimonials

> Verified client quotes and endorsements. Collect these regularly — social proof is one of the strongest sales tools we have.
> 

---

## How to Collect Testimonials

1. Request after a positive client milestone (first clean month-end, successful tax filing, etc.)
2. Make it easy: send a 2–3 question email or a Google Form
3. Ask specifically: *"What problem were you facing before working with us? What changed?"*
4. Offer to write a draft they can approve — reduces friction significantly
5. Always get written permission before publishing

---

## Testimonial Framework (For Requesting)

Send this to happy clients:

> *"We'd love to feature your experience with Nivy Tax. Would you be willing to answer 2 quick questions?*
> 

> *1. What challenge were you facing before working with us?*
> 

> *2. What's changed since we started working together?*
> 

> *Your feedback helps other businesses like yours find us. Thank you!"*
>
