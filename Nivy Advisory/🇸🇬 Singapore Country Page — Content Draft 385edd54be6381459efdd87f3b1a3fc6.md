## Why Businesses Use Singapore as a Holding Company Location

This is the #1 reason founders look at Singapore, and it deserves a direct answer rather than a passing mention.

| Driver | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- |
| Territorial tax system | Foreign-sourced income is generally not taxed in Singapore unless remitted and not otherwise exempt — lets group profits accumulate without immediate Singapore tax |
| Extensive treaty network | 90+ double-tax treaties reduce withholding tax on dividends, interest, and royalties flowing up to the Singapore holding entity |
| No capital gains tax | Gains on disposal of shares in subsidiaries are generally not taxed — attractive for exit and restructuring scenarios |
| No withholding tax on dividends paid out | Singapore does not levy withholding tax on dividends distributed to shareholders, regardless of where they're based |
| Political and regulatory stability | Predictable legal system and IRAS administration reduce structuring risk versus less stable hub jurisdictions |

**Where it gets complicated:** qualifying for treaty benefits requires the Singapore entity to demonstrate genuine substance — real management decisions made in Singapore, not just a registered address. Tax authorities in the subsidiary's home country increasingly scrutinise "letterbox" holding structures. We assess substance requirements as part of every holding-company engagement, not after the structure is challenged.
