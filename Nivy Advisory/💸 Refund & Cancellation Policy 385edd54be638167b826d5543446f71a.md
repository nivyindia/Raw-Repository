## 1. Overview

Nivy Advisory offers services under three engagement models: **Retainer (monthly)**, **Project-Based (fixed fee)**, and **Hybrid**. Because refund logic differs by model, this policy is split accordingly.

---

## 2. Retainer Services (e.g., ongoing bookkeeping, advisory)

- Retainers are billed **in advance**, monthly, for the upcoming service period.
- **Cancellation:** Either party may cancel with **30 days' written notice**. Work already delivered within the current billing cycle is non-refundable; any unused portion of a prepaid cycle beyond the notice period is refunded pro-rata.
- **Mid-cycle cancellation:** If a client cancels mid-cycle, Nivy Advisory will complete reconciliation/closing of the books up to the cancellation date as part of an orderly handover — this work is billable and is not refunded.
- **No refund for partial-month dissatisfaction once work has commenced** — instead, clients are encouraged to raise concerns immediately so the engagement can be corrected within the same cycle (see Section 5).

---

## 3. Project-Based Services (e.g., annual tax filing, entity incorporation, catch-up bookkeeping, one-time advisory engagements)

- Project-based engagements typically require a **deposit (commonly 50%)** before work begins, with the balance due on completion or per milestone, as specified in the signed engagement letter.

| Stage | Refund Eligibility |
| --- | --- |
| Before any work has started | Deposit refundable in full, minus any third-party costs already incurred on the client's behalf (e.g., government filing fees) |
| After research/data collection has started but before filing/deliverable submission | Deposit is non-refundable; covers time and resources already committed |
| After filing has been submitted to a tax authority or deliverable has been completed and delivered | No refund — the service has been rendered, regardless of subsequent outcome (e.g., tax authority assessment results, audit selection) |
- **Important:** Nivy Advisory cannot offer refunds based on the *outcome* of a filing (e.g., size of refund/liability, audit selection by a tax authority) since these outcomes are determined by the relevant government body, not by Nivy Advisory's service quality.

---

## 4. Hybrid Engagements

Where a client has both a retainer (e.g., bookkeeping) and a project-based component (e.g., annual filing) under one engagement, each component is governed by its respective section above. The engagement letter will itemize which fees fall under which model.

---

## 5. Service Quality Remediation (Before Refund)

If a client is dissatisfied with service quality, Nivy Advisory's first step is always **remediation, not refund**:

1. Client raises the concern in writing to their account manager within 14 days of the issue.
2. Nivy Advisory reviews and responds within 5 business days.
3. If a genuine service failure on Nivy Advisory's part is confirmed (e.g., a missed filing deadline caused by our error, not client-provided information delays), Nivy Advisory will, at its discretion, either re-perform the work at no charge or issue a partial refund/credit proportional to the affected work.

---

## 6. Non-Refundable Items (All Models)

- Government/regulatory filing fees, incorporation fees, or third-party software costs already paid on the client's behalf
- Work completed and delivered, regardless of subsequent client decisions
- Delays or errors caused by late, incomplete, or inaccurate information/documents provided by the client
- Missed deadlines caused by the client's failure to provide required documents within the agreed timeline

---

## 7. How to Cancel

- Email [**contact@thenivy.com**](mailto:contact@thenivy.com) (or the relevant regional hub email) with "Cancellation Request" in the subject line, including your account name and effective date requested.
- You will receive written confirmation and a final invoice/refund calculation (if applicable) within 5 business days.

---

## 8. Currency & Payment Method

Refunds are issued in the same currency and to the same payment method used for the original payment, where possible. Processing times depend on the original payment method and may take 5–10 business days.
