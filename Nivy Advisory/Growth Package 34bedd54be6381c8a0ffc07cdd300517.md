# Growth Package

> Full accounting service for growing businesses. Best for businesses with 100–500 transactions/month.
> 

---

## What's Included

- Everything in Starter Package, plus:
- Up to 500 transactions/month
- Accounts payable and receivable management
- Monthly management accounts (P&L + Balance Sheet + Cash Flow)
- Budget vs. Actual reporting
- Quarterly strategy review call (30 min)
- Dedicated Account Manager
- Priority email support (24-hour SLA)
- Annual tax prep package included

## Add-On Options

- Payroll Management
- Tax Support Package
- Fractional CFO (strategic overlay)

---

## Ideal Client Profile

- SMBs with 1–10 employees
- E-commerce businesses (multi-platform)
- Professional services firms (agencies, consultants)
- Companies seeking growth capital or investment
