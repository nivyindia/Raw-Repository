# 🏠 **1. HOME PAGE LAYOUT**

**Goal:** Authority + Trust + Conversion

1. Hero Section
    - Headline (Outcome-driven)
    - Subheadline (Who you help + How)
    - Primary CTA (Book Call / Get Audit)
    - Trust badges / logos
2. Who We Help
    - Target industries / segments
3. Problems We Solve
    - 4–6 pain points
4. Services Overview
    - Service cards (4–6)
5. Why Choose Us
    - Differentiators (4–6)
6. Our Process
    - Step-by-step delivery model
7. Proof & Results
    - Metrics, testimonials, case study highlights
8. Featured Case Studies
    - 2–4 cards linking to full pages
9. Tools & Technology
    - Logos / stack
10. CTA Section
- Strong conversion CTA
1. Footer
- Contact, legal, links
