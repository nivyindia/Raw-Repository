# Brand Voice & Tone

> This page defines how Nivy Tax sounds in all communications — from emails and proposals to social media and sales scripts.
> 

---

## Our Brand Personality

Nivy Tax is:

- **Authoritative** — We know what we're talking about. We speak with confidence.
- **Clear** — We explain complex topics simply. No unnecessary jargon.
- **Warm** — Professional but human. We're partners, not auditors.
- **Proactive** — We bring solutions, not problems.

Nivy Tax is NOT:

- Robotic or overly formal
- Casual to the point of seeming unqualified
- Vague or evasive about what we do and how much it costs

---

## Voice by Channel

| Channel | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email | Professional, warm, direct | "Here's what we found and what we recommend next." |
| LinkedIn | Expert, thought-leadership | "3 tax mistakes costing US e-commerce businesses thousands." |
| WhatsApp / DM | Conversational, responsive | "Happy to walk you through the options — want a quick call?" |
| Proposals | Formal, precise, value-focused | "Based on your requirements, we recommend the Growth Package." |
| Reports | Clear, structured, insightful | "Here's what changed this month and why it matters." |

---

## Key Phrases We Use

- ✅ "Let's simplify this for you..."
- ✅ "Here's what this means for your business..."
- ✅ "We'll handle it from here."
- ✅ "Based on your situation, we recommend..."

## Phrases We Avoid

- ❌ "As per my last email..."
- ❌ "We cannot be held responsible for..."
- ❌ "You should have..."
- ❌ Excessive legal disclaimers in sales conversations
