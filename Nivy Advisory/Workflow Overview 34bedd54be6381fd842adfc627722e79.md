# Workflow Overview

> High-level map of how client work moves through the Nivy Tax delivery system — from onboarding to monthly cycle to annual filing.
> 

---

## The Monthly Delivery Cycle

```
Client Data Received → Categorisation → Reconciliation → Review → Report Delivery → Client Communication
```

---

## Step-by-Step Monthly Process

### Week 1 (1st–7th): Data Collection

- Bank statements auto-imported via feed or manually uploaded by client
- Receipts and invoices collected via portal
- Payroll data confirmed (if applicable)
- Previous month queries resolved

### Week 1–2 (1st–14th): Transaction Processing

- All transactions categorised to correct accounts
- Invoices matched to payments
- Expenses coded by category
- Payroll entries posted

### Week 2 (8th–14th): Reconciliation

- All bank and credit card accounts reconciled
- Intercompany transactions matched (if multi-entity)
- Accruals and prepayments adjusted
- Open items investigated and resolved

### Week 2 (by 14th): Internal Review

- Senior accountant reviews completed work
- Variance analysis vs. prior month performed
- Tax position assessed
- Report pack prepared

### By 10th of Following Month: Client Delivery

- Monthly report pack delivered via portal
- Plain-English commentary included
- Key items flagged for client attention
- Response window open for client questions

---

## Annual Cycle (Key Milestones)

| Month | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| January | Prior year data finalisation |
| February–March | Tax preparation and review |
| March 15 | S-Corp / Partnership filing deadline |
| April 15 | Individual / C-Corp filing deadline |
| Ongoing (Q) | Estimated tax payments |
| December | Year-end planning and strategy |
