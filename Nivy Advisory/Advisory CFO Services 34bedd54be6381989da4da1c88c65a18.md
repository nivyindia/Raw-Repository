# Advisory / CFO Services

> Plain-language explanation of our Virtual CFO and advisory offering.
> 

---

## What Is a Virtual CFO?

A Virtual CFO is a senior financial expert who works part-time with your business — providing strategic oversight that growing companies need, without the cost of a full-time hire.

---

## What We Include

- Monthly 1:1 strategic financial review call
- Annual budgeting and financial planning
- Cash flow forecasting (3-month and 12-month rolling)
- KPI development and dashboard setup
- Investor-ready financial modelling and reporting
- Scenario planning (growth, downturn, expansion)
- Board / investor meeting prep support
- Business entity structuring advice
- M&A financial due diligence support

---

## Standard Accounting vs. Virtual CFO

| Standard Accounting | Virtual CFO |
| --- | --- |
| Records the past | Plans the future |
| Reports what happened | Advises what to do next |
| Reactive | Proactive |
| Compliance-focused | Strategy-focused |
