# 📅 Tax Deadline Calendars — 6 Countries — Content Draft

**Status:** Drafted (structure + content) — dates need annual confirmation before publishing

**Purpose:** Lead magnets per country, per Phase 2 plan. Each is intended as a downloadable (PDF/gated) one-pager.

---

## Lead Magnet Format (applies to all 6)

- Title: "[Country] Tax Deadline Calendar [Year]"
- Gated behind email capture
- One-page visual calendar + short explainer per deadline
- CTA at bottom: "Never miss a deadline — book a free consultation"
- Footer disclaimer: dates are general guidance, confirm specifics with an advisor

---

## 🇺🇸 United States

- Jan 31 — 1099/W-2 issuance
- Mar 15 — S-Corp / Partnership returns due
- Apr 15 — Individual & C-Corp returns due; FBAR due (auto-extends to Oct 15)
- Jun 15 — Extended deadline for US citizens abroad
- Oct 15 — Extended filing deadline (if extension filed)
- Quarterly — Estimated tax payments (Apr/Jun/Sep/Jan)

## 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

- Oct 5 — Register for Self-Assessment if newly required
- Oct 31 — Paper Self-Assessment deadline
- Jan 31 — Online Self-Assessment filing and payment deadline
- 9 months + 1 day after year-end — Corporation Tax payment due
- 12 months after year-end — CT600 filing deadline
- Quarterly — VAT returns (per registered period)

## 🇨🇦 Canada

- Apr 30 — T1 individual filing and payment deadline
- Jun 15 — T1 filing deadline for self-employed (payment still due Apr 30)
- 6 months after fiscal year-end — T2 corporate return deadline
- Monthly/Quarterly — GST/HST returns (per assigned frequency)

## 🇦🇺 Australia

- 28 days after quarter-end — BAS lodgment (quarterly lodgers)
- Oct 31 — Individual tax return deadline (self-lodged)
- May 15 — Extended deadline via registered tax agent

## 🇦🇪 UAE

- Within prescribed window of incorporation — Corporate Tax registration for new entities
- Within 9 months of financial year-end — Corporate Tax return filing and payment
- Quarterly (typical) — VAT returns

## 🇸🇬 Singapore

- Within 3 months of financial year-end — ECI filing
- Nov 30 — Corporate Tax return filing deadline (Form C-S/C)
- Quarterly (typical) — GST returns

---

## Critical Note Before Publishing

All specific dates above must be reconfirmed against the current tax year before this becomes a live lead magnet — several (UAE Corporate Tax especially) are subject to entity-specific variation, not single fixed dates. Recommend an annual review cycle each January to keep all 6 calendars current.
