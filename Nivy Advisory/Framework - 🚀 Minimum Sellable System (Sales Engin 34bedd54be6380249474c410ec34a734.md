# Framework - 🚀 Minimum Sellable System (Sales Engine Kit)

# 

## 🔹 1. Clear Offer (Most Important)

Not a service list — a result-based offer

Example:

“We manage your entire US bookkeeping, keep your books tax-ready, and give you clear monthly profit insights — so you always know where your money is going.”

### What to create:

- 1 strong headline
- 3–5 outcomes (not services)

---

## 🔹 2. 3 Fixed Packages

You cannot sell without this.

### Create:

- Starter
- Growth
- Scale

### Each must include:

- Price
- Deliverables
- Who it’s for

---

## 🔹 3. One-Page Service Document (Sales PDF)

This is what your VA sends.

### Must include:

- What you do
- Packages
- Process
- Tools (QuickBooks Online, Xero)
- Why choose you

### Format:

- Google Doc / Notion / PDF

---

## 🔹 4. Sample Output (Trust Builder)

Clients don’t buy without proof.

### Create:

- Sample P&L
- Sample dashboard
- Sample report

(Dummy data is fine)

---

## 🔹 5. VA Sales Script (Critical)

Your VA should follow a fixed system.

### Script must include:

- Opening message
- Qualification questions
- Package suggestion
- Objection handling
- Closing line

---

## 🔹 6. Qualification Questions

Your VA should ask:

- What business do you run?
- Monthly transactions?
- Current bookkeeping status?
- Which software?
- Need tax support?

---

## 🔹 7. Onboarding System

After client says “yes”

### You need:

- Access checklist
- Document checklist
- Welcome message

---

## 🔹 8. Payment System

Must be ready before selling.

### Use:

- Stripe
- Wise

---

## 🔹 9. Simple CRM / Tracking

Start simple.

### Use:

- Google Sheets

### Track:

- Leads
- Status
- Follow-ups
- Closed deals

---

## 🔹 10. Follow-Up System

Most sales happen here.

### Create:

- Day 1 follow-up
- Day 3 follow-up
- Day 7 follow-up

---

## ⚡ Reality Check

If you build these 10 things:

You can start closing clients within 7–15 days.

---

## 🧠 Important Understanding

Most people fail because:

- They try to build everything
- Instead of building a sellable minimum system

---

## 🚀 Next Step

Build 3 high-converting packages including:

- Pricing
- Deliverables
- VA scripts
- FAQs
- Client conversations

This will make the system fully operational and ready to sell.
