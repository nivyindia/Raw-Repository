## 1. What Are Cookies

- Cookies are **small text files** stored on your device when you visit a website.
- They help the website remember your preferences, improve functionality, and provide analytics.

---

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

### a) Essential / Strictly Necessary Cookies

- Required for the website to function properly
- Enable core features like:
    - Security
    - Login
    - Form submissions

### b) Performance / Analytics Cookies

- Help us **understand how users interact** with our website
- Examples: Google Analytics
- Collect anonymous data like page visits, session duration, clicks

### c) Functional / Preference Cookies

- Remember your choices and preferences:
    - Language selection
    - Country selection
    - Theme or layout preferences

### d) Marketing / Advertising Cookies

- Used to **personalize ads** or promotions
- Show relevant content based on your browsing behavior

---

## 3. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

- Improve website performance and user experience
- Understand visitor behavior and traffic patterns
- Remember your preferences across visits
- Deliver targeted marketing or advertising content
- Ensure website security and prevent fraud
