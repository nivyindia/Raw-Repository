# Monthly Reports

> Standards, templates, and delivery guidelines for monthly client reporting.
> 

---

## What Every Monthly Report Pack Includes

### 1. Cover Page

- Client name and reporting period
- Prepared by: Nivy Tax
- Delivery date
- Account Manager contact details

### 2. Executive Summary (1 Page)

- 3–5 bullet points summarising the month
- Key movements: revenue, expenses, net position
- Any critical items requiring client action
- One forward-looking note

### 3. Profit & Loss Statement

- Current month actuals
- Year-to-date actuals
- Comparison to prior month
- Comparison to budget (Growth and Scale clients)

### 4. Balance Sheet

- As at end of reporting period
- Comparison to prior month

### 5. Cash Flow Statement

- Operating, investing, and financing activities
- Opening and closing cash position

### 6. Budget vs. Actual (Growth and Scale only)

- Variance table with explanations
- Traffic light status (Green / Amber / Red)

### 7. Action Items List

- Any items the client must address
- With due dates and responsible party

---

## Report Delivery Standards

- Delivered by: 10th of the following month
- Format: PDF (primary) + Excel (on request)
- Channel: Secure client portal + email notification
- Language: English (plain, not technical)
