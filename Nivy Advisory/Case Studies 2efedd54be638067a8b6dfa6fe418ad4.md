Include:

- Title: **“Case Studies / Our Work”**
- Subtitle:
    
    *“Real success stories from startups, SMEs and enterprises across India, UAE and global markets.”*
    
- Trust strip:
    - 500+ Projects Delivered
    - 8+ Countries
    - 10+ Years Experience

CTA:

👉 View by Industry | Book Free Consultation

---

### 2. FILTER & SEARCH BAR (VERY IMPORTANT FOR SCALE)

Purpose: Let visitors find relevant stories.

Filters:

- By Country (India | UAE | Global)
- By Service (Tax, Audit, Setup, CFO, Compliance…)
- By Industry (Startup, Manufacturing, IT, Healthcare…)
- By Company Size (Startup | SME | Enterprise)

Search:

- “Search by keyword, service or industry…”

---

### 3. CASE STUDY CARDS GRID (MAIN CONTENT)

Each card should show:

- Client logo or industry icon
- Title:
    
    “Business Setup for FinTech Startup in UAE”
    
- Tags:
    - UAE
    - Startup
    - Company Formation
- 1–2 line result:
    
    “Incorporated in 5 days, saved 30% setup cost”
    

Buttons:

- 👉 View Case Study

---

### 4. FEATURED / HIGHLIGHTED CASE STUDIES (TOP PROJECTS)

⭐ “Featured Success Stories”

## 🔹 2️⃣ INDIVIDUAL CASE STUDY PAGE STRUCTURE (MOST IMPORTANT)

This is where enterprise clients decide to contact you.

Each case study page should follow this professional format:

---

### 1. HERO SECTION (CASE SUMMARY)

Include:

- Case title:
    
    **“GST Litigation Support for Manufacturing Company in India”**
    
- Client type:
    
    “Mid-size Manufacturing Firm | India”
    
- Services provided:
    
    “GST Advisory, Litigation, Compliance”
    

Quick result highlights:

- ✔ 40% penalty reduced
- ✔ Case closed in 3 months
- ✔ ₹2.5 Cr tax exposure saved

---

### 2. CLIENT BACKGROUND

Purpose: Context.

Include:

- Industry
- Company size
- Country / city
- Business problem background

Example:

> “The client is a mid-size manufacturing company operating in Maharashtra with annual turnover of ₹120 Cr…”
> 

---

### 3. BUSINESS CHALLENGES

Purpose: Show problem-solving ability.

Explain:

- Main issues faced
- Legal / tax / compliance risks
- Deadlines or penalties

Bullet points work well.

---

### 4. OUR APPROACH & STRATEGY (CORE SECTION)

Purpose: Show your expertise & methodology.

Include:

- Step-by-step process
- Analysis done
- Strategy designed
- Tools / frameworks used

Example:

- Compliance audit
- Risk assessment
- Representation before authorities
- Documentation & follow-ups

---

### 5. SOLUTION IMPLEMENTED

Purpose: Execution proof.

Explain:

- What exactly you did
- Team involved
- Timeline
- Coordination with client / authorities

---

### 6. RESULTS & BUSINESS IMPACT (MOST POWERFUL PART)

Purpose: Show measurable success.

Include:

- Quantified results:
    - % tax saved
    - Time reduced
    - Penalties avoided
    - Revenue impact

Show in:

- Bullet points
- Highlight boxes
- Before / After comparison

---

### 7. CLIENT TESTIMONIAL (LINKED)

Purpose: Emotional trust.

Include:

- Client quote
- Name / designation (or anonymised)
- Company logo if allowed

---

### 8. SERVICES USED & RELATED LINKS

Purpose: Funnel into services.

Show:

- Services provided (linked to service pages)
- Related industries
- Similar case studies

---

### 9. CONFIDENTIALITY DISCLAIMER (VERY IMPORTANT FOR CA FIRMS)

Add:

> “Client name and certain details have been modified or withheld to maintain confidentiality and comply with professional ethics.”
> 

This is critical for professionalism.

---

### 🔟 FINAL CTA – CONVERT ENTERPRISE CLIENTS

Purpose: Turn proof into enquiry.

Headline:

**“Facing a Similar Challenge?”**

Buttons:

- ✅ Book Free Consultation
- ✅ Speak to a Senior CA
- ✅ Request a Proposal
