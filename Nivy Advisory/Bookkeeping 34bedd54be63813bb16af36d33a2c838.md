---

## What Is Bookkeeping?

Bookkeeping is the process of recording every financial transaction in a business — money in (revenue) and out (expenses). Done correctly, it gives you a clear, real-time picture of your financial health.

---

## What We Include

- Monthly transaction categorization (all bank and card accounts)
- Bank and credit card reconciliation
- Accounts receivable and payable tracking
- Month-end close and financial statement preparation
- QuickBooks / Xero / Zoho Books management
- Catch-up bookkeeping (if months are behind)

---

## Who Needs This?

Any business with income and expenses — especially those preparing for tax season, seeking investment, or owners spending personal time on their own books.

---

## Common Questions

**Q: Do I need to give you access to my bank accounts?**

A: We connect securely via read-only bank feeds. No shared login credentials.

**Q: What if my books are several months behind?**

A: We offer catch-up bookkeeping. We'll get you current before starting monthly service.
