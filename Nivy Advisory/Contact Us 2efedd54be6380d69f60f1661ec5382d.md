# Contact Us

Perfect — the **Contact Us page** is one of the **highest-conversion pages** on your CA / advisory website.

For a **multi-country firm (India + UAE + future countries)** this page must be designed as a **lead engine + local trust builder**.

Below is the **enterprise-grade Contact Us Page Architecture** that you can directly reuse for every country.

---

## 🔹 OVERALL STRATEGY (VERY IMPORTANT)

Best practice:

- One **main Contact page per country**
    - `/in/contact-us`
    - `/ae/contact-us`

Inside each page:

- Country-specific offices
- Local phone numbers
- Local maps
- Country-specific forms

And a separate page for:

👉 **Book Free Consultation** (lead landing page, already planned)

---

## 🔹 1️⃣ HERO SECTION (TOP BANNER)

Purpose: Clear, simple, conversion-focused.

Include:

- Page Title: **“Contact Us”** / **“Get in Touch”**
- Subtitle:
    
    *“Speak with our tax, accounting and business setup experts in India, UAE and global markets.”*
    

Trust strip (very powerful):

- 500+ Clients
- 8+ Countries
- 10+ Years Experience
- 4.9★ Rating

Primary CTA:

👉 Book Free Consultation

Secondary:

👉 Talk to an Expert

---

## 🔹 2️⃣ QUICK CONTACT OPTIONS BAR (VERY IMPORTANT FOR CONVERSIONS)

Purpose: Instant communication.

Show as icon bar or cards:

- 📞 Phone (click to call)
- 💬 WhatsApp
- ✉️ Email
- 📅 Book Meeting

For multi-country:

- Country selector (India | UAE | Global)
- Auto-change numbers based on country

---

## 🔹 3️⃣ CONTACT FORM SECTION (CORE CONVERSION AREA)

Purpose: Capture qualified leads.

Form fields (best practice for CA firms):

Mandatory:

- Full Name
- Company Name
- Email
- Phone / WhatsApp
- Country
- City

Business fields:

- Service Interested In (dropdown)
- Industry (dropdown)
- Message / Requirement

Optional (for premium leads):

- Company Size
- Turnover Range

Features:

- CAPTCHA
- Privacy consent checkbox
- Auto-route to correct country team

CTA button:

👉 Submit Enquiry / Get a Call Back

---

## 🔹 4️⃣ OFFICE LOCATIONS & ADDRESSES (VERY IMPORTANT FOR TRUST + LOCAL SEO)

Purpose: Local credibility.

Structure:

### India Office(s)

- Office name
- Full address
- Phone
- Email
- Business hours
- Google Map embed

### UAE Office(s)

- Dubai / Abu Dhabi / etc.
- Address
- Phone
- Email
- Map

Optional:

- “Virtual Office / Online Support Available”

This section is critical for:

- 🔥 Local SEO (“CA firm near me”)
- 🔥 Trust for international clients

---

## 🔹 5️⃣ DEPARTMENT / SERVICE-WISE CONTACT (PREMIUM FEATURE – OPTIONAL)

Purpose: Faster routing of serious clients.

Sections like:

- Tax Advisory Team – [tax@nivyempires.com](mailto:tax@nivyempires.com)
- Business Setup Team – [setup@nivyempires.com](mailto:setup@nivyempires.com)
- Audit & Compliance – [audit@nivyempires.com](mailto:audit@nivyempires.com)
- Partnerships – [partners@nivyempires.com](mailto:partners@nivyempires.com)

This makes your firm look **large & enterprise-level**.

---

## 🔹 6️⃣ SUPPORT & WORKING HOURS SECTION

Purpose: Set expectations.

Include:

- Business hours (country-wise)
- Time zone
- Emergency / priority support note

Example:

> “Monday – Saturday | 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM (IST / GST)”
> 

---

## 🔹 7️⃣ FAQ SECTION (VERY GOOD FOR REDUCING FRICTION)

Purpose: Answer common doubts.

Examples:

- How soon will you contact me?
- Do you offer free consultation?
- Do you serve international clients?
- Can meetings be done online?

This increases form submission rate.

---

## 🔹 8️⃣ TRUST & COMPLIANCE STRIP (VERY IMPORTANT FOR CA FIRMS)

Purpose: Professional credibility.

Include:

- ICAI registration mention
- Trade license (UAE)
- Data privacy & confidentiality note

Example:

> “All enquiries are handled with strict confidentiality in accordance with professional ethics.”
> 

---

## 🔹 9️⃣ FINAL CTA SECTION – PUSH TO FREE CONSULTATION

Purpose: Strong closing conversion.

Headline:

**“Not Sure Where to Start?”**

Buttons:

- ✅ Book Free Consultation
- ✅ Talk to Our Senior CA
- ✅ Request a Call Back
