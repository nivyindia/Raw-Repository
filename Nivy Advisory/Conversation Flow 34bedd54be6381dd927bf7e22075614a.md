# Conversation Flow

> The complete map of a sales conversation from first contact to closed deal. Follow this structure on every call.
> 

---

## The 6-Stage Conversation Flow

```
Open → Qualify → Discover → Pitch → Handle Objections → Close / Next Step
```

---

### Stage 1: Open (2–3 min)

- Warm greeting, establish rapport
- Confirm available time: *"Do you have about 20 minutes?"*
- Set agenda: *"I'd love to learn about your situation first, then share what we do, and see if there's a fit. Does that work?"*

### Stage 2: Qualify (5–7 min)

- Ask the 7 core qualification questions (see: Qualification Questions)
- Listen more than you talk. Take notes.
- Don't pitch yet.

### Stage 3: Discover (3–5 min)

- Dig deeper into the biggest pain point identified
- *"You mentioned [issue] — can you tell me more about how that's affecting you?"*
- *"What have you tried to fix it?"*
- Let them articulate the problem in their own words.

### Stage 4: Pitch (5–7 min)

- Based on what you've learned, present **one recommended package**
- Use the service explanations (Section 5) as your guide
- Lead with the outcome they said they wanted, not the feature list
- *"Based on what you've told me, I think the [Package Name] is the best fit, because [specific reason]."*

### Stage 5: Handle Objections (3–5 min)

- See: Objection Handling (Section 7)
- Most common: price, timing, "I need to think about it"

### Stage 6: Close / Next Step (2–3 min)

- **If ready to proceed:** "Great — I'll send over the agreement and onboarding form today. Expect it within the hour."
- **If needs more time:** "No problem — I'll follow up [specific day]. Does [time] work?"
- **If not a fit:** "I appreciate your time. I'll keep you in mind if anything changes."
