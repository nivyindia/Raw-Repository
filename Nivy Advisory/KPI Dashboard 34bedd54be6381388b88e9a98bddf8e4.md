# KPI Dashboard

> The key performance indicators we track internally (for operations) and share with clients (for strategic oversight).
> 

---

## Client-Facing KPIs (Shared in Reports)

| KPI | Description | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gross Revenue | Total income before deductions | Monthly |
| Net Profit Margin | Net profit as % of revenue | Monthly |
| Cash Position | Bank balance at month-end | Monthly |
| Accounts Receivable (AR) | Total outstanding client invoices | Monthly |
| Accounts Payable (AP) | Total outstanding bills | Monthly |
| Burn Rate | Cash being spent per month (startups) | Monthly |
| Revenue vs. Budget | Actual vs. planned revenue | Monthly |
| Tax Liability Estimate | Running estimate for current year | Quarterly |

---

## Internal Operations KPIs (Nivy Tax Team)

| KPI | Target | Tracked By |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client Retention Rate | > 90% | Monthly |
| On-Time Report Delivery | 100% by 10th | Monthly |
| Service Accuracy Rate | > 99% | Monthly |
| Average Response Time | < 24 hours | Weekly |
| VA Conversion Rate | > 20% of qualified leads | Weekly |
| Client NPS Score | > 70 | Quarterly |
| Active Clients | (growth target) | Monthly |

---

## How We Track Internally

- CRM: Client pipeline and retention tracking
- Accounting software: Delivery accuracy review
- Notion: Project and task completion rate
- Monthly team review: All KPIs discussed and actioned
