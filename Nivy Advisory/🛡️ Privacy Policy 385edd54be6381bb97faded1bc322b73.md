## 4. How We Use Your Data

- To deliver the specific services scoped in your Client Engagement Letter
- To meet legal and regulatory obligations (tax filing, AML/KYC, record retention)
- To communicate with you about your engagement, invoices, and deadlines
- To send marketing communications, only where you have opted in, and you may unsubscribe at any time
- To improve our website and services through aggregated, anonymized analytics

---

## 5. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with:

- Relevant tax authorities (IRS, HMRC, CRA, ATO, FTA, IRAS) as required to deliver filing services
- Locally licensed partner firms, where services in a jurisdiction are delivered through a partner (disclosed to you at engagement)
- Software/sub-processors supporting service delivery (e.g., QuickBooks, Xero, cloud hosting, e-signature providers)
- Professional advisors (e.g., auditors, legal counsel) where necessary
- Regulators or law enforcement, where legally required

A current list of sub-processors is available on request.

---

## 6. International Data Transfers

Because we operate across the US, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, and Singapore, your data may be processed in a country other than your own. Where data is transferred out of the UK or EU, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

---

## 7. Data Retention

We retain personal and financial data for as long as needed to deliver services and to meet statutory record-keeping requirements, which vary by jurisdiction and service type (e.g., tax records are often required to be retained for several years after filing). After this period, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

---

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data (subject to our legal retention obligations)
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Request a copy of your data in a portable format
- Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact [**contact@thenivy.com**](mailto:contact@thenivy.com). We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (e.g., 30 days under GDPR-style regimes).

---

## 9. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, and a secure client portal for document exchange. While no system is 100% secure, we continuously review our practices to protect your data.
