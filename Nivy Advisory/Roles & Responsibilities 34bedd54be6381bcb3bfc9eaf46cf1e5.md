# Roles & Responsibilities

> This page defines every role within the Nivy Tax operational structure.
> 

---

## Core Team Roles

### 👤 Founder / CEO

- Overall strategy and vision
- Key client relationships and high-value deals
- Partner and franchise negotiations
- Final quality sign-off on all deliverables

### 💼 Account Manager / Client Success

- Primary point of contact for assigned clients
- Onboarding coordination
- Monthly reporting delivery
- Renewal and upsell conversations

### 🤖 Virtual Assistant (VA) — Sales

- Outbound and inbound lead handling
- Initial qualification calls
- Package recommendations and proposal sending
- Follow-up cadence management

### 📊 Bookkeeper / Accountant

- Daily/weekly transaction categorization
- Bank reconciliation
- Financial statement preparation
- Payroll processing

### 📈 Tax Specialist

- Tax return preparation and filing
- GST/VAT compliance
- IRS correspondence and audit defense
- Tax planning advisory

### ⚙️ Operations / Admin

- Internal systems and tools management
- Document control and archiving
- Scheduling and coordination support

## Responsibility Matrix (RACI)

| Task | CEO | Account Mgr | VA (Sales) | Bookkeeper | Tax Specialist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New client acquisition | A | R | R | — | — |
| Client onboarding | I | R | C | C | C |
| Bookkeeping delivery | I | A | — | R | — |
| Tax filing | A | I | — | C | R |
| Monthly reporting | I | R | — | C | C |
| Upsell / renewal | A | R | C | — | — |

*R = Responsible | A = Accountable | C = Consulted | I = Informed*
