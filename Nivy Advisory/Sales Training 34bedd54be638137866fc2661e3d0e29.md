# Sales Training

> This page is the complete sales training module for new VAs. Work through this before handling any live leads.
> 

---

## Module 1: Understanding What You're Selling

You're not selling bookkeeping. You're selling:

- **Peace of mind** (no more tax anxiety)
- **Time back** (10+ hours/month reclaimed)
- **Financial clarity** (knowing your numbers)
- **Professional credibility** (investors and lenders trust clean books)

Always sell the outcome, not the feature.

---

## Module 2: The Sales Mindset

- You are a **problem-solver**, not a vendor
- Your job is to find out if Nivy Tax can genuinely help — and if not, say so
- Pushing someone to buy a service they don't need is bad for everyone
- **The best sales call feels like a helpful conversation**, not a pitch

---

## Module 3: The Sales Process (Overview)

1. Open — Build rapport, set agenda
2. Qualify — Understand their situation
3. Discover — Find the real pain
4. Pitch — Match the right solution
5. Handle Objections — Address concerns honestly
6. Close / Next Step — Move forward or schedule follow-up

## Module 5: Handling Pressure

Sales conversations can be uncomfortable. Remember:

- Silence is okay — let the prospect think
- A "no" is better than a bad client
- Rejection is information, not failure
- Every objection is an opportunity to understand better
