# 1. Part 1 — Strategy Brain (Internal)

## 1.1 Positioning Statement

Nivy Advisory is an international CPA, accounting, and bookkeeping firm that gives growth-stage businesses and globally mobile individuals across the US, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, and Singapore a single accountable partner for tax, bookkeeping, and financial advisory — without the cost or fragmentation of hiring locally in every market.

**Tier:** Premium / Specialist

**Personality:** Precise, trustworthy, globally fluent, calm under deadline pressure

## 1.2 Vision & Mission

**Vision:** To be the most trusted cross-border accounting partner for businesses and individuals operating across Tier 1 and Tier 2 markets.

**Mission:** To remove the complexity of multi-jurisdiction tax, bookkeeping, and compliance — delivering CPA-grade accuracy with the responsiveness of an in-house team.

## 1.3 Core Values

1. **Compliance Without Compromise** — every filing meets the standard of the jurisdiction it's filed in, no shortcuts.
2. **One Relationship, Every Market** — clients don't need a different accountant per country.
3. **Radical Clarity** — financials explained in plain language, not jargon.
4. **Security First** — client financial data treated as the most sensitive asset we handle.
5. **Proactive, Not Reactive** — deadlines and liabilities flagged before they become problems.

## 1.4 Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

**Primary ICP — Tier 1 (US / UK / Canada)**

- Businesses: $250K–$10M revenue, 1–50 employees, e-commerce/SaaS/agencies/real estate
- Individuals: expats, remote workers, digital nomads with cross-border tax exposure (FBAR/FATCA, Self-Assessment, T1 non-resident filings)
- Decision maker: Founder, CFO/Controller, or the individual taxpayer themselves
- Pain: juggling local + home-country tax obligations, fragmented bookkeeping, missed deadlines, expensive local CPA fees

**Secondary ICP — Tier 2 (Australia / UAE / Singapore)**

- Businesses setting up or expanding into these hubs (often via free zones in UAE, or for tax-efficiency in Singapore)
- HNW individuals and expats relocating for work
- Pain: unfamiliarity with local entity/tax requirements, need a single firm that understands both home and host country rules

## 1.5 Competitive Differentiation

| Competitor Type | Their Gap | Our Advantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local CPA firms (single-country) | No cross-border view, client must hire separately per country | One firm, every jurisdiction we serve |
| Big 4 / large firms | Expensive, slow, built for enterprise not SMB/individuals | Right-sized pricing, founder/individual-friendly |
| DIY software (TurboTax, Xero alone) | No human judgment on cross-border complexity | Software-fluent humans who catch what tools miss |
| Offshore bookkeeping-only shops | Bookkeeping without tax/advisory layer, no CPA credential | Full-stack: bookkeeping + CPA tax + advisory under one roof |

**Our niche:** The only firm combining CPA-level tax credibility, day-to-day bookkeeping, and advisory — specifically built around the compliance overlap between a client's home country and host country.

## 1.6 Messaging System

**Primary value proposition:**

Nivy Advisory gives international businesses and individuals one accountable accounting partner across every market they operate in — so they stop reconciling between local CPAs and start operating with one clean financial picture.

**Elevator pitch (short):**

Nivy Advisory is a cross-border CPA, bookkeeping, and advisory firm built for businesses and individuals operating across the US, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, and Singapore.

**Elevator pitch (medium):**

We handle tax filing, bookkeeping, and financial advisory for businesses and individuals navigating more than one country's rules. Our clients get one team that understands both sides of the border — instead of hiring a separate accountant in every jurisdiction.

**Messaging pillars:**

1. **Cross-Border Fluency** — "We don't just file taxes in one country. We understand how two countries' rules interact."
2. **One Relationship, Every Market** — "One accountant. Every jurisdiction. Zero handoffs."
3. **CPA-Grade, Founder-Priced** — "Big-4 quality, without the big-4 invoice."
4. **Deadline-Proactive** — "We flag the deadline before it becomes a penalty."
5. **Plain-Language Financials** — "You'll understand your own numbers, not just receive them."

**Brand voice:** Precise, reassuring, direct. Never alarmist about compliance, never vague about deadlines or pricing.

**Never:** Fear-based tax messaging, generic stock-photo "global business" clichés, overpromising on refunds/savings.

## 1.7 Pricing & Packaging Logic

**Models:**

- Retainer (monthly) — bookkeeping, ongoing advisory
- Project-based (fixed fee) — annual tax filing, entity incorporation, catch-up bookkeeping
- Hybrid — retainer for bookkeeping + fixed fee for annual filing (most common for SMBs)

**Packaging tiers:**

| Tier | Scope | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔹 Essentials | Bookkeeping OR tax filing, single country | Solo founders, individual expats |
| 🔷 Growth | Bookkeeping + Tax, single or dual country | Scaling SMBs, dual-jurisdiction individuals |
| 💠 Global Partner | Full-stack (bookkeeping + tax + advisory), multi-country | Multi-entity businesses, HNW individuals |

**Anchor package:** Growth tier — best margin, matches the bulk of the ICP.

## 1.8 Trust & Proof Strategy

- Every team member's CPA/ACCA/CA credential number displayed (non-negotiable for financial services trust)
- Case studies follow: Context → Challenge → Solution → Result → Quote
- Security/compliance badges (SOC 2 / encryption / GDPR) displayed on every form-facing page
- Country-specific compliance checklists as lead magnets

# 5. Part 5 — International Marketing & Compliance Checklist

## 5.1 Regulatory/Compliance Disclaimers Per Jurisdiction

- Financial advice marketing is regulated differently everywhere: FTC + IRS Circular 230 (US), FCA-adjacent guidance (UK), ASIC (Australia), FTA rules (UAE).
- Each country page needs jurisdiction-specific disclaimer language, not one blanket disclaimer.
- Clarify where the firm holds direct CPA/CA/ACCA credentials vs. where it works through locally licensed partner firms (a US CPA is not automatically licensed in UAE or Singapore).

## 5.2 Data Privacy Compliance Per Region

- GDPR (UK), PIPEDA (Canada), Australian Privacy Act, Singapore PDPA, UAE data protection law all differ.
- Forms, cookie banners, and data-retention language need region-aware versions, not one generic privacy policy.

## 5.3 International Technical SEO Setup

- Decide URL structure deliberately: subdirectories (/us, /uae) vs. subdomains vs. ccTLDs — subdirectories usually right for one brand serving multiple countries.
- hreflang not needed between English-speaking Tier 1/2 markets, but consider an Arabic version for UAE depending on target audience.
- LocalBusiness structured data (schema markup) per regional office.

## 5.4 Multi-Currency & Payment Infrastructure

- Currency switcher on Pricing page (USD/GBP/CAD/AUD/AED/SGD).
- Payment processor must support local payment methods/rails per region (UAE clients often expect different rails than US ACH/card).

## 5.5 Local Trust Signals

- Country-specific certifications/memberships: AICPA (US), ICAEW/ACCA (UK), CPA Canada, CPA Australia, UAE/Singapore equivalents or partner-firm credentials.
- Region-specific testimonials and case studies — a UAE prospect trusts a UAE case study more than a US one.

## 5.6 Marketing Channel Compliance By Region

- Cold email rules differ: CAN-SPAM (US, lenient) vs. PECR (UK) / CASL (Canada) / increasingly strict UAE — stricter opt-in requirements for B2B outreach.
- Outreach templates need a compliance note per region before going live.

## 5.7 Time-Zone-Aware Booking & Support

- 12-hour spread between UAE/Singapore and US/Canada — booking widget and "office hours" messaging need to be explicit per region.

## 5.8 Local Partnerships/Referral Network

- Decide hub-and-local-partner model now (similar to Nivy Next's AMER/EMEA/MEASA/APAC hub structure) — affects wording on every country page (direct service vs. via named local partner firm).

## 5.9 Language/Spelling Localization

- US vs. UK/Australian/Singapore spelling ("organize" vs. "organise") and date formats (MM/DD/YYYY vs. DD/MM/YYYY) — small but an instant local-credibility signal.
