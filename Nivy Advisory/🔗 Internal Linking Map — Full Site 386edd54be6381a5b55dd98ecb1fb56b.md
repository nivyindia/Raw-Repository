## Blog Articles — Linking Rules

Every published blog article must:

1. Link to at least **2 commercial pages** (service page or country page relevant to the article topic)
2. Link to at least **1 other blog article** (once 3+ articles exist)
3. Be linked TO from the relevant country or service page (see "Related blog" slots in each page draft)

| Article topic | Must link to |
| --- | --- |
| US-UK tax treaty | US page + UK page + CPA & Tax |
| FBAR filing guide | US page + CPA & Tax |
| Canada-US tax comparison | Canada page + US page + CPA & Tax |
| UAE corporate tax 2023+ | UAE page + Compliance & Entity Setup |
| Singapore holding company | Singapore page + Advisory & CFO |
| MTD for UK businesses | UK page + Bookkeeping & Accounting |
