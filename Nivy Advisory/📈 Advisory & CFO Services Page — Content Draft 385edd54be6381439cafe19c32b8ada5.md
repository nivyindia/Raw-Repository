## The Gap Between Compliance and Strategy

Most accounting firms stop at compliance. They file your taxes, close your books, and send you a report. What they don't do is help you decide whether to incorporate in Singapore or the UAE, whether your current entity structure is creating unnecessary tax drag, or why your margins are compressing as you scale into a new market.

That's the gap CFO-level advisory fills. And for most SMBs, hiring a full-time CFO at £120K–£200K to fill it isn't the right answer — especially not when you're still building.

Nivy Advisory's advisory service gives you CFO-level insight built on top of the bookkeeping and tax data we already manage — not a disconnected consulting engagement that's working from the same numbers you already have.

## How It Works

1. **Financial health assessment** — before any advisory begins, we review your current books, entity structure, and cash position. We identify the three biggest financial risks and opportunities in your current setup. This takes one session and produces a written summary you keep.
2. **Strategic priorities alignment** — we agree on what advisory is actually for in your case: fundraising readiness, expansion modelling, margin improvement, or ongoing CFO-level oversight. Advisory isn't generic; it's scoped around your next 12 months.
3. **Cadence established** — monthly or quarterly, depending on the complexity of your situation. Monthly clients get a written financial narrative + one strategy call. Quarterly clients get a deeper review session each quarter with ongoing async access in between.
4. **Reporting infrastructure built** — we build or improve your KPI dashboard, reporting template, and forecast model in the tools you already use. You own everything we build.
5. **Ongoing: actuals vs. plan** — every period, we update the forecast with actuals, flag variances worth discussing, and update the financial narrative. You always know where you are, not just where you were.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What's the difference between advisory and just getting my accounts done?**

Compliance (tax + bookkeeping) tells you what happened. Advisory tells you what it means, what to do next, and how to make better financial decisions going forward. They're built on the same data — but advisory uses that data to drive decisions, not just report history.

**Do I need to be a Nivy Advisory bookkeeping client to access advisory?**

No — but it helps. When we're already managing your books, our advisory is built on live, accurate data rather than whatever you bring to us. If you have a separate bookkeeper, we'll need a data-sharing arrangement or a books review first.

**How many hours does fractional CFO work actually involve?**

It varies by cadence and complexity. Most Growth-tier clients need 3–5 hours per month of active advisory time. Global Partner clients typically need 8–12 hours. The retainer is scoped to what you need — not charged hourly once we're engaged.

**Can you build our investor data room / financial due diligence package?**

Yes. This is a common engagement ahead of a fundraise or acquisition — we prepare the financial statements, commentary, forecast model, and KPI dashboard in the format your investors or acquirers expect. Scoped as a fixed-fee project.

**What happens if our needs change month to month?**

Advisory retainers are reviewed quarterly. If your situation has significantly changed (new entity, new market, fundraise completed), we re-scope to match.
