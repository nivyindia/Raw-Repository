## The Real Cost of Messy Books

Bad bookkeeping doesn't announce itself as bad bookkeeping. It shows up as:

- A tax filing that takes three times longer than it should (and costs more)
- A funding round delayed because your financials can't be verified quickly
- A cash flow decision made on gut feel because the numbers aren't current
- A missed VAT deadline because no one was watching the reconciliation

Clean, current, correctly categorised books aren't a nice-to-have — they're the foundation every tax filing, every advisory decision, and every funding conversation is built on.

## How It Works

1. **Books health-check** — we review your current state: software, chart of accounts, reconciliation backlog, and any obvious misclassifications. We give you a plain-language summary of what's in good shape and what needs fixing before we start.
2. **Software setup or catch-up** — if you need a software migration, a chart of accounts rebuild, or a catch-up on months or years of unreconciled transactions, we scope and price this separately before ongoing service begins.
3. **Monthly close cadence established** — we agree a fixed schedule: documents due from you by X date, close completed and delivered by Y date. No chasing, no surprises.
4. **Ongoing reconciliation and reporting** — every month, we reconcile every account, categorise every transaction, handle multi-currency revaluation, and deliver a clean close package.
5. **Filing-ready hand-off** — at tax time, your books hand off to our CPA team (or your external accountant) with no rework required. That's the standard we hold every month's close to.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need bookkeeping if I already use Xero or QuickBooks?**

Yes — software categorises transactions based on rules and bank feeds, but it doesn't catch misclassifications, doesn't reconcile foreign currency accounts correctly without review, and doesn't prepare filing-ready financials. Software is the tool; the bookkeeper is the person who makes it accurate.

**What does "multi-currency bookkeeping" actually mean in practice?**

It means every transaction in a foreign currency is correctly recorded at the exchange rate on the transaction date, foreign currency bank accounts are revalued at month-end, and exchange gains/losses are captured on the P&L. Most small-business bookkeepers and software users skip or simplify one or more of these steps — creating discrepancies that surface at tax time.

**Can you take over from my existing bookkeeper?**

Yes. We do a handover review, identify any gaps or misclassifications in the existing records, and give you a clear scope of what needs to be fixed before we enter ongoing service. We handle the transition; your job is just to introduce us.

**What's catch-up bookkeeping and how is it priced?**

Catch-up means bringing your books current from whenever they were last properly reconciled. Pricing depends on how far back, how many transactions, how many accounts, and how many currencies. We scope it as a fixed-fee project before starting, so there are no surprises.

**Will my books be ready for tax filing at year-end?**

That's the standard we hold every monthly close to — not just at year-end. If you're also using our CPA & Tax service, the hand-off is internal and seamless. If you use an external accountant, we deliver a clean working paper file they can file from directly.
