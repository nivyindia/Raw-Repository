# Case Studies

> Client success stories that demonstrate Nivy Tax's expertise and results. Use in sales proposals and on the website.
> 

---

## How to Use Case Studies in Sales

Share a relevant case study **before** asking for a commitment. Match the case study to the prospect's industry and pain point — not just any story. The more similar the situation, the more powerful the proof.

---

## Case Study Template

When adding new case studies, use this structure:

**Client:** [Industry, size, location — anonymised if preferred]

**Challenge:** [What problem were they facing before Nivy Tax?]

**Solution:** [What specific services did we provide?]

**Result:** [Measurable outcome — time saved, tax saved, penalties avoided, etc.]

**Timeline:** [How quickly did results appear?]

## Sample Case Studies

### Case Study 1: E-Commerce Business — Multi-State Sales Tax Compliance

**Client:** US-based e-commerce retailer, $1.8M annual revenue, 10 states

**Challenge:** Receiving state tax notices for missing sales tax registrations. Unclear on nexus obligations.

**Solution:** Sales tax nexus review, retroactive filing for 3 states, ongoing compliance management

**Result:** Penalties avoided, clean compliance record established, estimated $12,000 in penalties prevented

**Timeline:** Full compliance achieved within 60 days

---

### Case Study 2: Startup — Catch-Up Bookkeeping + Tax Ready

**Client:** SaaS startup, 8 months in business, no accounting done since founding

**Challenge:** Books completely unorganised ahead of first fundraising round. Investor due diligence requiring 2 years of clean financials.

**Solution:** Full catch-up bookkeeping, financial statement preparation, tax filing for prior year

**Result:** Clean books delivered in 3 weeks. Fundraising round closed successfully.

**Timeline:** 3 weeks to clean books from zero

---

### Case Study 3: Professional Services Firm — Virtual CFO

**Client:** Consulting firm, $3.5M revenue, India-based with US clients

**Challenge:** No financial visibility, cash flow unpredictable, pricing decisions made by feel not data

**Solution:** Scale Package + Virtual CFO service. Monthly P&L, cash flow forecasting, pricing strategy review

**Result:** Improved gross margin by 8% in 6 months through pricing adjustments guided by financial data

**Timeline:** First strategic recommendations within 45 days of onboarding
