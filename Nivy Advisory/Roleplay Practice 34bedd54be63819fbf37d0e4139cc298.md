# Roleplay Practice

> Simulated client conversations to build VA confidence before going live. Practice each scenario until it feels natural.
> 

---

## How to Use This Section

1. Pair up with a colleague (or use this as a self-study script)
2. One person plays the VA, one plays the prospect
3. Work through each scenario without looking at the "ideal response"
4. Review and compare
5. Repeat until the response feels natural, not scripted

---

## Scenario 1: The Price-Sensitive Prospect

**Prospect:** *"How much does your bookkeeping service cost? I'm comparing a few options."*

**Poor response:** *"It's $X per month."* (Leads straight to price comparison with no context.)

**Ideal response:**

> *"Our pricing depends on the size and complexity of your business — typically between $X and $X per month. Before I give you a number, can I ask a couple of quick questions so I can point you to the right option? What does your current monthly transaction volume look like, and are you looking for just bookkeeping, or tax support too?"*
> 

---

## Scenario 2: The DIY Software User

**Prospect:** *"I already use QuickBooks. Why would I pay someone else to do what I'm already doing?"*

**Poor response:** *"Because we're professionals."* (Too vague, sounds defensive.)

**Ideal response:**

> *"That's a great question. QuickBooks is a powerful tool — and it works best when there's someone qualified managing it. Most business owners using it themselves find they're not 100% sure if everything is categorised correctly, and that uncertainty usually shows up during tax season. How confident are you that your books are audit-ready right now?"*
> 

---

## Scenario 3: The "I'll Think About It" Close

**Prospect:** *"This sounds interesting, but I want to think it over."*

**Poor response:** *"Sure, take your time."* (No next step = lost deal.)

**Ideal response:**

> *"Absolutely — I want you to feel fully comfortable. Can I ask: is there a specific part you'd like to think through? Sometimes there's a question I haven't answered. And just so I don't leave you hanging — would it be okay if I followed up on [specific day] to see where you land?"*
> 

---

## Scenario 4: The Already-Have-An-Accountant Prospect

**Prospect:** *"I have an accountant already — they do my taxes at year-end."*

**Ideal response:**

> *"That's great — a lot of our clients have the same setup. The difference is we take care of the bookkeeping all year round, so when tax season comes, everything is already clean and ready. It actually makes the tax process faster and often catches more deductions. Does your current accountant provide monthly bookkeeping, or just the annual filing?"*
> 

---

## Scenario 5: The Fully Engaged Inbound Lead

**Prospect:** *"I've been looking for an accounting firm. We're a US e-commerce business, $2M revenue, selling across 12 states. We need full bookkeeping + tax."*

**Ideal response:**

> *"You've come to exactly the right place. With multi-state sales, sales tax nexus compliance is critical — and most general bookkeepers aren't equipped for that. Our Growth Package with a Tax Support add-on would be the natural fit. Can I walk you through exactly what that would look like for your business?"*
>
