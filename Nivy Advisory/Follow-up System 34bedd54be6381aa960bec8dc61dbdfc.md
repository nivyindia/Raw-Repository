# Follow-up System

> Most deals close after 3–5 follow-ups. This page defines the cadence, templates, and rules for following up with prospects professionally.
> 

---

## The Golden Rule

> **Always schedule the next touch before ending a conversation.**
> 

If a prospect says *"I'll think about it"* — respond: *"No problem at all. When would be a good time to reconnect — would [specific date] work?"* Then block it in your calendar immediately.

---

## Standard Follow-up Cadence

| Touch | Timing | Channel | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1st Follow-up | 24 hours after call | Email | Send summary + proposal |
| 2nd Follow-up | 3 days later | WhatsApp / Email | Check if they have questions |
| 3rd Follow-up | 7 days later | Email | Add value (article, case study) |
| 4th Follow-up | 14 days later | Email / Call | Light check-in |
| 5th Follow-up | 30 days later | Email | Final soft close |
| Nurture | Monthly | Email newsletter | Keep top of mind |

---

## Follow-up Message Templates

### After the Call (24 hrs)

> *"Hi [Name], great speaking with you today. As discussed, I'm attaching a summary of what we'd handle for you and the recommended package. Let me know if you have any questions — happy to jump on a quick call. [Name]"*
> 

### Value-Add (Day 7)

> *"Hi [Name], I came across this [article / tip] on [relevant topic] and thought of you. Hope it's useful. Still happy to answer any questions about the proposal when you're ready."*
> 

### Final Soft Close (Day 30)

> *"Hi [Name], just one last check-in on our proposal — I want to make sure I've given you everything you need to make a decision. If the timing isn't right, no worries at all — just let me know and I'll close the loop on my end."*
>
