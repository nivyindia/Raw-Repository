## 1. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Personal data collected from clients, prospects, website visitors, partners, and employees

- Data stored in electronic systems, cloud services, or physical records

- **India:** Information Technology Act, 2000 & IT Rules

We may collect:

**a) Personal Data:**

- Name, email, phone, address, company information
- Service preferences, industry, business size

**b) Sensitive Data (if provided):**

- Tax identifiers, financial documents, corporate filings

**c) Non-Personal Data:**

- Browser type, IP address, device information
- Website usage patterns

## 3. How We Use Data

We use collected data for:

- Providing professional services (accounting, taxation, advisory)
- Responding to inquiries and service requests
- Compliance with local laws and regulatory requirements
- Billing, invoicing, and service administration
- Improving website and service performance
- Marketing communications (with consent)

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We **do not sell or rent personal data**. Data may be shared:

- With **trusted service providers** to deliver services (e.g., cloud storage, accounting software)
- With **regulatory authorities** as required by law
- In case of **merger, acquisition, or business transfer** (with notice to clients)

---

## 5. Data Retention

- Personal and business data is retained **only as long as necessary** for providing services or complying with legal obligations
- After the retention period, data is **securely deleted, anonymized, or archived** as required

## 6. Data Security

We adopt **industry-standard security measures** including:

- Secure servers and firewalls
- Access control and authentication
- Encryption of sensitive information
- Regular security audits and monitoring

Note: No system is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute protection.
