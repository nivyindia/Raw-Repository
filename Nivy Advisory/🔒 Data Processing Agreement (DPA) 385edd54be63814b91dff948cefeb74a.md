# 🔒 Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Applies to:** Business clients of Nivy Advisory who require a Data Processing Agreement for their own regulatory/compliance purposes (commonly requested by UK and EU-based clients under GDPR, and increasingly by Canadian, Australian, and Singaporean clients)

**Last updated:** [Insert date on publish]

> A DPA is a formal contract, typically signed alongside or as an addendum to the Client Engagement Letter — not just a public web page. The draft below is structured so it can be attached to an engagement letter or sent on request. Have this reviewed by a privacy lawyer before use — GDPR-style DPAs have specific mandatory clauses (Article 28 GDPR) that must be precise.
> 

---

## 1. Purpose of This Agreement

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between Nivy Advisory ("Processor") and the Client ("Controller") under the Client Engagement Letter ("Principal Agreement"), and governs Nivy Advisory's processing of personal data on the Client's behalf in connection with the services provided.

---

## 2. Roles of the Parties

- **Client** acts as the Data Controller for personal data relating to its own employees, customers, or other individuals that it shares with Nivy Advisory for the purpose of bookkeeping, tax, or advisory services.
- **Nivy Advisory** acts as the Data Processor, processing that personal data only on the Client's instructions and for the purposes described in the Principal Agreement.

---

## 3. Scope & Nature of Processing

| Detail | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Subject matter | Provision of accounting, bookkeeping, tax, and advisory services |
| Duration | For the term of the Principal Agreement, plus any legally required retention period thereafter |
| Nature of processing | Collection, storage, organization, reconciliation, reporting, and transmission of financial and payroll-related personal data |
| Categories of data subjects | Client's employees, contractors, customers, and/or vendors, as relevant to the engagement |
| Categories of personal data | Names, contact details, payroll/compensation data, banking details, tax identification numbers, and related financial information |

---

## 4. Processor Obligations

Nivy Advisory agrees to:

- Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Client, unless required otherwise by law
- Ensure personnel with access to personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures (encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, regular security review)
- Assist the Client in responding to data subject rights requests (access, correction, deletion) relevant to the data processed under this engagement
- Notify the Client without undue delay upon becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Client's data
- Delete or return all personal data at the end of the engagement, except where retention is required by law (e.g., statutory accounting record retention periods)

---

## 5. Sub-Processors

Nivy Advisory may engage sub-processors (e.g., cloud hosting providers, accounting software platforms such as QuickBooks/Xero) to support service delivery. Nivy Advisory will:

- Maintain a list of current sub-processors, available on request
- Ensure sub-processors are bound by data protection obligations equivalent to those in this DPA
- Notify the Client of any intended changes to sub-processors, giving the Client an opportunity to object on reasonable grounds

---

## 6. International Data Transfers

Given Nivy Advisory's operations span the US, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, and Singapore, personal data may be transferred and processed in jurisdictions other than the Client's own. Where required (e.g., transfers out of the UK/EU), Nivy Advisory will rely on an approved transfer mechanism (such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum) as applicable.

---

## 7. Security Measures

Nivy Advisory maintains administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal data, including but not limited to:

- Encrypted data storage and transmission
- Role-based access controls limiting data access to personnel who require it
- Regular review of access permissions and security practices
- Secure client portal for document exchange in place of unencrypted email where possible

---

## 8. Audit Rights

Upon reasonable written notice, the Client may request information reasonably necessary to demonstrate Nivy Advisory's compliance with this DPA. Nivy Advisory will cooperate with such requests, subject to confidentiality of information relating to other clients.

---

## 9. Liability

Liability under this DPA is subject to the limitation of liability terms set out in the Principal Agreement and Terms of Service, except where applicable data protection law prohibits such limitation.

---

## 10. Term & Termination

This DPA remains in effect for the duration of the Principal Agreement and survives termination to the extent necessary to fulfil data deletion/return and retention obligations.

---

## 11. Signatures

This DPA is incorporated into and signed alongside the Client Engagement Letter, or as a standalone addendum where requested.
