# Pricing Logic

> How our prices are set and what factors influence them. Use this internally and when clients ask why we charge what we do.
> 

---

## How We Price Our Services

Nivy Tax uses **value-based pricing** — not hourly billing. This means the price reflects the outcome we deliver, not the hours we spend.

### Key Pricing Factors

1. **Transaction volume** — Higher volume = more work = higher price
2. **Complexity** — Multi-state, multi-entity, international operations add complexity
3. **Industry** — Some industries (healthcare, e-commerce) require specialised knowledge
4. **Add-ons selected** — Payroll, tax filing, CFO services each carry their own fee
5. **Catch-up required** — Clients with overdue books may have a one-time cleanup fee before monthly service begins

---

## Pricing Philosophy

- We do not race to the bottom on price
- We compete on value, speed, and quality
- Our pricing is transparent — no hidden fees
- Clients on longer commitments (6 or 12 months) may receive preferred rates
