## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function properly, remember preferences, and understand how visitors use the site.

---

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

| Type | Purpose | Consent Required? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strictly Necessary | Site functionality, security, load balancing | No (essential) |
| Performance/Analytics | Understand site usage (e.g., Google Analytics) | Yes, in UK/EU-style regimes |
| Functional | Remember preferences (e.g., currency selection, region) | Yes, where non-essential |
| Marketing/Advertising | Track effectiveness of campaigns, retargeting | Yes |

---

## 3. Managing Your Preferences

On your first visit, you'll be shown a cookie banner allowing you to accept, reject, or customize non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time via the "Cookie Preferences" link in the website footer, or by adjusting your browser settings to block or delete cookies.

Note: blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect site functionality (e.g., booking forms, client portal login).

---

## 4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use, such as analytics providers, booking/scheduling widgets, and live chat tools. These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies governing their use of data.

---

## 5. Region-Specific Notes

- **UK/EU-style regimes:** Non-essential cookies require opt-in consent before being set (PECR/GDPR-style requirement).
- **US:** Certain states (e.g., California) require an opt-out mechanism for data "sale" or sharing for targeted advertising.
- **Australia, Canada, Singapore, UAE:** Best practice consent banner applied consistently across all regions for simplicity and trust, even where strictly mandatory rules differ.

---

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our cookie usage changes. Check back periodically for updates.
