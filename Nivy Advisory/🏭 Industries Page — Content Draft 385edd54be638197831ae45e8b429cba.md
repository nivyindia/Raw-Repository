## Industry 1 — E-commerce

**The challenge:** Multi-currency sales, marketplace payouts, sales tax/VAT nexus across states and countries, inventory accounting.

**How we help:** Multi-currency bookkeeping, sales tax/VAT nexus reviews, marketplace reconciliation (Amazon, Shopify, etc.), cross-border entity structuring as you expand into new storefronts.

## Industry 2 — SaaS

**The challenge:** Deferred revenue recognition, multi-country payroll for distributed teams, R&D tax credit eligibility, investor-ready reporting.

**How we help:** SaaS-specific revenue recognition, cap table-aware financial reporting, cross-border payroll compliance guidance, fundraising-ready financial packages.

## Industry 3 — Agencies

**The challenge:** Project-based revenue, contractor payments across countries, irregular cash flow, client billing in multiple currencies.

**How we help:** Project/client-level profitability tracking, contractor compliance (1099/IR35-aware), cash flow forecasting built for lumpy agency revenue.

## Industry 4 — Real Estate

**The challenge:** Cross-border property ownership, rental income reporting in multiple jurisdictions, entity structuring for asset protection, capital gains complexity on sale.

**How we help:** Multi-jurisdiction rental income filing, entity structuring guidance for property holding, capital gains planning support ahead of disposals.
