At **Nivy Empires**, we understand that plans can change. This Cancellation Policy outlines the process, timelines, and any fees applicable when a client wishes to **cancel booked services, consultations, or engagements**.

---

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to:

- **Consultations and advisory calls**
- **Accounting, taxation, audit, and compliance services**
- **Business setup and registration services**
- **Workshops, training sessions, or webinars**

> Note: The policy applies per booking or service agreement, unless otherwise specified.
> 

---

## 2. Cancellation Process

To cancel a service:

1. Submit a **written cancellation request** via email: [**support@nivyempires.com**](mailto:support@nivyempires.com)
2. Include:
    - Booking or invoice number
    - Service name
    - Reason for cancellation
3. Cancellation will be **acknowledged within 2 business days**

---

## 3. Cancellation Timelines & Fees

| Service Type | Cancellation Timeline | Refund / Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consultation / Advisory Call | 24 hours before scheduled time | Full refund / no fee |
| Accounting / Tax Filing / Compliance | 3 business days before service start | Full refund if service not started; otherwise partial refund per Refund Policy |
| Business Setup / Registration | 5 business days before submission to authorities | Refund minus any government / third-party fees |
| Workshops / Training / Webinars | 48 hours before start | Full refund; cancellations after deadline – no refund |

> Non-refundable items include government fees, bank charges, or third-party costs.
> 

---

## 4. Service Already Initiated

- If work has already commenced (e.g., accounting, tax filings, documentation submission):
    - Cancellation may only qualify for **partial refund or credit**
    - Refunds are determined **per Refund Policy and Service Agreement**

---

## 5. Rescheduling

- Clients may request **rescheduling instead of cancellation** where feasible
- Rescheduling must be done **at least 24–48 hours before the original schedule**
- Subject to **availability and confirmation** by Nivy Empires

---

## 6. Exceptions

- In cases where **Nivy Empires cannot provide the service** (e.g., unexpected delays, authority closures, staff unavailability):
    - Clients will be offered **full refund** or **rescheduling**
