At **Nivy Empires**, we are committed to providing **high-quality accounting, taxation, audit, and advisory services**. This policy outlines the process for **resolving disputes and handling complaints** to ensure transparency and client trust.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All clients, prospects, and business partners
- Disputes related to:
    - Service quality or deliverables
    - Payments, refunds, or invoices
    - Miscommunication or delays
    - Breach of Service Agreement

## 2. Informal Resolution

    **Email:** [support@nivyempires.com](mailto:support@nivyempires.com)
    
    **Phone:** [Insert Number]

---

## 3. Formal Complaint

If informal resolution is unsuccessful, clients may submit a **formal written complaint** including:

- Client Name / Company Name
- Service or invoice reference
- Nature of dispute
- Supporting documents

Submit to: [**complaints@nivyempires.com**](mailto:complaints@nivyempires.com)

---

## 4. Investigation & Resolution

- Nivy Empires will:
    - **Investigate the complaint** within 10–15 business days
    - **Communicate findings** and proposed resolution to the client
    - **Take corrective action** where applicable (refund, service adjustment, or clarification)
- Resolution methods include:
    - Mutual agreement / negotiation
    - Service adjustments or credits
    - Refunds (per Refund & Return Policy)

---

## 5. Escalation / Arbitration

- If the dispute is **not resolved amicably**, it may be escalated to:
    - **Arbitration or mediation** as per local law
    - **Jurisdiction of the client’s country**:
        - India → Indian courts / IT Act, Companies Act
        - UAE → UAE Federal law, DIFC / ADGM regulations
        - Other countries → Local applicable law
- Both parties agree to **good faith efforts** to resolve disputes before litigation.

---

## 6. Limitation

- Nivy Empires is **not liable for disputes arising from inaccurate information** provided by the client.
- Disputes related to **third-party services, government authorities, or external agencies** will be handled separately.
