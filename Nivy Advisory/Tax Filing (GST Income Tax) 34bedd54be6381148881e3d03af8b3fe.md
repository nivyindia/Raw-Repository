# Tax Filing (GST / Income Tax)

> Plain-language explanation of our tax compliance services.
> 

---

## What Is Tax Filing?

Tax filing is preparing and submitting returns to relevant authorities — IRS (USA), HMRC (UK), or Income Tax / GST departments (India). Must be done accurately and on time to avoid penalties.

---

## US Tax Services

- Federal and state income tax returns (individuals, LLCs, S-Corps, C-Corps)
- Estimated quarterly tax payments
- Sales tax / use tax compliance (multi-state)
- IRS correspondence and audit defense
- R&D tax credit analysis

## India Tax Services

- GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, annual GSTR-9)
- Income Tax Returns (ITR filing)
- Advance tax planning and TDS compliance

## International / Cross-Border

- Transfer pricing documentation
- Double taxation treaty (DTT) analysis
- VAT compliance (UK, EU)
