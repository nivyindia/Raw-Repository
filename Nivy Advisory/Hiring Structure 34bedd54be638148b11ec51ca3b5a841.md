# Hiring Structure

> This page outlines how Nivy Tax recruits, screens, and onboards new team members.
> 

---

## Hiring Philosophy

Nivy Tax hires for **attitude, aptitude, and adaptability** — then trains for skill. We prefer team members who take ownership and communicate proactively.

---

## Current Hiring Channels

- LinkedIn Jobs (primary for senior roles)
- Upwork / Fiverr (for freelance / project-based VA roles)
- Referrals from existing team members
- Campus placements for junior accountants (India-based)

---

## Standard Hiring Process

1. **Application Review** — Resume + cover screening (2–3 days)
2. **Initial Screening Call** — 15 min with Operations (culture + availability fit)
3. **Skills Assessment** — Practical task relevant to the role (1–2 hours)
4. **Interview** — 30 min with Account Manager or CEO
5. **Reference Check** — 2 references minimum
6. **Offer & Onboarding** — Contract + system access + training start

---

## Probation Period

- **Duration:** 60 days
- **Review:** Performance and culture fit assessment at Day 30 and Day 60
- Confirmed team members receive access to full training library and performance bonuses
