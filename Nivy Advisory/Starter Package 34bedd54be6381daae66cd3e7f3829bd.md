# Starter Package

> Entry-level bookkeeping service for early-stage businesses and solo operators. Best for businesses with < 100 transactions/month.
> 

---

## What's Included

- Monthly bookkeeping (up to 100 transactions)
- Bank and credit card reconciliation
- Profit & Loss Statement (monthly)
- Balance Sheet (monthly)
- QuickBooks / Xero management
- Email support (48-hour response SLA)
- Annual summary for tax preparation

## Not Included

- Tax filing (available as add-on via Tax Support Package)
- Payroll (available as add-on)
- Advisory / CFO services

---

## Ideal Client Profile

- Solo operators, freelancers, single-member LLCs
- Early-stage startups (< 1 year old)
- Businesses with simple, predictable revenue
- Clients currently doing their own books in spreadsheets

---

## VA Sales Tip

Position the Starter Package as *"a solid foundation"* and always mention the upgrade path. Plant the seed that Growth or Scale will make sense as they expand.
