# Opening Scripts

> These scripts are starting points — adapt them naturally to the conversation. The goal of every opening is to **earn the right to ask questions**, not to pitch immediately.
> 

---

## Script 1: Cold Outreach (LinkedIn / Email)

> *"Hi [Name], I work with [Starter / Growth / Scale] businesses in [industry] who are spending too much time on bookkeeping or worried about their US tax compliance.*
> 

> *I'd love to learn a bit about your setup and see if there's a fit. Would you be open to a 15-minute call this week?"*
> 

**Key principle:** Ask for a call, not a sale.

---

## Script 2: Referral Introduction

> *"Hi [Name], [Referrer] suggested I reach out — they mentioned you might be looking at outsourcing your accounting or bookkeeping.*
> 

> *I'm with Nivy Tax. We handle the full accounting and tax side for growing businesses. Would you have 15 minutes to chat and see if we could help?"*
> 

---

## Script 3: Inbound Lead (They Contacted Us First)

> *"Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out! I'd love to understand your situation a bit better before recommending anything.*
> 

> *Can you tell me a little about your business and what you're currently doing for bookkeeping / accounting?"*
> 

**Key principle:** Transition immediately into qualification questions. Don't pitch yet.

---

## Script 4: Re-engagement (Old Lead / Ghost)

> *"Hi [Name], just checking back in — I know we spoke a while back about your accounting setup. Things change quickly in business, and I wanted to see if now might be a better time to connect.*
> 

> *No pressure at all — just a quick check in."*
>
