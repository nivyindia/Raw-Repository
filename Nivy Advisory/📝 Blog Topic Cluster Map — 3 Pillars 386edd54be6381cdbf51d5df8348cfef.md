# The 3 Pillars

| Pillar | Core Question It Owns | Primary Audience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Cross-Border Tax** | "What do I actually owe, where, and when?" | Individuals & businesses with filing obligations in 2+ countries |
| **2. International Business Setup** | "How do I structure and operate across borders correctly?" | Founders setting up or expanding entities internationally |
| **3. Expat Finance** | "How do I manage my personal financial life across countries?" | Individual expats, digital nomads, HNW individuals |

These map directly to the two ICP segments in Part 1.4 (businesses vs. individuals) plus a split of the individual segment into compliance-focused (Pillar 1) vs. broader financial-life topics (Pillar 3) — which is where most expat search volume actually concentrates (e.g. "should I keep my 401k as a non-resident", not just "do I need to file").

# Internal Linking Rules for This Cluster System

- Every cluster article links back to its pillar page at least once, contextually (not just a footer link)
- Every pillar page links out to all of its current cluster articles, in-context within the relevant section (see outlines above)
- Every cluster article links to at least 1–2 commercial pages (service or country page) — already specified as a rule in the original Phase E1 action items and the global Internal Linking Map
- Pillar pages are the *only* blog content that should ever target a head/competitive keyword directly; cluster articles target long-tail variants and feed authority upward
