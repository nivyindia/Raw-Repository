# 📜 Engagement Letter, Professional Disclaimer & AML/KYC Notice

These three documents are easy to miss on a standard website build but carry real legal weight for an accounting/CPA firm specifically. Drafted here so they exist before launch.

---

# 1. Client Engagement Letter (Template)

> This is the single most important document in the entire system — it is signed per-client, per-engagement, before any billable work begins. It is not a public website page; it lives in your client onboarding flow / portal.
> 

**[Nivy Advisory Letterhead]**

**Re: Engagement for [Service — e.g., "2026 Annual Tax Preparation & Filing"]**

Dear [Client Name],

This letter confirms the terms of our engagement to provide [service description] for [Client/Company Name] ("Client") for the period [start date] to [end date / ongoing].

**1. Scope of Services**

We will perform the following: [itemized list — e.g., preparation and filing of Form 1040, review of supporting documentation provided by Client, communication with the IRS on Client's behalf for matters arising from this filing].

This engagement does **not** include: [explicitly excluded items — e.g., audit representation beyond standard correspondence, tax planning advice not separately scoped, bookkeeping cleanup beyond X hours].

**2. Client Responsibilities**

Client agrees to provide complete, accurate, and timely information and documentation. Nivy Advisory is not responsible for errors, omissions, penalties, or delays resulting from incomplete or inaccurate information provided by Client.

**3. Fees**

[Fixed fee / retainer amount], payable [terms — e.g., 50% deposit on signing, balance on completion]. See attached fee schedule.

**4. Limitation of Liability**

Nivy Advisory's liability for any claim arising from this engagement is limited to the fees paid by Client for the specific service giving rise to the claim. Nivy Advisory is not liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages, or for penalties/interest assessed by a tax authority due to Client-provided information.

**5. Confidentiality & Data Handling**

All Client financial information will be treated as confidential and handled per our Privacy Policy and applicable data protection law in [Client's jurisdiction].

**6. Term & Termination**

Either party may terminate this engagement per the terms of our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

**7. Governing Law**

This engagement is governed by the laws of [jurisdiction — set per client's country/entity].

Please sign and return to confirm your acceptance of these terms.

# 2. Professional Advice Disclaimer

> This is distinct from a generic website "information not advice" disclaimer. It specifically protects against claims that blog content, country pages, or marketing material constituted formal tax/financial advice.
> 

**Disclaimer**

The content on this website, including but not limited to blog posts, country guides, FAQs, and downloadable resources, is provided for **general informational purposes only** and does not constitute formal tax, accounting, legal, or financial advice.

Tax laws and regulations in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, UAE, Singapore, and all other jurisdictions referenced are subject to change and may be interpreted differently depending on individual circumstances. Reliance on general content published here, without a signed engagement for personalized advice, is at your own risk.

Formal advice is only provided under a signed Client Engagement Letter, scoped to your specific facts and circumstances. Nivy Advisory's professionals are licensed/credentialed as indicated on our About page; where we do not hold a direct license in your jurisdiction, services are delivered in partnership with locally licensed professionals, clearly identified at the engagement stage.

No content on this site should be used as a substitute for direct consultation with a qualified professional regarding your specific situation.

# 3. AML / KYC Client Verification Notice

> Increasingly required for accounting/bookkeeping firms across Tier 1 and Tier 2 markets (e.g., UK Money Laundering Regulations apply to accountancy service providers; UAE has AML obligations for designated non-financial businesses and professions, which can include accounting/bookkeeping). This should appear at onboarding, not just in a back-office policy.
> 

**Client Identity Verification Notice**

As a provider of accounting, bookkeeping, and tax services operating across multiple jurisdictions, Nivy Advisory is required — or chooses, as a matter of best practice even where not strictly mandated — to verify the identity of clients before commencing certain engagements, in line with anti-money laundering (AML) and know-your-client (KYC) standards applicable in the relevant jurisdiction.

As part of onboarding, we may request:

- Government-issued photo identification
- Proof of business registration/incorporation (for business clients)
- Source-of-funds information for certain higher-value engagements
- Beneficial ownership information for corporate entities, where applicable

This information is collected solely for compliance purposes, stored securely per our Privacy Policy, and not shared except where legally required (e.g., regulatory reporting obligations).

Nivy Advisory reserves the right to decline or pause an engagement where required identity verification cannot be completed.
