# Scale Package

> Comprehensive accounting and advisory for scaling businesses. Best for businesses with 500+ transactions/month or complex multi-entity/multi-state operations.
> 

---

## What's Included

- Everything in Growth Package, plus:
- Unlimited transactions
- Multi-entity or multi-state accounting
- Advanced financial modelling
- Monthly CFO-level review call
- Cash flow forecasting (rolling 12-month)
- KPI dashboard setup and maintenance
- IRS representation and audit defense
- Investor / board reporting package
- Dedicated senior accountant + account manager
- Same-day priority support

---

## Ideal Client Profile

- Established SMBs (10+ employees)
- Funded startups (Seed to Series B)
- Multi-state or international operations
- Companies preparing for acquisition or major fundraise

---

## VA Sales Tip

The Scale Package is a high-ticket conversation — always lead with the CFO-level value, not the transaction count. Focus on what a senior financial partner means for their growth ambitions.
