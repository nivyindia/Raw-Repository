## Top 5 Reasons (By Client Research)

### 1. 🌍 We Understand Both Worlds

Most local accountants don't understand international tax. Most global firms are too expensive. Nivy Tax fills the gap — global expertise at accessible pricing.

### 2. ⚡ We're Fast and Proactive

Clients consistently cite our **response speed** as a key differentiator. We don't wait for clients to ask — we update them before they wonder.

### 3. 🔒 We Keep Their Data Secure

We operate with encrypted portals, NDA-first policy, and GDPR-aligned data practices. For international clients, this is non-negotiable.

### 4. 👤 One Point of Contact

No ticket numbers, no rotating agents. Every client has a named person who knows their account inside-out.

### 5. 💼 We Think Like Business Partners

We flag opportunities, not just compliance issues. Clients often say we're the only accountant who talks to them about *growing* their business, not just filing their taxes.
