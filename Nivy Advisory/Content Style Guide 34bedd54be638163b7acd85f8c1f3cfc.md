# Content Style Guide

> Formatting and writing standards for all content produced by or on behalf of Nivy Tax.
> 

---

## Writing Principles

1. **Lead with the benefit, not the feature** — "Save 10 hours/month" not "We offer monthly bookkeeping"
2. **Use short sentences** — especially in emails and sales messages
3. **One idea per paragraph** — don't stack multiple points together
4. **Use numbers when possible** — specificity builds credibility
5. **Always have a clear CTA** — every message should have one next step

---

## Formatting Rules

- **Headings:** Title Case for H1, Sentence case for H2 and below
- **Lists:** Use bullets for 3+ items; write inline for 2 items
- **Bold:** Use to highlight key terms, not for decoration
- **Emojis:** Acceptable in WhatsApp / LinkedIn; avoid in formal proposals and reports
- **Currency:** Always specify — USD, INR, GBP (never just a $ sign without context)

---

## Terminology Standards

| Use This | Not This |
| --- | --- |
| Financial statements | Financials (too casual in proposals) |
| Tax compliance | Tax stuff |
| Bookkeeping | Bookkeeping services / Books |
| Virtual CFO | VCFO (spell out first mention) |
| Client | Customer (we use "client" for professional services) |
