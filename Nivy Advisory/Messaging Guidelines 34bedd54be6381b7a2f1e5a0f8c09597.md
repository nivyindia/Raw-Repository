# Messaging Guidelines

> This page defines the key messages, positioning statements, and talking points for all external communications.
> 

---

## Primary Positioning Headline

> **"Global Compliance. Seamless Accounting."**
> 

Alternatives:

- *"Your Numbers, Our Global Expertise."*
- *"Empowering Businesses Beyond Borders."*
- *"Precision Finance. Global Scale."*

---

## Core Messages (Use in Sales & Marketing)

### Message 1: Trust & Accuracy

*"We've handled accounting for businesses across the US, UK, and India — with a 99%+ accuracy record and zero missed deadlines."*

### Message 2: Time Savings

*"Most of our clients reclaim 10–15 hours per month that they were spending on bookkeeping and tax worries."*

### Message 3: Compliance Confidence

*"Never worry about a missed filing or IRS notice again. We track every deadline and flag issues before they become problems."*

### Message 4: Value vs. Cost

*"Our fees are a fraction of what a full-time in-house accountant costs — and you get a full team, not one person."*

## What to Emphasize by Audience

| Audience | Lead Message |
| --- | --- |
| US SMB owner | Time savings + compliance peace of mind |
| Indian company going global | Cross-border expertise + regulatory navigation |
| E-commerce founder | Sales tax automation + multi-platform integration |
| Startup / funded company | Clean books for investors + R&D credits |
