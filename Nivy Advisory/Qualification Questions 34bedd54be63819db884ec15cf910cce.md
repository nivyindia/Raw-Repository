# Qualification Questions

> Ask these questions before pitching anything. The goal is to understand the prospect's situation, needs, and readiness — then recommend the right fit.
> 

---

## The 7 Core Qualification Questions

### 1. Business Overview

> *"Can you tell me a bit about your business — what you do, how long you've been operating, and roughly how many transactions or clients you're handling each month?"*
> 

### 2. Current Accounting Setup

> *"What are you currently doing for bookkeeping and accounting? Is it in-house, a freelancer, or software like QuickBooks?"*
> 

### 3. Pain Point / Trigger

> *"What made you start looking for a new solution? Was there a specific issue — like tax season stress, inaccurate books, or just too much time spent on it?"*
> 

### 4. Compliance Status

> *"Are you currently up to date on your tax filings? Any pending notices or issues with the IRS or state?"*
> 

### 5. Geographic Scope

> *"Are you operating in one state or multiple? Any international operations or revenue streams?"*
> 

### 6. Budget / Expectations

> *"Do you have a rough budget in mind for accounting support? And what would success look like for you in the first 3 months?"*
> 

### 7. Decision Process

> *"If we find a good fit today, what does the decision-making process look like on your end? Is it just you, or are other stakeholders involved?"*
>

## Scoring: Is This a Good Fit?

| Signal | ✅ Good Fit | ⚠️ Caution | ❌ Not a Fit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Business size | $100K–$5M revenue | < $50K | < $20K |
| Pain level | High (stressed, errors, compliance risk) | Medium | Low / no pain |
| Budget | Open to $200+/mo | Wants lowest possible | "I need it free" |
| Timeline | Ready to start soon | 1–3 months | "Just exploring" |
