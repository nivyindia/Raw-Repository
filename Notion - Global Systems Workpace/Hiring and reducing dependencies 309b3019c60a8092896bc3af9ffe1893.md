---

### **1. Past Work & References**

- Check **portfolio or case studies** — look for measurable results.
- Ask for **references** and speak to 2–3 past clients.
- Verify **duration and scale of past projects** — not just claims.

### **2. Trial / Test Projects**

- Assign a **small, time-bound, measurable task** similar to your real work.
- Track **quality, speed, and adherence to instructions**.
- Use **real outcomes**, not just effort, as evaluation.

### **3. Metrics & KPIs**

- Define **specific performance metrics** upfront:
    - For agencies: leads generated, conversions, ROI.
    - For employees: tasks completed, response rate, accuracy.
- Evaluate **against expected benchmarks** during the trial.

### **4. Simulation / Case Study**

- Give them a **mock project or scenario** and see:
    - Problem-solving ability
    - Creativity / innovation
    - Communication & process handling

### **5. Background & Skill Verification**

- Check **certifications, LinkedIn profile, employment history**.
- Ask skill-specific **tests** (e.g., Excel for accounting, ad setup for digital marketing).

### **6. Behavioral & Cultural Fit**

- Observe **communication, responsiveness, and initiative**.
- See if they can **follow instructions** and adapt quickly.

---

### **1. Assign Realistic Mini-Projects**

- Give a **small, time-bound task** similar to what they will do.
- Example: For marketing — create 1 ad campaign or draft 2 email sequences. For accounting — reconcile 1 client sheet or prepare a small report.
- Evaluate **accuracy, speed, and quality**.

### **2. Simulation / Case Study**

- Create a **mock scenario** with realistic data and ask them to solve it.
- Measure **problem-solving, process understanding, and initiative**.

### **3. Skill-Specific Tests**

- Marketing: ad setup, analytics interpretation, copywriting test.
- Accounting: bookkeeping exercise, GST calculation, financial analysis.
- Use **objective scoring** to compare candidates.

### **4. Trial Period / Probation**

- Hire on a **short-term, paid trial (2–4 weeks)** with clear KPIs.
- Track **task completion, quality, response, and initiative**.

### **5. Behavioral & Process Evaluation**

- Observe **communication, learning speed, and adaptability**.
- See if they **follow instructions and handle feedback**.

### **6. Parallel Testing**

- If budget allows, test **2–3 candidates on the same mini-project**.
- Compare results **quantitatively and qualitatively** to pick the best performer.
