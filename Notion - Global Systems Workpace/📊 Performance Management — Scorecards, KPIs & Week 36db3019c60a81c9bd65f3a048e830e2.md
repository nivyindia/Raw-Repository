# 📊 Performance Management — Scorecards, KPIs & Weekly Review System

> **Department Purpose:** Make ecosystem health visible, measurable, and data-driven. Surface underperformers early, celebrate top performers consistently, and give the founder one place to see the entire system's health at a glance.
> 

> **Owner:** Founder (Phase 1) → Performance Lead (Phase 2+)
> 

> **Status:** Phase 1 Active Build
> 

> **Last Updated:** May 2026
> 

---

# 🧭 WHY THIS DEPARTMENT EXISTS

Without a performance management system:

- You discover problems only after they become crises
- Top performers don't get recognized and eventually disengage
- Underperformers linger too long because there's no data to act on
- The founder makes decisions based on gut feel rather than numbers
- You can't improve what you can't measure

With this system:

- Every participant knows exactly where they stand
- Team Leads can run reviews without founder involvement
- Decisions to advance, warn, or exit are based on scores, not feelings
- The founder reviews one dashboard weekly — not 20 individual conversations

# 👤 SCORECARD 1 — LEARNER SCORECARD

## Scoring Categories

| Category | Weight | How Measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consistency | 30% | Daily report submission rate |
| Communication | 25% | Response time + clarity of updates |
| Quality | 25% | Assignment accuracy, formatting, error rate |
| Speed | 10% | Task turnaround time |
| Initiative | 10% | Proactive questions, self-improvement actions |

## Detailed Scoring Guide

**Consistency (30 points)**

- 30 pts: Report submitted every day, zero misses
- 20 pts: Missed 1 day but communicated reason
- 10 pts: Missed 2–3 days
- 0 pts: Missed 4+ days or went silent

**Communication (25 points)**

- 25 pts: Responds within 4 hours, messages are clear, escalates proactively
- 15 pts: Responds within same day, reasonably clear messages
- 5 pts: Slow to respond, vague updates
- 0 pts: Ghosting or passive-aggressive communication

**Quality (25 points)**

- 25 pts: Work is accurate, well-formatted, exceeds the brief
- 15 pts: Mostly correct with minor fixable errors
- 5 pts: Multiple errors, missing fields, below standard format
- 0 pts: Unusable output or not submitted

**Speed (10 points)**

- 10 pts: Completes tasks well before deadline
- 7 pts: Completes on time
- 3 pts: Slightly late but communicates in advance
- 0 pts: Consistently late without communication

**Initiative (10 points)**

- 10 pts: Asks relevant questions, suggests improvements, self-reports progress
- 7 pts: Engages when prompted
- 3 pts: Passive — does only what's assigned, nothing more
- 0 pts: Disengaged, requires chasing

## Score → Action Decisions

| Score | Label | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 85–100 | ⭐ Star Performer | Fast-track to Operator. Highlight in community. |
| 70–84 | ✅ Solid | Continue. Assign slightly harder tasks. |
| 50–69 | 👀 Watch | Private check-in. Identify one specific weak area. Set 1-week improvement target. |
| 30–49 | ⚠️ At Risk | Formal improvement conversation. Clear expectations reset. One more week. |
| <30 | 🔴 Exit | Polite exit conversation. Clean, kind, final. |

# 📅 WEEKLY REVIEW RHYTHM

This is the non-negotiable weekly operating cadence. Every item has an owner. Nothing is skipped.

| Day | Activity | Owner | Time Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Team Leads submit weekend consistency report | Team Leads | 15 min |
| Wednesday | Mid-week outreach numbers check + flag any blockers | Operators | 10 min |
| Friday | Weekly Learner Scorecard update + top performer highlight | Team Lead / Founder | 30 min |
| Friday | Weekly Outreach Metrics Review | Operator Lead | 20 min |
| Sunday | Freelancer weekly update submission | Freelancers | 10 min |
| Sunday (EOD) | Founder reviews all inputs, flags issues for Monday | Founder | 20 min |

**Total founder time per week on performance review:** ~40 minutes (Phase 1). Reduces to ~20 minutes by Phase 3 as Team Leads absorb the Friday review.

# 🚨 PERFORMANCE ALERT TRIGGERS

These are automatic escalation signals. When these conditions occur, an action must happen within 48 hours — no exceptions.

| Alert Condition | Threshold | Required Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Batch-wide report submission rate drops below 50% | Any single week | Group message + individual check-ins. Diagnose cause before next week. |
| No meetings booked for 2 consecutive weeks | Week 2 | Review outreach message quality. Rebuild or retrain on messaging. |
| Freelancer misses weekly report 2 weeks in a row | Week 2 | Direct call with freelancer. Third miss = suspension. |
| CRM has not been updated in 5+ days | Day 5 | Operator Lead audits and updates. Find out why it stopped. |
| Zero new leads added for 3 consecutive days | Day 3 | Reassign lead research task. Check if blocker was communicated. |
| Learner score drops below 30 for 2 consecutive weeks | Week 2 | Exit conversation. Do not extend. |
| Revenue is zero for 6 weeks into Phase 1 | Week 6 | Full system review. Check: outreach volume, freelancer activity, offer clarity. |

# 📈 PHASE PROGRESSION METRICS

These are the gate conditions to move from one phase to the next. Do not advance until these are hit.

## Phase 1 → Phase 2 Gate

- [ ]  At least 60% of learners completed Week 1 tasks
- [ ]  Daily report submission rate reached 70%+
- [ ]  At least 3 learners qualified for Operator track
- [ ]  At least 1–2 freelancers onboarded
- [ ]  Minimum 5 SOPs documented
- [ ]  At least 1 institute outreach conversation initiated

## Phase 2 → Phase 3 Gate

- [ ]  2+ institute partnerships initiated
- [ ]  20–30 learners in Batch 2
- [ ]  3–5 freelancers actively working
- [ ]  At least 1 revenue event (any closed deal)
- [ ]  15+ SOPs documented
- [ ]  Reporting compliance rate at 80%+

## Phase 3 → Phase 4 Gate

- [ ]  3+ simultaneous learner batches running
- [ ]  2+ Team Leads managing daily operations independently
- [ ]  Monthly outreach volume at 1,000+ messages
- [ ]  8–12 freelancers in ecosystem
- [ ]  Monthly revenue between ₹25,000–₹75,000
- [ ]  Founder spending <1 hour/day on operations

# 📁 SOPs — PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT DEPARTMENT

## SOP-PERF-001 — Weekly Scorecard Execution

**Purpose:** Ensure every active learner is scored every Friday without fail.

**Trigger:** Every Friday at 4 PM

**Owner:** Team Lead

**Steps:**

1. Open Learner Scorecard Google Sheet
2. Review each learner's daily report submissions for the week (Consistency score)
3. Review communication log in WhatsApp/Discord (Communication score)
4. Review any assignments submitted this week (Quality score)
5. Calculate total score for each learner
6. Assign label: Star / Solid / Watch / At Risk / Exit
7. Send individual feedback messages to Watch and At Risk learners
8. Post top performer highlight in group channel
9. Share batch summary with Founder via WhatsApp by 6 PM Friday

**Checkpoint:** Every active learner has a score entered. No blank rows.

**Failure signal:** Scorecard not updated for 2+ consecutive weeks.

## SOP-PERF-002 — Monthly KPI Dashboard Update

**Purpose:** Keep the master KPI dashboard current and usable for founder decisions.

**Trigger:** Last Friday of every month

**Owner:** Performance Lead (Founder in Phase 1)

**Steps:**

1. Open Master KPI Dashboard sheet
2. Update all "Current Value" fields with month-end numbers
3. Change status emojis to reflect current state
4. Write a 3-sentence summary at top of dashboard: What improved, what didn't, one decision needed
5. Share with Founder
6. Archive the current month's data in the Monthly Archive tab
7. Reset "Current Value" fields for next month tracking

**Checkpoint:** Dashboard updated by last day of each month.

**Failure signal:** Dashboard values are from 2+ months ago.
