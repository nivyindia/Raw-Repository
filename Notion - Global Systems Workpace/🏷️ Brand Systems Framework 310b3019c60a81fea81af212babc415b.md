# 🧩 WHAT NEVER GOES AT BRAND LEVEL (IMPORTANT)

❌ Core HR rules

❌ Master legal templates

❌ Tool decisions

❌ Org design logic

❌ Culture definitions

❌ Approval authority

❌ KPI definitions

Brands **follow**, they don’t **decide**.

---

# 🧠 SIMPLE 3-QUESTION FILTER (USE ALWAYS)

Before creating ANYTHING, ask:

1️⃣ Will another brand reuse this in future?

→ YES → GLOBAL

2️⃣ Does this protect the entire group?

→ YES → GLOBAL

3️⃣ Is this only about customers, market, delivery of THIS brand?

→ YES → BRAND
