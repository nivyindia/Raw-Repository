---

# 🧭 WHY THIS EXISTS

Without a revenue tracker:

- Freelancers dispute commissions (no paper trail)
- The founder doesn’t know real monthly revenue vs. pipeline
- Payout timing becomes chaotic and trust erodes
- Tax and accounting become a mess at year-end
- You can’t identify which services, freelancers, or channels generate the most revenue

With this tracker:

- Every deal is logged the moment it closes
- Every payout is documented and timestamped
- Founder sees monthly revenue at a glance
- Freelancers trust the system because it’s transparent
- You can make service/channel decisions based on actual data

# 💸 COMMISSION STRUCTURE

## Standard Revenue Share Rates

| Role | Commission Rate | Basis | Payment Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Closer — Freelancer Cat. A | 20–30% | Deal value | Within 5 business days of client payment received |
| Delivery Specialist — Cat. B | Agreed per project | Delivery completion | Within 5 days of project sign-off |
| Mentor / Reviewer — Cat. C | ₹500–₹2,000 per batch | Batch completion | Monthly, last day of month |
| Senior Operator (Phase 3+) | 5–10% | Deals they supported | Monthly, with full reconciliation |

## How Commission Is Calculated

**Example:**

- Deal Value: ₹15,000
- Freelancer Commission Rate: 25%
- Commission Amount: ₹15,000 × 0.25 = **₹3,750**
- Company Retains: ₹11,250

**Multi-person deals (operator + closer):**

- Closer commission: 20%
- Supporting operator credit: 5%
- Company retains: 75%

> All commission splits must be agreed in writing before the deal is worked, not after it closes.

## Payout Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. **Payout only after client payment is confirmed received** — never before
2. **Payout within 5 business days** of client payment received
3. **Always send payout with a message** stating: Deal ID, amount, and period it covers
4. **Freelancer must confirm receipt** — log their confirmation in the Payout Log
5. **Never pay in cash** — always UPI or bank transfer for paper trail
6. **Disputes must be raised within 7 days** of payout — after 7 days, payout is considered accepted

## Payout Message Template

> Send this every time a payout is made.
> 

```
Hi [Name],

Payout for [Deal ID / Client Name]:
Deal Value: ₹[amount]
Your Commission ([X]%): ₹[commission amount]
Payment sent via [UPI/Bank Transfer] on [date].

Please confirm receipt when you see it.
Thank you for closing this one.
```

# 🔍 REVENUE ANALYTICS — QUESTIONS TO ANSWER MONTHLY

At the end of every month, the founder answers these 5 questions using the tracker data:

1. **Which service type generated the most revenue this month?**
    
    → Informs where to focus outreach training
    
2. **Which freelancer closed the most deals?**
    
    → Reward top performers. Diagnose underperformers.
    
3. **What is the average deal size this month?**
    
    → Tracking this shows whether service packaging is improving
    
4. **What is the gap between pipeline value and closed value?**
    
    → A large gap = closing problem. Review freelancer call quality.
    
5. **Is monthly revenue trending up, flat, or down vs. last month?**
    
    → Flat or down = immediate diagnosis needed before spending more on outreach

# 🛡️ REVENUE PROTECTION RULES

| Risk | Prevention |
| --- | --- |
| Freelancer invoices client directly | All invoices go through Founder. Freelancer never invoices client. |
| Client pays freelancer directly | Client payment details go to Founder’s account only. Stated in all proposals. |
| Commission dispute after deal closes | All commission rates agreed in writing (Revenue Share Agreement) before deal is worked. |
| Unpaid retainer clients | Net-30 payment terms max. Invoice day 1 of service. Follow up day 25. |
| Undocumented deals | No deal is considered "closed" until it’s in the Deal Tracker. |
