# 🤝 Freelancer Partnership Department — Full Operating System

> **Department Purpose:** Manage external execution partners who handle revenue-risk tasks — sales closing, service delivery, and quality mentoring — without creating employment obligations.
> 

> **Owner:** Founder (Phase 1) → Senior Operator (Phase 2+)
> 

> **Status:** Phase 1 Active Build
> 

> **Last Updated:** May 2026
> 

---

# 🧭 WHY THIS DEPARTMENT EXISTS

Freelancers are the revenue-execution layer of the ecosystem. They absorb income uncertainty that the company cannot bear as fixed salaries. They already have the mindset, skills, and self-management discipline. This department structures the relationship so both sides benefit without creating employment obligations or legal complexity.

**Without this department:**

- No one closes deals (leads go cold after outreach)
- Delivery is inconsistent and unmanaged
- Revenue share disputes happen with no documentation
- Client relationships are unprotected

**With this department:**

- Closers receive pre-qualified appointments and close them
- Delivery specialists execute work after closing
- All financial terms are documented in writing
- Client communication is protected from freelancer poaching

---

# 🗂️ THREE FREELANCER CATEGORIES

## Category A — Closers

**Role:** Take qualified appointments from Outreach Ops, run sales calls, close clients.

**Compensation:** 15–30% commission on closed deals (negotiated case-by-case based on deal size and complexity)

**Profile fit:**

- Has done sales before (doesn't matter what industry)
- Comfortable with rejection and long sales cycles
- Can communicate professionally by phone or video
- Owns their follow-through — doesn't need babysitting

**What they are NOT responsible for:**

- Lead research (Outreach Ops handles this)
- Service delivery after closing (Delivery Specialists handle this)
- Reporting on leads they didn't contact

---

## Category B — Delivery Specialists

**Role:** Execute client work after a deal is closed.

**Services they may cover (assign by skill):**

- Bookkeeping support and financial record organization
- LinkedIn optimization and outreach execution
- Virtual assistance (admin, scheduling, inbox management)
- Digital marketing execution (content, ads, scheduling)
- Website/app project coordination

**Compensation:** Project-based fee OR hourly share — agreed before project start.

**Profile fit:**

- Has a demonstrable skill in at least one service area
- Can meet deadlines and communicate proactively
- Has had at least one real client or employer (verifiable)

---

## Category C — Mentors / Reviewers

**Role:** Review learner assignments, guide operators, improve workflow quality.

**Compensation:** Per-batch fee (₹500–₹2000 per batch reviewed) OR revenue share on learners they advance.

**Profile fit:**

- Has completed the full ecosystem operator track, OR
- Has external experience in outreach, sales, or the relevant service area
- Willing to teach and give written feedback, not just verbal

# 📋 FREELANCER ONBOARDING PROCESS — 4 STEPS

## Step 1 — Application & Screening

**Form collects:**

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Name + contact | Basic info |
| Portfolio / work samples | Skill verification |
| Niche / specialization | Role assignment |
| Availability (hours/week) | Capacity check |
| Expected commission or fee structure | Expectation alignment |
| "Describe your last successful deal or project" | Experience verification |
| "How do you handle a prospect who ghosts you?" | Mindset check |

**Auto-reject signals:**

- Expects fixed salary
- Cannot describe any past deal or project
- Refuses to use shared CRM
- Unrealistic revenue expectations in first month

---

## Step 2 — System Training (1 Session, 45 Minutes)

**Agenda:**

| Time | Topic |
| --- | --- |
| 0–10 min | How the ecosystem works (pipeline flow overview) |
| 10–20 min | CRM usage — how to find, update, and close leads |
| 20–30 min | Reporting standards — what to submit and when |
| 30–40 min | Communication protocol — escalation rules, response times |
| 40–45 min | Revenue share agreement walkthrough |

**Must complete before client access:**

- Signed Revenue Share Agreement
- Submitted onboarding form confirming CRM training
- Added to Freelancer WhatsApp group

---

## Step 3 — Controlled Start (First 2 Weeks)

- Work on 1–2 leads only (supervised)
- All first-contact communication via company channels — no private client contact
- Daily brief update required for first 5 days
- Outreach Lead reviews all outgoing messages before they are sent (Phase 1)
- After 5 clean days: allowed to communicate independently within documented channels

**Goal of controlled start:** Verify they follow protocol, communicate professionally, and use the CRM correctly before giving full lead access.

---

## Step 4 — Full Collaboration

- After 2 clean supervised weeks: full lead access granted
- Weekly performance review (15 minutes)
- Quarterly renegotiation of terms if desired by either party
- Access to advanced batch assignments and higher-value leads based on close rate

**FREELANCER COLLABORATION AGREEMENT**

This agreement is between **[Company / Founder Name]** ("the Company") and **[Freelancer Full Name]** ("the Partner").

**Effective Date:** [Date]

**1. Nature of Relationship**

This is a collaboration agreement, not an employment contract. The Partner is an independent contractor and is not entitled to employee benefits, fixed salary, or guaranteed income.

**2. Scope of Work**

The Partner agrees to: [specify role — e.g., conduct sales calls on pre-qualified leads and close client agreements on behalf of the Company].

**3. Compensation**

The Partner will receive [X%] of the net revenue generated from deals they close. Payment will be made within [7/14] days of client payment confirmed.

**4. Lead Ownership**

All leads provided to the Partner are the property of the Company. The Partner agrees not to contact these leads outside of Company-designated channels during or after the collaboration period.

**5. Confidentiality**

The Partner agrees to keep all client information, lead data, and company processes confidential during and after the collaboration.

**6. Reporting**

The Partner agrees to submit weekly activity reports using the format provided. Failure to submit 2 consecutive weekly reports without prior notice constitutes a breach of this agreement.

**7. Termination**

Either party may end this collaboration with 7 days written notice. Deals already in progress at termination will be closed and commission paid per existing terms.

**Signed:**

Partner: _ Date: ___

Company:  Date: ___

# 🛡️ FREELANCER RISK CONTROL SYSTEMS

| Risk | Prevention System |
| --- | --- |
| Client poaching | All initial contact via company channels. NDAs for senior freelancers. |
| Ghosting | 2-miss rule: 2 missed updates = warning, 3rd = suspension |
| Inconsistency | Weekly scorecard review — consistent underperformance triggers conversation |
| Ego conflicts | Written communication norms in handbook. Founder escalation path documented. |
| Revenue disputes | All deals tracked in shared CRM. Invoices go through founder. |
| Quality complaints | Client feedback collected within 48 hours of delivery completion |

# 📊 FREELANCER PERFORMANCE SCORECARD

> Reviewed every Friday. Takes 5 minutes per freelancer.
> 

| Metric | Target | How Measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leads contacted this week | 20+ | CRM log |
| Meetings conducted | 2+ | CRM + self-report |
| Close rate (monthly) | 10–20% | CRM |
| Report submission rate | 100% | Weekly report tracker |
| Client satisfaction | No complaints in 30 days | Founder/client check |
| CRM accuracy | All touched leads updated | CRM audit |

**Score interpretation:**

- All 6 metrics hit: Star freelancer — offer better leads and higher commission tier
- 4–5 metrics hit: Solid — continue, give light feedback
- 2–3 metrics hit: Watch — private check-in, set 2-week target
- 0–1 metrics hit: Exit conversation

# 📁 FREELANCER HANDBOOK — CONDENSED VERSION

> Share this as a PDF with every new freelancer on Day 1. Keep it under 5 pages.
> 

## Section 1 — Who We Are

We are a business ecosystem that runs outreach operations, trains contributors, and closes clients across multiple service lines. You are a partner in the revenue-execution layer.

## Section 2 — How You Get Leads

Leads come from the Outreach Operations team. They are pre-researched, pre-contacted, and have expressed interest before you receive them. Your job is to close — not to find.

## Section 3 — Your Communication Rules

1. Respond within 4 hours during working hours (9 AM – 7 PM)
2. Submit weekly report every Sunday by 8 PM
3. Update CRM within 2 hours of any client interaction
4. Never contact a lead via personal channels
5. Escalate any client complaint to the Founder within 24 hours

## Section 4 — How You Get Paid

- Commission is calculated on confirmed client payment, not on proposal sent
- Payment is processed within 7–14 days of client payment
- Revenue share % is in your signed agreement
- All disputes resolved via the shared CRM record (source of truth)

## Section 5 — What Gets You Removed

- 2 missed weekly reports without notice
- Contacting leads outside company channels
- Sharing client data externally
- Consistent underperformance (below 2 metrics for 4 consecutive weeks)

# 📁 SOP LIBRARY — FREELANCER DEPARTMENT

## SOP-FREE-001 — Freelancer Application & Screening

**Purpose:** Ensure only serious, capable partners enter the ecosystem.

**Trigger:** Freelancer application form submitted

**Owner:** Founder

**Steps:**

1. Review application form response within 48 hours
2. Flag auto-reject signals (see list above)
3. For strong candidates: schedule 30-minute screening call
4. Screening call agenda: verify experience, confirm expectations, explain system
5. If suitable: send Agreement + Handbook within 24 hours of call
6. Log in Freelancer Tracker: Name / Category / Status / Agreement signed / Start date

**Checkpoint:** No freelancer starts work without signed agreement.

---

## SOP-FREE-002 — Freelancer Weekly Report Review

**Purpose:** Maintain visibility into freelancer activity and catch problems early.

**Trigger:** Every Monday morning

**Owner:** Founder / Outreach Lead

**Steps:**

1. Check report submission from all active freelancers (Sunday deadline)
2. Any missing: send nudge immediately, note in tracker
3. Review numbers: leads contacted, meetings, closes, CRM updates
4. Flag any freelancer below target on 2+ metrics
5. For flagged freelancers: schedule 10-minute check-in before Wednesday
6. Update Freelancer Scorecard

**Failure signal:** Freelancer submits no report for 2 consecutive weeks without notice.

---

## SOP-FREE-003 — Lead Handoff to Freelancer

**Purpose:** Ensure freelancers enter every client interaction fully prepared.

**Trigger:** Outreach Lead marks a lead as "Meeting Booked"

**Owner:** Outreach Lead

**Steps:**

1. Assign the meeting to a specific freelancer based on availability and niche
2. Prepare Lead Brief: name, company, role, service interest, conversation summary
3. Send Lead Brief via WhatsApp or Slack at least 24 hours before meeting
4. Freelancer confirms receipt of brief
5. After meeting: freelancer updates CRM with outcome (Meeting Done / Proposal Sent / Closed / Lost)

**Checkpoint:** Freelancer confirms brief received before meeting date.

**Failure signal:** Freelancer enters meeting without knowing lead context.

---

## SOP-FREE-004 — Freelancer Warning & Exit Protocol

**Purpose:** Handle underperformance professionally without dragging it out.

**Trigger:** Freelancer misses 2 metrics for 2+ consecutive weeks OR breaches agreement

**Owner:** Founder

**Steps:**

1. Send private message: specific issue identified, 2-week improvement window given
2. Document warning in Freelancer Tracker
3. Monitor for 2 weeks
4. If no improvement: exit conversation — clear, professional, final
5. Transition any active leads to another freelancer
6. Send offboarding message: thank them, confirm final commission due

**Checkpoint:** No freelancer exits without at least 1 documented warning (unless agreement breach).
