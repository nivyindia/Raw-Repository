# Collaboration

Below is a **complete outreach system** you can use to partner with **Digital Marketing Institutes**.

The messaging is designed to make it **win–win for institute owners, students, and your company**, while also helping you build a **steady pipeline of trained project workers**.

I structured it into 4 parts:

1️⃣ Institute Outreach Message

2️⃣ Follow-up Messages

3️⃣ Partnership Proposal (for Institute Owners)

4️⃣ Student-Facing Pitch (what institutes will show students)

---

# 1. Institute Outreach Message (First Message)

Send this on **LinkedIn / WhatsApp / Email** to institute owners.

Hello Sir/Ma'am,

I came across your institute and the work you are doing in digital marketing training. I would like to explore a collaboration opportunity that can help increase your students’ practical exposure and placements.

We run a digital services company that works with real clients and projects. We are looking to collaborate with selected institutes where we can:

- Train students on how real client projects work
- Provide live projects during their course
- Give practical exposure beyond classroom learning
- Offer performance-based job opportunities to top students

This can help your students gain real industry experience while studying, and it can also strengthen your institute’s placement support.

If you are open to discussing a mutually beneficial collaboration, I would be happy to share the complete proposal.

Looking forward to connecting.

---

# 3. Institute Collaboration Proposal

You can send this as **PDF or document**.

---

# Industry Project & Placement Collaboration Program

## A Collaboration Between Training Institutes and Industry

### Objective

This collaboration is designed to **bridge the gap between classroom learning and real industry work**.

Students often complete digital marketing courses but still lack **real client experience and confidence to work on projects**.

Our program introduces **live project exposure during the course itself**, helping students build practical skills before completing their training.

---

# How the Collaboration Works

### 1. Training Introduction During the Course

During the students' learning period, we introduce a **special industry project training module**.

Students learn:

- How agencies manage client projects
- Real digital marketing workflows
- Communication with clients
- Campaign execution methods
- Reporting and project delivery

---

### 2. Live Project Opportunities

Selected students get the opportunity to work on:

- Real client projects
- Internal agency projects
- Digital marketing campaigns

This gives them **real portfolio experience**.

---

### 3. Performance-Based Internship & Jobs

Students who perform well can receive:

- Internship opportunities
- Project-based paid work
- Full-time job opportunities

This helps institutes improve **placement success and reputation**.

---

# Benefits for the Institute

✔ Stronger placement results

✔ Students gain real industry exposure

✔ Increased credibility of training program

✔ Additional value-added training module

✔ Higher student satisfaction

---

# Benefits for Students

✔ Real project experience

✔ Portfolio building

✔ Mentorship from industry professionals

✔ Better job readiness

✔ Potential job opportunities

---

# Benefits for Our Company

✔ Build a trained talent pool

✔ Work with students already trained in digital marketing

✔ Scale project execution with trained teams

---

# Collaboration Model

The collaboration can work in a **simple structure**:

1. Institute introduces the program to students
2. Interested students join the industry project training
3. Students work on supervised real projects
4. Top performers receive internship or job opportunities

---

# Why This Program Works

Most digital marketing courses focus heavily on **theory and tools**.

But agencies require **execution skills and real project understanding**.

This program helps students **transition from learning to real work smoothly**.

# 4. Student Pitch (What Institute Will Tell Students)

This part is **very important**, because you already correctly identified:

> Students expect jobs, not extra training.
> 

So the messaging should focus on **projects, experience, and portfolio**, not training.

---

### Student Announcement

Industry Project Experience Program

Our institute has partnered with a digital marketing agency to give students an opportunity to gain real industry experience while they are still learning.

Through this program, interested students can:

- Work on real digital marketing projects
- Understand how agencies manage client campaigns
- Build a real portfolio before completing the course
- Gain practical experience beyond classroom training

Students who perform well may also receive internship or job opportunities with the agency.

This program is optional but highly recommended for students who want real industry exposure and faster career growth.

When approaching institutes, **never say you need workers**.

Instead position it as:

**Industry Project Program**

This makes it attractive.

Actual structure behind the scenes:

Students → Practice → Projects → Your workforce pipeline.
