# 📝 SOP FORMAT STANDARD

Every SOP written in this ecosystem must follow this exact format. No exceptions. Consistent format = anyone can find what they need instantly.

**Every SOP Must Contain:**

1. **Purpose** — One sentence: why does this SOP exist?
2. **Trigger** — What event or condition starts this process?
3. **Owner** — Who is responsible for executing and maintaining this SOP?
4. **Steps** — Numbered, simple, actionable. No paragraph walls.
5. **Checkpoints** — How do you know it was done correctly?
6. **Failure Signals** — What does a broken version of this process look like?
7. **Escalation** — Who to contact if stuck or something goes wrong?

## SOP-001 — Learner Application & Filtering

**Purpose:** Ensure only serious, realistic applicants reach the orientation session.

**Trigger:** Application form submitted by a candidate.

**Owner:** Training Lead

**Steps:**

1. Check Google Forms responses daily (set a fixed time — 9 AM recommended)
2. Review each submission against the Auto-Accept / Hold / Auto-Reject criteria
3. **Auto-Accept:** Send Orientation Invitation within 24 hours
4. **Hold:** Review manually. Decision within 48 hours. Invite or reject.
5. **Auto-Reject:** Send polite rejection message within 24 hours. Never ghost.
6. Log every applicant in the Applicant Tracker sheet (columns: Name / Date / Decision / Reason)
7. Share weekly applicant summary with Founder (total received, accepted, rejected)

**Auto-Reject Triggers:**

- Expects guaranteed placement or salary
- Less than 1 hour/day available
- Question 10 (acknowledgment) not confirmed
- Application clearly rushed (1-word answers throughout)
- Answered the resilience question with any variation of "quit"

**Rejection Message Template:**

> *"Hi [Name], thank you for applying. After reviewing your application, we feel this program may not be the right fit at this stage. Our ecosystem is built for people who can commit consistent time to practical execution work. We'd encourage you to revisit when you're ready for that level of commitment. Wishing you the best."*
> 

**Checkpoints:** No applicant waits more than 48 hours without a response.

**Failure Signal:** Applicants following up because they heard nothing.

**Escalation:** Training Lead → Founder if rejection message is disputed.

## SOP-002 — Orientation Delivery Checklist

**Purpose:** Set correct expectations before any training begins. Kill unrealistic expectations before they create problems.

**Trigger:** New batch has minimum 5 accepted applicants OR scheduled batch start date arrives.

**Owner:** Founder (Phase 1) → Training Lead (Phase 2+)

**Steps:**

1. Schedule orientation session (live Zoom/WhatsApp call OR send pre-recorded video link)
2. Send Orientation Deck link to all accepted applicants 24 hours before session
3. Run session following the 25-minute Orientation Agenda:
    - 0–3 min: Welcome + who we are (brief, no hype)
    - 3–8 min: What this ecosystem is
    - 8–13 min: What they will NOT get
    - 13–18 min: What they WILL get
    - 18–22 min: The Probability Math session
    - 22–25 min: Rules, reporting, consequences for ghosting
4. After session: send Acknowledgment Form link immediately
5. Set 48-hour deadline for form submission
6. Only share Module 1 access AFTER acknowledgment form is submitted
7. Log all attendees in Batch Tracker (columns: Name / Orientation Date / Form Submitted Y/N)

**Checkpoints:** 100% of advancing learners must have a submitted Acknowledgment Form on file.

**Failure Signal:** A learner in Module 2 who never submitted the Acknowledgment Form.

**Escalation:** Training Lead → Founder if a learner disputes the orientation content.

## SOP-003 — Daily Reporting Format & Collection

**Purpose:** Maintain visibility into learner activity every day without micromanaging.

**Trigger:** Every working day, 8 PM.

**Owner:** Team Lead

**Daily Report Format (Non-Negotiable):**

```
DAILY REPORT — [Name] — [Date]

1. Tasks completed today:
   - [List each with brief description]

2. Numbers:
   - Leads added to CRM: [#]
   - Messages sent: [#]
   - Replies received: [#]
   - Follow-ups sent: [#]

3. Blockers / challenges:
   - [Anything that slowed you down]

4. Plan for tomorrow:
   - [Top 3 tasks]

5. Questions (if any):
   - [For the Team Lead]
```

**Steps:**

1. At 8 PM: count report submissions vs. active learner count
2. Anyone not submitted: send WhatsApp nudge ("[Name], daily report is due today — please send it in!")
3. Final deadline: 10 PM. Anyone not submitted = mark "Missed" in tracker
4. Next morning: scan all reports for blockers, urgent issues. Flag to Founder if critical.
5. End of week Friday: calculate weekly submission rate per learner (feeds into Scorecard)
6. Any learner with 3 consecutive misses: trigger Warning Protocol (SOP-006)

**Checkpoints:** Batch-wide submission rate should be 70%+ every week.

**Failure Signal:** Submission rate drops below 50% (group clarity or morale issue).

**Escalation:** Team Lead → Founder if submission rate drops below 40%.

## SOP-004 — CRM Entry Standards

**Purpose:** Ensure all lead data is consistently formatted so anyone can pick up where someone else left off.

**Trigger:** Any new lead is identified or any existing lead has a status change.

**Owner:** Outreach Lead

**Standard Lead Sheet Fields (Required for every entry):**

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full Name | Yes | First + Last |
| Company | Yes |  |
| Role / Title | Yes |  |
| LinkedIn URL | Yes | Profile link |
| Email | If found |  |
| Phone | If found | WhatsApp preferred |
| Niche / Industry | Yes | e.g. Digital Marketing, Accounting |
| Lead Source | Yes | LinkedIn / Apollo / Manual / Referral |
| Status | Yes | See Status Codes below |
| Last Contact Date | Yes | Update after every touch |
| Next Follow-up Date | Yes | Set at time of last contact |
| Notes | Yes | Any relevant conversation context |

**Status Codes (use exactly these values):**

- `Not Contacted` — In CRM, no outreach yet
- `Connection Sent` — LinkedIn connection request sent
- `Connected` — Connected, no DM yet
- `DM Sent` — First message sent
- `Replied` — Lead has replied
- `Meeting Booked` — Call/meeting scheduled
- `Meeting Done` — Call completed, proposal pending
- `Proposal Sent` — Formal proposal or pitch sent
- `Closed Won` — Deal signed
- `Closed Lost` — Did not proceed (note reason)
- `Do Not Contact` — Requested removal or hostile

**Steps:**

1. New lead identified: add to CRM within 24 hours of finding
2. After any outreach touch: update Status and Last Contact Date immediately
3. Set Next Follow-up Date at time of every update
4. Weekly Friday: Team Lead audits 10% of entries for completeness
5. Any entry missing required fields: assign back to operator with 24-hour fix deadline

**Checkpoints:** Zero CRM entries with blank required fields after audit.

**Failure Signal:** Leads showing as "Not Contacted" for 7+ days with no reason.

**Escalation:** Outreach Lead → Founder if an operator repeatedly fails CRM standards.

## SOP-005 — Assignment Submission & Review

**Purpose:** Evaluate learner output quality and deliver useful feedback within 48 hours.

**Trigger:** Learner submits an assignment via Google Form.

**Owner:** Team Lead / Senior Operator Reviewer

**Steps:**

1. Learner submits assignment form (fields: Name, Module #, Google Drive link or file, self-assessment notes)
2. Reviewer checks submission within 48 hours of receipt
3. Score using rubric: Task Completion (30%) + Quality (30%) + Deadline (20%) + Communication (20%)
4. Write feedback note with minimum 3 lines: what was done well, what to improve, one specific action
5. Send feedback via WhatsApp or email within 48 hours
6. Log score in Learner Progress Tracker
7. If pass (70%+): unlock next module. Send access.
8. If fail (<70%): send specific revision guidance. Give 48-hour resubmission window.
9. Second fail: escalate to Founder for decision.

**Checkpoints:** No assignment unreviewed for more than 48 hours.

**Failure Signal:** Learner asking "did you get my assignment?" more than once.

**Escalation:** Team Lead → Founder if learner disputes the review.

## SOP-006 — Performance Scorecard Evaluation

**Purpose:** Identify star performers and at-risk learners every single week.

**Trigger:** Every Friday.

**Owner:** Team Lead

**Steps:**

1. Open Learner Scorecard Google Sheet
2. Score each active learner across 5 categories (see Scorecard 1 in Performance Management page)
3. Calculate total score and assign label
4. **Star Performers:** Send personal appreciation message + mention in group channel
5. **Watch / At Risk:** Schedule private check-in for the following Monday
6. **Exit candidates:** Escalate to Founder. Do not exit without Founder sign-off.
7. Share batch summary with Founder by 6 PM Friday (total scores, label distribution, top 3)

**Checkpoints:** Every active learner has a score entry for the current week.

**Failure Signal:** Scorecard not updated for 2+ consecutive weeks (operating blind).

**Escalation:** Founder reviews exit decisions. No learner is removed without Founder awareness.

## SOP-007 — Freelancer Onboarding (4-Step)

**Purpose:** Bring a new freelancer into the ecosystem with clear expectations, systems access, and a supervised start.

**Trigger:** Freelancer application approved and verbal commitment confirmed.

**Owner:** Founder

**Steps:**

**Step 1 — Application & Screening**

1. Send Freelancer Application Form link
2. Review for red flags: expecting fixed salary, refusing CRM use, can't describe their last deal
3. If approved: schedule 45-minute onboarding call

**Step 2 — Onboarding Session (45 minutes)**

1. Explain ecosystem structure + their role in it
2. Walk through CRM system (live demo, 15 minutes)
3. Review Reporting Standards + Communication Protocol
4. Explain Revenue Share Agreement terms
5. Send Freelancer Handbook (PDF) + Revenue Share Agreement
6. Require signed agreement before any lead access

**Step 3 — Controlled Start (First 2 Weeks)**

1. Assign 1–2 leads only (supervised)
2. All client communication must go through shared channels
3. Daily brief update required (not weekly yet)
4. Spot-check communication quality after first 3 interactions

**Step 4 — Full Collaboration**

1. After 2 clean weeks: grant full lead access
2. Move to weekly reporting cadence
3. Weekly performance review starts
4. Quarterly terms renegotiation option opened

**Checkpoints:** Signed agreement on file before any lead access is granted.

**Failure Signal:** Freelancer contacts leads before agreement is signed.

**Escalation:** Founder handles all freelancer disputes directly.

## SOP-008 — Institute Partnership Pitch Process

**Purpose:** Establish a structured, repeatable process for converting institute directors into active partners.

**Trigger:** Target institute identified and director contact found.

**Owner:** Founder

**Steps:**

1. Research institute: student base size, current placement track record, director name/LinkedIn
2. Send initial outreach message (LinkedIn or WhatsApp — keep under 75 words, no pitch yet)
3. On reply: request 15-minute call
4. Prepare for call: review pitch structure below
5. **Run 15-minute pitch:**
    - Their problem: students graduate without practical experience
    - Your solution: live execution tasks, SOPs, outreach training
    - What students get: portfolio, progression, skills
    - What institute gets: placement differentiation, co-branded certificates
    - What you need: batch access + 2 sessions/month
6. If interested: propose a free pilot workshop (1.5 hours)
7. Run pilot: 20-lead challenge in 48 hours. Present top 5 results to director.
8. If pilot is positive: draft MOU (simple 1-pager, not a legal contract)
9. Sign MOU. Set session schedule. Deliver first batch onboarding.

**What to NEVER Promise:**

- Guaranteed job placements
- Salary figures
- Income outcomes for students

**Checkpoints:** No MOU signed without a completed pilot first.

**Failure Signal:** Director makes implicit salary promises to students (issue a written correction immediately).

**Escalation:** Founder handles all institute relationship conflicts directly.

## SOP-009 — Outreach Message Templates & Usage

**Purpose:** Ensure all outreach is personalized, non-spammy, and follows tested formats.

**Trigger:** Any operator or learner begins outreach on any channel.

**Owner:** Outreach Lead

**LinkedIn Connection Request (under 300 characters):**

> *"Hi [Name], I noticed your work in [specific area]. I'm building skills in [relevant service] and would love to connect with professionals like you."*
> 

**First DM After Connection (personalized, under 75 words):**

> *"Hi [Name], thanks for connecting. I've been following your content on [topic] — really liked your post about [specific thing]. I work in [service area] and would love to learn from someone in your space. No agenda — just genuine interest in your work."*
> 

**Follow-Up DM (Day 5–7, if no reply):**

> *"Hi [Name], I know you're busy. Just following up briefly — I help [type of business] with [specific service]. If it's ever relevant, I'd love a quick 15-minute conversation. No pressure either way."*
> 

**Email Subject Lines (Test these in rotation):**

- "Quick question about [their company/content]"
- "Helping [their industry] with [specific problem]"
- "[Mutual connection / thing you noticed] — worth a quick call?"

**Rules for All Outreach:**

1. Never pitch in the first message
2. Every message must reference something specific about the recipient
3. Always include a low-commitment CTA ("happy to chat if relevant", not "book a call now")
4. Follow the 5-Touch Sequence — do not give up before Touch 3
5. Log every message sent in CRM immediately

**Checkpoints:** Reviewer spot-checks 10% of messages weekly for personalization quality.

**Failure Signal:** Identical copy-paste messages being sent to multiple contacts.

## SOP-010 — Revenue Share Calculation & Payout

**Purpose:** Ensure every revenue-linked participant knows exactly how their earnings are calculated and when they're paid.

**Trigger:** Deal closed by a freelancer or operator.

**Owner:** Founder

**Standard Revenue Share Structure:**

| Role | Share | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Closer (Freelancer Cat. A) | 20–30% of deal value | Paid after client payment received |
| Delivery Specialist (Cat. B) | Project fee or hourly rate | Agreed per project upfront |
| Mentor / Reviewer (Cat. C) | Per-batch fee | Paid monthly |
| Senior Operator (future) | 5–10% of deals they supported | Revenue-linked only |

**Payout Process:**

1. Deal closed: Freelancer notifies Founder immediately via WhatsApp
2. Founder verifies deal in shared CRM (checks: client signed, amount confirmed)
3. Founder calculates freelancer share based on agreed %, documents in Revenue Tracker
4. Client invoice sent. Payment expected within agreed terms.
5. Upon payment receipt: Founder initiates freelancer payout within 5 business days
6. Freelancer confirms receipt. Log in Revenue Tracker as "Paid".
7. Any disputes: resolve within 7 days. Unresolved disputes escalate to written record.

**Checkpoints:** Every closed deal logged in Revenue Tracker within 24 hours.

**Failure Signal:** Freelancer asks "when am I getting paid?" more than once for the same deal.
