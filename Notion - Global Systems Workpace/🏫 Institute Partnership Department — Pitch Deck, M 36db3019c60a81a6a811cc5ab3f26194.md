# 🧭 WHY THIS DEPARTMENT EXISTS

Recruiting learners one-by-one from job portals is slow, inconsistent, and requires constant effort. Institutes provide batches of motivated, already-paying students who want practical exposure. A single institute relationship can supply 10–30 learners per cohort with near-zero recruitment cost.

**One good institute partnership = multiple batches with zero ad spend.**

This department is designed to build, maintain, and scale those relationships systematically.

# 📋 PARTNERSHIP FUNNEL — 5 STEPS

## Step 1 — Identify & Qualify

**Qualification Criteria:**

- Active student base (50+ students/year)
- Has a placement or practical training gap (the key pain point)
- Director/owner is accessible and growth-oriented
- Not already locked into a competing program

**Research Method:**

1. Google: "[city] digital marketing institute" / "[city] accounting coaching"
2. LinkedIn: Search for institute directors or founders in your city
3. Instagram/Facebook: Look for institutes that post student success content
4. Word of mouth: Ask existing learners which institutes they came from

## Step 2 — Partnership Pitch

**Core message (memorize this):**

*"We give your students real industry workflow experience — not just certificates."*

**Pitch structure (15 minutes max):**

| Part | Content | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Their problem | Students graduate without practical skills. Placement rates suffer. | 3 min |
| 2. Your solution | Live execution tasks, SOPs, CRM experience, outreach training | 3 min |
| 3. Student outcome | Portfolio, progression path, real workflow exposure | 3 min |
| 4. Institute outcome | Stronger placement positioning, differentiation from competitors | 3 min |
| 5. What you need | Access to batch + 2 sessions/month (no money, no obligation) | 3 min |

**Pitch Deck (5 Slides):**

- Slide 1: Title — "Practical Industry Training for Your Students"
- Slide 2: The Problem — "Most graduates lack real execution experience"
- Slide 3: What We Do — Training, assignments, CRM, live outreach
- Slide 4: What Students Get — Portfolio, skills, progression, certificate
- Slide 5: The Ask — 1 free workshop to start. No obligation.

> ✅ **The pitch should end with an offer to run a FREE 1.5-hour pilot workshop. This removes all risk for the institute.**
> 

---

## Step 3 — Pilot Program

**The pilot exists to prove value before formalizing the relationship.**

**Pilot Structure:**

| Activity | Description | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | Who we are and why we're here | 10 min |
| Industry Reality Session | How outreach-based businesses work | 20 min |
| Probability Math | 1000 messages → 50 replies → 10 meetings → 2 clients | 15 min |
| Live Task Challenge | Collect 20 leads in a given format in 48 hours | 10 min |
| Q&A | Address student questions honestly | 15 min |
| Close | "Top performers will be invited to the full program" | 10 min |

**After the pilot:**

1. Collect pilot participant contact data (with permission)
2. Review the 20-lead tasks submitted within 48 hours
3. Identify top 3–5 performers by quality and submission speed
4. Prepare a short results summary for the institute director:
    - "X students attended, Y submitted the challenge, top Z showed strong potential"
5. Present results to director — this closes the formal partnership conversation

## Step 4 — Formal Partnership (MOU)

> Sign a Memorandum of Understanding — not a contract. Keeps it simple and low-stakes for the institute.
> 

---

**MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING**

Between: **[Your Company Name]** ("the Program") and **[Institute Name]** ("the Institute")

**Date:** [Date]

**1. Purpose**

The Program and the Institute agree to collaborate on providing practical industry training to enrolled students of the Institute at no cost.

**2. What the Program Provides**

- Training sessions (minimum 2/month)
- Practical assignments and SOPs
- Performance tracking and feedback for participating students
- Co-branded completion certificates
- Monthly progress reports

**3. What the Institute Provides**

- Access to a student batch for each cohort (minimum 10 students)
- A communication channel to inform students of sessions
- A space or virtual link for sessions

**4. What Is Explicitly Not Promised**

- No guaranteed job placement
- No guaranteed income for students
- No fixed payment to the Institute

**5. Duration**

This MOU is valid for 6 months, renewable by mutual agreement.

**6. Exit**

Either party may end the collaboration with 14 days notice.

**Signed:**

Program Representative: _ Date: __

Institute Representative:  Date: __

## Step 5 — Ongoing Integration

**Monthly cadence:**

- Run 2 training/workshop sessions with students
- Share a 1-page Monthly Progress Report with director
- Collect the best performers and move them to formal learner track

**Quarterly cadence:**

- Review meeting with director (20 minutes)
- Share batch outcomes: how many advanced, how many got certificates
- Discuss expanding batch size or adding a second cohort

**Annual cadence:**

- MOU renewal conversation
- Share year summary: total students trained, progression outcomes, any notable wins

# ❌ WHAT INSTITUTES SHOULD NEVER BE PROMISED

> Every promise must be in writing or not made at all.
> 

| Never Promise | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Guaranteed placements | Legally risky, practically impossible |
| Guaranteed salaries | You cannot control student performance |
| Income numbers ("students earn ₹X") | Misrepresentation risk |
| "Your students will get jobs" | Vague and unenforceable |
| Priority hiring for their students | Creates expectations you can't meet |

# 📁 SOP LIBRARY — INSTITUTE DEPARTMENT

## SOP-INST-001 — Institute Identification & Outreach

**Purpose:** Build a pipeline of potential institute partners systematically.

**Trigger:** Founder begins Phase 1 institute activities

**Owner:** Founder

**Steps:**

1. Research 10 target institutes using Google, LinkedIn, Instagram
2. Enter each in the Institute Partnership Tracker
3. Find the director's contact (LinkedIn DM or WhatsApp number from website)
4. Send initial outreach message (see template below)
5. Follow up once after 5 days if no response
6. After 2 no-responses: move to "Paused"

**Initial Outreach Message Template:**

> *"Hi [Name], I run a practical industry training program for students in [service areas]. I've been looking at institutes doing real work in [city] and came across [Institute Name]. I'd love to share what we do — I think your students could benefit from our hands-on execution training. Would a 15-minute call this week work? Happy to explain more."*
> 

---

## SOP-INST-002 — Pilot Workshop Delivery

**Purpose:** Deliver a high-quality first impression that converts to a formal partnership.

**Trigger:** Institute agrees to a pilot workshop

**Owner:** Founder

**Steps:**

1. Confirm logistics: date, time, format (in-person or virtual), student count
2. Prepare materials: slide deck, probability math visual, challenge brief
3. Send confirmation to institute director 48 hours before
4. Run session per pilot structure (see Pilot Program section above)
5. Collect student contact data during session
6. Review 48-hour challenge submissions
7. Prepare results summary within 5 days
8. Share results with director + use it to close formal MOU conversation

---

## SOP-INST-003 — Monthly Report & Review

**Purpose:** Maintain active, productive institute relationships.

**Trigger:** 1st of every month

**Owner:** Founder / Training Lead

**Steps:**

1. Pull progress data for each active institute batch (from Batch Tracker)
2. Fill Monthly Institute Report template
3. Send report to director by the 3rd of the month
4. Log send date in Institute Partnership Tracker
5. Every 3 months: schedule a 20-minute review call

**Failure signal:** Director stops responding to monthly reports. Schedule a call immediately.
