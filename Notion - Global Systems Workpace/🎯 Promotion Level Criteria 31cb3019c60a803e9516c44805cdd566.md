## Future Levels that can be included later

## **1. Technical / Expert Track (Individual Contributor Track)**

- Parallel to managerial progression.
- Allows employees/freelancers to **grow in skill and seniority without managing a team**.
- Example from tech companies: Software Engineer → Senior Engineer → Principal Engineer → Staff Engineer → Distinguished Engineer.
- Nivy Equivalent:
    - SEO Specialist → Senior SEO Specialist → Lead SEO → Principal SEO → SEO Architect
    - Can exist for Marketing, Content, Design, VA, Accounts, etc.
- **Benefit:** Retains top performers who don’t want to manage, gives them career progression & pay increase.

---

## **2. Project / Program Management Track**

- Focused on handling **cross-department projects** or high-value campaigns.
- Roles: Project Coordinator → Project Manager → Program Manager → Portfolio Manager
- Key KPI: Deliver multiple projects on time, client satisfaction, efficiency
- Can **span employees, freelancers, and partner collaborations**.

---

## **3. Client / Revenue-Facing Track**

- Some companies have **a separate track for client acquisition and revenue generation**, independent of technical/managerial track.
- Example: Account Executive → Senior Account Exec → Account Manager → Key Account Director → VP Client Success
- Helps to **retain high performers in sales without forcing them into HR/operations management**.

---

## **4. Partner / Ecosystem Engagement Track**

- Already discussed, but international companies often have **multiple levels for partner engagement beyond just 4 levels**:
    - Associate → Active → Senior → Strategic → Alliance → Global Partner → Exclusive Partner
- Key KPI: Number of deals, revenue contributed, co-branding effectiveness

---

## **5. Freelance / External Expert Track (Expanded)**

- Already expanded to 9 levels.
- Could further include:
    - **Revenue Share Partner / Fractional C-Level** – freelancers acting as part-time strategic directors or advisors.
    - **Industry Mentor / Coach Track** – external mentors guiding internal teams or partners.

---

## **6. Governance / Advisory Track**

- Not always formalized in small companies but common in international firms:
    - Internal Advisory Board → Executive Committee → Strategy Council
- Functions: Oversight, decision-making on high-level initiatives, inter-department alignment
- Could be applied in Nivy Empires for: high-value projects, partnerships, or ecosystem growth.

---

## **7. Training / Mentorship Track**

- Separate track for **internal trainers, mentors, and academy leaders**:
    - Junior Trainer → Senior Trainer → Training Manager → Head of Learning
- Can apply to employees, freelancers, and partners (Nivy Academy, Nivy Nexus).
- KPIs: Number of people trained, skill adoption, certification completion

---

### **Key Takeaways**

- Large companies usually **have 3–5 parallel tracks**:
    1. **Management Track** → team and department leadership
    2. **Technical / Specialist Track** → expertise without management
    3. **Revenue / Client Track** → business development and key accounts
    4. **Freelancer / External Expert Track** → project-based leadership
    5. **Partner / Ecosystem Track** → external engagement and growth
- Optional supporting tracks: **Training / Mentorship, Governance / Advisory**

---

💡 **Why this matters for Nivy Empires:**

- Allows **every individual (employee, freelancer, partner)** to see **career growth without forcing them into the wrong role**.
- Helps scale **teams, freelancers, and partners simultaneously**.
- Aligns KPIs across all tracks so **everyone is accountable and motivated**.
