# Partner / Collaborator Levels – Managed Version

# 

| Level | Type of Partner | Engagement / Role | Reward / Incentive | Who Manages | Team / Number of Partners Managed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **P1 – Associate Partner** | Small institutes, clubs, freelancers, new collaborators | Share content/messages, provide students/trainees, small-scale collaboration | Referral fees, token rewards, recognition | **Partner Coordinator / Team Lead** | 10–15 partners per coordinator |
| **P2 – Active Partner** | Medium institutes, colleges, training centers | Regular collaboration, co-host events, provide trainees, interns, part-time workers | Fixed monthly/quarterly incentives, access to training material, joint marketing | **Senior Partner Manager / Manager** | 5–10 partners per manager |
| **P3 – Strategic Partner** | Large institutes, production houses, top agencies | Long-term collaboration, co-branded programs, priority student/work allocation | Revenue share, higher incentives, strategic planning involvement, featured recognition | **Department Head / Director** | 3–5 partners per director |
| **P4 – Key Partner / Alliance** | High-value, national-level institutes, government programs, industry associations | Strategic tie-ups, exclusive collaboration, large-scale projects, content co-creation | Partnership contracts, profit share, co-branding, joint campaigns | **VP / Senior Director / C-Level** | 1–3 key partners per executive |

---

### **Notes / Guidelines**

1. **P1 Partners** → handled by **Team Leads / Coordinators**; quick tasks, easy scaling.
2. **P2 Partners** → handled by **mid-level managers**; need consistent follow-up, events, reporting.
3. **P3 Partners** → handled by **department heads**; strategic planning, high-touch engagement.
4. **P4 Partners** → handled by **VPs or C-level executives**; very high-value, limited number, often long-term contracts.
5. Team size is **approximate**, can be adjusted based on complexity and engagement frequency.
