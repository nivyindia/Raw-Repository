# 🛠️ Systems Build Checklist

## **1️⃣ Strategic & Foundational Systems**

These define **what the company is, why it exists, and how it will succeed**:

1. **Business Plan** – long-term goals, market analysis, revenue projections, exit strategy.
2. **Business Model** – revenue streams, pricing strategy, target customers, value proposition.
3. **Company Vision & Mission** – purpose, long-term goals, guiding principles.
4. **Operating System (Company OS)** – overarching system for managing people, processes, and projects.
5. **Company Policies** – HR, finance, compliance, ethics, communication guidelines.
6. **Organizational Structure** – departments, hierarchy, reporting lines, roles, decision authority.
7. **Brand Strategy** – identity, positioning, messaging, tone, brand guidelines.

---

## **2️⃣ Operational Systems**

These define **how the company works day-to-day**:

1. **SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures)** – for every core task (sales, marketing, product, delivery, support).
2. **Project Management System** – task allocation, timelines, accountability (e.g., Asana, Notion, Jira).
3. **Communication System** – Slack, Teams, or other internal communication with protocols.
4. **CRM System** – client management, pipeline, follow-ups, sales tracking.
5. **Finance & Accounting System** – invoicing, bookkeeping, payroll, budgets.
6. **Compliance & Legal System** – contracts, licenses, ISO, trademarks, labor laws.
7. **Quality Assurance / KPI System** – metrics, dashboards, audits, performance tracking.

---

## **3️⃣ Growth & Marketing Systems**

These drive **client acquisition, retention, and brand growth**:

1. **Inbound Marketing System** – content, lead magnets, email sequences, social media.
2. **Outbound Sales System** – outreach scripts, automation, follow-ups, pipeline tracking.
3. **Customer Support System** – tickets, queries, feedback loops, escalation.
4. **Referral / Partnership Programs** – structured incentives, tracking, and rewards.
5. **Marketing Analytics System** – measure campaigns, conversions, ROIs.

---

## **4️⃣ Human Capital Systems**

These manage **people, performance, and culture**:

1. **Hiring & Onboarding System** – role clarity, documentation, expectations, tests.
2. **Training & Development System** – skill improvement, mentorship, certifications.
3. **Performance Evaluation System** – KPIs, reviews, incentives, promotions.
4. **Backup / Contingency System** – redundancy plans for critical roles.
5. **Culture & Engagement System** – team rituals, feedback, motivation, alignment with vision.

---

## **5️⃣ Technology & Tools Systems**

These enable **automation, efficiency, and scalability**:

1. **IT Infrastructure** – cloud drives, software stack, hardware, security.
2. **Automation Systems** – email, CRM, workflow automations, reporting.
3. **Data & Analytics System** – business intelligence dashboards, metrics, forecasting.
4. **Knowledge Management System** – centralized repository of SOPs, training, documentation.

---

## **6️⃣ Risk & Legal Systems**

These protect the company from failures:

1. **Risk Management System** – operational, financial, market risks.
2. **Legal & Compliance System** – contracts, NDAs, IP protection, licenses.
3. **Insurance & Contingency System** – business insurance, liability coverage.
4. **Crisis Management System** – plan for unexpected events, PR, or operational disruption.

---

## **7️⃣ Innovation & Improvement Systems**

These ensure **continuous growth and adaptation**:

1. **Feedback Loops** – from clients, employees, partners.
2. **R&D / Product Development System** – ideas, testing, launch, iteration.
3. **Continuous Improvement System** – update SOPs, optimize processes, scale successful methods.
4. **Strategic Review System** – quarterly/annual review of strategy, goals, financials

## **1️⃣ Knowledge & Documentation Layer**

- **Playbooks & Manuals:** Beyond SOPs — include department-specific playbooks, escalation manuals, decision-making guides.
- **Historical Records:** Document past experiments, wins, failures, campaigns — these become a reference for new hires and decisions.
- **Templates & Frameworks:** Contracts, proposals, reports, emails, outreach scripts — reduces repeated creation work.

---

## **2️⃣ Financial & Funding Systems**

- **Budgeting & Forecasting:** Monthly, quarterly, and yearly budgets.
- **Cash Flow Management:** Real-time tracking, reserves, emergency funds.
- **Fundraising / Investor Relations System:** If you scale, process to attract investors, pitch decks, term sheets.
- **Profitability Analysis:** Track which products/services, clients, or channels make money.

---

## **3️⃣ Customer Lifecycle Systems**

- **Customer Journey Mapping:** Awareness → Consideration → Purchase → Retention → Advocacy.
- **Customer Success / Retention:** Onboarding, adoption, upselling, cross-selling.
- **Feedback & Reviews:** NPS surveys, testimonials, complaint resolution.

---

## **4️⃣ Culture, Leadership & Decision Systems**

- **Decision Matrix:** Who decides what — avoid bottlenecks.
- **Leadership Pipeline / Succession Plan:** Who can replace key people?
- **Core Values System:** Ensures culture survives as team grows.

---

## **5️⃣ Scalability & Automation Layer**

- **Process Automation:** Workflow automation, recurring tasks, repetitive communications.
- **Tech Stack Optimization:** Ensure all tools integrate and scale.
- **Platform & Infrastructure Readiness:** Servers, backups, cloud solutions for growth.

---

## **6️⃣ Market & Competitive Intelligence**

- **Market Monitoring System:** Competitor tracking, trends, demand changes.
- **SWOT / Gap Analysis Frameworks:** Helps guide product and marketing decisions.

---

## **7️⃣ Innovation & New Initiatives**

- **Idea Management:** Collect, vet, and prioritize new business ideas internally.
- **Experimentation System:** Small pilots for new services, products, or markets.
- **Strategic Pivot Framework:** Quickly decide if a direction is failing and shift.

### **The “Full Company Ecosystem” Checklist**

1. **Foundational & Strategy** – Vision, Mission, Business Plan, Business Model, Org Structure.
2. **Operations & Processes** – SOPs, Project Management, Communication, Finance, QA, Compliance.
3. **Marketing & Growth** – Inbound/Outbound, CRM, Referrals, Analytics.
4. **People & Culture** – Hiring, Training, Performance, Backup Plans, Engagement.
5. **Tech & Automation** – IT Infrastructure, Workflow Automation, Data & Analytics, Knowledge Base.
6. **Risk & Legal** – Risk, Compliance, Insurance, Crisis Management.
7. **Financial Systems** – Budget, Cash Flow, Profitability, Fundraising.
8. **Customer Systems** – Lifecycle, Success, Feedback, Journey Mapping.
9. **Innovation & Improvement** – R&D, Idea Management, Experiments, Strategic Reviews.
10. **Market Intelligence** – Competitors, Trends, SWOT, Pivot Frameworks.
11. **Knowledge Management** – Playbooks, Templates, Historical Records, Manuals.
12. **Scalability & Automation** – Automation, Tech Stack Integration, Infrastructure readiness.
13. **Leadership & Decision Making** – Decision matrix, succession plan, core values system

## **1️⃣ External Ecosystem / Partnerships**

- **Vendor & Supplier Management System** – contracts, reliability metrics, backup vendors.
- **Agency & Freelance Network** – vetted resources for flexible scaling.
- **Strategic Partnerships** – alliances, collaborations, co-marketing deals.
- **Community & Network** – industry networks, mentorship, B2B or client communities.

---

## **2️⃣ Legal & IP Deep Layer**

- **Intellectual Property Protection** – patents, trademarks, copyrights.
- **Regulatory Compliance** – industry-specific laws, international laws if scaling globally.
- **Contract Templates & Legal Workflow** – NDAs, client contracts, employee agreements, dispute management.

---

## **3️⃣ Crisis & Continuity Systems**

- **Business Continuity Plan (BCP)** – in case of disasters, tech failures, sudden departures.
- **Emergency Response Protocols** – tech downtime, PR crises, financial shocks.
- **Cybersecurity & Data Protection** – backup, disaster recovery, GDPR or other data rules.

---

## **4️⃣ Measurement & Feedback Loops**

- **KPIs & OKRs** – company, team, and individual metrics aligned to vision.
- **Dashboards & Reports** – real-time tracking for finance, sales, marketing, operations.
- **Decision-Making Frameworks** – criteria for major strategic moves, pivots, or investments.

---

## **5️⃣ Culture & Knowledge Sustainability**

- **Learning & Development System** – internal training, mentorship, skill growth.
- **Culture Rituals & Values** – onboarding, performance reviews, team rituals, recognition.
- **Knowledge Retention System** – prevents info loss when people leave, e.g., central wiki, SOPs, recordings.

---

## **6️⃣ Innovation & Future-Proofing**

- **R&D / Experimentation Pipeline** – small bets for new products or services.
- **Trend & Market Monitoring** – AI, analytics, market shifts, competitor moves.
- **Scalability Planning** – cloud infrastructure, automation, processes designed to scale without friction.
