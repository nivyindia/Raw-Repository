### **Key Guidelines**

1. **Team Lead (L6)**: Manages **5–10 people**, keeps communication tight.
2. **Manager (L7)**: Can manage **10–15 individual contributors** or **2–3 Team Leads**.
3. **Senior Manager / Department Lead (L8)**: Oversees **multiple managers**, indirectly **20–50 people**.
4. **Director / VP / C-Level**: Manages **department heads or directors**, scale increases at each level.
5. **Mentorship**: All juniors (L1–L5) should have a mentor / senior guiding them.
