## PART ONE: DEFINITIONS & RELATIONSHIP

### 1. Definitions

- **"Client"** means any third-party customer introduced to the Freelance Partner by the Company.
- **"Company Employees"** means individuals employed, contracted, or managed by the Company, including but not limited to operational staff, outreach assistants, and support personnel.
- **"Project"** means a specific engagement with a Client to deliver defined professional services.
- **"Confidential Information"** has the meaning set forth in Clause 21.
- **"Direct Project Expenses"** has the meaning set forth in Clause 10.
- **"Net Project Revenue"** has the meaning set forth in Clause 10.

### 2. Independent Contractor Status

The Freelance Partner is an **independent contractor**, not an employee, partner, or joint venture partner of the Company. The Company does not provide salary, employment benefits, worker's compensation, or fixed compensation. Income is generated solely through the project revenue-sharing model defined herein. The Freelance Partner is solely responsible for their own taxes, insurance, and benefits.

---

## PART TWO: SCOPE OF WORK & COLLABORATION

### 3. Scope of Services & Role

The Freelance Partner will act as an independent execution partner responsible for delivering professional services to Clients introduced by the Company.

The Freelance Partner agrees to:

- Attend client meetings arranged by the Company.
- Understand project requirements and scope.
- Suggest pricing, timelines, and deliverables.
- Execute and deliver the agreed-upon work professionally and on time.

### 4. Company Role & Support

The Company acts as a platform, facilitator, and client acquisition partner. The Company may support project operations through its internal systems and **Company Employees** who may assist with outreach, lead generation, scheduling, and operational coordination.

While Company Employees may assist with minor tasks, **final delivery responsibility for all Project work remains solely with the Freelance Partner.**

### 5. Guidance to Company Employees

The Freelance Partner may provide reasonable guidance to designated Company Employees for tasks such as outreach methods, lead generation, and basic client interaction, provided such tasks are within the scope of the Project. Company Employees remain under the Company's supervision. The Freelance Partner may delegate minor tasks, but final delivery responsibility remains with the Freelance Partner.

## PART THREE: LEAD GENERATION & PROJECT ALLOCATION

### 6. Lead Generation & Project Allocation

The Company will generate leads through its marketing, outreach, referrals, and operational systems. The Company will arrange meetings between the Freelance Partner and potential Clients.

**6.1 Best Efforts to Match Expertise:** The Company and its employees will use their best efforts to find and allocate work that matches the Freelance Partner's scope of expertise.

**6.2 Allocation Discretion:** Lead allocation will be at the Company's discretion and may depend on the Freelance Partner's expertise, availability, project complexity, and delivery capacity.

**6.3 Out-of-Scope Projects:** If a Client's requirement is outside the Freelance Partner's scope of expertise, the Company reserves the right to allocate such leads to the most suitable expert available within the Company ecosystem. The Freelance Partner acknowledges that they will not be entitled to such projects.

**6.4 No Guarantee:** The Company does not guarantee a minimum number of projects, leads, or any specific level of income. Project opportunities depend on market demand, lead generation success, and the Freelance Partner's availability.

### 7. Meeting Arrangement & Communication

The Company will coordinate and arrange client meetings. All client communication should preferably occur through channels approved or coordinated by the Company to ensure a unified approach.

### 8. Pricing, Negotiation & Proposal

The Freelance Partner may participate in scope discussion, pricing recommendations, and timeline planning. However, **final proposal approval and contract issuance remain the sole responsibility of the Company.** All proposals and service agreements will be issued under the **Company's brand and contractual structure.** Client contracts will be signed between the **Company and the Client**, with the Freelance Partner acting as the execution partner.

### 9. Scope Change Approval

If a Client requests work outside the agreed Project scope, the Freelance Partner must inform the Company before accepting or commencing any such additional work.

## PART FOUR: COMPENSATION & EXPENSES

### 10. Direct Project Expenses (Tools & Software)

**10.1 Definition of Direct Project Expenses:** "Direct Project Expenses" means verifiable third-party costs that are incurred specifically and exclusively for a Client's project. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Software subscriptions and licenses
- Stock photos, videos, fonts, and assets
- Domain name registrations and renewals
- Web hosting and server fees
- Paid advertising spend (if applicable)
- Third-party contractor payments (if pre-approved)
- Other project-specific tools or services

**10.2 Tools Suggested by Freelance Partner:** The Freelance Partner shall, during the project scoping stage, provide a written list of all tools, software, subscriptions, and third-party services required to execute the Project. The Freelance Partner confirms that these tools are necessary and appropriate for the work.

**10.3 Company Payment:** The Company shall purchase or subscribe to the suggested tools on behalf of the Project. All such tools shall remain the property of the Company as per Clause 14 (Tools & Software).

**10.4 No Unilateral Tool Selection:** The Freelance Partner shall not purchase or commit the Company to any tool or software expense without prior written approval from the Company. Any unauthorized expenses shall be the sole responsibility of the Freelance Partner.

### 11. Revenue Sharing Model

**11.1 Definition of Net Project Revenue:** "Net Project Revenue" means the total amount received from the Client for a Project, **minus** all Direct Project Expenses paid by the Company for that specific Project.

**11.2 Revenue Split:** For all projects sourced through the Company ecosystem, the revenue split shall be calculated on the **Net Project Revenue** as follows:

- **Freelance Partner Share:** 80% of Net Project Revenue
- **Company Share:** 20% of Net Project Revenue

**11.3 What Company Share Covers:** The Company share compensates the Company for client acquisition, platform operations, project coordination, administrative overhead, employee salaries, technology costs, and general business expenses.

**11.4 No Liability for Employee Compensation:** The Freelance Partner is not responsible for compensating Company Employees involved in lead generation or operations.

**11.5 Calculation Formula:**

```
Net Project Revenue = Total Client Payment - Direct Project Expenses
Freelancer Payment = 80% of Net Project Revenue
Company Payment = 20% of Net Project Revenue + Direct Project Expenses (recovered)
```

**11.6 Example:***Client Payment: ₹1,00,000Tools suggested by Freelancer and paid by Company: ₹15,000Net Project Revenue: ₹85,000Freelancer receives: ₹68,000 (80% of ₹85,000)Company receives: ₹17,000 (20% of ₹85,000) + ₹15,000 (tools recovered) = ₹32,000*

**11.7 Verification:** The Company shall provide receipts or invoices for Direct Project Expenses upon request by the Freelance Partner.

### 12. Payment Handling & Milestones

**12.1 Company Collection:** All client payments will be collected by the Company. Revenue distribution occurs **only after the client payment has been received and cleared by the Company.**

**12.2 Milestone Structure:** Projects may be divided into milestones. The Freelance Partner's share will be released only after the specific milestone is completed and approved, and the corresponding client payment has been received.

**12.3 Payment Timeline:** Once a milestone is completed and confirmed, the Freelance Partner's revenue share for that milestone will be dispatched **within 24 hours** of confirmation, provided the client payment for that milestone has been received.

**12.4 Client Payment Delay:** If a Client delays payment, the Company is not obligated to release the Freelance Partner's revenue share until the payment is received from the Client.

## PART FIVE: WORK VERIFICATION & QUALITY

### 13. Work Verification & Client Acceptance

**13.1 Submission for Review:** Completed work must be submitted to the Company for review and client presentation.

**13.2 Company Review Period:** The Company shall, within **5 business days** of submission, either:
a) Accept the work and forward it to the Client for final approval; OR
b) Provide the Freelance Partner with reasonable, specific comments on what is required for acceptance.

**13.3 Client Approval is Final:** Milestones are considered finally complete only when **the Client has confirmed acceptance** of the deliverables. The Company's internal review is an intermediary step to ensure quality before client presentation, but does not constitute final acceptance.

**13.4 Client Rejection & Revisions:** If the Client is not satisfied with the deliverables and requests revisions:
a) Both Parties agree to cooperate in good faith to address the Client's concerns.
b) The Freelance Partner agrees to complete reasonable revisions promptly to meet the agreed scope.
c) Both the Company and the Freelance Partner share the responsibility to ensure the Client's satisfaction and shall work together to resolve any issues.
d) "Revisions" does not include new work or scope changes, which shall be handled under Clause 9 (Scope Change Approval).

**13.5 Deemed Presentation Ready:** If the Company fails to respond under Clause 13.2 within the 5 business day timeframe, the work shall be deemed ready for client presentation. However, **payment shall only be released upon actual Client acceptance** under Clause 13.3.

### 14. Tools & Software

**14.1 Specification by Freelancer:** The Freelance Partner must clearly specify, at the project scoping stage, all tools and software that will be required to execute the Client's project, as detailed in Clause 10.2.

**14.2 Client Budgeting & Cost Recovery:** The cost of all required tools and software shall be included in the Client's project budget. These costs shall be recovered by the Company from the Client's payment **before** the revenue split is calculated, as detailed in Clause 11.

**14.3 Company Ownership & Control:** All tools, software, licenses, and platform accounts purchased by the Company for Client projects shall:
a) Be purchased by and remain the sole property of the Company;
b) Be registered under the Company's name (Billion Dreams United / Nivy) and email address;
c) Remain under the Company's exclusive administrative control at all times.

**14.4 Security & Continuity Clause:** In the event of any dispute between the Freelance Partner and the Company, or upon termination of this Agreement for any reason, the Company retains full and exclusive authority over all tools, software, and accounts it has purchased. The Company may, at its sole discretion, reassign access to these tools to another Freelance Partner to ensure project continuity and client protection. The Freelance Partner agrees to cooperate in any such transition and shall not take any action to prevent the Company's access to its own accounts.

**14.5 Responsibility for Suggestions:** The Freelance Partner acknowledges that they have recommended the specific tools for the Project. If a tool suggested by the Freelance Partner proves inadequate for the Project requirements, the Freelance Partner shall cooperate in good faith to identify a suitable alternative. Any additional costs for replacement tools shall be discussed and mutually agreed upon, with the understanding that the Client's needs remain the priority.

### 15. Project Execution & Responsibility

Once a Project is accepted, the Freelance Partner agrees to:

- Deliver work professionally and with due diligence.
- Maintain reasonable and professional communication with the Client.
- Meet agreed timelines and quality standards.
- Ensure the quality and completion of services delivered.

### 16. Project Exit / Withdrawal

If the Freelance Partner wishes to withdraw from an ongoing Project, they must:

- Provide at least 15 days' prior written notice to the Company; **OR**
- Refund to the Company their proportional share of any fees paid for unfinished work to facilitate a client refund.

The Freelance Partner agrees to cooperate fully in the smooth transition of the Project to another expert to protect the client relationship.

### 17. Unavailability & Reassignment

If the Freelance Partner becomes unavailable or fails to complete assigned work without valid justification, the Company reserves the right to reassign the remaining tasks to another qualified partner to protect the client relationship and project continuity.

## PART SIX: PROPRIETARY RIGHTS & CONFIDENTIALITY

### 18. Lead & Client Ownership

All leads and Clients introduced through the Company ecosystem remain **exclusive clients of the Company.** The Freelance Partner cannot claim independent ownership of these relationships.

### 19. Non-Circumvention of Clients

The Freelance Partner shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, contract with, or provide services independently to any Client introduced through the Company for a period of **12 months after the termination of this Agreement**, without obtaining prior written consent from the Company.

**For the avoidance of doubt, this clause applies only to Clients introduced by the Company and does not restrict the Freelance Partner from providing services to any other clients or businesses.**

### 20. Non-Solicitation of Personnel

The Freelance Partner shall not, during the term of this Agreement and for **12 months after its termination**, directly or indirectly solicit, recruit, or independently collaborate with any **Company Employee** for purposes outside the scope of this Agreement, without the prior written consent of the Company.

### 21. Confidentiality

The Freelance Partner agrees to hold in strict confidence and not disclose or use any Confidential Information of the Company or its Clients. "Confidential Information" includes, but is not limited to, client information, project details, Company processes, operational systems, financial data, internal communications, outreach methods, scripts, and templates. This obligation survives the termination of this Agreement.

### 22. Intellectual Property

**22.1 Company IP:** All systems, frameworks, templates, scripts, documentation, training materials, and operational processes provided by the Company to the Freelance Partner ("Company IP") shall remain the sole and exclusive property of the Company.

**22.2 Work Product for Clients:** All work product specifically created by the Freelance Partner for and delivered to a Client under a Project ("Work Product") shall be owned by the Client upon full payment by the Client. The Freelance Partner hereby assigns all rights, title, and interest in such Work Product to the Company (for the sole purpose of transferring it to the Client) or directly to the Client as directed by the Company.

**22.3 Portfolio Use:** The Freelance Partner may showcase completed work in their professional portfolio, provided that all Confidential Information is removed and Client permission is respected.

### 23. Data Protection

The Freelance Partner agrees to handle all client data, documents, login credentials, and sensitive information with strict security and confidentiality. Such information may only be used for the purpose of executing assigned Projects and must not be stored, shared, or reused outside the scope of the Project. The Freelance Partner shall comply with all applicable data protection laws.

## PART SEVEN: CONDUCT & COMPLIANCE

### 24. Conflict of Interest

The Freelance Partner must promptly disclose any situation that may create a conflict of interest with the Company or its Clients. The Freelance Partner shall not engage in any activities that directly compete with or negatively impact Projects being executed through the Company without prior written consent.

### 25. Professional Conduct & Reputation

The Freelance Partner agrees to maintain professional communication and ethical conduct when interacting with Clients and on behalf of the Company. Any behavior that damages the Company's reputation or client relationships may result in immediate termination of this partnership.

### 26. International Compliance

The Freelance Partner agrees to comply with all applicable local and international laws and regulations while delivering services, including but not limited to intellectual property laws, data protection requirements, and professional service standards in the relevant jurisdictions.

## PART EIGHT: DISPUTES, LIABILITY & TERMINATION

### 27. Refunds & Disputes

In the event of a client dispute regarding the quality or completion of work:

**27.1 Cooperation:** Both Parties agree to cooperate in good faith to investigate and resolve the issue. Both Parties share the responsibility to ensure Client satisfaction.

**27.2 Service Delivery Failure:** If the dispute arises from a clear **service delivery failure** by the Freelance Partner—defined as failure to deliver the agreed scope, gross negligence, or unprofessional conduct—the Freelance Partner shall be responsible for refunding their proportional revenue share of the disputed amount to the Company.

**27.3 Other Factors:** If the dispute arises from unreasonable client expectations, a change in client requirements, or factors outside the Freelance Partner's reasonable control, the Parties shall discuss a fair resolution, which may include the Company bearing the cost from its share.

**27.4 Client First:** In all cases, the Parties shall prioritize a resolution that is fair to the client and preserves the reputation of both the Company and the Freelance Partner.

### 28. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, or any loss of profits or revenues, arising from this Agreement.

The Company's total liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees **paid or payable** by the Company to the Freelance Partner in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. The Freelance Partner's total liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees **received** by the Freelance Partner from the Company in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

This limitation does not apply to claims arising from fraud, willful misconduct, breach of confidentiality (Clause 21), or breach of intellectual property rights (Clause 22).

### 29. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing **15 to 30 days' written notice** to the other party.

Upon termination, the Freelance Partner agrees to complete all active Projects that are near completion, or cooperate fully to properly transition all ongoing Projects to another expert designated by the Company.

### 30. Force Majeure

Neither party will be liable for delays or failures in performance resulting from causes beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters, war, terrorism, riots, embargoes, acts of civil or military authorities, fire, floods, accidents, strikes, or shortages of transportation, facilities, fuel, energy, labor, or materials.

## PART NINE: GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 31. Dispute Resolution

The Parties agree to first attempt to resolve any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement through informal negotiation and mutual discussion. If the dispute cannot be resolved within 30 days, the Parties agree to submit the dispute to mediation in Lucknow, India before a mutually agreed-upon mediator. If mediation is unsuccessful, the dispute may be resolved through binding arbitration in accordance with the laws of India.

### 32. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the **laws of India.** Any legal proceedings arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in **Lucknow, India.**

### 33. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous understandings, agreements, representations, and warranties, both written and oral, with respect to the subject matter. Any modifications to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

### 34. Principle of Fair Dealing

The Parties acknowledge that this Agreement represents a collaborative partnership. In all matters, both Parties agree to act in good faith and with fair dealing, prioritizing the client's best interests and the long-term success of their relationship. If any situation arises that is not explicitly covered by this Agreement, the Parties shall resolve it through mutual discussion and cooperation, guided by this principle.

### 35. Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
