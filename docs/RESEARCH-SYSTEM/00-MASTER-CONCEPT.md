# Research Repository System — Master Concept

## Purpose

This repository is the central **Research Intake + Curation + Promotion** system for the Nivy ecosystem.

It is not the final home for every researched solution. It is the controlled workspace where raw/unorganized research is captured, preserved, classified, deduplicated, evaluated, curated, validated, and prepared for promotion into the correct destination repository.

## Core Principle

> **REUSE → ADAPT → INTEGRATE → BUILD ONLY WHAT IS MISSING**

## Lifecycle

```text
RAW INPUT
  ↓
INTAKE
  ↓
CLASSIFY
  ↓
DEDUPE / RECONCILE
  ↓
EVALUATE
  ↓
CURATE
  ↓
VALIDATE
  ↓
TRANSFER-READY
  ↓
PROMOTE / ADAPT / INTEGRATE
  ↓
DESTINATION REPOSITORY
```

## Repository Responsibilities

- Accept unorganized research without forcing premature structure.
- Preserve source URL, source repository, original path, date, provenance and context.
- Detect duplicates and existing Nivy implementations.
- Identify reusable solutions, partial solutions and genuine gaps.
- Maintain a registry of research assets and their destination.
- Produce transfer-ready artifacts rather than scattering final material.
- Preserve history; do not silently delete or overwrite source material.

## What Does NOT Belong Here

- Secrets, credentials or private tokens.
- Untracked production configuration.
- Final canonical implementation that belongs exclusively to a destination repository.
- Arbitrary files with no provenance or useful research context.

## Destination Examples

- `nivyindia/Nivy-Next-AIOS`
- Company OS / Nivy Global repositories
- Brand-specific AIOS repositories
- Shared skills, agents, workflows or knowledge repositories

The exact destination is determined by the registry and promotion rules, not by the person dropping the raw research into intake.
