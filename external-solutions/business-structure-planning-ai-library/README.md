# Existing Business Structure, Planning & AI Asset Library

**Purpose:** A reusable research warehouse of existing prompts, AI skills, templates, frameworks, operating-model resources, SOP systems, and business-planning assets that can help design and operate an international company.

**Principle:** `DISCOVER → EVALUATE → REUSE → ADAPT → INTEGRATE → BUILD ONLY WHAT IS MISSING`

This folder is intentionally a **source/reference layer**. Do not treat any listed asset as automatically approved for production. Check license, maintenance, security, compatibility, and business fit before adoption.

## What is collected

- AI agent skills / `SKILL.md` libraries
- Prompt libraries and prompt-engineering resources
- Company strategy and annual-planning templates
- Organization design / operating-model references
- Business-model and GTM frameworks
- OKR and goal-management resources
- SOP/process documentation systems
- Department-level AI workflows for marketing, sales, finance, HR, operations, product and research
- Free/open-source and commercial toolkits
- Primary-source methodology references from major AI and strategy organizations
