# Sales Complete Micro-Workflow Resource Catalog

**Status:** v1.0 — 2026-09-16  
**Purpose:** Map reusable resources to the complete atomic Sales Department checklist in files 89, 92 and 93. This catalog intentionally includes resources from the previous discovery batches plus newly verified resources.

## Reuse rule

`DISCOVER → DEDUPE → VERIFY → LICENSE/COST → TEST → ADAPT → INTEGRATE → PILOT → PRODUCTION`

**Status meanings:**
- **Source-verified:** existence/capability verified from current source material.
- **Candidate:** relevant but requires testing.
- **Reference:** methodology/template rather than directly executable.
- **Commercial:** useful product/service; cost and terms require current verification.
- **Build gap:** capability should be composed from reusable parts unless testing proves a material gap.

## 5. Recommended Nivy composition

```text
SOURCE ACQUISITION
  ↓
IDENTITY / ENRICHMENT / DEDUPE
  ↓
CRM SYSTEM OF RECORD
  ↓
ACCOUNT + CONTACT + BUYING-COMMITTEE INTELLIGENCE
  ↓
PROSPECT RESEARCH
  ↓
OUTREACH / PROMOTION / REACTIVATION
  ↓
SCHEDULING + REMINDERS
  ↓
MEETING PREP
  ↓
DISCOVERY + REQUIREMENT ENGINEERING
  ↓
REQUIREMENT CONFIRMATION
  ↓
SOLUTION / DEMO / BUSINESS CASE
  ↓
PROPOSAL + PPT + RFP/RFQ
  ↓
PRICING / CPQ / DEAL DESK
  ↓
NEGOTIATION
  ↓
CONTRACT / CLOSE
  ↓
SALES → DELIVERY HANDOFF
  ↓
ONBOARDING / VALUE REALIZATION
  ↓
EXPANSION / RENEWAL
  ↓
ADVOCACY / REFERRAL
  ↓
REACTIVATION
```

Every transition should produce **evidence, owner, timestamp, next action, system-of-record update and exception state**.
