# Complete International Sales Department Operating Model

**Status:** v1.0 — 2026-09-16

## Purpose

This document defines the complete Sales Department scope for an international company before resource discovery begins.

The objective is not merely a sales funnel. It covers:

`Strategy → Planning → Organization → Market → Demand → Leads → Qualification → Opportunity → Solution → Proposal → Negotiation → Close → Handoff → Adoption → Expansion → Renewal → Advocacy`

plus the management systems that make the commercial organization predictable:

`KRA → KPI → Goals → Quotas → Capacity → Territories → Activities → Pipeline → Forecast → Revenue → Review → Improvement`

APQC's cross-industry process framework explicitly separates sales strategy, forecasting, budgets, sales goals/measures, lead/opportunity management, customer/account management, key-account planning, and proposal/bid/quote management. This model expands that foundation for an AI-native international company. See APQC sources listed in the research notes.

# 18. Sales maturity levels

## Micro / Solo

Founder-led sales, simple CRM, basic pipeline, manual qualification, basic forecast.

## Small

SDR/AE separation, structured stages, quotas, dashboards, basic sales ops.

## SMB

Sales management, forecasting, territories, enablement, compensation, account planning, automation.

## Mid-market

RevOps, specialized roles, formal sales engineering, CPQ, deal desk, advanced forecasting, structured enablement.

## International

Regional teams, country packs, multi-currency, multi-language, global/local governance, channel ecosystem.

## Enterprise

Complex buying committees, strategic accounts, global territories, sophisticated forecasting, governance, security/procurement, advanced analytics.

## Multinational

Global operating model + regional autonomy, entity/jurisdiction considerations, global compensation, cross-border governance, resilience and enterprise control plane.
