## 6. How these sources should be combined

Do NOT build the Company OS by copying one framework.

Use a layered synthesis:

1. **Strategy** — strategic planning, market analysis, business model, positioning.
2. **Capabilities** — capability map and maturity assessment.
3. **Operating model** — value creation, departments, decision rights, technology and resources.
4. **Processes** — APQC-style process taxonomy and ISO process approach.
5. **Roles** — role/responsibility mapping and decision rights.
6. **Goals** — OKRs/KPIs and review cadence.
7. **Execution** — operational plans, project systems and prioritization.
8. **Documentation** — SOPs, policies, playbooks and knowledge management.
9. **AI layer** — prompts, skills, agents, workflows and automation.
10. **Continuous improvement** — audits, retrospectives, metrics and process improvement.

This layered model is the recommended research architecture for future discovery; it is not claimed to be a single external standard.
