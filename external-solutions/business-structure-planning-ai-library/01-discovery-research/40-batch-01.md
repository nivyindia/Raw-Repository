## Reuse rule
DISCOVER → VERIFY SOURCE → CHECK LICENSE/TERMS → SANDBOX → EVALUATE → ADAPT → INTEGRATE → BUILD ONLY THE GAP.

Do not copy third-party code or documentation wholesale. Keep provenance and require human approval/least privilege before material external actions.
