## Nivy business-planning flow

Business Idea
→ Business Model
→ Business Plan
→ Company Structure
→ Departments
→ Roles
→ KPIs / OKRs
→ Processes / SOPs
→ People + AI Employees
→ Projects
→ Tasks
→ Automations
→ Revenue / Finance
→ Performance
→ Review / Improve

The planning UI should therefore not be a standalone canvas only. It should create structured company objects that can flow into the same Company Digital Twin used by the operating dashboard.

## Design rule

Business planning, company design and execution should become data-producing workflows, not dead documents. A plan should be able to create or update company entities, departments, roles, KPIs, projects, SOPs and AI agents after approval.
