## Key conclusion

The ideal-company comparison found additional enterprise capabilities, but most are now either:

1. already covered,
2. covered through composable existing assets, or
3. identified as implementation-depth gaps rather than unknown capabilities.

The targeted research found reusable candidates for board governance, treasury/control skills, ITSM, GRC, ESG, revenue assurance, entity information and corporate legal workflows. Candidate examples include LQGovernance-OpenBoard, Tessio, FreeITSM, security-atlas, riskready-community, sustainability-project, Rapo, revenue_leakage_system, EIOS and corporate legal skills. These require normal license, security, maintenance and functional validation before adoption.

## Architecture rule

Do not create one standalone application per capability. Prefer:

`Company OS + Systems of Record + Workflow Engine + Knowledge/Memory + Specialist Skills/Agents + MCP/API + Governance + Approval + Audit + Analytics`

## Transition rule

Broad discovery is now sufficiently complete. The standard operating mode should be:

`VERIFY → TEST → SELECT → ADAPT → INTEGRATE → PILOT → PRODUCTION`

New discovery should only be reopened when a genuinely new capability, jurisdiction, industry requirement, obsolete asset or material implementation gap appears.
