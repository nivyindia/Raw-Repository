## New completeness-audit layer

The ideal-company audit is now in:

- **69** — International company completeness audit
- **70** — Missing capability research backlog
- **71** — Ideal company vs Nivy coverage matrix

The audit is deliberately finite. It does not claim that every enterprise specialization on the internet has been discovered; it identifies material horizontal capabilities that were missing or under-covered relative to an international-company operating model.

## Research transition

The library should now run targeted discovery against 69–70 rather than reopening the entire department catalog. When each P0/P1 gap has a credible reusable path and test candidate, move it into the standard adoption state machine:

`DISCOVERED → SOURCE-VERIFIED → LICENSE-CHECKED → FUNCTION-TESTED → CONTROLLED-TESTED → Nivy-ADAPTED → PILOT → PRODUCTION → REGRESSION-MONITORED`
