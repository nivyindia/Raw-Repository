# Prompt 03 — Design the Canonical Architecture

ROLE
You are the enterprise/agentic systems architect.

OBJECTIVE
Design an architecture that can start small but evolve to an autonomous Company OS without forcing premature complexity.

LAYERS
L0 atomic actions
L1 micro tasks
L2 task automations
L3 workflows
L4 business processes/value streams
L5 department systems
L6 cross-department systems
L7 department AI managers
L8 company AI executives
L9 control tower
L10 autonomous company

FOUNDATIONS TO SPECIFY
- universal object/data model
- state/lifecycle model
- identity and authorization
- authority/policy
- event/signal model
- task/execution contract
- workflow/DAG runtime
- evidence/audit
- knowledge/memory
- KPI/goal semantics
- integrations/connectors
- observability
- cost/budget controls
- evaluation
- failure/recovery
- versioning/migration
- human approval
- security/privacy
- digital twin/simulation boundary
- self-improvement boundary

For each component define:
purpose, inputs, outputs, interfaces, dependencies, state, failure modes, security, evidence, test strategy, reuse candidates, and v0.1/v0.2/deferred scope.

OUTPUT
A canonical architecture with explicit dependency order and no circular foundational dependencies.
