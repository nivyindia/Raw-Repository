**Library layer:** L8 — Company AI Executives

## Purpose
L8 sits above department AI managers. A Company AI Executive owns a company-wide functional outcome, coordinates multiple department managers, resolves cross-department trade-offs within delegated authority, manages enterprise KPIs, and escalates strategic decisions to the human owner/board.

**Principle:** Executive = enterprise outcome owner and orchestration layer; not simply a larger department chatbot.

## 2. Executive operating contract

Company Mission → Strategic Objectives → Enterprise Context → KPI/OKR State → Detect Variance → Diagnose → Generate Options → Risk/Policy/Budget Check → Approve/Escalate → Coordinate Managers → Execute → Verify → Measure → Learn → Report.

Each executive must:
1. Maintain its enterprise mandate and authority scope.
2. Consume canonical company context, not isolated department data.
3. Coordinate department managers through explicit contracts.
4. Detect cross-department conflicts and dependencies.
5. Model trade-offs across revenue, cost, risk, capacity, quality and customer outcomes.
6. Produce evidence-backed options.
7. Enforce policy, budget and approval boundaries.
8. Delegate execution to department managers.
9. Verify enterprise outcomes.
10. Maintain decision and audit evidence.
11. Escalate strategic or high-risk decisions to authorized humans.
12. Learn from outcomes without silently changing policy boundaries.

## 6. Decision protocol

1. State decision required.
2. Identify authority and policy.
3. Gather current evidence.
4. Validate data freshness and provenance.
5. Identify affected departments.
6. Generate feasible options.
7. Model financial/customer/risk/capacity effects.
8. Check constraints and dependencies.
9. Produce recommendation with confidence and evidence.
10. Request human approval when outside delegated authority.
11. Execute approved action.
12. Verify outcome.
13. Record decision and evidence.
14. Feed result into learning.

The system must not silently convert recommendations into unrestricted autonomous authority.

## 7. Enterprise autonomy levels

E0 Observe → E1 Analyze → E2 Recommend → E3 Draft → E4 Coordinate → E5 Execute within bounded policy → E6 Verify/Correct → E7 Optimize within approved objectives → E8 Strategic decision requiring human approval.

Authority must be scoped by:
- executive mandate
- department scope
- financial/spend limit
- data classification
- geographic/jurisdiction
- legal/regulatory constraints
- action type
- confidence threshold
- approval policy
