# Department AI Manager & AI Workforce Reuse Catalog — September 2026

**Library layer:** L7 — Department AI Managers

## Purpose
L7 turns each department system into a managed AI workforce. An AI Manager owns departmental goals, plans work, delegates to AI employees and humans, enforces policy, monitors KPIs, handles exceptions, and reports outcomes upward.

**Principle:** AI Manager = manager/control layer, not merely a chatbot or single-task agent.

## 2. Universal AI Manager operating contract

Mission → Goals/KPIs → Context → Plan → Prioritize → Delegate → Execute → Verify → Measure → Exception → Escalate → Learn → Report.

Every manager must:
1. Maintain department mission and objectives.
2. Translate goals into executable plans.
3. Maintain work queues.
4. Delegate to AI employees, tools, workflows and humans.
5. Select skills, prompts, models and tools under policy.
6. Check authorization, risk, budget and approval requirements.
7. Monitor execution and SLAs.
8. Detect anomalies and exceptions.
9. Retry/fallback/compensate where permitted.
10. Escalate decisions outside authority.
11. Verify outcomes against acceptance criteria.
12. Update knowledge/memory and evidence.
13. Produce department reporting.
14. Optimize cost, quality, latency and throughput.
15. Retire or replace failing automations.

## 3. AI workforce composition

Managers should maintain capability-based rosters of:
- planners, researchers and analysts
- operators and workflow agents
- SDR/outreach and content agents
- data, finance, HR and recruiting agents
- support, engineering and QA agents
- security, legal and compliance agents
- procurement and vendor agents
- knowledge/RAG and reporting agents
- monitoring/observability agents
- human specialists and approvers

The roster is capability-based, not vendor-based.

## 6. Autonomy levels

A0 Observe → A1 Analyze → A2 Recommend → A3 Draft → A4 Request Approval → A5 Execute within policy → A6 Verify/Correct → A7 Proactively Optimize.

No manager may exceed delegated authority, spend limit, data scope, or action policy.
