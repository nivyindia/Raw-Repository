## Recommended architecture

Use the sources as complementary layers, not competing systems:

1. **Process taxonomy:** APQC
2. **Management principles:** ISO process approach
3. **Operating model:** McKinsey + Capstera
4. **Organization / decision rights:** Mutomorro + Atlassian
5. **Goals / execution:** Atlassian OKRs + Operational Planning
6. **SOP implementation:** OpenAIToolsHub + Stratisian
7. **AI execution:** skills/agents/workflows catalog in adjacent files

## Adoption rule

A framework becomes part of Nivy only after it is mapped to an actual business outcome, owner, process, artifact, automation opportunity and KPI. Framework collection without implementation mapping is not considered progress.
