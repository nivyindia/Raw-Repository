# Business Creation → Launch → Scale → Autonomous Company Master List — September 2026

**Purpose:** Define the complete capability universe required for an AI-native system that can take **any legitimate business idea/company concept** from research and validation through business-model design, formation, organization, product/service creation, branding, digital presence, launch, sales, delivery, funding, scaling, international expansion and ultimately bounded autonomous operation.

**Important:** This file is the **master scope/list first**. It is not yet the resource catalog. After scope approval/locking, every capability will be researched for reusable agents, skills, prompts, workflows, templates, SaaS, open source, APIs, MCP/A2A, systems and complete business-building platforms.

**Governing principle:**
DISCOVER → VALIDATE → DESIGN → MODEL → BUILD → PREPARE → LAUNCH → OPERATE → MEASURE → IMPROVE → SCALE → FUND → EXPAND → AUTONOMIZE → CONTINUOUSLY EVOLVE
