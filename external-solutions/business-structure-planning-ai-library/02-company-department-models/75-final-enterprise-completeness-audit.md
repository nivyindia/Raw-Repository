## What is NOT required

Do not create separate standalone systems for every row.

Prefer composition:

`Core Company OS + Systems of Record + Workflow Engine + Knowledge/Memory + Specialist Skills/Agents + MCP/API + Governance + Approval + Audit + Analytics`

For example:

- Treasury should connect finance + banking + approval + accounting.
- Entity management should connect legal + tax + compliance + banking + HR.
- SaaS management should connect IT + procurement + finance + identity.
- Internal audit should connect risk + controls + evidence + documents + workflow.
- ESG should connect procurement + finance + facilities + HR + data.

## Final transition

### Discovery
**Sufficiently complete for implementation.**

### Verification
**Next.** Verify licenses, dependencies, security, maintenance and current functionality.

### Functional testing
Test representative workflows with synthetic and controlled real-world data.

### Selection
Choose the smallest number of reusable components that cover the required capabilities.

### Adaptation
Normalize schemas, terminology, policies, brand/company context and connectors.

### Integration
Connect systems of record, MCP/API tools, workflows, approvals and audit.

### Pilot
Run controlled pilots with measurable KPIs and human oversight.

### Production
Promote only validated workflows.

## Research stopping rule

New external discovery should continue only when:

- a new business capability appears,
- a material implementation gap is found,
- an existing candidate becomes obsolete,
- a better reusable asset replaces an existing candidate, or
- a new jurisdiction/industry creates a genuinely different requirement.

Do **not** restart generic "find everything" research after this point.
