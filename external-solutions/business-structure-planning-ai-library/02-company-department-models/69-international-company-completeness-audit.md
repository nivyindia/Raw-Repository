# International Company Completeness Audit

**Status:** v1.0 — 2026-09-16
**Purpose:** Compare the ideal capability model of an international company with the reusable automation/AI library already discovered, and list only material capability gaps that are not adequately represented.

## Method

The audit compares:

`IDEAL INTERNATIONAL COMPANY CAPABILITIES → EXISTING Nivy DISCOVERY LIBRARY → GAP TEST → MISSING/UNDER-COVERED BACKLOG`

The existing library already covers the major horizontal domains: strategy, revenue, marketing, social/content/creative, web/ecommerce, SEO, analytics, CRM/customer lifecycle, documents, HR/workforce, finance, operations/PMO, legal/compliance, IT/cybersecurity, AI/automation, knowledge, partnerships, internationalization, product, procurement, customer/community/events, governance, AgentOps, process intelligence and workforce dashboards. See the master capability checklist and current cross-company analysis.

Current enterprise operating-model research also emphasizes that AI value comes from redesigning workflows, decisions, accountability, data and governance rather than adding isolated agents. citeturn0search2turn0search3turn0search5turn0search9

## Important conclusion

The audit does **not** mean these capabilities must become separate Nivy departments or separate applications. Several should be composed into shared control-plane workflows. The objective remains:

`REUSE → ADAPT → COMPOSE → INTEGRATE → BUILD ONLY THE REMAINING GAP`

Current enterprise research supports moving from isolated automation toward connected operating models spanning workflows, data, governance, people and accountability. citeturn0search5turn0search7turn0search8
