## 34. The often-missed layer: digital asset lifecycle

Search for the complete lifecycle:

`Brief → Research → Draft → Create → Review → Approve → Publish → Distribute → Measure → Repurpose → Archive`

Apply it to text, images, video, presentations, web pages, ads, product listings and documents.

## 35. The often-missed layer: channel operations

Do not only search for content generation. Search for the operational layer:

`Plan → Create → Approve → Schedule → Publish → Monitor → Respond → Escalate → Analyze → Improve`

Apply separately to each social, messaging, website, email, advertising and commerce channel.

## 36. The often-missed layer: revenue-to-delivery chain

Search for reusable systems spanning the complete customer lifecycle:

`Traffic → Lead → Qualification → Discovery → Proposal → Contract → Payment → Onboarding → Delivery → QA → Support → Renewal → Upsell → Referral → Case Study`

## 38. Why this expansion matters

Large digital-service operators explicitly combine content, design, campaigns, social, analytics, ecommerce, CRM/CMS, website management and technical documentation into integrated operations. citeturn0search0turn0search6

Global digital transformation providers also combine customer experience, applications, AI/data, cloud/DevOps, cybersecurity and digital marketing. citeturn0search2
