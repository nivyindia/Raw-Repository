## 10. Adoption rule

For every candidate record source URL, owner, exact version/commit, license, dependencies, required APIs/providers, security/privacy, maturity/activity, capability coverage, Nivy V1/V2/V3 relevance, reuse method and verification status.

A marketplace listing or README proves that a resource exists; it does not prove production readiness or suitability for Nivy.
