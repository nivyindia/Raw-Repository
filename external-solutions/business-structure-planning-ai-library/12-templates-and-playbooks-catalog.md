## Template handling rule

Templates should be copied into the Raw repository as **source references or adapted drafts**, not silently converted into canonical Nivy policies. Preserve original publisher, URL, license/access terms, research date and adaptation notes.

## Template-to-system pipeline

`Template → Nivy draft → owner review → process validation → automation mapping → approved canonical artifact`
