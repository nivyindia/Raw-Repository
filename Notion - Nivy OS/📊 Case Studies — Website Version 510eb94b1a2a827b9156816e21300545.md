### CASE STUDY 01 — Digital Marketing

**From scattered campaigns to a scalable lead engine.**

**Client:** B2B SaaS Company · UK Market · 20–50 employees

**Service:** Digital Marketing — Full-Funnel Overhaul

**The Challenge:**

This UK-based SaaS company was spending heavily on Google Ads with no clear attribution model. Their cost per lead was climbing, their conversion rates were declining, and their marketing team had no visibility into what was actually working.

**What We Did:**

We started with a full-funnel audit — mapping every touchpoint from ad impression to closed deal. From there, we rebuilt their Google Ads structure from scratch, redesigned three key landing pages for conversion, implemented GA4 with custom event tracking, and integrated their CRM for closed-loop reporting.

AI-powered bid optimisation was layered in to ensure budget was being allocated to the highest-intent search terms in real time.

**The Results:**

- 📈 3.2x improvement in lead quality
- 📉 41% reduction in cost per acquisition
- 📈 68% growth in qualified pipeline within 90 days
- 📈 First month of positive ROAS on Google Ads in over a year

**Timeline:** 3 months

> *“Nivy Next didn’t just fix our campaigns — they changed how we think about growth. The results in the first quarter exceeded everything we’d achieved in the prior year.”*
> 

> — Head of Marketing, B2B SaaS · United Kingdom

### CASE STUDY 02 — AI Automation

**From 48-hour response times to 5-minute AI-powered qualification.**

**Client:** Recruitment Firm · US Market · 10–30 employees

**Service:** AI Solutions & Automation

**The Challenge:**

This US recruitment firm was receiving 200+ inbound leads per week. Their team was spending the majority of their day manually qualifying candidates — resulting in burnout, slow follow-up, and a growing number of qualified leads going cold before anyone spoke to them.

**What We Did:**

We designed and deployed a custom AI lead qualification system that ingested inbound leads from multiple sources, scored them against their ideal candidate profile, and triggered personalised follow-up sequences automatically.

The system integrated with their existing CRM and included a daily digest for the team showing which leads required human attention.

**The Results:**

- ⏱️ 80% reduction in manual qualification time
- ⚡ Lead response time dropped from 48 hours to under 5 minutes
- 📈 22% increase in booked discovery calls
- 👤 Team capacity freed up for senior conversations

**Timeline:** 6 weeks

> *“The AI automation system they built saved us 30+ hours per week. Our team can now focus entirely on closing, not chasing.”*
> 

> — Founder, Recruitment Firm · United States
