**3. COMPENSATION & PAYMENT TERMS**

**3.1. Compensation Structure**

Associate shall be compensated by the Company based on a Fixed Fee Model, as detailed in this Section 3. This model replaces any and all previous compensation models, including but not limited to any profit-sharing or revenue-sharing arrangements (e.g., 80/20 splits). The Company will provide a separate Fee Schedule detailing the fixed fees for various services.

**3.2. Service Delivery Fees**

Upon the Company's acceptance of completed client work delivered by the Associate, the Associate shall be eligible to receive predetermined fixed fees. These fees are based on the project scope and complexity as defined in the separate Fee Schedule, which is incorporated into this agreement by reference.

**3.3. Performance Bonuses**

As a reward for exceptional performance and high client satisfaction, the Associate may be eligible for discretionary performance bonuses. These bonuses, if awarded, will typically range from 5% to 15% of the project's net profit and are granted solely at the discretion of the Company. Eligibility for and the amount of any bonus shall be determined based on criteria set by the Company, and the payment of a bonus in one instance does not guarantee future bonuses.

**3.4. Referral Fees**

If an Associate refers a new client to the Company and the Company enters into a contract with that client for work completed by service providers *other than the referring Associate*, the Associate shall receive a referral fee. This fee shall be 15% of the Company's Net Profit from that specific client project. "Net Profit" for this purpose is defined as the total revenue received from the client minus all direct costs incurred by the Company in delivering the service (e.g., software licenses, ad spend, payments to other service providers).

**3.5. Company-Provided Leads**

For all client leads generated and assigned by the Company to the Associate, the Associate shall be compensated only with the fixed Service Delivery Fee as per the Fee Schedule. Work on Company-provided leads makes the Associate ineligible for any Referral Fees or any additional bonus or percentage share related to that client.

**3.6. Payment Terms**

All payments due to the Associate under this agreement shall be processed and remitted within **15 days of the following month-end**. For example, for all work completed and approved in the month of September, payment will be processed by October 15th.

- Payments shall be made via bank transfer or another mutually agreed digital payment method.
- All fees shall be paid in the currency specified in the Fee Schedule, which will typically be the local currency (INR) or USD, based on the client's contract with the Company.
- The Company expressly reserves the right to set, negotiate, and adjust all client pricing, rates, and payment terms independently and without any relation to the compensation structure of the Associate.

**3.7. Fee Schedule**

The detailed Fee Schedule, listing the fixed fees for all services, shall be maintained as a separate document. The Company reserves the right to update the Fee Schedule periodically to reflect market changes. The Associate's continued provision of services to the Company after being notified of an updated Fee Schedule will constitute their acceptance of the new terms.
