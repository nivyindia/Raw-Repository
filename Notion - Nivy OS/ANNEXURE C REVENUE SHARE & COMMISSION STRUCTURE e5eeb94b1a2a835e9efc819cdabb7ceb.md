## **SECTION 2: SUB-PARTNER COMMISSION STRUCTURE**

### **2.1 Sub-Partner Override Commission**

**2.1.1 Master Partner Override:**

- **Rate:** 7.5% of all sub-partner generated revenue
- **Calculation:** Applied to gross revenue before sub-partner commissions
- **Payment:** Monthly with regular commission payments

**2.1.2 Sub-Partner Direct Commission:**

- **Rate:** 15-20% (based on Master Partner's discretion within range)
- **Tiering:** Master Partner may implement tiered structure for sub-partners
- **Management:** Master Partner responsible for sub-partner commission payments

### **2.2 Sub-Partner Commission Flow**

```
Client Payment → Company → Master Partner Commission → Sub-Partner Commission
     $100           ↓                ↓                       ↓
               Company Revenue   Master Partner (7.5%)   Sub-Partner (15-20%)
                                 = $7.50 override        = $15-20 direct

```

### **2.3 Sub-Partner Performance Requirements**

**Minimum Performance Threshold:**

- **Month 1-3:** No minimum (ramp-up period)
- **Month 4-6:** $2,000 monthly revenue per sub-partner
- **Month 7+:** $3,000 monthly revenue per sub-partner

**Underperforming Sub-Partners:**

- Below threshold for 2 consecutive months = probation
- Below threshold for 3 consecutive months = replacement required

## **SECTION 3: PERFORMANCE BONUSES & INCENTIVES**

### **3.1 Quarterly Excellence Bonus**

**3.1.1 Qualification Criteria:**

- Achieve 125% of quarterly revenue target
- Maintain 90%+ customer satisfaction score
- 100% reporting compliance
- No major compliance violations

**Example:**

- Annual Target: $325,000

- Annual Performance Bonus: 10% of $325,000 = $32,500

### **3.3 Sub-Partner Recruitment Bonus**

**3.3.1 Bonus Structure:**

- **$1,000** for every 3 sub-partners successfully recruited and activated
- **Activation Criteria:** Signed agreement + completed training + generating revenue

**3.3.2 Payment Schedule:**
