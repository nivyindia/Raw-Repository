## **1. Weekly KPI Dashboard**

A fast snapshot to track short-term movement.

### **Metrics to Include**

- **Revenue (week-on-week growth %)**
- **Number of new clients closed**
- **Lead-to-close conversion rate**
- **Avg. ticket size**
- **Total active projects**
- **Freelancer utilization rate (% of capacity used)**
- **Refunds / chargebacks this week**
- **Client satisfaction score (CSAT)**
- **Number of escalations / delays**
- **Cash collected vs. invoiced**

### **Purpose**

- Rapid pulse check
- Detect early drops in sales
- Track production bottlenecks

---

## **2. Monthly Financial Health Report**

### **Includes**

- **P&L Summary**
    - Revenue
    - COGS (freelancers, sales commissions)
    - Gross profit
    - Operating expenses (tools, ops, HR)
    - Net profit margin
- **Cash Flow Summary**
    - Opening cash
    - Inflows (payments received)
    - Outflows (payouts, tools, salaries, refunds)
    - Closing cash
- **AR (Accounts Receivable)**
    - Outstanding invoices
    - Aging report
- **AP (Accounts Payable)**
    - Pending payouts (freelancers + vendors)
- **Currency impact (USD-INR)**
- **Runway calculation** (months of cash buffer left)

### **Purpose**

- Validate profitability
- Prevent cashflow shocks
- Plan investments & hiring

## **4. Live Revenue Tracking**

### **Automation Sources**

- Stripe / PayPal / Wise
- Google Sheets revenue API
- CRM (GoHighLevel/HubSpot)
- Notion revenue board

### **Shows**

- Today’s revenue
- This week’s revenue
- MTD (Month-to-date)
- YTD (Year-to-date)
- Upcoming payments
- Late invoices
- Refund risk alerts

### **Purpose**

- Daily clarity
- Zero surprises
- Tracks trends in real time

---

## **5. Retention Dashboard**

### **Metrics**

- **Client churn rate**
- **Average client lifespan** (in months)
- **LTV (lifetime value)**
- **Renewal rate**
- **Upsell rate**
- **Drop-off reasons**
- **Client health score categories:**
    - *Green:* High usage, happy
    - *Yellow:* Reduced usage
    - *Red:* High risk of churn

### **Purpose**

- Understand why clients leave
- Identify upsell opportunities
- Improve long-term revenue predictability

---

## **6. Profitability Dashboard**

### **Breakdown**

- **Project-wise profitability**
    - Revenue
    - Freelancer cost
    - Tool cost allocation
    - Margin %
- **Client-wise profitability**
    - Some clients pay more but cost more—identify that
- **Service-wise profitability**
    - SEO vs paid ads vs design etc.
- **Team/Freelancer productivity metrics**
    - Hours spent vs output
    - Cost per hour vs value created
- **Net margin trends (month-on-month)**
- **Costs that must be reduced**
- **High-profit vs low-profit segments**

### **Purpose**

- Remove loss-making services
- Improve margins
- Build a high-efficiency delivery model
