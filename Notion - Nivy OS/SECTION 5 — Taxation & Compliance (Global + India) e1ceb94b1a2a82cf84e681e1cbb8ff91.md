# SECTION 5 — Taxation & Compliance (Global + India)

Here is a complete and professional **SECTION 5 — Taxation & Compliance (Global + India)** tailored for a digital marketing agency serving international clients (US/UK/EU/CA) with freelancers and sales reps in Tier 2/3 countries.

---

# 

This section ensures your international digital marketing agency stays **fully compliant** with Indian tax laws and global regulations while handling foreign clients, foreign remittances, and distributed teams.

---

## **1. GST Rules for Export Services**

Digital marketing services provided to **foreign clients** (outside India) are classified as **“Export of Services”** under GST.

### **Key points:**

- GST **0%** (no GST charged to clients)
- Must receive payment in **foreign currency** (USD/EUR/GBP/etc.)
- Client must be located **outside India**
- Service must be consumed **outside India**

### Two compliance options:

1. **Export with Payment of IGST**
    - You charge GST (IGST 18%) → then later apply for refund.
2. **Export under LUT (preferred)**
    - Charge **NO GST**
    - No refund process
    - Faster and simpler

---

## **2. LUT Filing Requirement**

To export services without charging GST, you must file an **LUT (Letter of Undertaking)**.

### LUT details:

- Filed annually on the GST portal
- Valid for April–March each year
- Allows you to bill foreign clients at **0% GST**
- Mandatory if you don’t want to charge IGST

---

## **3. TDS Deduction Rules for Freelancers (India)**

When paying Indian freelancers:

### **If freelancer does NOT have a company (individual):**

- TDS @ **10%** under Section 194J
- Applied when payment exceeds **₹30,000/year**

### **If freelancer is a company/OPC/Pvt Ltd:**

- No TDS under 194J for professional fees
- Payments can be made without deduction

### **If freelancers are outside India:**

- No TDS unless they are working from India

---

## **4. Income Tax on Foreign Receipts**

Foreign client payments received in India are treated as **business income**.

### For proprietorship / partnership:

- Taxed based on slab
- Eligible for **presumptive taxation 44ADA (50% profit)**
    
    (only if service qualifies as professional services)
    

### For companies (OPC/Pvt Ltd):

- **22% corporate tax rate** (under Section 115BAA)
- Additional 10% surcharge + 4% cess

### Important rules:

- Maintain FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate)
- Maintain bank advice records for forex conversion
- Maintain invoices in **USD + INR** formats

---

## **5. USD/INR Conversion Documentation**

When you receive payments from foreign clients:

### **Mandatory documentation:**

1. **Invoice in foreign currency** (USD/GBP/EUR)
2. **Bank credit report** (shows amount received + sender)
3. **FIRS/FIRA** (if applicable depending on bank)
4. **Bank conversion rate** (used for accounting in INR)

### **Accounting requirement:**

- Income must be recorded in books using the **exchange rate** on the date of receipt.

---

## **6. International Compliance Requirements**

Since you work with global clients, you must comply with international marketing and data privacy laws.

### **GDPR (Europe)**

Required if:

- You store/process any EU citizen data
- You run ads targeting EU users

Must have:

- Privacy policy
- Cookie consent
- Data protection officer (if large scale)

### **CAN-SPAM Act (USA)**

Required for:

- Email marketing to US audience

Must include:

- Unsubscribe link
- Valid sender address
- No misleading subject lines

### **UK Data Protection Act**

Required for:

- UK-based clients/users

Must follow:

- Data minimization
- Secure data storage
- Disclosure of tracking technologies

---

## **7. Sales Rep Compliance (Global)**

Your sales reps will be from Tier2/3 countries working commission-only.

### Compliance requirements:

- Issue **commission agreement contracts**
- Sales reps cannot misrepresent services
- GDPR compliance for EU prospect data
- Maintain logs of outreach messages
- Follow global anti-spam laws:
    - CAN-SPAM
    - GDPR
    - PECR (UK)

### Important:

- Sales reps are **not employees**
- No salary → reduces HR compliance burden
- Classified as **independent contractors**

---

## **8. Legal Entity Structure (India)**

Your operations can be structured under any of the following:

### **1. Sole Proprietorship**

Pros:

- Easiest to start
- Zero compliance
    
    Cons:
    
- High tax in higher slabs
- No limited liability
- Not ideal for global scaling

### **2. OPC (One Person Company)**

Pros:

- Limited liability
- Good for solo founders
- Better for international billing
    
    Cons:
    
- Moderate compliance
- Can’t add more founders easily

### **3. Private Limited Company (Recommended)**

Pros:

- Best for scaling internationally
- Easy to onboard foreign clients
- Lowest tax rate (22% corporate tax)
- Best credibility
    
    Cons:
    
- More compliance cost
- Annual audit required

### Ideal structure for your global agency:

✔ **Private Limited Company**

✔ LUT registered

✔ Freelancer TDS mechanism

✔ Sales reps as contractors
