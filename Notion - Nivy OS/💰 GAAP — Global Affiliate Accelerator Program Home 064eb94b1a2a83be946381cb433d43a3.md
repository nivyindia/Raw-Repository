## 🧠 What is GAAP? (Plain English)

GAAP is Nivy's growth army. Instead of a large in-house sales team, Nivy recruits students and part-time earners who:

1. Get free training on how to sell Nivy services
2. Receive a ready-made marketing kit (scripts, posters, content)
3. Promote Nivy offerings online via WhatsApp, Instagram, college groups, etc.
4. Earn commissions on every sale they bring in
5. Get certificates, badges, and career growth rewards

The funnel starts with **freebies** (free logos, website templates, visiting cards) that attract mass attention, then converts leads into paid service buyers step by step.
