# 🧩 THE UNIVERSAL PARTNER EXECUTION FRAMEWORK (U-PEF)

Think of this as **“How work gets done here”**.

Every external or internal entity must operate inside this — no exceptions.

---

## 1️⃣ THE FRAMEWORK (SIMPLE, MEMORABLE)

### 🔑 **O-R-O-C-R MODEL**

| Step | Meaning | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **O** | Objective | What outcome is expected |
| **R** | Responsibility | Who owns it |
| **O** | Output | What must be delivered |
| **C** | Checklist | How quality is ensured |
| **R** | Reporting | How results are shown |

If a partner cannot explain their work using **O-R-O-C-R**, they cannot work with you.

## 2️⃣ UNIVERSAL PARTNER BRIEF (WHAT YOU SEND ON DAY 1)

### 📄 PARTNER STARTER BRIEF
