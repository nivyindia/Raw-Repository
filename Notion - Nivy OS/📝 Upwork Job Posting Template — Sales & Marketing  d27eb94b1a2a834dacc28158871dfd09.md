## **1. Title**

**“Master Sales & Marketing Agency for Lead Generation, Partner Management & Sales Closure (Digital Marketing, IT & Tax Services)”**

---

## **2. Project Overview / Description**

We are a **multi-service company** offering **Digital Marketing, IT Development, and Tax & Accounting Services** to startups, SMEs, and professionals across India and abroad.

We are seeking a **Master Sales & Marketing Agency Partner** to **lead, manage, and scale our national & international sales operations** — from **lead generation and client closure** to **sub-partner management and performance training**.

The agency will act as our **Master Sales Partner**, responsible for building and managing a **network of sub-sales agencies or freelance partners**, training them, ensuring performance, and driving consistent revenue growth across regions.

## **3. Scope of Work / Deliverables**

### **A. Market & Network Development**

- Identify potential **territories, industries, and business segments** for expansion.
- Recruit, onboard, and manage **Sub-Sales Partners / Regional Sales Teams**.
- Develop and execute **territory development strategies** aligned with company goals.

### **B. Sales & Revenue Operations**

- Generate qualified leads via **Email, LinkedIn, and CRM campaigns**.
- Conduct outreach, proposals, demos, and **deal closures** for our core services.
- Maintain **CRM pipeline hygiene** and **sales forecasting dashboards**.
- Achieve monthly and quarterly **sales & revenue targets**.

### **C. Partner & Client Management**

- Provide **sales scripts, pitch decks, training content, and onboarding documents** for sub-partners.
- Monitor sub-partner performance and ensure **consistent client experience**.
- Maintain and update **partner communication logs, reports, and performance reviews**.

### **D. Marketing & Brand Alignment**

- Coordinate with HQ for **brand-approved marketing campaigns** and creatives.
- Execute **localized digital promotions, webinars, and events** to attract leads.
- Ensure **brand consistency** across all marketing materials and communications.

### **E. Training & Compliance**

- Conduct regular **sales training sessions**, **mentorship calls**, and **performance audits**.
- Prepare **all required training materials, documents, onboarding kits, and reporting formats** for sub-partners.
- Uphold ethical standards, data security, and compliance with all company policies.
