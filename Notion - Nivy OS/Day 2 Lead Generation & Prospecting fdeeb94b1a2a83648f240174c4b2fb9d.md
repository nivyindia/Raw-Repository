## 📹 **Video 1: The "Low-Hanging Fruit" Strategy** (12 minutes)

### **Businesses Already Paying But Not Using:**

**How to Spot Them:**

- They have a "Subscribe" form on their website but no welcome email
- Their last newsletter was 3+ months ago
- They're active on social media but silent on email
- They have customer lists but no communication system

**Real Examples from Our Students:**

- **Local Gym:** Paying $79/month for Mailchimp, last email sent 4 months ago
- **E-commerce Store:** 2,000 subscribers, no emails in 6 months
- **Consultant:** Weekly LinkedIn posts but no email sequence

### **The "They're Already Paying" Mindset:**

"These businesses are literally throwing money away every month. You're not selling them something new - you're helping them use what they're already paying for."

## 🔍 **Research Shortcuts**

### **Quick Email Marketing Check:**

1. **Sign up for their newsletter** - see what happens
2. **Check page source** - search for "Mailchimp", "ConvertKit", "Klaviyo"
3. **Social media links** - do they promote email signups?
4. **Google search** - "[business name] newsletter"

### **Red Flags (Good for You!):**

- "Subscribe to our newsletter" but no confirmation
- Blog with no email capture
- Active social media with no email promotion
- Beautiful website with broken email forms

### **Green Flags (Perfect Clients):**

- Clearly growing business
- Good website and branding
- Service-based model
- Competitors doing email well

## ❓ **FAQ - Day 2**

**Q: What if businesses don't have email signup forms?**
A: Even better! They need full setup. Position it as "I'll build your entire email system from scratch."

**Q: How many leads do I need?**
A: Start with 50 qualified leads. The law of numbers works - more leads = more clients.

**Q: What if I can't tell what email software they use?**
A: Don't worry about it. Focus on whether they're actively emailing their list or not.

**Q: Should I contact businesses with no email list?**
A: Yes! They need setup services. Package it as "I'll build your first 500 subscribers."

## 🚨 **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

1. **Targeting too big:** Start with small-medium businesses (10-200 employees)
2. **Being too general:** "Businesses" isn't a niche - be specific
3. **Skipping research:** Personalized outreach requires knowing their situation
4. **Getting discouraged:** This is a numbers game - trust the process
