## 🟢 **PHASE 0: PRE-JOINING (FILTER & SET EXPECTATIONS)**

**Goal:** Only serious people enter. No confusion later.

| Time | What Employee Receives | What You Communicate | Medium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Day 0 | Hiring Post | Role is part-time, performance-based, no salary | Social media |
| Day 0 | Auto-Reply | “Fill Quick Hire Form” | WhatsApp / DM |
| Day 0 | Google Form | Confirms they understand earnings model | Google Form |
| Day 0 | Auto-Confirmation | “Application received, wait for update” | Email |

👉 **Employee mindset:** “This is simple, clear, no pressure.”

## 🟡 **PHASE 1: SELECTION & WELCOME (START HERE ONLY)**

**Goal:** Zero overwhelm, instant action.

| Time | What Employee Gets | Purpose | Medium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 | **Welcome Email** | Motivation + clarity | HTML Email |
| Day 1 | **START HERE Page** | Single source of truth | Notion / Doc |
| Day 1 | Discord Invite | Community + support | Link |
| Day 1 | First Task (Very Easy) | Quick win | Start Here page |

### 🔹 What START HERE Contains (ONLY THIS):

- What this program is (1 min read)
- What they do daily (1 hour)
- How they earn
- First task (copy-paste)
- Where to ask doubts

👉 **Employee mindset:** “I can do this today.”
