# 🧩 Sales Partner Hiring Steps & Rounds

---

# **Step 1: Initial Application / Screening**

---

**Goal:** Collect essential info and pre-screen based on eligibility criteria.

**Actions / Round 1: Online Application**

- Share a **Partner Application Form** with mandatory fields:
    - Personal info (name, contact, location)
    - Education & professional experience
    - Business / sales experience
    - Digital tool proficiency (CRM, ERP, collaboration tools)
    - Investment capability (if franchise model)
    - Motivation & alignment questions
- Optional: Upload resume, certifications, or past sales reports

**Filter Criteria:**

- Minimum experience & education
- Relevant industry / sales experience
- Digital literacy / tool proficiency

---

## **Step 2: Pre-Screening / Qualification Check**

**Goal:** Verify that the candidate meets your **key must-have criteria**.

**Actions / Round 2: Pre-Screen Call (Phone/Zoom)**

- Discuss experience in sales, business, or client management
- Verify digital & CRM / ERP skills
- Ask scenario-based questions:
    - Handling multiple leads
    - Follow-up process
    - Reporting workflow
- Check availability and commitment level

**Filter Criteria:**

- Communication skills
- Tool knowledge
- Alignment with your brand & work ethics

---

## **Step 3: Work Proof Verification**

**Goal:** Assess their ability to actually perform sales work.

**Actions / Round 3: Work Proof Submission**

- Request **sample outputs or dashboards** from previous work:
    - CRM screenshots (anonymized)
    - Sales reports or client lists (confidential info masked)
    - Proposals or presentations they created
- Optional: References from past employers/clients

**Alternative / Supplement:**

- **Small Paid Test Project:**
    - Example: Generate X leads, send 5 cold outreach emails, submit a sales report
    - Check speed, quality, and accuracy

**Filter Criteria:**

- Proof of previous work
- Quality & structure of reports
- Execution efficiency

---

## **Step 4: Skill & Competency Assessment**

**Goal:** Evaluate real-world ability to sell & manage clients.

**Actions / Round 4: Practical / Assessment Round**

- Live CRM / ERP demonstration
- Scenario-based problem-solving:
    - “How would you handle 50 new leads per week?”
    - “How would you convert a hesitant prospect?”
- Communication role-play (call/email pitch simulation)
- Optional: Digital marketing / lead sourcing skill test

**Filter Criteria:**

- Competency in required tools
- Sales approach & strategy
- Problem-solving & client management skills

---

## **Step 5: Cultural & Motivation Fit**

**Goal:** Ensure the partner aligns with your **brand values, ethics, and long-term goals**.

**Actions / Round 5: Personal / Founder Interview**

- Discuss vision, motivation, and expectations
- Evaluate alignment with company values
- Confirm availability & commitment
- Assess willingness to follow processes & reporting structure

**Filter Criteria:**

- Cultural alignment
- Long-term motivation
- Professional integrity & ethics

---

## **Step 6: Final Selection & Offer**

**Goal:** Formalize the partnership.

**Actions / Round 6: Offer & Agreement**

- Send:
    - Sales Outsourcing / Partner Agreement
    - NDA
    - Authorization / Appointment Letter
- Provide:
    - Welcome Letter
    - Onboarding instructions
    - CRM / tool access

**Filter Criteria:**

- Agreement signed
- Willingness to comply with processes & reporting

---

## **Step 7: Onboarding & Training**

**Goal:** Ensure partner is fully equipped to start selling immediately.

**Actions:**

- Provide Partner Onboarding Kit (manual, scripts, marketing materials)
- Assign initial leads / territories
- Schedule training sessions (product, tools, sales approach)
- Set reporting & KPI expectations

---

### ✅ **Summary Table – Partner Hiring Rounds**

| Round | Purpose | Method | Filter / Checkpoint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Application / Screening | Online form | Eligibility (education, experience, investment) |
| 2 | Pre-Screen Call | Phone/Zoom | Communication, tool proficiency, alignment |
| 3 | Work Proof Verification | File submission / Test project | Sales experience & execution |
| 4 | Skill Assessment | Live demo / scenario test | CRM/ERP skill, problem-solving, lead handling |
| 5 | Cultural Fit | Founder / HR interview | Motivation, integrity, alignment with brand |
| 6 | Final Selection | Offer & Agreement | Signed contract & NDA, commitment |
| 7 | Onboarding | Training + kit | Ready to start selling independently |

---

💡 **Pro Tips:**

- Make **rounds 3–4 optional for very experienced partners** if they provide strong proof.
- Use a **scoring system** for each round to filter objectively.
- Automate application collection via Google Forms / Typeform and link scoring sheets.
- Include **conditional rejection messages** after each round to save time.
