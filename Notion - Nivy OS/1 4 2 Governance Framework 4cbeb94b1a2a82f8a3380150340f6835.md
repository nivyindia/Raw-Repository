## 

| **Section** | **Description** | **Key Roles Involved** | **Tools / Documentation** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1️⃣ Governance Structure** | Defines how the agency is directed, managed, and controlled. | Board of Directors, CEO, COO, CFO, CMO, HR Head, Compliance Officer | Org Chart, Company Constitution, Founders Agreement |
| **A. Board of Directors** | Sets strategic direction, approves budgets, ensures compliance, and protects shareholders’ interests. | Chairperson, CEO, Independent Advisors | Board Charter, Meeting Minutes |
| **B. Executive Committee** | Handles day-to-day management decisions and implementation of board directives. | CEO, COO, CFO, CMO, CTO | ExCom SOP, Monthly Dashboard |
| **C. Departmental Heads Council** | Aligns department goals with company strategy; cross-functional decision-making. | Department Heads (Sales, Marketing, Operations, HR, IT, etc.) | Departmental Governance Report |
| **D. Advisory Board (Optional)** | Brings external expertise for marketing trends, finance, and expansion. | Industry Experts, Investors | Advisory Engagement Letter |

| **Section** | **Description** | **Key Roles Involved** | **Tools / Documentation** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **2️⃣ Policy & Code Framework** | Governs behavior, compliance, and ethics across the company. | Compliance Officer, HR, Legal | Policy Repository in Outline/Nextcloud |
| **A. Code of Conduct & Ethics** | Defines professional standards, transparency, anti-bribery, confidentiality, and conflict of interest rules. | All Employees | Code of Ethics Manual |
| **B. Data Privacy & Security Policy** | Aligns with GDPR, data protection, and client confidentiality. | CTO, Legal | IT Security SOPs |
| **C. HR & Equal Opportunity Policy** | Recruitment, diversity, non-discrimination, anti-harassment. | HR Head | Employee Handbook |
| **D. Communication Policy** | Internal and external communication guidelines. | PR, HR, CEO | Brand & Communication SOP |
| **E. Financial & Procurement Policy** | Financial approvals, vendor selection, and audit controls. | CFO | Procurement Manual |

| **Section** | **Description** | **Key Roles Involved** | **Tools / Documentation** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **3️⃣ Decision-Making Hierarchy** | Establishes who approves what, ensuring speed with accountability. | CEO, COO, Managers | RACI Matrix, Approval Flowchart |
| **Levels:** |  |  |  |
| Level 1 – Strategic | Board & CEO approve company-wide strategy & large budgets. | Board, CEO | Board Meeting Docs |
| Level 2 – Tactical | Department heads implement strategies and approve mid-level expenses. | COO, Department Heads | Departmental Plans |
| Level 3 – Operational | Team leads execute tasks, campaigns, and projects. | Managers, Team Leads | Task Trackers, Dashboards |

| **Section** | **Description** | **Key Roles Involved** | **Tools / Documentation** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **4️⃣ Compliance & Internal Controls** | Ensures legal, ethical, and operational compliance. | Compliance Officer, Internal Auditor | Compliance Dashboard |
| **A. Legal & Regulatory Compliance** | Trademark, contracts, tax laws, marketing laws. | Legal Advisor | Legal Register |
| **B. Financial Control** | Budget tracking, invoice verification, spending caps. | CFO | Accounting Software, Audit Logs |
| **C. Data Security & Access Control** | Permission-based access to tools and data. | IT/CTO | Role-Based Access Docs |
| **D. Performance & Policy Audits** | Quarterly audits to ensure alignment with goals and policies. | Internal Audit Team | Audit Reports |

---

| **Section** | **Description** | **Key Roles Involved** | **Tools / Documentation** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **5️⃣ Documentation & Reporting Standards** | Defines how information is documented and shared. | All Department Heads | Nextcloud / Outline |
| **A. Reporting Cadence** | Weekly reports → Department → Executive → Board | Managers → COO → CEO | Templates in Outline |
| **B. Standard Templates** | Consistent SOPs, project reports, and dashboards. | PMO | Version-controlled documents |
| **C. Version Control & Knowledge Base** | Ensures all documents are updated and accessible. | IT, HR | Git/Outline/Nextcloud Integration |

| **Section** | **Description** | **Key Roles Involved** | **Tools / Documentation** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **6️⃣ Risk & Crisis Management Framework** | Protects business continuity during crises. | Risk Officer, COO, HR, Legal | Risk Register |
| **A. Risk Identification & Mitigation** | Identify operational, financial, and reputational risks. | Department Heads | Risk Assessment Form |
| **B. Business Continuity Plan (BCP)** | Defines steps during outages, data loss, or PR crises. | CEO, CTO | BCP Manual |
| **C. Crisis Communication Plan** | Unified communication during crisis situations. | PR, Legal | Crisis SOP |
