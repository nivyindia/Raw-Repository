# What Is This Folder?

When building the NIVY VA Autonomous System, multiple frameworks and architectural models were designed. These documents contain the thinking behind the system — the principles, models, and phase plans that shaped everything in Active System Hub.

They are preserved here for:

- Future system rebuilds or expansions
- Onboarding new Ops Heads or system architects
- Understanding why certain decisions were made
