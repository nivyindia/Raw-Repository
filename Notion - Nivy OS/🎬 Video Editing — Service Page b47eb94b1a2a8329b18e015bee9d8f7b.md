## Detailed Solution Breakdown

### 🎬 Short-Form Video (Reels, TikToks, Shorts)

- Raw footage review and selection
- Hook-first editing structure
- Captions, graphics, and motion elements
- Sound design and music
- Platform-specific aspect ratio exports

### 📺 Long-Form Video

- YouTube videos, webinars, interviews
- Pacing and structure editing
- B-roll integration
- Intro/outro branding
- Chapter markers and description support

### 📊 Ad Creatives (Video)

- UGC-style and polished ad formats
- Hook testing (multiple cut variants)
- Subtitles and text overlays
- CTA overlays

### 🎙️ Podcast Production

- Audio clean-up and equalisation
- Video podcast editing
- Audiogram creation for social distribution
- Thumbnail design (optional add-on)
