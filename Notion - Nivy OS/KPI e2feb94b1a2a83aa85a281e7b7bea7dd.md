# KPI

---

### **The Essential Email Marketing KPI Dashboard**

Think of these KPIs in three tiers: **Delivery & Engagement** (top of funnel), **Conversion** (middle/bottom funnel), and **Health & Revenue** (overall impact).

---

### **Tier 1: Delivery & Engagement Metrics**

*(The Foundation - Is anyone getting and opening your emails?)*

| KPI | What It Measures | Why It's Important | Good Benchmark* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Deliverability Rate** | % of emails that successfully reached the inbox (not bounced). | The foundation. If it's not delivered, no other metric matters. | >95% |
| **2. Bounce Rate** | % of emails that could not be delivered. **Soft Bounce** (temporary) vs. **Hard Bounce** (permanent). | High bounce rates hurt sender reputation. Clean your list regularly. | <2% |
| **3. Open Rate** | % of recipients who opened your email. | Measures subject line effectiveness and sender recognition. | 15-25% |
| **4. Click-Through Rate (CTR)** | % of recipients who clicked on one or more links in your email. | Measures engagement with your email's content and call-to-action. | 2-5% |
| **5. Click-To-Open Rate (CTOR)** | (Unique Clicks / Unique Opens) x 100 | Measures how effective your email content was **for those who opened it**. A high CTOR means your content resonated. | 20-30% |

> Benchmarks vary widely by industry. Use these as a general guide.
> 

---

### **Tier 2: Conversion & Action Metrics**

*(The "So What?" - Are emails driving valuable actions?)*

| KPI | What It Measures | Why It's Important |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **6. Conversion Rate** | % of recipients who completed a desired action (e.g., purchase, sign-up, download) after clicking. | The ultimate measure of an email's effectiveness in driving business goals. |
| **7. Revenue Per Email** | Total revenue generated divided by the number of emails sent. | Directly ties email marketing efforts to bottom-line revenue. |
| **8. List Growth Rate** | The rate at which your email list is growing (new subscribers minus unsubscribes/bounces). | Measures the health and attractiveness of your list long-term. |
| **9. Forward/Share Rate** | % of recipients who forwarded the email or clicked a "share" button. | Indicates high-value content that drives word-of-mouth marketing. |

---

### **Tier 3: Health & Longevity Metrics**

*(The "At What Cost?" - Are you maintaining a healthy list and reputation?)*

| KPI | What It Measures | Why It's Important | Good Benchmark* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **10. Unsubscribe Rate** | % of recipients who unsubscribed after a campaign. | Measures list satisfaction. A sudden spike signals irrelevant content. | <0.5% |
| **11. Spam Complaint Rate** | % of recipients who marked your email as spam. | **CRITICAL.** High complaint rates destroy sender reputation and deliverability. | <0.1% (1 per 1000) |
| **12. List Churn** | The total % of subscribers lost (unsubscribes + bounces) over a period. | Helps you understand if your growth is outpacing your attrition. | Monitor trends |

---

### **The Most Important KPI: It Depends on Your Goal**

The "most important" KPI changes based on the goal of your specific campaign:

- **Goal: Brand Awareness** → Focus on **Open Rate** and **Share Rate**.
- **Goal: Traffic to Blog/Website** → Focus on **Click-Through Rate (CTR)**.
- **Goal: Lead Nurturing** → Focus on **Click-To-Open Rate (CTOR)** and **Engagement Over Time**.
- **Goal: Sales/Promotion** → Focus on **Conversion Rate** and **Revenue Per Email**.
- **Goal: List Health** → Focus on **List Growth Rate**, **Unsubscribe Rate**, and **Spam Complaint Rate**.

---

### **Advanced KPIs for Sophisticated Analysis**

Once you've mastered the basics, track these:

- **Email ROI:** `(Revenue from Email - Cost of Email) / Cost of Email`. The ultimate measure of profitability.
- **Engagement Over Time:** How long subscribers stay engaged before becoming inactive. Helps with list re-engagement campaigns.
- **Subscriber Lifetime Value (LTV):** The total revenue a subscriber generates over their entire time on your list.
- **Platform/Device Breakdown:** Which devices (mobile vs. desktop) and email clients (Gmail, Outlook, etc.) your subscribers use. Critical for design optimization.

### **Actionable Framework: What to Do When a KPI is Off**

| If this KPI is LOW... | ...Try This Fix |
| --- | --- |
| **Low Open Rate** | A/B test subject lines, test "from" name, segment your list, improve send time. |
| **Low CTR** | Improve email copy, make CTAs more prominent, use more engaging visuals, increase personalization. |
| **Low Conversion Rate** | Ensure landing page matches email promise, simplify the conversion process, offer a stronger incentive. |
| **High Unsubscribe Rate** | Review content relevance, ensure you're setting proper expectations at sign-up, avoid over-sending. |
| **High Bounce Rate** | Implement a double opt-in, clean your list regularly, remove inactive subscribers. |

**Pro Tip:** Don't just look at numbers in isolation. The real power comes from connecting the dots. For example, a campaign might have a low **CTR** but a very high **Conversion Rate**, meaning the few people who clicked were highly qualified. That's a very different story than a low CTR with a low Conversion Rate.

Start by tracking the Tier 1 metrics religiously, then gradually incorporate Tier 2 and 3 as your program matures.
