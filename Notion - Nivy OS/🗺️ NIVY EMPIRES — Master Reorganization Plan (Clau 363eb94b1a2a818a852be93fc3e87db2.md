# 🚨 CRITICAL ISSUES FOUND

**Issue 1 — Massive Duplication (20+ confirmed sets)**

Every major page has at least one duplicate. Users cannot know which version is live. This is the #1 risk.

**Issue 2 — Two Parallel Company OS Systems Running Simultaneously**

Zone 1 (Nivy OS, 11 databases) and Zone 2 (Nivy HQ, 5 live operational databases) run separately with overlapping databases and navigation. One must become the master.

**Issue 3 — Nivy Empires Brands Are Plans-Only, Not Operational**

Nivy Careers, Nivy Growth, Nivy Artisan, Nivy AIMF have detailed business plans but zero operational pages — no SOPs, no tasks, no KPIs, no dashboards.

**Issue 4 — Nivy Next Zone 4 Is Disconnected From Everything**

The standalone Nivy Next brand OS (Zone 4) is a third parallel system, not connected to Zone 1 or Zone 2.

**Issue 5 — Personal Content Mixed with Business Content**

Archive contains matrimonial research, entertainment/dating models, and community ideas mixed with real business content.

**Issue 6 — No Sandbox / Processing Pipeline Exists**

Research Inbox → Draft Zone → Review Queue → Migration Queue flow does not exist anywhere, despite being in the original blueprint.

**Issue 7 — VA Operations Not Connected to Master OS**

16 VA SOPs in Zone 2 don't link to the master sop_database in Zone 1.

**Issue 8 — Three Critical Databases Are Missing**

KPI DB (standalone), Tools DB, and Experiments DB don't exist. analytics_database is not purpose-built for KPI threshold tracking.
