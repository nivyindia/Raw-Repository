# 📄 ONBOARDING STEPS

## 🔹 Step-by-Step Process

1. **Client Confirmation & Deal Lock**
    - Finalize package, scope, pricing
    - Share proposal (PDF/Doc)
    - Confirm start date
2. **Agreement & Legal (MANDATORY)**
    - Send **Service Agreement / Contract**
    - Include scope, timelines, payment terms, termination clause
    - Sign **NDA (if required)**
    - Take **NOC (No Objection Certificate)** for ad account handling (if applicable)
    - Confirm **data/privacy compliance** (GDPR/basic compliance for US clients)
3. **Invoice & Payment**
    - Raise invoice (advance/full as per plan)
    - Payment confirmation required before onboarding proceeds
4. **Welcome & Communication Setup**
    - Send welcome message
    - Create WhatsApp/Slack/Email thread
    - Share point of contact (Account Manager)
    - Define communication timings (timezone clarity)
5. **Onboarding Form Submission**
    - Send onboarding form link
    - Ensure 100% completion
    - Verify clarity of goals and expectations
6. **Access Collection & Verification**
    - Request all required accesses
    - Verify admin-level permissions
    - Test access functionality
7. **Brand & Asset Collection**
    - Logos, brand kit, past creatives
    - Content references
    - Existing ad data (if any)
8. **Internal Team Assignment**
    - Assign Account Manager
    - Assign Content/Ads Team
    - Assign Designer/Copywriter
9. **Kickoff Call (VERY IMPORTANT)**
    - Discuss goals, expectations, timelines
    - Align on strategy direction
    - Clarify deliverables and reporting
10. **Strategy & Planning Phase**
- Initial audit (social/ads/website)
- Competitor analysis
- Strategy draft creation
1. **Approval & Execution Start**
- Share plan with client
- Get approval
- Start execution

# 📄 ONBOARDING FORM TEMPLATE

## 🔹 Business Information

- Business Name
- Website URL
- Industry/Niche
- Location
- Business Model (Service/Product)

## 🔹 Contact Details

- Client Name
- Email
- Phone/WhatsApp
- Timezone

## 🔹 Business Goals

- Primary goal (leads/sales/branding)
- Monthly targets
- KPIs (if defined)
- Short-term vs long-term goals

## 🔹 Target Audience

- Ideal customer type
- Location
- Age group
- Interests/behavior

## 🔹 Current Marketing Status

- Platforms currently used
- Running ads? (Yes/No)
- Monthly ad budget
- Past marketing experience

## 🔹 Competitors

- Top 3 competitors
- Competitor links

## 🔹 Branding Assets

- Logo
- Brand colors
- Fonts
- Brand guidelines (if any)

## 🔹 Expectations

- Preferred communication style
- Reporting frequency
- Approval timelines
