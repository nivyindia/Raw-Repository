## **1. Tools Stack (Core + Optional)**

A modern digital marketing agency relies heavily on software. Categorized into:

**A. Essential Tools (Month 1 onwards)**

- **Project Management**: ClickUp / Notion / Trello
- **Communication**: Slack + Zoom / Google Meet
- **File Storage**: Google Workspace
- **Time Tracking (for freelancers)**: Hubstaff / Toggl
- **Contracts & Signatures**: DocuSign / Zoho Sign
- **Invoicing & Payments**: Zoho Books / Stripe / Payoneer
- **Password Management**: LastPass / 1Password

**B. Marketing Tools**

- **Email Marketing**: MailerLite / instantly / Lemlist
- **SEO Tools**: Ahrefs / SEMrush
- **Social Media Management**: Metricool / Buffer
- **Ads Management**: PPC Tools (AdEspresso, Revealbot)
- **Creative/Design**: Canva Pro + Adobe Suite (as needed)

**C. Automation Tools**

- Make.com or Zapier
- Airtable
- Sheet Automations (Apps Script)

**Estimated Monthly Tools Budget:**

- Lean: $150–$250
- Medium: $350–$600
- Advanced scaling: $1,000+

---

## **2. HR / Operations Cost (Future Scaling)**

Even though your model starts lean with freelancers, as operations grow you will add internal team members.

**Typical roles added Stage 1–Stage 3:**

- **Operations Manager** (₹35k–₹60k)
- **Finance & Accounts Assistant** (₹20k–₹40k)
- **Recruitment/HR Executive** (₹20k–₹35k)
- **Data/Reporting Analyst** (₹25k–₹50k)

**When these roles are needed:**

- After 20–30 active monthly clients
- When daily task management crosses 40–60 tasks
- When you manage 30–50 freelancers

---

## **3. QC Team Cost (Quality Control)**

QC ensures every client gets consistent, high-quality work regardless of the freelancer.

**QC Roles:**

- Content QC
- Ads QC (Meta + Google)
- Design QC
- Funnel QC
- SEO QC

**Typical Cost (India Tier 2/3):**

₹20k–₹50k per QC specialist

OR

₹400–₹800 per QC project (freelancer model)

**QC is activated when:**

- Client complaints rise
- Output varies between freelancers
- You begin offering guaranteed performance

---

## **4. Project Manager Cost**

PM is one of the most important operational roles.

**Responsibilities:**

- Manage freelancers
- Coordinate client deadlines
- Ensure SOPs are followed
- Track delivery timelines
- Bridge communication

**Cost Options:**

- **Freelance PM**: ₹25k–₹40k part time
- **Full-time PM**: ₹40k–₹70k
- **Senior PM (international)**: $800–$1,500

**PM required when:**

- You hit 10–15 active clients monthly
- Delivery starts to lag behind sales

---

## **5. Customer Success Cost**

Customer Success ensures **retention**, upsells, and recurring revenue.

**Roles include:**

- Client communication
- Reporting & performance summaries
- Onboarding & offboarding
- Renewal negotiation

**Cost Range:**

- Tier 2/3 executive: ₹25k–₹45k
- International success manager: $700–$1,200

**Required when:**

- You reach 15–20 monthly retainers
- You want to maintain 80–90% retention

---

## **6. Office / Remote Infrastructure**

Even if you start fully remote, some costs exist.

**Remote Setup Costs:**

- Zoom / Google Meet
- High-speed internet reimbursements
- Laptop leasing (future)
- Virtual address for business

**Physical Office (future optional):**

- Small coworking space: ₹5k–₹15k per seat
- Private office: ₹30k–₹80k per month
- Utility costs: ₹5k–₹15k

**When to move from remote to hybrid:**

- Team size > 12–15
- Need for stable performance and QC

---

## **7. Maintenance and Upgrades**

**Recurring costs include:**

- Website hosting
- Domain renewals
- Tool renewals
- New software onboarding
- SOP upgrades and automation
- Security tools (malware protection, backups)
- Hardware upgrades (once you hire full-time staff)

**Annual maintenance budget:**

₹50k–₹2 lakh

depending on size + tools.
