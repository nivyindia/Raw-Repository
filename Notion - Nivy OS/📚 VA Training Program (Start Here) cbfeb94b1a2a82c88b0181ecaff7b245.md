## 🔄 Full Lead Lifecycle — Understand This Before Day 1

> This is the engine behind everything you do. Every action you take moves a lead forward through this flow. If you don't understand this, all the work you do will feel random.
> 

LEAD → MESSAGE → REPLY → INTERESTED → BOOKED → CALL → CLOSED

### What each stage means:

**1. LEAD** — A person who fits your Ideal Client Profile (ICP). Found on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, etc. Added to CRM as "New".

**2. MESSAGE** — You send the first outreach message. CRM status: "Messaged". If no reply → start follow-up sequence.

**3. REPLY** — They respond to any message (even a "not interested" is a reply). CRM status: "Replied". Now you enter conversation mode.

**4. INTERESTED** — They show buying signals: asking questions, responding positively, engaging with what you said. CRM status: "Interested". Move toward booking immediately.

**5. BOOKED** — Call is confirmed with a date and time. CRM status: "Booked". Prepare and submit the Call Handover Form to your supervisor.

**6. CALL** — The sales call happens (handled by the closer, not the VA). Your job: ensure the lead shows up and the handover info is complete.

**7. CLOSED** — The lead becomes a paying client. This is the revenue outcome your work creates.

### ⚠️ Where VAs lose leads:

| Drop-off Point | Why It Happens | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Message → No Reply | Wrong targeting, weak opener | Improve ICP + use real scripts |
| Reply → Goes Cold | Slow response, no follow-up | Reply fast, follow up 5x |
| Interested → Not Booked | Too much chatting, no call ask | Ask for call after 2nd message |
| Booked → No Show | Poor handover, no reminder | Send confirmation + handover form |

> Your CRM must always reflect exactly where each lead sits in this flow. A CRM that's out of date = leads falling through the cracks = lost revenue.
>

## 📋 Master Reporting System — Standard Format by Stage

> One format. Used every day. Fields are added as you progress through stages — never removed.
> 

### 🟢 BASE REPORT — Active from Stage 1 (Day 1)

Every VA submits this every day, no exceptions:

DAILY REPORT | Date: | Stage: | Leads Generated: __ | Messages Sent: __ | Follow-Ups Sent: __ | Replies Received: __ | Calls Booked: __ | Platforms Used: __ | Issues / Blockers: __

### 🟡 STAGE 2 ADDITION — Added from Day 2

[+] Platforms Used: (LinkedIn / WhatsApp / Email / Instagram / Facebook) | [+] New Platform Added Today: (Yes / No — which one?)

### 🟠 STAGE 3 ADDITION — Added from Week 2

[+] Reply Rate: __% (Replies ÷ Messages × 100) | [+] Booking Rate: __% (Calls ÷ Replies × 100) | [+] Time Breakdown: Lead Gen __hrs | Outreach __hrs | Follow-ups __hrs

### 🔴 STAGE 4 ADDITION — Added from Week 3

[+] Qualified Leads Today: __ (passed ICP check) | [+] Objections Handled: __ (how many, which type) | [+] Call Handovers Submitted: __ (one per booked call)

### 🟣 STAGE 5+ ADDITION — Added from Month 2

[+] Show-Up Rate This Week: __% (Calls that showed ÷ Calls booked) | [+] Pipeline Value (est.): __ (number of Interested leads × avg deal size)

### ⚠️ Reporting Rules

- Submit by **7:00 PM every day** — no exceptions
- No report = no record of work = no pay for that day
- Never leave a field blank — write 0 if zero
- Never combine two days into one report
- If a field doesn't apply to your stage yet, skip it — don't invent numbers
