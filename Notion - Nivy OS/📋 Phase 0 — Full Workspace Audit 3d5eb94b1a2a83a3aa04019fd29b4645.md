## 🔍 Key Problems Identified

1. **No company-wide "Start Here" page** — a new person has no entry point to understand what Nivy is, what the different divisions do, and where to go
2. **Teamspace names don't explain themselves** — "Dump", "GAAP", "Growth Engine" mean nothing to a newcomer without context
3. **Duplicate/overlapping pages** — "Company Overview" appears in multiple teamspaces without clarifying which one is the real master
4. **Auto-generated placeholder content** — many pages still say "Auto-generated sample content. Edit as needed."
5. **No cross-teamspace navigation** — there are no links connecting the teamspaces to each other
6. **Empty shell teamspaces** — 5 teamspaces (Nivy Academy, Nivy Alliance, Nivy Care Foundation, Nivy Digital HQ) have zero content
7. **The all_pages_index database exists but is not visible/promoted** — this is a great asset that is hidden
8. **Mixed page naming styles** — some pages have emojis, some have numbers, some are plain text, no consistent convention
9. **No glossary or terminology guide** — terms like "GAAP", "Growth Engine", "Chairs" are used without explanation
10. **No ownership or last-updated info on most pages**
