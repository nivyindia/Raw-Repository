# ✅ **1. Competitor Categories You Must Track**

A digital agency has **5 types of competitors**:

1. **Local competitors** (city/state)
2. **National-level agencies** (India/global)
3. **Niche-specialized competitors**
    - SEO-only
    - PPC-only
    - Social media-only
    - Website-only
4. **Freelancer competition**
    
    (low cost but high volume)
    
5. **High-end premium agencies**
    
    (charge high but give ideas + positioning)
    

👉 You must track at least **10 competitors total**:

- 3 local
- 3 national
- 2 niche
- 2 high-end
