## Your daily 5 tasks as a supervisor

1. Run morning brief — assign each VA their targets
2. Spot-check 5 messages per VA — flag script violations immediately
3. Review midday checkpoint — identify anyone below 50% by 1 PM
4. Push underperformers before end of shift
5. Submit team summary report by 7:00 PM
