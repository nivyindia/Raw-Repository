# 🧩 SALES PARTNER OBOARDINNG KIT

---

# 🧭 1. **Onboarding Kit (Digital + Physical)**

---

Purpose: To make your new partners feel trusted, guided, and excited.

### 🔹 Digital Items:

- **Welcome Email + Portal Access**
    
    – Personalized welcome note
    
    – Login credentials (CRM, dashboard, or resource hub)
    
- **Onboarding Manual (PDF or Web)**
    
    – Step-by-step guide from Day 1
    
- **Partner Dashboard Access (optional)**
    
    – Lead reporting, commission tracker, leaderboard
    
- **Digital Partner ID / Certificate**
    
    – E-badge or shareable certificate of authorization
    
- **Training Videos & Recorded Webinars**
    
    – Product demo, sales strategy, objection handling
    
- **Knowledge Base / FAQ Portal**
    
    – Searchable Q&A hub (Google Drive, Notion, or website)
    

### 🔹 Physical (Offline) Items:

- **Welcome Kit Box (optional for premium partners)**
    
    Contents may include:
    
    - Welcome letter from Founder
    - Printed company brochure
    - Partner certificate (framed or laminated)
    - Branded pen, notepad, lanyard, badge
    - Small gift (e.g., mug, keychain, notebook)
- **Partner Handbook (Printed)**
    - Compact physical version of onboarding manual
- **Product Catalogue (Printed)**
    - With visuals, pricing, and benefits

## 💰 2. **Financial & Incentive Plans**

Purpose: Build motivation and transparency.

### 🔹 Commission Structures:

- Fixed % commission per sale
- Tiered structure (e.g., 10% → 15% → 20% after hitting targets)
- Hybrid model (small retainer + performance bonus)

### 🔹 Incentive Add-ons:

- **Monthly/Quarterly bonuses**
- **Milestone rewards (e.g., first 10 clients closed)**
- **Annual performance awards**
- **Referral commission** (if they bring another partner)
- **Profit sharing** for top performers

### 🔹 Supporting Documents:

- Commission Sheet (with example calculations)
- Payout Schedule & Policy
- Partner Invoice Template
- Leaderboard (monthly rankings)
- Sales Tracker / CRM Access Sheet

## 🪄 10. **Presentation & Packaging Tips**

- All digital materials stored in a **Google Drive or Notion hub** with folders.
- Each folder with a short README file explaining purpose.
- Use consistent **branding, color, and tone** across all assets.
- Name your pack like:
    
    👉 “Global Sales Partner Success Kit” or “Authorized Sales Partner Toolkit 2025”
