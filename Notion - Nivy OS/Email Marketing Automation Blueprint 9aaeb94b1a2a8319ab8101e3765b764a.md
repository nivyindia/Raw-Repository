# Email Marketing Automation Blueprint

# 

## **PHASE 1: EMAIL EXTRACTION**

### **Sources & Methods:**

1. **Website Visitors**
    - Exit-intent popups: "Wait! Get Free [Service] Before You Leave"
    - Content upgrades: "Download this template" in blog posts
    - Landing pages with lead magnets
2. **Social Media**
    - "Comment 'YES' + Email for free [service]"
    - Lead ads that auto-fill forms
    - Bio link to free offer landing page
3. **Partnerships**
    - Co-host webinars
    - Guest blog exchanges
    - Joint free tools
4. **Cold Extraction**
    - Industry directories
    - LinkedIn Sales Navigator
    - Local business lists

## **PHASE 2: LEAD MAGNET DELIVERY**

### **Immediate Auto-responder Sequence (Days 1-3)**

**Email 1 - Instant (0 minutes):***Subject: "Your [Lead Magnet] is Being Prepared!"*

- Thank you message
- What to expect timeline
- Bonus: "Quick tip to get started"

**Email 2 - Delivery (24 hours):***Subject: "Your [Lead Magnet] is Ready!"*

- Main value delivery
- Step-by-step how to use
- "Most people also need..."

**Email 3 - Value Add (48 hours):***Subject: "Quick question + bonus resource"*

- Engagement question
- Additional free resource
- Soft pitch for next step

## **PHASE 3: RELATIONSHIP BUILDING**

### **Nurture Sequence (Days 4-14)**

**Email 4 - Education (Day 4):***Subject: "How [Business] achieved [Result]"*

- Case study relevant to their industry
- Proof of results
- Subtle mention of your service

**Email 5 - Social Proof (Day 7):***Subject: "3 [Industry] secrets that work in 2024"*

- Valuable insights
- Customer testimonials
- "We helped achieve this"

**Email 6 - Problem Agitation (Day 10):***Subject: "Are you making these [industry] mistakes?"*

- Common pain points
- Consequences of inaction
- Your solution mention

**Email 7 - Solution Preview (Day 14):***Subject: "The simple way to fix [problem]"*

- Your methodology
- Benefits overview
- Call-to-action for discovery call

## **PHASE 4: QUALIFICATION & SALES**

### **Sales Sequence (Days 15-30)**

**Email 8 - Offer (Day 15):***Subject: "Your personalized [service] plan is ready"*

- Customized offer based on their engagement
- Specific benefits for THEIR business
- Limited-time bonus

**Email 9 - Urgency (Day 18):***Subject: "Last chance for [bonus]"*

- Reminder of expiring offer
- Social proof reinforcement
- Strong call-to-action

**Email 10 - Final Offer (Day 22):***Subject: "We're extending your special offer"*

- "By popular request" angle
- Final opportunity
- Alternative: lower-priced entry offer

**Email 11 - Breakup (Day 30):***Subject: "Last email about [service]"*

- Respectful closure
- Alternative resource suggestions
- Final opportunity

## **PHASE 5: RE-ENGAGEMENT**

### **Win-back Sequence (After 30 days inactive)**

**Email 12 - Check-in (Day 60):***Subject: "Are you still struggling with [problem]?"*

- Concerned tone
- New insight or tip
- Offer to help

**Email 13 - New Opportunity (Day 90):***Subject: "New way to solve [problem]"*

- Updated methodology
- Fresh case study
- Special offer for returning leads

**Email 14 - Final Attempt (Day 120):***Subject: "We're updating our records"*

- Confirm interest
- Option to unsubscribe
- One final value offer

## **AUTOMATION RULES & TRIGGERS**

### **Behavior-Based Pathways:**

**If Lead:**

- **Opens pricing page** → Send case studies + offer
- **Downloads multiple resources** → Send consultation invite
- **Ignores emails** → Send re-engagement sequence
- **Requests info** → Immediate sales call booking

### **Lead Scoring:**

- +10: Opens email
- +20: Clicks link
- +30: Downloads resource
- +50: Visits service page
- +100: Requests consultation

### **Segmentation:**

- **By Industry:** Different case studies
- **By Behavior:** Different offer sequences
- **By Engagement Level:** Different frequency

## **TECH STACK FOR AUTOMATION**

1. **Email Platform:** ConvertKit/Sendinblue
2. **CRM:** HubSpot (free)
3. **Tracking:** Google Analytics
4. **Scheduling:** Calendly
5. **Landing Pages:** Carrd/WordPress

## **KEY METRICS TO TRACK**

- **Opt-in Rate:** >25%
- **Open Rate:** >30%
- **Click Rate:** >5%
- **Conversion to Lead:** >3%
- **Lead to Customer:** >10%
- **Revenue Per Email:** Track via UTM codes

## **IMPLEMENTATION TIMELINE**

**Week 1:** Setup lead magnets + auto-responder
**Week 2:** Build nurture sequence
**Week 3:** Create sales sequence
**Week 4:** Test and optimize
**Month 2:** Scale successful elements

This blueprint ensures every email address you collect moves systematically from stranger to customer with minimal manual intervention.
