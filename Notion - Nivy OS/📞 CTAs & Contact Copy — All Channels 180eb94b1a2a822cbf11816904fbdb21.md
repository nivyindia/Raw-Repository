## ⚠️ CTA RULES — ALWAYS FOLLOW

- Never use more than one primary CTA per page/section
- Keep button text to 4–6 words maximum
- Always pair a CTA button with a low-friction sub-text
- For social media, use action words: DM, Comment, Share, Follow
- Never say "Click here" — always describe the outcome
- Standardise email to [**contact@thenivy.com**](mailto:contact@thenivy.com) on all platforms
