## 🔸 DEPARTMENT 1: SALES

**Mission:**

Drive predictable, recurring revenue through outbound, inbound, and partnership channels.

**Key Yearly Goals:**

- 30% MoM revenue growth
- 35% lead-to-client conversion
- 90% client retention

**KPIs:**

- Leads Generated per Month
- Conversion Rate (%)
- Average Deal Value
- Monthly Revenue per Sales Rep

**Core SOPs:**

- Lead Qualification → Proposal → Follow-up → Closure → Handover to Operations

**Tools:** HubSpot, Pipedrive, ZoomInfo, Notion CRM

**Dashboard Metrics:**

- Pipeline Health
- Sales by Channel
- Conversion Trends
- Client Retention
