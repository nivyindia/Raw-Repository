# Whatsapp Setup

### **Phase 1: Foundation & Setup**

Before any automation, you need the right tools and compliance measures.

**1. Choose Your Tools:**

- **WhatsApp Business API (WABA):** The gold standard. Accessed through a **Business Solution Provider (BSP)** like:
    - **Gupshup**
    - **MessageBird**
    - **Interakt**
    - [**Zoko.io**](https://zoko.io/)
    - [**Respond.io**](https://respond.io/)
- **WhatsApp Business App:** Good for very small teams or individual freelancers, but has automation limits (no official API).
- **CRM Integration:** Your WABA provider should integrate with your CRM (e.g., HubSpot, Zapier, [Make.com](https://make.com/)) to sync data seamlessly.

**2. Set Up Your Business Profile:**

- **Profile Picture:** Your agency's logo.
- **Business Name:** Your official agency name.
- **Category:** "Digital Marketing Agency" or "Advertising/Marketing"
- **Business Description:** A clear, benefit-driven description with a CTA (e.g., "Ready to grow? Message us for a free audit!").
- **Catalog:** Showcase your services (e.g., "SEO Package," "Social Media Management," "PPC Campaign Setup").
- **Website & Social Links:** Add links to your website, portfolio, and LinkedIn.

**3. Compliance is CRUCIAL:**

- **Opt-In:** You **must** have explicit opt-in from users before sending them automated messages. This is a legal requirement in many regions and a platform rule.
- **Opt-Out:** Every message must include a clear way to opt-out (e.g., "Reply STOP to unsubscribe").
- **24-Hour Window:** You can only send *template messages* (pre-approved, non-promotional messages) outside of the 24-hour window after a user's last message. Inside that window, you can send free-form, conversational messages.
