# 📍 NAVIGATION BY ROLE

## If you’re a VA or new joiner

1. Open [Global Dashboard](https://www.notion.so/363eb94b1a2a81a6a013f3e798760669)
2. Find your brand
3. Open that brand’s Division Home → your role dashboard will be listed there

## If you’re a manager or supervisor

1. Open your brand’s Division Home
2. Open Manager Dashboard → QC queue, team tasks, KPI snapshot

## If you’re looking for a specific SOP

1. Open the relevant brand’s Division Home
2. Go to SOP Index → filter by department or function

## If you’re looking for research or a concept

1. Search in knowledge_database (filter by brand)
2. Or open the brand’s Knowledge Index

## If you’re onboarding a new team member

1. Open Global Systems → Universal Training Hub
2. Day 1–7 track is there
