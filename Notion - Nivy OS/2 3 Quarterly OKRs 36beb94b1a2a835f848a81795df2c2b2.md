## 📆 5️⃣ QUARTERLY OKR LOOP

*(Objectives & Key Results Framework)*

Quarterly OKRs break your **Yearly Goals** into **90-day action systems**, ensuring every department and leader executes measurable outcomes aligned with company strategy.

---

### 🔹 PURPOSE

- Create focus and accountability in short, measurable cycles.
- Translate strategic goals into actionable targets.
- Maintain organizational agility and adaptability each quarter.

---

### 🔹 STRUCTURE

Each OKR should define:

- **Objective:** Ambitious, qualitative goal.
- **Key Results:** 3–5 measurable outcomes that define success.
- **Owner:** Department Head or Project Lead.
- **Linked Pillar:** Which Strategic Pillar it supports.
- **Review Frequency:** Weekly / Monthly progress review.

---

### 🔁 OKR REVIEW LOOP (Every Quarter)

**Step 1 – Planning (Week 1):**

Each department sets OKRs aligned to yearly goals.

**Step 2 – Execution (Weeks 2–11):**

Teams execute OKRs, updating dashboards weekly.

**Step 3 – Review (Week 12):**

Leadership conducts an OKR Review Meeting:

- Score: 0.0 – 1.0 scale per KR
- Analyze wins, blockers, and next actions
- Plan next quarter OKRs

---

### 🔹 OKR SCORING METHOD

| Score | Description | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.0 | Fully Achieved | Scale & Document |
| 0.7–0.9 | Mostly Achieved | Optimize Process |
| 0.4–0.6 | Partial Progress | Investigate Blockers |
| 0.0–0.3 | Not Achieved | Root-Cause Review |
