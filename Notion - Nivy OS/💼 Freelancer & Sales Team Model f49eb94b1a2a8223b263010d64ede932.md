# 

Your agency runs on a **distributed, scalable ecosystem**:

This section defines the operational, financial, and compliance structure for both groups.

---

# **🧑‍💻 FREELANCERS**

## **1. Marketplace-Style Unlimited Freelancer Model**

- You operate like an internal Upwork/Fiverr:
    - Unlimited freelancers can register.
    - They get projects only based on skill match + availability.
    - No fixed salary → 100% variable cost model.
- Benefits:
    - 0 bench cost
    - Infinite scalability
    - Low delivery cost → high margins
    - On-demand specialists (SEO, ads, content, funnels, etc.)

---

## **2. Skill-Based Cost Tiers**

Define 3 cost categories to control margins:

### **Tier A (₹1,000 – ₹2,500/hr or ₹15k–₹40k per project)**

- 5+ years experience
- Handles complex funnels, SEO, ads strategy
- Client-facing capable
- Used for high-ticket clients

### **Tier B (₹500 – ₹1,000/hr or ₹8k–₹20k per project)**

- 2–4 years experience
- Works on execution-heavy tasks
- Ideal for most retainers

### **Tier C (₹200 – ₹500/hr or ₹3k–₹10k per project)**

- New freelancers
- Works on repetitive or bulk tasks:
    - Social media posts
    - Basic website tasks
    - Ad creatives
    - Research & data

This structure maintains predictable **unit economics**.

---

## **3. Work Quality Score System**

Every freelancer gets a dynamic score:

| Metric | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Timeliness | 30% |
| Quality of Output | 40% |
| Communication | 20% |
| SOP Compliance | 10% |
- Score range: 0–100
- A+ (90–100): Priority assignments
- B (75–89): Standard assignments
- C (60–74): Low-risk assignments
- <60: Warning, re-training, or removal

This reduces delivery failure risks.

---

## **4. Performance-Based Payouts**

Reward high performers, penalize low performers:

### **A+ Rated Freelancers**

+5–10% bonus

Priority in high-paying projects

Faster payouts

### **B Rated Freelancers**

Normal payout

Eligible for training sessions

### **C Rated Freelancers**

- 10% to -20% payout

Given low-risk tasks

On 2 strikes → probation

### **Fraud / Low Quality Repeated**

Immediate remove + reporting + blacklist

---

## **5. Duplicate / Fraud Work Prevention**

Freelancer fraud is common in low-cost countries. Prevent it through:

### **Tools:**

- Grammarly for content originality
- Copyscape/WriterZen for plagiarism
- Snagit + Loom for proof-of-work
- Git or version history for designs
- Time-tracked Trello cards

### **Process:**

- Mandatory loom video explaining the work
- Random audits
- Source file delivery rule
- No upfront 100% payment

---

## **6. Bypass Prevention (Client ↔ Freelancer)**

This protects your agency from revenue leakage.

### **Methods:**

- Client never knows freelancer identity
- NDA + non-circumvention clause signed
- Use in-house email IDs for client communication
- Deliverables always submitted via agency portal
- Encrypted watermark on drafts
- Remove metadata from files

### **Penalties:**

- Immediate contract termination
- ₹50,000–₹1,00,000 bypass fine
- Legal notice for contract violation

---

## **7. SLA for Delivery**

Define guaranteed standards of service.

### **Core SLAs**

| SLA Area | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Delivery Time | 48–72 hours for most tasks |
| Revision Cycle | 2 major + 1 minor |
| Communication | Response within 12 hours |
| Quality | Must meet agency SOP level |
| Confidentiality | No sharing of client files |

### **Escalation Steps**

1. Warning
2. Task reassigned
3. Payout reduced
4. Freelancer removed

# **💼 SALES TEAM**

## **1. Commission-Only Model**

Global commission-only salespeople from Tier 2/3 nations.

### **Commission Structure**

- 15–25% on first month
- 10–15% monthly recurring (retainers)
- No base salary → zero risk

### **Benefits**

- Unlimited sales reps
- No fixed cost
- Incentive-driven
- Global talent pool

---

## **2. Script + Training Cost**

Sales team receives:

- Sales script
- Objection handling sheet
- Email templates
- LinkedIn message templates
- Pricing sheet
- Case studies
- Portfolio deck

Training cost per rep (one-time):

- ₹1,000–₹5,000 (materials, videos, assessments)

---

## **3. Lead Distribution Strategy**

Define how inbound + outbound leads are distributed.

### **Outbound**

- Sales reps generate their own leads
- Agency provides tools (optional)

### **Inbound**

You control distribution:

- Round-robin allocation
- Priority to high-performing reps
- Lead score-based matching

### **Goal:**

Maximize conversions with minimal lead wastage.

---

## **4. Performance Benchmarks**

Every sales rep is evaluated monthly.

| KPI | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Calls Made | 100–200/week |
| Emails Sent | 300–500/week |
| LinkedIn DMs | 200–400/week |
| Conversion Rate | 5–20% |
| Revenue Target | $2,000–$10,000/month |

Low-performers → training or removal

High-performers → better commissions + leads

---

## **5. International Tax Compliance**

Sales reps from different countries must follow:

### **Compliance Rules**

- Sign W-8BEN (non-US contractor form)
- Provide passport/national ID
- Provide payment method verification
- Comply with GDPR & data handling
- Comply with CAN-SPAM for cold emails

### **Agency Requirements**

- Maintain contractor agreement
- Issue annual payment summaries
- No employment-style obligations (to avoid misclassification)

---

## **6. Sales QA (Quality Assurance)**

A dedicated system to maintain sales quality.

### **QA Actions**

- Random call audits
- Script adherence check
- Lead handling time monitoring
- Misrepresentation prevention
- Ethical sales SOP compliance

### **Score Factors**

- Clarity
- Professionalism
- Accuracy
- Compliance
- Conversion efficiency

Low QA score → retraining or termination
