# ⚙️ Action Plan — Outsourcing Sales & Marketing

Of course. Here is a complete, step-by-step action plan to find, onboard, and scale with the right outsourcing partner for your Accounting, Taxation, and Digital Marketing services.

---

### **Phase 1: Foundation & Strategy (Week 1-2)**

*Before you talk to a single partner, get your own house in order.*

### **The Complete Action Plan for Outsourcing Sales & Marketing**

| Step | Task | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1.1** | **Define Your Ideal Client (ICP):** Create a clear profile. E.g., "E-commerce business with 20+ employees, based in Mumbai, revenue over ₹5 Cr." | A 1-page ICP document. |
| **1.2** | **Package Your Services:** Create 3-4 clear service packages with names and starting price points. E.g., "Startup Compliance Pack," "Growth CFO Package," "E-commerce Marketing Suite." | A Service Menu with pricing. |
| **1.3** | **Craft Your Core Value Propositions:** Answer "Why should a client choose us?" for each service line. Prepare a list of common objections and responses. | A Sales Playbook (basic version). |
| **1.4** | **Set Clear Goals & Budget:** Define what success looks like in 3 months (e.g., "15 qualified meetings," "3 closed deals"). Set your total budget for the partner program. | A document with 3-month KPIs and budget. |

---

### **Phase 2: Partner Identification & Outreach (Week 3-4)**

*Find and contact potential partners.*

| Step | Task | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **2.1** | **Create a Target Partner List:** Identify 10-15 potential partners from the categories below. | A spreadsheet with contacts. |
|  | **For Accounting/Tax:** Business lawyers, startup coaches, financial advisors. |  |
|  | **For Digital Marketing:** Web development agencies, IT managed service providers. |  |
|  | **For Both (Sales):** Specialized B2B telemarketing/sales agencies. |  |
| **2.2** | **Develop Your Outreach Kit:** <br> - A short "Partnership Proposal" PDF. <br> - A generic email template. <br> - A script for a follow-up call. | Your outreach collateral. |
| **2.3** | **Execute Outreach:** Contact 3-5 partners per week. Personalize each message. | Scheduled introductory calls. |

---

### **Phase 3: Evaluation & Onboarding (Week 5-6)**

*Select the right partner and get them started.*

| Step | Task | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **3.1** | **Conduct Introductory Calls:** Use the first call to present your opportunity and assess their interest and expertise. | A shortlist of 2-3 interested partners. |
| **3.2** | **Formalize the Agreement:** <br> - **For Referral Partners:** Draft a simple **Referral Agreement** with clear fee structure (e.g., 15% of first contract). <br> - **For Sales Agencies:** Use the **Hybrid "Graduation" Model** contract. | A signed partnership agreement. |
| **3.3** | **Kick-off & Knowledge Transfer:** Conduct a 2-hour training session. Provide all foundational documents (Service Menu, ICP, Sales Playbook, Brand Guidelines). | A trained and informed partner. |

---

### **Phase 4: Pilot Program & Execution (Month 2-4)**

*Run a structured 3-month pilot.*

| Step | Task | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **4.1** | **Start Small:** Begin with a low-risk target market (e.g., India/SEA). Set a realistic monthly target (e.g., 10 qualified meetings). | A live, active pilot program. |
| **4.2** | **Implement Weekly Check-ins:** A 30-minute call every Monday to review previous week's results and the current week's plan. | Consistent communication and feedback. |
| **4.3** | **Track Everything:** Use a shared system (e.g., a simple Google Sheet) to track leads, meetings, and deals. | Clear, transparent performance data. |

---

### **Phase 5: Review, Scale, or Pivot (Month 4)**

*Decide the future of the partnership.*

| Step | Task | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **5.1** | **Conduct a Formal Review:** After 3 months, analyze the results against the KPIs set in Phase 1. Did they hit 65%+ of the targets? | A Performance Review Report. |
| **5.2** | **Make the Go/No-Go Decision:** <br> - **If they SUCCEEDED:** Move them to the **"Junior Partner"** status with a higher fixed fee and access to better markets (UK/US). <br> - **If they FAILED:** Terminate the pilot. Use the learnings to find a better partner. | A strategic decision on the partnership's future. |
| **5.3** | **Plan for Scale:** If successful, work with the partner to create a 6-month growth plan, including hiring their first junior BDE. | A scaled partnership model. |

---

### **Your "Graduation Model" Payment Structure (For Sales Partners)**

This is your core operational model.

| Phase | Fixed Stipend | Commission | Conditions | Target Markets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pilot (Months 1-3)** | ₹15,000/month | 10% of contract value | Score >65% on Work+Exam | India, SEA, Africa |
| **Junior Partner (Month 4+)** | ₹25,000/month | 15% of contract value | Consistent performance | Add UAE, Saudi Arabia |
| **Scaling Partner (Unlocked)** | ₹50,000+/month | 20% of contract value | Hits team targets for 3 months | **UK, US, Europe** |
