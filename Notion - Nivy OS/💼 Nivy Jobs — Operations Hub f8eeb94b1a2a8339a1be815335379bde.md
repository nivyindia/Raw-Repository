## 📝 Hiring Process — Stage by Stage

**Stage 1 — Role Definition**

- Write the Job Description using the JD template
- Get sign-off from the division lead
- Set salary band + employment type

**Stage 2 — Posting**

- Post to: LinkedIn, Indeed, Internshala (IN), Seek (AU), Reed (UK)
- Use the [Hiring Post Templates](%F0%9F%93%9D%20Hiring%20Post%20Templates%20%E2%80%94%20All%20Platforms%2075deb94b1a2a823fadaf01ef0d173a07.md) for each platform
- Add to the Internal Job Listings page

**Stage 3 — Screening**

- Review applications within 3 business days
- Use the [Recruitment & Filtering Process](%F0%9F%94%8D%20Recruitment%20&%20Filtering%20Process%206adeb94b1a2a8305942081e8129f1dfa.md)
- Shortlist max 5 candidates per role

**Stage 4 — Interview**

- 2-stage process: Discovery Call (30 min) → Task/Panel Interview
- Use the [Freelancer Screening Questions](%F0%9F%94%8D%20Freelancer%20Screening%20Questions%20&%20Questionnaire%202ffeb94b1a2a834fa74501861bb2e02e.md) for contract roles

**Stage 5 — Offer & Onboarding**

- Send offer letter within 48 hours of decision
- Trigger [Employee Onboarding Checklist](%F0%9F%91%8B%20Employee%20Onboarding%20Checklist%20%E2%80%94%20Nivy%20d68eb94b1a2a82f2b7230154cd33a9f8.md) on Day 1
