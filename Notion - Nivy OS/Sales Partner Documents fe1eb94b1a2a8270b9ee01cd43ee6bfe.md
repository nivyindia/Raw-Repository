## 🧾 **01_Proposal_Documents**

### Purpose: To pitch and onboard potential sales partners.

**Files to include:**

1. 📊 *Market Overview & Partner Opportunity Summary*
2. ⚙️ *Ideal Partner Criteria*
3. 📘 *Sales Outsourcing Proposal (Main PDF or Deck)*
4. 📈 *Partner Growth Path (Levels: Bronze, Silver, Gold)*
5. ❓ *FAQ Sheet*

---

## 🏢 **02_Company_Profile**

### Purpose: Build trust & credibility.

**Files to include:**

1. 🧾 *Company Brochure (PDF)*
2. 🏗️ *Company Presentation (PowerPoint/Slides)*
3. 📜 *About Us – One Pager*
4. 🧩 *Registration Certificates (GST, MSME, ROC, etc.)*
5. 💬 *Client Testimonials or Case Studies*
6. 🎨 *Brand Kit (logo, colors, fonts)*

---

### On boarding materials

---

**Files to include:**

1. 📚 *Partner Onboarding Manual / Guide*
2. 📋 *Onboarding Checklist*
3. 🗓️ *Training Schedule or Orientation Plan*
4. 📧 *Contact Directory (Team points of contact)*
5. 🧾 *System Access / CRM Setup Guide*

3. 📑 *Objection Handling Sheet*
4. 🎯 *Sales Funnel or Process Map*
5. 📊 *Lead Reporting Format / CRM Sheet*

## 💸 **07_Financial_and_Payout_Docs**

### Purpose: Maintain clarity and trust in payments.

**Files to include:**

1. 📊 *Commission & Incentive Structure Sheet*
2. 🗓️ *Payout Policy (payment dates, method)*
3. 📄 *Sample Partner Invoice Template*
4. 📈 *Performance Tracking / Bonus Sheet*

### Partner Documents

---

**Files to include:**

1. 🧾 *Sales Outsourcing / Channel Partner Agreement*
2. 🔒 *Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)*
3. 🪪 *Partner Authorization / Appointment Letter*
4. 💌 *Welcome Letter to Partner*

## 🧩 **08_Optional_Add-ons**

### Purpose: Add professionalism & differentiation.

**Files to include:**

1. 🏆 *Partner Certificate / ID Card Template*
2. 💡 *Partner Success Stories (mini case studies)*
3. 📬 *Partner Newsletter Template*
4. ⚖️ *Legal Framework Summary (Data & IP Protection)*
5. 📅 *Timeline & Milestones (30/60/90-day plan)*

## ⚙️ **SUGGESTED ORDER OF FLOW (PROCESS TIMELINE)**

| Stage | Partner Receives | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1️⃣ Inquiry | Proposal Deck + Brochure | Attract partner, present opportunity |
| 2️⃣ Evaluation | FAQ + Commission Sheet + Sample Contract | Answer doubts, confirm interest |
| 3️⃣ Agreement | Legal Docs (Agreement, NDA, Letter) | Formal sign-up |
| 4️⃣ Onboarding | Manual + Checklist + Training Schedule | Guide and activate partner |
| 5️⃣ Selling | Sales Scripts + Product Info + CRM Access | Begin operations |
| 6️⃣ Review | Performance Sheet + Payout Docs | Track and reward results |
