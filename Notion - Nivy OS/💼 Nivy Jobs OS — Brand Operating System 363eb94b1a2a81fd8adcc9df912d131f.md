## 🎯 CEO Office

### Vision

> **Match the world’s fastest-growing businesses with pre-vetted global talent — faster, more reliably, and at a fraction of traditional recruitment costs.**
> 

### 90-Day Priorities

1. Define core job categories (Digital, Tech, Finance, Ops, Admin)
2. Build candidate pipeline: 100+ pre-vetted profiles
3. Sign first 3 employer clients
4. Build ATS (Applicant Tracking System) in Notion using clients_database + tasks_database
5. Place first 5 candidates

### Business Model

- **Employer fee:** 15–20% of first year salary on successful permanent placement
- **Retainer model:** Employer pays monthly fee for ongoing talent pipeline access
- **VA placement:** Flat fee £500–1,500 per VA placement

## 👥 Talent Acquisition

### Candidate Pipeline

```
Candidate Applies / Sourced
        ↓
CV Screen (30-second check: relevant experience? English proficiency? Role fit?)
        ↓
Initial Interview (20 min — assess communication, reliability, skills)
        ↓
Skills Test (role-specific task: 1–2 hrs)
        ↓
Background Check (references, LinkedIn verification)
        ↓
Approved → Added to Talent Pool (clients_database: Type = Candidate)
        ↓
Matched to Employer Brief → Intro call scheduled
        ↓
Employer Selects → Offer + Placement
```

## 🏢 Employer Relations

### Employer Onboarding

1. Intake form: role requirements, salary range, timeline, culture fit
2. Brief confirmation call (20 min)
3. SLA agreed: 5 CVs within 5 working days
4. Shortlist presented → Employer selects for interview
5. Placement → Invoice raised
6. 90-day replacement guarantee if placement leaves
