## 📈 How Divisions and Departments Work Together

Divisions are **business units** (they serve clients or communities).

Departments are **functional support teams** (they serve the divisions).

Example: Nivy Global (division) needs a proposal made → they use the Marketing department's templates and the Sales department's scripts from Nivy OS.
