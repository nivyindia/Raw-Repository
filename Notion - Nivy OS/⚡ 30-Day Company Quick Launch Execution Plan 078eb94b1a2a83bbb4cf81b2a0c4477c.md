### ✅ KEY PRINCIPLES FOR QUICK START

1. **Execution > Documentation**: Only SOPs for tasks are needed; skip manuals.
2. **Community-first**: Community executes all roles initially.
3. **Micro-tasks → Larger roles**: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Elite.
4. **Daily proof submission**: Keeps accountability high.
5. **Top performers lead**: Promotes internal leadership fast.
6. **Revenue early**: Commission-based tasks as soon as Gold tier is ready.
7. **Parallel execution**: Jobs, learning, marketing, creators all start simultaneously.
