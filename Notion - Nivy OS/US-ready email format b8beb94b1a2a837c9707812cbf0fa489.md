# PART 1: WHAT US CLIENTS EXPECT (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

## 1️⃣ Clear WHO you are (No ambiguity)

US decision-makers immediately look for:

- Legal company name
- Business role (broker, facilitator, marketplace, sourcing partner)
- Where you operate (US-focused even if global)

**Must include at least ONE of these:**

- Company website
- LinkedIn company page
- Business email domain (not Gmail)

❌ “We are a group helping businesses”

✅ “We are a supplier-buyer matching partner for US construction materials”

---

## 2️⃣ WHY you are contacting THEM (Context)

Cold outreach is accepted in the US **only when relevance is clear**.

**Must answer in first 2 lines:**

- Why them
- Why now
- Why this industry

Example:

> We work with US construction and roofing companies to reduce sourcing costs by matching active buyers with vetted suppliers.
> 

---

## 3️⃣ WHAT you do (Simple, not salesy)

US audiences hate fluff.

Use:

- Bullet points
- Plain English
- No exaggerated claims

Example:

- Aggregate supplier pricing
- Match verified buyers with best-priced suppliers
- Charge only if a deal closes

---

## 4️⃣ WHAT you do NOT do (Huge trust booster)

This is powerful in the US.

Explicitly state:

- No upfront fees
- No obligation
- No exclusivity
- No spam

Example:

> There’s no signup fee, no obligation, and no exclusivity required.
> 

---

## 5️⃣ WHY they should trust you (Proof without bragging)

Choose **1–2 only**, not everything:

- Industries served
- Process clarity
- Transparent model
- Human review before deals

Example:

> Every match is reviewed manually to ensure pricing, quality, and availability align.
> 

---

## 6️⃣ CLEAR, LOW-FRICTION CTA

US users avoid:

- Long forms too early
- “Book a call” immediately
- Aggressive language

First email CTA should be:

- Click
- Confirm
- Acknowledge intent

Example:

> Just click one option below so we can route this correctly.
> 

---

## 7️⃣ COMPLIANCE & LEGAL SIGNALS (MANDATORY)

Even if not legally required, US buyers EXPECT these:

✔ Physical address or country mention

✔ Unsubscribe link

✔ Why they’re receiving the email

✔ No deceptive subject lines

Add one clean footer line:

> You’re receiving this because your business operates in the construction supply chain.
> 

---

## 8️⃣ PROFESSIONAL TONE (US STANDARD)

Avoid:

- Emojis
- Over-friendly language
- “Hope you’re doing great!!!”
- ALL CAPS

Use:

- Calm
- Direct
- Respectful
