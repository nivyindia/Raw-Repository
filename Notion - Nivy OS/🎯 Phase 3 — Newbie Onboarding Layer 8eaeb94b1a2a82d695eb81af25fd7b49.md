# 🎯 Phase 3 — Newbie Onboarding Layer

> This phase is about what we ADD (not change) to make the workspace understandable for someone who has never seen it before. Think of it as a welcome kit built into Notion itself.
> 

> 📍 **Where you are:** Knowledge Base Restructuring Plan → Phase 3 | Completed: April 2026
> 

---

## 🎯 The Goal

When a new team member joins Nivy and opens Notion for the first time, they should be able to:

1. Understand what Nivy is and what each division does — **within 5 minutes**
2. Find their relevant teamspace without asking anyone
3. Know who owns what
4. Know what pages are important vs. drafts vs. archived

---

## 🏠 What to Create: Global "Start Here" Page

This is the most important new page in the whole plan. Create it at the workspace root (or pin it in the Home Dashboard).

**Page title:** `🏠 Start Here — Welcome to Nivy`

**Content structure:**

```
👋 Welcome callout — "Welcome to Nivy! This page will help you get oriented in the first 10 minutes."

🏢 Section 1: What is Nivy?
  [3-4 sentence description of the company and what it does]

🇬🇳 Section 2: Our Divisions
  [Table: Division name | What it does | Teamspace link]

🦭 Section 3: Key Terms & Glossary
  [Table: Term | Plain English meaning]

🗺️ Section 4: Workspace Map
  [List of all teamspaces with one-line description and link to home page]

📧 Section 5: Who to Contact
  [Table: Role | Person | For questions about]

✅ Section 6: Your First 3 Things
  1. Read your department Overview page
  2. Read the Company Wiki
  3. Check the all_pages_index to see everything that exists
```

---

## 💡 What to Add to Every Department Overview Page

At the top of each Overview page (Sales Overview, HR Overview, Finance Overview, etc.), add a standard callout block:

```
💡 What is this page?
This is the [Department Name] overview. It covers dashboards, SOPs, and key documents for this department.
New to this department? Read this page top to bottom, then explore the sub-pages in the sidebar.
👤 Department Lead: [Name or TBD]
🔄 Last Updated: [Month Year]
```

---

## 📖 What to Add to Every SOP Page

SOPs should always start with:

```
⚙️ What is this SOP?
[One line: This SOP explains how to...]
👤 Who should follow this: [Role]
⏱️ Estimated time: [e.g. 30 minutes]
🔄 Last reviewed: [Month Year]
```

---

## 🗃️ Promote the all_pages_index

The all_pages_index database already exists and tracks every page by category, department, status, and owner. Most team members will never find it. We need to:

1. Link to it prominently from the Home Dashboard
2. Mention it in the "Start Here" page
3. Rename it to something more human: **"🗃️ Master Pages Index"**
4. Ensure it has entries for all key pages

---

## 🎓 Newcomer Checklist (add to Start Here page)

This is a simple toggle block or checklist that a new member can work through:

```
📋 Your Onboarding Checklist
□ Read "What is Nivy?" section above
□ Identify your division/department
□ Open your department’s Teamspace
□ Read the Overview page for your department
□ Check the all_pages_index to understand what exists
□ Read at least one SOP relevant to your role
□ Say hi to your team lead
```

## 🚊 Quick Navigation Banners

At the top of each teamspace home page, we add a coloured callout banner that links to the 3-4 most used pages. Example for Nivy Global:

```
📌 Quick Links:
• 💡 What is Nivy Global?
• 📈 Strategy & Plans
• 🤝 Partnerships & Sales
• ⚖️ Official Documents
```

This means anyone landing anywhere can immediately navigate to the right place.
