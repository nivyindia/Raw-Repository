# **PHASE 1: HANDOVER RECEIPT & INITIAL REVIEW**

### **Objective**

Receive verified buyer-supplier match from Growth & Qualification Lead and ensure all information is complete, accurate, and UAE-compliant before deal negotiation.

---

## **Step 1.1: Receive Handover**

**Inputs Required:**

- Buyer & Supplier contact details
- Verified documents (trade license, VAT certificate, ISO/ESMA certifications)
- Product details, MOQ, timeline, payment terms
- ICP fit & scoring

**Tasks:**

- Download CRM handover package
- Verify all required files are attached
- Check completeness of lead profile

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All required documents & info present | ✅ Yes → proceed Step 1.2 | ❌ Missing critical info → request clarification from Growth Lead |
| Lead profile complete (contact info, product, timeline, MOQ) | ✅ Yes → proceed Step 1.2 | ❌ Missing mandatory data → request clarification or put on hold |
| ICP alignment indicated | ✅ Yes → proceed Step 1.2 | ❌ ICP not aligned → put on hold / reject |

---

## **Step 1.2: Initial Compliance Check**

**Tasks:**

- Verify authenticity of trade license (DED / Freezone portal)
- Verify VAT registration (Federal Tax Authority portal)
- Verify ISO / ESMA / local certifications (if required for product)
- Confirm ICP alignment, product, MOQ, timeline

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trade license valid & active | ✅ Yes → proceed Phase 2 | ❌ Invalid / expired → reject / return to Growth Lead |
| VAT registration valid (if required) | ✅ Yes → proceed Phase 2 | ❌ Invalid → follow-up; reject if unresolved |
| ISO / ESMA / product certifications valid | ✅ Yes → proceed Phase 2 | ❌ Invalid or missing → request correction; reject if unresolved |
| ICP alignment matches Phase 6 verification | ✅ Yes → proceed Phase 2 | ❌ ICP mismatch → hold / return to Growth Lead |
| Product, MOQ, timeline feasible | ✅ Yes → proceed Phase 2 | ❌ Any mismatch → hold / reject |

# **PHASE 2: DEAL FINALIZATION / NEGOTIATION**

### **Objective**

Negotiate and confirm deal terms with buyer & supplier, ensuring UAE-specific compliance, pricing, payment, and logistics feasibility.

---

## **Step 2.1: Schedule Deal Discussion**

**Tasks:**

- Arrange meeting/call with buyer & supplier (Sun–Thu, 9 AM–6 PM UAE time)
- Confirm both parties can attend

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Both parties confirm availability | ✅ Yes → Step 2.2 | ❌ One/both unavailable → reschedule once; if repeated → tag as “On Hold” |
| Meeting scheduled & recorded in CRM | ✅ Yes → Step 2.2 | ❌ Not recorded → must record before proceeding |

---

## **Step 2.2: Confirm Pricing & Payment Terms**

**Tasks:**

- Finalize price (AED / FOB / CIF)
- Confirm payment method: Advance / LC / 30–60 day credit

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Both parties agree on price | ✅ Yes → Step 2.3 | ❌ Price rejected → renegotiate; fail deal if unresolved |
| Both parties agree on payment terms | ✅ Yes → Step 2.3 | ❌ Payment terms rejected → renegotiate; fail deal if unresolved |
| Terms documented in CRM | ✅ Yes → Step 2.3 | ❌ Not documented → follow up until complete |

---

## **Step 2.3: Confirm Delivery Timeline & Logistics**

**Tasks:**

- Validate supplier can meet agreed timeline
- Confirm UAE logistics: local shipping, customs clearance

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supplier confirms capacity to deliver on time | ✅ Yes → Step 2.4 | ❌ Supplier cannot deliver → renegotiate or fail deal |
| UAE logistics feasible (delivery, customs, port clearance) | ✅ Yes → Step 2.4 | ❌ Logistics not feasible → renegotiate or fail deal |
| Timeline documented | ✅ Yes → Step 2.4 | ❌ Not documented → follow up before proceeding |

---

## **Step 2.4: Deal Agreement Documentation**

**Tasks:**

- Prepare deal confirmation (PO, signed email, or contract)
- Attach all supporting documents (buyer & supplier info, verified docs, agreed terms)
- Store in CRM

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agreement signed by both parties | ✅ Yes → proceed to Phase 3 | ❌ Not signed → follow up; fail deal if unresolved |
| Agreement contains price, payment, delivery, product, ICP details | ✅ Yes → proceed to Phase 3 | ❌ Missing mandatory fields → request completion |
| All documents attached to CRM | ✅ Yes → proceed Phase 3 | ❌ Missing docs → attach before proceeding |
