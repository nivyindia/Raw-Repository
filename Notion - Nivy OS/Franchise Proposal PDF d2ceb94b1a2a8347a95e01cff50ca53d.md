### **Supporting Legal Document: The Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD)**

Remember, in the U.S., the **Franchise Proposal** is a marketing document, but the **FDD** is the legal document that provides the detailed facts. Your proposal should always be consistent with your FDD. The FDD contains the 23 Items, including:

- **Item 1:** The Franchisor and Any Parents, Predecessors, and Affiliates.
- **Item 5:** Initial Fees.
- **Item 6:** Other Fees (Royalties, Ad Fund).
- **Item 7:** Estimated Initial Investment.
- **Item 19:** Financial Performance Representation (Optional).
- **Item 20:** Outlets and Franchisee Information.
- **Item 21:** Financial Statements of the Franchisor.

**Final Recommendation:** Before finalizing your franchise proposal and FDD, it is **critical** to engage a qualified franchise attorney to ensure all documents are legally compliant and protect your agency.
