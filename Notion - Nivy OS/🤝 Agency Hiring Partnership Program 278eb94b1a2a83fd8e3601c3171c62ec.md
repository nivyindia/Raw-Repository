# 🤝 Agency Hiring Partnership Program

## Agency Onboarding – Complete Preparation Checklist

### 1️⃣ Legal & Business Setup

- Registered business entity (CIN / GST / Trade License / local equivalents)
- NDA template for agencies
- Partnership / Collaboration Agreement template
- Founder Control & Governance Clauses (decision-making, exit, replacement)
- IP / Confidentiality clauses
- Revenue attribution & payment terms clauses

### 2️⃣ Agency Screening & Application

- Google Form / Online Application (to capture):
    - Agency type / domain
    - Years of operation
    - Team size & structure
    - Services offered & core expertise
    - Tools & technology used
    - Founder / POC details
    - Portfolio / case studies / website
    - Business registration proof
    - International experience
    - Multi-select options for services and tools
    - Auto filter for minimum eligibility (years, projects, team size, international exposure)

### 3️⃣ Contribution & Responsibility Framework

- Partner Contribution Matrix (Common + Domain-specific contributions)
- Minimum contributions required from each agency
- Rules: ongoing contribution mandatory, one-time contributions clarified
- Examples of contributions: leads, clients, referrals, data, execution, case studies, tools, talent, market access

### 4️⃣ Internal Operations & Control

- Partner Access & Power Matrix (who sees what)
- Governance framework: decision rights, approval flow, escalation
- Program Manager roles & responsibilities
- Reporting & KPI tracking system
- CRM / tools access & permissions for agencies

### 5️⃣ SOPs & Operational Documents

- Standard Operating Procedures (per domain)
    - Lead Generation / Data Scraping SOP
    - Email Outreach / Campaign SOP
    - Sales / Closing SOP
    - Paid Ads / Social Media SOP
    - SEO / Content / Creative SOP
    - Communication SOP (internal & external)
    - Trial Project SOP for new partners
- Delivery tracking templates

### 6️⃣ Revenue & Payment Systems

- Revenue Attribution Logic (how project money is split)
- Payment schedule & thresholds
- Scenarios-based split tables (different lead sources, execution partners, contribution types)
- Legal clauses for revenue disputes

### 7️⃣ Proposal & Partner Pitch Documents

- Agency Partnership Proposal Template (email + PDF version)
- Service Offering Decks / Brochure
- Case Studies & Success Stories for marketing & onboarding
- Brand Guidelines for partner use

### 8️⃣ Onboarding & Training

- Onboarding checklist for new agency
- Orientation for systems, SOPs, tools, reporting
- Assigned Program Manager / Point of Contact
- Trial project assignment with scoring

### 9️⃣ Monitoring & Scaling

- Partner performance scoring (Gold / Silver / Bronze)
- Lead contribution tracking & quality scoring
- Continuous feedback & escalation mechanism
- Expansion plan: regional / country / state-wise coverage
- Top agency mentoring / controlling smaller agencies

### 10️⃣ Additional Items to Add

### 1️⃣ Legal / Risk Management

- Dispute Resolution SOP (how conflicts between partners are handled)
- Liability clauses for data breaches, IP misuse, or client mishandling
- Force Majeure / exit conditions for sudden partner dropouts

### 2️⃣ Quality Control & Standards

- Partner quality audit checklist (periodic review of outputs)
- Standardized KPIs per domain (emails sent, leads generated, conversions, etc.)
- Feedback / correction loop for failed or substandard work

### 3️⃣ Tech & Automation

- Centralized dashboard for tracking agency contributions, deliveries, and revenue splits
- Automated notifications for missed contributions or deadlines
- Version control for SOPs & templates

### 4️⃣ Marketing & Brand Alignment

- Partner brand usage guidelines (how they can show they work with your company)
- Co-marketing SOP (case studies, joint campaigns, cross-promotion)

### 5️⃣ Strategic Scaling

- Regional / Tier-wise partner expansion roadmap
- Mentorship / top-agency oversight program for smaller partners
- Global client acquisition strategy aligned with partner network

### 6️⃣ Retention & Motivation

- Recognition / reward framework for high-performing partners
- Long-term incentive or bonus programs tied to contribution & quality
