# **3. Retainer vs Project Price**

### **Retainer Model Advantages**

- Predictable revenue
- Higher LTV
- Stronger relationships
- Flexible delivery

**Recommended Retainer Duration:**

→ 3–6 month minimum contracts

---

### **Project Model Advantages**

- Good for cash flow
- Entry point for new clients
- Helps close unsure buyers

### **Balanced Strategy**

- Use projects to acquire clients
- Convert them to retainers using a 45–60 day upgrade system
- Offer 15–20% discount if they switch to retainer

---

# **4. Seasonal Pricing Changes**

Marketing budgets shift across the year, especially in Western markets.

### **Seasonal Demand**

- **Q1 (Jan–Mar):** Big brand budgets → premium pricing
- **Q2 (Apr–Jun):** Medium pricing → test new packages
- **Q3 (Jul–Sep):** Lowest demand → discounts + audits + entry offers
- **Q4 (Oct–Dec):** MOST DEMAND (Black Friday + Xmas + Q4 growth) → surge pricing

### **Seasonal Strategy**

- Offer **limited-time bundles** during Q3
- Add **priority pricing** during Q4
- Increase **retainer minimums** in peak seasons
- For audits: raise price in Q4 (high competition season)

# **5. A/B Testing Monthly**

Every month A/B test:

### **(A) Pricing Variants**

- $497 vs $597 vs $697
- $997 vs $1,200 vs $1,500
- $2,500 vs $3,500

Goal:

→ Find the psychological sweet spot for conversion + margin.

---

### **(B) Bundles**

- Ads + CRO vs Ads + Content
- SEO + Content vs SEO + PR
- Social + Ads vs Social + Email

---

### **(C) Free vs Paid Trials**

- Paid audit vs free audit
- Free setup vs paid setup
- 30-day test project

---

### **(D) Monthly vs Quarterly Contracts**

- Test if clients prefer:
    - Monthly flexible
    - 3-month locked discount
    - 6-month premium service

Track:

- Close rates
- Upsell potential
- Refunds
- Churn rate
- Average client LTV
