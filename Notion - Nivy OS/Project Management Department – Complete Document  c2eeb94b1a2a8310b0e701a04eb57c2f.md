# Project Management Department – Complete Document Checklist

# 

### **1. Strategy & Planning**

1. Project Management Strategy / Department Plan
2. Project Portfolio Overview (all ongoing and upcoming projects)
3. Project Prioritization & Selection Guidelines
4. Resource Allocation & Capacity Planning Documents
5. Department Goals & KPIs (on-time delivery, budget adherence, client satisfaction)

---

### **2. Project Initiation**

1. Project Charter Templates
2. Scope of Work (SOW) Templates
3. Project Proposal & Approval SOPs
4. Client Requirements Gathering Template
5. Risk Assessment / Feasibility Study Templates

---

### **3. Project Execution & Processes**

1. Project Plan / Gantt Chart Templates
2. Task Assignment & Workflow SOPs
3. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for Project Delivery
4. Milestone Tracking Templates
5. Change Request & Scope Management SOP

---

### **4. Resource & Team Management**

1. Team Roles & Responsibility Guidelines
2. Resource Utilization / Allocation Templates
3. Project Communication Plan Templates
4. Task Prioritization & Escalation SOPs
5. Training & Onboarding Checklist for Project Team Members

---

### **5. Quality & Risk Management**

1. Quality Assurance / Review SOPs
2. Risk Management & Mitigation Plans
3. Issue Tracking & Resolution Templates
4. Compliance & Audit Guidelines

---

### **6. Reporting & Monitoring**

1. Project Status Report Templates (weekly, monthly)
2. Dashboard Templates (progress, budget, milestones, KPIs)
3. Post-Mortem / Lessons Learned Templates
4. Client Update & Communication Templates

---

### **7. Tools & Systems**

1. Project Management Software SOPs (Asana, Trello, Jira, ClickUp, etc.)
2. Reporting & Analytics Guidelines
3. Workflow Automation SOPs
4. Documentation of Integrations & Tools

---

### **8. Optional / Advanced**

1. Resource Pool & Backup Planning Documents
2. Vendor / Freelancer Management SOPs
3. Project Costing & Budget Tracking Templates
4. Strategic Project Evaluation & ROI Templates
5. Continuous Improvement / Process Optimization Log
