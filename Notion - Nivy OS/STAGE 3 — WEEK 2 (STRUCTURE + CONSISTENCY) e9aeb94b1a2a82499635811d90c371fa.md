# 💬 STEP 3 — BETTER CONVERSATIONS

---

## When someone replies:

Follow this flow:

1. Acknowledge
2. Ask 1–2 questions
3. Understand problem
4. Move toward call

---

👉 Keep messages:

- Short
- Clear
- Natural

# 🧠 STEP 4 — SIMPLE QUALIFICATION THINKING

Before booking call, check:

- Is this a real business?
- Do they need help?
- Are they interested?

---

👉 If NO → Don’t waste time
