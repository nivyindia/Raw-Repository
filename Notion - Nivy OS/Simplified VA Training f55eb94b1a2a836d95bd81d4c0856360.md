## 🧠 Key Principles

1. Execution > Training
2. Teach only when needed
3. Promote performers only
4. System runs on KPIs
5. One niche per VA initially
6. Follow-up drives results

A complete company roadmap that starts simple, expands intelligently, and scales into a full performance-driven organization.
