# Outreach Plan

Here’s a **complete semi-short roadmap** for reaching out and onboarding businesses to **Nivy Alliance**, including tools, platforms, and steps for **Tier-1 & Tier-2 countries**.

---

## **1️⃣ Strategy Overview**

Goal: Attract verified businesses and service providers, onboard them into a **reciprocal, high-intent ecosystem**.

Key Principle: **Give demand to receive demand** → only qualified members gain access.

---

## **2️⃣ Step-by-Step Reachout & Onboarding Process**

### **Step 1: Define ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)**

- **Tier-1 countries**: US, UK, Germany, France, Canada, Australia, Singapore
    - Mid-large companies, $100K+ outsourcing annually, professional service buyers, IT/marketing/logistics/consulting.
- **Tier-2 countries**: India, Brazil, Mexico, South Africa, Eastern Europe
    - SMBs, fast-growing companies, $50K+ outsourcing annually, ready for global partnerships.

---

### **Step 2: Collect Prospects**

**Sources / Platforms / Tools:**

| Type | Tools / Platforms | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LinkedIn** | Sales Navigator, LinkedIn Groups, company pages | Find executives, decision-makers, join relevant groups |
| **WhatsApp** | WhatsApp Business, Broadcast Lists | Direct outreach, follow-ups, small group invites |
| **Email** | Hunter.io, Apollo.io, Skrapp | Collect verified business emails |
| **Company Databases** | Crunchbase, Angel.co, Clutch, ZoomInfo | Identify target companies |
| **Social Media** | Twitter, Facebook (business pages), Instagram | Soft outreach, brand visibility |
| **Job Boards / Forums** | Indeed, Glassdoor, Stack Overflow, local startup forums | Identify companies hiring services (signal of outsourcing needs) |
| **Communities** | Slack/Discord groups for startups & SMBs | Build relationships, soft invite |

---

### **Step 3: Initial Contact**

- **Tier-1**: Personalized LinkedIn messages, professional emails, verified WhatsApp voice notes
- **Tier-2**: Mix of LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and local social media channels

**Message Type**:

- Introduce **Nivy Alliance**, emphasize **trusted introductions & reciprocal participation**
- Invite them to **submit application or schedule verification call**

---

### **Step 4: Qualification**

- Check:
    - Company legitimacy
    - Outsourcing budget / procurement activity
    - Decision-maker availability
    - Alignment with ICP

Tools: LinkedIn, company website, internal checklist, Zoom/Teams call

---

### **Step 5: Onboarding**

1. **Share Nivy Alliance Overview** (landing page or PDF)
2. **Collect First Requirement** via Google Form / WhatsApp / Email
3. **Verification Call** (15–20 min) to confirm ICP fit
4. **Admin Approval & Group Access**
    - Buyer’s details hidden until qualified match
    - Introduce reciprocal rules and gamification levels

---

### **Step 6: Post-Onboarding Engagement**

- Weekly updates / nudges for new requirements
- Encourage contribution for **higher access levels**
- Track interactions in Google Sheet / CRM / Notion dashboard
- Share success stories & testimonials

---

## **3️⃣ Key Channels per Tier**

| Channel Type | Tier-1 Countries | Tier-2 Countries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LinkedIn Groups** | B2B, Marketing, SaaS, Procurement, Industry-specific groups | Local business, startup, SME groups |
| **WhatsApp** | Direct invites, broadcast updates, curated discussion groups | Same + regional business networks |
| **Email** | Personalized, automation via Apollo, HubSpot, Mailshake | Same + regional email lists |
| **Social Media** | Twitter, LinkedIn company pages, Instagram | Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, local forums |
| **Community Forums** | Slack & Discord for corporates, professional networks | Startup & SME forums, regional business boards |

---

## **4️⃣ Recommended Tools / Softwares**

- **CRM & Tracking:** HubSpot, Zoho, Notion, Google Sheets dashboards
- **Outreach Automation:** Apollo.io, Lemlist, Mailshake
- **Verification & Research:** LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Crunchbase, Clutch, Hunter.io
- **Communication:** WhatsApp Business, Zoom / Teams / Google Meet, Gmail / Outlook
- **Documentation & Forms:** Google Forms / Typeform, Notion SOPs

---

### ✅ **Quick Checklist for Execution**

1. Define ICP & segmentation per Tier
2. Collect prospects via LinkedIn, WhatsApp, email, databases
3. Initial contact → introduce Alliance → invite to apply
4. Qualification → check budget, company legitimacy, outsourcing needs
5. Verification → collect requirement → schedule call
6. Admin approves → add to Nivy Alliance → start controlled introductions
7. Post-onboarding engagement → feedback loop → gamification progression
