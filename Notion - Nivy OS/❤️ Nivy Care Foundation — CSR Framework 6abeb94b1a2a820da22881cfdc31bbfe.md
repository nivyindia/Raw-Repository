## 🌱 Our Commitments

Nivy Care Foundation is built on three non-negotiable principles:

1. **Measurable Impact** — Every programme has clear KPIs. If we can't measure it, we can't improve it.
2. **Community-First** — We listen before we build. Partners lead, we support.
3. **Transparent Reporting** — Annual impact reports, published and shared publicly.
