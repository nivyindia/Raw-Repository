## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nivy Growth is a global B2B client acquisition and business intelligence agency. It operates across 50 countries by deploying outreach systems, inbound lead funnels, and trained business development employees to identify decision-makers, gather business intelligence, and convert them into long-term retainer clients for Nivy's accounting, digital marketing, and advisory services.

**Why Global Now:**

- India saturates fast — international clients pay 5–30x more per retainer
- A single UK/US/UAE client = 10–15 Indian clients in revenue
- Automation makes multi-country outreach feasible with a lean team
- English-speaking markets have high trust in Indian service providers

**Core Value Proposition:**

- For accounting clients: Save money on compliance, GST/VAT, tax planning
- For marketing clients: Get more leads, clients, and revenue
- For Nivy: Predictable retainer revenue from 50 countries simultaneously

## 7. COMPLETE SOP

### Daily SOP — BD Executive

**Morning (9–11am)**

- [ ]  Pull 50 new contacts from Apollo/LinkedIn/directories into CRM
- [ ]  Launch outreach sequences for today's batch (automated via Instantly/WATI)
- [ ]  Review all replies from previous 24 hours
- [ ]  Book discovery calls for hot leads via Calendly
- [ ]  Post 1 LinkedIn B2B content piece

**Afternoon (12–3pm)**

- [ ]  Conduct discovery calls (use SOP script below)
- [ ]  Log call notes and action items in Notion
- [ ]  Send proposals within 2 hours of positive call
- [ ]  Score and update lead status in HubSpot

**Evening (4–6pm)**

- [ ]  Follow up with Day 2/7 sequences manually if needed
- [ ]  Update daily metrics dashboard
- [ ]  Prepare tomorrow's prospect list (50 contacts)
- [ ]  Submit EOD report to manager

### Discovery Call SOP (5-Step Framework)

1. **Situation** — "Tell me about your current setup — do you have an accountant/agency already?"
2. **Problem** — "What's your biggest challenge right now — leads, compliance, or growth?"
3. **Implication** — "If that stays unsolved for 6 months, what happens to the business?"
4. **Case Study** — Present the most relevant client result for their industry/country
5. **Next Step** — Offer the free audit → move to proposal within 48 hours
