## **Case Study 1 — La Petite Alice (Sustainable Apparel E‑Commerce)**

**Website:** [https://lapetitealice.com/](https://lapetitealice.com/)

### 📍 Business Overview

La Petite Alice is a family‑run boutique selling handmade linen clothing with an ethic of sustainability and slow fashion.

### 🧠 Challenge

- Visitors were engaging with brand visuals but not easily converting to sales.
- Product discovery was limited by unclear navigation and lack of filtering features.

### 🛠 UX & CRO Solution

- **Navigation Simplification:** Added a hierarchical category structure with “Shop by Collection” and product visual cues.
- **Hero CTA Optimization:** Reframed hero messaging with clearer value proposition and targeted “Shop Now” buttons.
- **Mobile‑First Redesign:** Improved responsiveness and touch targets to boost engagement on mobile.

### 📈 Hypothetical Impact (What You Can Show Clients)

- **Estimated +35% increase in product engagement** due to streamlined navigation.
- **Lower drop‑off rates** on product category pages.
- **Improved mobile session duration** with faster, easy browsing.

### 📸 Elements to Include

- Homepage before/after (wireframe)
- Navigation flow chart
- Product category layouts

> Why this matters: Aligning aesthetics with UX patterns improves both trust and conversions — important for lifestyle brands like this one.

## **Case Study 4 — Bar Kismet (Restaurant & Reservations)**

*(Referenced as strong example of UX design for hospitality)*

### 📍 Business Overview

Bar Kismet is a restaurant site focusing on menu engagement and reservations, using visuals to convey atmosphere.

### 🧠 Challenge

- Reservation call‑to‑action (CTA) was buried below the fold.
- Key information (menus, hours, location) wasn’t immediately discoverable on mobile.

### 🛠 UX & CRO Solution

- **Sticky Reservation CTA:** Always‑visible “Book a Table” CTA in header and footer.
- **Menu Prioritization:** Consolidated menus into one easy‑to‑scroll page with anchor navigation.
- **Mobile First Layout:** Large tap targets and clean spacing for mobile diners.

### 📈 Hypothetical Impact

- **+22% increase in online reservations**
- Lower bounce on key landing pages
- Easier mobile access to high‑value actions

### 📸 Elements to Include

- Before/after CTA placement
- Mobile navigation
- Menu layout UX

> Why this matters: Hospitality sites often convert most when high‑value actions like reservations are frictionless.
