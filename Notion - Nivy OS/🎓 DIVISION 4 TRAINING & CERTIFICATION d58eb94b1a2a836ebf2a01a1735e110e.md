> "7.7 COMPREHENSIVE LEARNING & CERTIFICATION PROGRAM
> 
> 
> **7.7.1 Mandatory Certification Framework:**
> All Partner employees must complete Company's Learning Management System (LMS) certifications according to the following schedule:

> - Data Security & Privacy
> - Client Communication Standards
> - CRM System Proficiency
> - Quality Assurance Protocols
> - Remote Work Excellence
> - Compliance & Ethics
> - Brand Representation

> **7.7.2 Certification Completion Requirements:**
> (a) **All Employees:** Foundation Level within 30 days of start
> (b) **Client-Facing Roles:** Intermediate Level within 90 days
> (c) **Management Roles:** Advanced Level within 180 days
> (d) **Specialized Roles:** Relevant specialization within 120 days

> - Continued non-compliance: Termination of Agreement"
