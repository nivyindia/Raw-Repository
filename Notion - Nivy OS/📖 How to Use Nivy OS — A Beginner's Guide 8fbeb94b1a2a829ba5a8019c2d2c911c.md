# 📖 How to Use Nivy OS — A Beginner's Guide

> 🟢 **Status: Live** | Owner: Operations Lead | Last Updated: April 2026
> 

This guide is for anyone who is new to Nivy or new to Notion. It explains **what Nivy OS is, what each database does, and when to use each one** — in plain English, no tech experience needed.

---

## 🧠 What is Nivy OS?

Nivy OS stands for **Nivy Operating System**. Just like your phone or laptop has an operating system (iOS, Android, Windows) that runs everything in the background, Nivy OS is the system that organises all of Nivy's information.

It is a collection of **8 databases** — think of each database as a well-organised filing cabinet for a specific type of information.

You don't need to understand all 8 databases on Day 1. Just know where to go when you need something.

---

## 🗂️ The 8 Databases — Plain English Explanations

### 1. 🏢 departments_database

**What it is:** A directory of every department inside Nivy.

**What's inside:** Department name, head of department, goals, KPIs, linked SOPs, linked projects.

**When to use it:** When you need to understand what a specific department does, who leads it, or what projects/SOPs belong to it.

**Example:** *"I need to know who leads the Marketing department and what their KPIs are."*

---

### 2. 🚀 projects_database

**What it is:** A tracker for all projects happening across every Nivy division.

**What's inside:** Project name, status, owner, deadline, linked department, notes.

**When to use it:** When you want to check the status of a project, find what projects are active, or add a new project.

**Example:** *"What projects is the Sales team currently working on?"*

---

### 3. 📋 sop_database

**What it is:** All Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) — step-by-step guides for how we do specific tasks.

**What's inside:** SOP title, department, status, owner, linked processes.

**When to use it:** When you need to know **how to do something** at Nivy — onboarding, sending proposals, running campaigns, etc.

**Example:** *"How do we onboard a new client? There should be an SOP for that."*

---

### 4. 📁 company_documents_database

**What it is:** A library of all official Nivy company documents.

**What's inside:** Contracts, agreements, policies, official letters, annexures.

**When to use it:** When you need an official document — a contract template, a policy, or a signed agreement.

**Example:** *"I need the Service Agreement template to send to a client."*

---

### 5. 📊 reports_database

**What it is:** A collection of all reports generated across the company.

**What's inside:** Sales reports, marketing reports, financial reports, operational summaries.

**When to use it:** When you need historical data, performance numbers, or to review past reports.

**Example:** *"What were our sales numbers last quarter?"*

---

### 6. 📌 templates_database

**What it is:** A library of reusable templates for common tasks.

**What's inside:** Proposal templates, SOP templates, email templates, report formats, meeting note formats.

**When to use it:** Before creating any document from scratch — check here first. There's likely already a template.

**Example:** *"I need to write a client proposal. Let me grab the proposal template."*

---

### 7. 📈 analytics_database

**What it is:** A database for tracking business analytics and performance metrics.

**What's inside:** Campaign performance data, traffic analytics, conversion data.

**When to use it:** When you need to review business performance numbers or add new analytics data.

**Example:** *"How is our website traffic performing this month?"*

---

### 8. 🗃️ all_pages_index

**What it is:** A master index of **every single page** in the Nivy workspace.

**What's inside:** Every page name, its category (Business Plan, Department, SOP, Project, etc.), status, owner, and parent section.

**When to use it:** When you're looking for a specific page and can't find it, or when you want to see an overview of everything that exists.

**Example:** *"I can't find the Finance Overview page. Let me search the all_pages_index."*

---

## 🔑 Golden Rules for Using Nivy OS

1. **Before creating a new document**, check the templates_database — don't reinvent the wheel
2. **Before asking someone where something is**, check the all_pages_index first
3. **When you create a new page**, add it to the all_pages_index so others can find it
4. **When you finish a project**, update its status in the projects_database
5. **When a process changes**, update the relevant SOP in the sop_database

---

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I need to create a new SOP. Where do I add it?**

A: Add it directly inside the sop_database. Use the SOP template from templates_database.

**Q: I finished a project. What should I update?**

A: Mark it as complete in the projects_database. Also update the all_pages_index status if needed.

**Q: I can't find the page I'm looking for.**

A: Open all_pages_index and search. Or press Ctrl/Cmd + P in Notion to search the whole workspace.

**Q: Who manages Nivy OS?**

A: The Operations Lead owns Nivy OS. For questions, reach out to them directly.
