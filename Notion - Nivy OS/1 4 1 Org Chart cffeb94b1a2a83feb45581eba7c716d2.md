## **1.4.1 ORGANIZATIONAL CHART — DIGITAL MARKETING AGENCY (ENTERPRISE & FRANCHISE MODEL)**

### 🏛 **TOP MANAGEMENT**

- **Founder & CEO** — Vision, strategy, culture, and expansion leadership
- **Chief Operating Officer (COO)** — Oversees daily operations and inter-departmental coordination
- **Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)** — Branding, marketing strategy, and creative growth
- **Chief Sales Officer (CSO)** — Sales operations, partnerships, and franchise revenue
- **Chief Delivery Officer (CDO)** — Ensures quality, timely, and scalable service delivery
- **Chief Technology Officer (CTO)** — Automation, software systems, and tech stack
- **Chief Financial Officer (CFO)** — Finance, accounting, audits, and partner royalties
- **Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)** — People, hiring, learning, and culture

---

### 🌍 **GLOBAL STRUCTURE**

- **Global HQ**
    - CEO, C-Level Team, Strategy & Legal Advisors
    - Global Operations Head
    - Global Marketing & Brand Office
    - Master Franchise Director
    - Partner & Investor Relations Team
- **Country HQs**
    - Country Manager
    - Finance & Legal Officer
    - Country-level Sales & Marketing Lead
    - Regional Franchise Coordinator
- **Regional Partners / Master Franchisees**
    - Regional Director
    - Franchise Business Manager
    - Local Sales Head
    - Delivery Coordinator
    - HR/Recruitment Partner

---

### 🧩 **CORE DEPARTMENTS**

### 1️⃣ OPERATIONS DEPARTMENT

**Head:** COO / Global Operations Head

**Teams:**

- Operations Managers (Global, Country, Regional)
- PMO (Project Management Office)
- Quality Assurance & Workflow Optimization Team
- Admin & Legal Support Staff
- SOP & Compliance Documentation Team

**Functions:**

- Maintain operational SOPs
- Define project timelines and deliverables
- Optimize internal automation workflows
- Standardize tools and software usage across countries

---

### 2️⃣ MARKETING DEPARTMENT

**Head:** CMO

**Sub-units:**

- **Brand & Strategy Team**
    - Brand Manager
    - Marketing Strategist
    - Product Marketing Specialist
- **Creative Team**
    - Graphic Designers
    - Video Editors
    - Copywriters & Content Writers
- **Digital Marketing Team**
    - SEO / SEM Specialists
    - Paid Media Managers (Google, Meta, LinkedIn)
    - Email & Automation Specialists
- **Analytics & Reporting**
    - Data Analysts
    - Campaign Performance Reviewers
    - ROAS & KPI Dashboard Maintainers

**Functions:**

- Manage brand presence, campaigns, and storytelling
- Run and optimize multi-channel ad campaigns
- Maintain performance dashboards and ROI analytics

---

### 3️⃣ SALES DEPARTMENT

**Head:** CSO

**Sub-units:**

- **Master Sales Partners**
    - Regional Sales Managers
    - Territory Development Executives
    - Partner Success Coordinators
- **Franchise & Channel Sales**
    - Franchise Business Managers
    - Affiliate / Reseller Program Leads
    - CRM & Pipeline Team
- **Inside Sales & Lead Nurturing**
    - SDRs (Sales Development Reps)
    - Account Executives
    - Client Acquisition Managers

**Functions:**

- Acquire clients & franchise partners
- Maintain lead pipelines and CRM tracking
- Oversee partner onboarding & enablement
- Manage revenue targets & commission structures

---

### 4️⃣ SERVICE DELIVERY DEPARTMENT

**Head:** CDO

**Sub-units:**

- **Performance Marketing Team**
    - SEO Specialists
    - Ads Managers
    - Analytics & Conversion Experts
- **Content & Social Media**
    - Social Media Managers
    - Content Strategists
    - Copywriters
- **Web & Automation**
    - Web Developers
    - UI/UX Designers
    - CRM Integration Experts
- **Video & Branding**
    - Video Editors
    - Brand Identity Designers
- **Client Success & QA**
    - Client Success Managers
    - Quality Reviewers
    - Feedback & Retention Team

**Functions:**

- Deliver all digital marketing services
- Ensure quality, timely execution, and client satisfaction
- Continuously optimize service SOPs and delivery timelines

---

### 5️⃣ TECHNOLOGY DEPARTMENT

**Head:** CTO

**Sub-units:**

- **Infrastructure & Security**
    - System Administrators
    - Network Engineers
    - Cybersecurity Team
- **Software Development**
    - Full Stack Developers
    - QA Testers
    - DevOps Engineers
- **Automation & Product Innovation**
    - AI Workflow Engineers
    - Product Managers
    - No-Code App Builders

**Functions:**

- Manage internal tools, tech integrations, and Nextcloud/CRM infrastructure
- Build automation systems and dashboards
- Maintain IT security and backups

---

### 6️⃣ FINANCE & LEGAL DEPARTMENT

**Head:** CFO

**Sub-units:**

- **Finance & Accounting**
    - Accountants
    - Auditors
    - Taxation Officers
- **Partner & Franchise Finance**
    - Royalty Management
    - Financial Forecasting
    - Payout & Revenue Share Processing
- **Legal & Compliance**
    - Legal Advisors
    - Contract Management Team
    - Risk & Audit Analysts

**Functions:**

- Maintain budgets, P&L, and franchise payments
- Oversee contracts, NDAs, and global compliance
- Manage internal audits and risk frameworks

---

### 7️⃣ HUMAN RESOURCES DEPARTMENT

**Head:** CHRO

**Sub-units:**

- **Recruitment & Onboarding**
    - Talent Acquisition Team
    - HR Coordinators
    - Orientation Trainers
- **L&D & Performance**
    - Skill Trainers
    - Learning Program Designers
    - Performance Review Committee
- **Culture & Engagement**
    - Events & Recognition Team
    - Wellness & Employee Relations Team
- **HR Operations**
    - Payroll Team
    - Attendance & Leave Tracking
    - Compliance & Documentation

**Functions:**

- Hire and train global workforce
- Maintain HR policies and employee records
- Promote wellness, growth, and retention

---

### 8️⃣ PARTNER NETWORK

**Head:** CSO / COO

**Sub-units:**

- **Master Franchisees** — Manage territories
- **Regional Partners** — Operate under master franchise
- **Affiliate / Reseller Partners** — Lead-based collaborations
- **Agency Collaborators** — White-label and outsourcing partners

**Functions:**

- Expand business footprint through partnerships
- Maintain franchise growth and KPIs
- Handle royalty reports, audits, and performance reviews

---

### 9️⃣ ADMINISTRATION & SUPPORT

**Head:** COO / Admin Head

**Sub-units:**

- Office Administration
- Procurement & Logistics
- Travel & Facility Management
- IT & General Support Staff

**Functions:**

- Ensure smooth backend operations and employee support
- Manage documentation, office assets, and vendor relations

---

### 📘 **Governance Framework**

- **Leadership Council:** CEO, C-Level, Country Managers
- **Decision Matrix (RACI):** Defines accountability for all departments
- **Communication Channels:**
    - Internal: Nextcloud, Slack, Notion
    - External: Email, CRM, Partner Portals
- **Meeting Cadence:**
    - Weekly Department Syncs
    - Monthly Leadership Review
    - Quarterly Business Review (QBR)
