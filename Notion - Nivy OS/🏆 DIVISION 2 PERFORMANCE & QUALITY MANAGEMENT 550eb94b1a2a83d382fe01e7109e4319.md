## **⚠️ RISK MITIGATION - DIVISION 2**

### **Performance Risks:**

- **Risk:** Partners may game the quality scoring system
- **Mitigation:** Multiple assessment methods, random audits, mystery shopping
- **Risk:** Graduated targets may still be too aggressive for some markets
- **Mitigation:** Market-specific adjustments, regular calibration, flexibility for exceptional circumstances

### **Quality Risks:**

- **Risk:** Subjectivity in quality scoring leads to disputes
- **Mitigation:** Clear rubrics, calibration training, appeal process, multiple assessors
- **Risk:** Client feedback fatigue or manipulation
- **Mitigation:** Survey rotation, verification methods, balanced scoring weights

### **Implementation Risks:**

- **Risk:** System complexity overwhelms partners
- **Mitigation:** Phased rollout, comprehensive training, dedicated support
- **Risk:** Data integration challenges between systems
- **Mitigation:** API-first approach, fallback manual processes, gradual automation
