17. **Signatures & Witness Section**
18. **Annexures**

• Territory map
• Onboarding checklist
• Sub-Partner structure

**Step 4. Legal Verification**
Before signing:
• Get it **reviewed by a legal consultant** (especially for international clauses).

After signing:
• Add the partner to your **onboarding system**.

    ◦ Marketing toolkit

### **ARTICLE 4: TERM AND TERMINATION**

**4.1. TERM OF AGREEMENT**

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of twelve (12) months ("Initial Term"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with the provisions of this Article 4.

**4.2. RENEWAL CONDITIONS**

**4.2.1.** This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month periods ("Renewal Terms") unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal to the other Party at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the then-current Term.

**4.2.2.** The renewal of this Agreement is contingent upon Master Partner meeting or exceeding at least eighty percent (80%) of all Key Performance Indicators specified in Annexure A during the preceding Term.

**4.2.3.** Company reserves the right to modify the terms and conditions, including compensation structure and performance metrics, for any Renewal Term by providing written notice to Master Partner at least forty-five (45) days prior to the expiration of the then-current Term.

**4.3. TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE**

Either Party may terminate this Agreement for any reason by providing thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party.

**4.4. TERMINATION FOR CAUSE**

Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice to the other Party if the other Party:

**4.4.1.** Materially breaches any provision of this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within ten (10) business days after receiving written notice specifying the breach in reasonable detail;

**4.4.2.** Becomes insolvent, files a petition for bankruptcy, has a receiver appointed for its business or assets, or ceases to conduct business in the normal course.

**4.5. IMMEDIATE TERMINATION FOR SPECIFIED BREACHES**

Notwithstanding Section 4.4, Company may terminate this Agreement immediately without providing a cure period upon occurrence of any of the following events:

**4.5.1.** Any breach by Master Partner of Sections 5.4 (Communication Protocol), 9 (Intellectual Property), 10 (Confidentiality), or 11 (Restrictive Covenants);

**4.5.2.** Consistent failure by Master Partner to meet the Key Performance Indicators as defined in Annexure A, constituting a pattern of underperformance;

**4.5.3.** Any direct contact or communication by Master Partner with Company's clients in violation of Section 5.4.2;

**4.5.4.** Any violation by Master Partner of the ethical standards or compliance requirements set forth in Annexure G or Annexure H.

**4.6. EFFECTS OF TERMINATION**

Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement for any reason:

**4.6.1.** Master Partner shall immediately cease all use of Company's Intellectual Property, including but not limited to trademarks, logos, and marketing materials;

**4.6.2.** Master Partner shall promptly return to Company or, at Company's option, destroy all Confidential Information in its possession or control;

**4.6.3.** Company shall pay Master Partner all earned but unpaid commissions through the effective date of termination, calculated in accordance with Annexure C;

**4.6.4.** Master Partner shall deliver to Company all completed and in-progress Work Product, including all related documentation and materials;

**4.6.5.** Master Partner shall provide Company with a complete list of all Sub-Partners and their current status.

**4.7. POST-TERMINATION ASSISTANCE AND TRANSITION**

**4.7.1.** Master Partner shall provide reasonable transition assistance to Company for a period not exceeding thirty (30) days following termination to ensure orderly transfer of services and relationships.

**4.7.2.** Such transition assistance shall include, but not be limited to:

(a) Completing or handing over all in-progress work;

(b) Providing comprehensive status reports on all active projects;

(c) Facilitating introduction and knowledge transfer to Company or its designee;

(d) Transferring all login credentials, access points, and administrative controls.

**4.7.3.** Company shall compensate Master Partner for transition services at the standard rates outlined in the most recent Project Brief or as otherwise agreed in writing.

**4.8. SURVIVAL OF PROVISIONS**

The following provisions shall survive any termination or expiration of this Agreement: Articles 4 (Term and Termination), 9 (Intellectual Property), 10 (Confidentiality), 11 (Restrictive Covenants), 12 (Liquidated Damages), 13 (Indemnification), 14 (Limitation of Liability), 15 (Governing Law and Dispute Resolution), and 16 (Miscellaneous Provisions).
