# 📊 Marketing Assets Checklist

Got it! To make **Nivy** appear as a **reputable, established, and “big” company**, you need a combination of **marketing assets, content, and proof of credibility**. These help clients instantly trust you, even if you’re still a small-to-medium agency. I’ll break it down systematically.

---

## **1️⃣ Brand Identity & Visuals**

Your brand must look professional and consistent across all touchpoints.

**Must-Have Assets:**

1. **Logo & Logo Variations** – Color, black/white, icon-only.
2. **Brand Style Guide** – Fonts, colors, tone of voice, imagery guidelines.
3. **Corporate Stationery** – Email signatures, business cards, letterhead, invoices.
4. **Website Design** – Clean, professional, mobile-friendly; shows services, team, portfolio, and client testimonials.
5. **Branded Social Media Templates** – For posts, ads, stories (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook).

---

## **2️⃣ Online Presence & Digital Proof**

Clients often research online. You need to **look big and established**:

**Must-Have Assets:**

1. **Website with Company Overview** – About us, mission, vision, services, portfolio.
2. **Client Portfolio / Case Studies** – Show successful projects, results, numbers, and logos (even small clients look better if presented professionally).
3. **Team Pages** – Professional photos, designations, and experience.
4. **Testimonials & Reviews** – From clients or partners.
5. **LinkedIn Company Page** – Active with posts, updates, and thought leadership.
6. **Google Business Profile / Local Listings** – Shows legitimacy, reviews, and contact info.
7. **Content Marketing** – Blogs, guides, videos that position you as an expert.

---

## **3️⃣ Marketing Collateral**

Physically or digitally shareable materials create trust.

**Must-Have Assets:**

1. **Company Brochure (PDF)** – Corporate-style, showing services, packages, process, team, portfolio.
2. **Service Decks / Pitch Decks** – Tailored for clients; visually strong, professional.
3. **Proposal & RFP Templates** – Ready-to-send professional documents.
4. **Case Study Templates** – Consistent format with problem → solution → results.
5. **Email Templates** – For outreach, follow-ups, and newsletters.
6. **One-Pagers for Services** – Quick overview documents for prospects.

---

## **4️⃣ Thought Leadership & Authority**

Make your company appear knowledgeable and influential.

**Must-Have Assets:**

1. **Blog / Knowledge Base** – Share insights on digital marketing, tax, accounting trends.
2. **LinkedIn Articles / Posts** – Educational, not salesy.
3. **Video Content / Explainers** – Short videos introducing services, showcasing expertise.
4. **Whitepapers or Guides** – Downloadable content showing depth of knowledge.
5. **Webinars / Online Workshops** – Establish authority in your niche.

---

## **5️⃣ Social Proof & Recognition**

Social proof makes small companies look big and trusted.

**Must-Have Assets:**

1. **Client Logos on Website** – Even small clients help.
2. **Testimonials & Case Studies** – Highlight measurable results.
3. **Certifications & Awards** – Google Partner, HubSpot, or any industry-specific certification.
4. **Media Mentions / PR** – Articles, podcasts, or guest posts.
5. **Team Credentials** – LinkedIn profiles, certifications, and experience.

---

## **6️⃣ Advertising & Lead Generation (Optional but Powerful)**

When prospects see paid ads and professional content, it reinforces credibility.

**Must-Have Assets:**

1. **LinkedIn Ads / Retargeting Campaigns** – Target businesses in your niche.
2. **Google Ads / Display Ads** – Professional, branded campaigns.
3. **Social Media Campaigns** – Consistent and visually high-quality.

---

## **7️⃣ Client Experience Materials**

Big companies appear organized, reliable, and polished.

**Must-Have Assets:**

1. **Onboarding Pack** – Welcome email, guide, process timeline.
2. **Project Templates** – Status updates, reporting, milestone trackers.
3. **Professional Invoices & Reports** – Well-designed and automated if possible.

---

### ⚡ **Summary: Marketing Materials to Appear Big**

1. Brand & Visuals → Logo, style guide, stationery, website, social templates.
2. Online Presence → Website, LinkedIn, portfolio, reviews, thought content.
3. Collateral → Brochures, decks, proposals, one-pagers.
4. Thought Leadership → Blogs, videos, guides, webinars.
5. Social Proof → Testimonials, logos, certifications, PR.
6. Ads & Lead Generation → LinkedIn, Google, social media campaigns.
7. Client Experience → Onboarding packs, reports, professional templates.
