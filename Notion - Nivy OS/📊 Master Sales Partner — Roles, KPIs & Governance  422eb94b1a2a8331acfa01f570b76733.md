## **2. Master Sales Partner — Roles & Responsibilities**

**Purpose:** To define what master partners will do regionally.

### A. Strategic Responsibilities

- Territory development & sub-partner recruitment
- Brand representation
- Building strategic local alliances

### B. Sales & Revenue

- Achieve sales targets (own + sub-partners)
- Lead allocation & conversion management
- Regional pricing control
- Revenue & reporting

### C. Training & Development

- Onboard & train sub-partners
- Help hire and train local employees
- Customize sales materials per region

### D. Marketing & Promotion

- Regional marketing & events
- Manage local visibility and co-branding

### E. Operations & Administration

- KPI tracking, CRM updates, communication with HQ
- Compliance & quality assurance

### F. Financial Management

- Commission handling & payout approvals
- Budgeting & profitability tracking

### G. Growth & Innovation

- Market research & regional expansion ideas
- Promote automation & digital tools

### H. Support & Retention

- Client success & feedback
- Conflict resolution among partners
