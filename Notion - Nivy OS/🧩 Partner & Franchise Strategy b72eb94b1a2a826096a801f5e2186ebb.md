# 🧩 Partner & Franchise Strategy

---

## 

This framework allows your **digital marketing agency** to expand globally by creating structured, revenue-sharing ecosystems — including **strategic partners, white-label agencies, and full-service franchises** under your brand system.

---

### 🔹 PURPOSE

To scale your agency’s operations and reach **without directly increasing internal overhead**, by empowering vetted partners and franchise owners to deliver services under your systems, branding, and standards.

---

### 🔹 PARTNER MODEL OVERVIEW

| Partner Type | Description | Revenue Share | Control Level | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Strategic Partner** | Brings leads or clients, shares revenue per project | 10–25% | Low | Freelancers, consultants |
| **White-label Partner** | Delivers your services under their brand using your backend team | 25–40% | Medium | Local agencies |
| **Regional Franchise Partner** | Operates under your brand in a defined geography | 40–60% | High | Country/state franchise |
| **Channel Partner** | Promotes your SaaS, tools, or systems (if any) | 10–20% | Low | B2B referral platforms |

---

### 🔹 FRANCHISE STRATEGY OVERVIEW

Your agency functions as a **“parent organization”**, providing:

- Standardized brand assets
- Operating SOPs & policies
- Training & onboarding system
- Marketing support
- Technology platform access (CRM, dashboards, etc.)

Franchise partners operate independently but must adhere to **quality, process, and reporting standards**.

---

### 🔹 FRANCHISE STRUCTURE FLOW

```
HQ (Parent Agency)
   ↓
Franchise Director (Internal)
   ↓
Regional Franchise Managers
   ↓
Local Franchise Units
   ↓
Client Accounts & Teams

```

---

### 🔹 PARTNER/FRANCHISE DEVELOPMENT STEPS

**PHASE 1 — Setup**

1. Define franchise model, pricing, and revenue-sharing structure.
2. Create “Franchise Starter Kit”:
    - Company profile deck
    - Service catalog
    - Operational SOPs
    - Training guide
3. Prepare onboarding system in Notion or LMS.

**PHASE 2 — Recruitment**

1. Identify qualified agencies or consultants.
2. Run digital campaigns to attract franchise inquiries.
3. Conduct interviews & capability assessments.

**PHASE 3 — Onboarding**

1. Sign agreements (legal, branding, confidentiality).
2. Provide access to CRM, project dashboards, and shared assets.
3. Train team on service delivery standards & reporting systems.

**PHASE 4 — Monitoring**

1. Monthly performance review via dashboards.
2. Franchise audit every quarter (sales, delivery, finance).
3. Provide continuous improvement feedback.
