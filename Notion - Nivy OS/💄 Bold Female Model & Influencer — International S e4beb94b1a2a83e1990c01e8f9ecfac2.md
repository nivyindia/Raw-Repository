## 💡 Instagram Algorithm Tips

- Post Reels 4-5x/week (they get 3x more reach than photos)
- Post feed photos 3-4x/week
- Stories DAILY (minimum 5 frames)
- Use all 3 content types: Reels, Carousels, Single photos
- Collab with brands using the Collab Post feature
- Use location tags on every post
- Go LIVE at least once a week
- Reply to every comment in first 60 minutes

# 🔍 10. GOOGLE DISCOVERABILITY STRATEGY

## Make Your Name & Brand Searchable on Google

1. **Personal website** at `[stagename].com`
    - H1: "[Name] | International Fashion Model & Influencer"
    - Meta title: "[Name] — International Model, Influencer & Brand Ambassador"
    - Meta description: "[Name] is a bold international fashion model and influencer. View portfolio, press, and brand collaboration info."
    - Include: portfolio gallery, press section, services, contact
2. **Google My Business** (if working independently with bookings)
3. **Press features**: get quoted or featured in fashion blogs, magazines, podcasts
4. **YouTube channel** — YouTube videos rank #1 on Google for many fashion/beauty searches
5. **Pinterest boards** — Pinterest pins rank on Google Image Search
6. **LinkedIn articles** — rank on Google within 48 hours
7. **Wikipedia / Wikidata** entry (once you hit major press milestones)
8. **IMDb** (if doing commercial/TV work)
