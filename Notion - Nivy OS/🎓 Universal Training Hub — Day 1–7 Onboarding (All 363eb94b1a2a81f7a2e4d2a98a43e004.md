# 🎓 Universal Training Hub — Day 1–7 Onboarding (All Brands)

> **Purpose:** Universal onboarding track for all new team members joining any Nivy brand. Day-by-day structure. Brand-specific modules are added after Day 7 in the relevant Brand OS.
> 

> Built: May 18, 2026 | Phase 5
> 

---

## 🎯 How to Use This Hub

- **New joiner:** Work through Day 1–7 in order
- **Manager:** Assign this page on Day 1, check in at end of Day 3 and Day 7
- **After Day 7:** Proceed to your brand-specific SOP Index and department hub
- **Questions:** Ask your manager — don't guess

---

## 📅 Day 1 — Orientation

**Goal:** Understand who Nivy is, what your role is, and set up your tools.

### Morning (2 hrs)

- [ ]  Read the Company Overview page (Nivy HQ > Company Overview)
- [ ]  Review the Brand Registry — understand all 10 brands and their status
- [ ]  Read the Naming Conventions & Versioning Guide
- [ ]  Understand the workspace structure: Nivy HQ > Global Systems > Brand OS > Research Lab

### Afternoon (2 hrs)

- [ ]  Set up all required tools (list provided by your manager)
- [ ]  Get access to Notion — confirm you can see your Brand OS section
- [ ]  Read your job description and confirm role responsibilities with your manager
- [ ]  Introduction call with your direct manager

**End of Day 1 check:** Can you navigate to Nivy HQ, find your brand, and find the SOP Index?

---

## 📅 Day 2 — Systems & Databases

**Goal:** Understand how the workspace databases work and how to log your work.

### Tasks

- [ ]  Read the tasks_database guide (how to create, assign, and update tasks)
- [ ]  Create your first task entry: "Day 2 Onboarding — [Your Name]"
- [ ]  Read the sop_database — find the 3 SOPs most relevant to your role
- [ ]  Review your department hub (linked from your Brand OS)
- [ ]  Understand the Research Pipeline: Research Inbox > Draft Zone > Review Queue

**End of Day 2 check:** Have you created a task in tasks_database and found your 3 most relevant SOPs?

---

## 📅 Day 3 — SOPs & Processes

**Goal:** Learn the specific SOPs for your role. Understand quality standards.

### Tasks

- [ ]  Read all SOPs tagged with your brand + department (filter in sop_database)
- [ ]  Shadow your manager or a senior team member for a live task
- [ ]  Read the Universal HR Policies (this page > Global Systems Hub > HR Policies)
- [ ]  Review the Code of Conduct
- [ ]  End-of-day check-in with manager (15 minutes)

**End of Day 3 check:** Can you explain the quality standard for your top 3 tasks?

---

## 📅 Day 4 — Tools & Workflows

**Goal:** Be operational on all tools required for your role.

### Tasks

- [ ]  Set up all tools on the Tech Stack list (provided by manager)
- [ ]  Complete your first real task under supervision (manager reviews the output)
- [ ]  Log output in tasks_database (Output Link field)
- [ ]  Review the Outreach Log DB if your role involves outreach
- [ ]  Review the Content Calendar DB if your role involves content

**End of Day 4 check:** Have you completed one real task with your output logged?

---

## 📅 Day 5 — Brand Deep Dive

**Goal:** Deeply understand the specific brand you're working for.

### Tasks

- [ ]  Read your brand's Master Dashboard (Nivy HQ > Brand OS > [Your Brand])
- [ ]  Read Strategy section: Vision, Mission, ICP, Business Plan
- [ ]  Read 3 client case studies or past delivery reports
- [ ]  Review your brand's SOP Index end-to-end
- [ ]  Review your brand's Knowledge Index for the concepts most relevant to your role

**End of Day 5 check:** Can you explain your brand's ICP in under 60 seconds?

---

## 📅 Day 6 — Independent Work

**Goal:** Execute tasks independently. Ask questions proactively.

### Tasks

- [ ]  Complete your full task list for the day (assigned by manager)
- [ ]  Log all outputs in tasks_database
- [ ]  Identify one thing you're unclear on — document it and ask your manager
- [ ]  Review Global Templates Library — identify templates relevant to your role
- [ ]  Read AI Prompt Vault — find 3 prompts you can use in your daily work

---

## 📅 Day 7 — Review & Onward

**Goal:** Confirm you're ready to work independently. Get feedback. Plan Week 2.

### Tasks

- [ ]  Submit self-assessment: What do you understand well? What needs more clarity?
- [ ]  Manager review: Output quality review from Days 4–6
- [ ]  Agree on Week 2 targets (tasks, KPIs, deadlines)
- [ ]  Identify your training gap — what brand-specific modules do you need next?
- [ ]  Access your brand's Role Dashboard (if built) to see your default view

**End of Day 7 check:** Has your manager confirmed you're cleared for independent work?
