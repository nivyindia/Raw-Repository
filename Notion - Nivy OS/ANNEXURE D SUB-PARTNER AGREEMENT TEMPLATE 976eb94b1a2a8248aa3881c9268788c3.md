# ANNEXURE D: SUB-PARTNER AGREEMENT TEMPLATE

# 

This Annexure D forms an integral part of the Master Sales Agency Partner Agreement dated [Date] between Billion Dreams United (Nivy) and [Master Partner Company Name].

## **SUB-PARTNER AGREEMENT**

**This Sub-Partner Agreement** ("Agreement") is made and entered into as of [Date] by and between:

**Master Partner:** [Master Partner Company Name], having its principal place of business at [Address] ("Master Partner")

**Sub-Partner:** [Sub-Partner Company Name], having its principal place of business at [Address] ("Sub-Partner")

**Third-Party Beneficiary:** Billion Dreams United (Nivy), having its principal place of business at 548-Cha/166 Deep Nagar, Manak Nagar, Lucknow - 226011, India ("Company")

---

## **ARTICLE 1: APPOINTMENT & AUTHORITY**

### **1.1 Limited Appointment**

Master Partner hereby appoints Sub-Partner as a non-exclusive sales sub-partner for the territory of [Specific City/Region] ("Territory"), and Sub-Partner accepts such appointment.

### **1.2 No Direct Company Relationship**

Sub-Partner acknowledges that this Agreement is with Master Partner only. Sub-Partner has no direct contractual relationship with Company and shall not represent itself as an agent, employee, or direct partner of Company.

### **1.3 Territory Restrictions**

Sub-Partner shall only market and sell Company's services within the designated Territory unless expressly authorized in writing by Master Partner.

---

## **ARTICLE 2: OBLIGATIONS OF SUB-PARTNER**

### **2.1 Performance Standards**

Sub-Partner shall meet the following minimum performance standards:

- **Months 1-3:** $1,000 monthly revenue
- **Months 4-6:** $2,000 monthly revenue
- **Months 7+:** $3,000 monthly revenue
- **Customer Satisfaction:** Minimum 85% CSAT score
- **Reporting Compliance:** 100% on-time reporting

### **2.2 Brand Compliance**

Sub-Partner shall strictly adhere to the brand guidelines provided by Master Partner, including:

- Use of approved marketing materials only
- No modification of Company branding
- Proper disclosure of sub-partner relationship
- Compliance with all trademark usage rules

### **2.3 No Direct Client Contact**

Sub-Partner shall not directly contact Company's clients. All client communications must be through Master Partner's approved systems and channels.

### **2.4 Training Requirements**

Sub-Partner must complete all required training programs:

- Product knowledge certification within 30 days
- Sales process training within 45 days
- Compliance and data security training within 60 days

---

## **ARTICLE 3: COMPENSATION STRUCTURE**

### **3.1 Commission Rates**

Sub-Partner shall receive commission on revenue generated according to the following structure:

**Tiered Commission:**

- **$0 - $10,000 quarterly:** 12% commission
- **$10,001 - $25,000 quarterly:** 15% commission
- **$25,001+ quarterly:** 18% commission

### **3.2 Performance Bonuses**

**Quarterly Excellence Bonus:**

- 3% additional bonus for achieving 125% of quarterly target
- Paid within 15 days of quarter-end

**Client Retention Bonus:**

- 2% bonus on renewals and repeat business
- Paid upon successful client renewal

### **3.3 Payment Terms**

- **Payment Cycle:** Monthly
- **Reporting Deadline:** 3rd of each month
- **Payment Date:** 10th of each month
- **Minimum Payout:** $100 (amounts below accumulate)

### **3.4 Expense Responsibility**

Sub-Partner is responsible for all expenses incurred in performing services under this Agreement, including but not limited to:

- Marketing and advertising costs
- Travel and entertainment expenses
- Office and equipment costs
- Telephone and internet expenses

---

## **ARTICLE 4: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY**

### **4.1 Company IP Protection**

Sub-Partner acknowledges that all Company intellectual property, including trademarks, service marks, trade secrets, and proprietary information, remains the exclusive property of Company.

### **4.2 Limited License**

Sub-Partner is granted a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use Company's marketing materials and trademarks solely for the purpose of performing services under this Agreement.

### **4.3 No IP Creation**

Sub-Partner shall not create any derivative works, modifications, or new intellectual property based on Company's IP without prior written consent.

### **4.4 Work Product Assignment**

All work product created by Sub-Partner in connection with this Agreement shall be deemed the property of Company, and Sub-Partner hereby assigns all rights to such work product to Company.

---

## **ARTICLE 5: CONFIDENTIALITY**

### **5.1 Confidential Information**

Sub-Partner shall maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary and confidential information of Company and Master Partner, including but not limited to:

- Client lists and contact information
- Pricing structures and commission rates
- Business strategies and marketing plans
- Technical processes and methodologies

### **5.2 Non-Disclosure**

Sub-Partner shall not disclose, publish, or disseminate any confidential information to any third party during the term of this Agreement or for three years following its termination.

### **5.3 Data Security**

Sub-Partner shall implement reasonable security measures to protect confidential information and comply with all applicable data protection laws.

---

## **ARTICLE 6: TERM AND TERMINATION**

### **6.1 Term**

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for twelve (12) months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Article.

### **6.2 Termination for Convenience**

Master Partner may terminate this Agreement for any reason with thirty (30) days written notice to Sub-Partner.

### **6.3 Termination for Cause**

Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party:

- Materially breaches any provision of this Agreement
- Becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy
- Engages in fraudulent or illegal activities

### **6.4 Performance-Based Termination**

Master Partner may terminate this Agreement if Sub-Partner:

- Fails to meet minimum performance standards for two consecutive months
- Receives consistent customer complaints
- Violates brand or compliance guidelines

### **6.5 Effects of Termination**

Upon termination:

- Sub-Partner shall immediately cease using Company IP
- All confidential information shall be returned or destroyed
- Sub-Partner shall receive all earned but unpaid commissions
- All ongoing client relationships shall be transferred to Master Partner

---

## **ARTICLE 7: RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS**

### **7.1 Non-Solicitation**

During the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months following termination, Sub-Partner shall not:

- Solicit or accept business from Company's clients
- Induce clients to terminate relationships with Company
- Solicit employees or contractors of Company or Master Partner

### **7.2 Non-Competition**

During the term of this Agreement, Sub-Partner shall not engage in any business activities that compete with Company's services within the Territory.

### **7.3 Non-Circumvention**

Sub-Partner shall not attempt to circumvent the relationship between Master Partner and Company by engaging in business directly with Company.

---

## **ARTICLE 8: INSURANCE AND INDEMNIFICATION**

### **8.1 Insurance Requirements**

Sub-Partner shall maintain at its own expense:

- Professional liability insurance of at least $100,000
- Cyber liability insurance if handling client data
- General commercial liability insurance

### **8.2 Indemnification**

Sub-Partner shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Master Partner and Company from any claims, damages, or losses arising from:

- Sub-Partner's breach of this Agreement
- Sub-Partner's negligent or willful misconduct
- Sub-Partner's violation of any laws or regulations

---

## **ARTICLE 9: DISPUTE RESOLUTION**

### **9.1 Governing Law**

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of [Jurisdiction], without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

### **9.2 Dispute Resolution Process**

- **Step 1:** Informal negotiation between parties (15 days)
- **Step 2:** Mediation with mutually agreed mediator (30 days)
- **Step 3:** Binding arbitration in [City, Jurisdiction]

### **9.3 Company as Third-Party Beneficiary**

Company shall have the right to enforce the terms of this Agreement as a third-party beneficiary, particularly with respect to intellectual property and confidentiality provisions.

---

## **ARTICLE 10: MISCELLANEOUS**

### **10.1 Independent Contractor**

Sub-Partner is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, or partner of Master Partner or Company.

### **10.2 Assignment**

Sub-Partner may not assign this Agreement without Master Partner's prior written consent.

### **10.3 Notices**

All notices shall be in writing and sent to the addresses specified above, unless changed by written notice.

### **10.4 Entire Agreement**

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements.

### **10.5 Amendments**

No amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.
