## SECTION 2: BUSINESS TYPE & SCALE (ANTI-FREELANCER FILTER)

**5️⃣ What best describes your business?**

*(Multiple choice)*

- Registered company
- Startup (pre-registered)
- Agency / Firm
- Manufacturer / Supplier
- Distributor / Trader
- Other (explain)

**6️⃣ Business Size**

*(Multiple choice)*

- 2–10 people
- 11–50 people
- 50+ people

## SECTION 3: CORE RULE ACKNOWLEDGEMENT (MANDATORY)

**7️⃣ This ecosystem works on reciprocity.Do you understand that access is earned by sharing real demand, not by selling services?***(Required – Multiple choice)*

- Yes, I understand and agree
- No (Application will be rejected)

---

## SECTION 4: DEMAND DECLARATION (MOST IMPORTANT)

**8️⃣ What is your CURRENT or UPCOMING business requirement?**

*(Paragraph – REQUIRED)*

> Examples:
> 
> - Services you need now
> - Vendors you regularly outsource to
> - Partners you are actively looking for

❌ Do NOT write what services you provide

❌ Fake or generic answers lead to rejection

---

**9️⃣ Is this requirement:**

*(Multiple choice)*

- Immediate (0–30 days)
- Short-term (1–3 months)
- Recurring / Ongoing
