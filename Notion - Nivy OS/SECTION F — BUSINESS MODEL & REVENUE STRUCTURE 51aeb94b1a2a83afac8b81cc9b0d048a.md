## **1. Business Model Overview**

**Nivy Nexus** operates as a **B2B trade facilitator and supply chain enabler**.

Our core model is **verification-driven deal facilitation**, where we **connect buyers and suppliers**, manage communication, and coordinate logistics without taking ownership of goods.

We act as a **neutral, process-oriented intermediary**, ensuring:

- Transactions are **transparent and risk-free**
- Only **verified buyers and suppliers** participate
- Processes are **structured, scalable, and secure**

---

## **2. Revenue Model**

Nivy Nexus generates revenue primarily through **success-based fees and service charges**:

1. **Commission / Success Fee**
    - Charged **only after a deal is confirmed and executed**
    - Percentage varies depending on deal size and complexity
2. **Service Fees (Optional / Premium Services)**
    - Fees for **priority supplier matching, verification support, or expedited delivery coordination**
    - Pre-agreed with buyer or supplier

> We do not charge blind upfront fees for unverified deals or unverifiable requirements.
> 

---

## **3. Fee Principles**

- **Aligned with deal success** — we succeed when our clients succeed
- **Transparent** — all fees and payment terms are agreed in writing before any transaction
- **Risk-free upfront** — no fees unless services or facilitation lead to actual outcomes

---

## **4. Risk & Responsibility Boundaries**

- Nivy Nexus **does not own or sell products**
- Payments are coordinated, but we do not act as the primary financial party unless explicitly agreed
- Liability is limited to **verification, facilitation, and process management**

---

## **5. Incentive Alignment**

- Our revenue model **aligns with buyer and supplier interests**
- Ensures **high-quality supplier selection and deal transparency**
- Encourages **repeatable and long-term partnerships**

---

## **6. Optional Premium Services**

- Priority supplier verification
- Custom sourcing for specific industries
- Export documentation guidance
- Logistics & delivery coordination support

> These optional services help scale operations for high-volume buyers or complex sourcing requirements.
>
