## 🔹 ABOUT NIVY

NIVY is a **multi-service global business ecosystem** delivering integrated solutions across **NIVY Advisory (Accounting & Financial Services), NIVY Next (Digital Marketing & Lead Generation), Technology Development, NIVY Nexus (B2B Marketplace), and NIVY Care Foundation (CSR & Social Initiatives).**

Since 2018, NIVY has enabled startups, SMEs, corporates, and international clients to **grow sustainably, comply reliably, and leverage technology** for competitive advantage. With offices in India, UAE, and the Philippines, NIVY combines **professional integrity with scalable global execution.**
