## **PHASE 7–9 (Shortlisting → Soft Confirmation → Handover)**

---

# **PHASE 7: SHORTLISTING & MATCHING (UAE)**

### **Objective**

Internally select the **best-fit buyers and suppliers** based on qualification, verification, ICP fit, and UAE-specific business requirements.

---

## **Step 22: Internally Shortlist Buyers**

**Input:** Qualified & Verified buyers from Phase 6

**Shortlisting Rules:**

- Score High or Medium → Eligible
- ICP fit = mandatory
- Buying timeline = within 3 months
- Budget meets MOQ & supplier pricing range
- Compliance requirements met (GST / MOHAP / ESMA)

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Meets all criteria → move to internal matching
- **Fail:** Fails any mandatory criteria → tag “Rejected – Buyer”

---

## **Step 23: Internally Shortlist Suppliers**

**Input:** Qualified & Verified suppliers from Phase 6

**Shortlisting Rules:**

- Score High or Medium → Eligible
- Capacity ≥ buyer requirement
- MOQ matches buyer needs
- Certifications / licenses valid in UAE
- Delivery timelines feasible
- Payment terms acceptable (Advance / LC / Credit)

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Meets all criteria → move to internal matching
- **Fail:** Fails any mandatory criteria → tag “Rejected – Supplier”

---

## **Step 24: Perform Internal Buyer–Supplier Matching**

**Matching Rules (UAE-specific):**

- Product compatibility (exact SKU / category)
- Budget alignment (buyer vs supplier)
- MOQ & capacity alignment
- Delivery timelines (local UAE logistics or export timelines)
- Payment term compatibility (Advance / LC / 30–60 days)
- Compliance alignment (MOHAP / ESMA / local approvals)

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** All criteria met → move to Step 30 Soft Confirmation
- **Fail:** Any mandatory mismatch → tag “Cannot Match – Hold” → consider alternative suppliers

---

# **PHASE 8: SOFT CONFIRMATION (UAE)**

### **Objective**

Get preliminary **agreement from buyer & supplier** before handing over to deal execution team.

---

## **Step 30: Send Soft Confirmation to Buyer**

**Rules:**

- Provide matched supplier info (general, not confidential details)
- Request confirmation of interest
- Include timelines and rough pricing range

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Buyer confirms → proceed
- **Fail:** Buyer declines / no response after 48h → hold or reject

---

## **Step 31: Send Soft Confirmation to Supplier**

**Rules:**

- Provide matched buyer info (general, no sensitive financial details)
- Request confirmation of ability & interest to supply
- Include timelines and MOQ

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Supplier confirms → proceed
- **Fail:** Supplier declines / cannot meet requirements → hold or consider alternative buyer

---

## **Step 32 & 33: Receive Confirmations**

**Tracking:**

- CRM update: “Soft Confirmed – Buyer” / “Soft Confirmed – Supplier”
- Follow-up if no response within 24–48h
- Only **both sides confirmed** → move to Phase 9

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Both buyer & supplier confirm → ready for Handover
- **Fail:** Any side declines → return to Phase 7 for alternative match

---

# **PHASE 9: HANDOVER (UAE)**

### **Objective**

Transfer fully verified and soft-confirmed buyer-supplier match to **Deal & Account Manager** for negotiation, closing, and delivery tracking.

---

## **Step 34: Finalize Deal-Ready Match**

**Checklist:**

- Both buyer & supplier soft-confirmed
- All compliance & ICP criteria verified
- Pricing, MOQ, delivery timeline preliminarily aligned

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** All criteria satisfied → proceed to Step 35
- **Fail:** Any mismatch → return to Phase 7 for alternate match

---

## **Step 35: Prepare Deal Handover Summary**

**Contents:**

- Buyer & supplier contact info
- Verified documents summary (licenses, VAT, certifications)
- Product / quantity / pricing / timeline / payment terms
- ICP / compliance notes
- Any flags / follow-up points

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Summary complete → ready for handover
- **Fail:** Missing critical info → collect before transfer

---

## **Step 36: Transfer Case to Deal & Account Manager**

**Handover Rules:**

- Upload all documents & CRM records
- Notify manager via email / Slack / WhatsApp
- Include notes on potential risks / special instructions

**Pass/Fail Condition:**

- **Pass:** Manager confirms receipt → case active
- **Fail:** Any missing data → update before confirmation

---

### **Phase 7–9 Deliverables (UAE)**

✔ Shortlisted, verified, and matched buyer-supplier pairs

✔ Soft confirmations received from both sides

✔ Handover summary completed and transferred to Deal & Account Manager

✔ All rejected / failed leads tagged clearly in CRM
