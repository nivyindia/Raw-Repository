# CFO / Finance Department – Complete Document Checklist

# 

### **1. Financial Strategy & Planning**

1. Financial Strategy / Department Plan
2. Annual & Quarterly Budgeting Documents
3. Long-Term Financial Forecasts (3–5 years)
4. Capital Allocation & Investment Planning Documents
5. Revenue & Profit Growth Plans

---

### **2. Accounting & Financial Operations Oversight**

1. Accounts Payable SOP
2. Accounts Receivable SOP
3. Payroll Management SOP
4. Bank Reconciliation & Cash Management SOP
5. Expense Management & Approval Guidelines
6. Client Billing & Invoicing SOP
7. Internal Audit & Control Procedures

---

### **3. Financial Reporting**

1. Monthly / Quarterly / Annual Financial Statements (P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow)
2. KPI Dashboards (Revenue, Margin, Collections, Liabilities)
3. Budget vs Actual Reports
4. Forecasting & Scenario Analysis Templates
5. Departmental Financial Reports & Summaries

---

### **4. Risk & Compliance**

1. Risk Management & Contingency Planning Documents
2. Tax Compliance Documentation (GST, TDS, Income Tax, etc.)
3. Financial Policies & Procedures Manual
4. Audit Reports & Regulatory Compliance Checklists
5. Confidentiality & Data Security Guidelines

---

### **5. Investment & Capital Management**

1. Investment Evaluation Templates
2. Funding & Loan Documentation
3. Capital Expenditure Approval SOP
4. Asset Management & Depreciation Guidelines

---

### **6. Stakeholder Communication**

1. Investor / Board Reporting Templates
2. Key Client / Strategic Partner Financial Reports
3. Executive Summaries for CEO / Board

---

### **7. Tools & Systems**

1. Accounting Software SOPs (QuickBooks, Zoho Books, Tally, etc.)
2. Financial Analysis Templates (ratios, margin analysis, KPIs)
3. Automation & Integration Guidelines

---

### **8. Optional / Advanced**

1. Merger & Acquisition / Partnership Evaluation Templates
2. Treasury & Cash Flow Optimization Documents
3. Scenario Planning & Sensitivity Analysis Templates
4. Strategic Risk Assessment Reports
