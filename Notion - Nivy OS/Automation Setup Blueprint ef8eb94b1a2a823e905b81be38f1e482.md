# **Universal Lead Generation Automation Blueprint**

## **Core Funnel Architecture**

```
[Traffic Source] → [Lead Magnet] → [Instant Lead Capture] → [Multi-Channel Nurture] → [Automated Qualification] → [Sales Handoff]

```

## **1. Lead Magnet Matrix**

- **Immediate:** "Free [Service] in 24 Hours"
- **Intermediate:** "Free [Tool/Template] Download"
- **Advanced:** "Free [Audit/Consultation]"

## **2. Capture System**

- **Primary:** WhatsApp opt-in (highest open rates)
- **Secondary:** Email + SMS capture
- **Trigger:** Any form submission → instant WhatsApp message

## **3. Multi-Channel Nurture Sequence**

**WhatsApp (Minutes):**

- 0 min: "Thanks! Your [service] is processing"
- 30 min: "Option menu: 1-A, 2-B, 3-C"
- 24 hrs: "Your delivery + upgrade offer"

**Email (Days):**

- Day 1: Delivery + "What's your biggest challenge?"
- Day 3: Case study + next free service offer
- Day 7: "Your friend referral bonus active"

**SMS (Strategic):**

- Day 2: "Quick question: Need help with [related service]?"
- Day 5: "Limited spots for free [next service]"

## **4. Automated Qualification**

**Scoring System:**

- +10 points: Responds to WhatsApp
- +10 points: Requests additional service
- +20 points: Answers qualification question
- +50 points: High-value business (revenue >₹10L/year)

**Triggers:**

- Score >50: Auto-schedule call
- Score >30: Send premium offer
- Score <10: Continue nurture sequence

## **5. Sales Handoff Automation**

- **Hot leads:** Auto-assign to available BDE
- **Warm leads:** Book in calendly for next day
- **Cold leads:** Continue educational sequence

## **6. Retargeting Framework**

- **Level 1:** Didn't opt-in → "Missed your free service?"
- **Level 2:** Opt-in but no engagement → "Your service is ready"
- **Level 3:** Engaged but didn't buy → "Special limited-time offer"

## **Universal Tools Stack**

- **CRM:** HubSpot (free)
- **Automation:** Zapier/Make
- **Communication:** WhatsApp API + Email + SMS
- **Scheduling:** Calendly
- **Tracking:** Google Analytics

## **Implementation Rules**

1. **Test:** 3 lead magnets, 2 messaging styles
2. **Scale:** Double budget when CPA < LTV/3
3. **Optimize:** Weekly review of response rates
4. **Expand:** Add channels after 100 leads/month

## **Success Metrics**

- **Response Rate:** >40% (WhatsApp)
- **Cost Per Lead:** <₹100
- **Lead to Qualified:** >25%
- **Time to First Response:** <5 minutes

**This blueprint works for:**

- Social media ads
- Website traffic
- Referral campaigns
- Cold outreach
- Content marketing
- Partnership channels
