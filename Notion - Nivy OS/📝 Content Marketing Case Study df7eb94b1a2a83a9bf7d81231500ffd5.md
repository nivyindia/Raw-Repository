# ✍️ FEATURED CONTENT MARKETING CASE STUDIES (Tier-1)

## ✅ 1. B2B SaaS Content Engine — USA

**Client Type:** B2B SaaS Company | United States

**Industry:** Software / Technology

**Services:** Content Strategy, SEO Blogs, Lead Magnets, Thought Leadership

### 🎯 Objective

Build a scalable content engine to drive qualified inbound leads and reduce paid acquisition dependency.

### ⚠️ Challenge

- Low organic visibility
- No structured funnel content
- Poor lead-to-demo conversion

### 🛠 Strategy

- TOFU–MOFU–BOFU content framework
- SEO pillar pages + cluster blogs
- High-conversion gated assets (ebooks, templates, whitepapers)

### 🚀 Execution

- 40+ SEO blogs in 6 months
- 5 long-form guides + 3 lead magnets
- CRM + marketing automation integration

### 📈 Results

- **+327% organic traffic in 7 months**
- **+211% inbound demo requests**
- **43% cost per lead**
