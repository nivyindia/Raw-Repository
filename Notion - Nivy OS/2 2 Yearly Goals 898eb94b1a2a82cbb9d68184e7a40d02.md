## 🎯 4️⃣ YEARLY ORGANIZATIONAL GOALS

Your yearly goals act as the **North Star** for the company — they define what success looks like for the next 12 months across revenue, clients, systems, team, and global presence.

---

### 🔹 STRUCTURE

Each annual goal should include:

- **Objective** — What you want to achieve.
- **KPI / Target** — How you’ll measure success.
- **Owner** — Department or leader responsible.
- **Review Frequency** — How often progress is tracked.
- **Alignment** — Which strategic pillar it supports.

---

### 🧭 YEAR 1 (EXAMPLE: FOUNDATION & AUTOMATION YEAR)

| Goal Area | Objective | KPI / Target | Owner | Review | Strategic Pillar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Revenue Growth** | Achieve $100M global annual revenue | $8.3M/month average | CEO | Monthly | Growth & Profitability |
| **Client Base Expansion** | Onboard 1,000+ recurring international clients | Retention >85% | CMO | Quarterly | Growth & Scaling |
| **Systemization & SOPs** | 100% core processes documented | 150+ SOPs live | COO | Quarterly | Systemization |
| **Automation & Tech Stack** | Automate 60% of workflows | 200+ automations built | CTO | Monthly | Automation |
| **Global Expansion** | Open 5 international franchise branches | 1 new franchise/quarter | VP Growth | Quarterly | Scaling |
| **Training & Culture** | Train all 2000 employees in company systems | 100% training completion | HR Director | Quarterly | People & Culture |
| **Profitability** | Maintain 30% net profit margin | Profit margin ≥ 30% | CFO | Monthly | Profitability |
| **Client Success** | Maintain 95%+ client satisfaction score | Avg rating ≥ 4.8/5 | Client Success Head | Monthly | Growth & Retention |

### 🔹 GOVERNANCE OF YEARLY GOALS

1. **CEO + Board Meeting (Annual):** Set final goals for the year.
2. **Department Planning (Q1):** Each HOD defines their OKRs based on yearly goals.
3. **Quarterly Reviews:** Track OKR progress & budget allocation.
4. **Annual Review:** Measure achieved % vs target and realign for next year.
