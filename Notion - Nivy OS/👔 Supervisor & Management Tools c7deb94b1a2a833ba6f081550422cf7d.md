## 🎯 Your Role as a Supervisor

You are the engine that keeps the VA team running. Your job is not to do outreach — it is to make sure your VAs do it correctly, consistently, and at volume. A well-run team of 10 VAs should generate 10 to 20 booked calls per day. That only happens if you manage daily.

**You own three outcomes:**

1. **Output** — your team hits their KPI targets every day
2. **Quality** — every message your VAs send meets the script standard
3. **Accuracy** — every lead in the CRM is correctly updated

## 📋 Your 5 Daily Responsibilities

| Time | Task | What Good Looks Like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morning (start of shift) | Run morning brief — assign each VA their daily targets | Every VA knows their target before they start outreach |
| During shift | Spot-check 5 messages per VA — flag any script violations immediately | Zero unaddressed deviations by end of shift |
| Midday (1 PM) | Review midday checkpoint reports — identify anyone below 50% | Underperformers identified and coached before the afternoon |
| Afternoon | Push underperformers — coach or escalate to manager | No VA ends the day below 40% without a documented reason |
| End of shift (by 7 PM) | Submit team summary report | Full team numbers submitted before 7:00 PM |

## ⚠️ Escalation Rules

Use this to decide when to handle something yourself and when to escalate to your manager.

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| VA missing daily report once | Message them directly. Mark absent if no response by 7:30 PM. |
| VA below 50% KPI for 3 consecutive days | Schedule a 1:1 coaching session. Document the outcome. |
| VA deviating from approved scripts | Flag immediately in QC system. Retrain on script before next session. |
| VA below 60% for 5+ consecutive days | Escalate to manager with written summary. Do not make removal decision alone. |
| Suspected data breach or CRM export | Escalate to management immediately. Do not inform the VA. |
| VA requests salary dispute or grievance | Direct to [⚖️ Dispute & Grievance Process](%E2%9A%96%EF%B8%8F%20Dispute%20&%20Grievance%20Process%2047beb94b1a2a822ab2ae81c45405e37d.md). Do not handle informally. |
