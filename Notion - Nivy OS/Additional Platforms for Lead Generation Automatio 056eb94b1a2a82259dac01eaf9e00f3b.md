# Additional Platforms for Lead Generation Automation

# **VIDEO PLATFORMS**

### **YouTube Automation:**

- **Auto-respond to comments:** "Thanks! Download the free template here: [link]"
- **End-screen CTAs:** Automated lead capture forms
- **Description bots:** Auto-DM when users comment specific keywords
- **Channel memberships:** Automated onboarding sequences

### **TikTok Automation:**

- **Auto-reply to comments:** "DM for free guide"
- **Bio link tools:** Instant lead capture
- **Video responses:** Automated follow-up to engaged users
- **Live stream chatbots:** Auto-respond to questions with offers
