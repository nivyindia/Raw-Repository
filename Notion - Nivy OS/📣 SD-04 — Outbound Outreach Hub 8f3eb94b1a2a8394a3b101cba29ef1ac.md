# 🔎 OVERVIEW {#overview}

Outbound outreach is the proactive engine that fills the pipeline before inbound takes over. This department manages all first-contact outreach across every channel.

---

# 📡 CHANNEL STRATEGY {#channels}

## LinkedIn Outreach

- **Target:** Decision makers — founders, marketing heads, ops directors
- **Method:** Connection request → Personalized note → 3-message sequence
- **Tool:** PhantomBuster (auto connection) + manual follow-up
- **Daily Limit:** 50 new connections (LinkedIn safety limit)

## Cold Email

- **Target:** US/UK/UAE SMBs needing VA or digital services
- **Method:** Apollo lead list → Instantly campaign → 7-email sequence
- **Tool:** [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) (free 50/month) + Instantly (free trial)
- **Daily Limit:** 100 emails/day per domain

## WhatsApp Outreach

- **Target:** Indian market — local businesses, founders
- **Method:** WhatsApp Business broadcast + personal follow-up
- **Tool:** WhatsApp Business + WATI (when ready)

## Social DM Outreach

- **Target:** Instagram + Twitter/X followers of competitor accounts
- **Method:** ManyChat automated welcome DM + human follow-up
