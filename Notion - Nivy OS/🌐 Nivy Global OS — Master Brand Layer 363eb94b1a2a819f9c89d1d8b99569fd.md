## 📌 Section 1 — Company Master Docs

### Vision

> **To build the world's most integrated multi-brand business ecosystem — where every brand operates at world-class standards, every system runs without the founder, and every market we enter is dominated through superior execution.**
> 

### Mission

> **Nivy Empires exists to help businesses grow faster, operate smarter, and scale further — through digital services, advisory, talent, technology, and global market access.**
> 

### Core Values

1. **Execution over ideas** — A good idea executed is worth more than a great idea planned
2. **Systems before people** — Build the process, then hire into it
3. **Quality is non-negotiable** — Every output represents all of Nivy
4. **Founder independence** — If it requires the founder's daily input, it's not built yet
5. **Compounding improvement** — Every mistake is a system update

## 📊 Section 7 — Cross-Brand KPI Framework

> **Definitions and measurement standards only. Each brand tracks its own actuals in KPI DB (filtered by Brand).**
> 

### Universal KPI Definitions

| KPI Category | Metric | Definition | Measurement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue | MRR | Monthly Recurring Revenue — all active retainers | Monthly |
| Revenue | New MRR | Revenue from new clients this month | Monthly |
| Revenue | Churn Rate | % of MRR lost from cancellations | Monthly |
| Sales | Leads Generated | New contacts added to Lead Tracker DB | Weekly |
| Sales | Outreach Sent | Total outreach messages sent across all channels | Weekly |
| Sales | Reply Rate | Replies ÷ Outreach Sent × 100 | Weekly |
| Sales | Meetings Booked | Discovery calls scheduled | Weekly |
| Sales | Win Rate | Deals won ÷ Proposals sent × 100 | Monthly |
| Delivery | On-Time Delivery Rate | Tasks delivered on/before deadline ÷ total tasks | Weekly |
| Delivery | QC Pass Rate | Tasks approved first-time ÷ total tasks reviewed | Weekly |
| Delivery | Client Satisfaction | Post-project score (1–10, collected via form) | Per project |
| Team | Task Completion Rate | Done tasks ÷ assigned tasks × 100 | Weekly |
| Team | Time to Onboard | Days from hire to first independent output | Per hire |

### Alert Thresholds (default — override per brand)

- Reply Rate < 10% → Review outreach quality
- Win Rate < 20% → Review proposal quality + ICP fit
- On-Time Delivery < 90% → Review capacity + task planning
- QC Pass Rate < 85% → Review SOP quality + training
- Churn Rate > 5% → Urgent — review client satisfaction + delivery
