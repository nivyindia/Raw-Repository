- Construction, interior & material suppliers and buyers **don’t trust unknown middlemen**
- Buyers want:
    - Better price
    - Same or better quality
    - Reliable delivery
- Suppliers want:
    - Genuine buyers
    - Assured payment
    - No time waste
- Existing B2B platforms = **low-quality leads, no real verification**

## 2️⃣ Market Understanding (Key Insights)

- **UAE** is ideal:
    - High construction & interior demand
    - Faster decisions than Tier-1 Western countries
    - Buyers are **open to switching suppliers** if:
        - Price is better
        - Quality is same
        - Risk is low

    - Certifications
    - Long vendor approval cycles
    - Legal and compliance barriers

## 5️⃣ Trust & Proof System (Decision)

✅ **Manual but strong verification (Phase 1)**

We decided to collect & show:

- Trade license
- Years in business
- Past clients / projects
- GST / VAT / Govt registrations
- Product certifications
- Warehouse / factory details
- Previous sales range (approx)
- Owner/authorized signatory

Badges:

- ✔ Verified Supplier
- ✔ Verified Buyer
- ✔ Govt Registered
- ✔ Repeat Seller

## 6️⃣ Sales & Deal Flow (Decision)

**Step-by-step process we finalized:**

1. Reach out to buyer/supplier
2. Understand requirement
3. Verify documents
4. Match buyer ↔ supplier
5. Price negotiation
6. Buyer pays **to YOU**
7. Supplier delivers
8. Buyer confirms
9. You release payment (minus commission)

👉 You act as **trusted transaction controller**
