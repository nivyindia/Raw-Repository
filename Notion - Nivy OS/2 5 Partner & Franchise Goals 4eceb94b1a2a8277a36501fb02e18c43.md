# 2.5 Partner & Franchise Goals

# DEPARTMENT 6: FRANCHISE & PARTNERSHIPS

**Mission:**

Expand global presence via franchise, white-label, and strategic partnerships.

**Key Yearly Goals:**

- 10 new franchises
- 20+ strategic partnerships
- Partner revenue = 25% of total

**KPIs:**

- Active Partners
- Partner Revenue Contribution
- Partner ROI %
- Brand Compliance Score

**Core SOPs:**

- Partner Identification → Onboarding → Training → Performance Audit

**Tools:** PartnerStack, CRM, Notion Partner Portal

**Dashboard Metrics:**

- Partner Pipeline
- Partner Performance
- Expansion Progress
