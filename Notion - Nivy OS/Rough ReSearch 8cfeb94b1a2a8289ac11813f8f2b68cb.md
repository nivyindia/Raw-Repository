## In conclusion: which is “better” depends on your priorities

If I summarize:

- If your priorities are **lower cost**, **bigger candidate volume**, and you can invest in training / you don't need perfect English / accent from day one, **India** may be better.
- If your priority is **communication quality**, **closer cultural fit with Western markets**, and you want quicker ramp-up of sales/BD quality, **Philippines** might give you better ROI despite somewhat higher cost.
- If your BDE role targets markets in US / Western Europe, where communication, accent, rapport matter a lot, Philippines may reduce friction. If targeting Asia or Middle East, India might suffice with less cost.
- Also consider attrition: a cheaper hire isn't always better if turnover is so high that you lose continuity, don’t meet targets, keep hiring & training etc.
