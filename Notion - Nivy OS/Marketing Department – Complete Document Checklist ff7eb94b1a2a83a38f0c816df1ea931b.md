# **1. Strategy & Planning**

1. Marketing Strategy / Plan
2. Marketing Budget & Spend Plan
3. Competitor Analysis & Market Research Reports
4. Customer Persona & Segmentation Document
5. Campaign Plans & SOPs
6. Marketing KPI Dashboard / Reporting Templates

### **2. Branding & Positioning**

1. Branding Guidelines (logo, colors, fonts, tone)
2. Brand Story & Messaging Framework
3. Service / Product Pricing Packages
4. Discount & Offers Guidelines
5. Portfolio / Brochures / Case Studies

---

### **3. Content & Communication**

1. Website Content & Copywriting Guidelines
2. Social Media & Content Calendar
3. Email Marketing Templates & Sequences
4. SEO & SEM Guidelines
5. Event & Webinar Materials
6. Press Releases & Public Relations Materials
7. Crisis Communication / PR Response Templates
8. Influencer / Collaboration Guidelines
9. Referral / Affiliate Program Guidelines

---

### **4. Assets & Tools**

1. Content Repository / Asset Library (images, videos, graphics)
2. Promotional Collateral Templates (banners, posters, ads)
3. Marketing Automation SOPs
4. Analytics & Tracking Documentation

---

### **5. Compliance & Policies**

1. Marketing Policies & Compliance Guidelines (copyrights, legal disclaimers, GDPR, CAN-SPAM)
2. Event Marketing SOPs
