## 📋 Process: How Nivy Next Works

**Stage 1 — Idea Intake**

Any Nivy team member can propose a new product or initiative. Log it in the projects_database with Brand = Nivy Next and Status = Idea.

**Stage 2 — Validation**

The Nivy Next lead reviews the idea against: market demand, Nivy's existing assets, and resource requirements. Timeline: 2 weeks.

**Stage 3 — Build**

Approved ideas move into active build. A project is created in projects_database. Tasks are assigned in tasks_database.

**Stage 4 — Launch**

Once MVP-ready, Nivy Next coordinates with Nivy Global (distribution) and Digital Marketing Ops (promotion).

**Stage 5 — Graduate**

If the product/service reaches sufficient scale, it becomes a standalone Nivy division or sub-brand.

## 📊 Decision Framework

When evaluating a new idea for Nivy Next, score it across these 4 axes:

| Axis | Question | Score (1–5) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 💰 Revenue Potential | Can this generate £50k+/year? |  |
| ⚡ Speed to Launch | Can we MVP in under 90 days? |  |
| 🔗 Synergy | Does it leverage existing Nivy assets? |  |
| 🌍 Market Pull | Is there clear demand right now? |  |

Ideas scoring 16+ move to validation. Below 12 = park for later.
