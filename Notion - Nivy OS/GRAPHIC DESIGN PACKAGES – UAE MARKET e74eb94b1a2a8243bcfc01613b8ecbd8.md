# FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (GRAPHIC DESIGN)

**Q1. Do you provide source files?**

Yes. Higher packages include all source files (AI / PSD). Starter package includes only JPEG / PNG.

**Q2. How many revisions are included?**

Starter: 2 revisions, Growth: up to 4, Performance: unlimited within package, Enterprise: unlimited.

**Q3. Can you design Arabic content?**

Yes. All packages support bilingual content (English + Arabic).

**Q4. What platforms do you design for?**

Social media, print, websites, banners, ads, email templates, presentations.

**Q5. Do you create branding / logos?**

Yes. Starter and Growth include basic logos; Performance and Enterprise include full branding kits.

**Q6. What is the typical delivery time?**

Depends on package & workload. Starter: 3–5 working days per design. Growth: 2–4 days. Performance & Enterprise: 1–3 days per asset depending on complexity.

**Q7. Can I request urgent designs?**

Yes. Urgent delivery is available at extra charge.

**Q8. Who owns the designs?**

You retain 100% ownership of all delivered designs and source files.
