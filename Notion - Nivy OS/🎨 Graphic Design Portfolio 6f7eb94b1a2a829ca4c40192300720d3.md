## 📌 HOW TO PRESENT THESE AS CLIENT‑READY CASE STUDIES

### 📄 Case Structure (use this for your portfolio)

1. **Project Title & Client**
2. **Industry & Business Goals**
3. **Challenges / Pain Points**
4. **Design Strategy & Solutions**
5. **Deliverables (Logo, Packaging, Report, Posters, etc.)**
6. **Visual Examples (Screenshots / Mockups)**
7. **Outcome / Impact**
8. **Tools Used (optional)**

🖼 Include visuals: logo versions, posters, packaging, reports, signage, before/after layouts.
