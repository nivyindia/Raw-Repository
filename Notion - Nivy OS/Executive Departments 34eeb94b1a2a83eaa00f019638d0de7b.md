```
CEO / Founder
│
├── Chief Operating Officer (COO)
│   ├── Sales & Marketing Division Head
│   ├── Project Delivery Division Head
│   └── Technical Division Head
│
├── Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
│   └── Finance & HR Division Head
│
├── Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
│   └── Technical Division Head (dotted line)
│
├── Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)
│   └── Sales & Marketing Division Head (dotted line)
│
├── Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)
│   └── HR Unit (dotted line)
│
├── Chief Product Officer (CPO)
│   └── Product Teams (where applicable)
│
├── Chief Revenue Officer (CRO)
│   └── Sales & Customer Success Teams
│
├── Chief Customer Officer (CCO)
│   └── Customer Success & Service Teams
│
├── Chief Strategy Officer (CSO)
│   └── Strategic Initiatives
│
└── Chief Legal Officer (CLO)
    └── Legal & Compliance Teams
```
