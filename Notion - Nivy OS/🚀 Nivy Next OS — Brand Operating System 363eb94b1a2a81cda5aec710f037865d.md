## 💰 Sales Department

### Sales Process

```
Prospect Identified (ICP match)
        ↓
Enriched (LinkedIn + email verified)
        ↓
Outreach Sent (cold email / LinkedIn DM / WhatsApp)
        ↓
Reply Received → Qualify (BANT: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline)
        ↓
Discovery Call (30 min — uncover pain, confirm fit)
        ↓
Proposal Sent (within 24 hrs of discovery call)
        ↓
Follow Up (Day 2, Day 5, Day 10 if no response)
        ↓
Negotiation → Agreement signed + NDA signed
        ↓
Client Onboarded (Kick-off call + project set up in Notion)
```

### ICP — Nivy Next

- **Industry:** Professional services, e-commerce, SaaS, recruitment, healthcare
- **Size:** 5–100 employees
- **Revenue:** £500K–£20M
- **Pain:** No consistent lead flow, over-reliant on referrals, no systematic outreach or digital presence
- **Decision maker:** Founder, MD, Head of Marketing
- **Channels:** LinkedIn, cold email, Google Search (UK-primary); WhatsApp (UAE)
- **Disqualifiers:** Under £200K revenue, B2C only, no marketing budget at all

## ⚙️ Operations Department

### How Work Flows at Nivy Next

```
New Client Won
     ↓
Kick-off call → Project created in projects_database
     ↓
Tasks broken down → Assigned in tasks_database (Brand=Next)
     ↓
VAs execute → Outputs logged (Output Link field)
     ↓
QC Review (Manager) → QC Status = Approved
     ↓
Delivery to Client → Client Delivery Report sent
     ↓
Weekly check-in call → Feedback logged
     ↓
Monthly KPI review → Report in reports_database
```
