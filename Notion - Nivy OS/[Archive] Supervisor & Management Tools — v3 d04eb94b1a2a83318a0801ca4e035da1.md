## Your daily 5 tasks as a supervisor

1. Run morning brief — assign each VA their targets for the day
2. Spot-check 5 messages per VA — flag any script violations immediately
3. Review midday checkpoint reports — identify anyone below 50% by 1 PM
4. Push underperformers before end of shift — coach or escalate
5. Submit team summary report by 7:00 PM
