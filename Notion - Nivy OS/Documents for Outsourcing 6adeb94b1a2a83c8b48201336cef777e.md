## **1. Planning & Scope Documents**

1. **Scope of Work (SOW)**
    - Details exactly what tasks will be outsourced
    - Includes timelines, deliverables, and performance expectations
2. **Project Requirements / Brief**
    - Background information about your business
    - Goals, target audience, processes, and current systems
3. **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
    - Metrics to measure the outsourcing partner’s performance
    - Examples: leads generated, sales conversion rates, content quality, response time

---

## **2. Legal & Contractual Documents**

1. **Outsourcing / Service Agreement**
    - Roles and responsibilities
    - Deliverables and deadlines
    - Payment terms and penalties
    - Termination clauses
2. **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)**
    - Protects sensitive business information
3. **Non-Compete Agreement (optional)**
    - Prevents the partner from working with competitors during or after engagement
4. **Intellectual Property (IP) Assignment / Ownership**
    - Ensures work produced (content, designs, software, processes) belongs to you

---

## **3. Partner Selection & Proposal Documents**

1. **Request for Proposal (RFP) / Request for Quotation (RFQ)**
    - Sent to potential outsourcing agencies
    - Includes project details, expectations, timelines, budget, and evaluation criteria
2. **Proposal Evaluation Sheet**
    - Used to compare multiple agency proposals side by side
3. **Reference / Background Check Forms**
    - For verifying past clients, case studies, and reputation

---

## **4. Operational & Onboarding Documents**

1. **Onboarding Checklist**
    - Access to systems (CRM, email, cloud storage)
    - Tools and software credentials
    - Branding and marketing material
    - Internal process documentation
2. **Communication Plan**
    - Points of contact, escalation process
    - Frequency of reports and meetings
3. **Workflow / Process Documentation**
    - Step-by-step instructions for how tasks should be completed
4. **Reporting Templates**
    - Standardized formats for weekly/monthly updates
    - Example: lead tracker, campaign performance dashboard

---

## **5. Payment & Performance Documents**

1. **Payment Schedule / Terms Document**
    - How and when payments will be made
    - Retainers, milestones, or commission structures
2. **Performance Review Template**
    - For evaluating the partner’s delivery periodically
    - Helps determine renewals, bonuses, or corrective action

---

## **6. Optional Supporting Documents**

1. **Service Level Agreement (SLA)**
    - Defines minimum service standards and response times
2. **Brand Guidelines / Marketing Kit**
    - Ensures partners use your branding correctly
3. **Training Materials / SOPs**
    - Standard Operating Procedures for repetitive tasks
    - Helps maintain quality and consistency
4. **Franchise / Authorization Letter (if multi-level partners)**
    - Allows regional partners to recruit or manage sub-partners under your brand

---

✅ **In short:** At minimum, for outsourcing, you need:

**SOW + Contract + NDA + RFP + Onboarding Documents + Reporting Templates + Payment Terms.**

Everything else (SLA, training materials, brand guidelines) ensures **quality, consistency, and scalability**.
