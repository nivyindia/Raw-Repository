## 💰 Sales Department

### Two-Sided Marketplace Model

**Suppliers** (UAE businesses selling goods/services):

- Free basic listing → Paid featured listing £150/month
- Commission on closed deals: 5–10%

**Buyers** (UK/AUS/US businesses sourcing from UAE):

- Free access to directory

## 🇺🇦 UAE Outreach

### UAE-Specific Outreach Rules

- **WhatsApp first** — Most UAE businesses respond faster on WhatsApp than email
- **Personal tone** — Relationship-first; avoid overly corporate language
- **Bilingual messages** — Send in English + offer Arabic follow-up
- **Timing:** Sunday–Thursday (UAE working week); avoid Friday
- **Best response window:** 10 AM–12 PM GST
