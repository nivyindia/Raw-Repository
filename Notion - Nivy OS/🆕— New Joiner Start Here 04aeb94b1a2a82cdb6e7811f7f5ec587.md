# Step 2 — Understand the System in 60 Seconds

Nivy runs a VA-powered client acquisition engine. Here is how it works:

1. **VAs find leads** — they research businesses that match our Ideal Client Profile (ICP)
2. **VAs send outreach** — personalised messages on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Email, and Instagram
3. **VAs follow up** — 80% of bookings come from follow-ups 3, 4, or 5
4. **VAs book calls** — when a lead shows interest, the VA schedules a sales call
5. **Closers close deals** — the sales team takes the call and converts leads to clients
6. **Revenue is generated** — the VA team has directly created it

Your job, regardless of your role, is to make one part of this engine run better.

# Step 3 — Know the Rules

These 5 rules apply to everyone at Nivy, regardless of role:

1. **Document everything** — if it is not in the CRM or the report, it did not happen
2. **Submit on time** — daily reports by 7:00 PM, no exceptions
3. **Follow the SOP first** — do not improvise before you understand the system
4. **Escalate, don't guess** — if you are unsure, ask your supervisor or check the FAQ before taking action
5. **Protect data** — lead lists, client names, and CRM data are confidential. Do not export, share, or screenshot without permission
