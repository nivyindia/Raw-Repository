**What is Nivy Academy?**

Nivy Academy is Nivy's training, education, and professional development division. It will serve Nivy team members, GAAP affiliates, and the broader Nivy community with structured learning content, certification programmes, and skills development aligned with Nivy's business ecosystem.

## 🎯 Vision & Mission

**Vision:** To be the go-to learning platform for anyone entering Nivy's ecosystem — whether as a team member, affiliate, partner, or community learner.

**Mission:** Deliver structured, practical, and internationally relevant training that turns newcomers into confident contributors and scales Nivy's human capital without heavy hiring.
