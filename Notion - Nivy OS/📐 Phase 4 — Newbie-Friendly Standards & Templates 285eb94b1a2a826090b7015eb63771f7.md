# 📐 Phase 4 — Newbie-Friendly Standards & Templates

> This section defines the standards every page should follow after restructuring. It also contains copy-paste templates that can be applied to any new page in the Nivy knowledge base.
> 

---

## 💡 Why Standards Matter

Imagine joining Nivy on your first day. You open Notion and see:

- A page called `"Overview "`
- A page called `"ANNEXURE E: APPROVED PRODUCTS/SERVICES & PRICING"`
- A page called `"New page"`

You have no idea what any of these are, where to start, or whether they're current.

With standards, every page tells you immediately:

- **What it is** (from the title)
- **Whether it's current** (from the status tag)
- **Who owns it** (from the owner field)
- **What's inside** (from the 2-sentence description at the top)

---

## 🏷️ Standard 1: Every Page Must Have a Status Tag

At the top of every page, add one of these callout blocks:

**🟢 Live / Active:**

> 🟢 **Status: Live** | Owner: [Name] | Last Updated: [Month Year]
> 

**🟡 Draft / Work in Progress:**

> 🟡 **Status: Draft** | Owner: [Name] | Last Updated: [Month Year]
> 

**🟠 Needs Review:**

> 🟠 **Status: Needs Review** | Owner: [Name] | Last Updated: [Month Year] | Please review by: [Date]
> 

**🖤 Archived:**

> 🖤 **Status: Archived** | This page is kept for historical reference. Do not use for active work.
> 

---

## 📚 Standard 2: Every Page Needs a Plain-English Description

The very first line after the status tag should answer: *"What is this page and who should read it?"*

**Bad example:**

> ANNEXURE E: APPROVED PRODUCTS/SERVICES & PRICING
> 

**Good example:**

> This page lists all approved products and services that Nivy Global offers, along with their official prices. Use this as your reference when creating client proposals or pricing quotes.
>

## 📢 Onboarding Guide for New Team Members

*This is the text that should go into every division's Start Here page*

### Welcome to Nivy Digital — Here's How Our Notion Works

**Nivy uses Notion as our company knowledge base.** Think of it like a shared digital office — every document, plan, SOP, and project lives here.

**How it's organised:**

- **Nivy HQ** — Start here. Company overview, org chart, glossary.
- **Nivy OS** — The engine room. All databases, SOPs, projects.
- **Division Teamspaces** — Nivy Global, Nexus, Next, Academy, etc. Each is its own focused area.

**The 3 things you need on Day 1:**

1. Read the Glossary so you understand our terms
2. Find your division's teamspace and read its Start Here page
3. Check your name in the Team Directory

**If you can't find something:** Search in Notion (Ctrl/Cmd + P) or ask your team lead.

**If something looks wrong or outdated:** Leave a comment on that page and tag your team lead.

## 🔄 Maintenance Standards (Ongoing)

| Task | Frequency | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review page statuses (are they still Live/Draft?) | Monthly | Team leads |
| Check for blank pages needing content | Monthly | Department heads |
| Archive pages no longer in use | Quarterly | Any team member |
| Update Team Directory | When someone joins/leaves | HR / Nivy Jobs |
| Review and update SOPs | Every 6 months | Process owners |
| Add new pages to all_pages_index database | When creating any new page | Creator |
