# ANNEXURE J: INSURANCE REQUIREMENTS

This Annexure J forms an integral part of the Master Sales Agency Partner Agreement dated [Date] between Billion Dreams United (Nivy) and [Master Partner Company Name].

## **SECTION 1: MANDATORY INSURANCE COVERAGE**

### **1.1 Required Insurance Policies**

**1.1.1 Professional Liability Insurance (Errors & Omissions):**

- **Minimum Coverage:** $2,000,000 per claim and in aggregate
- **Coverage Scope:** Errors, omissions, negligent acts
- **Retroactive Date:** Must precede Agreement Effective Date
- **Extended Reporting Period:** Minimum 12 months post-termination

**1.1.2 Cyber Liability Insurance:**

- **Minimum Coverage:** $1,000,000 per incident
- **Coverage Must Include:**
    - Data breach response costs
    - Regulatory defense and penalties
    - Business interruption losses
    - Cyber extortion and ransomware
    - Network security liability

**1.1.3 General Commercial Liability Insurance:**

- **Minimum Coverage:** $1,000,000 per occurrence
- **Coverage Must Include:**
    - Bodily injury and property damage
    - Personal and advertising injury
    - Products and completed operations
    - Fire damage legal liability

**1.1.4 Workers' Compensation Insurance:**

- **Coverage:** Statutory requirements in all operating jurisdictions
- **Employer's Liability:** $500,000 minimum
- **Coverage Territory:** Worldwide where employees are located

### **1.2 Additional Recommended Coverage**

**1.2.1 Directors and Officers Liability:**

- **Recommended Coverage:** $1,000,000
- **Coverage:** Management decisions and oversight

**1.2.2 Crime/Fidelity Insurance:**

- **Recommended Coverage:** $500,000
- **Coverage:** Employee dishonesty, theft, fraud

**1.2.3 International Liability:**

- **Recommended Coverage:** $1,000,000
- **Coverage:** Operations in foreign jurisdictions

## **SECTION 2: INSURANCE POLICY REQUIREMENTS**

### **2.1 Policy Conditions & Endorsements**

**2.1.1 Additional Insured Status:**

- Company must be named as Additional Insured
- Coverage must be primary and non-contributory
- Waiver of subrogation in favor of Company
- Separation of insureds provision

**2.1.2 Policy Endorsements Required:**

- **30-Day Notice of Cancellation:** To Company
- **Coverage Territory:** Worldwide operations
- **Amendment:** No material changes without 30-day notice
- **Claims Made:** Extended reporting period if policy cancelled

### **2.2 Insurance Carrier Requirements**

**2.2.1 Carrier Qualifications:**

- **Financial Rating:** A.M. Best rating of "A-" or better
- **Licensing:** Admitted carriers in jurisdictions of operation
- **Experience:** Minimum 5 years writing similar coverage
- **Claims Handling:** 24/7 claims reporting capability

**2.2.2 Prohibited Carriers:**

- Non-admitted carriers without prior approval
- Carriers with poor claims payment history
- Companies with financial instability
- Unrated or below "B+" rated carriers

## **SECTION 3: CERTIFICATE OF INSURANCE REQUIREMENTS**

### **3.1 Certificate Specifications**

**3.1.1 Required Information:**

- Insurance company name and contact information
- Policy numbers and effective/expiration dates
- Coverage types and limits
- Additional insured endorsements
- Cancellation notice provisions

**3.1.2 Certificate Delivery:**

- **Initial Certificate:** Within 30 days of Agreement signing
- **Renewal Certificates:** 30 days prior to policy expiration
- **Updated Certificates:** Immediately upon policy changes
- **Format:** PDF via email to designated Company contact

### **3.2 Verification & Compliance**

**3.2.1 Company Verification Rights:**

- Right to verify coverage directly with carriers
- Access to policy documents upon request
- Audit of insurance compliance annually
- Right to require increased coverage if risk profile changes

**3.2.2 Non-Compliance Consequences:**

- **Immediate Notice:** Of any policy lapse or cancellation
- **Cure Period:** 15 days to obtain replacement coverage
- **Suspension:** Business operations suspended during lapse
- **Termination:** Material breach if not cured within 30 days

## **SECTION 4: CLAIMS REPORTING & MANAGEMENT**

### **4.1 Incident Reporting Procedures**

**4.1.1 Immediate Notification:**

- **24 Hours:** For any incident that may lead to a claim
- **48 Hours:** Written notice with incident details
- **Ongoing Updates:** Weekly until claim resolution
- **Final Report:** Upon claim closure

**4.1.2 Reportable Incidents:**

- Any actual or alleged data breach
- Professional errors or omissions claims
- Workplace injuries or accidents
- Property damage or third-party injuries
- Regulatory investigations or actions

### **4.2 Claims Cooperation**

**4.2.1 Investigation Assistance:**

- Provide complete access to relevant information
- Cooperate with insurance carrier investigations
- Assist in defense preparation and strategy
- Make employees available for interviews and testimony

**4.2.2 Settlement Authority:**

- No settlement without Company consent if Company is named
- Company approval required for settlement terms
- Joint defense agreement for shared interests
- Confidentiality in settlement negotiations

## **SECTION 6: INSURANCE COSTS & BUDGETING**

### **6.1 Cost Responsibility**

**6.1.1 Premium Payments:**

- Master Partner responsible for all insurance costs
- No reimbursement from Company for premiums
- Budget for annual premium increases (5-10%)
- Consideration for deductible and self-insured retention

**6.1.2 Deductibles and Retentions:**

- **Maximum Deductible:** $10,000 per claim without approval
- **Higher Deductibles:** Require Company written consent
- **Self-Insured Retention:** Must be disclosed and approved
- **Claim Expenses:** Outside policy limits unless specified

### **6.2 Insurance Budget Planning**

**6.2.1 Annual Budget Allocation:**

- **Professional Liability:** 40-60% of insurance budget
- **Cyber Liability:** 20-30% of insurance budget
- **General Liability:** 10-20% of insurance budget
- **Other Coverages:** 10-20% of insurance budget

**6.2.2 Cost Management Strategies:**

- Multi-year policies for premium stability
- Bundled coverage for premium discounts
- Risk management programs for premium reductions
- Regular market reviews for competitive pricing
