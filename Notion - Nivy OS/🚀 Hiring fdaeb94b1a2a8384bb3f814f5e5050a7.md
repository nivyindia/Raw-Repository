## 🧠 Ideal VA Profile

Use this as a filter when screening applicants. A candidate who meets 4 out of 5 criteria is worth interviewing.

| Criteria | What to Look For | Red Flag |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Communication | Basic to strong English writing. Clear, professional messages. | Cannot write a coherent 3-sentence message |
| Tool Access | Working laptop or desktop. Stable internet. Access to LinkedIn. | No laptop, mobile-only, or unreliable connection |
| Availability | Can commit to defined daily hours and submitting a report every day | Cannot commit to a schedule, wants flexible no-check-in work |
| Attitude | Willing to follow SOPs precisely without improvising in the first 30 days | Wants to do it “their way” before understanding the system |
| Coachability | Responds positively to feedback. Asks questions. Takes notes. | Defensive when corrected. Repeats mistakes without acknowledgement. |

> Prior experience is not required. Attitude and consistency beat background every time.
