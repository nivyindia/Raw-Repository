# Sample Engagement Letter – Outline (1)

## 

### **1. Header Information**

- Firm Name, Address, Contact Details
- Client Name, Address, Contact Details
- Date of Engagement
- Subject: *Engagement Letter for [Service Type – e.g., Tax Advisory / Accounting & Compliance Services]*

---

### **2. Introduction & Purpose**

Briefly explain the purpose of the letter:

> “This letter confirms our understanding of the terms and objectives of our engagement and the nature and limitations of the services we will provide.”
> 

---

### **3. Scope of Services**

Clearly outline what services are included, e.g.:

- Preparation and filing of tax returns (corporate / individual)
- Accounting and bookkeeping support
- Payroll processing
- Financial statement preparation and review
- Regulatory compliance and advisory
- Any exclusions (e.g., audit, assurance, or legal representation unless specified)

---

### **4. Responsibilities**

### **Firm’s Responsibilities**

- Deliver all agreed services with **due professional care**, diligence, and in accordance with applicable **accounting, auditing, and tax standards**.
- Maintain the **confidentiality and security** of all client information, documents, and communications in line with professional ethics and data protection laws.
- Keep the client **informed of key findings, progress updates, and regulatory developments** that may impact their business or compliance obligations.
- Ensure that work is performed only by **qualified professionals or supervised personnel** in adherence to firm quality standards.

---

### **Client’s Responsibilities**

- Provide **complete, accurate, and timely information** and supporting documentation necessary for the execution of services.
- Ensure **prompt communication, review, and approval** of deliverables, queries, and filings to meet statutory deadlines.
- Retain **full responsibility for management decisions**, internal controls, and compliance actions arising from the firm’s advice or reports.
- Inform the firm promptly of any **significant changes** in business structure, ownership, or financial position that may affect the engagement.

---

### **5.Deliverables & Timelines**

- The Firm will provide **specified deliverables** such as financial statements, management reports, statutory filings, and tax returns in accordance with the agreed scope of work.
- Each deliverable will follow a defined **schedule or frequency** (monthly, quarterly, or annually), mutually agreed upon at the start of the engagement or as updated from time to time.
- All submissions, queries, and approvals will be managed through **secure communication channels** (e.g., email, client portal, or shared workspace) to ensure accuracy, timeliness, and confidentiality.
- Any adjustments to timelines due to **regulatory changes, client delays, or unforeseen circumstances** will be communicated and agreed upon promptly.

---

### **6. Fees & Payment Terms**

- The Firm’s professional fees shall be based on the **agreed fee structure**, which may be **fixed, retainer-based, hourly, or project-specific**, depending on the nature and duration of the engagement.
- **Invoices** will be raised as per the **billing frequency** mutually agreed upon (e.g., monthly, quarterly, or upon completion of defined milestones).
- **Payments** are due within **[X] days** from the date of invoice, unless otherwise stated. Late payments may attract interest or suspension of services until settlement.
- Any **out-of-pocket expenses** (such as statutory filing fees, travel, or third-party costs) incurred in connection with the engagement shall be **reimbursed at actuals**, with prior client approval where applicable.
- All fees are **exclusive of applicable taxes**, which will be charged as per prevailing laws.

---

### **7. Confidentiality & Data Protection**

- The Firm is committed to **protecting all client information** in accordance with recognized international standards, including **GDPR, SOC 2, and ISO certifications**, ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data.
- All work will be conducted using **secure systems, encrypted communications, and restricted access controls** to prevent unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure.
- Client data will only be **shared with authorized personnel, regulators, or third parties** as required by law or agreed in writing, and strictly for the purposes of fulfilling the engagement.
- Both parties shall **take all reasonable measures** to safeguard sensitive information throughout and after the term of the engagement.

---

### **8. Limitation of Liability**

- The Firm’s liability for any claims, losses, or damages arising from this engagement shall be **limited to the total fees paid by the Client for the specific services under this engagement**.
- The Firm shall **not be liable for any indirect, consequential, or incidental losses**, including but not limited to loss of profit, business interruption, or reputational damage.
- Nothing in this engagement letter shall **limit liability for fraud, willful misconduct, or gross negligence** as determined under applicable law.
- The Client acknowledges and agrees that the limitations set forth are **reasonable and form an integral part of the terms of this engagement**.

---

### **9. Professional Standards & Compliance**

- All services will be performed in accordance with **applicable professional standards**, including those set by **CPA, ACCA, ICAI**, and relevant local regulations, ensuring accuracy, integrity, and compliance.
- The Firm commits to maintaining the **highest ethical and professional conduct**, acting with independence, objectivity, and due care throughout the engagement.
- The Client can expect that all advice, reports, and recommendations are provided **with professional judgment, transparency, and in line with recognized best practices**.
- Any updates to regulatory or professional standards affecting the engagement will be **communicated promptly** to ensure ongoing compliance.

---

### **10. Term & Termination**

- This engagement shall commence on **[Start Date]** and continue until **[End Date]** or until terminated by either party in accordance with the terms below.
- Either party may **terminate the engagement** by providing **[30] days’ written notice** to the other party, unless otherwise mutually agreed.
- Upon termination, the Firm will **complete any work in progress** to the extent feasible and provide the Client with all deliverables prepared up to the termination date.
- The Client shall **settle all outstanding fees and reimbursable expenses** incurred up to the effective date of termination.
- Termination shall **not affect any rights or obligations accrued prior to termination**, including confidentiality obligations and limitation of liability provisions.

---

### **11. Dispute Resolution**

- In the event of any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this engagement, the parties shall first attempt to **resolve the matter amicably through good-faith negotiations**.
- If the dispute cannot be resolved through negotiation within a reasonable period, it shall be referred to **mediation or arbitration** in accordance with the rules of **[specify arbitration institution, e.g., ICC or local arbitration rules]**.
- The engagement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of **[India / UK / UAE]**, and any arbitration or legal proceedings shall be subject to the **exclusive jurisdiction** of the competent courts or arbitration forums in that jurisdiction.
- Both parties agree to **act in good faith and cooperate fully** to resolve disputes efficiently, minimizing disruption to services.

---
