### **Company Description**

**1. Company Overview**
Nivy Tech LLC is a Texas-based limited liability company operating as a full-service digital marketing agency. We provide data-driven marketing solutions—including Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Pay-Per-Click (PPC) advertising, social media management, and content strategy—primarily to small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs). Our core operational model is based on monthly retainer agreements, where we act as an outsourced, expert marketing department for our clients. We are in the early growth stage, preparing for an official market launch upon securing initial funding.

**3. Mission Statement**
Our mission is to empower small and medium-sized businesses to thrive online by delivering transparent, effective, and scalable digital marketing strategies that drive measurable growth and maximize return on investment.

**4. Vision Statement**
Our vision is to become the most trusted and results-driven digital marketing partner for SMBs in the Southwestern United States, recognized for our integrity, innovation, and unwavering commitment to client success.

**5. Core Values**
Our company culture and every client interaction are guided by four fundamental principles:

- **Transparency:** We believe in radical honesty. Our clients have 24/7 access to their performance data through our proprietary dashboard. There are no hidden fees, confusing reports, or secrets. We operate as an open book.
- **Accountability:** We take ownership of our results. We are not successful unless our clients' goals are met. Our strategies are built around achieving specific, measurable Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) that directly impact our clients' bottom lines.
- **Expertise:** We are committed to mastery. The digital landscape is constantly evolving, and we dedicate significant resources to continuous learning, certification, and innovation to ensure our strategies are cutting-edge and effective.
- **Partnership:** We view ourselves as an extension of our clients' teams. We invest time in understanding their business intricacies, goals, and challenges. Our success is intrinsically linked to their success.
