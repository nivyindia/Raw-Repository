# 

A strong international payment strategy ensures predictable revenue, lower risk, and global compliance. This section outlines SOPs and policies to manage payments, refunds, chargebacks, and bad debt — all tailored for agencies serving Western clients with offshore delivery teams.

---

## **1. Payment Collection SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)**

**A. Before Starting Work**

- Send **formal proposal + contract** via PandaDoc / DocuSign.
- Collect **mandatory upfront payment** (40–60% depending on service).
- Create client folder in CRM + accounting tool (Wave/QuickBooks/Odoo).

**B. Accepted Payment Channels**

- Wise Business Account (primary)
- Payoneer
- Stripe (for card payments)
- Bank wire transfer (US/EU/UK)

**C. Payment SOP Steps**

1. Send invoice with clear due dates and currency (USD/EUR/GBP).
2. Automate reminders:
    - 3 days before due
    - On due date
    - 3 days after due
3. Provide multiple payment options (Wise link, Stripe link).
4. Start project work only after receiving upfront payment.
5. Log payment in accounting system for reconciliation.

---

## **2. Payment Milestone Strategy (International Best Practice)**

To reduce non-payment risk, use milestone-based billing:

### **Marketing Services (SEO, SMM, Ads)**

- **50% upfront**
- **50% after mid-month update** or before delivering final report

### **Websites/Funnels**

- **40% upfront**
- **30% on design approval**
- **30% before launch**

### **Consulting + Retainers**

- **100% paid in advance for every month**
- No work without renewal

### Why Milestones?

✔ Eliminates bad debt

✔ Maintains cash flow

✔ Reduces risk of clients ghosting

✔ Matches Western agency standards

## **3. Refund Policy (Western Standard)**

Refund rules to protect agency margins and avoid disputes:

### **Refunds Allowed:**

- Overpayment
- Duplicate payment
- Service not delivered AND no work started
- If agency misses deadlines by more than 30 days (rare)

### **Refunds Not Allowed:**

- If work has begun
- If client delays milestone approvals
- If client changes requirements
- For performance outcomes (ads, SEO, etc.)
- If client fails to provide content/access/logins

### **Refund Window:**

Strict **7-day refund window** from payment date.

### **Refund Fee (industry standard):**

- 10–15% processing fee
    
    (covers currency conversion, Payoneer/Wise charges, time spent)

## **4. Chargeback Prevention**

Chargebacks are common in international card payments (Stripe). Prevent them by:

### A. Documentation

- Signed contract
- Project timeline
- Scope of work
- Proof of communication
- All deliverables logged

### B. Transparent Reporting

Share weekly/monthly updates via Loom + email.

### C. Strict Access Policy

Do not give full admin access until payment is fully cleared.

### D. Pre-chargeback Actions

If a client threatens chargeback:

- Offer resolution call
- Provide proof of work
- Provide partial refund if reasonable

### Chargeback Win Rate Boost

Maintain **clear evidence** and always respond within 3 days.

## **5. Bad Debt Reserve (2–5%)**

Set aside **2–5% of total revenue** as a financial safety buffer.

Why?

- International clients may delay or cancel
- Currency drops
- Chargebacks
- Non-payment of last milestone

Example:

If you make $10,000/month → keep $200–$500 aside.

---

## **6. Late Fee or Penalty Structure**

Standard global late fee rules:

### **Retainers**

- 3-day grace → no fee
- 4–14 days → **5% late fee**
- 15–30 days → **10% late fee** applied
- After 30 days → work paused + account frozen

### **Projects**

- No milestone approval → project auto-pauses after 7 days
- Restart fee: **$50–$150** depending on complexity

Clear penalty rules prevent misuse and force discipline.

## **7. Debt Collection Escalation Steps**

### **Level 1 — Friendly Reminder**

- Automated reminders + 1 email

### **Level 2 — Project Pause Notice**

- Work paused
- Client informed that delay is impacting delivery

### **Level 3 — Legal Reminder**

- Send email from a legal template
- Reference contract clauses

### **Level 4 — Debt Recovery Partner**

For Western clients:

- Collectors like **Summit A*R, Daniels Silverman, Atradius**

For Indian clients:

- Local recovery agencies

### **Level 5 — Write-Off**

- After 90–120 days, mark as **bad debt** and close books
- Covered by 2–5% reserve

## **8. How to Protect Revenue Using Contracts**

Your contract must include:

### **A. Scope of Work Clearly Defined**

- Deliverables
- Timelines
- What is NOT included
- Number of revisions
- Responsibility matrix

### **B. Payment Protection Clauses**

- No work without upfront payment
- Milestones must be paid before delivery
- All payments non-refundable once work starts

### **C. Dispute Resolution**

- US/UK/EU clients → arbitration first, then courts
- Jurisdiction clearly stated

### **D. Chargeback Clause**

- Any chargeback without prior dispute discussion = breach of contract
- Client liable for legal and recovery fees

### **E. Access & IP Rights**

- Final ownership only after full and final payment

### **F. Termination Clause**

- Minimum 30-day notice
- Payment for work completed is mandatory
