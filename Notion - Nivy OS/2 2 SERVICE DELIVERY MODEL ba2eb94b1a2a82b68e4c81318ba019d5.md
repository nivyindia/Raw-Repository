# 2.2 SERVICE DELIVERY MODEL

## **2.2 SERVICE DELIVERY MODEL**

### **Client Success Framework:**

### **Phase 1: Strategic Partnership Onboarding**

- **(Days 1-30)**

**Week 1: Discovery & Planning**

- Business Growth Workshop (4 hours)
- Complete marketing and business audit
- Goal setting and KPI establishment
- Technology stack assessment

**Week 2-3: Strategy Development**

- Comprehensive marketing strategy creation
- Implementation timeline development
- Team introduction and role assignments
- Communication protocol establishment

**Week 4: Launch Preparation**

- Final strategy presentation and approval
- Campaign setup and technical implementation
- Reporting dashboard configuration
- First month action plan finalization

### **Phase 2: Execution & Optimization**

***(Ongoing Monthly Cycle)***

**Weekly Rhythm:**

- Monday: Campaign performance review
- Wednesday: Team sync and progress update
- Friday: Client weekly summary report

**Monthly Rhythm:**

- Week 1: Monthly planning and goal setting
- Week 2: Mid-month performance review
- Week 3: Optimization implementation
- Week 4: Monthly reporting and analysis

**Quarterly Rhythm:**

- Quarterly Business Review (QBR) preparation
- Strategy refinement and adjustment
- Performance bonus calculations
- Forward-looking planning session

---

### **Team Structure & Roles:**

### **Dedicated Client Pod:**

- **Growth Strategist**: Primary client contact, strategy development
- **Channel Specialist**: Execution expert (SEO, PPC, Social, Content)
- **Data Analyst**: Performance tracking and insights
- **Project Coordinator**: Timeline management and communication

### **Support Resources:**

- Creative team (design, video, copywriting)
- Technical development resources
- Strategic advisory board

## **2.4 QUALITY ASSURANCE & CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT**

### **Service Delivery Standards:**

### **Performance Guarantees:**

- 48-hour response time guarantee for client communications
- 99.9% uptime for managed campaigns and technologies
- Monthly performance reporting delivered by 5th business day
- Quarterly strategy reviews with documented action plans

### **Quality Control Processes:**

- Weekly internal campaign reviews
- Monthly client satisfaction surveys
- Quarterly service delivery audits
- Annual technology stack evaluation

### **Client Success Measurements:**

### **Quantitative Metrics:**

- Client revenue growth against targets
- Campaign ROI and performance metrics
- Client retention and satisfaction scores
- Referral rates and client advocacy

### **Qualitative Metrics:**

- Client strategic alignment
- Communication effectiveness
- Partnership satisfaction
- Strategic value perception

## **2.5 OPERATIONAL POLICIES**

### **Client Relationship Management:**

### **Communication Protocol:**

- Primary: Slack for daily communication
- Secondary: Email for formal communications
- Scheduled: Weekly video calls for updates
- Strategic: Monthly executive reviews

### **Escalation Procedures:**

- Level 1: Growth Strategist (immediate issues)
- Level 2: Operations Manager (persistent challenges)
- Level 3: Agency Principal (strategic concerns)

### **Financial Operations:**

### **Billing Structure:**

- Retainer invoices issued monthly in advance
- Performance bonuses calculated quarterly in arrears
- Project fees structured with 50% deposit, 50% on completion
- Net 15 payment terms for all invoices

### **Guarantee Terms:**

- Clear performance metrics established in contract
- 90-day performance evaluation period
- Remediation plan before guarantee invocation
- Specific conditions and exclusions clearly defined
