## 🪝 PART 3: HOOK TEMPLATES

Use these as openers for any post. Customise the specifics.

**Problem Hooks:**

- “Most [audience] are spending money on [problem area] and getting nothing back. Here’s why.”
- “You’re not bad at marketing. Your setup is.”
- “We audited 47 growth-stage businesses. 83% had the same broken funnel.”

## 📝 PART 4: CAPTION FRAMEWORKS

### Framework 1: HOOK → PROBLEM → INSIGHT → CTA

*(Best for: LinkedIn thought leadership, educational Instagram carousel)*

```
[Hook — 1–2 lines]

[Problem — paint the pain in 2–3 lines]

[Insight — the shift, the lesson, the breakthrough in 3–4 lines]

[CTA — 1 clear action]
```

**Example:**

> Most agencies run your campaigns. Nivy Next builds your growth system.
> 

> 
> 

> The difference: campaigns end. Systems compound.
> 

> 
> 

> We’ve watched businesses spend £200K on ads with no CRM, no attribution, and no nurture sequence. The ads weren’t the problem. The infrastructure was.
> 

> 
> 

> We fix the infrastructure first. Then the ads work.
> 

> 
> 

> If your current marketing feels like a hamster wheel — DM us “AUDIT” and we’ll tell you exactly what’s broken.
> 

---

### Framework 2: RESULT → CONTEXT → BREAKDOWN → CTA

*(Best for: case study posts, proof posts)*

```
[Result stat — bold, specific]

[Context — who, what industry, what challenge]

[Breakdown — what we changed in 3–5 bullet points]

[CTA]
```

**Example:**

> 68% pipeline growth in 90 days.
> 

> 
> 

> A B2B SaaS company in the UK was spending heavily on Google Ads with no attribution and a landing page converting at 2.1%.
> 

> 
> 

> What we changed:
> 

> • Rebuilt the entire campaign architecture (ICP-driven keywords)
> 

> • Rewrote landing page copy and redesigned the layout
> 

> • Integrated HubSpot with automated lead scoring
> 

> • Cut wasteful spend by 40% and redirected it to top-performing terms
> 

> 
> 

> 90 days later: CPA down 41%, pipeline up 68%.
> 

> 
> 

> Your campaigns might not need more budget. They might need better structure. → [www.thenivy.com](http://www.thenivy.com)
>

[CTA]
