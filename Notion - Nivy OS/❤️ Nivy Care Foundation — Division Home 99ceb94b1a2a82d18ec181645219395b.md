**What is Nivy Care Foundation?**

Nivy Care Foundation is Nivy's social impact and Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) division. It channels Nivy's resources, partnerships, and team volunteer efforts toward community development, education access, and social good — anchored in India but with global ambition.

## 🌱 Vision & Mission

**Vision:** Build a credible, measurable social impact arm that reflects Nivy's values — and creates real, documented change in the communities we operate in.

**Mission:** Design and execute CSR programmes, community partnerships, and volunteer initiatives that are transparent, outcome-focused, and aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
