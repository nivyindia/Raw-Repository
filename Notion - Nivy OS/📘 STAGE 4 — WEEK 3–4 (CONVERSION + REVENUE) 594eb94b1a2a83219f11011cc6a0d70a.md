# 🎯 STEP 1 — FOCUS ON APPOINTMENT QUALITY

---

## Before booking a call, check:

- Do they have a real business?
- Do they actually need help?
- Are they interested and engaged?

---

👉 If YES → Book

👉 If NO → Don’t push

---

---

## 💡 RULE

> Fewer but better calls = more revenue
> 

---

---

# 💬 STEP 2 — BASIC OBJECTION HANDLING

---

## When lead says:

### ❓ “Not interested”

Reply:

- “No problem, just wanted to check — are you currently handling this internally?”

---

### ❓ “Too expensive”

Reply:

- “Got it — let’s first see if this can actually help your business, then you can decide”

---

### ❓ “I’ll think about it”

Reply:

- “Sure, what part are you unsure about?”

---

---

## ⚠️ RULE

- Don’t argue
- Don’t force
- Keep conversation going

---

---

# 💰 STEP 3 — PRICING AWARENESS (BASIC)

---

## If client asks pricing:

👉 DO NOT explain everything

---

### Say:

> “It depends on your requirement — we can go through it quickly on a call”
> 

---

---

## ⚠️ RULE

- Don’t send full pricing randomly
- Don’t negotiate
- Move to call

---

---

# 📞 STEP 4 — CALL BOOKING (IMPROVED)

---

## When to book:

- Lead is engaged
- Asking questions
- Shows interest

---

## What to say:

> “Let’s discuss this on a quick call — I’ll show you exactly how it works”
> 

---

---

# 🧾 STEP 5 — CALL HANDOVER SYSTEM (VERY IMPORTANT)

---

## After booking, you MUST prepare details

---

## 📋 Handover Format:

```
Client Name:
Business Type:
Requirement:
Conversation Summary:
Call Date & Time:
```

---

---

## 📌 Example:

```
Client Name: John Smith
Business: Roofing Company
Requirement: Needs more leads
Summary: Interested in ads + SEO
Call Time: Tomorrow 4 PM
```

---

---

## 🎯 WHY THIS IS IMPORTANT

👉 Helps closer:

- Understand client
- Prepare better
- Close faster
