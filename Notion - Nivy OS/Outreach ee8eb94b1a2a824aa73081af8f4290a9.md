# Outreach

### **The Multi-Channel Outreach Battle Plan**

**Phase 1: Foundation & Setup (Days 1-3)**

**Goal:** Prepar call e all assets and systems to avoid friction during execution.

1. **Define Your Ideal Client Profile (ICP):**
    - Who are they? (e.g., "CEO of a digital marketing agency with 1-10 employees")
    - What problems do they have that you solve? (e.g., "need more leads," "need to reduce freelancer management overhead")
    - **Without this, your outreach will be wasted.**
2. **Create Your "Master Message Bank":**
    - **Channel-Specific Templates:** Craft 3-4 variations of your first message for *each* channel (WhatsApp, LinkedIn DM, Email, etc.). Keep them short, personal, and focused on the client's problem.
    - **Example (LinkedIn Connection Request):** "Hi [Name], I saw you're also in the digital marketing space. I help agency owners like yourself generate more leads without increasing overhead. Would it be okay to share a quick idea?"
    - **Example (WhatsApp):** "Hi [Name], [Mutual Connection?] mentioned you're growing your agency. I have a system that helped them get 15+ qualified leads a week. Open to a quick chat?"
3. **Setup Your Tech Stack:**
    - **CRM:** HubSpot CRM (Free). Create a deal pipeline.
    - **Tracking Spreadsheet:** Google Sheet with columns: `Date | Channel | Action | Target Profile | Message Variant | Response (Y/N) | Lead? | Notes`.
    - **Scheduling:** Calendly linked to your calendar.
    - **Design:** Canva templates for quick social posts.
4. **Profile Optimization:**
    - Ensure *your* social profiles (especially LinkedIn) are professional and clearly state what you do and who you help. This is your landing page for when people check you out.

**Phase 2: The Daily Execution Rhythm (Ongoing)**

**Goal: Hit your daily touch targets with disciplined consistency.**

**The Daily Schedule:**

- **Block 1 (9:00 AM - 11:00 AM): Content & Social Posting.**
    - Schedule your **5 daily posts** (Reels, Group Posts, Image Posts) using your scheduler.
    - Execute your **100 social actions** (Likes, Comments, Shares). Focus on your ICP's content.
- **Block 2 (11:00 AM - 1:00 PM): Proactive Outreach #1.**
    - Focus on **LinkedIn and Email.**
    - Send your **50 LinkedIn Connection Requests** with personalized notes.
    - Send your **50 Personalized Emails.**
- **Block 3 (2:00 PM - 4:00 PM): Proactive Outreach #2.**
    - Focus on **WhatsApp, Facebook/Instagram DMs, and Telegram.**
    - Send your **25 WhatsApp** messages.
    - Send your **30 FB/IG DMs.**
    - Send your **10 Telegram DMs.**
- **Block 4 (4:00 PM - 5:00 PM): Follow-ups & Admin.**
    - Respond to all incoming messages.
    - **Log every single action and its result** in your tracking spreadsheet.
    - Send Calendly links to positive responses and schedule calls immediately.

**Phase 3: Tracking, Analysis, and Optimization (Weekly)**

**Goal: Turn activity into data, and data into better results.**

1. **Weekly Analysis (Every Friday):**
    - Review your tracking spreadsheet.
    - **Key Question:** Which channel had the highest lead conversion rate?
    - **Key Question:** Which message variant got the most positive replies?
2. **Weekly Adjustment:**
    - **Double Down:** Allocate more time and energy to the top 2 performing channels.
    - **Pivot or Pause:** Reduce or stop activity on channels with a <1% response rate.
    - **Refine:** Tweak your losing messages or replace them with new variants based on what you learned.
3. **Hiring Plan:**
    - Once you consistently hit your targets and have a proven conversion rate, **document your entire process.**
    - Your first hire will be a **Virtual Assistant** who you will train to execute *exactly* this battle plan, while you move into a full-time sales closer role.

### **Critical Rules for Success**

1. **Personalization is Key:** Never blast generic messages. Use their name, their company name, or reference a recent post. Automation is for scaling, not for the initial touch.
2. **Provide Value First:** Your first message should be about *them*, not you. Offer a tip, a piece of advice, or a resource (lead magnet).
3. **The 7-Touch Rule:** Don't give up after one message. Have a follow-up sequence (e.g., Day 1: Initial message, Day 3: Follow-up, Day 7: Final follow-up).
4. **Track Relentlessly:** If you don't measure it, you can't improve it. The spreadsheet is your most important tool.
5. **Compliance:** Be aware of platform rules (especially WhatsApp and LinkedIn) to avoid bans. Be human, not a spam bot.

This plan is a grind, but it's a purposeful grind. By following this structure, you are not just "doing social media," you are running a targeted, data-driven customer acquisition campaign.

### **Global Business Messaging & Outreach Guide**

This table summarizes the primary channels for **personalized business communication** after initial contact. The first point of contact should almost always be a professional **Email** or **LinkedIn** message.

| **Region** | **Country** | **Best First Contact** | **Primary Business Messaging Apps** | **Key Insight & Cultural Note** | **Move to Chat After Permission** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **West** | USA | **Email** is king. **LinkedIn** InMail is excellent. | **Slack, MS Teams, Email Gandhi** | Apps like Slack/Teams are for **established** relationships. Cold messaging on personal apps (WhatsApp/SMS) is seen as intrusive and unprofessional. | Only if specifically invited. "Let's move this to Slack for the project." |
| **West** | Canada | **Email**, **LinkedIn** | **Slack, MS Teams, Email** | Very similar to the USA. Professional communication is valued highly. | Only after a relationship is established. |
| **West** | UK | **Email**, **LinkedIn** | **WhatsApp, Slack, Email** | More open to WhatsApp than the US, but **only after a relationship has been established.** It's still a personal space. | Can ask: "Are you on WhatsApp for quicker comms?" after building rapport. |
| **West** | Australia / NZ | **Email**, **LinkedIn** | **WhatsApp, Slack, Email** | Similar to the UK. WhatsApp is common but used cautiously for business. Professionalism is key. | Use once the client suggests it or after you've built trust. |
| **SE Asia** | Singapore | Email / LinkedIn | **WhatsApp** | The universal standard for business. Very professional and expected. | Quickly and naturally to **WhatsApp**. |
| **SE Asia** | Indonesia | Email / LinkedIn | **WhatsApp** | Absolutely dominant for all business levels. A WhatsApp Business number is standard. | Quickly and naturally to **WhatsApp**. |
| **SE Asia** | Malaysia | Email / LinkedIn | **WhatsApp** | The clear leader for all communication. WeChat is used within Chinese-Malaysian communities. | Quickly and naturally to **WhatsApp**. |
| **SE Asia** | Thailand | Email / LinkedIn | **LINE** | **LINE is non-negotiable.** It's the absolute king of all communication. Businesses have official profiles with rich features. | Essential to move to **LINE**. Not using it is a disadvantage. |
| **SE Asia** | Vietnam | Email / LinkedIn | **Zalo** | **Zalo is the dominant local champion.** It's a sign of respect and cultural understanding to use it. | Essential to move to **Zalo**. Shows you understand the market. |
| **SE Asia** | Philippines | Email / LinkedIn | **FB Messenger, Viber** | Very high Facebook penetration. Messenger is a standard customer service channel. Viber is popular for groups. | Very acceptable to move to **Messenger** or **Viber**. |
