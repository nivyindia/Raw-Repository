# ✅ **1. EDUCATIONAL CONTENT (40%)**

**Goal:** Build authority + trust

**Best Formats:** Reels, Carousels, Posts, Articles

### **A. Reels (Short Educational)**

Include:

- Hook within 1–2 seconds: “Stop doing this…do this instead.”
- A clear step-by-step tip
- On-screen text + captions
- Quick transitions
- CTA: “Save this for later”

Examples:

- “3 reasons your ads aren’t converting”
- “How to choose your marketing budget”

---

### **B. Carousels (Deep value)**

Include:

- Strong first slide “attention card”
- 4–7 slides of actionable steps
- One pain → one solution
- Final slide CTA: “DM us ‘Growth’ for free audit”

Examples:

- “7 tools every business needs in 2025”
- “5 landing page mistakes hurting your conversions”

---

### **C. Image Posts**

Include:

- Single quick tip
- Clean visuals
- One pain point addressed
- CTA: Save / Share

Examples:

- “Best posting times for 2025”
- “Top 5 free SEO tools”

---

### **D. Text Posts**

Include:

- Bold insight
- Industry perspective
- Micro storytelling
- No fluff, 1–2 lines max per point

Examples:

- “Marketing isn’t about selling. It’s about understanding buyers deeper than competitors.”

---

### **E. Articles**

Include:

- Step-by-step frameworks
- Data, charts, research
- Professional tone
- CTA at the end

Examples:

- “Complete Digital Marketing Roadmap for SMEs in 2025”

---

# ✅ **2. CASE STUDIES / SOCIAL PROOF (20%)**

**Goal:** Convert leads into clients

**Best Formats:** Reels, Carousels, Testimonials, Screenshots

### **A. Reels**

Include:

- Before → After results
- Breakdown of what you did
- Voiceover + stats
- CTA: “Want results like these? DM ‘Results’”

Examples:

- “From 5 leads to 50 leads/week—here’s how we scaled them.”

---

### **B. Carousels**

Include:

- First slide = “CASE STUDY: 3x more leads”
- Client challenge → Strategy → Results
- Clear screenshots
- CTA slide

---

### **C. Screenshot image posts**

Include:

- Real dashboard screenshots
- Short explanation
- Client confidentiality (blur sensitive fields)

---

### **D. Text posts**

Include:

- Story of journey
- Key learning
- Soft CTA

---

### **E. Articles**

Include:

- Detailed breakdown
- Metrics
- Tools used
- Process framework

---

---

# ✅ **3. OFFERS & CTAs (15%)**

**Best Formats:** Images, Carousels, Reels

- Who it’s for
- Limited-time urgency

Examples:

- “FREE 20-minute audit for first 10 businesses”

---

### **B. Carousels**

Include:

- Pain → Offer → Benefits → Call to action
- Easy to read layout

---

### **C. Image Posts**

Include:

- Clean graphic
- Offer & deadline
- Contact info

---

### **D. Text-Only Posts**

Include:

- Direct CTA
- Short problem → solution structure

---

### **E. Articles**

Include:

- Detailed description of service
- Success stories
- Links to book meeting
