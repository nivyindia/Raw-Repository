# **DEAL & ACCOUNT MANAGER — SOP (UAE COMPANIES)**

## **PHASE 3–6 WITH PASS/FAIL CONDITIONS**

---

# **PHASE 3: ORDER CONFIRMATION & PAYMENT TRACKING**

### **Objective**

Ensure buyer officially confirms order, supplier acknowledges, and payment is tracked before goods are dispatched.

---

## **Step 3.1: Buyer Order Confirmation**

**Tasks:**

- Obtain official purchase order (PO) or signed email confirmation from buyer
- Confirm quantity, product, timeline, and payment terms

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PO received or email confirmation signed | ✅ Yes → Step 3.2 | ❌ Not received → follow-up once; if >3 days → put deal “On Hold” |
| Buyer confirms quantity, product, timeline, payment terms match | ✅ Yes → Step 3.2 | ❌ Any mismatch → clarify; fail deal if unresolved |
| CRM updated with buyer confirmation | ✅ Yes → Step 3.2 | ❌ Not updated → update before proceeding |

---

## **Step 3.2: Supplier Acknowledgement**

**Tasks:**

- Confirm supplier can fulfill order, MOQ, and timeline
- Confirm compliance & readiness to deliver

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supplier confirms order acceptance | ✅ Yes → Step 3.3 | ❌ Supplier cannot fulfill → return to Phase 7 Growth Lead for alternate supplier |
| Supplier confirms timeline & MOQ | ✅ Yes → Step 3.3 | ❌ Cannot meet timeline or MOQ → fail or reschedule deal |
| Supplier confirms UAE compliance requirements met | ✅ Yes → Step 3.3 | ❌ Non-compliant → fail deal |

---

## **Step 3.3: Payment Tracking**

**Tasks:**

- Track initial payment / advance according to agreed terms
- Confirm receipt in supplier’s account before release of goods

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payment received on time | ✅ Yes → Phase 4 | ❌ Payment delayed > agreed period → escalate / pause order |
| Payment amount matches agreement | ✅ Yes → Phase 4 | ❌ Amount mismatch → clarify; fail deal if unresolved |
| Payment logged in CRM | ✅ Yes → Phase 4 | ❌ Not logged → update before proceeding |

---

# **PHASE 4: DELIVERY TRACKING & LOGISTICS**

### **Objective**

Track goods from supplier to buyer, confirm delivery, and ensure UAE-specific compliance and logistics.

---

## **Step 4.1: Supplier Dispatch Tracking**

**Tasks:**

- Confirm shipment dispatched
- Collect tracking info, airway bill, Bill of Lading, or delivery note

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shipment dispatched on agreed date | ✅ Yes → Step 4.2 | ❌ Delay → follow up immediately; escalate if repeated |
| Tracking info / documentation available | ✅ Yes → Step 4.2 | ❌ Missing docs → supplier must provide; fail if unresolved |
| CRM updated with shipment status | ✅ Yes → Step 4.2 | ❌ Not updated → update immediately |

---

## **Step 4.2: Buyer Receipt Confirmation**

**Tasks:**

- Confirm buyer received goods in correct quantity & quality
- Record feedback / complaints if any

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer confirms receipt | ✅ Yes → Phase 5 | ❌ No confirmation → follow up; put deal “On Hold” |
| Quantity matches PO | ✅ Yes → Phase 5 | ❌ Quantity mismatch → initiate dispute / replacement |
| Quality meets agreed standard | ✅ Yes → Phase 5 | ❌ Quality issue → initiate replacement / dispute resolution |

---

# **PHASE 5: POST-DEAL FOLLOW-UP & ISSUE RESOLUTION**

### **Objective**

Ensure compliance, resolve disputes, and track final payments.

---

## **Step 5.1: Quality & Compliance Check**

**Tasks:**

- Verify goods meet UAE standards (ESMA, MOHAP, Dubai Municipality)
- Check certifications and product compliance

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goods compliant with UAE regulations | ✅ Yes → Step 5.2 | ❌ Non-compliant → coordinate supplier replacement / refund |
| Buyer satisfied with quality | ✅ Yes → Step 5.2 | ❌ Buyer reports issue → initiate resolution |

---

## **Step 5.2: Payment Settlement Tracking**

**Tasks:**

- Ensure final payment received per contract
- Confirm payment released to supplier

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Final payment received | ✅ Yes → Step 5.3 | ❌ Payment delayed → escalate finance / legal |
| Payment matches agreed terms | ✅ Yes → Step 5.3 | ❌ Amount mismatch → investigate; fail deal if unresolved |
| CRM updated with payment status | ✅ Yes → Step 5.3 | ❌ Not updated → update immediately |

---

## **Step 5.3: Feedback Collection**

**Tasks:**

- Collect buyer & supplier feedback
- Record satisfaction, disputes, and lessons learned

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feedback received from both parties | ✅ Yes → Phase 6 | ❌ No feedback → follow up once; if still missing → mark as “No Feedback” |
| Feedback logged in CRM | ✅ Yes → Phase 6 | ❌ Not logged → update CRM |

---

# **PHASE 6: DEAL CLOSURE & CRM UPDATE**

### **Objective**

Finalize all deal details, close in CRM, and prepare reporting.

---

## **Step 6.1: Close Deal in CRM**

**Tasks:**

- Update status: Closed – Success / Closed – Failed
- Attach all supporting documents (PO, invoices, payment, tracking, communications)

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All documents attached | ✅ Yes → Step 6.2 | ❌ Missing docs → attach before closing |
| Deal status updated correctly | ✅ Yes → Step 6.2 | ❌ Incorrect status → correct before proceeding |

---

## **Step 6.2: Record Lessons Learned**

**Tasks:**

- Document delays, disputes, compliance issues
- Note best-performing suppliers / buyers

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lessons recorded | ✅ Yes → Step 6.3 | ❌ Not recorded → follow up before archive |

---

## **Step 6.3: Archive / Reporting**

**Tasks:**

- Prepare weekly/monthly report
- Include success rate, pending issues, failed deals

**Pass/Fail Conditions:**

| Condition | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Report complete and accurate | ✅ Yes → Archive | ❌ Missing data → complete before archiving |
| CRM fully updated | ✅ Yes → Archive | ❌ Missing info → update CRM |
