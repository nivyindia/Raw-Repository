## 🔑 Core Philosophy

> **We are not rebuilding. We are revealing.**
> 

> The content is solid. The problem is navigation, naming, and context. A new person landing in this workspace has no idea where to start, what each teamspace means, or how the parts connect. We fix that — without touching actual content.
> 

### Three rules we follow:

1. **Preserve everything** — no page, database, or content gets deleted
2. **Add context, don't replace it** — we add "What is this?" callouts and intro sections to existing pages
3. **Structure for the newcomer** — every section should answer: *What is this? Why does it exist? Where do I go next?*
