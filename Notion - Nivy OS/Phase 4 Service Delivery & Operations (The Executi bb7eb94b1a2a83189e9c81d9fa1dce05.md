# Phase 4: Service Delivery & Operations (The "Execution Engine")

# **1. Client Onboarding System**

### **A. Pre-Kickoff Process (Week Before Start)**

**Document Collection & Access Setup:**

- Create standardized client intake form
- Gather logins: Google Analytics, Search Console, social media accounts
- Set up email groups and folder structures
- Prepare NDA and access agreements

**Internal Briefing Package:**

- Client background and business goals
- Key stakeholders and decision-makers
- Previous marketing efforts and results
- Competitor analysis summary
- SWOT analysis specific to client

### **B. The Kickoff Meeting Structure**

**1-Week Onboarding Timeline:**

- **Day 1:** Contract signed, welcome email sent
- **Day 2:** Access requests and intake forms sent
- **Day 3-4:** Initial audits and research conducted
- **Day 5:** Formal 90-minute kickoff meeting

**Kickoff Meeting Agenda:**

- Team introductions and roles (30 mins)
- Review of business goals and KPIs (30 mins)
- Communication protocol establishment (15 mins)
- Next steps and 30-day plan (15 mins)

### **C. First 30-Day Success Plan**

**Week 1: Discovery & Audit**

- Comprehensive marketing audit
- Technical SEO analysis
- Competitor deep dive
- Baseline performance metrics established

**Week 2-3: Strategy Development**

- Customized marketing strategy document
- Channel-specific tactical plans
- Content calendar creation
- Implementation timeline

**Week 4: Initial Implementation & Reporting**

- Launch first campaigns
- Deliver first performance report
- Strategy refinement session

## **3. Client Communication & Reporting System**

### **A. Communication Protocol**

**Response Time SLAs:**

- Emergency issues: 2 hours response
- General inquiries: 4 business hours
- Strategy questions: 24 hours
- Monthly reporting: Scheduled dates

**Preferred Communication Channels:**

- **Slack/Teams:** Day-to-day quick questions
- **Email:** Formal requests and documentation
- **Odoo Portal:** Project updates and file sharing
- **Video Calls:** Monthly strategy meetings

### **B. Monthly Reporting Structure**

**Executive Summary Dashboard:**

**Channel-Specific Deep Dives:**

- SEO performance with ranking updates
- PPC performance with ROI calculations

- Content performance analysis

### **C. Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs)**

## **6. Crisis Management & Scope Control**

### **A. Scope Creep Prevention**

**Change Request Process:**

- Formal change request form
- Impact assessment documentation
- Timeline and budget adjustments
- Client approval requirement

**Out-of-Scope Services List:**

- Website development beyond minor changes
- Crisis management public relations
- Extensive content creation beyond agreement
- Additional platform management

### **B. Performance Crisis Protocol**

**Immediate Response Framework:**

- Within 2 hours: Acknowledge and assess
- Within 4 hours: Initial investigation complete
- Within 24 hours: Action plan presented
- Within 48 hours: Resolution implemented

**Communication Protocol:**

- Transparent updates every 4 hours
- Root cause analysis documentation
- Prevention plan development
- Client confidence rebuilding strategy
