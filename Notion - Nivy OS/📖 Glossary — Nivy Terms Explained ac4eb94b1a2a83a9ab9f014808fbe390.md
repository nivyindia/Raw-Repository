# 📖 Glossary — Nivy Terms Explained

> 🟢 **Status: Live** | Owner: Nivy HQ Admin | Last Updated: April 2026
> 

New to Nivy? Confused by our terms? This page explains **every acronym, name, and internal term** used across our Notion workspace — in plain English. No jargon, no assumptions.

---

## 🔤 A — D

**ANNEXURE**

An annexure is an attachment to an official document. When you see "ANNEXURE A" or "ANNEXURE E", it means "Attachment A" or "Attachment E" to a larger contract or proposal. These are official supporting documents.

---

## 🔤 G

**GAAP**

Stands for **Global Affiliate Accelerator Program** (Nivy's internal use), but also refers to the **finance and accounting teamspace** that follows GAAP (Generally Accepted Accounting Principles) standards. In our workspace, "GAAP" = our finance/accounting team and their Notion space.

**Growth Engine**

Nivy's marketing and online presence engine. It covers everything related to social media, content strategy, online presence building, and competitor research. Think of it as the fuel that drives Nivy's visibility and lead generation.

---

## 🔤 N

**Nivy OS (Operating System)**

The master hub of the Nivy Notion workspace. It contains all the core databases: departments, projects, SOPs, reports, templates, company documents, and analytics. Just like a computer's operating system runs everything in the background, Nivy OS runs the admin backbone of all our divisions.

**Nivy Global**

The international expansion division of Nivy Digital. They handle partnerships, franchise deals, client proposals, and global business development.

**Nivy Nexus**

A buyer-seller community platform built and managed by Nivy. Nexus connects businesses, freelancers, and buyers — currently focused on UAE markets. It has its own outreach team, social media, and community rules.

**Nivy Next**

The innovation and new initiatives arm of Nivy Digital. When Nivy is building something new (a product, a new service, a new platform), it lives in Nivy Next until it's ready to become its own division.

**Nivy Academy**

Nivy's internal learning and training platform. It provides onboarding programmes, skill courses, and certifications for staff and community members.

**Nivy Alliance**

Manages Nivy's B2B partnerships and the network of community contributors. Nivy Alliance governs who gets access to what, how contributors are recognised, and how partnerships are structured.

**Nivy Advisory**

Nivy's consulting division. They advise clients on digital strategy, brand building, and business growth. Think of them as Nivy's external-facing consulting arm.

**Nivy Care Foundation**

Nivy's CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) arm. They run social impact projects, community outreach, and purpose-driven initiatives.

**Nivy Jobs**

Handles all recruitment for Nivy — posting job openings, managing applications, coordinating interviews, and onboarding new hires.

**Nivy HQ**

This teamspace. The front door to the entire Nivy Notion workspace. Contains company-wide information, the glossary, org chart, and team directory.

---

## 🔤 S — Z

**SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)**

A step-by-step guide explaining how to do a specific task or process at Nivy. SOPs ensure everyone follows the same process, every time. You'll find all SOPs in Nivy OS → sop_database.

**Teamspace**

A section in Notion that belongs to a specific team or division. Think of it like a folder that only that team usually works in. Examples: "Nivy Global" is a teamspace, "Nivy Nexus" is a teamspace.
