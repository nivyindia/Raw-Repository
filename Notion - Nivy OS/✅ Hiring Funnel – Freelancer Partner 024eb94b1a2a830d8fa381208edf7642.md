# ✅ Hiring Funnel – Freelancer Partner

Here is the **best-performing strategy** for your type of program 👇
**✅ Do NOT put clarifications in the social media post.**
It **kills reach, reduces conversions**, and scares people before they even understand the value.
Your social media post should be: Boo Boo booty do you**⚡ Attractive + simple + opportunity-focused**
Not detailed, not defensive, not full of rules.
**Purpose of the post:**

👉 Get maximum freelancers to comment “INTERESTED” or DM.
**Purpose of the inbox:**

👉 Give full clarity before onboarding.

**1. Social Media Post**
• Short
• Exciting
• Attractive
• Focused on growth & opportunity
• No expectations, no conditions
• Only highlights benefits & career vision
**Goal:** Maximize leads.
**2. WhatsApp DM / Inbox Stage**
Here is where you send:
• Clarification message
• Expectations
• Roles & responsibilities
• Earnings structure
• Partnership model
• Skill test
• FAQ
**Goal:** Filter & educate.
**3. Short Video Explanation (Optional but powerful)**
A 2–3 minute video of you explaining:
• What this program is
• What it is NOT
• What you expect
• What they get
**Goal:** Instant understanding + trust.
**4. Onboarding Call or Skill Test**
• Role alignment
• Level assignment
• Answer questions
**Goal:** Qualification.
**5. Agreement + Onboarding**
• NDA
• Partnership agreement
• Tools access
• Training + SOP
• Assign first internal tasks
**Goal:** Proper partnership start.
**✅ What to include in the social media post**
**ONLY these things (no clarifications):**
✔ The opportunity

✔ The growth path

✔ The skills you’re hiring

✔ That it's a partnership

✔ That it's for freelancers

✔ That they get mentorship + projects

✔ CTA to comment “INTERESTED”
**❌ What NOT to include in the social media post**
These must NOT be in the public post:
✘ No details about internal work

✘ No “you must contribute without payment”

✘ No revenue split details

✘ No hierarchy details

✘ No rules or conditions

✘ No onboarding steps

✘ No long explanation
These are only for the **inbox stage**.
**🔥 Conclusion
✔ Social Media = Marketing (Attract)
