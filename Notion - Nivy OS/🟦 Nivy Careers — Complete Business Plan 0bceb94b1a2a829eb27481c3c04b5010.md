# 📋 Executive Summary {#executive-summary}

Nivy Careers is a global recruitment and talent placement business that operates across three revenue tracks:

1. **Employer-Side (B2B):** Recruiting talent for businesses worldwide — charging placement fees, retainers, or per-hire fees
2. **Candidate-Side (B2C):** Career services for job seekers — resume writing, LinkedIn optimization, job application management, visa/immigration guidance
3. **VA Marketplace (Hybrid):** Placing Nivy-trained virtual assistants with international clients as a done-for-you staffing solution

The business leverages **50+ job platforms**, **social media recruitment marketing**, and a **proprietary talent pipeline** built through VA training programs, to deliver fast, quality placements at scale.

# 💼 Business Model Overview {#model}

## Three Revenue Tracks

### Track A — Employer-Side Recruitment (B2B)

Nivy sources and places candidates for businesses. Revenue models:

- **Contingency Placement:** 10–25% of first-year salary (paid on hire)
- **Retained Search:** Upfront retainer ($2,000–$10,000) + success fee
- **Staff Augmentation:** Monthly retainer per placed contractor ($300–$2,000/month/person)
- **VA Staffing:** $500–$2,000/month per VA placed with client

### Track B — Career Services (B2C)

Revenue from job seekers:

- Resume writing: $50–$300
- LinkedIn profile optimization: $100–$500
- Job application management: $200–$1,000/month
- Career coaching: $100–$500/session
- Visa + immigration guidance: $300–$1,500

### Track C — Nivy VA Marketplace

Pre-trained VAs placed with clients globally:

- Client pays: $500–$2,000/month/VA
- VA earns: $300–$1,000/month
- Nivy margin: $200–$1,000/month/VA placed

# ⚙️ Automation Architecture {#automation}

## End-to-End Recruitment Automation

```
[Employer Lead Gen] → [Outreach Sequence] → [Job Brief Received] → [Auto-Source Candidates] → [AI Screening] → [Shortlist Delivered] → [Interview Scheduled] → [Offer Sent] → [Placement Fee Collected]
```

## Automation Flows

### Flow 1 — Employer (Client) Acquisition

- **Tool:** [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) → HubSpot → [Instantly.ai](http://Instantly.ai)
- **Trigger:** Daily scrape of companies in target countries, industry, size
- **Action:** Automated email sequence (5 touches over 21 days)
- **Focus:** "We can find your next hire in 14 days or less"

### Flow 2 — Job Posting Distribution (Multi-Board)

- **Tool:** Workable
- **Action:** Post once → distributes to 20+ job boards automatically
- **Boards:** Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter, StepStone, etc.
- **Time saved:** 4–6 hours per job post

### Flow 3 — Candidate Application Management

- **Tool:** Workable ATS
- **Action:** All applications funnel into single pipeline
- **AI Screen:** Auto-reject unqualified based on must-have criteria
- **Human Review:** Recruiter reviews top 20% of applicants

### Flow 4 — Interview Scheduling

- **Tool:** Calendly embedded in emails
- **Action:** Candidates self-schedule → Zoom link auto-generated
- **Follow-up:** Automated reminders at 24hr and 1hr before

### Flow 5 — Offer + Contract

- **Tool:** PandaDoc + Zapier
- **Trigger:** Candidate selected in ATS
- **Action:** Offer letter auto-generated, sent for e-signature
- **On Sign:** Invoice auto-generated in QuickBooks, sent to employer

### Flow 6 — B2C Career Services Funnel

- **Tool:** Typeform (intake) → Notion (workflow) → Stripe (payment)
- **Flow:** Candidate submits form → auto-priced based on service → Stripe payment link sent → Notion task created for team
