# 🔹 DAILY / WEEKLY ROUTINE BREAKDOWN (DETAILED)

### **Admins**

- Verify new members (daily)
- Moderate chats & remove spam (daily)
- Post daily engagement content (polls/memes)
- Respond to complaints / reports

### **Ambassadors**

- Invite new members via social media (daily/weekly)
- Organize weekly icebreakers
- Promote VIP / events carefully to interested members
- Moderate small groups if needed

### **Campus Heads**

- Organize weekly mini-meetups
- Recruit 5–20 members per week
- Coordinate with City Head for events
- Ensure compliance in local micro-groups

### **City Heads**

- Organize bi-weekly city parties / meetups
- Tie up with partners (clubs, studios, cafes)
- Track event revenue, attendance
- Approve VIP / adult participation
- Recruit new Campus Heads

### **Event Coordinator**

- Plan all events (weekly & monthly)
- Manage ticketing, logistics, venue booking
- Coordinate with Content Head for promotions

### **Content & Media Head**

- Capture photos/videos of events
- Post daily engagement content in Telegram / Discord / Instagram
- Prepare newsletter / monthly recap

### **Core Team**

- Approve VIP / adult content
- Analyze community metrics (growth, revenue, engagement)
- Recruit / replace high-level ambassadors
- Manage partnerships and revenue streams
