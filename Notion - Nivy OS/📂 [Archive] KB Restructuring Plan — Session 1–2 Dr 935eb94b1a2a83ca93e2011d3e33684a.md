## 🔑 Key Principles

- **Preserve everything** — No content deleted, only reorganised
- **Plain English** — Every page title and description must make sense on Day 1
- **One source of truth** — Eliminate duplicates by merging, not deleting
- **Logical hierarchy** — Company → Division → Department → Team → Page
- **Consistent naming** — Emojis + clear titles on every page
- **Start Here pages** — Every teamspace gets a welcome/onboarding section
