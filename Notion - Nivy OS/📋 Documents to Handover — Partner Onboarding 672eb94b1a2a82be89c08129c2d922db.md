# 📋 Documents to Handover — Partner Onboarding

Of course. Here is a detailed, comprehensive handover checklist for an outsourcing Sales and Marketing partner, structured for clarity and completeness.

### **A Complete Handover Checklist for Sales & Marketing Outsourcing**

---

### **1.0 Strategic Foundation & Business Context**

- **1.1 Company Overview:** Provide company history, mission, vision, values, and long-term business objectives.
- **1.2 Product/Service Deep Dive:** Detailed information on all products/services, including features, benefits, use cases, and technical specifications.
- **1.3 Target Market Analysis:** Define the total addressable market (TAM), serviceable available market (SAM), and serviceable obtainable market (SOM).
- **1.4 Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):** Detailed firmographic, technographic, and demographic criteria for target companies.
- **1.5 Buyer Personas:** Detailed profiles for key decision-makers and influencers within the ICP, including their roles, challenges, goals, and pain points.
- **1.6 Unique Value Proposition (UVP):** Clear, compelling statements on why customers should choose you over competitors.
- **1.7 Key Messaging:** Approved elevator pitches, core value statements, and messaging pillars for different segments.

### **2.0 Goals, Targets & Performance Metrics**

- **2.1 Primary Goals:** Define the primary objective (e.g., Lead Generation, Market Penetration, Brand Awareness, Direct Sales).
- **2.2 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
    - *Sales KPIs:* Number of Qualified Leads, Conversion Rate, Sales Cycle Length, Average Deal Size, Pipeline Value.
    - *Marketing KPIs:* Website Traffic, MQLs, SQLs, Cost Per Lead, Social Media Engagement.
- **2.3 Quantitative Targets:** Set specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) targets for each KPI (e.g., "Generate 50 SQLs per month").
- **2.4 Reporting Cadence:** Define the frequency and format of performance reports (e.g., Weekly Dashboard, Monthly Business Review).

### **3.0 Sales Process & Methodology**

- **3.1 Defined Sales Process Stages:** Map out each stage from initial contact to closed-won/lost (e.g., Prospecting > Qualification > Discovery > Proposal > Negotiation > Closed).
- **3.2 Sales Methodology:** Specify the methodology to be used (e.g., MEDDIC, SPIN, Challenger Sale).
- **3.3 Lead Qualification Criteria:** Define what constitutes a Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL) and Sales Qualified Lead (SQL) using a framework like BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline) or similar.
- **3.4 Lead Handoff Process:** A clear, step-by-step procedure for transferring a qualified lead from the outsourcing team to the internal sales team, including required data entry and notification.
- **3.5 Pipeline Management:** Guidelines on how the pipeline should be maintained, reviewed, and forecasted in the CRM.

### **4.0 Marketing & Sales Enablement**

- **4.1 Marketing Collateral:** Provide access to all relevant materials:
    - Sales Decks, Brochures, Case Studies, White Papers, Datasheets.
    - Product Demos and Video Library.
- **4.2 Sales Tools & Technology:** Provide logins and training for:
    - Customer Relationship Management (CRM) System.
    - Marketing Automation Platform (e.g., HubSpot, Marketo).
    - Prospecting Tools (e.g., LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ZoomInfo).
    - Communication Tools (e.g., Email, Calendly).
- **4.3 Content & Social Media Guidelines:** Approved content for different channels, brand voice guidelines, and social media policy.

### **5.0 Competitive Landscape**

- **5.1 Competitor List:** A list of primary and secondary competitors.
- **5.2 Competitor Analysis:** For each key competitor, provide their strengths, weaknesses, market positioning, and pricing.
- **5.3 Competitive Battle Cards:** One-page summaries on how to counter specific competitors and articulate your key differentiators.

### **6.0 Commercial & Legal Framework**

- **6.1 Master Service Agreement (MSA):** The signed overarching contract.
- **6.2 Scope of Work (SOW):** A detailed document outlining specific tasks, deliverables, and timelines.
- **6.3 Payment Structure & Schedule:** Detailed breakdown of fees, retainer models, commission structures, bonuses, and the invoicing/payment schedule.
- **6.4 Pricing & Discounting Authority:** Provide the official price list, approved discount tiers, and the process for requesting special pricing approval.
- **6.5 Legal & Compliance Guidelines:** Document all legal requirements, including data privacy (GDPR, CCPA), telemarketing regulations (DNC lists), and industry-specific compliance.

### **7.0 Brand, HR & Operational Governance**

- **7.1 Brand Guidelines:** Comprehensive document covering logo usage, color palette, fonts, and tone of voice.
- **7.2 Hiring & Staffing Guidelines:** Requirements for the profiles of agents/representatives the partner will hire, including any necessary background checks or skill assessments.
- **7.3 HR Policies:** Any specific policies the partner's staff must adhere to (e.g., confidentiality, code of conduct).
- **7.4 Quality Assurance (QA) Framework:**
    - Define QA metrics (e.g., call scoring, email quality).
    - Outline the call/session recording and monitoring process.
    - Set up the frequency and format of QA feedback sessions.
- **7.5 Point of Contact & Escalation Matrix:** A clear list of who to contact for specific issues (strategic, operational, technical, financial) and the escalation path for unresolved problems.

### **8.0 Administrative & Financial Processes**

- **8.1 Invoicing Process:** Detailed instructions on how to submit invoices, required supporting documentation, and the designated approver.
- **8.2 Approval Workflows:** Clear processes for getting approvals on marketing campaigns, contracts, or special pricing.
- **8.3 Territory & Market Boundaries:** Explicit definition of geographical territories, industry verticals, or customer segments the partner is authorized to target.

This checklist provides a robust foundation for a successful partnership, ensuring both strategic alignment and smooth day-to-day operations.
