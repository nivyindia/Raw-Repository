# **PHASE 3: OUTREACH PREPARATION**

### **Objective**

Convert cleaned lead lists into **ready-to-execute outreach batches** with correct messaging, sequencing, and tracking.

---

## **Step 5: Prepare Outreach Batches**

---

### **5.1 Input Required**

- Buyer lead list (Cleaned)
- Supplier lead list (Cleaned)
- Approved ICP (Phase 1)
- Outreach email templates (buyer & supplier)
- Tracking sheet / CRM access

---

### **5.2 Batch Creation Rules**

| Parameter | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Batch Size | 30–50 leads per batch |
| Buyer & Supplier | Separate batches |
| Geography | Same region per batch |
| Industry | Same sub-industry per batch |
| Sender Account | 1 sender per batch |
| Sending Window | Business hours only |

---

### **5.3 Outreach Batch Structure (Sheet / CRM Fields)**

**Mandatory Fields:**

- Batch ID
- Lead Type (Buyer / Supplier)
- Company Name
- Contact Name
- Email
- Phone / WhatsApp
- LinkedIn URL
- Outreach Status
- Last Contacted Date
- Next Follow-Up Date
- Response Status
- Notes

---

### **5.4 Email Warm-Up & Safety Rules**

- New email → max 20 emails/day
- Aged email → max 50–80/day
- No links in first email
- Plain-text only (no images)
- Personalization mandatory (company name / industry line)

---

### **5.5 Phase-3 Deliverables**

✔ Buyer outreach batches ready

✔ Supplier outreach batches ready

✔ Tracking system live

✔ Green signal for outreach execution

# **PHASE 4: EMAIL & MULTI-CHANNEL OUTREACH**

### **Objective**

Initiate conversations, generate replies, and move qualified responses into **qualification scheduling**.

---

## **Step 6: Send First Buyer Email Outreach**

[Email sequence SUpply CHain](Email%20sequence%20SUpply%20CHain%20285eb94b1a2a832da8bb014174b4dd59.md)

### **Buyer Email – Purpose**

Identify **active demand** (buying requirement, sourcing pain, expansion need).

**Execution Rules**

- Send from assigned sender account
- Personalize first 2 lines
- CTA = conversation, not selling

**Buyer Email CTA Examples**

- “Are you currently sourcing this?”
- “Open to exploring verified suppliers?”

---

## **Step 7: Send First Supplier Email Outreach**

### **Supplier Email – Purpose**

Identify **capable, willing suppliers** open to new buyer connections.

**Execution Rules**

- Capacity-focused language
- No pricing discussion yet
- CTA = interest confirmation

**Supplier Email CTA Examples**

- “Open to new bulk buyers?”
- “Can we understand your supply capacity?”

---

## **Step 8: Send Buyer Follow-Up Emails**

### **Follow-Up Schedule**

| Follow-Up | Day |
| --- | --- |
| Follow-Up 1 | Day 3 |
| Follow-Up 2 | Day 6 |
| Follow-Up 3 | Day 10 |

**Rules**

- Shorter than first email
- New angle each follow-up
- Stop after 3 follow-ups if no response

---

## **Step 9: Send Supplier Follow-Up Emails**

### **Follow-Up Schedule**

Same as buyers

**Rules**

- Highlight buyer demand signal (without names)
- Emphasize verified buyers
- Stop after 3 follow-ups

---

## **Step 10: WhatsApp / LinkedIn Nudges**

### **When to Use**

- Email unopened after 2 attempts
- Phone / LinkedIn available
- Lead fits ICP strongly

---

### **WhatsApp Nudge Rules**

- Text only
- Max 2 nudges
- Business tone
- Reference email sent

**WhatsApp Message Structure**

- Who you are
- Why reaching out
- Soft permission ask

---

### **LinkedIn Nudge Rules**

- Connection note ≤ 300 characters
- No pitch
- Industry-based context

---

## **Step 11: Track Responses & Engagement**

---

### **Response Categories**

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Interested | Ready for qualification |
| Maybe Later | Follow-up in 30 days |
| Not Interested | Close |
| Wrong Contact | Update lead |
| No Response | Continue follow-ups |

---

### **Data to Capture for Each Response**

- Response date
- Channel responded on
- Buyer / Supplier intent level
- Any initial requirement / capacity info
- Next action date

---

### **Handoff Rule**

- Only **Interested** responses move to **Phase 5: Qualification**
- No calls without recorded interest
