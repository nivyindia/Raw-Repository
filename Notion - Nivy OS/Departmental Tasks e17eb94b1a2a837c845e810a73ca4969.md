# Departmental Tasks

Of course. Here is a comprehensive, department-wise breakdown of goals, tasks, reports, tools, hierarchy, and skills required to build and run your automated lead generation agency.

### **Agency Organizational Blueprint**

---

### **1. Hierarchy & Organizational Chart**

```
                                |-> Lead Generation Manager
                                |-> [Automation Tools]
                                |
                |-> Sales Team -|-> Sales Closers (x2) [Commission-Only]
                |               |-> Sales Admin (Optional)
                |
Founder/CEO -|-> PM Team -|-> Project Manager (Head of Delivery)
                |         |-> Junior PM (Optional for scale)
                |
                |-> Production Team -> Freelancers (Specialized: SEO, PPC, Content)
                |
                |-> Admin -> Finance & Ops (Optional/Part-time initially)

```

### **2. Departmental Breakdown**

### **Department: Lead Generation**

- **Goal:** Generate a consistent, predictable flow of qualified leads to meet sales targets.
- **Key Metric:** Number of Qualified Leads/Month, Lead-to-Appointment Conversion Rate, Cost per Qualified Lead.

| Aspect | Details |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Key Tasks** | 1. **Audience Building:** Identify and build targeted lists of ideal clients (e.g., using [Apollo.io](http://apollo.io/), ZoomInfo). <br> 2. **Campaign Setup:** Create and A/B test automated email sequences ([Instantly.ai](http://instantly.ai/)) and LinkedIn messaging flows ([Expandi.io](http://expandi.io/)). <br> 3. **Calendar Management:** Maintain and optimize the Calendly (or equivalent) setup for booking calls. <br> 4. **Performance Monitoring:** Daily check of campaign metrics (open rates, reply rates, positive response rate). <br> 5. **Lead Handoff:** Ensure qualified leads are properly tagged and assigned in the CRM. |  |  |
| **Tools** | [Instantly.ai](http://instantly.ai/), [Expandi.io](http://expandi.io/), Waalaxy, Phantombuster, Calendly, [Apollo.io](http://apollo.io/), CRM. |  |  |
| **Skills Required** | Data Analysis, Copywriting, Understanding of Cold Outreach Compliance (GDPR/SPAM), A/B Testing, Proficiency in Automation Software. |  |  |
| **Reports** | **Daily:** Campaign performance dashboard (Sent, Open, Reply Rates). <br> **Weekly/Monthly:** Leads Generated, Cost Per Lead, Conversion Rate to Appointment. |  |  |

---

### **Department: Sales**

- **Goal:** Convert qualified leads into paying clients at target profit margins.
- **Key Metric:** Sales Conversion Rate, Average Deal Size, Total Revenue Closed.

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Key Tasks** | 1. **Follow-up:** Immediately contact new leads assigned in CRM. <br> 2. **Conduct Sales Calls:** Use the mandatory script and pitch deck on every call. <br> 3. **Negotiate & Close:** Handle objections and close deals within predefined pricing. <br> 4. **Contracting:** Send agreements via e-signature and ensure they are fully executed. <br> 5. **Handoff:** Schedule the kickoff call with the PM and introduce the client. <br> 6. **CRM Hygiene:** Log all call notes, activities, and update deal stages meticulously. |
| **Tools** | CRM, VoIP Phone System (OpenPhone), Zoom, DocuSign/Pandadoc, Email. |
| **Skills Required** | Excellent Communication, Persuasion, Objection Handling, Active Listening, Urgency, Resilience, Adherence to Process. |
| **Reports** | **Daily:** Calls made, deals closed. <br> **Weekly:** Sales Pipeline Value, Conversion Rate by Closer, Forecast for the month. |

---

### **Department: Project Management (Client Delivery)**

- **Goal:** Ensure 100% client retention through flawless execution and communication.
- **Key Metric:** Client Satisfaction (CSAT) Score, On-Time Delivery %, Client Retention Rate.

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Key Tasks** | 1. **Client Onboarding:** Run kickoff calls, set expectations, gather all assets. <br> 2. **Project Planning:** Break down client scope into tasks and milestones in PM tool. <br> 3. **Freelancer Management:** Assign tasks to the right freelancer, brief them clearly. <br> 4. **Quality Control (QC):** Review all deliverables against the checklist before client sees them. <br> 5. **Client Communication:** Serve as the single point of contact. Send updates and reports. <br> 6. **Milestone Approval:** Approve completed work and initiate freelancer payments. |
| **Tools** | Asana/Trello/ClickUp, Slack/Teams, Time Tracking (Clockify), Google Drive, Email. |
| **Skills Required** | Extreme Organization, Exceptional Communication, Client Management, Quality Focus, Basic understanding of all services (SEO, Ads, etc.), Ability to Enforce Deadlines. |
| **Reports** | **Weekly:** Client Health Score, Project Status (On Track/At Risk), Milestones Completed. <br> **Monthly:** Client Retention Report, Freelancer Performance Report (for CEO). |

---

### **Department: Production (Freelancers)**

- **Goal:** Execute all client work to the quality standard defined in the "Service Blueprint", on time.
- **Key Metric:** First-Time Pass Rate (Quality), On-Time Completion %.

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Key Tasks** | 1. **Task Acceptance:** Acknowledge and accept tasks in the PM tool. <br> 2. **Execution:** Complete work according to the "Service Blueprint" specifications. <br> 3. **Time Tracking:** Log all time spent on the task accurately. <br> 4. **Submission:** Deliver work through the PM tool by the deadline. <br> 5. **Revisions:** Complete any feedback changes provided by the PM promptly. |
| **Tools** | PM Tool (Asana), Time Tracking (Clockify), Google Docs, Design/SEO/Ad Tools as needed. |
| **Skills Required** | Expertise in their niche (SEO, PPC, Design, etc.), Attention to Detail, Ability to Follow Instructions, Time Management. |
| **Reports** | **N/A** (They are assessed by the PM). The PM generates reports *on* their performance. |

---

### **Department: Finance & Operations**

- **Goal:** Ensure financial health, legal compliance, and smooth operational workflow.
- **Key Metric:** Profit Margin, Cash Flow, Days Sales Outstanding (DSO).

| Aspect | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Key Tasks** | 1. **Invoicing:** Send invoices to clients upon contract signing/milestone achievement. <br> 2. **Payables:** Process payments to freelancers and software vendors. <br> 3. **Bookkeeping:** Maintain accurate financial records. <br> 4. **Reporting:** Prepare monthly P&L, Cash Flow, and Balance Sheet reports. <br> 5. **Contract Management:** Maintain master copies of all client and freelancer contracts. |
| **Tools** | QuickBooks Online/Xero, Excel/Sheets, Bank Account, DocuSign. |
| **Skills Required** | Basic Accounting, Organization, Attention to Detail, Discretion. |
| **Reports** | **Monthly:** Profit & Loss Statement, Accounts Aged Receivable, Cash Flow Forecast. |

---

### **3. Founder/CEO Goals & Responsibilities**

- **Strategic Goals:**
    - Achieve **$X MRR** (Monthly Recurring Revenue) by [Date].
    - Maintain a **Company Profit Margin of Y%**.
    - Scale to **Z Clients** under management.
- **Key Tasks:**
    - **System Design:** Continuously improve the "Agency Playbook" and operational systems.
    - **Hiring & Training:** Hire for key roles (PM first) and ensure they are trained to standard.
    - **Quality Assurance:** Randomly review sales call recordings and delivered client work.
    - **Financial Oversight:** Review monthly financial reports and adjust strategy accordingly.
    - **Business Development:** Explore partnerships and new service offerings.
- **Key Reports to Review:**
    1. **Weekly:** Sales Pipeline, Lead Generation Report.
    2. **Weekly/Monthly:** PM's Client Health & Retention Report.
    3. **Monthly:** P&L Statement and Cash Flow Forecast.

This blueprint provides a complete framework to build your agency from the ground up, ensuring every role has a clear purpose, measurable goals, and the tools needed to succeed.
