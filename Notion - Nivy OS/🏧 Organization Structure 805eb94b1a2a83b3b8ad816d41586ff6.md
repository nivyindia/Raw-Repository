# **Outsource Department: Your Path to Business Ownership**

---

We provide the systems, brand, and support; you bring the ambition. Choose the model that fits your goals.

---

### **Our Service & Partner Ecosystem**

We manage a network of specialized professionals and agencies to deliver comprehensive solutions to our clients. This includes:

- **Accounting & Bookkeeping:** Certified service providers and small agencies (Project-based contract)
- **Digital Marketing:** Expert freelancers and specialized agencies (Project-based contract)
- **Sales & Marketing (Lead Generation):** Focused freelancers and agencies (Fix and Commission-based payment)
- **Business Mentorship & Coaching:** Certified professionals (Per-client fee structure)
- **Complete Franchise Operations:** Turnkey business solutions.

---

### **Your Partnership Options**

### **Option 1: The Agency Owner**

You are the face of the business and the driving force behind sales. We act as your complete back-office delivery team.

- **Your Role:** Generate leads and manage client relationships using our proven methods and scripts.
- **Our Role:** Our dedicated team of experts delivers all promised services flawlessly.
- **Your Earning Potential:** You earn a **25% commission** on the total value of every project you close.
- **Ideal For:** Entrepreneurs who want to start quickly without managing service delivery, or sales-focused individuals who want to focus exclusively on client acquisition.

### **Option 2: The Certified Freelancer**

You deliver the services yourself, empowered by our established brand, training, and operational support.

- **Your Role:** Generate leads and deliver your freelancing services (e.g., digital marketing, accounting) directly to clients.
- **Our Role:** We provide the training, guidance, business systems, brand authority, and ongoing support you need to succeed.
- **Your Earning Potential:** You retain **85% of the revenue** you generate.
- **Our Fee:** A 15% platform fee on your revenue covers access to our brand, training, systems, and support network.
- **Ideal For:** Skilled freelancers ready to scale their own practice, or entrepreneurs who want to build and manage their own delivery team.

---

### **Scalable Partnership Structure**

To ensure localized support and growth, we operate a multi-tiered partnership network:

- **Regional Partners:** Oversee strategic growth within a larger region (e.g., a state or country).
- **Local Partners:** Manage operations, freelancer support, and client acquisition within a specific city or territory.

This structure ensures every partner has the guidance and community needed to thrive.

### **The Organizational Structure: Roles & Responsibilities**

This structure is designed for scalability, starting with you wearing multiple hats and expanding as volume grows.

**1. Founder / CEO (You)**

- **Core Responsibility:** System Architect & Growth Strategist. You ensure the machine works and grows.
- **Key Tasks:**
    - Oversee all departments and ensure inter-departmental harmony.
    - Final approval on high-value client contracts.
    - Manage the company's P&L, cash flow, and financial health.
    - Develop and refine the overall business strategy.
    - Ultimate authority on enforcing the rules of the "Agency Playbook."
- **Key Metric:** Overall Company Profitability, Client Lifetime Value (LTV).

**2. Lead Generation Manager (Initially: Automated Software + You)**

- **Core Responsibility:** Fill the top of the sales funnel with qualified leads.
- **Key Tasks:**
    - **Software Management:** Set up, monitor, and optimize the automated lead generation software (e.g., [Instantly.ai](http://instantly.ai/), [Expandi.io](http://expandi.io/), Waalaxy).
    - **Campaign Strategy:** Create target audience lists, write email/LinkedIn message sequences, and A/B test subject lines/CTAs.
    - **Lead Qualification:** Ensure lead scoring is set up in the CRM to filter out unqualified prospects.
    - **Channel Management:** Run campaigns across Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.
    - **Calendar Management:** Oversee the Calendly-like tool to ensure it's synced and functioning correctly.
- **Tools:** [Instantly.ai](http://instantly.ai/), [Expandi.io](http://expandi.io/), Waalaxy, Phantombuster, Calendly, HubSpot Sales Hub.
- **Key Metric:** Number of Qualified Leads Generated per Week/Month.

**3. Sales Closer (Commission-Only Closer)**

- **Core Responsibility:** Convert qualified leads into paying clients.
- **Key Tasks:**
    - Conduct sales calls using the mandatory script and pitch deck.
    - Follow up with leads via email and other approved channels.
    - Negotiate and close deals within the predefined pricing structure.
    - Hand over signed clients to the Project Manager with all necessary details.
    - Log all activities and call outcomes in the CRM.
- **Tools:** CRM, VoIP Phone System, Zoom, Agency Email Alias.
- **Key Metric:** Conversion Rate %, Total Revenue Closed.

**4. Project Manager (PM) (Your FIRST CRUCIAL Hire)**

- **Core Responsibility:** The single point of contact for the client. The guardian of client satisfaction and on-time delivery.
- **Key Tasks:**
    - **Client Onboarding:** Conduct kickoff calls, gather all client logins/assets, and set expectations.
    - **Task Delegation:** Break down client projects into milestones and tasks in the PM tool. Assign them to the correct freelancers.
    - **Quality Control (QC):** Review all work from freelancers against the "Quality Checklist" *before* it is sent to the client.
    - **Client Communication:** Send regular updates, reports, and answers to client questions. Handle any client concerns.
    - **Milestone Approval:** Review completed tasks, approve them, and trigger payments to freelancers.
- **Tools:** Asana/Trello/ClickUp, Slack, Time Tracking Software, Google Workspace.
- **Key Metric:** Client Satisfaction (CSAT) Score, On-Time Delivery %, Client Retention Rate.

**5. Freelancers / White-Label Partners (Execution Team)**

- **Core Responsibility:** Execute the specialized work to a high standard, on time.
- **Key Tasks:**
    - Accept tasks assigned in the PM tool.
    - Complete work according to the exact specifications in the "Service Blueprint."
    - Submit work for review by the deadline.
    - Make any revisions requested by the Project Manager.
    - Log all time spent on tasks.
- **Key Metric:** Quality Score (based on revisions needed), On-Time Completion %.

### **Operational Workflow & Deadlines**

This is the lifecycle of a lead from capture to delivery.

| Stage | Owner | Task | Standard Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Lead Captured** | Lead Gen Software | Automated email/LinkedIn message sent. | Instant |
| **2. Lead Qualified** | Lead Gen Manager | Lead books a call via Calendly. | N/A |
| **3. Sales Call** | Sales Closer | Conduct sales call using script. | Within 24h of booking |
| **4. Proposal Sent** | Sales Closer | Send proposal & contract via email. | Within 4h of call |
| **5. Client Onboards** | Project Manager | Conduct kickoff call & gather assets. | Within 24h of signing |
| **6. Task Delegation** | Project Manager | Create project plan & assign first tasks. | Within 8h of kickoff |
| **7. Work Execution** | Freelancer | Complete the task. | As per task deadline |
| **8. Quality Control** | Project Manager | Review work against checklist. | Within 4h of submission |
| **9. Client Delivery** | Project Manager | Deliver approved work to client. | Within 1h of QC approval |
| **10. Reporting** | Project Manager | Send weekly/monthly performance report. | Every Friday/EOM |

By implementing this structure and workflow, you create a predictable, scalable, and manageable system. You are not just a freelancer broker; you are the CEO of a process-driven marketing agency.
