## 🏢 What is Nivy?

Nivy is a multi-division company operating across business services, digital marketing, global partnerships, education, and social impact. At its core, Nivy is a **platform business** — we orchestrate, connect, and facilitate rather than doing everything in-house. We work with external partners, affiliate networks, and specialist teams to deliver value at scale with a lean internal structure.

Each division has a focused purpose, but they share the same operating system, values, and leadership structure.

## 🧠 Key Terms & Glossary

*Words used inside Nivy that might confuse a newcomer. Bookmark this section.*

| Term | Plain English Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **GAAP** | Global Affiliate Accelerator Program — Nivy's student/affiliate sales network. NOT the accounting term. |
| **Growth Engine** | The system and thinking behind how Nivy's business model was designed and scales |
| **Nivy OS** | Nivy Operating System — the internal manual and brain of the whole company |
| **Pocket Money Partners** | Part-time contributors who earn commission by helping promote or sell Nivy services |
| **Chairs** | Role titles in Growth Engine (e.g. Founder Chair, Deal Chair) — they describe responsibilities, not hierarchy |
| **Micro-agencies** | Small external partner teams (2–5 people) who do market discovery and deal facilitation |
| **Platform model** | Nivy connects the right parties and facilitates deals — we don't deliver everything ourselves |
| **Success fee** | Nivy's revenue model — we earn a % only when a deal closes, not upfront |
| **SOP** | Standard Operating Procedure — a step-by-step guide for how a task should be done |
| **KPI** | Key Performance Indicator — a measurable target to track performance |
| **Division Home** | The entry page for every teamspace — your first stop in any section |
| **MSME** | Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises — Indian business registration category used in GAAP |
| **Nivy Nexus** | Nivy's buyer-seller community platform, UAE-focused |
| **Nivy Next** | Nivy's innovation arm — where new products and sub-brands are incubated |
| **VA** | Virtual Assistant — remote staff who support delivery and sales |
