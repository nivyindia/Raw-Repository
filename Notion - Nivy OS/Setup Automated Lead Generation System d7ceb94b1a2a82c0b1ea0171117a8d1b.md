# Setup Automated Lead Generation System

### **Project: Automated Lead Generation System Setup**

**Objective:** To create a hands-off lead generation machine that operates 24/7 across multiple channels, educating prospects and booking qualified meetings directly into your calendar.

**Timeline: 6-8 Days for Full Implementation**

---

### **Phase 1: Foundation & Strategy (Day 1-2)**

**Goal:** Define your offering, audience, and messaging.

| **Day** | **Task** | **Details** | **Output** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Define Services & Packages** | Create 3 clear service packages.**Example:**1. **Starter:** Lead Gen Audit ($499)2. **Growth:** Ongoing Lead Gen Management ($1,500/mo)3. **Enterprise:** Full Funnel Management ($3,500/mo) | Service Menu Document with pricing, deliverables, and ideal client for each package. |
| **1** | **Ideal Client Profile (ICP)** | Define exactly who you target:- Industry- Company Size- Job Title (e.g., Marketing Director, CEO)- Pain Points | ICP "Avatar" Document |

| **2** | **Lead Magnet Creation** | Create a high-value free offer to capture emails.**Examples:**- "5 Cold Email Templates That Got Our Clients 50+ Meetings"- "The Automated Lead Gen Audit Checklist" | A PDF checklist or guide (designed in Canva). |

### **The Automated Customer Journey (Once Live)**

1. **Prospect is Found:** Your tool finds a target on LinkedIn.
2. **Auto-Connect & DM:** They receive a connection request, then a value-based DM.
3. **Offer Lead Magnet:** The DM offers your free guide/audit.
4. **Click Link:** They click to your landing page and enter their email.
5. **Auto-Download & Nurture:** They get the PDF instantly and are entered into an email nurture sequence.
6. **Auto-Invite to Book:** A follow-up email/DM invites them to book a call using your Calendly link.
7. **Meeting Booked:** They book a slot. You and the prospect receive confirmations.
8. **CRM Logged:** The lead and all interactions are automatically logged in your CRM for you to review before the call.

### **Software Setup Tasks for Automated Outreach System**

This list extracts only the technical configuration steps, providing a clear checklist for you or a tech assistant to execute.

---

### **Category 1: CRM & Meeting Booking (Core Hub)**

| Software | Setup Tasks |
| --- | --- |
| **HubSpot (Free CRM)** | 1. Create a free account. <br> 2. Customize the deal pipeline stages: `Lead` -> `Contacted` -> `Meeting Booked` -> `Proposal Sent` -> `Closed Won` -> `Closed Lost`. <br> 3. Create required contact properties: `Lead Source`, `Channel`, `Notes`. <br> 4. Install the HubSpot tracking code on your website/landing page. |
| **Calendly** | 1. Create a free/pro account. <br> 2. Set your available meeting hours and buffer times. <br> 3. Create a meeting event type (e.g., "15-Minute Discovery Call"). <br> 4. Connect your calendar (Google/Outlook) to avoid double-booking. <br> 5. Customize the booking page with your logo and questions (e.g., "What is your biggest challenge?"). <br> 6. **Integration:** Connect Calendly to HubSpot CRM to automatically create a contact and log the meeting when someone books. |

---

### **Category 2: Email Outreach Automation**

| Software | Setup Tasks |
| --- | --- |
| [**Instantly.ai](http://instantly.ai/) / [SmartReach.io](http://smartreach.io/)** | 1. Create an account. <br> 2. **Connect Sending Domains:** Add and verify 2-3 domains for sending (e.g., `mail.youragency.com`). Warm them up using the tool's built-in warmup feature. <br> 3. **Create Sending Accounts:** Create email accounts for each domain (e.g., `john@mail.youragency.com`). <br> 4. **Build Sequences:** Create your first email sequence: <br>    - Email 1: Value-based outreach. <br>    - Email 2: Follow-up (add more value). <br>    - Email 3: Breakup email / final offer. <br> 5. **Settings:** Set up open/click tracking. Configure bounce handling. <br> 6. **Import Leads:** Upload your first list of target emails and assign them to the sequence. |

---

### **Category 3: LinkedIn & Social Automation**

| Software | Setup Tasks |
| --- | --- |
| [**Expandi.io](http://expandi.io/) / Dux-Soup** | 1. | **SocialBee / [Later.com](http://later.com/)** | 1. Create an account. <br> 2. **Connect Social Profiles:** Connect your LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, Pinterest company pages/profiles. <br> 3. **Create Categories:** Match your content pillars (e.g., "Tips," "Case Studies," "Behind the Scenes"). <br> 4. **Setup Posting Schedule:** Define how many times per day/Day to post on each platform. <br> 5. **Upload Batched Content:** Upload all your pre-made carousels, images, and video posts. Write captions and hashtags. <br> 6. **Configure RSS Feeds** (Optional): Add industry blog RSS feeds for automatic content sharing. |
