# **SECTION — Payment, Compensation & Milestone System**

At **Nivy Next**, your earnings are designed to grow with your skills, responsibility, and contribution.

We follow a **transparent, role-based, and milestone-driven** payment structure that ensures fairness, clarity, and security for all freelancers.

---

# **1. Growth-Driven Earning Philosophy**

Your earnings increase as your:

- Responsibilities increase
- Skills improve
- Contribution deepens
- Leadership expands

There is **no limit** to how fast you can grow from **Intern → Junior → Mid-Level → Senior → Manager**.

Your performance determines your growth speed.

---

# **2. Fair Role-Based Compensation System**

Payments are based on:

- Your assigned role
- Task & project complexity
- Required skill level
- Your contribution & responsibility
- Company-defined payout structure

**Seniors and managers earn more; interns and juniors earn proportionately less.**

Your exact payout for each task/milestone is **decided and approved before work begins**.

---

# **3. Transparency Before Starting Work**

Before you start any project, you receive a clear breakdown of:

- Your role
- Your responsibilities
- Your payout per task/milestone
- Delivery expectations

There are **no surprises** and **no hidden terms**.

---

# **4. Milestone-Based Payment System**

Every project is divided into **clear, achievable milestones**, ensuring consistent workflow and regular payouts.

### **Example Milestone Structure — Website Project**

- **Milestone 1:** Design Approval (30% payment)
- **Milestone 2:** Frontend Development (40% payment)
- **Milestone 3:** Final Delivery & Launch (30% payment)

### **Payment Release Timeline**

- Payment is released **within 3–5 business days** after client approval of each milestone.
- This ensures **regular cash flow** without waiting for full project completion.

---

# **5. Holdback Amount System (15–20%)**

To ensure quality delivery and protection for both you and the client:

- **15–20%** of every milestone is held as security
- Released **within 7 days** after final project sign-off
- Encourages smooth completion and ensures project stability

### **Example Flow — $100 Project**

- Milestone 1: $30 → You get **$24**, $6 held
- Milestone 2: $40 → You get **$32**, $8 held
- Milestone 3: $30 → You get **$24 + $14 released**

This system guarantees payment security and consistent motivation.

---

# **6. Team-Based Revenue Sharing**

When multiple freelancers work on a project, payouts are divided based on:

- Experience & skill level
- Contribution & responsibility
- Complexity of assigned work

### Key Principles:

- **Fair Distribution:** Based on contribution, not equal split
- **Transparent Communication:** Your share is shared before project assignment
- **Flexibility:** Adjustments possible for special tasks or expert roles
- **Motivation:** Higher responsibility → higher reward

---

# **7. Payment Release Process**

1. Complete your milestone
2. Submit it for internal review
3. Client approves
4. Payment is released within **3–5 business days**

This ensures predictable and stable cash flow.

---

# **8. Payment Terms & Security Benefits**

### **Freelancer Benefits**

- **Regular Income:** Paid per milestone — not at the end of the project
- **Clear Expectations:** Know exactly what you have to deliver
- **Payment Security:** Holdback system reduces payment risk
- **Motivation Boost:** Frequent payouts keep work consistent
- **Growth Opportunity:** Higher responsibility = higher earning potential

---

# **9. Internal Confidentiality — Project Value is Not Disclosed**

For internal fairness, client-side project value is **never disclosed**.

Your payout depends on:

- Experience
- Market rates in your country
- Complexity of your tasks
- Contribution level
- Responsibility scope

—not on what the client pays the agency.

Anyone from intern level to senior/managerial level with skills in digital marketing, lead generation, data research, outreach, email marketing, design, development, or virtual assistance can join.

**3. Do I need experience to join?**
No. We accept interns (0–1 year), junior freelancers (1–2 years), mid-level freelancers (2–4 years), and senior/manager-level freelancers (4+ years). Your level is decided based on your skills, experience, portfolio, and interview/skill-test performance.

**4. Is there an interview or skill test?**
Yes. To determine your correct level, tasks, and compensation tier, an interview or skill test may be taken before onboarding.

You can start approaching small clients for small payments to build your confidence. Most partners receive their first client project within 2–3 weeks of completing onboarding.

**6. What kind of work will I get?**
You may receive client-facing tasks, internal company growth tasks, or a mix of both. Client work includes lead generation, email marketing, social media management, and more. Internal work includes SEO, PR, creating SOPs, and other company growth activities.

**7. How is my role assigned?**
Your role is assigned based on your skill level, experience, portfolio quality, interview/skill test results, and project requirements to ensure you receive suitable responsibilities.

**8. Can I focus only on delivery, not sales?**
Yes. You can choose to work only on assigned projects while our sales team acquires clients for you.

**9. What happens if I get a project outside my skillset?**
You can bring the client to us, our specialist team will deliver the project, and you will earn a referral commission without doing the work yourself.

**10. What if I can’t handle a client or project?**
If a client requires work beyond your expertise, our senior team handles it, and you still earn your share or a referral commission. You are never left to manage difficult clients alone.

**11. How does Nivy Next ensure quality in project delivery?**
Quality is ensured through a clear hierarchy of roles, defined responsibilities, senior-level reviews, milestone-based checks, and structured workflows to ensure every deliverable meets company standards.

**12. How exactly do I get paid and when?**
Payments are milestone-based. After each milestone is completed and approved by the client, your payment is released within 3–5 business days. A 15–20% holdback is retained for security and released after final project completion.

**13. How are payments calculated?**
Payments are based on your skill, experience, and project contribution. We provide full transparency on the payout structure before a project starts.

**14. Is there any hidden cost or fee?**
No. There are no hidden fees, and the joining fee is $0. Only a transparent revenue-sharing percentage applies for projects where we provide additional resources.

**15. Can I work with my existing clients under Nivy Next?**
Yes. You can onboard your existing clients under our brand to access delivery support, tools, and legal protection. A revenue share applies only for projects where we provide additional resources.

Our senior support team handles client communication, problem-solving, and quality checks. You are never left to manage difficult situations alone.

**17. How much time do I need to commit daily?**
We recommend 4–6 hours per day for optimal growth. Beginners can start with 2–3 hours per day and scale up as projects increase.

Absolutely. Once you reach the Team Partner level, we help you recruit, train, and manage freelancers. You earn a percentage from your team’s projects while we handle contracts and payments.

No freelancer gets instant projects without effort. However, with our proven client acquisition methods, you will receive projects faster than you would working alone.

Beginner freelancers with some knowledge can start with internal tasks and outreach to learn while working. Those with no prior knowledge can join our Digital Marketing Course and move to client projects once qualified.

Working in domains like Accounting & Bookkeeping or other recurring services provides you with a monthly income, which grows as you retain clients.

Yes. All projects come with written agreements, payment clarity, and contracts that protect you from non-payment, client disputes, and delivery issues.

You remain a freelancer, meaning you can choose your hours, select projects, and maintain your independence.

Senior mentors guide you through your first projects, allowing you to learn without risking clients or revenue, which accelerates your growth safely.

Your growth can be both financial and positional. As you deliver consistently, you can be promoted to higher roles, receive larger tasks, manage teams, and earn higher payouts.

Both options exist. Interns and juniors often receive short-term tasks, while mid-level and senior freelancers can be placed on long-term client retainers or internal strategic roles based on performance.

**30. Will I receive mentorship or guidance during projects?**
Yes. Seniors and managers are available to guide you through tasks, help solve challenges, and provide ongoing learning support.

The freelancer levels are: **Intern → Junior → Mid-Level → Senior/Manager**. Your level is determined by your experience and skills.

- Their contribution to the overall project success

Yes. Before you begin any project or milestone, you will receive a clear payout amount from the Company. There are no hidden changes or surprise reductions.

To maintain internal confidentiality and ensure fairness, the Company does not disclose client-side project values. Freelancers receive the payout defined for their specific role and responsibilities.

It is project-based. Your payout may vary from project to project depending on your assigned role, the difficulty of the tasks, and the project's internal structure.

Yes. Payouts increase with greater experience, higher skill level, increased project responsibility, and leadership or management roles.

### **41. How do interns, juniors, mid-level, and seniors differ in payment?**

Each level has its own internal payout structure, ensuring fair compensation, a clear hierarchy, and growth-based earning opportunities.

Yes. You receive payments after each milestone is completed and approved, ensuring a steady and reliable income flow.
