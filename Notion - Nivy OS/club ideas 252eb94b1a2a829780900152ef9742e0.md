# 🔹 7 — ENGAGEMENT STRATEGY

- **Mix online + offline**: Daily quizzes, memes, polls online; weekly meetups offline
- **Gamification**: Create leaderboards, badges, and rewards
- **VIP / adult separation**: Only optional participants join adult/creator contests
- **Content creation**: Every event generates content for social media & marketing
- **Consistency**: Daily / weekly engagement keeps members active & loyal

---

✅ Key takeaway:

If your club includes **fun games, contests, quizzes, trips, gamified activities, social events, and creative challenges**, you will:

- Keep members engaged **daily & weekly**
- Create a **pipeline of potential VIP / adult members**
- Build trust and loyalty
- Generate content for social media, marketing, and revenue
