# CMO / Marketing Department – Complete Document Checklist

# 

### **1. Marketing Strategy & Planning**

1. Marketing Strategy / Annual Marketing Plan
2. Brand Strategy & Positioning Document
3. Campaign Strategy Documents (digital, offline, email, events)
4. Marketing Goals & KPIs (lead generation, conversion, ROI)
5. Budget Allocation & Spend Plan

---

### **2. Branding & Messaging**

1. Branding Guidelines (logo, colors, fonts, tone, messaging)
2. Brand Story & Value Proposition
3. Company Portfolio & Brochures
4. Product / Service Pricing Packages
5. Discount & Offer Guidelines
6. Case Studies & Testimonials

---

### **3. Content & Communication**

1. Content Calendar & Campaign Plans
2. Website Content Guidelines & SEO Strategy
3. Social Media Strategy & Content Guidelines
4. Email Marketing & Automation SOPs
5. Event / Webinar Marketing SOPs
6. Press Releases & PR Guidelines
7. Influencer / Collaboration Guidelines
8. Referral / Affiliate Marketing Guidelines

---

### **4. Marketing Operations & SOPs**

1. Marketing Tools / Software Guidelines (Ads Platforms, CRM, Analytics)
2. Marketing Automation SOPs
3. Lead Generation & Nurture Templates
4. Campaign Execution SOPs
5. Analytics & Tracking Documentation
6. Content Repository / Asset Library
7. Promotional Collateral Templates

---

### **5. Reporting & Monitoring**

1. Marketing Dashboard Templates (leads, conversions, campaign ROI)
2. Campaign Performance Reports
3. SEO / SEM / Paid Ads Performance Reports
4. Marketing Post-Mortem & Lessons Learned Templates

---

### **6. Policies & Compliance**

1. Marketing Policies & Compliance Guidelines (copyright, GDPR, advertising laws)
2. Event Marketing SOPs
3. Crisis Communication / PR Response Templates

---

### **7. Training & Knowledge Base**

1. Onboarding Checklist for Marketing Team Members
2. Training Manuals for Tools & Campaign Execution
3. FAQ / Knowledge Base for Internal Marketing Processes

---

### **8. Optional / Advanced**

1. Market Research & Competitive Intelligence Reports
2. Innovation & New Campaign Proposal Templates
3. Strategic Partnership / Co-Marketing Templates
4. Customer Persona & Segmentation Updates
