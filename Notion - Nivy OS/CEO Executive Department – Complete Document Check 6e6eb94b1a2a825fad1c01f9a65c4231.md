# CEO / Executive Department – Complete Document Checklist

# 

### **1. Strategy & Vision**

1. Company Mission, Vision, and Values Document
2. Strategic Business Plan (3–5 year roadmap)
3. Annual Business Plan & Objectives
4. Expansion / Growth Strategy Documents (market entry, global expansion)
5. Innovation & Product/Service Development Roadmap

---

### **2. Governance & Policy**

1. Corporate Governance Guidelines
2. Board / Advisory Committee Documentation (if applicable)
3. Company Policies Overview (high-level summary for all departments)
4. Risk Management & Contingency Planning Documents
5. Legal & Compliance Oversight Guidelines

---

### **3. Financial Oversight**

1. High-Level Financial Reports & Dashboards (P&L, cash flow, balance sheet)
2. Budget Approval & Capital Allocation Guidelines
3. Investment & Funding Documents (loans, equity, partnerships)
4. Revenue Growth & Profitability Analysis Reports
5. Financial Forecasting & Scenario Planning

---

### **4. Operations & Performance Monitoring**

1. Department KPI Dashboards & Executive Summaries
2. Business Performance Reports (monthly, quarterly, yearly)
3. Operations & Process Review Templates
4. Strategic Initiatives Tracking Documents
5. Quality & Client Satisfaction Oversight Templates

---

### **5. Stakeholder Management**

1. Investor / Partner Reporting Templates
2. Key Client / Strategic Partner Communication SOPs
3. PR & Corporate Communications Guidelines (CEO-level messaging)
4. Networking & Partnership Documentation

---

### **6. Leadership & Team Management**

1. Organization Structure / Org Chart Updates
2. Succession Planning Documents
3. Executive Team Roles & Responsibility Guidelines
4. Leadership KPIs & Performance Evaluation Templates
5. Talent Development & Retention Strategy

---

### **7. Innovation & Growth Initiatives**

1. New Market / Product Feasibility Study Templates
2. Competitive & Market Intelligence Reports
3. Strategic Partnership / Acquisition Documents
4. Technology & Automation Planning Documents
5. Expansion & Global Operations SOPs

---

### **8. Optional / Advanced**

1. Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Strategy & Reports
2. Strategic Risk Assessment & Audit Reports
3. M&A / Investment Analysis Templates
4. CEO Dashboard Templates (high-level, real-time performance tracking)
5. Annual / Quarterly Board Presentations
