## 🏢 Nivy Alliance – Important Operating Points (Summary)

### 1️⃣ Core Departments (Final)

1. **Marketing & Sales** – bring clients, build trust, onboard to Nivy Alliance
2. **Program Manager / Coordination** – single bridge between client, delivery & finance
3. **Delivery** – execute digital marketing & accounting work
4. **Finance / Accounting** – invoice, payment control, approval
5. **Founder** – strategy, escalation, final authority

---

### 2️⃣ Department Responsibilities (Key Rules)

- **Sales**
    - Outreach, lead qualification, requirement collection
    - ❌ Cannot promise pricing, timelines, or delivery
- **Program Manager**
    - Task assignment, coordination, quality & deadline control
    - ✅ Only communication bridge
- **Delivery**
    - Executes work only
    - ❌ No direct DM with clients
    - ❌ No work without payment approval
- **Finance**
    - Makes invoices, tracks payments, approves work
    - 🚨 No payment = no delivery
- **Founder**
    - Oversees system, profit, partnerships

---

### 3️⃣ Communication System

- **Platform:** Discord (single company server)
- Client + Delivery + PM in same channels
- **DMs disabled**
- All communication logged in channels
- Program Manager always involved

---

### 4️⃣ Payment Control

- Invoice is **made by Finance / Accounting Dept**
- Delivery starts **only after Finance marks payment approved**
- Payment status visible in Discord
- Sales cannot interfere in payment decisions

---

### 5️⃣ Team Structure Rule

- **1 Senior → 3–5 juniors (start)**
- **1 Senior → 5–7 juniors (ideal)**
- ❌ 10–15 per senior is not recommended (quality drops)

---

### 6️⃣ Hiring & Growth Logic

- Seniors hire & train juniors
- Juniors grow into seniors
- Each department trains its own people
- System grows without founder daily involvement

---

### 7️⃣ Global Non-Negotiable Rules

- No DM between client & delivery
- No verbal commitments
- No work without payment approval
- Program Manager controls workflow
- Everything documented

---

### 8️⃣ Core Philosophy

> Sales brings business
Finance protects money
Program Manager protects sanity
Delivery protects quality
> 

This structure = **self-growing, payment-secure, scalable company**.
