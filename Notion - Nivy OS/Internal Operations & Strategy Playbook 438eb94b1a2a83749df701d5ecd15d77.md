## 2. STRATEGIC ROLE OF THIS COMMUNITY

This community is **NOT an end product**.

It is an **entry funnel**.

### Primary Objectives

1. Attract large volume of people at low cost
2. Filter serious contributors from free users
3. Build talent pool (jobs, freelancers, interns)
4. Create future sales & ambassadors
5. Feed selected members into **Nivy Alliance**

Think of this as:

> Top of funnel → Filtering layer → Talent & revenue engine
> 

---

## 3. OVERALL FUNNEL FLOW (INTERNAL)

Jobs & Learning Community

→ Contributors (via posting & tasks)

→ Certified Contributors

→ Paid Tasks / Team Leads

→ Discord (execution layer)

→ Nivy Alliance (premium layer)

→ Revenue (services, courses, partnerships)

No one skips steps.

---

## 4. PLATFORM & CONTROL LOGIC

### Tier Usage

- **WhatsApp**: Volume, Tier-3 countries, free value
- **Telegram**: Slightly better quality, learning-focused
- **Email + LinkedIn**: Tier-1 professionals & companies
- **Discord**: Execution, delivery, work coordination
- **Website**: Authority, verification, certificates

Each platform has a **clear boundary**.

Never mix purposes.

---

## 5. COMMUNITY CONTROL PRINCIPLES

1. Members see only **next step**, not full roadmap
2. Rewards are **manual, not automatic**
3. Promotions are **invite-based**
4. Certificates are **controlled centrally**
5. Monetization logic is **never public**

If members understand the whole system, they will try to game it.

---

## 6. GAMIFICATION ENGINE (INTERNAL LOGIC)

### Point System (Internal Reference)

| Action | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Valid job post | +5 |
| Valid free course post | +5 |
| Task completion | +5 |
| Invite 1 active contributor | +2 |
| Fake / spam post | –10 |
| Rule violation | –5 to ban |

Points are used for:

- Ranking
- Filtering
- Shortlisting for tasks
- Certificates & roles

Points are **not publicly debated**.

## 7. ROLE & PROMOTION LADDER (INTERNAL)

### Role Levels

1. **Member**
    
    – Default state
    
    – No privileges
    
2. **Contributor**
    
    – Minimum 5 valid contributions
    
    – Eligible for certificate
    
3. **Verified Contributor**
    
    – Consistent quality
    
    – Eligible for paid tasks
    
4. **Team Lead**
    
    – Manages 10–15 contributors
    
    – Responsible for output & discipline
    
5. **Community Manager**
    
    – Oversees multiple leads
    
    – Moderation + reporting
    
6. **Alliance Partner**
    
    – Trusted, long-term contributor
    
    – Revenue-linked role
    

⚠️ Promotions are **manual and discretionary**.

## 8. CERTIFICATION STRATEGY (INTERNAL USE)

Certificates are used as:

- Motivation tool
- Filtering mechanism
- External credibility asset
- Employer/client trust signal

### Rules

- Issued monthly or quarterly
- Revocable if misuse detected
- Verification controlled by Nivy
- No auto-issuance

Certificates should **never promise employment**.

### Task Types

- Post verification
