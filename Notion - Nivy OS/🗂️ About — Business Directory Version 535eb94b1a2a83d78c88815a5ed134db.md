### 📄 Clutch / GoodFirms — Primary Description (250 words)

Nivy Next is an AI-first digital services company headquartered in Lucknow, India, delivering measurable growth outcomes to businesses across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and UAE.

Founded as the digital innovation division of Nivy, we combine strategic intelligence with hands-on execution across seven integrated service verticals: Digital Marketing, AI Solutions & Automation, Website Development, App Development, Graphic Design, Video Production, and Virtual Assistant Services.

Our differentiation lies in three fundamentals. First, AI is not a feature we added — it is embedded in how we think, build, and operate across every service line. Second, we operate with a single-team accountability model: one point of contact, one strategy, seven capabilities. Third, we are genuinely international — with market-aware delivery across five Tier-1 geographies, not just offshore execution.

Our clients are growth-stage founders, CMOs, and operators who need a trusted partner to scale their digital presence, automate their operations, and generate qualified pipeline — without managing multiple vendors or tolerating inconsistent quality.

We offer three engagement models: Starter (single service, rolling), Growth (multi-service, 3 months+), and Partner (full-stack, 6 months+). All engagements include dedicated account management, defined KPIs, and transparent weekly reporting.

**Core Services:** Digital Marketing · AI Automation · Web Development · App Development · Graphic Design · Video Production · Virtual Assistant Services

**Markets Served:** US · UK · Canada · Australia · UAE
