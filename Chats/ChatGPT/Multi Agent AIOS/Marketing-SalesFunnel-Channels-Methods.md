# Marketing & Sales Funnel — Channels aur Methods ki Poori List

Ye list aapke uploaded **Marketing** aur **Sales Funnel** knowledge-base folders se banayi gayi hai. Total **22 Marketing modules** aur **54 Sales Funnel stages** hain.

---

## 🟦 PART 1: MARKETING (Track M) — 22 Channels/Methods

### Phase A — Foundation
| # | Channel/Method | Kya karta hai |
|---|---|---|
| M01 | Brand and Positioning Foundation | Brand voice, mission-vision-values, positioning statement, visual identity define karta hai |
| M02 | Channel and Platform Selection | Decide karta hai kaunse acquisition channels par resources lagane hain |
| M03 | Content Pillars and Messaging Framework | Recurring content themes/pillars aur messaging framework banata hai |

### Phase B — SEO Engine
| # | Channel/Method | Kya karta hai |
|---|---|---|
| M04 | Keyword Research and Mapping | Keywords research karke content/pages par map karta hai |
| M05 | On-Page SEO Implementation | Pages ka on-page SEO (title, meta, structure) implement karta hai |
| M06 | Technical SEO and Site Health | Site speed, crawlability, technical health maintain karta hai |
| M07 | Off-Page SEO and Authority Building | Backlinks aur domain authority build karta hai |

### Phase C — Content Engine
| # | Channel/Method | Kya karta hai |
|---|---|---|

| M09 | Long-Form Content Production | Blog posts, pillar pages, whitepapers, case studies likhta hai |
| M10 | Content Repurposing and Distribution Engine | Ek long-form piece ko sab channels ke format me convert karta hai |

### Phase D — Social Channels (Organic)

### Phase E — Email
| # | Channel/Method | Kya karta hai |

### Phase F — Growth
| # | Channel/Method | Kya karta hai |
|---|---|---|

### Phase G — Community & Partners
| # | Channel/Method | Kya karta hai |
|---|---|---|

| # | Channel/Method | Kya karta hai |

## Quick Summary
- **Marketing (Track M):** 22 modules — Brand → SEO → Content → Social (6 channels) → Email → Growth Hacking → Community/PR → Analytics → CRM Bridge
