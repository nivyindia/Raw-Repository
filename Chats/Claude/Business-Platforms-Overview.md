### Do these platforms work like Upwork or Fiverr?

**No.** They are **not bidding marketplaces**.

Their workflow is usually:

1. Register an account.
2. Complete profile verification.
3. Take qualification or language/skill tests.
4. Sign NDA or contractor agreement (if required).
5. Get matched or assigned to available projects.
6. Complete work.
7. Receive payment based on hours, completed tasks, or approved projects.

| Payment Model | Platforms |

| Monthly | Long-term contractor roles at Invisible Technologies, iMerit |

### For building an agency

These platforms fall into three broad models:

| Model | Platforms | Can you supply a team? |
|--------|-----------|------------------------|
| Individual contractor | Outlier AI, TELUS Digital AI, WeLocalize, RWS | Usually **No** |
| Project-based contributor | Appen, OneForma, DataForce, Defined.ai | **Sometimes**, depending on the project |
| Enterprise/vendor model | Scale AI, iMerit, Invisible Technologies, SuperAnnotate | **Yes**, typically through business partnerships or vendor agreements rather than individual accounts |

If your goal is to build a company that provides AI workforces, the most relevant platforms to study are the **enterprise/vendor model** companies, because they recruit, train, and manage large teams rather than relying on freelancers bidding for work.
