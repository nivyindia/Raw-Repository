# Enterprise Business Model Layers

Large international companies often think of their business model in six interconnected layers:

| Layer | Focus |
|--------|-------|
| Strategic Model | Vision, market positioning, competitive advantage |
| Commercial Model | Customers, pricing, revenue streams, sales |
| Operational Model | Delivery processes, supply chain, quality |
| Organizational Model | Teams, governance, decision-making |
| Financial Model | Costs, margins, investment, cash flow |
| Technology Model | Digital platforms, AI, automation, data |

## Best Practice

Many Fortune 500 companies don't rely on a single "Business Model Canvas." Instead, they maintain a set of connected models:

1. **Business Model** – How value is created, delivered, and captured.
2. **Operating Model** – How the organization executes the business model.
3. **Financial Model** – How the business generates sustainable returns.
4. **Go-to-Market (GTM) Model** – How customers are acquired and retained.
5. **Growth Model** – How the company scales over time.

Together, these provide a comprehensive blueprint that investors, executives, and leadership teams can use to guide decision-making and execution.
