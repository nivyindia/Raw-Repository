## 🔹 1. VISION & CORE CONCEPT

**Vision:**

To build the largest youth lifestyle & social ecosystem that connects students, professionals, creators, and entertainment industries through safe communities, events, career growth, and creator opportunities.

**Core Idea:**

Start with a **non-adult youth fun & career club** → build massive trusted community → layer nightlife, travel, discounts → identify creator talent → later launch **separate creator & adult brands internationally**.
