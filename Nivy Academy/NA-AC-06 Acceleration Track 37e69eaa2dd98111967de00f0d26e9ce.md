# NA-AC-06 | Acceleration Track

**Doc ID:** NA-AC-06 · **Dept:** Academic · **Phase:** 1 · **Audience:** Freelancers, Experienced Professionals, Agency Owners · **Status:** 🟢 Live

**Tags:** acceleration, advanced, freelancers, client acquisition, scaling, agency

---

## 🎯 Who This Track Is For

- Freelancers who have skills but can’t get international clients
- Experienced professionals not getting promoted or switching careers
- Small agency owners who can’t scale beyond 2–3 clients
- Anyone who already knows basic DM and wants to level up to global standard

---

## 🗓️ 6-Week Acceleration Plan

### Week 1 — Repositioning as a Global Consultant

- Auditing your current positioning vs. international standard
- Rewriting your pitch, bio, and LinkedIn to attract USA/UK clients
- The "Consultant" vs. "Freelancer" language shift
- **Task:** Rewrite your LinkedIn headline + summary + cold intro

### Week 2 — CRM & Lead Pipeline Architecture

- Setting up a professional CRM (Bitrix24 / Notion)
- Building a 100-lead pipeline from scratch
- Segmenting leads by industry, company size, intent signals
- **Task:** Set up full CRM + add 50 segmented leads

### Week 3 — Global Outreach (USA/UK Tactics)

- Advanced cold email sequences (3-touch, 5-touch frameworks)
- LinkedIn outreach at scale (without getting banned)
- WhatsApp Business for international clients
- **Task:** Launch a 3-touch outreach campaign to 30 prospects

### Week 4 — High-Ticket Closing & Negotiation

- How to price international projects (retainer models)
- The closing call framework (discovery → pitch → close)
- Handling "we’ll think about it" and extended silence
- **Task:** 2 live mock closing calls with feedback

### Week 5 — Agency Operations & Delegation

- Systemising your service delivery with SOPs
- Hiring and managing VAs for delivery
- Client communication workflows (tools, cadence, reporting)
- **Task:** Build 1 SOP for your core service delivery

### Week 6 — Scaling, Retainers & International Expansion

- Moving from project to retainer model
- Building a referral network of international partners
- Nivy Global Alliance: how to collaborate with Nivy for overflow
- **Task:** Draft a 3-month growth plan for your business

---

## 🏆 Outcomes

- Signed retainer with first international client
- Professional CRM with 100+ leads pipeline
- Complete portfolio + case studies
- Alliance partnership with Nivy (optional revenue-share)
