# Accelerated Entry Path (AEP)

## One programme. Multiple entry points.

> **The premise:** The International Skills Programme is one 26-week programme. But not every student needs to start at Week 1. Students who already have demonstrable skills in Phases 1–2 content (professional setup, digital marketing) can enter directly at Phase 3 (Week 11) or Phase 4 (Week 15) — after passing a diagnostic assessment that confirms their readiness.
> 

The AEP is not a shorter or easier programme. The remaining phases are completed in full at the same standard. The only thing that changes is the starting point.

---

## Who Should Apply for AEP?

**Strong AEP candidates:**

- Working professionals in marketing, sales, or business development with 1+ years of experience
- Freelancers already earning on Upwork, Fiverr, or direct from clients
- Career changers who have completed Google, HubSpot, Meta, or similar certifications independently
- Graduates of short digital marketing courses who want the full international skills credential
- Students who completed the old Nivy Foundation Track (NA-AC-02/03/04) and want to upgrade to ISC

**AEP is NOT appropriate for:**

- Complete beginners with no professional or freelance experience
- Students who have never used Apollo, LinkedIn for outreach, or any email marketing tool
- Students uncertain about which service to offer or which market to target
- Students who cannot confidently answer the diagnostic questions without external help

## The Diagnostic Assessment

All AEP applicants complete a **2-part Diagnostic Assessment** before being assigned an entry point. This is not a barrier — it is a tool to place students correctly so they are set up to succeed, not overwhelmed.

### Part A — Written Self-Assessment (submitted async)

Submit responses to the following questions (write 150–300 words per question):

1. **Professional background:** What is your current or most recent work experience? What digital marketing or sales tasks have you performed? Be specific.
2. **Tools:** Which of the following have you actively used (not just heard of)? [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io), LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEMrush or Ahrefs, Mailchimp or Klaviyo, HubSpot CRM, Looker Studio, GA4, Zapier. For each you list, describe one specific thing you did with it.
3. **Outreach:** Have you ever run a cold email or LinkedIn outreach campaign? If yes: what was the niche, what tool did you use, what was the reply rate, and what happened next?
4. **Sales:** Have you ever conducted a discovery call with a potential client? If yes: describe the structure you used and the outcome.
5. **Income:** Are you currently earning anything from digital marketing or business development work (freelance, employed, or hybrid)? If yes: how much and from what?

### Part B — Diagnostic Task (submitted within 48 hours)

Complete both tasks:

**Task 1 — Mini Brand Audit:**

Choose any real international business (US, UK, or Australian company). Conduct a 30-minute audit using at least 2 tools (SimilarWeb, SEMrush, Facebook Ad Library, Ahrefs, or Google Search Console). Write a 1-page audit report: what they are doing well, what the top 3 gaps are, and what you would recommend as the first priority. This tests Phase 1–2 content mastery.

**Task 2 — Positioning Statement + Service Package:**

Write your positioning statement ("I help [target client] get [result] using [method] in [timeframe]"). Then outline one service package: name, deliverables, price in USD, and a 2-sentence pitch. This tests professional identity clarity from Phase 1.

## Entry Point Decision

The mentor reviews both parts and assigns one of three outcomes:

| Outcome | Criteria | Entry Point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Entry at Phase 3 (Week 11)** | Strong in Phases 1–2: brand audit is solid, positioning clear, has used 4+ tools listed. Weak or no outreach/sales experience. | Student completes Phases 3–6 + Phase 7 if needed. Total: ~16 weeks |
| **Entry at Phase 4 (Week 15)** | Strong in Phases 1–3: has run outreach campaigns, used AI tools, has CRM experience. Weak in structured sales/closing. | Student completes Phases 4–6 + Phase 7 if needed. Total: ~12 weeks |
| **Standard Entry (Phase 1, Week 1)** | Gaps in foundational areas that would create problems later. AEP not recommended — student starts from Phase 1. | Full 26-week programme |

> **Important:** Mentor decisions are final. A student who disagrees with their entry point assignment may request a reconsideration review with the Academic Head, but the outcome of that review is binding.

## What AEP Students Must Complete Before Starting

Regardless of entry point, all AEP students must complete the following before their first phase begins:

**Before Phase 3 entry:**

- [ ]  ISP-01 Practical Submission (professional setup package — same 8 items as ISP-01 Module Exam, self-assessed and mentor-reviewed in the diagnostic call)
- [ ]  ISP-02 written summary: one-page overview of digital marketing channels you have actively worked with and your proficiency level in each
- [ ]  [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) account active with a verified 5-contact test export (from ISP-01 Lesson 1.3b)
- [ ]  7-Day Daily Log Compliance Test passed (from NA-AC-08) — must be submitted the week before the first phase begins

**Before Phase 4 entry (additional to above):**

- [ ]  Evidence of at least one prior outreach campaign: tool used, list size, reply rate (screenshot or export accepted)
- [ ]  ISP-03 written summary: list of AI tools you have used and one example use case per tool
- [ ]  Completed prompt library of at least 5 templates (can be from prior work, not required to rebuild)

## Graduation & Certificate

AEP students receive the **same ISC certificate** as standard programme graduates, on the same criteria:

- Pass all remaining Phase exams (30 MCQ each, 70% pass mark)
- Pass the Phase 3 Formative Checkpoint (Enrichment D assessment from ISP-03) if entering at Phase 3
- Pass the Final Graduation Assessment (50 MCQ + Capstone, 80% pass mark)

**There is no separate "Accelerated" certificate.** The ISC is the ISC regardless of entry point — what matters is completing the programme at standard, not how long it took.

## AEP Application Process

1. Contact your assigned Nivy academic coordinator and state you want to apply for AEP
2. Receive the Diagnostic Assessment brief (Parts A + B) — complete and submit within 5 working days
3. Mentor reviews and schedules a 30-minute diagnostic call within 3 working days of submission
4. Entry point assigned. Enrolment confirmed. Programme start date agreed.
5. Complete the pre-start checklist for your assigned entry point (above)
6. Begin your assigned phase on the agreed start date

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I skip the diagnostic and just start at Phase 4?**

No. The diagnostic protects you as much as it protects the programme standard. Entry without a diagnostic risks placing you in content you are not ready for, which typically leads to falling behind, struggling on assessments, and lower quality final work.

**Q: What if I have completed the old Nivy Foundation Track (NA-AC-02/03/04)?**

Foundation Track graduates are strong AEP candidates. Your Month 1–3 deliverables (opportunity report, LinkedIn profile, prospect list, media kit, outreach sprint data, discovery call) can be submitted as your diagnostic portfolio in lieu of Part B. Mentor will review and may assign Phase 3 or Phase 4 entry accordingly.

**Q: What happens to my pace — can I rush through phases?**

No. Each phase has a minimum duration (4 weeks for Phases 3–5, 4 weeks for Phase 6). You cannot compress them further. The minimum duration exists because the simulation days, outreach sprints, and sales roleplays require real time to run meaningfully.

**Q: Will my certificate show that I entered at an accelerated point?**

No. The certificate records programme completion and the ISC standard. It does not record your entry point.
