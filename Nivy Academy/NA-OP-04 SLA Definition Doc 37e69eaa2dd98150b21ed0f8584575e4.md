## ⚠️ Critical Distinction: "Placement Assistance" ≠ "Job Guarantee"

Nivy Academy provides **Placement Assistance** — not a guaranteed job offer from a specific company. This is an important legal and ethical distinction.

---

## 📌 What "Placement Assistance" Means (What We Do)

- Share your resume and portfolio with our **partner network** of companies and agencies
- Refer top-performing students to **Nivy Jobs** openings (internal first)
- Provide **interview coaching** for specific roles you apply to
- Write a **Reference Letter** from Nivy on your behalf
- Connect you with **freelance clients** through the Nivy Alliance network
- Conduct **monthly check-ins** during the Success Buffer to refine strategy
- Provide **access to job listings** curated for DM/VA roles in India and internationally

---

## ❌ What "Placement Assistance" Does NOT Mean

- Nivy does NOT guarantee a job offer from any specific company
- Nivy does NOT guarantee a minimum salary unless explicitly stated in writing
- Nivy does NOT have a fixed list of hiring partners with committed seat allocations
- Nivy is NOT responsible for a student failing an interview after preparation support

---

## 💬 How to Communicate This to Students

> *"We provide full placement support — resume reviews, partner referrals, mock interviews, and direct introductions to our network. We’ve helped students land roles with agencies, international clients, and companies in our partner ecosystem. While we can’t guarantee a specific job offer (no honest institution can), we guarantee we will actively support you until you’re placed or your 6-month window expires."*
>
