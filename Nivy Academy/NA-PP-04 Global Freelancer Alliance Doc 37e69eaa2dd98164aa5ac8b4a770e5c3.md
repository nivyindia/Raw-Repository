# NA-PP-04 | Global Freelancer Alliance — Onboarding Guide

---

## What is the Global Freelancer Alliance (GFA)?

The GFA is Nivy Academy's internal network of certified graduates who are eligible to take on real project work for Nivy's clients and sub-brands (Nivy Artisan, Nivy Growth, Nivy Careers, Nivy Next) as freelance delivery partners.

---

## Eligibility

- Completed Practitioner tier or above
- Passed Final Graduation Assessment (NA-AX-04) with Grade B or higher
- Completed at least one Practical Task with a "Ready for Client Work" rating

---

## Onboarding Steps

1. **Application** — Submit GFA interest form (linked from Student Progress Dashboard)
2. **Skills Verification** — Mentor confirms readiness based on Practical Task Rubrics (NA-AX-03)
3. **NDA Signing** — All GFA members sign the standard NDA (NA-OP-02) before receiving any client information
4. **Profile Creation** — Member profile created in internal GFA roster (skills, availability, rate band)
5. **First Assignment** — Member is matched to a low-stakes starter project to build track record

---

## Working Arrangements

- Members are paid per-project or per-deliverable, rates set per project type
- Nivy retains a coordination fee on all GFA project income (percentage TBD per project)
- Members must log all work via the Daily Log Template (NA-OP-06)

---

## Performance & Removal

- Members with consistent quality issues receive one written warning and a coaching session
- Repeated issues or confidentiality breaches result in removal from the Alliance

---

## Growth Path

GFA members with strong track records become eligible for:

- Direct Employer Placement (Model 4, see NA-PP-01)
- Internal hiring consideration at Nivy (Model 5)
- Becoming a mentor/reviewer for future Nivy Academy cohorts
