# Why Phase 0 Exists

With 382 tasks across 23 phases and a 2-year horizon, the plan is large enough to create paralysis. Phase 0 solves that by picking the 15 highest-leverage tasks from the existing plan and completing them before Wave 1 starts on July 1. Every item here is already in scope; Phase 0 just accelerates it.

Three rules for every item:

1. Completable by one person in 3 days or less.
2. Produces a visible, shareable output.
3. Either generates revenue, unblocks downstream work, or builds team confidence.
