## 1. PROGRAM DETAILS

The student is enrolling in the **[Track Name]** program at Nivy Academy, Lucknow, India, for a duration of **3 months** (with an optional 3-month Success Buffer as described in Clause 5).

## 2. FEES & PAYMENT

- Total fee: ₹[AMOUNT]
- Payment mode: [UPI / Bank Transfer / Razorpay]
- Fee is non-refundable except as described in Clause 6 (Refund Policy)

## 3. STUDENT OBLIGATIONS

The student agrees to:

- Attend minimum **80% of live sessions**
- Submit **all 12 weekly tasks** on time
- Log **minimum 150 outreach attempts** in the CRM over 3 months
- Maintain **Daily Logs** every working day
- Sign the NDA (NA-OP-02) before accessing any client data
- Follow the **Rules of Engagement** (NA-OP-05) at all times
- Not engage with any Nivy client directly outside the program

## 4. NIVY ACADEMY OBLIGATIONS

Nivy Academy agrees to:

- Provide access to all program materials, SOPs, and tools
- Assign a dedicated mentor for weekly check-ins
- Provide a graded evaluation for all submitted tasks within 48 hours
- Issue a Certificate of Completion upon successful graduation
- Provide Placement Assistance as defined in the SLA (NA-OP-04)

## 5. SUCCESS BUFFER

Students who complete the 3-month program without securing placement are automatically enrolled in a **3-month Success Buffer** at no additional cost, subject to meeting all obligations in Clause 3.

## 6. REFUND POLICY

A student is eligible for a **Performance-Linked Refund** ONLY if ALL of the following are true at the end of the 6-month period:

- Attended ≥80% of live sessions
- Submitted all 12 weekly tasks
- Logged ≥150 outreach attempts in CRM
- Completed Final Graduation Assessment
- Actively participated in all 3 Buffer months
- Did not violate NDA or Rules of Engagement

**Refund amount:** 80% of program fee (20% retained as admin charge)

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All materials, SOPs, scripts, templates, and strategies provided by Nivy Academy remain the exclusive intellectual property of Billion Dreams United. The student may not share, resell, or distribute these materials.

## 8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Any disputes shall first be addressed through internal mediation. If unresolved, jurisdiction shall be **Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India**.
