# NA-GR-06 | International Cohort Plan — Philippines, Africa, Middle East Rollout

---

## Purpose

Outlines how Nivy Academy expands beyond India to international student cohorts, leveraging the existing International landing page (NA-MK-03) and brochure (NA-MK-05).

---

## Target Regions & Rationale

<table header-row="true"><tr><td>Region</td><td>Why This Region</td><td>Primary Audience</td></tr><tr><td>Philippines</td><td>Large existing VA/outsourcing workforce, English proficiency, strong freelance culture</td><td>Aspiring VAs and freelancers wanting to move into digital marketing</td></tr><tr><td>Africa (Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa focus)</td><td>Fast-growing youth population, high demand for remote-work skills training, strong English-speaking markets</td><td>Graduates and job seekers targeting remote international roles</td></tr><tr><td>Middle East (UAE, Saudi expat communities)</td><td>High concentration of South Asian expat job seekers and small business owners needing marketing support</td><td>Expat job seekers and small business support roles</td></tr></table>

---

## Rollout Phases

### Phase A — Soft Launch (Philippines)

- Run International landing page (NA-MK-03) and brochure (NA-MK-05) with Philippines-specific testimonials (sourced via NA-GR-04 once available)
- Pilot cohort of 10-15 students, priced at International tiers (USD)
- Identify 1-2 local partner channels (online communities, freelancer groups) for outreach

---

### Phase B — Africa Expansion

- Adapt cold email/outreach templates (NA-MK-07) for region-specific institute/college partnerships (Model 1, NA-PP-01)
- Explore partnership with at least one training institute or NGO focused on youth employment

---

### Phase C — Middle East Expansion

- Focus on direct-to-consumer marketing (ads, NA-MK-09) targeting expat communities rather than institute partnerships
- Consider WhatsApp-based outreach (NA-MK-06) given high WhatsApp usage in target demographic

---

## Operational Considerations

- Time zones: mentor call scheduling (1:1s, group calls per pricing tiers NA-MK-01) must accommodate at least 2 international time zone bands
- Payment: ensure USD payment processing is reliable for each target region
- Currency/pricing already defined in International pricing column (NA-MK-01)

---

## Success Criteria

Each region's pilot cohort (Phase A/B/C) is evaluated against: enrollment numbers vs. target, completion rate, and placement/GFA conversion rate, before committing to a full regional rollout.
