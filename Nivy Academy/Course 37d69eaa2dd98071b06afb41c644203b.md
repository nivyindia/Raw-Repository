# Course

Aapne bilkul sahi point pakda hai—sirf teen options se market cover karna mushkil hota hai. Jab aapke paas **₹2,000 se lekar ₹5,00,000 tak** ka range hai, toh aapko "Value Ladder" (सीढ़ी) banani chahiye. Iska matlab hai ki customer ek chhote level se shuru kare aur aapki service ki quality dekh kar khud bade packages par upgrade ho jaye.

Yahan 5-tier pricing model hai jo har tarah ke customer ko cover karega:

### **The "Profit Ladder" Pricing Model**

| **Tier** | **Price Range** | **Audience** | **What They Get (Value)** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. The Trailblazer** | ₹2,000 - ₹3,500 | Students | Basic self-paced modules + Digital Certification. |
| **2. The Practitioner** | ₹7,500 - ₹12,000 | Job Seekers | Course + 4-Week Internship + Outreach Templates. |
| **3. The Accelerator** | ₹25,000 - ₹45,000 | Freelancers | Live Coaching + 3-Month Internship + CRM Access. |
| **4. The Scaler** | ₹99,000 - ₹1,50,000 | Small Business Owners | Agency "White-label" access + Lead Gen setup + 1-on-1 Mentorship. |
| **5. The Mastermind** | ₹3,00,000 - ₹5,00,000 | Agency Owners | Full Team Training + My Team's Direct Shadowing + Client Procurement Support. |

### **Har Tier ki "Strategic Role" (Kyun ye zaroori hain?)**

1. **The Trailblazer (Entry):** Ye sirf **"Trust"** banane ke liye hai. Iska maqsad profit nahi, balki customer ka database banana hai.
2. **The Practitioner (The Engine):** Ye aapka sabse zyada bikne wala (best-seller) product hona chahiye. Yahan se aapko **"Free Labour"** (Outreach karne wale interns) milte hain.
3. **The Accelerator (The Professional):** Ye freelancers ke liye hai jo serious hain. Yahan aapka time kam lagta hai kyunki ye group coaching hoti hai.
4. **The Scaler (The Profit):** Ye business owners ke liye hai. Inhe result chahiye, toh ye aapko high price dene mein nahi katraenge.
5. **The Mastermind (The Powerhouse):** Ye aapka "High-Ticket" offer hai. Yahan aap sirf 1-2 clients lete hain lekin aapki income ka bada hissa yahan se aata hai.

### **Is Strategy ko execute karne ke liye tips:**

- **The "Decoy" Effect:** Apne landing page par "Accelerator" (₹25k-₹45k) ko "Most Popular" mark karein. Jab log ₹25k aur ₹5L mein compare karenge, toh unhe ₹25k bahut reasonable lagega.
- **Upgrade Path:** Jo "Trailblazer" (₹2k) le, use 15 din baad automatic email/message bhejein: *"Ready to start real outreach? Upgrade to 'Practitioner' for just the difference amount."*
- **The "Shadowing" Factor (Elite Tiers ke liye):** ₹3 Lakh+ ke package mein log aapka **"Time"** kharidte hain. Unhe promise karein ki aap unke business ke liye strategy banayenge aur apni team se unke kaam ko review karwayenge.

### **Claude/AI ke liye "Mega-Prompt"**

Aap ab is prompt ka use karke apne saare course ka content ek saath banwa sakte hain:

> *"I am launching a 5-tier training ecosystem for Digital Marketing and Virtual Assistant services. My tiers range from ₹2,000 to ₹5,00,000.
> 
> 1. Create a **Value Delivery Plan** for each tier, ensuring that as the price increases, the student gets more 'Done-for-You' services rather than just 'Information'.
> 2. Write a **Conversion Script** to upsell a 'Trailblazer' customer into the 'Practitioner' tier.
> 3. Provide a **Structure for the 'Mastermind' (₹5L tier)**. What specific high-value deliverables (like agency-audits, lead-gen systems, or team-hiring support) can I offer to justify this price?
> 4. Keep the tone professional, results-oriented, and focused on ROI for the customer."*
