## 📌 Upgrade Policy

- Students can upgrade from Foundation → Expert or Expert → Professional at any time during their active enrolment by paying the price difference (no re-enrolment fee).
- Upgrades are processed within 24 hours.
- Modules already completed carry over fully on upgrade.
- Downgrade is not available — contact support for refund/transfer queries.
