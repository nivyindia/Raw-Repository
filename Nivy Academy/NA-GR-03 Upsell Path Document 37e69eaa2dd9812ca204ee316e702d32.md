# NA-GR-03 | Upsell Path — Trailblazer to Elite Journey

---

## Purpose

Maps how students move up the 5-tier pricing ladder (NA-MK-01) over time, both during and after the core program.

---

## The Upgrade Journey

### Stage 1: During Core Program (Month 1-3)

- Trailblazer/Starter students showing strong engagement (per Daily Logs and early task scores) are flagged by mentors as "Upgrade Candidates" by end of Month 1
- Mentor has a dedicated conversation by Month 2 presenting the value of upgrading to Practitioner — framed around added 1:1 mentor calls and placement assistance
- Upgrade = pay the price difference; remaining program content is unlocked immediately

---

### Stage 2: At Graduation

- Practitioner graduates with Grade A performance are offered the **Accelerator Track** as a follow-on (Acceleration Track content, NA-AC-06) at a loyalty discount
- Framing: "You've proven you can do the basics — now go further, faster."

---

### Stage 3: Post-Placement (Alumni)

- Alumni who are placed and stable (6+ months, per Alumni Tracker NA-GR-01) are offered **Elite tier add-ons**: weekly mentor access, Advisory Board invitation track, and co-branded case study development
- This stage doubles as a retention mechanism — keeps high-performers engaged with Nivy long-term

Upsell conversions are tracked in the Alumni Tracker (NA-GR-01, "Upsell Status" field) to measure program revenue per student over their full lifecycle, not just initial enrollment.
