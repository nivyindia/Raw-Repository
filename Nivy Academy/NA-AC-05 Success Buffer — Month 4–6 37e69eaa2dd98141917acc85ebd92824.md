## 🎯 Purpose of the Success Buffer

The Success Buffer is a **3-month extended support period** for students who complete the core 3-month program but haven’t yet secured placement. It protects the student AND protects Nivy’s refund policy by ensuring every student gets maximum support before a refund is considered.

---

## 🗓️ What Happens During the Buffer

### Monthly Cadence (Months 4, 5, 6)

| Activity | Frequency | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1:1 Mentor Check-in Call | Weekly | 30 min |
| Outreach Review (CRM audit) | Bi-weekly | 20 min |
| Interview coaching session | On demand | 45 min |
| New lead list refresh | Monthly | Mentor-provided |
| Application tracking & feedback | Ongoing | Async |

### Buffer Includes

- Continued access to all tools (Apollo, CRM, SOP Library)
- Access to new job postings via Nivy Jobs
- Priority referral to partner companies
- Revised resume + portfolio review (once per month)
- Access to new batch’s live sessions (audit-only)

## ⚠️ Refund Eligibility Criteria

A student is eligible for a refund ONLY if ALL of the following are true:

- [ ]  Attended minimum 80% of live sessions
- [ ]  Logged minimum 150 outreach attempts in CRM
- [ ]  Submitted all 12 weekly task assignments
- [ ]  Completed Final Graduation Assessment
- [ ]  Actively participated in all 3 Buffer months
- [ ]  Did not violate NDA or Rules of Engagement

> **Note:** If any criterion is unmet, the refund is void. This is defined in the Student Agreement (NA-OP-01).

## 📋 Buffer Activation — Formal Enrollment Process

The Success Buffer is not automatic. A student must formally enroll using the Buffer Activation Form. Without enrollment, no Buffer support is provided and the student is not covered for refund eligibility consideration.

**Step 1 — Eligibility Check (Mentor)**

The mentor reviews the student's record at the end of Month 3. The student is eligible for the Buffer if:

- The 3-month Foundation Track is fully complete (all 12 weeks attended or caught up)
- At least 80% of weekly tasks have been submitted
- The Graduation Assessment has been attempted (pass or fail)
- No paid client engagement has been secured as of the last day of Month 3

**Step 2 — Buffer Activation Form**

The mentor shares the Buffer Activation Form with the eligible student. The student must complete it within 5 days of Month 3 ending.

**Buffer Activation Form — Fields:**

| Field | What to Enter |
| --- | --- |
| Full Name |  |
| Email Address |  |
| Cohort / Batch |  |
| Mentor Name |  |
| Month 3 Graduation Assessment result | Pass / Fail / Pending |
| Total outreach attempts in CRM (lifetime) | Number |
| Number of weekly tasks submitted (out of 12) | Number |
| Brief description of what you've tried so far | 100 words max |
| Why you haven't secured placement yet (honest answer) | 100 words max |
| What you need most from the Buffer (support focus) | 100 words max |

> The form is submitted to the mentor. The mentor countersigns and uploads it to the student's record in NA-SM-02 (internal operations). The student receives a confirmation message within 2 business days.
> 

**Step 3 — Buffer Start Date**

The Buffer begins on the Monday following mentor countersignature. The student receives a message confirming their Buffer start date, their assigned Buffer mentor (may be a different mentor), and the schedule for their first weekly check-in call.

**Mentor Sign-Off Requirement:**

At the end of each Buffer month, the mentor must complete a one-paragraph written assessment of the student's progress and submit it to the Operations team. This ensures no student is left unsupported and creates a paper trail if a refund is later requested.

## 🚪 Buffer Exit Criteria — Precise Definition

A student formally exits the Buffer when ONE of the following conditions is met:

**Exit Condition A — Income Earned:**

The student receives a payment of a minimum **₹7,500 / $100 USD / £80 GBP equivalent** from a paying client for a legitimate freelance service or consulting engagement.

- Evidence required: screenshot of payment receipt (bank transfer, PayPal, Razorpay, Stripe, Wise, or equivalent)
- One-time micro-tasks under ₹500 do NOT count

**Exit Condition B — Employment Offer:**

The student receives and accepts a written job offer (full-time or part-time, remote or on-site) for a role related to their Nivy Academy training.

- Evidence required: screenshot or PDF of the offer letter or official offer message on company letterhead or official email domain

**Exit Condition C — Freelance Contract Signed:**

The student signs a recurring retainer contract (minimum 1 month, minimum ₹7,500 / $100 USD equivalent per month) with a client.

- Evidence required: signed contract (can be redacted for client privacy)

**What does NOT count as a Buffer exit:**

- Verbal promises of work with no payment or contract
- Unpaid internships or trial periods
- One-time tasks under the minimum threshold
- Referrals or introductions that have not converted to paid work

**After Exit:**

The mentor logs the exit in NA-SM-02. The student receives a "Graduate Placed" status in the HQ registry. They are then eligible for Freelancer Alliance consideration (criteria on NA-AC-01).

**If a student reaches the end of Month 6 without meeting an exit condition:**

The student's case is reviewed by the Nivy Academy leadership. Options include: one additional buffer month (exceptional cases only, at Nivy's discretion), escalation to the refund review process (per NA-OP-01), or reassignment to a different track or niche.

## 🗓️ Guest Session Request Process (Month 5)

Month 5 includes access to a guest session with a Nivy mentor experienced in the student's target niche. Here is how to request and arrange it:

**How a student requests a guest session:**

1. Student messages their Buffer mentor via Slack or WhatsApp with: "I'd like to request a guest session for Month 5. My target niche is [industry/role type]. Preferred format: [live call / async video]."
2. Mentor forwards the request to the Operations team ([operations@nivyempires.com](mailto:operations@nivyempires.com) or internal Slack #academy-ops).
3. Operations team matches the student with a suitable guest mentor and confirms within 5 business days.
4. Student receives a calendar invite for a 45-minute session.

**Format of the guest session:**

- 10 min: Student presents their current situation (where they are, what they've tried)
- 20 min: Guest mentor shares niche-specific insight, dos and don'ts, and introduces their own outreach or placement story
- 15 min: Q&A and personalised advice

**If no suitable guest mentor is available:**

Operations will offer an alternative — either a group workshop session or an async video response from a senior Nivy team member within the niche.
