# 🌍 International Skills Programme (ISP) — Overview

## Programme Mission

To produce professionals who can independently win, deliver, and retain international clients — from the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and the global remote market — using Digital Marketing, AI, Virtual Assistance, Lead Generation, Sales, and Client Closing skills.

This is not a course. This is a **professional training system** built to the same standard as corporate onboarding at a global agency.

---

## 🏗️ Programme Architecture

| Phase | Duration | Focus | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Phase 1: Foundation** | Weeks 1–4 | Mindset, tools, digital landscape, professional standards | Student is workplace-ready in attitude and basics |
| **Phase 2: Digital Marketing Mastery** | Weeks 5–10 | SEO, Content, Social Media, Ads, Analytics, Email | Can execute full digital marketing campaigns independently |
| **Phase 3: AI & Virtual Assistance** | Weeks 11–14 | AI tools, VA workflows, automation, productivity systems | Can operate as an AI-powered VA delivering 2x output |
| **Phase 4: Lead Generation & Outreach** | Weeks 15–18 | LinkedIn, Apollo, cold email, DM, targeting, lists | Can generate 50–100 qualified leads per week |
| **Phase 5: Sales, Closing & Psychology** | Weeks 19–22 | Discovery calls, objection handling, proposals, closing | Can close $500–$5,000 international contracts |
| **Phase 6: Client Management & Delivery** | Weeks 23–24 | Onboarding, reporting, retention, upselling, referrals | Can manage long-term international client relationships |

**Total Duration:** 24 Weeks (6 Months)

**Format:** Daily lessons + daily tasks + weekly targets + module exams + live practicals

## 🎯 Target Outcome by Graduation

Every student who completes ISP must be able to:

- Build and manage a complete digital marketing campaign for an international client
- Use AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, automation platforms) to deliver professional-grade work
- Generate 50+ qualified international leads per week using Apollo, LinkedIn, and cold email
- Run a discovery call, handle objections, present a proposal, and close a deal in USD/GBP/AUD
- Onboard a client professionally, deliver work to deadline, report results, and retain the relationship
- Earn a minimum of $500–$2,000/month from international freelance or remote work within 90 days of graduation

## 📐 Daily / Weekly / Monthly Training Structure

### Every Day:

- 1 lesson (video + written content): 45–60 minutes
- 1 practical task: 30–60 minutes
- Daily log submission (what I learned, what I did, what I struggled with)
- Mentor review flag if stuck

### Every Week:

- Weekly target review (did you hit your numbers?)
- 1 peer or group review session
- Weekly quiz (10 MCQs on the week's content)
- Task portfolio submission (all daily tasks compiled)

### Every Month:

- Module exam (30 MCQs + 1 practical submission)
- Mentor 1:1 review call
- Progress report card updated
- Promotion to next phase (conditional on passing score)
