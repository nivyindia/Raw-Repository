# NA-AC-05 | Success Buffer (Month 4–6)

## Theme: "Stay in the Game Until You Win"

---

## What is the Success Buffer?

The Success Buffer is an optional extension available to students who:

- Completed all 3 months of the Foundation Track
- Passed the Final Assessment
- Have not yet landed a paying client or job offer within 30 days of graduation

It is **not** a sign of failure. It is a structured support system to bridge the gap between learning and earning.

---

## Who Qualifies?

| Condition | Required? |
| --- | --- |
| Completed Foundation Track (all 3 months) | ✅ Yes |
| Submitted all monthly task sets | ✅ Yes |
| Passed Final Assessment (60%+) | ✅ Yes |
| No paying client within 30 days of graduation | ✅ Yes |
| Maintained daily logs (60%+ of program days) | ✅ Yes |

---

## What Happens in the Buffer Period?

### Month 4: Deep Diagnosis

- 1:1 mentor audit of everything the student has done
- Identify exact bottleneck: Is it the niche? The message? The platform? The offer?
- Rebuild the outreach system from scratch if needed
- Daily accountability check-ins (3x/week)
- Target: 200 outreach contacts during this month

### Month 5: Pivot & Push

- Try a different niche or service offering if Month 4 data suggests it
- Introduce warm outreach (referrals, communities, local businesses)
- Help student apply for entry-level digital marketing roles if freelancing isn't converting
- Guest session with a Nivy mentor who has experience in the student's target niche
- Target: First meeting or interview booked

### Month 6: Close or Place

- Full focus on closing any open conversations
- Portfolio and LinkedIn refresh
- Active job application support (resume, cover letter, interview prep)
- Introduction to Nivy's freelancer network for sub-contracting opportunities
- Target: First income earned (freelance OR employment)

---

## Buffer Period Rules

- Student must submit weekly activity reports
- Minimum 20 outreach contacts per day
- Must attend all scheduled mentor calls during this period
- Buffer cannot be extended beyond 3 months (Month 4–6 total)
