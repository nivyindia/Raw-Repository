## Rubric 1: Prospect List (NA-AC-02 Task)

| Criteria | 1 (Poor) | 2 (Below Avg) | 3 (Average) | 4 (Good) | 5 (Excellent) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quantity (50 leads submitted) | <20 | 20–30 | 31–40 | 41–49 | 50+ |
| Data completeness (all fields filled) | <40% | 40–60% | 61–75% | 76–90% | 91–100% |
| Niche relevance (leads match chosen niche) | <50% | 50–65% | 66–75% | 76–90% | 91–100% |
| Email validity (no obvious fake/broken emails) | <50% | 50–70% | 71–80% | 81–90% | 91–100% |

**Total: /20 — Pass: 12+**

## Rubric 2: Cold Email Templates (NA-AC-03 Task)

| Criteria | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subject line quality (curiosity, relevance) | Generic, no hook | Weak hook | Decent | Good hook | Compelling, niche-specific |
| Opening line (personalised, not generic) | "Dear Sir/Madam" | Slightly personal | Mentions company | Mentions specific pain | Highly personalised |
| Value proposition (clear benefit stated) | None | Vague | General benefit | Specific benefit | Specific + outcome-focused |
| CTA (clear next step) | No CTA | Weak CTA | Generic CTA | Clear CTA | Clear + low-friction CTA |
| Tone (professional, not salesy) | Pushy/unprofessional | Slightly salesy | Neutral | Professional | Confident + consultative |

**Total per email: /25 — 3 emails = /75 — Pass: 45+**
