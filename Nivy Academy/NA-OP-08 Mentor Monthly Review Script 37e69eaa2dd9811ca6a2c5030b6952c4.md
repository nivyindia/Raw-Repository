# NA-OP-08 | Mentor Monthly Review Script

**Doc ID:** NA-OP-08 · **Dept:** Operations & Legal · **Phase:** 1 · **Audience:** Mentors · **Status:** 🟢 Live

**Tags:** mentor, review, monthly call, 1:1, student check-in, coaching

---

## 🎯 Purpose

This script guides mentors through the monthly 1:1 review call with each student. The call should be **30 minutes max**. It is structured, not casual. It is a performance review, not a chat.

---

## 🗓️ Pre-Call Checklist (Mentor does this before the call)

- [ ]  Pull student’s CRM log for the month
- [ ]  Check task submission rate (how many of 4 weekly tasks submitted)
- [ ]  Note attendance %
- [ ]  Review last month’s call notes
- [ ]  Identify 1 strength and 1 area of concern to address

---

## 🎤 Call Script (30 minutes)

### Opening (2 min)

> "Hey [Name], thanks for joining. This is your Month [X] review. I want this to be honest and useful for you, so let’s dive in. How are you feeling about the program so far?"
> 

*[Let them speak for 60-90 seconds. Listen for energy level and confidence.]*

### Numbers Review (8 min)

> "Let’s look at the numbers together. This month you logged [X] outreach attempts. The minimum is 50 per month. You submitted [X] out of 4 tasks. Your attendance was [X]%. What do these numbers tell you?"
> 

*[Let them self-assess first before you comment. Then add your observation.]*

### Wins (5 min)

> "I want to highlight something you did well this month: [specific win]. That’s the standard we want consistently. Can you tell me what made that work?"
> 

### Challenge Area (7 min)

> "The one area I want us to focus on this month is [challenge]. I noticed [specific behaviour/gap]. What’s getting in the way?"
> 

*[Listen, then offer a specific action they can take this week to address it.]*

### Next Month Plan (5 min)

> "For next month, I want you to commit to 3 specific things. Based on what we discussed, what are they?"
> 

*[Student names them. Mentor confirms or adjusts. Both agree.]*

### Close (3 min)

> "Good. I’m going to log these in your CRM record now. We’ll check against them next month. Any questions for me before we close?"
> 

---

## 📝 Post-Call: Mentor Logs in CRM

- Date of call
- Student energy level: High / Medium / Low
- Key issue discussed
- 3 commitments made by student
- Mentor action required (if any)
- Flag: ✅ On Track / ⚠️ Watch / 🔴 At Risk
