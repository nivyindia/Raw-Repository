# NA-GR-02 | Referral Program — Rules & Discount Logic

---

## Program Name

**Nivy Academy "Pay It Forward" Referral Program**

---

## How It Works

1. Current student or alumnus refers a friend using a unique referral code (format: NA-REF-[StudentID])
2. Referred friend enrolls in any paid tier (Starter and above)
3. Both referrer and referee receive a discount/credit

---

## Reward Structure

<table header-row="true"><tr><td>Referrer Status</td><td>Reward to Referrer</td><td>Reward to New Student</td></tr><tr><td>Current Student</td><td>10% credit toward next tier upgrade or extension</td><td>10% off enrollment fee</td></tr><tr><td>Alumnus (within 12 months of graduation)</td><td>₹2,000 / $30 cash reward per successful enrollment</td><td>10% off enrollment fee</td></tr><tr><td>Alumnus (12+ months, active GFA member)</td><td>₹3,000 / $45 cash reward + priority GFA project access</td><td>15% off enrollment fee</td></tr></table>

---

## Conditions

- "Successful enrollment" = referred student completes payment for Starter tier or above
- Reward is paid/credited within 7 days of the referred student's payment confirmation
- Maximum of unlimited referrals per person, but cash rewards capped at ₹20,000/$300 per calendar year per referrer (escalations beyond this reviewed case-by-case)
- Discounts cannot be combined with other active promotions unless explicitly approved

---

## Tracking

- Referral codes and outcomes tracked in the Alumni Tracker (NA-GR-01) via the "Referral Count" field
- Enrollment form (NA-SM-04) must include a "Referred by" field to capture referral codes at signup

---

- Referral program details included in: Welcome Kit (NA-OP-05), Month 3 placement prep sessions, and post-graduation Experience Letter delivery email
