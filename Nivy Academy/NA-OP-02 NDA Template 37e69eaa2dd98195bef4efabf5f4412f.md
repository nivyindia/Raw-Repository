## 1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" includes but is not limited to:

- Client names, email addresses, phone numbers, and business details
- Outreach scripts, email templates, and SOP documents
- Lead lists, CRM data, and pipeline information
- Pricing, business strategies, and internal processes
- Any information labeled as "confidential" or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

The student agrees to:

- Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential
- Not share any client data with third parties under any circumstances
- Not use client contacts for personal business outside the Nivy ecosystem
- Not take screenshots, exports, or copies of CRM data for personal use
- Not approach Nivy clients directly for personal freelance work

## 3. DURATION

This NDA remains in effect for **2 years** from the date of signing and survives program completion or early exit.

## 4. CONSEQUENCES OF BREACH

- Immediate removal from the program with no refund eligibility
- Legal action under applicable Indian law
- Recovery of any damages caused to Nivy or its clients

## 5. EXCLUSIONS

This NDA does not apply to information that:

- Was already publicly known at the time of disclosure
- Was independently developed by the student without using Nivy’s information
