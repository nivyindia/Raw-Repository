# Why À La Carte Pricing Exists

With the Module Catalog (QW-01) now built — 76 modules across 23 phases — Phase 20 calls for each major topic to be sellable as a standalone certificate course. This lets Nivy Academy capture revenue from:

1. Students who only want one skill (e.g. "just SEO" or "just GA4")
2. Past students wanting to add a new specialisation later
3. Corporate/B2B buyers wanting a single module for a team
