# NA-AX-01 | Assessment Framework & Rules

**Doc ID:** NA-AX-01 · **Dept:** Assessment · **Phase:** 1 · **Audience:** Students, Mentors · **Status:** 🟢 Live

**Tags:** assessment, framework, grading rules, evaluation policy

---

## 🎯 Purpose

This document defines how every student is evaluated throughout the Nivy Academy program. All mentors, coordinators, and students must read this before the program begins.

---

## 📊 Assessment Weightage

| Component | Weight | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly Practical Tasks | 40% | Every week (12 tasks) |
| Module Quiz (MCQ) | 20% | End of each module |
| CRM Activity Log | 20% | Ongoing (daily) |
| Final Graduation Assessment | 20% | Week 12 |
| **Total** | **100%** | — |

---

## 🗓️ Grading Scale

| Score | Grade | Certificate Tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 90–100% | A+ | Gold Certificate + Top Performer Badge |
| 80–89% | A | Gold Certificate |
| 70–79% | B | Standard Certificate |
| 60–69% | C | Participation Certificate only |
| Below 60% | Fail | No certificate; buffer period offered |

**Minimum pass mark to receive placement assistance: 70% (Grade B or above)**

---

## ⏱️ Submission Rules

- All tasks must be submitted by **Sunday 11:59 PM** of the respective week
- Late submissions: -10% penalty per day
- Extensions: Allowed once per month with mentor approval (24-hour advance notice required)
- Plagiarism / copied work: Automatic zero + written warning

---

## 📝 Mentor Grading SOP

1. Receive submission via Notion / Google Form
2. Grade within **48 hours** of deadline
3. Add comments in the student’s CRM record
4. Update the Student Progress Dashboard (NA-SM-02)
5. Flag any student scoring below 60% to Academic Lead immediately
