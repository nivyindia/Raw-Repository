# NA-AX-01 | Assessment Framework

## Philosophy

Assessment at Nivy Academy is **performance-based**, not just knowledge-based. Students are graded on what they *do*, not just what they *know*.

---

## Assessment Types

| Type | Weight | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Module Tasks (practical submissions) | 40% | End of each module |
| Monthly Mentor Review | 20% | End of Month 1, 2, 3 |
| Final Graduation Assessment | 30% | End of Month 3 |
| Professionalism Score | 10% | Ongoing (daily logs, responsiveness, discipline) |

---

## Grading Scale

| Score | Grade | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 90–100% | A+ — Distinction | Certificate + Experience Letter + Priority Placement |
| 75–89% | A — Merit | Certificate + Placement Assistance |
| 60–74% | B — Pass | Certificate Issued |
| 45–59% | C — Conditional Pass | 30-day extension given, re-assessment required |
| Below 45% | Fail | No certificate. Option to re-enroll at discounted rate. |

---

## Pass Criteria (Minimum to Graduate)

1. Complete and submit all 3 monthly task sets
2. Attend minimum 2 of 3 monthly mentor calls
3. Score 60%+ overall
4. Maintain daily log for at least 60% of program days
5. Complete the 7-Day Trial Task before formal enrollment

---

## Assessment Integrity Rules

- All work must be original
- AI tools are allowed but must be disclosed
- Plagiarism results in immediate disqualification
- Late submissions accepted within 5 days; penalised 10% per day
- Students may appeal a grade within 7 days of receiving it
