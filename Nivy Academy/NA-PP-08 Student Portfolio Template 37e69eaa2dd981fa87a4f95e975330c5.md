# NA-PP-08 | Student Portfolio Template

---

## Purpose

A standardized portfolio format every graduating student fills out to showcase their work for job applications, freelance profiles (Upwork, LinkedIn), and GFA applications.

---

## Portfolio Sections

### 1. Profile Summary

- Name, photo, location, languages spoken
- One-line positioning statement (e.g., "Digital Marketing Specialist focused on B2B lead generation and outreach")
- Tier completed + certification badge

---

### 2. Skills Snapshot

Table of tools and proficiency level (Beginner / Working / Proficient):

[Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io), LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Email outreach platforms, CRM tools, Canva/design basics, ChatGPT/Claude for content & research, Basic ad platforms (Meta/LinkedIn Ads)

---

### 3. Practical Project Highlights

For each major Practical Task completed during the program:

- Project name/description
- Tools and approach used
- Outcome/result (e.g., "Generated 45 qualified leads in 2 weeks via cold email sequence")
- Mentor rating/feedback quote (if positive)

---

### 4. Sample Work Attachments

- Sample outreach message or email sequence written
- Sample lead list structure (anonymized)
- Sample ad copy or landing page section written

---

### 5. Certifications & Letters

- Apprenticeship Certificate (NA-PP-06)
- Experience Letter (NA-PP-07)
- Any module-level badges earned

---

### 6. Contact & Availability

- Email, phone, LinkedIn
- Availability (full-time / freelance / hours per week)
- Rate expectations (if freelancing via GFA)

---

## Usage Guidance

Mentors should review portfolios during Month 3 (Live Projects & Placement Prep, see NA-AC-04) and help students select their strongest 2-3 project highlights rather than listing everything.
