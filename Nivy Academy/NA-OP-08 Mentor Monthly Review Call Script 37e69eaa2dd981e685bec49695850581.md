# NA-OP-08 | Mentor Monthly Review Call Script

*Use this guide for the monthly 1:1 mentor review call with each student. Duration: 45–60 minutes.*

---

## Before the Call

- Review the student's daily logs for the month
- Note any missed log days or submissions
- Review their task submissions and scores
- Check their outreach numbers (contacts, replies, meetings)
- Come with 2–3 specific observations (positive and constructive)

---

## Call Structure

### Opening (5 min)

*"Hey [Name], great to connect. Before I share my observations, I want to hear from you first. How do you feel this month went?"*

Let them speak first. Don’t interrupt. Note their self-awareness level.

---

### Module Review (10 min)

- Ask: *"Walk me through the tasks you completed this month. What was the hardest one?"*
- Discuss any submissions that were below standard
- Celebrate what they did well (specifically — not generic praise)

---

### Outreach Numbers Review (10 min)

- Ask the student to share their CRM / outreach tracker
- Review: Total contacts | Reply rate | Positive replies | Meetings booked
- Ask: *"What message got the best response? Show me."*
- Identify patterns: What's working? What isn’t?
- Set outreach targets for next month

---

### Mindset & Blockers Check (10 min)

- Ask: *"What’s been the biggest challenge this month — practical or mental?"*
- Listen for signs of: discouragement, distraction, imposter syndrome, life circumstances
- Address blockers directly. Don’t let them fester.
- Ask: *"If you could change one thing about how you worked this month, what would it be?"*

---

### Goal Setting for Next Month (10 min)

Set 3 concrete targets together:

1. Outreach target (number of contacts)
2. Skill to develop (e.g. "write 3 better proposals")
3. Habit to build (e.g. "fill in daily log every day without fail")

Write these down and confirm the student has noted them.

---

### Closing (5 min)

*"You’re doing [honest assessment]. The one thing I want you to focus on this month is [single priority]. You’ve got this. Any final questions for me?"*

---

## Post-Call

- Update the student's record in NA-SM-02 (Progress Dashboard)
- Send a follow-up WhatsApp message within 2 hours:
    
    > *"Great call today [Name]. Your targets for [Month]: 1) [Target 1] 2) [Target 2] 3) [Target 3]. I’m here if you need anything. Let’s make next month count."*
    > 
- Log any concerns in the student's mentor notes
