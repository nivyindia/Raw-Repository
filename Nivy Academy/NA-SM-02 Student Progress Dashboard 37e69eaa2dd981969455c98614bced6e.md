## Red Flag Indicators

Flag a student for urgent mentor intervention if:

- Daily log compliance drops below 50% in any 2-week period
- No contacts reached for 5+ consecutive days
- Mentor call missed without prior notice
- Task submission more than 7 days overdue
- Student expresses desire to quit
