# NA-MK-01 | 5-Tier Pricing Strategy

---

## Pricing Philosophy

Nivy Academy uses a **decoy pricing model** with 5 tiers. The middle tiers are designed to be the most attractive, and the top tier creates aspiration. Every tier should feel like a deal compared to the one above it.

---

## The 5 Tiers

| Tier | Name | Price (India) | Price (International) | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Trailblazer | ₹9,999 | $149 | Students, freshers with very limited budget |
| 2 | Starter | ₹19,999 | $299 | Job seekers, recent graduates |
| 3 | Practitioner ⭐ | ₹34,999 | $499 | Primary target — working professionals |
| 4 | Accelerator | ₹54,999 | $799 | Experienced freelancers, serious career switchers |
| 5 | Elite | ₹89,999 | $1,299 | Entrepreneurs, agency founders, top performers |

*⭐ Practitioner is the anchor/decoy tier — most students should land here.*

---

## What Each Tier Includes

### Tier 1 — Trailblazer

- Access to recorded curriculum (self-paced)
- Community WhatsApp group
- 1 mentor check-in call (Month 3 only)
- Certificate of Completion (if tasks submitted)
- No placement assistance

### Tier 2 — Starter

- All of Tier 1
- Monthly group mentor call (not 1:1)
- Access to SOP Library
- Basic placement assistance (job board access)
- Certificate of Completion

### Tier 3 — Practitioner ⭐

- All of Tier 2
- 3 x monthly 1:1 mentor calls
- Live feedback on outreach messages
- Full placement assistance (referrals + network)
- Performance Guarantee (3-month extension if criteria met)
- Apprenticeship Certificate

### Tier 4 — Accelerator

- All of Tier 3
- Acceleration Track access (skip basics, go advanced)
- Priority placement (top of referral queue)
- Experience Letter from Nivy
- Access to Nivy Freelancer Alliance as a delivery partner
- Personal brand audit session

### Tier 5 — Elite

- All of Tier 4
- Weekly 1:1 mentor calls (not monthly)
- Direct introduction to 3 potential clients/employers by Abhi personally
- Co-branded case study after first client win
- Invitation to Nivy Advisory Board (after 6 months)
- Lifetime access to all future curriculum updates

## Upgrade Path

Students can upgrade tiers at any point during the program by paying the difference. This should be mentioned in Month 1 mentor calls for Tier 1/2 students showing strong performance.
