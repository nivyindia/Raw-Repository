# 🎯 Phase 20–23 — Modular Course Architecture, Granular Assessment & Scorecard System

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Created:** June 15, 2026 | **Owner:** Abhishek Dayal
> 

> **Trigger for this page:** Two structural requests — (1) package major topics (SEO, Web Development, Virtual Assistance, etc.) as **standalone, sellable, teachable courses/modules** without undoing the recent unification into one 26-week Programme, and (2) move assessment from "end of phase only" to **a checkpoint after every small sub-topic** (mini-quiz + hands-on task + SOP check + accuracy score), **plus a graded Practical Exam after every major topic**, all rolling up into **per-task, per-module, and overall scorecards**.
> 

---

# 📋 Overview — The Core Idea: One Content Library, Two Wrappers

This plan does **not** re-fragment the unified 26-week International Programme (ISP-01–ISP-06, the result of Phase D unification). Instead, it adds a **packaging and assessment layer on top of the same lesson library**:

| Wrapper | What it is | Who it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Programme (existing)** | The full 26-week ISC journey, phases delivered in sequence | Standard students, Accelerated Entry Path |
| **Standalone Modules (new)** | The same lessons, repackaged as 1–8 week mini-courses with their own certificate, price, and entry point | Working professionals who want *one* skill, corporate clients (Phase 15), upsell/cross-sell to alumni |

Nothing is rewritten twice — a module "borrows" its lessons from the Programme's lesson library and adds its own checkpoints, practical exam, certificate, and price tag.

# 🎯 PHASE 21 — Granular Assessment Architecture (Checkpoints + Practical Exams)

> **Why this matters:** Currently assessment happens mostly at the *end* of a 4–6 week phase (Phase Practical + Phase Exam). By the time a student is tested on On-Page SEO, they may have moved on to Off-Page SEO weeks ago and forgotten the details. This phase adds a lightweight checkpoint **after every small sub-topic** and a fuller, marked **Practical Exam after every major topic** — which also gives each standalone module (Phase 20) its own clean assessment story.
> 

## 📌 P21.1 — Sub-Topic Checkpoint Framework (New Standard)

| # | Task | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P21.1.1 | **Define the "Sub-Topic Checkpoint" template** — a standard 4-part block run immediately after each small sub-topic (e.g. On-Page SEO, Off-Page SEO, Schema Markup): (1) **Mini-Quiz** — 5 MCQs, pass = 4/5; (2) **Hands-On Task** — mentor-graded against a rubric, scored 0–10; (3) **SOP Compliance Checklist** — yes/no items confirming the task followed the documented SOP; (4) **Accuracy Score** — 0–100%, comparing the student's output against a mentor-verified answer key | 🔴 Pending |
| P21.1.2 | **Apply Sub-Topic Checkpoints to every Phase 1 depth module** — each of the 60 P1.x tasks (On-Page SEO, Off-Page SEO, Technical SEO, Core Web Vitals, Schema Markup, etc.) gets its own checkpoint, built alongside the module content itself | 🔴 Pending |

# 🧑‍🏫 PHASE 23 — Grading Operations & Mentor Capacity (Supports Phases 21–22 at Scale)

> **Why this matters:** Adding a checkpoint after every small sub-topic + a marked Practical Exam after every major topic multiplies the number of gradable submissions several-fold. Without an operations plan, this either overloads mentors or causes grading delays that defeat the purpose (immediate reinforcement). This phase is the "this can also be improved" addition — it makes Phases 21–22 operationally sustainable.
>
