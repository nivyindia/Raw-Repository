## 🎯 Purpose

The Final Graduation Assessment is a 2-part evaluation conducted at the end of Month 3 (Week 12). Passing this exam is required to receive the official **"Global Digital Marketing Consultant"** certificate and placement assistance.

---

## 🗓️ Assessment Structure

### Part 1 — Written Exam (MCQ + Short Answer)

- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Format:** 30 MCQ (from quiz banks) + 5 short answer questions
- **Pass mark:** 70%
- **Topics covered:** All 3 months — market knowledge, outreach, tools, closing, client management

**Sample Short Answer Questions:**

1. A UK client emails saying they’re unhappy with last month’s results. Write a professional response. (100 words max)
2. You have 50 leads in Apollo. Describe your 5-step process to convert them into qualified meetings. 
3. A client asks for a service outside your original scope. How do you handle this? What do you say?
4. What is a retainer, why is it better than a project, and how would you pitch one to an existing client?
5. Describe the "Reverse Psychology" sales approach in your own words and give one real example.

---

### Part 2 — Practical Capstone Project

- **Duration:** 1 week (submitted by end of Week 12)
- **Task:** Choose one real company. Complete the following:
    - Full website + social media audit
    - Identify top 3 pain points with evidence
    - Write a personalised cold email pitch
    - Build a 20-lead list for that company’s ICP
    - Record a 3-minute mock pitch video
- **Submitted via:** Notion form / Google Drive link
- **Evaluated using:** NA-AX-03 rubrics (quality, completeness, professionalism, CRM log)

---

## 🏆 Graduation Criteria

| Requirement | Minimum Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Part 1 Written Exam | 70% or above |
| Part 2 Capstone Project | Score 14/20 or above |
| CRM Activity | Minimum 150 logged interactions |
| Portfolio | Minimum 2 case studies submitted |
| Attendance | Minimum 80% of live sessions |

**All 5 must be met to receive Gold or Standard Certificate.**
