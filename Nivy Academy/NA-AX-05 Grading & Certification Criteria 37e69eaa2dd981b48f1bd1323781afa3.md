## 🏅 Certificate Tiers

| Grade | Score | Certificate | Benefits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A+** | 90–100% | 🥇 Gold + Top Performer | Priority placement + Nivy hire consideration + LinkedIn recommendation |
| **A** | 80–89% | 🥇 Gold | Placement assistance + Experience Letter |
| **B** | 70–79% | ⭐ Standard | Placement assistance + Experience Letter |
| **C** | 60–69% | Participation | Participation certificate only (no placement) |
| **Fail** | Below 60% | None | Buffer period offered; re-assessment in Month 5 |

---

## 📊 How Final Score is Calculated

| Component | Weight | Max Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly Practical Tasks (12 tasks) | 40% | 40 |
| Module Quizzes (3 quizzes) | 20% | 20 |
| CRM Activity Log (daily) | 20% | 20 |
| Final Graduation Assessment | 20% | 20 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **100** |

---

## 🎖️ What Each Certificate Includes

### Gold Certificate (A / A+)

- Full name + program title
- Program duration (3 months)
- Grade + percentage achieved
- "Global Digital Marketing Consultant" designation
- Nivy Academy stamp + Abhi’s signature
- QR code for online verification
- For A+: "Top Performer" seal

### Standard Certificate (B)

- Full name + program title
- Program duration
- "Digital Marketing Professional" designation
- Nivy Academy stamp + signature
- QR code for verification

### Participation Certificate (C)

- Name + program name
- "Participated in Nivy Academy 3-Month Program"
- No designation, no verification QR

---

## 🔄 Re-Assessment Policy

- Students who score below 70% may retake the Final Assessment once during the Success Buffer period
- Re-assessment fee: ₹500 (admin charge)
- Maximum re-assessment: 1 time only
- Re-assessment result replaces original Final Assessment score only (not overall score)
