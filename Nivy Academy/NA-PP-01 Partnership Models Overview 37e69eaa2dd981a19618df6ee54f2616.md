# NA-PP-01 | Partnership Models Overview

---

## Purpose

This document defines the 5 partnership models through which Nivy Academy creates placement and revenue pathways for students and the academy.

---

## The 5 Partnership Models

### Model 1 — Institute / College Tie-Ups

Nivy Academy partners with colleges and training institutes to offer this program as an add-on certification course or placement-linked elective. Revenue is shared per enrolled student, and Nivy provides curriculum, mentors, and certification.

### Model 2 — Global Freelancer Alliance

Top-performing graduates are onboarded as delivery partners for Nivy's own client projects (digital marketing, lead gen, VA work). They earn project-based income while building a verified track record.

### Model 3 — Agency Placement Network

Nivy maintains relationships with digital marketing agencies and outsourcing firms that hire junior digital marketers and VAs. Graduates are referred directly into open roles via warm introductions.

### Model 4 — Direct Employer Placement

For Elite and Accelerator tier students, Abhi personally introduces graduates to 2-3 potential employers or clients based on their specialization (lead gen, paid ads, content, etc.).

### Model 5 — Alumni Referral & Internal Hiring

Graduates who perform exceptionally can be absorbed into Nivy's own operations (BDU, sub-brands) as freelancers or full-time staff, creating an internal talent pipeline.
