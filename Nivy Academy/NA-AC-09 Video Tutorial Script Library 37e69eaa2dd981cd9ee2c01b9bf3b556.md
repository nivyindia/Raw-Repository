# NA-AC-09 | Video Tutorial Script Library

*This library contains script outlines for each module's video tutorial. Use these as the basis for recording Loom or studio videos to accompany the curriculum.*

---

## Module 1 — Global Market Fundamentals

**Video 1.1: Why International Clients?**

- Hook: "An Indian freelancer charging $500/month to a US client is earning what most Indian salaries can't match."
- Explain currency arbitrage opportunity
- Show 3 real examples of Indian freelancers working with global clients
- CTA: "In the next video, I'll show you exactly where to find these clients."

**Video 1.2: The Top 5 Niches**

- Walk through: E-commerce, SaaS, Coaches, Real Estate, Healthcare
- For each: what they need, what they pay, how to find them
- CTA: "Pick one niche before our next session."

## Module 2 — Tool Stack Training

**Video 2.1: [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) Walkthrough (Step-by-Step)**

- Screen recording: Create account → Search leads → Filter by niche/location/title → Export list
- Show a real search for "marketing director" in "United States" in "e-commerce"
- CTA: "Build your first 50-lead list using this exact process."

**Video 2.2: ChatGPT for Outreach**

- Screen recording: Open ChatGPT → Use the cold email prompt → Personalise the output
- Show before/after: generic email vs AI-personalised email
- CTA: "Use this prompt to write your first 10 outreach emails."

## Module 3 — The Consultant Mindset

**Video 3.1: Stop Sounding Like a Beginner**

- Read two versions of the same pitch — one weak, one strong
- Break down exactly what changed and why
- CTA: "Record your own intro video and compare."
