## SOP 01: [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) — Building a Lead List

**Objective:** Generate a list of 50 qualified leads in a target niche.

**Steps:**

1. Go to [apollo.io](http://apollo.io) and create a free account
2. Click **"Search"** in the left menu → select **"People"**
3. In filters, set:
    - **Job Title:** e.g. "Marketing Director", "Founder", "CEO"
    - **Location:** e.g. United States, United Kingdom, Australia
    - **Industry:** e.g. E-commerce, Software, Health & Wellness
    - **Employee Count:** 1–50 (best for freelancers to target small-mid companies)
4. Review the results — scan for relevance
5. Select leads by checking the boxes
6. Click **"Export"** → export to CSV
7. Open in Google Sheets, clean the data (remove blanks, duplicates)
8. Add columns: "Outreach Status", "Date Contacted", "Reply", "Notes"

**Quality Check:** At least 80% of leads should have verified emails before you send.

## SOP 02: LinkedIn Outreach — Connect & Message

**Objective:** Send connection requests + follow-up messages to warm up prospects.

**Steps:**

1. Search for your target ICP on LinkedIn using filters (Title + Location + Industry)
2. Visit each profile before sending a request — look for a personalisation hook (recent post, shared connection, company news)
3. Send a connection request **without a note** (higher acceptance rate)
4. Once accepted (usually 24–48 hours), send Message 1:
    
    > *"Hi [First Name], thanks for connecting! I noticed you're growing [Company] in [space]. I work with [niche] companies on [specific result]. Happy to share what's been working if it's relevant.”*
    > 
5. If no reply in 5 days, send Message 2 (follow-up):
    
    > *"Hey [First Name], just following up on my last message. Even a yes or no helps me know if this is relevant for you. No hard feelings either way!"*
    > 
6. Log every interaction in your CRM (Notion)

## SOP 03: CRM Management in Notion

**Objective:** Keep every prospect and client organised and followed-up consistently.

**Steps:**

1. Open your Student CRM page in Notion (use template NA-SM-02)
2. For every new lead, create a new row with:
    - Name, Title, Company, Email, LinkedIn URL
    - **Stage:** Prospect
    - **Date Added:** today's date
3. After every touchpoint, update:
    - **Last Contact Date**
    - **Stage** (update as they move through the pipeline)
    - **Notes** (what did they say? what was their objection?)
    - **Next Action + Next Action Date**
4. Every Monday morning: filter for all leads where Next Action Date = this week. Work through them.
5. Every Friday: review your pipeline. How many are in each stage? What's your conversion rate from Contacted → Replied?

## SOP 04: ChatGPT for Outreach

**Objective:** Generate personalised, high-quality outreach messages in minutes.

**Steps:**

1. Open ChatGPT ([chat.openai.com](http://chat.openai.com)) or Claude ([claude.ai](http://claude.ai))
2. Use this prompt template:
    
    > *"Write a cold email to [Name], [Title] at [Company]. They are a [industry] company based in [country]. I offer [your service]. The email should be under 120 words, have a personalised opening based on [specific detail about them], mention a specific result I can deliver, and end with a soft CTA to schedule a 15-minute call. Tone: professional but warm."*
    > 
3. Review the output — never send AI output without reading it first
4. Edit for authenticity: add your name, make it sound like you
5. Run it through Grammarly before sending

**Rule:** Every AI-generated email must be reviewed and personalised by you before sending. Never send raw AI output.
