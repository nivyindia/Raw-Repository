# 🧭 Course Improvement Plan — Unify Foundation Track + ISP into ONE International Programme

## 1. Why This Plan Exists

Nivy Academy currently runs **two parallel curricula** that cover overlapping ground:

- **Foundation Track** (NA-AC-02/03/04) — 12 weeks, "Apprenticeship Certificate"
- **International Skills Programme / ISP** (ISP-01–06) — 24 weeks, "International Skills Certificate (ISC)"

Both teach digital marketing, lead generation, outreach, negotiation/closing, and AI/VA skills — but at different depths, in different sequences, with different SOPs, and leading to two different certificates. This creates confusion for students, mentors, and partners about "which course is the real one."

**Goal of this plan:** Take the **best content, SOPs, and frameworks from both**, and merge them into **one single, internationally benchmarked programme** with **one architecture, one assessment system, and one certificate**.

| Gaps | No standalone DM mastery phase (SEO/PPC/Analytics absent); no contracts/invoicing depth; no formal brand-audit/KPI framework | Lead-gen/outreach (Phase 4) and AI/VA (Phase 3) lack the tactical SOP depth Foundation Track has (e.g. exact Apollo list-building steps, Outreach Sprint counting methodology) |
| Add-ons | NA-AC-07 (VA+AI), NA-AC-08 (Corporate Discipline — daily log, CRM, Slack, LinkedIn, deadline standards), NA-AC-06 (Acceleration), NA-AC-05 (Success Buffer) | None — self-contained |

| International benchmarking | Not explicitly benchmarked | Benchmarked against Google/HubSpot/Meta certs, Upwork/Fiverr/LinkedIn standards |

**Conclusion:** ISP's **6-phase, 24-week architecture** is the stronger international-grade backbone — broader scope, benchmarked, and already the audit's "deployment standard." But Foundation Track's **SOPs, tactical scripts, and Corporate Discipline standards** are more execution-ready and should be folded into the ISP backbone rather than discarded.

## 3. Proposed Unified Course Architecture

### New Single Programme: **"Nivy Global Professional Programme" (working title — keep or revise)**

- **One track. One 24-week journey. One certificate.**
- Built on the **ISP 6-phase architecture** as the skeleton.
- Each phase enriched with the best matching content from Foundation Track.

## 5. Unified Assessment & Certification Framework

**One certificate:** All graduates receive the **Nivy Academy International Skills Certificate (ISC)**.

| Assessment Type | Frequency | Pass Mark | Weight | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily Task + Daily Log | Daily | Submitted = Pass | Habit score (gate to next week) | NA-AC-08 standards layer, applied programme-wide |
| Weekly Quiz (10 MCQs) | Weekly | 7/10 | 20% | ISP system, already present in all 26 weeks (Foundation weeks gained quizzes in Session 3) |
| Module/Phase Practical | Per Phase | Mentor-approved against rubric | 40% | Foundation Track rubrics (Session 3) + ISP practical specs, merged per phase |
| Phase Exam (30 MCQs) | End of each phase | 21/30 (70%) | 40% | ISP system |
| Final Graduation Assessment (Capstone + Panel) | End of Programme (Week 26) | 80% overall | Certification gate | ISP-06 capstone, enriched with Foundation Track's portfolio + discovery-call deliverables |

### Interim Milestone (replaces the old separate "Apprenticeship Certificate")

At the end of **Phase 1 (Week 4)**, students who pass receive a **"Professional Foundations Badge"** — an in-programme milestone recognising job/freelance readiness, *not* a separate qualification. This preserves the motivational value of an early credential without maintaining two certificate brands.

> ⚠️ **Decision needed from leadership:** Confirm whether existing Apprenticeship Certificate holders are "grandfathered" as equivalent to the new Foundations Badge, or whether a bridging assessment is required. This affects current/past cohorts and should be resolved before Phase 1 of execution begins (see Section 6).

## 7. Open Risks / Decisions Needing Owner Input

- **Brand decision (A2):** Renaming the programme/certificate has marketing and partner-facing implications (existing partners may already reference "ISC" or "Apprenticeship Certificate").
- **Grandfathering (A3):** Past graduates' certificates must remain valid — the plan should not retroactively devalue completed credentials.
- **Timeline impact:** New total duration is 26 weeks vs the old 12-week Foundation Track. Marketing materials, pricing, and student expectations for the "fast" track need to reflect that the only fast path is now the Accelerated Entry diagnostic, not a separate shorter programme.
- **NA-AC-09 dual-tree (A5/E2):** Already flagged in the audit as needing human confirmation — should be resolved early so video planning isn't done twice.
