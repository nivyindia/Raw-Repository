**STUDENT ENROLLMENT AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is entered into between **Nivy Academy** (hereinafter “the Academy”) and the undersigned student (hereinafter “the Student”) on the date of enrollment.

---

## 1. Program Enrollment

The Student agrees to enroll in the **Nivy Academy Digital Marketing & Business Development Program** for a duration of **3 months (Foundation Track)** or as applicable based on the chosen track.

## 2. Fees

The Student agrees to pay the program fee as per the selected pricing tier. Fees must be paid in full or as per the agreed installment plan before access to program materials is granted.

## 3. Student Obligations

The Student agrees to:

- Attend all scheduled mentor calls (minimum 2 of 3 monthly)
- Submit all module tasks within the stipulated deadlines
- Maintain daily activity logs as required
- Behave professionally with mentors, staff, and fellow students
- Not share program materials with third parties

## 4. Academy Obligations

Nivy Academy agrees to:

- Provide access to all curriculum, tools guidance, and mentor sessions as described
- Conduct monthly review calls with the Student
- Issue a certificate upon successful completion and passing of assessments
- Provide placement assistance to students who meet graduation criteria

## 5. Placement Assistance Clause

“Placement Assistance” means the Academy will share job referrals, freelance opportunities, and network access with the Student. It does **not** guarantee employment or a specific income level.

## 6. Refund Policy

Refunds are governed by the separate **Refund & Guarantee Policy (NA-OP-03)**. No refunds are issued after Day 14 of enrollment.

## 7. Confidentiality

The Student agrees to maintain confidentiality of all proprietary Academy materials, client information encountered during training, and internal processes.

## 8. Termination

The Academy reserves the right to terminate a Student’s enrollment without refund if the Student:

- Behaves unprofessionally or disrespectfully
- Shares proprietary materials externally
- Fails to meet minimum engagement requirements for 2 consecutive weeks without notice

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.
