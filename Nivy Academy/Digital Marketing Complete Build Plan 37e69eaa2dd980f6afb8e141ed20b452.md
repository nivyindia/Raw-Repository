## 🧠 The Logic: Department-Based Architecture

Just like an international company has **departments** (HR, Product, Sales, Operations, etc.), Nivy Academy will have **6 Departments** inside Notion. Every page lives in a department. Everything is tagged with metadata. A **Master Index** sits at the top so anyone — you, a mentor, a student coordinator — can find anything in 2 clicks.
