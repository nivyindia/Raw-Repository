# NA-PP-09 | Mock Interview Question Bank (Role-wise)

---

## Purpose

Used by mentors during Month 3 placement prep to run mock interviews tailored to each student's target role.

---

## A. Digital Marketing Executive Role

1. Walk me through how you would research and build a target audience for a new product launch.
2. What's the difference between a cold email and a warm follow-up, and how do you adjust tone between the two?
3. How do you measure whether an outreach campaign is working?
4. Describe a time you had to follow up with a prospect multiple times without sounding pushy.
5. How would you prioritize leads if you had 100 contacts and only 2 hours a day for outreach?

---

## B. Virtual Assistant (VA) Role

1. What tools have you used for scheduling, CRM management, and reporting?
2. How do you handle a situation where you're given unclear instructions from a client?
3. Describe your daily workflow for managing multiple ongoing tasks for different clients.
4. How do you maintain confidentiality when handling client data?
5. Give an example of how you've used AI tools (ChatGPT/Claude) to speed up a task.

---

## C. Lead Generation Specialist Role

1. How do you build and qualify a lead list using LinkedIn Sales Navigator or Apollo?
2. What's your process for personalizing outreach at scale?
3. How do you handle a high rate of non-responses in an outreach campaign?
4. Describe a negotiation scenario where the prospect pushed back on price.
5. How do you decide when to hand off a lead to a closer/account manager?

---

## D. General / Behavioral Questions (All Roles)

1. Tell me about a project from your training where you didn't get the result you expected. What did you do?
2. How do you stay organized when juggling multiple deadlines?
3. Why do you want to work in [digital marketing / virtual assistance / lead generation]?
4. How do you handle feedback that feels overly critical?
5. Where do you see yourself in this field in 2 years?

---

## Mentor Notes

- Run at least 2 mock sessions per student before placement: one role-specific, one behavioral
- Score using the same rubric style as Practical Task Rubrics (NA-AX-03) — clarity, confidence, structure, specificity
