# NA-SM-05 | Professionalism Interview Script

*A structured 5-question filter interview for mentors/coordinators to conduct after receiving the application form. Duration: 15–20 minutes via WhatsApp call or Google Meet.*

---

## Before the Call

- Review the applicant’s form responses
- Note any red flags (vague answers, low commitment hours, “not sure” on commitment)
- Have the scoring sheet open

---

## Opening (2 min)

*"Hi [Name], thanks for applying to Nivy Academy. I’m [Your Name], and I’ll be conducting a quick 15-minute screening call today. This isn’t a test — it’s just a conversation to see if we’re a good fit for each other. Sound good? Great, let’s start."*

---

## The 5 Questions

### Q1: Tell me about yourself — who are you and what do you do right now?

**What to listen for:** Clarity, confidence, self-awareness. Are they a good communicator?

**Green flags:** Clear intro, honest about current situation, shows ambition

**Red flags:** Rambling, defensive, no direction

---

### Q2: Why digital marketing specifically? Why not something else?

**What to listen for:** Genuine motivation vs "I just want to make money online"

**Green flags:** Has done some research, mentions specific interest in a niche or skill

**Red flags:** "I heard it’s easy money" or extremely vague

---

### Q3: Walk me through your week. How many hours realistically can you give this program?

**What to listen for:** Honest self-assessment, realistic time management

**Green flags:** Has thought about this, has a plan

**Red flags:** "I’ll figure it out" or overpromising ("I can do 8 hours/day" when they’re working full-time)

---

### Q4: Have you ever started something and not finished it? What happened?

**What to listen for:** Self-awareness, accountability, growth mindset

**Green flags:** Honest answer + lesson learned

**Red flags:** Blames external factors entirely, no reflection

---

### Q5: If you don’t get results in Month 1, what will you do?

**What to listen for:** Resilience, problem-solving mindset

**Green flags:** "I’ll ask my mentor, analyse what went wrong, adjust my approach"

**Red flags:** "I’ll ask for a refund" or silence

---

## Closing (2 min)

*"Thank you so much for your time today. Based on our conversation and your application, we’ll send you a decision within 24–48 hours. If you’re selected, you’ll receive the Trial Task details. Any questions for me?"*
