# 🎯 Cohort Selection Criteria

The pilot needs students who will give honest, useful feedback on the new Checkpoint/Practical Exam/Scorecard system — not just students who are easiest to reach. Target profile:

| Criterion | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- |
| Currently enrolled in Expert or Professional tier | Per pilot protocol — these tiers include P1.1 (SEO Mastery) in their curriculum already, so no extra enrolment admin needed |
| Has NOT yet started or completed P1.1 (SEO Mastery) | Need students encountering this content for the first time — the checkpoints/exam must be tested on first-pass learners |
| Mix of engagement levels (not just top performers) | If only the most engaged students are picked, completion-rate metrics (≥90% checkpoint completion target) will be artificially inflated and won't reflect real Wave 1 rollout |
| Available for live sessions in July 2026 (Wave 1, W1–W4) | Pilot delivery format is live + async; students on extended breaks during July should be excluded |
| Willing to give structured feedback (NPS survey + faculty feedback form) | The pilot's entire purpose is feedback — cohort members are effectively beta testers and should be briefed as such |

**Recommended size:** 8 students (within the 5–10 range from the pilot protocol) — large enough that a couple of drop-offs won't invalidate the completion-rate metrics, small enough for SEO Faculty to give individual attention during this first run.
