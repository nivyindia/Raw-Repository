# Phase 7 — Extended Support

## Theme: "Stay in the Game Until You Win"

> **What this is:** An optional, structured 12-week post-graduation support period for ISP graduates who have completed the full programme and passed the Graduation Assessment but have not yet earned their first international income within 30 days of receiving their ISC certificate. This is not a sign of failure — it is a safety net designed into the programme architecture.
> 

---

## Who Is Eligible?

| Condition | Required |
| --- | --- |
| Completed ISP Phases 1–6 in full (Weeks 1–26) | ✅ Yes |
| Passed the Final Graduation Assessment (80%+ or mentor-panel approved) | ✅ Yes |
| ISC certificate issued | ✅ Yes |
| No confirmed paying client or employed income within 30 days of graduation | ✅ Yes |
| Daily Log maintained at 70%+ across the programme | ✅ Yes |
| All Capstone deliverables submitted (no outstanding items) | ✅ Yes |

> **Note:** Students who did not maintain the Daily Log standard (below 70% completion) are not eligible for Phase 7 support until they complete a 7-day restart cycle (7 consecutive Daily Logs submitted on time). This reinforces that the buffer is for students who did the work — not a second chance for students who disengaged.
>

## Phase 7 Activation — How to Enter

**Step 1:** Contact your assigned mentor within 30 days of receiving your ISC certificate. State that you are requesting Phase 7 activation.

**Step 2:** Complete the **Buffer Activation Form** (submitted to mentor):

- What outreach activity did you complete during and after graduation (specific numbers)?
- How many discovery calls did you book from ISP-04 and post-graduation outreach?
- What was the outcome of each call?
- What do you believe the current blocker is (niche, messaging, offer, platform, personal confidence)?
- What is your income target and minimum acceptable first engagement?

**Step 3:** Mentor reviews the form and either activates Phase 7 or schedules a diagnostic call first (if the form reveals an unclear picture).

**Step 4:** Phase 7 begins with a 1:1 Diagnostic Session (see Month 1 below).

## Phase 7 Structure — 3 Months (12 Weeks)

### Month 1 — Diagnose & Rebuild

**Focus:** Find the exact bottleneck and fix it.

**What happens:**

- 1:1 mentor audit of all outreach activity since Phase 4: Apollo list quality, cold email open/reply rates, LinkedIn DM conversion, Upwork proposals submitted
- Mentor scores the following areas 1–5: niche clarity, ICP definition, messaging quality, offer packaging, sales call performance, LinkedIn profile strength
- Lowest-scoring 2 areas become the Month 1 rebuild focus
- Daily accountability check-ins: 3x per week (Monday / Wednesday / Friday) — 15-minute check-in call or async Loom
- Outreach target: 200 unique contacts this month (20 per working day)
- Weekly activity report submitted every Sunday by 6:00 PM

**Month 1 Exit Criteria (required to continue to Month 2):**

- 200 contacts reached (evidenced by CRM export or outreach tool screenshot)
- At least 1 discovery call booked (from Month 1 outreach)
- Rebuilt LinkedIn profile meeting the ISP Graduate LinkedIn Checklist from Lesson 26.2
- All 4 weekly activity reports submitted on time

---

### Month 2 — Pivot & Push

**Focus:** If Month 1 data shows the niche or offer isn't converting, pivot now before burning more contacts.

**What happens:**

- Mentor reviews Month 1 outreach data: open rate, reply rate, positive reply rate, call conversion rate
- If reply rate is below 2%: messaging and subject line are the problem → rebuild cold email sequence with mentor input
- If reply rate is above 2% but call conversion is low: offer or pricing is the problem → repackage service offer
- If calls are booking but not closing: sales process is the problem → targeted role-play sessions (ISP-05 SPIN drills)
- If all metrics are above benchmark but no income: niche may be too competitive → introduce warm outreach and community-based lead generation (Facebook Groups, Reddit, Slack communities)
- Guest session: a Nivy mentor or alumni with direct experience in the student's target niche shares their outreach approach and answers questions
- Job application pivot (if appropriate): if freelancing is not converting after 8 weeks total effort, mentor supports pivot to remote employment applications — resume tailoring, LinkedIn keyword optimisation for recruiters, interview preparation
- Target: First meeting, interview, or trial project confirmed by end of Month 2

**Month 2 Exit Criteria:**

- At least 1 discovery call conducted (not just booked)
- At least 1 proposal sent OR 1 job application submitted
- Monthly activity summary submitted

---

### Month 3 — Close or Place

**Focus:** Convert the pipeline to income — freelance or employed.

**What happens:**

- Full focus on closing any open conversations from Months 1–2: follow-up sequences, re-engagement messages, final proposals
- Portfolio and LinkedIn refresh (mentor reviews and suggests final improvements)
- Active job application support if applicable: cover letter templates, interview practice (mock call with mentor), salary negotiation basics
- Introduction to Nivy's freelancer network: sub-contracting opportunities with Nivy-affiliated clients where a graduate can take on delivery work under a senior Nivy team member (earning while building portfolio)
- Alliance eligibility re-check: if all criteria are now met, formal Alliance invitation is considered
- Target: First income earned — freelance (any amount) or employment offer accepted

**Month 3 Exit Criteria (Phase 7 completion):**

- At least 1 income-generating engagement confirmed (paid client, employed role, or Alliance sub-contract), OR
- A documented record of 300+ total contacts, 5+ discovery calls, and 3+ proposals — plus mentor certification that the student has genuinely exhausted the Phase 7 support scope

## Phase 7 Rules

- Student must submit a **weekly activity report** every Sunday (template below) — missed reports result in a mentor check-in call the following Monday
- Minimum **20 outreach contacts per working day** maintained throughout
- Must attend all scheduled mentor calls (cancel with 24-hour notice or the session is forfeited for that week — not rescheduled)
- Phase 7 **cannot be extended beyond 12 weeks** (3 months). After 12 weeks, formal support ends. Students remain part of the Nivy community and can access the Placement Tracker independently.
- Phase 7 is available **once per student** — it cannot be re-entered after completion

## Phase 7 Exit — What Happens Next

**If income is earned during Phase 7:**

Phase 7 formally closes. Student graduates from the support programme. Mentor submits a completion note. Student is flagged for Alliance consideration at the next quarterly review.

**If Phase 7 completes without income:**

The student receives a formal written summary from their mentor: what was attempted, what the data showed, what the recommended next steps are independently. This is provided with honesty and without judgment — the goal is to leave the student with clarity about what to do next, not guilt about what didn't work.
