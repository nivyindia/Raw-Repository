## Our Commitment

Nivy Academy believes in results. We don’t just teach — we hold your hand until you’re earning. Our refund policy reflects this commitment.

---

## Refund Schedule

| Timeframe | Refund Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Within 7 days of enrollment (no materials accessed) | 100% refund |
| Within 14 days of enrollment | 50% refund |
| After Day 14 | No refund |
| After completing the program | Not applicable |

---

## Performance Guarantee (Conditional)

For students on the **Practitioner Tier and above**, Nivy Academy offers a conditional performance guarantee:

**If you:**

1. Complete all 6 modules
2. Submit all tasks on time
3. Attend all 3 monthly mentor calls
4. Run a minimum of 100 outreach contacts per month
5. Maintain daily logs for 80%+ of program days
6. Participate in the placement process

**And you still don’t land a client or job offer within 6 months of program completion...**

→ We will give you an **additional 3 months of mentorship for free**.

> Note: This is not a cash refund. It is an extended mentorship guarantee.
>

## Non-Refundable Items

- One-time registration/application fee
- Any third-party tool costs (Apollo, LinkedIn Premium, etc.)
- Trial Week access

## Refund Process

To request a refund, email [[academy@nivyacademy.com](mailto:academy@nivyacademy.com)] with subject: **REFUND REQUEST — [Your Name] — [Batch Number]**. Include reason and enrollment date. Refunds are processed within 10 business days.
