# NA-AX-04 | Final Graduation Assessment

---

## Overview

The Final Graduation Assessment takes place in **Week 12** of the Foundation Track. It is a **2-part assessment**: a written capstone exam and a live project presentation.

**Pass Score:** 60% overall

**Time Allowed:** Exam — 90 minutes | Presentation — 30 minutes

---

## Part 1: Capstone Exam (Written) — 50 Marks

### Section A: Multiple Choice (20 marks)

20 questions drawn from the Module Quiz Bank (NA-AX-02). 1 mark each.

### Section B: Short Answer (15 marks)

Answer 3 of the following 5 questions (5 marks each):

1. Describe the ideal prospect profile for the niche you worked with during the program. Include: industry, job title, company size, geography, and pain points.
2. You've sent 200 cold emails and received a 3% positive reply rate. What does this tell you, and what would you change?
3. A client says: "I like your proposal but I need to think about it." Write your exact response.
4. Explain how you would onboard a new client in the first 48 hours after they sign the contract.
5. What is a retainer model and why is it better for a freelancer than project-based work?

### Section C: Scenario Analysis (15 marks)

Read the following client brief and write a full proposal (Problem, Solution, Deliverables, Timeline, Price, CTA):

> *"We are a US-based e-commerce brand selling eco-friendly home products. We're getting website traffic but our conversion rate is low and we're not getting repeat customers. We have a budget of $800–$1,200/month."*
> 

---

## Part 2: Live Project Presentation — 50 Marks

The student presents their **Portfolio + Outreach Results** to their mentor in a 30-minute video call.

### Presentation Structure (10 min)

- Who they targeted (niche + ICP)
- What outreach approach they used
- Results: contacts reached, replies, meetings, proposals, closes
- Best-performing message (show it)
- Lessons learned

### Portfolio Walkthrough (10 min)

- Walk through 2–3 portfolio projects
- Explain the problem, approach, and result for each

### Q&A from Mentor (10 min)

- Mentor asks 3–5 challenging questions based on the presentation
