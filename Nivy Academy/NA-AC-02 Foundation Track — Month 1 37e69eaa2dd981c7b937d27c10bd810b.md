# NA-AC-02 | Foundation Track — Month 1

**Doc ID:** NA-AC-02 · **Dept:** Academic · **Phase:** 1 · **Audience:** Students, Mentors · **Language:** English · **Status:** 🟢 Live

**Tags:** curriculum, month 1, foundation, mindset, tools, lead identification

---

## 🎯 Month 1 Goal

Shift the student from a passive learner to a confident "Business Consultant" who can identify a company’s pain points and position themselves as the solution — not just a service provider.

---

## 🗓️ Week-by-Week Lesson Plan

### ☀️ Week 1 — Global Market Fundamentals

**Objective:** Understand how USA/UK markets work and what they expect from vendors.

| Topic | Format | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USA vs UK market differences (culture, tone, expectations) | Video + Notes | 90 min |
| Business communication etiquette (Email, Zoom, LinkedIn) | Video + Template | 60 min |
| Understanding outsourcing: Why do Western companies hire from India? | Video | 45 min |
| Time zone management & professional scheduling | SOP | 30 min |
| **Task:** Write a professional intro email to a US business owner | Practical | Graded |

### ☀️ Week 2 — The Consultant Mindset

**Objective:** Learn to approach companies as a problem-solver, not a vendor.

| Topic | Format | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Consultant vs. Freelancer" — the mindset shift | Video | 60 min |
| How to research a company in 10 minutes (LinkedIn, website, reviews) | Live Demo | 75 min |
| The "Pain Point" Framework — finding what a company is losing money on | Workshop | 90 min |
| The "Reverse Psychology" approach to sales | Video | 60 min |
| **Task:** Research 3 real companies and identify their top pain point | Practical | Graded |

### ☀️ Week 3 — Tool Stack Mastery

**Objective:** Become proficient in the core tools used in international digital marketing.

| Tool | What You Learn | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) | Lead extraction, email finding, list building | Hands-on |
| LinkedIn Sales Navigator | Advanced prospect search, InMail strategy | Hands-on |
| Meta Business Suite | Ad accounts, page management, scheduling | Hands-on |
| ChatGPT / Claude | Email drafting, research, content creation | Hands-on |
| Canva | Professional graphics for outreach & proposals | Hands-on |
| Bitrix24 / Notion CRM | Pipeline setup, deal tracking, reporting | Hands-on |
| **Task:** Build a list of 20 qualified leads using Apollo + LinkedIn | Practical | Graded |

### ☀️ Week 4 — Lead Identification & Audit Methodology

**Objective:** Build your first real lead list and learn how to audit a prospect’s business.

| Topic | Format | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What makes a "qualified lead"? (ICP — Ideal Customer Profile) | Video | 60 min |
| Building a targeted lead list (industry + geography filter) | Workshop | 90 min |
| How to conduct a Website Audit (SEO, speed, UX gaps) | Live Demo | 75 min |
| How to conduct a Social Media Audit (engagement, content, gaps) | Live Demo | 60 min |
| The "Audit-to-Pitch" conversion: turning audit findings into a proposal | Template | 45 min |
| **Task:** Complete a full audit of one real company + draft a pitch | Practical | Graded |
