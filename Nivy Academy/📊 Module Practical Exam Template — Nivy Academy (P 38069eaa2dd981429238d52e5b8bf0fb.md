## Section B — Applied Task (40 marks)

> **Scenario:** [Give the student a realistic brief, e.g. "You've been hired by a Varanasi-based clothing brand that sells on their own website and Amazon India. Your task is to build them a 3-month SEO strategy."]
> 

**Deliverables required:**

1. [Specific output — e.g. Keyword research report (minimum 50 keywords, categorised by intent)]
2. [Specific output — e.g. Technical SEO audit report using Screaming Frog — at least 5 issues identified with fixes]
3. [Specific output — e.g. Content brief for 3 pillar pages]

**Submission format:** Google Doc / Notion page / Spreadsheet — share link in LMS

**Marking Rubric:**

| Criteria | Excellent (10) | Good (7) | Satisfactory (5) | Needs Work (0–3) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy of research | Fully correct, detailed | Minor errors | Some errors | Significant errors |
| Use of correct tools | All tools used properly | Most tools correct | Some tool issues | Tools not used |
| Practical application | Directly applicable to scenario | Mostly applicable | Generic answer | Not relevant |
| Presentation quality | Professional, well-structured | Clear | Adequate | Hard to follow |

## Section C — Strategy Brief (30 marks)

> **Brief prompt:** [e.g. "Write a 400–600 word executive summary of your SEO strategy for the brand in Section B. Include: current situation assessment, 3-month goal, 3 priorities, 1 risk and how you'd mitigate it."]
> 

**Marking Rubric:**

| Criteria | Excellent (10) | Good (7) | Satisfactory (5) | Needs Work (0–3) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strategic thinking | Clear, original, insight-driven | Good strategy | Basic | No strategy |
| Use of course concepts | Correctly applies 5+ concepts | 3–4 concepts | 1–2 concepts | Not applied |
| Clarity of writing | Concise, professional | Clear | Readable | Hard to follow |

---

## Section D — Reflection (10 marks)

> **Self-assessment prompt (200 words max):**
> 

> "What was the hardest part of this exam for you? What would you do differently on a real client project? What concept do you still want to learn more about?"
> 

**Marking:** Marked for honest engagement and self-awareness, not for having the right answer. 10/10 if the student reflects seriously. 5/10 if generic. 0–3 if not completed.
