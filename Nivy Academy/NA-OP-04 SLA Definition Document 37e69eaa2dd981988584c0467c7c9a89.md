# NA-OP-04 | SLA Definition Document

## What "Placement Assistance" Actually Means

---

## Purpose

This document legally and operationally defines what Nivy Academy's "Placement Assistance" means, to avoid misunderstandings with students and their families.

---

## What Placement Assistance IS

✅ Sharing relevant job openings and freelance opportunities with qualified graduates

✅ Introducing graduates to companies and individuals in Nivy's professional network

✅ Providing referral letters and experience letters to support job/client applications

✅ Offering interview preparation and portfolio review sessions

✅ Including graduates in the Nivy Freelancer Alliance for sub-contracting work

✅ Sharing the graduate's profile in Nivy's partner network upon request

---

## What Placement Assistance is NOT

❌ A guarantee of employment with any specific company

❌ A guarantee of a minimum monthly income

❌ A job placement agency service (Nivy is not a recruiter)

❌ A promise that a client or employer will hire the graduate

❌ A financial liability if the graduate does not find work

---

## Eligibility for Placement Assistance

A student is eligible for Placement Assistance **only if they have:**

1. Completed all 3 months of the Foundation Track
2. Scored 60%+ on their Final Graduation Assessment
3. Submitted all required monthly task sets
4. Maintained daily logs for 60%+ of program days
5. Attended minimum 2 of 3 monthly mentor calls

*Students who do not meet these criteria are not eligible for Placement Assistance.*

---

## Service Level Commitments (What Nivy Will Do)

| Commitment | Timeline |
| --- | --- |
| Share the graduate's profile with network partners | Within 7 days of graduation |
| Send minimum 3 relevant opportunities per month | For 3 months post-graduation |
| Provide an Experience Letter (A/A+ students only) | Within 5 business days of request |
| Conduct a portfolio review call | Once, within 14 days of graduation |
| Introduce to Nivy Freelancer Alliance | Within 10 days of graduation (if eligible) |
