# NA-AX-06 | 7-Day Trial Task (Admission Filter)

**Doc ID:** NA-AX-06 · **Dept:** Assessment · **Phase:** 1 · **Audience:** Applicants, Coordinators · **Status:** 🟢 Live

---

## 🎯 Purpose

The 7-Day Trial Task is a **free, pre-enrollment filter** given to every applicant before they pay the program fee. Only applicants who complete and pass this task are allowed to join the paid program.

**Goal:** Filter out "tourists" (certificate hunters with no commitment) and retain only serious, action-oriented learners.

---

## 🗓️ The 7-Day Trial Task

### What the Applicant Must Do (All 5 tasks, in 7 days):

**Day 1–2 — Research Task**

Find 5 real companies (USA or UK based) that:

- Have a website
- Are active on LinkedIn or Instagram
- Appear to be spending on digital marketing (ads, content, etc.)

For each company, write 2–3 sentences on: *what they do, who their customers are, and one gap you noticed in their online presence.*

**Day 3–4 — Cold Email Task**

Write 1 personalised cold email for one of the 5 companies you found.

- Subject line must be specific to the company
- Must identify a pain point in the opening line
- Must have a clear CTA
- Max 120 words

**Day 5 — Tool Setup Task**

- Create a free [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) account
- Search for decision-makers at one of your 5 companies
- Screenshot the results and submit

**Day 6 — Reflection Task**

Write 100–150 words answering: *"What did you learn from this task, and what does it tell you about what you need to improve?"*

**Day 7 — Submit All Work**

Submit all 4 tasks via the Google Form link provided at registration.

---

## 📊 Evaluation Criteria

| Criteria | Pass Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Research quality | Companies are real, pain points are specific |
| Cold email | Personalised, professional, under 120 words |
| Tool setup | Apollo account created, screenshot submitted |
| Reflection | Shows self-awareness and genuine interest |
| Submission timing | Submitted within 7 days of receiving the task |

**Outcome:** Pass = invited to enroll and pay. Fail = offered 1 retry in 7 days.
