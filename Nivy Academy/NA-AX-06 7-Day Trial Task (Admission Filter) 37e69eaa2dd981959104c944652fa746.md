# NA-AX-06 | 7-Day Trial Task (Admission Filter)

## Purpose

Before a student is formally enrolled, they must complete a 7-day trial task. This filters out low-commitment applicants and ensures only serious students enter the program.

---

## Instructions for Applicant

Welcome to the Nivy Academy Trial Week. This is your first real test — and it’s not about being perfect. It’s about showing up, doing the work, and proving you’re ready.

You have **7 days** to complete the following:

---

## Trial Tasks

### Day 1–2: Research Task

- Choose one global niche (e.g. E-commerce, SaaS, Coaches)
- Research 20 international companies in that niche
- Submit a Google Sheet with: Company Name, Country, Website, LinkedIn URL, What service they might need

### Day 3–4: Outreach Task

- Write 3 cold email templates targeting that niche
- Each email should have: Subject line, Personalised opening, Value proposition, Clear CTA
- Submit all 3 for review

### Day 5–6: Tool Task

- Create a free [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) account
- Build a list of 10 real leads in your chosen niche
- Export and submit the list

### Day 7: Reflection Task

- Record a 1–2 minute Loom video answering:
    1. What did you learn this week?
    2. What was hardest?
    3. Why do you want to join Nivy Academy?

---

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criteria | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Quality of research (accuracy, depth) | 25% |
| Cold email quality (clarity, hook, CTA) | 30% |
| Apollo list completion | 20% |
| Loom video (confidence, clarity, honesty) | 25% |

**Pass Score: 60%+**

Students who pass are invited to formally enrol. Students who score 45–60% are given one more week. Below 45% are not accepted for the current batch.
