## AD CREATIVE NOTES

- Use real student faces (with permission) where possible
- Avoid stock photos — use authentic, Indian-context imagery
- Colour palette: Nivy brand colours (update with brand guidelines)
- Video ads: 15–30 seconds max for FB/IG Reels; 30–60 seconds for LinkedIn
- Always A/B test headline variants before scaling budget
