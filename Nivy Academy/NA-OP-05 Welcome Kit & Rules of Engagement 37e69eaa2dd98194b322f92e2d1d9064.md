## What You’ve Signed Up For

This is not a course. It’s a **professional training program**. The difference:

- Courses give you information. We give you *accountability*.
- Courses end when you watch the videos. We end when you *get results*.
- Courses have no consequences. Here, missed submissions have *real consequences*.

---

## The 10 Rules of Engagement

1. **Show up every day.** Even if it’s just 30 minutes — log it.
2. **Submit on time.** Late is penalised. Missing is not acceptable without prior notice.
3. **Ask questions — but do your research first.** If Google can answer it in 30 seconds, try that first.
4. **Your mentor’s time is sacred.** Come to every call prepared with updates, questions, and blockers.
5. **Be honest.** If something isn’t working, say so. We can only fix what we know is broken.
6. **Respect confidentiality.** What happens in this program stays in this program.
7. **No excuses, only plans.** If something goes wrong, come with a plan, not just a problem.
8. **Your work is your reputation.** Every task you submit reflects your professional standard.
9. **Compete with yourself.** This is not about beating other students. It’s about becoming better than you were yesterday.
10. **See this through.** The students who quit in Week 3 never find out what Week 10 feels like.
