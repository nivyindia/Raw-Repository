**NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT**

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date of signature between:

**Disclosing Party:** The client or company sharing confidential information

**Receiving Party:** The student / contractor engaged through Nivy Academy

---

## 1. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any data or information, oral or written, that relates to the Disclosing Party's business, including but not limited to: customer lists, financial data, marketing strategies, product plans, pricing, business processes, and any materials marked as confidential.

## 2. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

- Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential
- Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent
- Use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of delivering agreed services
- Take reasonable security measures to protect the information

## 3. Exclusions

This Agreement does not apply to information that:

- Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party
- Was already known to the Receiving Party before disclosure
- Is required to be disclosed by law or court order

## 4. Duration

This Agreement remains in effect for **2 years** from the date of signing, or until the Confidential Information becomes publicly available.

## 5. Return of Materials

Upon request or termination of the engagement, the Receiving Party will return or destroy all Confidential Information and confirm destruction in writing.

## 6. Remedies

Breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm. The Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies available.
