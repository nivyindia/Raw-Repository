# The Situation

Nivy Academy is upgrading its curriculum, assessment system, and programme structure significantly across 2026–2028. Students who enrolled under the current structure need clarity on three questions:

1. Does the new system apply to them?
2. Will their existing progress be recognised?
3. What do they need to do differently, if anything?

This page is the internal plan. The student-facing notice (QW-12) is a 1-page summary derived from it.

---

# 📜 Transition Policy

## Students Mid-Programme (Enrolled Before July 1, 2026)

| Scenario | Policy |
| --- | --- |
| Student is in Foundation tier | Completes current Foundation curriculum. New depth modules (Phase 1) are offered as **optional bonus content** — not required for their certificate. |
| Student is in Practitioner tier | Same as above. New modules made available on LMS as optional extras from Wave 1 completion (Oct 2026 onward). |
| Student is in Expert/Professional tier | Given the **choice** to opt into the new modular system. If they opt in, their existing progress is mapped to the closest equivalent new module. If they opt out, they complete under original structure. |
| Student has already completed the programme | Not affected. Their certificate stands. New certificate names (if any change) apply only to new enrolments. |

## New Students (Enrolled July 1, 2026 Onward)

All new students enrol under the updated structure from Wave 1. Sub-topic checkpoints and Module Practical Exams apply from day one.

# 📄 Student-Facing Notice (Template — QW-12 Output)

---

**Subject: Important update about your Nivy Academy programme**

Dear [Student Name],

We are upgrading the Nivy Academy curriculum to make it more practical, internationally competitive, and career-ready. Here is what you need to know:

**What is changing:**

- New in-depth modules are being added across all major subjects (SEO, PPC, Social Media, Content, and more)
- A new assessment system with shorter checkpoints after each topic and a practical exam at the end of each module
- A student scorecard so you can track your progress across every skill area

**What this means for you:**

- If you are currently enrolled, your programme continues under your existing structure. You will not lose any progress.
- New modules will be made available to you as **bonus content** once they are published
- You will have the option to take the new assessments for additional certificates

**What is NOT changing:**

- Your enrolment, fees, or course duration
- Your existing assignments and marks
- Your certificate (the name and standard remains the same for your cohort)

**Questions?** Contact [support email / WhatsApp number]. We will respond within 24 hours.

With gratitude,

Abhishek Dayal

Founder, Nivy Academy

# ⚠️ Risks to Manage

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Students feel confused or anxious about changes | Send notice early (before Jul 1), keep language simple, emphasise continuity |
| Students demand the new content immediately | Set clear dates in the notice (Wave 1 content available Oct 2026); offer waitlist signup |
| Expert/Pro students want to opt in but progress mapping is unclear | Academic Head maps equivalences before Jul 20; create a simple 1-page transition guide per tier |
| Current students feel the new cohorts are getting a better product | Offer all depth modules free to current students as bonus content once live |
