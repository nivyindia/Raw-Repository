## 🎯 Program Mission

Convert learners into globally-employable Digital Marketing professionals and Virtual Assistants who can generate leads, close clients, and work independently for international companies — or join Nivy as full-time/freelance workforce.

---

## 📍 The Two Tracks

| Track | For | Duration | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Foundation Track** | Beginners, Students, Freshers | 3 months + 3-month buffer | Job-ready DM/VA professional |
| **Acceleration Track** | Freelancers, Experienced, Agencies | 6 weeks intensive | Client acquisition + scaling |

---

## 🗓️ Foundation Track — Month-by-Month Map

### Month 1 — Foundation & Consultant Mindset

*Goal: Move from "Student" to "Business Consultant"*

- Global Market Fundamentals (USA/UK focus)
- The Consultant vs. Service Provider Mindset
- Tool Stack Mastery (LinkedIn, Apollo, Meta Suite, ChatGPT)
- Lead Identification & List Building
- Website/Social Media Audit Methodology
- AI-Enhanced Workflow Introduction

### Month 2 — Active Outreach & Client Acquisition

*Goal: Generate leads and close first client*

- High-Conversion Cold Email & LinkedIn Outreach
- Instagram DM & WhatsApp Outreach Scripts
- The "Bridge" Strategy (upselling services)
- Handling Objections ("We have an agency already")
- Negotiation, Pricing, and Closing Techniques
- CRM Workflow & Pipeline Management
- Profit-Sharing Model Walkthrough

### Month 3 — Execution, Scaling & Placement Prep

*Goal: Deliver results on live projects and secure placement*

- Live International Project Management
- Client Retention Strategies
- Monthly Reporting & Client Communication
- Resume & Portfolio Building (live project evidence)
- Mock Interviews (Client-Facing Roles)
- Final Assessment & Graduation

### Months 4–6 — Success Buffer

*Goal: Ensure 100% placement or refund eligibility*

- Weekly Mentor Check-ins
- Continued Tool Access
- Ongoing Interview Coaching
- Placement Refinement Support
