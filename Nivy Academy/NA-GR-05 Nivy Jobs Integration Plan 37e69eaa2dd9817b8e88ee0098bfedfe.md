# NA-GR-05 | Nivy Jobs Integration Plan

---

## Purpose

Defines how Nivy Academy feeds graduates into Nivy's broader hiring and job-placement pipeline (referred to internally as "Nivy Jobs" / Nivy Careers sub-brand), creating a closed-loop talent ecosystem.

---

## The Integration Loop

```
Nivy Academy (Training) → Graduates & GFA Members → Nivy Careers Talent Pool → Placed at Nivy sub-brands (Artisan, Growth, Next) or external partners → Alumni success stories → Feed back into Nivy Academy marketing (NA-GR-04) → New enrollments
```

---

## Integration Touchpoints

### 1. Graduate Handoff

Upon graduation, every student's profile (Portfolio NA-PP-08, Assessment results, Placement Tracker status) is made visible to Nivy Careers' internal hiring coordinators as a standing talent pool entry.

### 2. Priority Internal Roles

Nivy sub-brands (Nivy Artisan, Nivy Growth, Nivy Next) post open roles first to the Nivy Academy graduate/GFA pool before external hiring, for relevant junior digital marketing, outreach, or VA positions.

### 3. GFA as a Pre-Hire Trial

GFA project work (NA-PP-04) effectively functions as a working interview — strong GFA performers are fast-tracked for full-time roles at Nivy without a separate hiring process.

### 4. Feedback Loop to Curriculum

Hiring managers at Nivy sub-brands provide quarterly feedback on skill gaps observed in placed graduates; this feedback is routed back to Dept 01 (Academic) to refine curriculum (NA-AC series).

## Success Metric

% of Nivy sub-brand junior hires sourced from Nivy Academy graduates — tracked annually as a core KPI for the Academy's strategic value to Nivy overall.
