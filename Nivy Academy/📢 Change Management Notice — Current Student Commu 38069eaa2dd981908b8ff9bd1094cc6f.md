# ❓ FAQ — Current Student Questions

**Q: I’m halfway through my course. Do I need to start over?**

No. All your progress is saved. New modules will appear in your dashboard as they’re added — they don’t reset anything you’ve already done.

**Q: Will my certificate change?**

Your certificate will reflect the programme you enrolled on. If you complete the upgraded modules (which you’ll have access to), your certificate will note the upgraded curriculum version. We’ll communicate the exact wording once the design is finalised.

**Q: I enrolled on [old programme name]. Does that still exist?**

The content you enrolled for is all still there and being upgraded. The *names* of programmes may change as part of the tier restructure — if that affects you, we’ll contact you individually before any change is made to your account.

**Q: What if I don’t want to do the new checkpoints and practical exams?**

The new assessments only apply to new modules added from July 2026 onward. If you’ve already completed a module before July 2026, you won’t be asked to redo any assessments.

**Q: Will the live sessions change?**

The schedule and format of live Q&A sessions may be updated as the new modules roll out. You’ll receive at least 2 weeks’ notice before any session timing changes.

**Q: I haven’t started yet. Should I wait?**

No — start now. The sooner you begin, the sooner you get access to the new content as it comes out. There is no benefit to waiting.

**Q: What if I have a complaint about the changes?**

We take this seriously. Email [FILL IN support email] with subject line “Curriculum Change Query” and Abhishek will personally review it within 48 hours.

**Q: Will pricing change for current students?**

No. Your existing pricing is locked in for your current enrolment. Any pricing changes only apply to new enrolments after [FILL IN date].
