## ✅ The 5 Non-Negotiable Rules

**Rule 1: Log Everything**

Every call made, every email sent, every reply received — it goes into the CRM the same day. No log = it didn’t happen. Unlogged outreach does not count toward your refund eligibility.

**Rule 2: Submit Every Task**

All 12 weekly tasks are mandatory. Late by 1 day = -10% penalty. Missing a task without prior mentor approval = written warning.

**Rule 3: Show Up**

Minimum 80% attendance at live sessions is required. If you miss a session, watch the recording and submit a 100-word summary to your mentor within 48 hours.

**Rule 4: Communicate Early**

If you are struggling, stuck, or falling behind — tell your mentor 3 days before the deadline, not after. Silence is the fastest way to fail.

**Rule 5: No Client Contact Outside Nivy**

You will interact with real clients through our ecosystem. Do not approach them for personal work. This is an NDA violation and grounds for immediate removal.
