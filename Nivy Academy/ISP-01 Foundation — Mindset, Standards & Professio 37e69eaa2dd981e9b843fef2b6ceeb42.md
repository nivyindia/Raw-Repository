## Lesson 1.3b — Lead-Gen & AI Tooling Setup *(merged from NA-AC-02)*

**Duration:** 60 min

**Content:**

[Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) is your primary lead-generation tool for the whole programme — set it up now so it's ready to use when Phase 4 (Lead Generation & Outreach) begins.

[**Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) Setup SOP:**

1. Go to [apollo.io](http://apollo.io) → Sign up for a free account
2. Verify your email and complete your profile (name, title: "Digital Marketing Consultant", company: your name or "Freelance")
3. Install the Apollo Chrome Extension — this lets you find emails directly from LinkedIn profiles
4. Connect your Gmail/Outlook account to Apollo (Settings → Integrations → Email)
5. Test: Search for 5 contacts in any industry, export their details to a CSV — confirm emails appear
6. Bookmark Apollo and add it to your browser toolbar — you will use this every day

**AI Prompting — The RCTF Framework**

AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude) are amplifiers, not shortcuts. Use this framework for every prompt you write — in this phase and throughout the programme:

- **R — Role:** Tell the AI who it is. ("You are a professional B2B copywriter.")
- **C — Context:** Give the background. ("I am a digital marketing freelancer targeting SaaS companies in the USA.")
- **T — Task:** State exactly what you need. ("Write a cold email subject line for a SaaS founder who struggles with lead generation.")
- **F — Format:** Specify the output. ("Give me 5 options, each under 8 words.")

> A full AI prompting deep-dive with 10 worked examples continues in Phase 3 (AI Tools & Virtual Assistance) — this lesson installs the habit early so it's already part of your workflow before Phase 3 begins.
> 

**Daily Task 1.3b:**

2. Using the RCTF framework, write 3 subject lines for a cold email to a SaaS company with AI assistance. Then write 3 more yourself without AI. Submit both sets in your Daily Log with a short note on which performed better and why.
