## Slide 2 — The Problem

Most college graduates complete their degrees without practical, job-ready skills in digital marketing, lead generation, or client communication. Employers report a major gap between academic knowledge and real-world execution ability.

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution

Nivy Academy offers a hands-on, outcome-driven training program where students learn by doing — real outreach, real tools (Apollo, LinkedIn, CRM, AI), and real client-facing tasks, not just theory.

---

## Slide 4 — Program Overview

- 3-month Foundation Track + optional 3-month Success Buffer
- Modules: Market Fundamentals, Tool Stack, Consultant Mindset, Outreach & Lead Gen, Negotiation & Closing, Execution & Scaling
- Assessment-based certification with practical task rubrics

---

## Slide 5 — What the Institute Gets

- Co-branded certification program for students
- Placement assistance pipeline for graduates
- Revenue share on every enrolled student
- Zero infrastructure burden — Nivy delivers all content, mentorship, and assessment

---

## Slide 6 — What Students Get

- Practical skills in digital marketing & business development
- Real outreach experience using professional tools
- Apprenticeship Certificate + Experience Letter
- Placement assistance through Nivy's partner network
