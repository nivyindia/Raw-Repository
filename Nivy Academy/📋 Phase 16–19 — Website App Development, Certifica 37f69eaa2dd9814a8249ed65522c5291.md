# 🤖 PHASE 19 — AI Curriculum: International Benchmark Alignment

> **Why this matters:** ISP-03 and the planned Phase 2 (AI for Digital Marketers) are strong on **applied AI tool usage** (Claude, ChatGPT, Canva AI, Apollo automation). But benchmarked against **Google AI Essentials** (5-module structure: Intro to AI → Productivity → Prompting → Responsible AI → Staying Ahead), **NASSCOM FutureSkills AI Foundation** (India's government-backed AI certification, co-developed with Microsoft/IBM/AWS), and **IBM SkillsBuild AI Fundamentals**, three things are missing: (1) foundational AI *literacy* — what ML/LLMs actually are, (2) a structured *responsible AI* module beyond the ethics phase, and (3) **agentic AI** — autonomous multi-step AI workflows, which is now the fastest-growing area in both Google's and Microsoft's AI curricula.
>
