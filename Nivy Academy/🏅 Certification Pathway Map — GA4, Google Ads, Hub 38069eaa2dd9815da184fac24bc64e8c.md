# 🏅 Certification Pathway Map

**Objective:** Every graduating Expert & Professional student should hold at least **3 of the 5 certifications** below. These are all free, industry-recognised, and directly strengthen the student's CV.

---

## 1️⃣ Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Certification

**Provider:** Google | Skillshop

**Cost:** Free

**Exam format:** 50 questions | 75 minutes | 80% pass mark | Retake: 24 hours

**Validity:** 12 months

**Link:** [https://skillshop.exceedlms.com/student/path/508845](https://skillshop.exceedlms.com/student/path/508845)

### Nivy Course Alignment

| GA4 Exam Topic | Nivy Module Reference |
| --- | --- |
| GA4 interface & reports | P1.6 — Analytics Fundamentals |
| Events & conversions setup | P1.6 — Analytics Depth |
| Audiences & segments | P1.6 + P1.5 (Email) |
| Exploration reports | P1.6 — Analytics Depth |
| Data stream setup | P1.6 — Analytics Depth |
| Attribution modelling | P1.6 — Advanced Analytics |

### Study Path

1. Complete Nivy Analytics modules (P1.6) — estimated 3–4 hours
2. Read Google's free GA4 study guide on Skillshop (6–8 hours)
3. Practice with [Measureschool's GA4 practice exam](https://measureschool.com)
4. Take the official exam

### Practice Resources

- [Skillshop GA4 Course](https://skillshop.exceedlms.com)
- [Google Analytics Demo Account](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6367342)
- [Measureschool Free Practice Exam](https://measureschool.com/ga4-certification-practice)

## 4️⃣ Meta Certified Digital Marketing Associate

**Provider:** Meta Blueprint

**Cost:** Free (exam only is $150 — see note)

**Exam format:** 60 questions | 60 minutes | 700/1000 pass mark

**Validity:** 12 months

**Link:** [https://www.facebook.com/business/learn/certification](https://www.facebook.com/business/learn/certification)

> ⚠️ **Note for students:** The Meta certification exam has a $150 fee. The **Blueprint free courses** (linked below) are the preparation path — the cert itself is optional for now, but Blueprint badges are free. We recommend all students complete the Blueprint learning path.
> 

### Nivy Course Alignment

| Meta Blueprint Topic | Nivy Module Reference |
| --- | --- |
| Facebook & Instagram Ads | P1.2 — SMM Depth |
| Ad formats & placements | P1.2.3 |
| Campaign objectives | P1.2 + P1.3 |
| Audience Insights | P1.2.4 |
| Ads Manager fundamentals | P1.2.2 |
| Pixel & Conversions API | P1.6 + P1.3 |

### Study Path

1. Complete Nivy SMM modules (P1.2)
2. Complete Meta Blueprint free online courses (6–8 hours)
3. Earn free Blueprint digital badges
4. Optional: Book the paid certification exam ($150)

### Practice Resources

- [Meta Blueprint Free Courses](https://www.facebook.com/business/learn)
- [Social Media Examiner Meta Ads study guide](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com)
