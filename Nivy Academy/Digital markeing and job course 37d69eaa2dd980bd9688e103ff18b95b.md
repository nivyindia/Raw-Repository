There is one crucial element that will tie this entire model together and ensure it stays profitable and scalable: **The "Operational Framework" (How you actually manage the workflow).**

Since you are dealing with multiple stakeholders (students, clients, and partners), you need a structured way to manage them without getting overwhelmed. Here are the missing pieces:

### 1. The "Performance Dashboard" (Accountability System)

To make your "Money-Back Guarantee" and "Placement Assistance" work, you must be able to prove that a student did (or didn't) do the work.

- **CRM/Tracking:** Every student should have a simple tracker (like a shared Google Sheet or a simple CRM) where they log:
    - Number of prospects contacted.
    - Responses received.
    - Meetings set.
- **Why this matters:** This is your **Evidence File**. If a student asks for a refund, you check this dashboard. If they haven't met the minimum activity targets, you don't owe them a refund. It protects you from lazy students.

### 2. The "Quality Control" (Gatekeeping)

You mentioned that you only want to teach active learners. You need a **"Filtering Mechanism"** before they officially join the paid program.

- **The 7-Day Trial:** Instead of taking their money immediately, give them a 7-day "Introductory Module." If they can complete a small, simple task (like finding 5 valid prospects in a specific industry), they are "Qualified" for the full program.
- **The Benefit:** This filters out the "tourists" (those just looking for a certificate) and keeps your training environment professional and high-energy.

### 3. The "Client Relationship" (Sustainability)

Since you are using a "Reverse Psychology" approach (finding requirements for companies), you need a **"Client Success Manager"** role.

- **The Loop:** A student brings a lead $\rightarrow$ You (or your team) help close the deal $\rightarrow$ The work is delivered.
- **Retainers:** Don't just look for one-off projects. Aim to turn these leads into **monthly retainers**. This provides a steady stream of income for you and a guaranteed "Apprenticeship" spot for your students.

### 4. Legal Protection (The "Paperwork" Layer)

Since you are offering a money-back guarantee and handling international clients, your documentation must be airtight.

- **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA):** Ensure students sign an NDA so they don't take your client acquisition strategies or steal your leads for their own benefit.
- **Service Level Agreement (SLA):** Clearly define in your contract what "Placement Assistance" means. It should be defined as *assistance*, not a *guarantee of a job offer from a specific company*. This protects you legally while still keeping the marketing promise.

Most institutes are "Fragile"—if the market changes, they lose money. Your model is "Anti-Fragile" because:

- **If the market is good:** You make money from clients and training fees.
- **If the market is tough:** You pivot to focus on "Job Seeking/Upskilling" (because in a tough market, people are desperate for skills that guarantee jobs).
- **If you get a massive influx of students:** You can use them as your "Workforce" to scale your own agency's operations in the USA/UK.
