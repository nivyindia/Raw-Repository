# 🛠️ Hiring Decision Guide

| Option | Best When | Typical Lead Time | Cost Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Existing team member** | They have the skill, just need scoping | Immediate | None |
| **Freelance contractor** | Specialist skill, 1–3 month need | 1–2 weeks to source | Medium |
| **Part-time hire** | Ongoing need across 6+ months | 3–4 weeks | Medium–High |
| **Full-time hire** | Core role, 2+ years of work | 6–8 weeks | High |
| **Reassign from another role** | Bandwidth exists elsewhere | Immediate | None |
