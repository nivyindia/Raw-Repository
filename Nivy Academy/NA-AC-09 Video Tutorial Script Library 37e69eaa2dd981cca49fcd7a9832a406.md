## 🎬 VID-02: Understanding USA/UK Markets

**[SCRIPT OUTLINE]**

- Open: "Why do US companies hire from India?" (hook)
- Explain: Cost arbitrage + talent quality
- Key difference: USA = direct, fast, ROI-focused. UK = formal, relationship-first
- Communication expectations (email response time, meeting punctuality)
- What they HATE: vague answers, missed deadlines, over-promising
- What they LOVE: proactive updates, clear metrics, professional tone
- Close: "In this program, you will be trained to the standard they expect."

**[SCRIPT OUTLINE]**
