# NA-GR-04 | Testimonial Collection System

---

## Purpose

A repeatable system for collecting, storing, and using student/alumni testimonials in marketing materials (landing pages NA-MK-02/03, brochures NA-MK-04/05, ad copy NA-MK-09).

---

## Collection Triggers

Testimonials are requested at 3 key moments:

1. **End of Program** — immediately after Final Graduation Assessment (NA-AX-04), while experience is fresh
2. **Placement Confirmation** — when Placement Tracker (NA-PP-05) status moves to "Joined"
3. **6-Month Alumni Check-in** — via Alumni Tracker (NA-GR-01), for longer-term outcome stories

---

## Collection Form (Questions)

1. What was your situation before joining Nivy Academy?
2. What specific skills or results did you gain from the program?
3. Describe one moment or task that was a turning point for you.
4. Where are you now (role/company/freelance status)?
5. Would you recommend Nivy Academy, and to whom specifically?
6. May we use your name, photo, and quote in our marketing materials? (Yes/No + signature)

---

## Storage & Organization

- All testimonials stored in a central "Testimonial Bank" with fields: Student Name, Batch, Tier, Quote (full + short version), Photo (if consented), Outcome Tag (e.g., "Placed in 2 months", "Now freelancing internationally"), Consent Status, Date Collected
- Testimonials tagged by theme: Career Change, International Client Work, Freelance Success, Career Growth, Confidence/Mindset Shift

## Compliance Note

Never publish a testimonial without explicit written consent. Track consent status in the Testimonial Bank at all times; remove immediately if consent is withdrawn.
