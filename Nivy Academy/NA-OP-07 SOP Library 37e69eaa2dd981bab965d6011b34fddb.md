## 📦 SOP-01: [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) Lead Extraction

**Step 1:** Log in to [apollo.io](http://apollo.io) (free account)

**Step 2:** Click "Search" → "People"

**Step 3:** Apply filters:

- Job Title: Marketing Manager / Business Owner / CEO / Founder
- Location: United States / United Kingdom / Australia
- Company Size: 10–200 employees
- Industry: [Per your ICP]

**Step 4:** Click "Save" to create a list. Name it: "[ICP]-[Date]"

**Step 5:** Export or manually copy verified emails

**Step 6:** Enter each lead into your CRM with fields: Name, Company, Email, LinkedIn URL, Industry, Status = "New"

## 📦 SOP-02: LinkedIn Outreach

**Step 1:** Search for target ICP using LinkedIn search filters (title, location, industry)

**Step 2:** Visit profile — spend 60 seconds reading their recent posts and about section

**Step 3:** Send connection request with note (max 300 characters):

> *"Hi [Name], I came across your profile while researching [Industry]. I work with companies like yours on [service]. Would love to connect and share some ideas."*
> 

**Step 4:** After connection accepted (within 48 hours), send a value-first message (NOT a pitch)

**Step 5:** Log the interaction in CRM immediately: Status = "Contacted", Channel = LinkedIn

**Step 6:** Follow up on Day 3 if no response. Final follow-up Day 7.

## 📦 SOP-03: CRM Pipeline Logging

**Lead Status Tags:**

- **New** — Added to list, not yet contacted
- **Contacted** — First outreach sent
- **Replied** — Any response received (positive or negative)
- **Meeting** — Call or Zoom booked
- **Closed Won** — Deal signed
- **Closed Lost** — Not interested / rejected
- **Follow-up** — Awaiting next action

**Logging Rule:** Every interaction must be logged within **24 hours.** Notes field must have at least 1 sentence describing what happened.

## 📦 SOP-04: Cold Email with ChatGPT

**Prompt to use:**

> *"You are a B2B cold email expert. Write a personalised cold email for a company called [Company Name] that does [what they do]. I am reaching out as a Digital Marketing Consultant. I noticed [specific pain point you found]. Keep it under 120 words. Subject line must be specific. CTA must be a simple question, not a sales pitch. Tone: professional, direct, USA business standard."*
> 

**After getting the output:**

1. Personalise the first line manually (add something specific you found on their website)
2. Check for grammar using Grammarly
3. Send from your professional email (not Gmail personal)
4. Log immediately in CRM
