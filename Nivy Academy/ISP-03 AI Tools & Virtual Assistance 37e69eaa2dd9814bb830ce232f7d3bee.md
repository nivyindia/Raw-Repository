# Week 11 — AI Tools Mastery

## Lesson 11.1 — The AI Toolkit for International Professionals

**Content:** Overview of the AI landscape: Claude (best for writing/analysis), ChatGPT (versatile, widely used), Gemini (Google ecosystem), Perplexity (research), Midjourney (images), ElevenLabs (voice), Runway ML / Pika / CapCut AI (AI video generation — accessible alternatives to Sora). How to choose the right AI for the right task. Prompt engineering basics.

> ⚠️ **Note on AI Video Tools:** OpenAI's Sora is currently restricted to a limited rollout and is not practically accessible to most users globally, including in India. For this module, use **Runway ML** ([runway.ml](http://runway.ml)) or **Pika** ([pika.art](http://pika.art)) as your AI video generation tools — both offer free tiers, are widely used by international freelancers, and are hireable skills. Sora should be understood conceptually but not used for task submissions until it becomes widely available.
> 

**Daily Task:** Use Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini to write the same LinkedIn post about a digital marketing tip. Compare outputs. Write a 200-word analysis of which performed best and why.

## Lesson 11.2 — Advanced Prompt Engineering

**Content:** Role prompting, chain-of-thought prompting, few-shot prompting, system prompts vs user prompts, iterative refinement, how to get AI to match a client's brand voice, creating reusable prompt templates for recurring tasks

**Daily Task:** Create a prompt template library: 10 reusable prompts for tasks like blog drafting, cold email writing, SEO meta tags, social media captions, and meeting summaries. Submit as a Notion page.

**SOP — AI Prompt Template Creation:**

1. Identify the task type
2. Write Role: "You are a [role] for a [type of business]..."
3. Add Context: client name, industry, audience, tone
4. Add Format requirements: length, structure, output format
5. Add Constraints: what NOT to include
6. Test with 3 inputs and refine
7. Save to Prompt Library

## Lesson 11.3 — AI for Content Production at Scale

**Content:** Using AI to produce a month's worth of content in one day, AI-assisted blog writing (research → outline → draft → edit workflow), AI for social media captions, email subject lines, ad copy variants, YouTube scripts. The human edit layer — why you always review AI output.

**Daily Task:** Produce 1 full blog post (800 words), 5 social media posts, and 1 email newsletter using AI tools. Time yourself. Submit with a note on how long it took vs doing it manually.

## Lesson 11.4 — AI for Research, Analysis & Reporting

**Content:** Using Perplexity for competitor research, Claude for data analysis and summarising long documents, ChatGPT for creating formatted reports, AI for building SOP drafts, meeting notes transcription ([Otter.ai](http://Otter.ai), [Fireflies.ai](http://Fireflies.ai))

**Daily Task:** Record a 5-minute mock client call. Use [Otter.ai](http://Otter.ai) to transcribe it. Use Claude to summarise it into action items. Submit transcript + AI summary.

**Weekly Quiz 11 + Weekly Target:** Prompt library of 10 templates submitted and approved.

### 📌 Enrichment C — Apollo Automation Walkthroughs (extends Lesson 12.4)

The following step-by-step walkthrough is added alongside the Zapier/Make content in Lesson 12.4, as Apollo automation is a distinct billable skill:

#### Setting Up an Automated Multi-Step Apollo Sequence

1. Log in to [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) → navigate to **Sequences**
2. Click **New Sequence** → name it (e.g. "Cold Outreach — UK Agencies")
3. Add **Step 1:** Email — write your initial cold email (use the Cold Email Generator prompt from Enrichment A above)
4. Set delay: 3 business days → add **Step 2:** Follow-up email (use Follow-Up Sequence Builder prompt)
5. Set delay: 4 business days → add **Step 3:** LinkedIn connection request message (brief, no pitch)
6. Under each step, configure the **trigger condition:** "Only send if no reply received"
7. Under **Settings:** set sending window (Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm prospect's local time), daily send cap (max 50/day to avoid spam flags), and tracking (open + click enabled)
8. Save and launch with your filtered lead list

#### Segmentation for Smarter Targeting

Before launching a sequence, filter your Apollo lead list by:

- **Industry** (e.g. SaaS, e-commerce, marketing agencies)
- **Company size** (10–200 employees for warm prospect targeting)
- **Job title** (decision-maker level: Founder, CEO, Head of Marketing, VP Sales)
- **Geography** (UK, USA, or AUS depending on your outreach window)

**Daily Task (extends Lesson 12.4):** Build one 3-step Apollo sequence with at least one trigger condition configured. Submit a Loom walkthrough (2–3 minutes) showing the sequence live with trigger settings visible.
