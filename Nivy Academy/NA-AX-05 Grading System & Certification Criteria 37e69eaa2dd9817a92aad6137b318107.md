# NA-AX-05 | Grading System & Certification Criteria

---

## Overall Score Calculation

| Component | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Module Tasks (practical submissions, 3 months) | 40% |
| Monthly Mentor Review Scores | 20% |
| Final Graduation Assessment | 30% |
| Professionalism Score (logs, attendance, discipline) | 10% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

---

## Grade Bands

| Score | Grade | Label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 90–100% | A+ | Distinction |
| 75–89% | A | Merit |
| 60–74% | B | Pass |
| 45–59% | C | Conditional Pass |
| Below 45% | F | Fail |

## Professionalism Score Breakdown

| Sub-Criteria | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Daily logs submitted (80%+ of program days) | 40 pts |
| Mentor calls attended (all 3) | 30 pts |
| Responsiveness (replies within 24h consistently) | 20 pts |
| No disciplinary incidents | 10 pts |
| **Total** | **100 pts** |

*Score mapped to 10% of overall grade.*

---

## Certificate Tiers

### 🏅 Nivy Academy Apprenticeship Certificate

- Issued to all students who score 60%+
- Signed by the Academy Director
- Includes: Student name, program name, completion date, grade

### 📜 Nivy Academy Merit Certificate

- Issued to students scoring 75%+
- Includes all of the above + "Merit" designation

### 💎 Nivy Academy Distinction Certificate + Experience Letter

- Issued to students scoring 90%+
- Experience Letter signed by Abhi, addressed to future employers/clients
- States: role trained for, skills demonstrated, duration, performance level

---

## Re-Assessment Policy

- Students with a Conditional Pass (C) get a 30-day extension
- They must re-submit the tasks they scored lowest on
- Re-assessment is scored on the same rubrics
- Maximum grade achievable after re-assessment: B (Pass)
- Students who Fail re-assessment are offered a re-enrollment at 50% of program fee
