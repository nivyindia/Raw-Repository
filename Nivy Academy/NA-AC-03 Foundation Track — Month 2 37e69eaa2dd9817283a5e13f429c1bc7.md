## 🎯 Month 2 Goal

Student goes from knowing the tools to actively using them — sending real outreach, getting real replies, and closing their first client or qualified meeting.

---

## 🗓️ Week-by-Week Lesson Plan

### ☀️ Week 5 — High-Conversion Outreach

**Objective:** Write outreach messages that actually get replies.

| Topic | Format | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why most cold emails fail (and what to do instead) | Video | 60 min |
| The AIDA framework for cold outreach | Workshop | 75 min |
| Writing a cold email that converts (USA/UK tone) | Template + Live Write | 90 min |
| LinkedIn connection request + follow-up sequence | Template | 60 min |
| WhatsApp / Instagram DM scripts (India market) | Template | 45 min |
| **Task:** Send 20 cold emails and log responses in CRM | Practical | Graded |

### ☀️ Week 6 — The Bridge Strategy & Upselling

**Objective:** Learn to identify additional needs beyond the initial service.

| Topic | Format | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The "Bridge" method: finding hidden requirements | Video | 60 min |
| How to cross-sell and upsell without being pushy | Workshop | 75 min |
| Introducing the full Nivy service suite to prospects | Script | 45 min |
| Referral mining: getting leads from leads | Video | 45 min |
| **Task:** Create a "Bridge Pitch" for 3 different industry types | Practical | Graded |

### ☀️ Week 7 — Objection Handling & CRM Mastery

**Objective:** Never lose a deal because of a poor response to an objection.

| Topic | Format | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top 10 objections in B2B sales + exact responses | Script Bank | 90 min |
| "We already have an agency" — how to respond | Role Play | 60 min |
| "Not interested" — the 3-step save | Script | 45 min |
| CRM pipeline management: stages, notes, follow-ups | Hands-on | 75 min |
| Daily log submission system (accountability) | SOP | 30 min |
| **Task:** Log 30 outreach attempts in CRM with status tags | Practical | Graded |

### ☀️ Week 8 — Negotiation, Closing & Profit Sharing

**Objective:** Close the deal professionally and understand how revenue is shared.

| Topic | Format | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing conversations: how to quote without losing the deal | Video | 60 min |
| Scope definition: deliverables, timelines, payment terms | Template | 60 min |
| The closing script: from "I’ll think about it" to "Let’s go" | Script | 75 min |
| Profit-sharing explained: how student earnings work | Policy Doc | 45 min |
| **Task:** Conduct 2 mock closing calls (recorded) | Practical | Graded |
