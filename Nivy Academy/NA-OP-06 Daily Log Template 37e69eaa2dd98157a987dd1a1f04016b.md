## 📌 What is the Daily Log?

The Daily Log is a **mandatory daily record** every student submits. It takes 5 minutes to fill. It is your proof of work — and the key evidence used to verify refund eligibility.

**Submit via:** Your CRM (Bitrix24 or Notion) every working day by **8 PM.**

---

## 📝 Daily Log Format

```
DATE: [DD/MM/YYYY]
STUDENT NAME: [Full Name]
BATCH: [Batch Number]

--- TODAY'S OUTREACH ---
Prospects Contacted: [Number]
Channels Used: [ ] Email  [ ] LinkedIn  [ ] WhatsApp  [ ] Instagram  [ ] Phone
New Leads Added to CRM: [Number]

--- RESPONSES ---
Positive Replies: [Number]
Negative/No Interest: [Number]
Meetings Booked: [Number]
Follow-ups Pending: [Number]

--- TASK PROGRESS ---
Weekly Task Status: [ ] Not Started  [ ] In Progress  [ ] Submitted
Any blockers today? [Yes/No] — if yes, describe in 1-2 lines:

--- TOMORROW'S PLAN ---
Top 3 things I will do tomorrow:
1.
2.
3.

--- MENTOR FLAG ---
Do I need mentor help today? [ ] Yes  [ ] No
If yes, what about? (1 line):
```

---

## 🚨 Reminder Rules

- Missing 3 logs in a month = written warning
- Mentor reviews logs every Monday morning
- Coordinators run a CRM audit every 2 weeks
- A log with zero outreach must still be submitted (write "0 outreach today + reason")
