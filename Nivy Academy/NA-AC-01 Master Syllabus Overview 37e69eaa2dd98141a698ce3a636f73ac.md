# Nivy Academy — International Programme

## Programme Brochure & Master Overview

---

## Programme Name

**Nivy Academy International Programme** (built on the ISP architecture)

## Programme Mission

To train motivated individuals from India and internationally into confident, income-earning digital marketing and business development professionals — capable of winning and retaining international clients within 6 months.

---

## The Single Programme — At a Glance

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Duration** | 26 weeks (6 months + 1 week) |
| **Structure** | 6 phases + optional Phase 7 (Extended Support) |
| **Entry Points** | Standard (Phase 1) or Accelerated (Phase 3 or 4, by diagnostic) |
| **Certificate** | Nivy Academy International Skills Certificate (ISC) |
| **Assessment** | Weekly quizzes + Phase exams + Final 50-MCQ + Capstone panel |
| **Standards Layer** | NA-AC-08 Corporate Discipline — active from Day 1 across all phases |

**One track. One 26-week journey. One certificate.**

There is no longer a separate Foundation Track, Acceleration Track, or parallel ISP. All students follow the same 6-phase architecture. Experienced students may enter at Phase 3 or Phase 4 via the [Accelerated Entry Path (ISP-AEP)](%E2%9A%A1%20ISP-AEP%20Accelerated%20Entry%20Path%2037f69eaa2dd981bc80e8c5b5957b0506.md) — the same phases, same assessments, compressed timeline.

## Programme Architecture — 6 Phases

| Phase | Title | Weeks | Core Focus | Key Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Phase 1** | Foundation: Mindset, Standards & Professional Setup | 1–4 | Professional identity, tool setup, business English, contracts, brand auditing, positioning | Professional online presence, Apollo list, media kit, 7-Day Compliance Test passed |
| **Phase 2** | Digital Marketing Mastery | 5–10 | SEO, Google Ads (PPC), Social Media (Meta + LinkedIn Ads), Content Marketing, Email Marketing, Analytics & Reporting | 90-day integrated campaign plan delivered; Looker Studio report built |
| **Phase 3** | AI Tools & Virtual Assistance | 11–14 | AI tool ecosystem, prompt engineering, Canva AI, Apollo automation, VA workflows, CRM management, automation (Zapier/Make) | Prompt library, CRM live, automation running, 3-day VA simulation passed |
| **Phase 4** | Lead Generation & Outreach Systems | 15–18 | ICP definition, Apollo list building, cold email, LinkedIn DM, Upwork proposals, pipeline management, Outreach Sprint | 50 verified leads, cold email campaign live, 5 proposals, 3 discovery calls booked |
| **Phase 5** | Sales Psychology, Calls & Client Closing | 19–22 | Sales psychology, discovery call structure (SPIN), BANT qualification, proposals, objection handling, negotiation, closing | Discovery call passed (70/100 rubric), full proposal submitted, first client closing attempted |
| **Phase 6** | Client Management, Delivery & Retention | 23–26 | Onboarding, delivery systems, AI-accelerated output, multi-client management, retainers, retention, upselling, referrals, portfolio, graduation | ISC Capstone submitted and passed; portfolio live; Placement Assistance activated |

**New total duration: 26 weeks** (Weeks 23–26 represent the expanded Phase 6, incorporating the Delivery Systems and Portfolio content previously in NA-AC-04 Weeks 11–12).

## Entry Points

### Standard Entry — Phase 1 (Week 1)

For beginners, job seekers, fresh graduates, and anyone without prior digital marketing or outreach experience. Starts at the beginning and completes all 26 weeks.

### Accelerated Entry Path (AEP) — Phase 3 or Phase 4

For experienced freelancers, working professionals, and career changers who can demonstrate existing Phase 1–2 (or Phase 1–3) competency. Entry assigned after a 2-part Diagnostic Assessment. Same phases, same certificate, ~12–16 weeks remaining.

→ Full details: [ISP-AEP — Accelerated Entry Path](%E2%9A%A1%20ISP-AEP%20Accelerated%20Entry%20Path%2037f69eaa2dd981bc80e8c5b5957b0506.md)

## Professional Foundations Badge — Phase 1 Milestone

At the end of Phase 1 (Week 4), students who pass the Phase 1 Module Exam (30 MCQ + practical, 70%+) and the 7-Day Compliance Test receive a **Professional Foundations Badge** — an in-programme milestone recognising professional readiness.

This replaces the old Apprenticeship Certificate as an early credential. It is a milestone, not a separate qualification — the full ISC remains the programme's single graduating certificate.

> ⚠️ **Leadership decision required (A3):** Confirm whether existing Apprenticeship Certificate holders (graduates of the old Foundation Track) are grandfathered as equivalent to the Professional Foundations Badge, or whether a bridging assessment is required. This affects current and past cohorts and must be resolved before the first unified cohort begins.

## Assessment Framework — Unified Across All Phases

| Assessment Type | Frequency | Pass Mark | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily Task + Daily Log | Daily | Submitted = Pass | Habit score (gate to next week) |
| Weekly Quiz (10 MCQs) | Weekly | 7/10 | 20% |
| Phase Practical | End of each phase | Mentor-approved against rubric | 40% |
| Phase Exam (30 MCQs) | End of each phase | 21/30 (70%) | 40% |
| Final Graduation Assessment (50 MCQ + Capstone Panel) | End of Phase 6 (Week 26) | 40/50 MCQ + 75/100 Capstone | Certification gate |

**One certificate:** All graduates receive the **Nivy Academy International Skills Certificate (ISC)** upon passing the Final Graduation Assessment.

## 📋 Placement Assistance — What It Means

Placement Assistance is Nivy's commitment to help graduates find income-generating opportunities. It is managed by the Partnerships & Placement department (Dept 06).

**What Placement Assistance includes:**

- Access to the **Nivy Placement Tracker** (NA-PP-05), where active job and freelance opportunities are listed
- Introduction to Nivy's network of hiring partners, clients, and collaborators
- CV and portfolio review by a Nivy mentor before submission
- Referrals to freelance projects where Nivy has existing client relationships
- Introductions to partnership organisations where graduates have been placed previously

**Eligibility:**

- Completed the International Programme (Phases 1–6) in full — AEP students included
- Passed all Phase assessments (no outstanding exams)
- Portfolio submitted (Lesson 26.1 case studies, minimum 3) before requesting placement support
- Attended the graduation mentor call

**How to access it:** Contact your assigned mentor after graduation and request a Placement Support session.

> ⚠️ Placement Assistance is a best-effort support service — it is not a job guarantee. Nivy does not guarantee employment or minimum income. The goal is to create pathways, not to place every student.

## 🤝 Nivy Freelancer Alliance — Invitation Criteria

The Nivy Freelancer Alliance (documented in [NA-PP-04 — Global Freelancer Alliance Doc](NA-PP-04%20Global%20Freelancer%20Alliance%20Doc%2037e69eaa2dd98164aa5ac8b4a770e5c3.md)) is a vetted network of Nivy graduates who take on delivery work for Nivy's client projects and partner organisations.

**To be considered for an invitation, a student must:**

- Have completed the International Programme (Phases 1–6) in full — AEP students included
- Have demonstrated at least one successful real client delivery (not mock)
- Have maintained a Daily Log at 70%+ completion across the programme
- Have received a mentor recommendation
- Have passed the Corporate Discipline standards (NA-AC-08) with no unresolved flags

**Invitations are issued quarterly.** Meeting the criteria qualifies a student for consideration — it does not guarantee an invitation.
