---

## 🎯 Why This Module Exists

The #1 complaint from international clients about Indian freelancers is not skill — it’s **professionalism**: missing deadlines, inconsistent communication, no reporting, and no accountability. This module teaches students to behave like employees of a global company from Day 1.

---

## 🗓️ Module Topics

### 1. International Communication Standards

- Email etiquette (subject lines, tone, response time expectations)
- Zoom / Google Meet professional conduct (camera on, background, punctuality)
- How to say "no" or "delayed" professionally
- WhatsApp vs. Email: what goes where
- **Template provided:** Professional delay notification email

### 2. Reporting & Accountability

- What is a Daily Log and why it exists
- How to fill the Daily Log (CRM entries, call notes, next steps)
- Weekly Report format (sent to mentor every Friday by 5 PM)
- Monthly Review preparation (self-assessment before 1:1)
- **Template provided:** Weekly Report Template

### 3. Deadline & Task Management

- How to break down a task into daily sub-tasks
- Using Notion / Trello for personal task tracking
- The 3-day rule: raise issues 3 days before a deadline, never after
- **Exercise:** Create a personal weekly task board in Notion

### 4. Client Communication Protocol

- First contact: what to say in 30 seconds
- Meeting notes: how to document and share
- Raising issues with a client without sounding incompetent
- **Role Play:** Deliver bad news to a client professionally

### 5. CRM Hygiene

- Every prospect interaction must be logged within 24 hours
- Lead status tags: New / Contacted / Replied / Meeting / Closed / Lost
- Notes field is mandatory for every update
- Zero tolerance: unlogged outreach = unverified outreach (counts as zero)

---

## ✅ Discipline Checklist (Weekly)

- [ ]  Daily Log submitted every working day
- [ ]  CRM updated with all interactions
- [ ]  Weekly Report sent to mentor by Friday 5 PM
- [ ]  No unanswered student/client messages older than 24 hours
- [ ]  All tasks for the week completed or escalated in advance
