## Why This Module Exists

Most students lose clients not because they lack skills — but because they lack **professionalism**. International clients expect a certain standard of communication, discipline, and reliability. This module teaches exactly that.

This is a **mandatory module** for all students, taken during Week 3–4 of Month 1.

---

## Section 1: Daily Logging & Accountability

**The Daily Log Rule:**

Every student must complete a Daily Log by 9:00 PM each day. It takes 5 minutes. It builds the habit of tracking your own performance — which is what every client will expect from you.

**Daily Log Fields:**

- Date
- Contacts reached today (#)
- Replies received (#)
- Positive replies (#)
- Tasks completed
- What went well
- What didn't go well
- Plan for tomorrow

## Section 2: International Communication Standards

**Email Etiquette:**

- Always reply within 24 hours (business days)
- Use proper salutations: "Hi [First Name]" not "Dear Sir/Madam"
- Keep emails under 150 words unless a detailed proposal
- End every email with a clear next step or CTA
- No typos. Use Grammarly.

**WhatsApp / Messaging:**

- Use for quick updates only — not long discussions
- Voice notes are acceptable if the client uses them first
- Never use abbreviations like "u", "r", "gonna" with clients

**Meeting Etiquette:**

- Join calls 2 minutes early
- Camera on (unless the client prefers otherwise)
- Send a meeting agenda 1 hour before the call
- Send a follow-up summary within 2 hours after the call

## Section 3: CRM Hygiene

**Every prospect and client must be logged in your CRM with:**

- Full name and title
- Company and website
- Contact info
- Stage (Prospect / Contacted / Replied / Meeting Booked / Proposal Sent / Closed / Lost)
- Last touchpoint date
- Notes from last conversation
- Next action and date

**Rule:** If it's not in the CRM, it didn't happen.

---

## Section 4: Deadline Management

- Never miss a deadline without prior notice (minimum 24 hours)
- If you're going to be late: notify the client, give a new deadline, and honour it
- Build buffer time into every project: if it takes 3 days, tell the client 5
- Use Notion or Google Calendar to track every deadline

## Section 2B: Slack Communication Standards

Slack is the primary internal communication tool used by most US and UK remote companies. Mastering Slack etiquette before you join a client team will set you apart from 90% of remote workers.

**Channel Discipline:**

- Read pinned messages in every channel before you post
- Post in the correct channel — don't drop work updates in #general
- Use threads to reply to existing conversations, not a new top-level message
- Use @channel or @here only if it's genuinely urgent for the whole group

**Direct Messages:**

- Start a DM with context — not just "Hey" (this wastes the recipient's time)
- If a conversation gets longer than 5 messages, move it to a call or video note
- Don't DM your manager for everything — use the team channel so others can benefit

**Notification Settings:**

- Set status to 🟢 Available during your working hours, 🌙 Out of Office when offline
- Schedule messages for the recipient's working hours if across time zones
- Acknowledge messages with a reaction emoji if you can't reply immediately (this tells the sender you've seen it)

**Thread Etiquette:**

- If someone posts a question in a channel, respond in the thread — not as a new message
- Summarise long threads with a "TL;DR" if you're adding to a 20+ message thread
- Never share sensitive information (passwords, contracts, personal data) in Slack — use encrypted file sharing

**Professional Tone on Slack:**

- Keep it professional but human — Slack is less formal than email but still client-facing
- Avoid sarcasm and emojis in ambiguous contexts until you know the team culture
- Proofread before sending — typos in Slack reflect just as poorly as in email

## Section 2C: LinkedIn Messaging Standards

LinkedIn is often the first point of contact with international clients and employers. Your LinkedIn message is your first impression.

**Connection Request Notes:**

- Always add a personalised note — never send blank connection requests to people you don't know
- Note template: "Hi [Name], I came across your profile while researching [industry/topic]. I'd love to connect and learn more about your work at [Company]. — [Your name]"
- Keep it under 300 characters (LinkedIn's limit for connection notes)

**InMail and Message Etiquette:**

- Subject line: Short and specific — "VA role enquiry — 3 years B2B experience" not "Hello"
- Body: 3 short paragraphs max — who you are, why you're reaching out, and one clear ask
- No mass copy-paste messages — personalise every message with one specific detail about the recipient
- Follow up once after 5–7 days if no reply — then move on

**Profile Hygiene:**

- Respond to messages within 48 hours during active job search
- Turn on "Open to Work" if actively searching — this is not considered desperate by most international recruiters
- Never say "Please find my CV attached" on LinkedIn — share a link to your portfolio or Notion profile instead

## Section 4B: Notion Deadline Tracker — Setup Walkthrough

> **Required tool:** Notion (free plan is sufficient)
> 

**Step 1 — Create your Personal Workspace in Notion**

- Open Notion → Click "Add a page" in the left sidebar
- Title it: "📅 My Deadline Tracker — [Your Name]"
- Set the icon to 📅

**Step 2 — Create a Table**

- Click inside the new page → type /table → select "Table — Full page"
- This creates a database where each row = one task or deadline

**Step 3 — Set up these columns:**

| Column Name | Type | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task Name | Title | What needs to be done |
| Client / Context | Text | Who this is for |
| Deadline | Date | When it's due |
| Status | Select | Not Started / In Progress / Done / Overdue |
| Priority | Select | 🔴 Urgent / 🟠 High / 🟡 Medium / 🟢 Low |
| Notes | Text | Any special instructions |

**Step 4 — Add a filter for "This Week"**

- Click "Filter" at the top of the table
- Add filter: Deadline → is within → the next 7 days
- Save this as a view called "📅 This Week"

**Step 5 — Add a second view: "Overdue"**

- Click "+ Add a view" → Table → name it "⚠️ Overdue"
- Filter: Status = Overdue OR Deadline = before today
- This view shows what you've missed — review it every morning

**Step 6 — Daily Habit**

- Open your Deadline Tracker every morning as your first action
- Mark completed tasks as "Done"
- Check the Overdue view — if anything is there, notify your client/mentor immediately with a revised deadline

> ✅ Once your Deadline Tracker is set up, submit a screenshot to your mentor via your submission Notion page as proof of completion.
