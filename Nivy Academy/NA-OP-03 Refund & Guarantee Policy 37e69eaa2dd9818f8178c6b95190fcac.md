# NA-OP-03 | Refund & Guarantee Policy

**Doc ID:** NA-OP-03 · **Dept:** Operations & Legal · **Phase:** 1 · **Audience:** Students, Marketing Team · **Status:** 🟢 Live

**Tags:** refund, guarantee, money-back, policy, performance-linked

---

## 🎯 Our Guarantee Philosophy

Nivy Academy offers a **Performance-Linked Money-Back Guarantee** — not a "just ask and get it back" refund. This means: if you do the work and we still can’t get you placed, we give your money back. If you don’t do the work, you don’t get a refund.

This is fair, transparent, and stated clearly at enrollment.

---

## ✅ Refund Eligibility Checklist

All 6 conditions must be met:

- [ ]  Attended ≥80% of live sessions (log provided by Nivy)
- [ ]  Submitted all 12 weekly practical tasks
- [ ]  Logged ≥150 outreach attempts in CRM over 3 months
- [ ]  Completed the Final Graduation Assessment (Part 1 + Part 2)
- [ ]  Participated in all 3 months of the Success Buffer
- [ ]  Did not violate the NDA or Rules of Engagement

---

## ❌ Non-Refundable Situations

- Student dropped out before completing Month 3
- NDA or Rules of Engagement violated
- Less than 150 CRM outreach logs submitted
- Tasks not submitted (any unsubmitted weekly tasks)
- Refund requested after the 6-month window
- Program fee paid under a promotional / discounted rate (Founding Batch)

---

## 💸 Refund Amount

- Full-price enrollees: **80% of program fee** refunded (20% admin retention)
- Processing time: **15 business days** after approval
- Mode: Original payment method only

---

## 📄 For Marketing Use (What to Say)

> *"We offer a Performance-Linked Money-Back Guarantee. If you complete all program requirements over 6 months, work with your mentor, log your outreach, and still don’t land a role or client — we’ll refund 80% of your fee. No fine print, no tricks. Just show us you did the work."*
> 

---

## 📌 Internal Note

This policy is the backbone of our marketing claim. Every mentor and coordinator must be able to explain this clearly. The CRM log and daily logs are our **evidence file** in any refund dispute. Without them, we cannot verify student effort.
