# The Problem

The Master Implementation Plan implicitly assumes dedicated specialist faculty and staff across 15+ distinct roles. Without a resourcing plan, the risk is that Wave 1 begins with the same 1–2 people wearing every hat, creating bottlenecks that push every milestone back.

This page answers: **Do we have the people and budget to execute this plan?**
