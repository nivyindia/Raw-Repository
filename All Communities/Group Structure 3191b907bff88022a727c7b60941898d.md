# Group Structure

# NIVY GLOBAL FLEXIBLE ECOSYSTEM – FINAL STRUCTURE

This document defines a **scalable, flexible, global community architecture** that can accommodate any industry, including entertainment agencies and their talent, while remaining user-friendly.

---

# 🌍 UMBRELLA BRAND: NIVY GLOBAL ECOSYSTEM

- One central ecosystem with dynamic hubs
- All networks connected
- Flexible industry tagging for users
- Premium / verified layers for high-value members

---

# 1️⃣ Nivy Nexus

**General Hub:** Global Business Hub (Open Entry)

- Announcements, cross-industry discussions, success stories
- Users select industry tags: IT, Marketing, Entertainment, Fitness, etc.

**Subgroups:**

- By Business Stage: Idea Stage, Early Stage, Growth Stage, SME, Enterprise
- By Function: Sales & Marketing, Vendors & Suppliers, Franchise & Distribution, Strategic Partnerships, Procurement
- By Capital: Investors, Fundraising Startups

**Premium Layer:** Verified Business Circle, CXO Private Network, High-Value Partnership Club

---

# 🔟 PROJECTS, TENDERS & OPPORTUNITIES NETWORK

**General Hub:** Global Projects & Opportunities Hub (Open Projects Hub)

**Subgroups:**

- Open Business Projects, Government Tenders, Private Contract Opportunities
- RFP & RFQ Notices, Joint Venture Opportunities
- Subcontracting Exchange, International Trade Leads
- Entertainment Projects & Event Collaborations (agency-managed)

**Internal Control Layer:** Verified Project Board, Managed Projects (Handled Internally)

**Note:** All projects are **tagged by industry, role, and skill** for filtered visibility to relevant members.

---

# 🔁 ACCESS FLOW MODEL

Landing Page → Choose Category → Join General Hub → Fill Verification Form → Assigned to Relevant Subgroups → Optional Upgrade to Premium

**Dynamic Filtering:** Users only see subgroups and projects relevant to their industry / experience / role.

---

# CORE PRINCIPLES

- One General Hub per vertical for simplicity
- Dynamic subgroups filtered by industry & role
- Premium & verified layers for high-value members
- Internal project management for quality & delivery
- Flexibility to add new industries without creating entirely new hubs
- Maintain moderation, verification, and clear user engagement paths

---
