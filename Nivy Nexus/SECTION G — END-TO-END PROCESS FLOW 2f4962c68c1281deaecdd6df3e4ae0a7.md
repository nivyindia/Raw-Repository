## **1. High-Level Process**

**Buyer → Nivy Nexus → Verified Supplier → Delivery → Payment Release**

This simple flow ensures **transparent, risk-free, and predictable transactions** for all parties.

---

## **2. Step-by-Step Flow**

### **Step 1: Buyer Requirement Submission**

- Buyer shares product specifications, quantity, quality standards, delivery timeline, and target pricing.
- Nivy Nexus validates **feasibility and completeness** of the requirement.

### **Step 2: Supplier Matching & Shortlisting**

- Nivy Nexus identifies **verified suppliers** from its network.
- Shortlisted suppliers match **quality, capacity, compliance, and pricing expectations**.

### **Step 3: Supplier Verification & Screening**

- Verification of **legal documents, capacity, past transaction credibility, and compliance readiness**.
- Only qualified suppliers move forward.

### **Step 4: Deal Coordination**

- Pricing, negotiation support, and commercial terms are coordinated.
- Samples, if required, are organized.
- All communication goes through Nivy Nexus for **clarity and record-keeping**.

### **Step 5: Order Confirmation**

- Buyer confirms selected supplier.
- Nivy Nexus ensures **all documentation, agreements, and timelines** are aligned.

### **Step 6: Delivery Coordination**

- Dispatch and logistics are tracked by Nivy Nexus.
- Updates are shared with the buyer.
- Supplier performance monitored to ensure timely delivery.

### **Step 7: Payment Release**

- Payment is released based on **pre-agreed milestones** or after delivery confirmation.
- Nivy Nexus ensures **secure and transparent transaction flow**.

### **Step 8: Post-Deal Follow-Up**

- Buyer feedback is collected.
- Supplier performance is recorded.
- Insights are used for **continuous improvement and verification of future deals**.
