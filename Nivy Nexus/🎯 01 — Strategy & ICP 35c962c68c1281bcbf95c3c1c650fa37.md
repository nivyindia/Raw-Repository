# How To Make Enquiries Real (Demand Aggregation)

You act as a **Demand Aggregator** — not a fake buyer. This is how:

1. **Your own genuine needs** — Every growing business needs bookkeeping, SEO, and developers. Post them.
2. **Your clients' needs** — If you have even one client, ask what vendors they're looking for. Post those.
3. **Community needs** — Once the Alliance has members, post their requirements anonymously.
4. **Category research** — "We're evaluating logistics partners for Q3" is a real statement if you intend to explore it.

This is not deception. Procurement teams and buying communities do this every day.

# LinkedIn Search Formula (Copy & Use)

```
(Founder OR Director OR Partner OR Head)
AND (SaaS OR Ecommerce OR Agency OR IT OR Logistics)
AND (outsourcing OR vendors OR partners)
```

**Filters:** Geography → UK first | Company size → 11–500 | Activity → Posted in last 30 days
