### 3 Quick Rules to Make Sure Every Post Converts:

1. **Change the Deliverables (The Checklist):** Never use generic terms like "operations" if they do a specific trade. A roofing company wants to see "commercial installs," not "back-office execution."
2. **Change the Document Requested:** Don't ask a Law Firm or a Construction company for a "service deck." Ask a Law Firm for a *"Firm Overview,"* a Construction company for a *"Capability Statement,"* and a Tech Agency for an *"Agency Portfolio."*
3. **Verify the Form Fields:** Ensure your application link goes to a form that adapts to the niche they select, or create separate short forms so a medical clinic isn't answering questions designed for a software agency.
