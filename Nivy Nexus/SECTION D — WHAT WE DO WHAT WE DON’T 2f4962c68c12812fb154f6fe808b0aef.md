## **1. What We Do**

**Nivy Nexus** is a **B2B trade facilitation and supply chain enablement service** that ensures **verified, safe, and efficient transactions** between buyers and suppliers.

Our services include:

1. **Buyer Requirement Analysis**
    - Understand product specifications, quantity, target price, and delivery timelines.
2. **Supplier Discovery & Shortlisting**
    - Match buyer requirements with **verified suppliers** in our network.
3. **Supplier Verification & Screening**
    - Legal registration checks
    - Manufacturing / supply capability verification
    - Past trade history & references
    - Compliance readiness (GST, export norms if applicable)
4. **Deal Coordination & Communication**
    - Negotiation support
    - Sample sharing coordination
    - Documentation guidance
5. **Delivery & Logistics Support**
    - Monitor dispatch and track deliveries
    - Coordinate with logistics partners as needed
6. **Payment Release Coordination**
    - Ensure payments are released **securely and as per agreed milestones**
7. **Post-Deal Follow-up**
    - Confirm buyer satisfaction and supplier performance
    - Collect feedback to improve future transactions

> Our goal is to reduce risk, save time, and make every deal predictable and efficient.
> 

---

## **2. What We Don’t Do**

To maintain clarity, Nivy Nexus clearly defines boundaries:

- **We are not traders, brokers, or resellers**
- **We do not manufacture or stock goods**
- **We do not guarantee profits or deals**
- **We do not work with unverified or suspicious parties**
- **We do not take responsibility for post-delivery product usage**
- **We do not conduct unsupervised financial transactions**

> This ensures transparency and protects both buyers and suppliers.
> 

---

## **3. Key Takeaways**

- Nivy Nexus **facilitates, verifies, and coordinates**, but does **not replace the buyer or supplier**.
- All transactions are **structured, risk-aware, and ethical**.
- Our **success is aligned** with clean and successful deals.
