## 2️⃣ FIRST MESSAGE (AFTER CONNECTION ACCEPTED)

Send within **24 hours**.

> Hi {{Name}},
> 
> 
> Thanks for connecting.
> 
> I’m curating **Nivy Alliance** — a **private, invite-only business alliance** where companies engage through **shared demand**, not service promotion.
> 
> No cold pitching. No spam.
> 
> I came across {{their company / role}}, and it looked like a potential fit.
> 
> If this sounds relevant, I can explain how access works.
> 

**Stop here. Wait.**

---

## 3️⃣ FOLLOW-UP (ONLY IF THEY REPLY POSITIVELY)

### When they say *“Interested / Sounds good / Tell me more”*

> Great.
> 
> 
> Just to clarify upfront — access to **Nivy Alliance** is based on reciprocity.
> 
> Each member shares **one genuine current or recurring business requirement** (what they outsource or procure).
> 
> This keeps the alliance high-trust and spam-free.
> 
> If that works for you, I’ll share the onboarding step.
> 

---

## 4️⃣ QUALIFICATION MESSAGE (CRITICAL FILTER)

Send **only after agreement**.

> Perfect.
> 
> 
> To proceed, please reply with the following (in one message):
> 
> 1. Company name
> 2. Your role
> 3. Website or LinkedIn page
> 4. **Your current or recurring business requirement**
> 5. Timeline (immediate / 1–3 months / ongoing)
> 
> We review manually to protect alliance quality.
> 

⚠️ Promotional replies = silent rejection.

## 6️⃣ POLITE REJECTION MESSAGES (USE AS NEEDED)

### ❌ No Real Demand

> Thanks for sharing.
> 
> 
> At the moment, we’re onboarding only businesses with **clear, active outsourcing or procurement needs**.
> 
> You’re welcome to reconnect once a genuine requirement exists.
> 

---

### ❌ Seller / Freelancer Detected

> Appreciate the message.
> 
> 
> Nivy Alliance is strictly demand-led and not designed for service promotion.
> 
> We won’t be able to proceed.
> 

---

### ❌ Capacity / Balance (High-Status)

> Thanks for the details.
> 
> 
> We’re currently limiting onboarding to maintain **demand–supply balance**.
> 
> We may reopen selectively later.
> 

No further replies after this.

## 🧠 WHY THIS WORKS ON LINKEDIN

- LinkedIn users hate pitches → you don’t pitch
- Tier-1 people respond to **standards, not benefits**
- You position yourself as **curator**, not recruiter
- Demand-first language filters freelancers automatically

This feels like:

> “I’m being evaluated for access.”
> 

Not:

> “Someone is trying to sell me something.”
>

- ❌ Never debate rejections
