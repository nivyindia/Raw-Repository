**Premium pitch:** "Beyond compliance, you need to know if your business is actually profitable and where cash is going. Our Virtual CFO service gives you that — without the cost of a full-time hire."

# Pricing Conversation Rules

1. **Never quote a price in the first message.** Say: "It depends on your situation — let’s have a quick call and I’ll give you an exact number."
2. **Always anchor high.** Mention your Premium package first so the Growth package feels like a bargain.
3. **Give three options, not one.** Clients choose between packages, not between you and a competitor.
4. **Lead with ROI, not features.** "This will save you £2,000 in penalties" beats "we file your VAT return".
5. **Never discount in the first conversation.** If they push on price, offer to adjust scope, not price.
