### Intern Lifecycle (Trial → Probation → Full-Time)

| Stage | Duration | Commitment | Pay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Trial** | Month 1 (Weeks 1–4) | 3–4 hrs/day | Commission only (₹6,600/deal) |
| **Probation** | Months 2–3 | 3–4 hrs/day | Commission only; performance tracked |
| **Full-Time** | Month 4+ (passing probation) | Full day | Fixed salary + commission (negotiate based on output) |

**Probation pass criteria:** ≥4 client deals closed in any 4-week period, ≤2 missed daily report days per month.

**Rule:** Every team lead was a top-performing intern first. Never hire an external manager before testing internally.
