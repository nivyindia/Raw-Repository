# ⚠️ Why This Matters First {#why-first}

Every outreach message you send — contact form, LinkedIn DM, cold email — will result in the recipient Googling "Nivy" within 60 seconds. What they find determines whether they reply.

**Current state:** Compromised WordPress site, no landing page, no Google Business Profile, social links missing from website. Every outreach attempt is being undermined by a broken digital presence.

**Required state:** Clean professional website, dedicated Alliance landing page, active social profiles, verified Google listing. This section tells you exactly how to get there.

---

You need **two separate web presences** — one for Nivy (the parent company) and one for Nivy Alliance (the community product). They serve different audiences and have different conversion goals.

| Website | URL | Audience | Goal |

# 🔵 Website 1 — [thenivy.com](http://thenivy.com) (Corporate / Services) {#thenivy}

**Purpose:** Credibility for prospects who Google you after receiving an email, LinkedIn message, or referral. This is where clients verify you are real before booking a call.

## Pages Required

| Page | What It Must Contain | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage | Hero headline (what Nivy does + who for), 3 core services, trust signals (years, clients, locations), CTA to book a call | 🔴 Critical |
| Services | Accounting & Tax, Digital Marketing, Job Placement, Healthcare, IT/Digital — each with deliverables, pricing tier, CTA | 🔴 Critical |
| About | Nivy story, mission, team photos (even 2–3 people), Lucknow HQ, NGO (Nivy Care Foundation) mentioned | 🔴 Critical |
| Contact | Form, phone, email, WhatsApp link, Google Maps embed, office hours | 🔴 Critical |
| Case Studies | 2–3 client outcomes (anonymised if needed) — numbers, before/after, industry | 🟡 Important |
| Blog / Resources | SEO articles on accounting, tax, digital marketing for UK/India businesses — 1 per week | 🟡 Important |
| Nivy Alliance Page | Short intro to the Alliance with link to the Alliance landing page | 🟡 Important |
| Careers / Jobs | Clean jobs page replacing the compromised subdomain — open roles, CV upload | 🟢 Phase 2 |
| NGO / Nivy Care Foundation | Social impact section — builds trust and differentiates from competitors | 🟢 Phase 2 |

## Technical Requirements (Non-Negotiable)

- Fix and clean the compromised WordPress install before doing anything else
- SSL certificate active (HTTPS)
- Page speed score above 85 on Google PageSpeed Insights
- Schema markup for Local Business (name, address, phone, services)
- Google Analytics 4 + Google Search Console connected
- Cookie consent banner (required for UK visitors — UK GDPR)
- Footer links: Home, Services, About, Contact, Privacy Policy, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook
- Remove broken [localhost](http://localhost) URL from footer entirely

# 🔵 Website 2 — Nivy Alliance Landing Page {#alliance-landing}

**Purpose:** The single page you send to every prospect. It converts curiosity into a form submission. One high-converting page — not a full website.

## URL Options (Best to Easiest)

1. [**nivallianz.com**](http://nivallianz.com) — standalone domain, most premium feel
2. [**nival.co.uk**](http://nival.co.uk) — short, UK-focused
3. [**thenivy.com/alliance**](http://thenivy.com/alliance) — easiest to set up, uses existing domain

## Page Sections (In This Exact Order)

| Section | Content | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Hero | "Nivy Alliance — A Private Business Exchange for Verified UK Companies" | Subline: "Get matched with trusted suppliers, partners, and clients. Invite-only." | CTA: Apply To Join | Creates immediate clarity + exclusivity |
| 2. The Problem | "Finding reliable outsourcing partners is slow, risky, and expensive. LinkedIn is noisy. Directories are full of unverified vendors." | Resonates with exact ICP frustration |
| 3. The Solution | 3 bullets: Verified members only | Real outsourcing needs posted daily | Direct introductions, no middlemen | Explains Alliance without jargon |
| 4. Who It's For | Icons for: Digital Agencies | SaaS Companies | IT Firms | E-commerce Brands | Accounting Practices | Logistics Companies | Right people feel seen, wrong people exit |
| 5. What Members Get | Weekly outsourcing leads | Private Slack community | Introductions to verified partners | Priority matching by category | Value proposition made concrete |
| 6. Social Proof | "Be among the first 50 founding members — founding members get priority matching for life" (or real testimonials when available) | Removes fear of joining something empty |
| 7. Application Form | Embed Tally: Company name, Your role, Website, What services you outsource, What services you offer | CTA: Apply For Membership | The conversion action |
| 8. Footer | Nivy Alliance | Operated by Nivy | [thenivy.com](http://thenivy.com) | [contact@thenivy.com](mailto:contact@thenivy.com) | LinkedIn link | Trust and legal compliance |

**Design brief:** Dark navy or slate background, white text, one accent colour (gold or electric blue), no stock photos. Use Framer (free tier) or Webflow (free tier) — do NOT use a generic template.

# 📱 Social Media Platform Strategy {#social-media}

You need presence on **6 platforms**. Each serves a distinct role. Do not try to run all simultaneously from Day 1 — follow the priority sequence.

---

# 🔵 Platform 1 — LinkedIn (HIGHEST PRIORITY) {#linkedin}

**Role:** Primary revenue channel. All outreach, authority-building, and client acquisition happens here.

## What You Need

**Personal Profile (Abhi):**

- Headline: "Helping UK & International Businesses Find Trusted Partners | Founder @ Nivy Alliance | Accounting • Marketing • Digital"
- Professional headshot (not a selfie)
- Nivy Alliance banner image
- 500+ connections before heavy outreach begins
- Featured section: link to Alliance landing page
- About section: 150 words, keyword-rich, ends with CTA

**Nivy Company Page:**

- Full logo + banner
- About: 250 words, keyword-rich
- Website: [thenivy.com](http://thenivy.com)
- Specialities tagged (accounting, tax, digital marketing, outsourcing)
- Location: UK + India

**Nivy Alliance Company Page (separate):**

- Same standards as Nivy page
- About focused on the Alliance community, not services
- Link to Alliance landing page

## Posting Schedule (Personal Profile)

| Day | Post Type | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Enquiry post (as buyer) | "We're looking for a UK-based SEO agency for a 6-month retainer..." |
| Tuesday | Value / insight post | "3 things UK businesses get wrong about outsourcing accounting..." |
| Wednesday | Enquiry post (different category) | "Urgently need an IT support firm in Manchester..." |
| Thursday | Social proof / case study | "We helped a London e-commerce brand cut their tax bill by 30%..." |
| Friday | Alliance community post | "Nivy Alliance is open to 10 more founding members this week..." |

# 🔵 Platform 2 — Instagram (BRAND AUTHORITY) {#instagram}

**Role:** Visual credibility. Younger founders and e-commerce brands check Instagram before trusting a B2B contact.

**Handle:** @thenivy (preferred) or @nivyalliance

**Bio:** `Nivy Alliance | Private B2B Exchange | UK Business Community | Apply → [link in bio]`

**Content (3× per week):**

- Behind-the-scenes: building the Alliance, milestones, workspace screenshots
- Quote cards: business, outsourcing, entrepreneurship themes
- Reels: "What is Nivy Alliance in 60 seconds", "How to find trusted UK business partners"
- Anonymised client wins: "A member just saved £18,000 in tax this year"

**Tool:** Canva (batch-create 30 posts in one sitting) + Buffer (auto-schedule)

# 🔵 Platform 3 — Facebook (GROUP OUTREACH) {#facebook}

**Role:** UK business groups are where SMB owners gather. You post enquiries there in Phase 1.

**What you need:**

# 🔵 Platform 4 — WhatsApp Business {#whatsapp}

**Role:** High open rate direct outreach. Best for UAE market, Indian diaspora in UK, warm contacts.

**Setup:**

- WhatsApp Business account (not personal) with Nivy logo, description, website, hours
- Business Catalogue: list 3 main services with prices
- Broadcast lists by segment: UK leads | UAE leads | India leads | Warm contacts
- Message limit: 15 contacts per broadcast to start (stay within platform limits)

---

# 🔵 Platform 5 — YouTube (AUTHORITY BUILDING — Phase 2+) {#youtube}

**Role:** Longest content shelf life. One video on "How to outsource accounting as a UK startup" can drive leads for 3 years.

**Start:** 1 video per month minimum from Month 2

**First 4 video topics:**

1. What is Nivy Alliance and how it works (explainer)
2. How to find trusted outsourcing partners in the UK
3. Accounting and tax tips for UK small businesses
4. Case study: how we helped [client type] reduce costs

---

# 🔵 Platform 6 — Google Business Profile {#google}

**Role:** When anyone Googles "Nivy", this appears as the Knowledge Panel on the right side — showing address, hours, phone, reviews, photos. Without it, you are unverifiable.

**Setup at:** [google.com/business](http://google.com/business) (free)

**Fields to complete:**

- Business name: Nivy
- Category: Business Consulting, Accounting, Digital Marketing
- Address: Lucknow office address
- Phone: official Nivy number
- Website: [thenivy.com](http://thenivy.com)
- Hours: Mon–Fri 9AM–6PM IST
- Photos: minimum 5 (office, team, logo)
- Services: all 6 verticals listed

**After setup:** Request a Google review from every client. 10+ reviews at 4.5+ stars transforms perceived credibility overnight.
