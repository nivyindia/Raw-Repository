# Key Compliance Principles

1. The demand aggregation method is **ethical and legal** — you act as a procurement facilitator, not a fake buyer
2. Never promise pricing or deliverables you cannot confirm from a supplier
3. Document all client agreements in writing before work begins
4. **GDPR compliance required** for UK/EU outreach — do not store personal data without consent
5. Always disclose your role as a service provider when pitching on discovery calls
