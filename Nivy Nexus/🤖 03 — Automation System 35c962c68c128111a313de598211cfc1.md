# Automation Progression

| Phase | What’s Automated | Volume | Unlock After |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Manual (Phase 1) | Nothing — all human | 10 forms/day | Starting point |
| Semi-Auto (Phase 2) | Contact forms + Gmail monitoring + Tally→Slack | 50–100/day | 1 paying client |
| Full AI (Phase 3) | Scrape + AI enrich + outreach + qualify + CRM + Slack posts | 100–200/day | 5 paying clients |

# The Automation Funnel (Phase 3)

```
SCRAPE (Apify \u2192 Google Maps / Clutch / LinkedIn)
\u2193
AI ENRICH (Claude API \u2192 personalised message + lead score A/B/C)
\u2193
AUTO OUTREACH (n8n + Puppeteer \u2192 contact forms OR Gmail \u2192 cold email)
\u2193
REPLY DETECTION (Gmail Trigger \u2192 Claude API \u2192 HOT / WARM / COLD)
\u2193
HOT \u2192 Calendly link sent automatically
WARM \u2192 Alliance invite email sent automatically
COLD \u2192 Archived, follow-up stopped
\u2193
FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE (Day 3 + Day 7 \u2192 Day 14 = COLD archived)
\u2193
CRM LOGGING (every action auto-logged in Notion Lead Tracker)
\u2193
SLACK CONTENT (weekly posts to #opportunities, #resources, #general)
```
