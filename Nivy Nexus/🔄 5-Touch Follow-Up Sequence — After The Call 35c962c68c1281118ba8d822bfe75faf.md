# 🔄 5-Touch Follow-Up Sequence — After The Call

> This sequence runs when a HOT lead doesn’t convert on the call, or when you’ve sent a proposal and received no reply. Follow it exactly. Never chase more than 5 times.
> 

---

# When To Use This

- Lead showed interest on the call but didn’t commit
- Proposal sent, no reply after 48 hours
- Lead said "let me think about it" and went quiet
- Lead asked for more information and then went cold

**Never use this sequence for COLD leads.** If they said no clearly, archive them and move on.

---

# The 5-Touch Sequence

## Touch 1 — Proposal / Summary (Same Day As Call)

**Send within 2 hours of the call ending.**

```
Subject: Great speaking with you — here’s what we discussed

Hi [Name],

Great speaking with you today.

As discussed, here’s a quick summary:

Challenge: [Their specific problem from the call]
Recommended solution: [Package name]
What’s included: [3 bullet points from the package]
Monthly investment: [£X/month]

Next step: If you’d like to go ahead, just reply to this email and I’ll send over the agreement and onboarding details.

Happy to jump on another quick call if any questions come up.

[Your Name]
[Phone]
[Calendly Link]
```

---

## Touch 2 — Value Add (Day 3)

**Send 3 days after Touch 1 if no reply.**

```
Subject: Thought this might be useful

Hi [Name],

Following up on my message from [Day].

While I had you in mind, I put together a short checklist of the top 5 accounting mistakes UK [agencies/startups/ecom brands] make in their first 3 years — and how to avoid them.

[Paste the checklist inline OR attach a PDF]

No pressure on the proposal — just thought it was relevant given what you shared.

Happy to talk through any of it if useful.

[Your Name]
```

**Value asset options:**

- Top 5 accounting mistakes UK agencies make
- Why most Meta Ads campaigns fail in month 2 (and how to fix it)
- HMRC penalty guide for LTD companies
- 3 signs your Google Ads spend is being wasted

---

## Touch 3 — Direct Check-In (Day 7)

**Send 7 days after Touch 1 if still no reply.**

```
Subject: Quick check-in

Hi [Name],

Just checking in on the proposal I sent over.

Have things changed at your end, or is the timing just not right?

Either way is fine — I’d rather know so I can point you to the right resource at the right moment.

[Your Name]
```

> Keep this short. No pitch. Just a genuine check-in.
> 

---

## Touch 4 — Different Angle (Day 14)

**Send 14 days after Touch 1 if still no reply.**

```
Subject: Different question

Hi [Name],

I’ve followed up a couple of times and not heard back — which usually means one of three things:

1. The timing isn’t right
2. You’ve already sorted it another way
3. My email is going to spam

If it’s 1 or 2 — no problem, just let me know and I’ll close the loop on my end.

If it’s 3 — a quick reply with just “got it” fixes that.

Either way, hope things are going well at [Company Name].

[Your Name]
```

> This email gets a high reply rate because it’s self-aware and removes pressure.
> 

---

## Touch 5 — Final Breakup (Day 30)

**Send 30 days after Touch 1. This is your last message.**

```
Subject: Closing the loop

Hi [Name],

I don’t want to clutter your inbox, so this is my last message for now.

If the timing isn’t right, completely understood. I’ll check back in a few months.

If you ever need help with [accounting / marketing] down the line, you know where I am.

In the meantime, you’re welcome to join Nivy Alliance — it’s free and members find it useful for leads and referrals:
[Tally Form Link]

All the best,
[Your Name]
```

> Always end with the Alliance invite. Even if they don’t become a client, they become a community member — and community members refer clients.

# Response Routing After Each Touch

| Response | Action |
| --- | --- |
| "Yes, let’s go ahead" | Send agreement + onboarding details immediately |
| "Not yet, follow up in X weeks" | Note the date, set a Notion reminder, respect the timeline |
| "Not interested" | Thank them, offer Alliance invite, archive as COLD |
| No reply after Touch 5 | Archive as COLD, move on |
| Referred someone else | Log as REFERRAL SOURCE, thank them, nurture relationship |
