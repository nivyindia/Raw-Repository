# **Nivy Alliance – पूरी बिज़नेस कम्युनिटी सिस्टम (हिंदी में)**

---

## **स्टेप 0: तैयारी और प्लानिंग**

1. **कम्युनिटी का उद्देश्य और फ़िलॉसफी तय करें**
    - Motto: “डिमांड दो → डिमांड पाओ”
    - उद्देश्य: सभी verified व्यापारियों को genuine leads / contacts देना।
    - Fake / Spam / Non-serious entries रोकना।
    - Small, Big और Startup businesses सब कवर होंगे।
2. **Platform तय करें**
    - **WhatsApp / Telegram चैनल** → Learning और Demand Exchange के लिए
    - **Google Form / Typeform** → Demand submit करने के लिए
    - **Google Sheet / Airtable** → डेटा स्टोर और Verification के लिए
    - **Make / Zapier** → Automation के लिए (Messages, Alerts, Verification)
3. **Optional Services तय करें**
    - Chartered Accountant → GST, Tax, Accounting
    - Digital Marketing → Lead generation, Campaigns

---

## **स्टेप 1: चैनल और संचार तैयार करना**

1. WhatsApp / Telegram चैनल बनाएं
    - नाम: `Nivy Alliance – Business Demand Exchange`
    - Pinned Messages: Rules, Sample Demands, PDF Guide link
    - Learning / Pending चैनल नए सदस्यों के लिए
2. **Welcome / Rules / Tips PDF** तैयार करें
    - Community Constitution
    - Step-by-step guide (हिंदी)
    - Example Demands
    - Optional: Nivy Empires services promotion
3. **Google Form / Typeform** बनाएं
    - Fields: Name, Business, Location, WhatsApp Number, Email, Demand Details, Rules Agreement
    - Submission → Auto-store in Sheet / Airtable

---

## **स्टेप 2: मेंबर्स को Invite करना**

1. Initial Broadcast Message भेजें (पहले ही draft किया गया)
2. सभी नए मेंबर पहले **Learning / Pending चैनल** में join होंगे

---

## **स्टेप 3: Automation Setup**

1. **Verification Automation**
    - Form submission → Make/Zapier → demand check
    - Verified → Auto-send welcome + access message with matching contacts
    - Non-verified → Reminder, only demand text visible
2. **Daily Rules & Tips Broadcast**
    - Pinned message / Scheduled message
    - Sample demands + contribution system explanation
3. **Demand Exchange Automation**
    - Verified member submits demand → Auto alert to matching verified members
    - Non-verified / unmatched → Only see demand text

---

## **स्टेप 4: Members का Onboarding**

1. Members पढ़ें **PDF / Notion Guide**
    - Step-by-step: कैसे demand submit करना है, claim करना है, rules follow करना है
2. First demand submit → Member बने **Verified / Active**
3. Verified members को मिलेंगे **matching demands + contact info**

---

## **स्टेप 5: Daily Operations**

1. Channel में daily tips / rules / sample demands post करें
2. New submissions monitor करें
3. Automated verification → Verified members notified automatically
4. Contribution / Gamification → Top performers track करें
5. Optional: Nivy Empires services promotion (Verified members के लिए)

---

## **स्टेप 6: Scale & Optimize**

1. Engagement track करें → Leaderboard, Contribution Points
2. Encourage सभी business types → join & submit demands
3. Optimize automation:
    - Auto-reminder for pending submissions
    - Auto-matching demands to verified members
4. Gradually manual intervention कम करें
5. Channel clean रखें → केवल relevant, verified content

---

## **स्टेप 7: Materials Ready करना**

- **Step-by-step PDF guide** (हिंदी) → self-learning
- **Google Form / Airtable** → Verified Demand collection
- **Pinned Messages** → Rules, Constitution, Sample Demands
- **Automation Flows** → Make / Zapier templates
- **Broadcast Automation** → Daily rules, demand alerts

### 🔹 Key Philosophy

- Community पूरी तरह **verified, safe, growth-oriented** है
- Fake / spam entries blocked
- Small, big, startup businesses सभी लिए equal opportunity
- Member submit करेगा demand → तभी contact unlock होगा
