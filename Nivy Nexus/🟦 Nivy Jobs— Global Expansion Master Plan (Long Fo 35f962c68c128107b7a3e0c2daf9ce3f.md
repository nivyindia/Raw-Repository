## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nivy Careers is a worldwide recruitment and training commission pipeline. Recruiter employees post real job and internship listings across 20+ platforms in 50 countries, attracting qualified and unqualified candidates. Qualified candidates enter the recruitment and placement pipeline. Unqualified candidates are converted into paid learners through partner training programs, generating recurring course commissions. Over time this builds a self-feeding global talent pool and multiple recurring revenue streams simultaneously.

**The Global Opportunity:**

- 3.3 billion people are in the global workforce
- 1.5 billion are actively seeking new opportunities or upskilling
- Digital skills training is a $325 billion market
- Recruitment is a $650 billion global industry
- Nivy Careers captures value at both ends of the talent gap

**Core Value Proposition:**

- For job seekers: Free access to real opportunities + a clear upskilling path to employability
- For training partners: Qualified, warm, conversion-ready leads globally
- For hiring companies: Pre-screened, trained, and motivated talent
- For Nivy: Multiple revenue streams from a single application — zero cost per candidate acquired

**Revenue Target:**

- Month 6: ₹40L/month
- Month 12: ₹1 Crore/month

## 2. BUSINESS MODEL & HOW IT WORKS

### Revenue Streams

| Stream | Description | Revenue Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Course Commission | % of course fee when unqualified candidate enrolls | $20–$500 per conversion |
| Placement Fee | One-time fee from employer when candidate placed | $150–$5,000 per placement |
| Recruitment Retainer | Monthly fee from companies for ongoing hiring | $200–$2,000/month |
| Resume / Profile Services | Paid CV + LinkedIn makeover | $10–$100 per candidate |
| Training Partner Retainer | Fixed monthly fee for lead supply agreements | $500–$5,000/month per partner |
| Talent Pool Licensing | Access to pre-screened candidate database | $200–$2,000/access |
| Bootcamp Revenue Share | Co-branded training programs | 30–50% of revenue |

### The Global Core Loop

```
POST JOBS (20+ platforms per country)
         ↓
CANDIDATE APPLIES (Google Form / Platform native)
         ↓
AUTO-SCORED (n8n + AI)
         ↓
    ┌───────┬───────┐
QUALIFIED      UNQUALIFIED
    ↓               ↓
RECRUITMENT  TRAINING OFFER
PIPELINE       SENT
    ↓               ↓
PLACEMENT   COURSE ENROLLED
    FEE             ↓
    +           COMMISSION
RETENTION     EARNED
HIRING          ↓
         ALL CANDIDATES
         Added to Global
         Talent Database
         ↓
         Future placements
         + upsell opportunities
```

### Why This Model Wins Globally

- Zero cost per lead — job listings attract candidates for free
- Every rejection becomes revenue — unqualified = training commission
- Compounding talent database — gets more valuable every month
- Network effects — placed candidates refer others
- Cross-sell to Nivy Growth — placed employees work at Nivy Growth clients

- Send Unqualified candidates → training conversion message

**Evening Block (4–6pm)**

- Follow up with warm leads from previous 3 days
- Update candidate pipeline in CRM
- Send daily numbers report to manager
- Schedule next day's job posts via Buffer

### Candidate Qualification Checklist

**Qualified (send to placement pipeline):**

- Completed relevant course or degree in target field
- Has portfolio, practical project, or live work samples
- Has internship or part-time work experience
- Demonstrates usable skills in application form
- CV/LinkedIn shows consistent skill development

**Unqualified (send to training funnel):**

- Fresher with no practical skills or portfolio
- Has degree but no applied knowledge demonstrated
- Interested but missing required certifications
- Shows enthusiasm but needs structured upskilling
