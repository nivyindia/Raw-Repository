# Nivy Community + Enquiry Growth Engine — Summary

## Nivy Community + Enquiry Growth Engine — Summary

You’re building more than a lead-generation system.

The goal is to create a **business ecosystem** that continuously attracts business owners, nurtures relationships, generates referrals, builds authority, and converts members into clients/partners.

---

# 1. Core Acquisition Method

Use the **Enquiry Method**:

Instead of directly pitching services, post opportunity-style content such as:

- Looking for agency partners
- Seeking UK business owners for collaborations
- Looking for businesses open to referral partnerships
- Seeking founders for growth networking

This attracts people who self-qualify.

Flow:

```
Enquiry post
→ Responses
→ Lead capture
→ AI qualification
→ Community invite
→ Relationship nurture
→ Partnerships / Clients / Referrals
```

---

# 2. Main Strategy Shift

Do NOT think:

**“How do I sell services?”**

Think:

**“How do I build a business-owner network around Nivy?”**

The community becomes your:

- lead generation engine
- authority engine
- referral engine
- partnership engine
- talent network
- distribution network

---

# 3. Group Positioning

Do NOT create obvious sales groups.

Avoid:

- Nivy Marketing Sales Group

Create value-driven communities like:

- Nivy Business Growth Network
- Nivy Agency Partner Circle
- Nivy Startup Growth Community
- Nivy AI Automation Network
- Nivy Tax & Business Network

People join communities that offer opportunity and value.

---

# 4. Community Structure

Best model = **Hybrid**

### Main Group

Broad networking + opportunities

Purpose:

- founder introductions
- announcements
- referral exchanges
- events
- business opportunities

---

### Niche Groups

Targeted communities

Examples:

- Agency Partners
- AI Automation
- Tax & Accounting
- Startups
- Local Businesses
- E-commerce
- Freelancer Ecosystem

---

### Premium Inner Circle

Later stage

For advanced/high-value businesses.

---

# 5. Should You Mix Business Types?

Answer: **Partially**

### Mix in main group for:

- networking
- visibility
- broad opportunities

### Separate in niche groups for:

- targeted discussions
- relevant content
- higher engagement

Later, segment by company size:

- Starter businesses
- Growth businesses
- Advanced founder circle

- 35% opportunities
- 25% educational value
- 20% discussions
