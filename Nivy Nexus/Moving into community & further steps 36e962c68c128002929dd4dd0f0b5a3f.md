# WHEN THEY DM AFTER THE POST

## STEP 1 — Natural Opening

Hi ,

Thanks for reaching out regarding the accounting partnership post.

Would love to understand your setup better so we can explore possible collaboration opportunities.

Could you share a few missing details you haven't sent over dm?

- Are you Freelancer or company?
- Name of Company/Freelancer
- Your Location
- Team size?
- Which services do you mainly handle?
- How many international clients you have served till date?
- Which software/tools do you work on?
- Which countries/markets are you currently working for?
- Any Links/Portfolio to refer
- - Name and contact of company president (since its a collaboration)

# NEXT STEP — RELATIONSHIP BUILDING

Now ask softer business-growth questions.

Examples:

- “Are you mainly trying to grow internationally currently?”

- pain points,
- future monetization opportunities,
- segmentation.
