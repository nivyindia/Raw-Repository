# Strategic Master Plan & KPIs

This is the master operational blueprint covering all departments, their KPIs, daily/weekly/monthly targets, hiring, and processes.

---

## Department SMART Goals

| Department | SMART Goal |
| --- | --- |
| Management | Increase company revenue by 15% in 12 months by expanding into 2 new regional markets |
| Operations | Improve production efficiency by reducing process delays by 20% in 6 months via automation |
| Sales | Achieve 25% increase in conversions next quarter via better lead qualification + new CRM |
| Marketing | Grow website traffic by 40% and generate 500 qualified leads in 6 months via SEO + paid ads |
| Finance & Accounts | Reduce monthly operational expenses by 10% in 9 months via vendor renegotiation |
| HR | Improve employee retention by 15% in 12 months via career development program |
| Customer Support | Increase CSAT from 82% to 90% in 6 months by reducing ticket resolution time by 25% |
| IT & Technology | Reduce system downtime to <1% annually via infrastructure upgrades |
| Admin | Implement centralized asset tracking in 4 months to reduce equipment loss by 50% |
