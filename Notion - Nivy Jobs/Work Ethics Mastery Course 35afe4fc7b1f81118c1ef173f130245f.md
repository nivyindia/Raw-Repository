# Work Ethics Mastery Course

**Framework:** Learn → Practice → Master → Lead

**Audience:** New hires, existing employees, and future managers

**Objective:** Systematically build and ingrain world-class work ethics, transforming participants into reliable, respected, and high-impact professionals.

---

## Level 1: The Foundation (Basic) — “The Reliable Employee”

**Objective:** Understand and consistently apply the non-negotiable basics of professional conduct.

### Module 1: The Pillars of Personal Discipline

- **1.1 The Power of Punctuality** — Why “on time” is actually late. Impact on teams and projects.
- **1.2 Deadline Integrity** — Treating deadlines as promises. Personal time management techniques.
- **1.3 Process Adherence** — Why SOPs exist. The cost of shortcuts. Following instructions precisely.

### Module 2: Core Accountability

- **2.1 Ownership Mindset** — “It’s my job” vs. “It’s not my fault.” The language of ownership.
- **2.2 The Art of the Apology** — How to take responsibility for mistakes professionally.
- **2.3 Solution-Oriented Thinking** — Bring a potential solution with every problem you identify.

### Module 3: Fundamental Respect & Integrity

- **3.1 Professional Presence** — Respectful communication with everyone, regardless of role.
- **3.2 Absolute Honesty** — The long-term cost of a single lie. Maintaining transparency in reporting.
- **3.3 Confidentiality** — What information is sensitive and how to protect it.

**Assessment:** Quiz + 30-day manager evaluation of punctuality and task completion

**Badge Earned:** The Anchor (Symbolizes reliability and stability)

---

## Level 2: The Practitioner (Intermediate) — “The Valuable Team Player”

**Objective:** Move beyond basics and actively contribute to a high-performing team culture.

### Module 4: Quality & Precision Mastery

- **4.1 Do It Right The First Time** — The German mindset of quality. Calculating the cost of rework.
- **4.2 Attention to Detail** — Proofreading, data checking, and error-spotting exercises.
- **4.3 Kaizen (Continuous Improvement)** — Japanese philosophy. Find and suggest daily improvements.

### Module 5: Proactive Communication & Initiative

- **5.1 American-Style Proactiveness** — Identify opportunities and volunteer for them.
- **5.2 Clear & Direct Communication** — Avoiding vagueness. Setting realistic commitments.
- **5.3 Giving & Receiving Feedback** — Constructive criticism frameworks. Don’t take feedback personally.

### Module 6: Teamwork & Collective Success

- **6.1 Singaporean Accountability** — Team-based accountability. We succeed or fail together.
- **6.2 The Chinese Collective Focus** — How your individual output affects the entire team.
- **6.3 Knowledge Sharing** — Helping others without being asked. Documenting your work for the team.

**Assessment:** Kaizen Project + 360-degree feedback from peers

**Badge Earned:** The Catalyst (Symbolizes initiative and positive change)

---

## Level 3: The Master (Advanced) — “The Ethics Champion & Leader”

**Objective:** Internalize ethics to become a role model and mentor for others.

### Module 7: Strategic Impact & Work-Life Symphony

- **7.1 From Task-Doer to Value-Creator** — How your role impacts company revenue and goals.
- **7.2 German Work-Life Boundaries** — Mastering deep work to protect personal time.
- **7.3 Sustainable Performance** — Avoiding burnout. Managing energy, not just time.

### Module 8: Persuasive Leadership & Influence

- **8.1 Leading Without Authority** — How to influence people and processes without a formal title.
- **8.2 Mentorship & Coaching** — How to teach these ethics to newer team members.
- **8.3 Building a Personal Brand** — Becoming known for integrity, reliability, and excellence.

### Module 9: Global Ethics & Advanced Scenarios

- **9.1 Navigating Ethical Dilemmas** — Case studies on pressure, conflicts of interest, whistleblowing.
- **9.2 Cross-Cultural Professionalism** — Adapting style for global clients (Chinese, Japanese, German, American, Singaporean norms).
- **9.3 The Legacy of a Professional** — How your ethics define your long-term career and reputation.

**Assessment:** Capstone project — develop and present a training session on one work ethic + mentor a Level 1 employee

**Badge Earned:** The Ethics Champion
