# Employee Club Formation Policy

*For Official Circulation — HR Circular*

---

## 1. Purpose

To encourage employees to form interest-based clubs that promote creativity, learning, networking, and social media presence while aligning with company values and brand growth.

## 2. Eligibility & Formation Rules

- Minimum **5 members** required
- Open to all employees; cross-department collaboration encouraged
- Must align with company values; no politics, religion, or sensitive topics
- **HR Department approval** required for recognition

## 3. Types of Clubs Allowed

- **Technical** — AI, Coding, Digital Marketing, Accounting, Startups
- **Creative** — Content, Photography, Design, Videography
- **Cultural** — Music, Dance, Theatre, Art
- **Sports & Fitness** — Cricket, Football, Yoga, Gym
- **CSR/Community** — NGO tie-ups, education, volunteering
- **Social Media & PR** — Memes, reels, contests, trend creation

## 4. Club Structure (Required Roles)

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| President/Lead | Main coordinator |
| Vice Lead | Backup support |
| Social Media/PR Head | Content creation & promotions |
| Finance & Resource Coordinator | Budget & logistics |

## 5. Permissible Activities

- Student engagement programs (workshops, webinars, hackathons)
- Company-wide fun & learning events
- Online contests & campaigns for students
- Promote company culture on social media with approved hashtags

## 6. Social Media Guidelines

- Content must be positive, student-friendly, and brand-compliant
- Use company-approved hashtags and tagging
- No sharing of confidential info without HR approval
- HR provides brand guidelines & templates to all clubs

## 7. Funding & Support

- Initial budget sanctioned by HR on approval
- Sponsorships allowed with prior HR/Finance approval
- Monthly financial report to HR required

## 8. Recognition & Rewards

- Monthly recognition in newsletters/social media
- **Annual Awards:**
    - Best Performing Club
    - Best Social Media Impact
    - Best Student Engagement Initiative
- Opportunities to represent company at external college events

## 9. Student Engagement Rules

- Collaborations with colleges/universities must be routed via HR/BD
- Clubs can run **Student Ambassador Programs** under HR supervision
- All external communication must carry company branding

## 10. Compliance

> Any violation of company policy will result in **club suspension** and may lead to **disciplinary action**.
>

# Club Playbook — Step by Step

**Step 1: Form Your Club**

Gather 5+ members, decide a theme/purpose, submit proposal to HR.

**Step 2: Structure Your Club**

Select President, Vice Lead, PR Head, Finance Coordinator. Register names with HR.

**Step 3: Plan Activities**

- Students → Workshops, contests, webinars
- Social Media → Memes, reels, trending posts
- Employees → Fun events, sports, volunteering

**Step 4: Branding & Social Media**

Use company logo, hashtags, and templates. Tag company handles in all posts.

**Step 5: Collaborate with Students**

Plan college tie-up events. Run contests that excite students (e.g. “Create a Reel on Future of AI”). Reward winners with certificates, vouchers, or internships.

**Step 6: Report & Recognition**

Submit monthly activity reports to HR. Share engagement results. Compete for Best Club Awards.

**Step 7: Grow Your Impact**

Invite students as interns/ambassadors. Run cross-club collaborations (e.g. Tech + Social Media Club → Hackathon + Reel Challenge).
