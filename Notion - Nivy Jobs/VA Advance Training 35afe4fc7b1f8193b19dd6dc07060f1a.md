## Marketing Funnel with KPI

Understand how leads move from awareness to conversion, and what KPIs to track at each stage.

| Funnel Stage | Goal | KPI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | Reach new audience | Impressions, Reach, Followers |
| Interest | Engage audience | Likes, Comments, Shares, Click Rate |
| Consideration | Nurture leads | Email opens, Link clicks, Profile visits |
| Intent | Move to action | Enquiries, Form fills, DMs |
| Conversion | Close the sale | Clients closed, Revenue |
| Retention | Repeat business | Client retention rate, Upsells |

## Client Interaction Manual

### Handling First Contact

1. Greet professionally and introduce yourself and the company
2. Ask qualifying questions: budget, goals, timeline, current pain points
3. Listen more than you talk — understand their need first
4. Share relevant success stories or case studies
5. Offer a free audit or consultation as a next step

### Objection Handling

| Objection | Response Strategy |
| --- | --- |
| “It’s too expensive” | Focus on ROI, not cost. Show what they’ll gain. |
| “We already have someone” | Ask what results they’re getting. Position as supplement. |
| “I need to think about it” | Set a follow-up time. Ask what information would help. |
| “We’re not interested” | Thank them, leave door open, follow up in 30 days. |

### Follow-Up Rules

- Follow up within 24 hours of first contact
- Use WhatsApp, email, and LinkedIn in sequence
- Never follow up more than 3 times without a response
- Always add value in every follow-up (tip, case study, etc.)

## Roles of Team Leads, Supervisors & Mentors

**Team Lead:**

- Assign daily tasks and review completion
- Conduct daily stand-up (10–15 min)
- Escalate blockers to supervisor
- Review and approve weekly reports

**Supervisor:**

- Monitor team KPIs weekly
- Conduct performance reviews bi-weekly
- Coordinate between team leads and management
- Handle client escalations

**Mentor:**

- Available 12 PM – 7 PM for support
- Guide freshers through live client interactions
- Provide skill feedback and improvement plans
- Conduct mock calls and role-plays
