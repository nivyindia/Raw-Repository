# Sales Department SOP

## 1. Purpose

To define standard procedures for the Sales Department to ensure consistent lead management, client acquisition, and revenue growth in alignment with company goals.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all sales executives, managers, and supporting staff involved in B2B/B2C sales, client relationship management, and sales reporting.

## 3. Objectives

- Generate qualified leads and convert them into paying customers
- Maintain healthy client relationships to increase retention and referrals
- Achieve monthly, quarterly, and annual sales targets
- Maintain accurate and timely records of all sales activities

---

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

**Sales Executive:**

- Prospect, qualify, and follow up on leads
- Schedule and conduct sales meetings or demos
- Maintain CRM data and follow-up logs
- Submit daily and weekly reports

**Sales Manager:**

- Monitor performance against targets
- Train and mentor the sales team
- Approve pricing negotiations and special discounts
- Review reports and coordinate with marketing for lead flow

**Sales Support / Coordinator:**

- Prepare quotations, contracts, and proposals
- Ensure smooth onboarding after deal closure
- Assist in follow-up calls/emails

---

## 5. Sales Process Flow

### Step 1 — Lead Generation

- Sources: Marketing campaigns, referrals, networking, inbound inquiries, cold outreach
- Tools: CRM, LinkedIn, Email campaigns, Calls
- **KPI:** Minimum 20 new leads per week per executive

### Step 2 — Lead Qualification (BANT)

- Criteria: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline
- Mark as “Qualified” in CRM before proceeding

### Step 3 — Sales Pitch / Presentation

- Schedule meeting within 3 business days of qualification
- Use approved sales deck & pitch script

### Step 4 — Proposal & Negotiation

- Share proposal within **24 hours** after pitch
- Handle objections using solution-based selling
- Get managerial approval for discounts >10%

### Step 5 — Closing the Sale

- Secure written confirmation (email/PO)
- Collect advance payment if applicable
- Generate invoice via accounts

### Step 6 — Handover to Operations

- Submit all client onboarding details to respective department within **1 working day**

### Step 7 — Post-Sale Follow-Up

- Check in after 7 days, 30 days, and quarterly for upsell opportunities
- Actively encourage referrals

---

## 6. Reporting & Documentation

| Report Type | Contents | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily Report | Leads contacted, meetings scheduled, follow-ups, deals closed | Daily |
| Weekly Report | Conversion ratio, pipeline value, top prospects, challenges | Weekly |
| Monthly Review | Achievements vs targets, revenue generated, improvement plan | Monthly |

---

## 7. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Qualified leads generated | Min 20/week per executive |
| Lead-to-meeting conversion rate | Track & improve monthly |
| Meeting-to-deal conversion rate | Track & improve monthly |
| Monthly revenue target achievement | 100% |
| Average deal value | Track per executive |
| Customer retention rate | 85%+ |

---

## 8. Tools & Resources

- CRM Software (HubSpot / Zoho CRM)
- Company Sales Deck
- Pricing Sheet & Proposal Templates
- Email Tracking Tool
- Call Recording & Monitoring Tool

---

## 9. Daily & Weekly Checklists

**Daily Sales Checklist:**

- [ ]  Check CRM for pending follow-ups
- [ ]  Contact new leads (minimum 10 calls/emails)
- [ ]  Update lead status in CRM
- [ ]  Send proposals & follow-up emails
- [ ]  Submit end-of-day report

**Weekly Sales Checklist:**

- [ ]  Review personal KPIs
- [ ]  Update manager on top 5 hot prospects
- [ ]  Attend sales training session
- [ ]  Review lost deals and reasons

---

## 10. Compliance Rules

- All communication must follow company brand guidelines
- Discounts beyond allowed limits require written managerial approval
- All client data must be updated in CRM within **24 hours**
